# If you can’t access your content on Apple HomeView

You should see compatible apps, widgets, and other content downloaded on your paired iPhone.

You may not see personal content 1,2 on Apple HomeView if:

- You deleted the same app from your paired iPhone.

- You locked or restricted an app or widget on your paired iPhone. Or the Owner (Resident) or an Admin in the Home app set restrictions for HomeView that you can't change. [Learn more](https://support.apple.com/fr-fr/148407) .

- [HomeView doesn’t recognize you](https://support.apple.com/fr-fr/127250) . When this occurs, HomeView won't sign you into your profile and you'll only be able to use non-personal content.

- There are pending [HomeView software updates](https://support.apple.com/fr-fr/149085) .

- Your HomeView and iPhone aren't on the same Wi-Fi network.

- Your HomeView and iPhone aren't set to the same language. 3

HomeView supports a variety of Apple apps and many third-party apps and widgets. However, developers determine the availability of third-party content. If an "App Unavailable" message indicates that the app or widget is only available on your companion device, [contact the app developer](https://support.apple.com/fr-fr/102435) .

If you use HomeView with an iPad, you can't use third-party audio or video apps, or add remote widgets for controlling devices.

## If HomeView shows a "Device Not Found" or “Unable to Connect” message

If your paired, powered-on iPhone or iPad isn't detected by HomeView or hasn’t been recently authenticated, HomeView will display an error message. Make sure to:

- Update your [iPhone to iOS 27.2 or later](https://support.apple.com/fr-fr/118575) , or your [iPad to iPadOS 27.2 or later](https://support.apple.com/fr-fr/118575) .

- [Sign into your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/fr-fr/111001) .

- Turn Bluetooth on.

- Connect to the same Wi-Fi network as HomeView.

If your paired iPhone or iPad is having network-related issues, this will also affect HomeView.
