# Use your Apple Watch after service

Here's information on how to use your serviced Apple Watch.

- Hold your Apple Watch near your iPhone.

- [Pair your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204505) . When you pair your Apple Watch, you will be given the option to restore it from a backup if you made one.
