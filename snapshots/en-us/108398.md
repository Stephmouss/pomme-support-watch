# Set up Apple Pay

To set up Apple Pay, add a debit or credit card to the Wallet app on your iPhone, Apple Watch, or other compatible device.

To use Apple Pay, you need:

- [A compatible device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102896) 1 with the latest version of [iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , [watchOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108926) , [macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) , or [visionOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118481) .

- A supported card from a participating card issuer.

- An Apple Account signed in to your Apple device.

- [Face ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108411) , [Touch ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102528) , [Optic ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120145) , or a [passcode set on your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119586) .

Learn if your [country/region supports Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102775) . To check if your card is compatible with Apple Pay, contact your card issuer.

## How to add a debit or credit card on your iPhone

If you have an eligible card, you can tap your card on your iPhone to add it. Or, you can add your card manually. If you have any issues adding a card, contact your card issuer.

- In the Wallet app, tap the Add Card button .

- Tap Cards Found For You to add a card that you used previously.

- Tap Debit or Credit Card to add a new card.

- Tap Continue.

- To add your card:

- Tap or hold your card close to your iPhone to add it.

- To manually add your card, tap Enter Card Details Manually and follow the onscreen instructions.

- Some banks or card issuers might require further authentication. They might ask you to provide more information or to download an app before approving your card for use with Apple Pay.

Eligible card issuers will also allow you to connect your account to [view transaction details and account information in the Wallet app](https://support.apple.com/111117) .

To remove a card from the Cards Found For You screen, tap Edit, tap the Remove button , and tap Delete. Then, tap Delete Card to confirm and remove the card from all of your devices.

## How to add a debit or credit card to your Apple Watch

If you have a paired Apple Watch, you have the option to add the card to your watch.

- On your paired iPhone, open the Apple Watch app.

- In the My Watch tab, tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Tap Add Card.

- Tap Debit or Credit Card to add a new card.

- Tap Cards Found For You to add a card that you used previously.

- Tap Continue.

- To add your card:

- Tap or hold your card close to your iPhone to add it.

- If you're unable to add your card by tapping or holding it close to your iPhone, position your debit or credit card in the frame to scan it.

- To manually add your card, tap Enter Card Details Manually and follow the onscreen instructions.

- If necessary, verify your information with the bank or card issuer. They might ask you to provide more information or to download an app before approving your card for use with Apple Pay.

Follow the steps on the screen in the Wallet app on your Apple Watch to manually add your debit or credit card to your watch.

## How to add a debit or credit card for Apple Pay on your Mac, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- On your device, open Wallet & Apple Pay settings.

- On your Mac model with Touch ID, go to System Settings (or System Preferences), then click Wallet & Apple Pay.

- On your iPad or Vision Pro, go to Settings, then tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Select Add Card. 3,4,5,6

- Follow the steps on the screen to add a card.

- Verify your information with your bank or card issuer. They might ask you to provide information.

On Mac models without built-in Touch ID, you can complete your purchase using Apple Pay on your compatible iPhone or Apple Watch: On your iPhone, go to Settings, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, and then tap turn on Allow Payments on Mac.

Learn what to do if you [can't add a card to Wallet to use with Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/108792) .

1. Make sure that you sign in to iCloud on all your devices. On your iOS device, you need to set up Face ID, Touch ID, or a passcode. On your Apple Watch, you need to set up a passcode. If you sign out of iCloud or remove your passcode, all credit, debit, prepaid, transit, and student ID card will be removed from that device.

2. In most countries and regions, you have to be 13 years of age or older to use Apple Pay. In some regions, the minimum age might be higher. The age that children might be eligible to use Apple Pay is the same age that they can leave a Family Sharing group voluntarily. See [About settings and features for kids under 13](https://support.apple.com/102617) to determine the age at which parental consent is no longer required by country or region.

3. In China mainland, you can use Apple Pay on the web in Safari only on [compatible iPhone and iPad models](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102896) using iOS 11.2 or later.

4. In China mainland, some international Mastercard and Visa debit or credit cards can be added to the Wallet app from the card issuer’s app and used for cross-border transactions. Check with your card issuer for eligibility. Make sure you have the latest version of iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS installed on your device. This feature is not available on macOS or visionOS.

5. In Italy, you can't add Maestro cards to iPad or Mac. In Australia, you can't add eftpos cards to your iPad or Mac. In the Netherlands, you can add Maestro cards to all [compatible devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102896) except Mac.

6. In South Korea, you can only add your local debit or credit card to Apple Wallet using your card issuer's mobile app.
