# Find your version of the irregular rhythm notification feature

Due to regulations in your country or region, version 1 or version 2 might not be available. You can verify which version of the irregular rhythm notification feature you have on your Apple Watch or iPhone.

## On Apple Watch

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Heart, then tap Irregular Rhythm Notifications.

- Scroll down to view the version number.

## On iPhone

- Open the Health app.

- Tap Search , then tap Heart.

- Tap Irregular Rhythm Notifications.

- Scroll down to Options, then tap About.

The irregular rhythm notification feature version number is listed next to Feature Version.
