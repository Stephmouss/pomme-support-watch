# Download Apple Music, Apple TV, Apple Devices, and iTunes for Windows

Access your music, movies, TV shows, and Apple devices in their own dedicated apps: Apple Music app, Apple TV app, and Apple Devices app.

## Use the Apple Music app, Apple TV app, and Apple Devices app for Windows

Download and install the three dedicated apps designed specifically for listening to music, watching movies and TV shows, and managing your Apple devices.

[Image: Apple Music app icon]

Apple Music app

Listen to and manage music from your iTunes library, including your iTunes Store purchases.

[Download the Apple Music app for Windows from the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/store/detail/apple-music-preview/9PFHDD62MXS1?hl=en-us&gl=us&rtc=1)

[Learn more about the Apple Music app for Windows](https://support.apple.com/guide/music-windows/welcome)

[Image: Apple TV app icon]

Apple TV app

Watch and manage movies and TV shows from your library, including your Apple TV app purchases.

[Download the Apple TV app for Windows from the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/store/detail/apple-tv-preview/9NM4T8B9JQZ1)

[Learn more about the Apple TV app for Windows](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvapp-windows/welcome)

[Image: Apple Devices app icon]

Apple Devices app

Manually update, back up, restore, manage, and sync your iPhone or iPad.

[Download the Apple Devices app for Windows from the Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/store/detail/apple-devices-preview/9NP83LWLPZ9K)

[Learn more about the Apple Devices app for Windows](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome)

After you download the Apple Music app, Apple TV app, and Apple Devices app, you can check for updates in the Microsoft Store.

## Manage podcasts and audiobooks for Windows

[Image: Apple Podcasts app icon]

[Image: Apple Books app icon]

On your Windows PC, you manage your podcasts and audiobooks in iTunes. [Download iTunes for Windows from the Microsoft Store](https://www.microsoft.com/p/itunes/9pb2mz1zmb1s?rtc=1&activetab=pivot:overviewtab) .

If you need help downloading iTunes from the Microsoft Store or managing iTunes for Windows, [contact Microsoft](https://support.apple.com/en-us/103190) . After you download iTunes for Windows, you can [check for updates in the Microsoft Store](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/get-updates-for-apps-and-games-in-microsoft-store) .

[Learn how to use iTunes for Windows in the User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/itunes/welcome/windows)

Store availability and features might vary by country or region. [See what you can buy in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .

Apple Music availability might vary by country or region. The Apple TV app and features of the Apple TV app aren't available in all countries and regions. [Learn what's available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
