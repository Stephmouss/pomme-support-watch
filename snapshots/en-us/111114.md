# What types of content can I share with my family?

You can share content that you purchase from Apple including music, movies, TV shows, apps, and books. You can also share your Apple subscriptions including Apple One.

## What you can share

- Music from the iTunes Store.

- Movies and TV shows from the Store in the Apple TV app.

- Books from the Book Store in Apple Books.

- Apps that you can purchase or download from the App Store. Not all apps support Family Sharing.

- Subscriptions and in-app purchases from participating apps. Not all subscriptions and in-app purchases can be shared.

- Subscriptions from Apple, including:

- Apple One Family and Premier plans

- Apple Music family subscription

- Apple Arcade

- Apple Fitness+

- Apple News+

- Apple Podcasts Subscriptions

- Apple TV

- Apple TV channels

- iCloud+

App developers determine if an app can be shared within Family Sharing. To check if an app supports Family Sharing, go to the App Store, select the Apps tab or search for the specific app. Then scroll down until you find the Supports section. If Family Sharing is listed, then the app can be shared within your Family Sharing group. If you have additional questions about the app specifically, contact the app developer for more information.

## What you can't share

- Individual subscriptions to Apple Music, Apple One, and subscriptions and in-app purchases from non-participating apps.

- Student subscriptions, such as a student subscription to Apple Music.

- Consumable in-app purchases, such as coins or gems.

- Items that are no longer available in the App Store, iTunes Store, Books Store, or Apple TV app.

- Purchases that you or another member of your family group have hidden.

- Content that was assigned through a child's school using Apple School Manager.

## Learn more

- If you want kids to get permission before downloading or buying new content, [set up Ask to Buy](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105055) . You can't use Ask to Buy to prevent kids from downloading content that someone in your family group has already purchased.

- Some apps, content, and subscriptions aren't available in all countries and regions. [Find out what's available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .

- In order to share in-app purchases from participating apps, make sure that Share My Purchases is turned on for the account that made the purchase, as well as the account they want to share the purchase with. [Learn more about the different types of in-app purchases](https://support.apple.com/102383) .

- Changes to Family Sharing settings might take up to 30 minutes to take effect.
