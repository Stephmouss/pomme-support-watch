# If you can't accept iCloud Terms and Conditions

Getting an error message when you try to accept the new iCloud terms and conditions? Update your device.

When you try to accept the new iCloud terms and conditions on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro, you might see an error message that says: "Cannot Complete Action. This action cannot be completed at this time." To fix this issue, update your iPhone or iPad to the [latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) [,](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) or your Apple Vision Pro to the [latest version of visionOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118481) .
