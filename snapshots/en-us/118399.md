# How to cancel Apple Music

Learn how to cancel your subscription on the web.

## How to cancel Apple Music

- Go to [music.apple.com](https://music.apple.com/) .

- Choose Sign In at the top of the page. If you don't see Sign In, skip this step.

- Tap or click the My Account icon .

- Choose Settings.

- Scroll to Subscriptions and choose Manage.

- Choose Cancel Subscription.

## After your subscription expires

After your subscription to Apple Music expires, you'll no longer be able to play songs from the Apple Music catalog. Any playlists added from the Apple Music catalog or created using music from the Apple Music catalog will also no longer be available. If you use Sync Library, it will turn off when your subscription expires. Any music and playlists you had on your device before you subscribed to Apple Music will still be available.

## If you still need help

- If you couldn't cancel your subscription at music.apple.com, [cancel your subscription in Settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) .

- If you pay for Apple Music through Google Play, you can cancel your subscription only in the Apple Music app on your Android device or at [play.google.com](https://play.google.com) .

Canceling subscriptions is slightly different in Israel, South Africa, and Türkiye. Learn about [special circumstances for these countries and regions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108098) .
