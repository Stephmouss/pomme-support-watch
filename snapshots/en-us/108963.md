# How to scan documents on your iPhone or iPad

You can use the Notes app to scan documents and add signatures on your iPhone or iPad.

[Image: Play the video.]

## Scan a document in Notes

- Open Notes and select a note or create a new one.

- Tap the Attachment button , then tap Scan Documents.

- Place your document in view of the camera. In iOS 26, you can also select options such as flash or an image filter.

- If your device is in Auto mode, your document will automatically scan. If you need to manually capture a scan, tap the Shutter button or press one of the Volume buttons. Then drag the corners to adjust the scan to fit the page, then tap Keep Scan.

[Image: Shutter button]

- Tap the Done button or add additional scans to the document.

[Image: In Notes, you can scan documents automatically or after you adjust what should be included in your scan.]

In an open note, you can also tap the More button , then tap Scan to scan a document.

[Learn how to use the Continuity Camera to scan notes from your iPhone or iPad to your Mac](https://support.apple.com/102332)

[Learn how to scan a document in Notes in iOS 17 and earlier](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/scan-text-and-documents-iph653f28965/17.0/ios/17.0)

## Sign a document in Notes

- Open Notes, then tap the document in the note.

- Tap the Markup button .

[Image: tap Markup]

- Tap the Add button , then tap Signature and add a saved signature or create a new signature. Then you can adjust the size of the signature box and place it wherever you want on the document.

[Image: Add button]

[Image: Signature]

- Tap Done.

To manually sign your document, follow steps 1-2, then select a tool to use and sign the document with your finger or an [Apple Pencil with a compatible iPad](https://support.apple.com/108788) .

## Use Files to scan a document

- In the Files app, tap the More button .

- Tap Scan Documents.

- Place your document in view of the camera. In iOS 26, you can also select options such as flash or an image filter.

- If your device is in Auto mode, your document will automatically scan. If you need to manually capture a scan, tap the Shutter button or press one of the Volume buttons. Then drag the corners to adjust the scan to fit the page, then tap Keep Scan.

- Tap the Done button or add additional scans to the document.

- Choose a location, then tap Save.
