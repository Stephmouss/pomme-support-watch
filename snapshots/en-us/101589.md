# Connect to the internet with your Mac

Use Wi-Fi, Personal Hotspot, or Ethernet to connect your Mac to a network that provides access to the internet.

- [Connect to a Wi-Fi network](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101589#wifi)

- [Connect using a Personal Hotspot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101589#hotspot)

- [Connect using Ethernet](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101589#ethernet)

## Connect to a Wi-Fi network

- From the Wi-Fi menu in the menu bar, choose a network.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- If asked, enter the Wi-Fi network's password or agree to terms and conditions.

[Image: Wi-Fi menu, showing a Personal Hotspot, a known network, and an unknown network.]

- [If your Mac shows a privacy warning or weak-security warning about the Wi-Fi network](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102766)

- [If your Mac connects to the Wi-Fi network, but not to the internet](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101588)

- [If you need help with the Wi-Fi password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102511)

- [If you want to reset the network settings on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105129)

- [If you want to check for Wi-Fi issues caused by wireless interference](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102319)

## Connect using Personal Hotspot

With most carrier plans, you can share the cellular data connection of your iPhone or iPad (Wi-Fi + Cellular) with your Mac. [Learn how to set up Personal Hotspot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111785) .

## Connect using Ethernet

To use a wired connection to your network, connect an Ethernet cable between your router or modem and the [Ethernet port on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109523) . If your Mac doesn't have an Ethernet port, you might be able to use an Ethernet adapter, such as the [Belkin USB-C to Gigabit Ethernet Adapter](https://www.apple.com/search/Belkin-USB-C-to-Gigabit-Ethernet-Adapter) .

Learn more about [how to connect your Mac to the internet using Ethernet](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/connect-mac-internet-ethernet-mh119392/mac) .
