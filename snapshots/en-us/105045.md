# How to keep your Magic Mouse tracking smoothly

Learn what to do if your Magic Mouse is jittery, lags, or is slow to respond.

## Clean the sensor

[Image: The bottom of a Magic Mouse, showing the location of the sensor that needs to be cleaned to resolve tracking issues.]

Find the sensor on the bottom of the Magic Mouse, then use compressed air to spray the sensor or use a cloth to wipe the sensor. If you use compressed air, do not invert the air can while you're spraying.

## Check track mouse settings

In System Settings, you can [change mouse tracking, double-click, and scrolling speed](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/change-your-mouses-response-speed-mchlp1138/mac#:~:text=On%20your%20Mac%2C%20choose%20Apple,may%20need%20to%20scroll%20down.)&text=Drag%20the%20slider%20next%20to,the%20effect%20of%20your%20changes.) .

## Check for interference

Learn how to [resolve Bluetooth issues caused by wireless interference](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201542) .

## Get service for your Magic Mouse

If your mouse is still slow to respond, you might need service. [Get service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
