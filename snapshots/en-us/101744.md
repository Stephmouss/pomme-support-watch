# If your iPhone sends text messages from your email address

With Messages in iOS, you can start conversations with your phone number or with your email address. Set your preference in Settings.

## Use your phone number to start conversations in Messages

- Open Settings.

- Tap Apps.

- Tap Messages.

- Tap Send & Receive.

- In the "Start New Conversations From" options, tap the phone number that you want to use.

New messages now come from your phone number. If you have existing Messages conversations that you started with your email address and you want to use your phone number instead, you need to start a new conversation.

[Image: In Settings > Apps > Messages > Send & Receive, you can choose to use either a phone number or email address for new conversations under "Start New Conversations From."]
