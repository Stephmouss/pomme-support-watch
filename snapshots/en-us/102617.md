# Create an Apple Account for your child

Creating an Apple Account for your child helps make sure that they can use their device and Apple services safely with age-appropriate settings in place. It allows you to easily set age-based parental controls.

## How to create an Apple Account for your child

Parents or guardians have to create an Apple Account for children under 13 ( [age requirements vary by country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125666) ). Creating a child account allows you to manage your child’s Screen Time and parental controls.

If you haven’t yet, first [set up Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380) . If you’re the [Family Sharing organizer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105062) or a Family Sharing parent or guardian, you can create an Apple Account for your child with age-appropriate settings in place.

When you create an Apple Account for your child, you might be asked to confirm that you’re an adult.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- If you haven’t yet, [set up Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380) .

- On the Family Sharing organizer’s device, go to Settings, then tap Family.

- Tap the Add Member button .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

[Image: iPhone Settings showing Family settings, with the Add Member button highlighted.]

- Tap Create Child Account, then tap Continue.

[Image: iPhone showing a Family Sharing invitation, including the option to Create Child Account.]

- Enter the child’s name and birth date. Be sure to enter the correct birthday.

- Follow the onscreen instructions to finish setting up the account. For your child’s Apple Account, you can use their email address, the suggested @icloud.com address, or their Game Center nickname.

### On your Mac

- If you haven’t yet, [set up Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380) .

- On the Family Sharing organizer’s device, go to the Apple menu , click System Settings, then click Family.

- Click Add Member.

[Image: Mac System Settings showing Family settings, including the button to Add Member.]

- Click Create Child Account.

[Image: Mac System Settings showing Family Sharing invitation, and the Create Child Account button.]

- Follow the onscreen instructions to finish setting up the account. For your child’s Apple Account, you can use their email address or their Game Center nickname. Be sure to enter the correct birthday.

## If you need to connect your child’s device to your family group

If you set up a new iPhone or iPad and choose a child or teen age range, you can choose to [connect that account to a Family Sharing group later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380#connect) .

## Reset a child’s Apple Account password

If you are the Family Sharing organizer or a Family Sharing parent or guardian, you can help reset a child account’s Apple Account password using your iPhone.

[Learn how to reset your child’s Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101567#childaccount)

## Settings for kids by country and region

Some Apple Account settings and features have varying requirements for children and teens. Age requirements can vary by country or region.

[Learn more about region-specific rules for managing an Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125666)
