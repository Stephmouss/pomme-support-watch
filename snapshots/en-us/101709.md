# GarageBand for Mac (v10.0): Loops might sound different in Tracks and Loop Browser

Some Apple loops might sound different when played in the Tracks area compared to when you preview them in the Loop Browser.

The loops might have been created with the Global Echo and Reverb effects on. To resolve the discrepancy in sound, enable Global Echo and Reverb in the Audio/MIDI Preferences in GarageBand 10.

[Image: Global Echo and Reverb option in the Audio/MIDI Preferences in GarageBand 10.]
