# Create an Alchemy sound library in Logic Pro for Mac

To organize your own custom presets in Alchemy, you can create a sound library then save your presets in the new library, which you can copy and transfer to another Mac.

## Create a sound library

After you create your sound library, you can access it in the Alchemy browser.

- Create a new sound library folder for your presets in ~/Music/Audio Music Apps/Plug-In Settings/Alchemy/.

- Create subfolders named after the category of presets you want to create. Use these names for your subfolders so Alchemy can properly categorize them in the browser: Arpeggiated, Bass, Brass, Drums, Guitars, Keys, Leads, Mallets, Organs, Pads, Rhythmic, Sound Effects, Soundscapes, Strings, Synths, Vocals and Woodwinds.

- To save a preset in your new sound library, load your preset, click Save in Alchemy, choose your new sound library folder, then choose the appropriate subfolder. For example, save a custom bass preset in ~/Music/Audio Music Apps/Plug-In Settings/Alchemy/ My New Sound Library /Bass.

- If you're using your own samples in a preset, you can create a separate folder for samples or you can consolidate samples in the same folder as the associated preset file:

- To create a separate folder for samples, create a new folder in ~/Music/Audio Music Apps/Alchemy Samples/. Name the folder after your new sound library, then save your samples there.

- To consolidate samples, in Alchemy recall the preset, choose File > Save Consolidated, select the subfolder containing the associated preset, then click Save.

- To access your sound library in the Alchemy browser, click Browse, click the pop-up menu at the top of the third or fourth column, then choose Sound Library. Your library should appear in the list.

## Use an Alchemy sound library on another Mac

You can copy your sound library to use it on another Mac.

On the source Mac:

- Locate the sound library presets folder you created.

- Control-click your presets folder in ~/Music/Audio Music Apps/Plug-In Settings/Alchemy/, then choose Compress.

- If you created a separate folder for samples, Control-click the subfolder in ~/Music/Audio Music Apps/Alchemy Samples/ that contains your samples, then choose Compress.

- Skip this step if you consolidated samples in the presets subfolders.

- To transfer your sound library to another Mac, use an external drive, [use AirDrop](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT203106) , [use iCloud Drive](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT203052) , or use file sharing.

On the destination Mac:

- Unzip the files.

- Copy the unzipped presets folder to ~/Music/Audio Music Apps/Plug-In Settings/Alchemy/.

- Copy your sound library samples subfolder to ~/Music/Audio Music Apps/Alchemy Samples/.

- Open Logic Pro, then open Alchemy.

- In Alchemy, choose File > Refresh Library to see the sound library you moved to this Mac.
