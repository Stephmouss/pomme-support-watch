# If you don't see your discounted subscription pricing offer

If you have an issue seeing a discounted price when you subscribe to an app or service through the App Store, try these steps.

## Make sure that you're eligible

An app developer might offer a discounted subscription that requires you to be subscribed to something else in order to receive that discount. Make sure that you meet all the conditions for the discounted subscription price, as defined by the app developer. Offers might be available for only a limited time. To make sure that the device or subscription that you're trying to purchase qualifies for the offer, read the terms and conditions.

For help finding the terms and conditions for the offer, [contact the app developer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102435) .

For help with a promotion for an Apple subscription, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/HT211767) .

## If you use Family Sharing

Subscriptions shared from other members of your Family Sharing group don't make you eligible to see the discounted price of an offer at time of purchase. The same individual must subscribe to both subscriptions in order to receive the discounted pricing.

## If you already receive a discounted price

You'll always see only the lowest price available to you, even if you're eligible for multiple discounts on the same subscription.

## Update the software on your device

If you still don't see your discounted subscription pricing offer, update to the latest version of [iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT204204) , [macOS](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201541) , [watchOS](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT204641) , or [tvOS](https://support.apple.com/HT202716) . Then try to subscribe again.

## Why did my subscription price go up?

If you cancel another subscription that was required to receive a discount, your discounted subscription will renew at full price. If you request a refund on a subscription that was required to receive a discount, re-subscribe to the dependent subscription at the full price before it expires in order to not lose access to any content. You can check to see if you're receiving any discounts from a subscription before cancelling in Settings.

If the developer increases the price of their subscription for everybody, you'll be [notified before it occurs](https://support.apple.com/HT213252) .
