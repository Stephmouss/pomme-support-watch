# How to sync or export your bookmarks on devices with iOS 10 or earlier or macOS Sierra 10.12.5 or earlier

If you have a device with iOS 10 or earlier or macOS Sierra 10.12.5 or earlier, learn how to update your device to continue syncing your bookmarks with iCloud Bookmarks, or export your bookmarks.

If you’re using iOS 11 or later or macOS Sierra 10.12.6 or later, no action is required.

## About bookmarks in iOS 10 or macOS Sierra or earlier

Starting December 18, 2024, devices will require iOS 11 or later, iPadOS 11 or later, and macOS Sierra 10.12.6 or later to sync bookmarks or access bookmarks on [iCloud.com](https://icloud.com/) . This is in accordance with our [published minimum software requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118308) . If your device is already running updated software, no action is required.

Devices with iOS 10 and earlier or macOS Sierra 10.12.5 and earlier will no longer be able to sync bookmarks across devices and new bookmarks will no longer be uploaded from those devices. You won’t lose any bookmarks already on your devices and you’ll still be able to create new bookmarks on those devices. However, new bookmarks won’t sync across devices with iOS 10 or earlier and macOS Sierra 10.12.5 or earlier. They also won’t be recoverable on [iCloud.com](https://icloud.com/) .

Moving forward, iCloud Bookmarks can keep your Safari bookmarks up to date across all your devices — just make sure that all of your devices are updated to iOS 11 or later and your Mac is updated to macOS Sierra 10.12.6 or later. You can also sync bookmarks to a Windows PC using iCloud for Windows and the iCloud Bookmarks browser extension.

## Update your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch and use iCloud Bookmarks

- [Back up your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118426) .

- [Update your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) to iOS 11 or later.

- After the backup is complete, check that iCloud Bookmarks is turned on: On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings, tap your name, then select iCloud. Under Apps Using iCloud, tap Show All, then make sure that Safari is turned on.

- Open Safari on your device.

## Update your Mac and use iCloud Bookmarks

- [Back up your Mac](https://support.apple.com/mac-backup) .

- [Update your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) to macOS Sierra 10.12.6 or later with Safari 11 or later.

- After the backup is complete, check that iCloud Bookmarks is turned on:

- On your Mac with macOS Ventura or later: Go to Apple menu  > System Settings. Click your name, then click iCloud. Under Apps Using iCloud, tap Show All, then make sure that Safari is turned on.

- On your Mac with macOS Monterey or earlier: Go to Apple menu  > System Preferences, then click Apple ID. In the sidebar, select iCloud. Then make sure that Safari is turned on.

- Open Safari on your Mac.

## Update your PC and use iCloud Bookmarks

If you use iCloud for Windows, [update to the latest version of iCloud for Windows](https://support.apple.com/en-us/103232) . Then, [set up iCloud Bookmarks](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud-windows/icw4a50de3c3) . If you can’t update to iCloud for Windows 11 or later, you can export your bookmarks from your browser to share or sync with another browser or device.

## If you can’t update your device

If you can’t update one of your devices to iOS 11 or later, macOS Sierra 10.12.6 or later, or iCloud for Windows 11 or later and you want to share your bookmarks with another browser or device, you can copy or export your bookmarks.

### Copy or export your bookmarks

On your iPhone or iPad, you can copy bookmarks: Open Safari, tap the Bookmarks tab, then touch and hold the folder or bookmark that you'd like to copy. Select Copy Contents, then paste in a note or file, or paste in another browser and save as a new bookmark.

On your Mac, you can [export your bookmarks](https://support.apple.com/108306#bookmarks) : Open Safari, then choose File > Export > Bookmarks. Choose where you want to export the file, then click Save. The file’s name is Safari Bookmarks.html unless you change it.

If you’re not able to update all of your devices, your iCloud Bookmarks might not sync between your devices.
