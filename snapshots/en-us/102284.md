# Download your Apple Card statements or export your transactions

Learn how to download your Apple Card monthly statements or export your transactions on your iPhone, iPad, or online at [card.apple.com](https://card.apple.com) .

## Download a monthly statement from your iPhone or iPad

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card. On your iPad, open Settings, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap Apple Card.

- Tap Card Balance.

- Scroll down and select the monthly statement that you want to export.

- Tap Download PDF Statement for a PDF of your statement. When the file appears, tap the share button to email, print, save, or share it.

## Export transactions from a monthly statement from your iPhone or iPad

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card. On your iPad, open Settings, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap Apple Card.

- Tap Card Balance.

- Scroll down and select the monthly statement, then tap Export Transactions.

- Choose the file format for the transactions, then you'll see options to email, AirDrop, or save the file to the Files app and more.

## Export transactions within a date range from your iPhone or iPad

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card. On your iPad, open Settings, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap Apple Card.

- Tap Card Balance.

- Under Statements, tap Export Transactions.

- Choose a start date and end date, choose the file format for the transactions, then tap the done button .

## Download your statements online at card.apple.com

- Go to [card.apple.com](http://card.apple.com) and sign in with your Apple Account.

- In the sidebar, click Statements.

- Find the statement you want, then click the download button for a PDF of your statement.

## Export transactions within a date range at card.apple.com

- Go to [card.apple.com](http://card.apple.com) and sign in with your Apple Account.

- Click Statements, then click Export Transactions.

- Choose a start date and end date, choose the file format for the transactions, then click Export.
