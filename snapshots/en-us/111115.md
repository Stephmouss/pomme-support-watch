# Use Memoji on your iPhone or iPad Pro

You can create a Memoji to match your personality and mood, then send it in Messages and FaceTime. And with a compatible iPhone or iPad Pro, you can create an animated Memoji that uses your voice and mirrors your facial expressions.

## How to create your Memoji

- Open Messages and tap the Compose button to start a new message. Or go to an existing conversation.

- Tap the Plus button .

- Swipe up, then tap Memoji .

- Swipe right and tap New Memoji.

- Customize the features of your Memoji — like skin tone, hairstyle, eyes, and more.

[Image: An iPhone showing how to create a Memoji]

- Tap Done.

## How to share Memoji stickers in Messages

Your Memoji automatically become sticker packs that live in your keyboard and you can use them in Messages, Mail, and some third-party apps.

To share a Memoji sticker:

- In Messages, open a conversation or start a new conversation.

- Tap the Plus button , then tap Stickers .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap the Memoji tab .

- Select the sticker that you want to send.

[Image: An iPhone showing how to select a Memoji sticker]

- Tap the Send button .

You can also touch and hold a Memoji sticker to peel and stick it onto any bubble in the conversation.

Memoji Stickers aren't supported on iPad Air 2.

## How to use an animated Memoji in Messages

- Make sure that you have a [compatible iPhone or iPad Pro](https://support.apple.com/102854) .

- Open Messages and tap the Compose button to start a new message. Or go to an existing conversation.

- Tap the Plus button .

- Swipe up, then tap Memoji .

- Swipe left to pick your Memoji.

- Tap the Record button to start recording. Tap the Stop button to stop recording. You can record for up to 30 seconds. To choose a different Memoji with the same recording, tap another Memoji that you created. To create a Memoji sticker, touch and hold the Memoji and drag it to the message thread. To delete a Memoji, tap the Trash button .

[Image: An iPhone showing how to use an animated Memoji in Messages]

- Tap the Send button .

## How to use an animated Memoji in FaceTime

- Make sure that you have a [compatible iPhone or iPad Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102854) .

- Open [FaceTime](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105088) and make a call.

- When the call starts, tap More .

- Tap the Memoji button .

- Tap the Memoji that you want to use.

[Image: An iPhone showing how to create an animated Memoji in FaceTime]

- Continue your FaceTime call with your customized Memoji. Or tap the Close button to continue without a Memoji or go back to the FaceTime menu.

To change your Memoji or to remove it during a FaceTime call, repeat steps 2-4.

## Manage your Memoji

Want to change a Memoji that you already created? You can edit the features, duplicate an existing Memoji with new features, or delete a Memoji.

- Open Messages and tap the Compose button to start a new message. Or go to an existing conversation.

- Tap the Plus button .

- Swipe up, then tap Memoji .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Swipe to the Memoji that you want, then tap the More button .

- Choose Edit, Duplicate, or Delete.

If you want your Memoji on all of your [compatible devices](https://support.apple.com/102854) , [you need two-factor authentication enabled](https://support.apple.com/102660) for your Apple Account, and you need to be signed into iCloud with the same Apple Account on all the devices. You also need to have iCloud Drive turned on:

- In the Settings app, tap [Your Name].

- Tap iCloud.

- Tap iCloud Drive and turn on Sync this iPhone.

To use an animated Memoji, you need a [compatible iPhone or iPad Pro](https://support.apple.com/102854) .
