# Fix sync errors -5000, -69, 13010, and 13014

Learn what to do if you have trouble syncing music from iTunes or Finder on your Mac to your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch.

## Update your software

- Update your Mac to [the latest version of macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033) .

- Update your device to the [latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- If you're using macOS Mojave or earlier, [update to the latest version of iTunes.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290)

- Sync again.

## Consolidate the media files in your music library

Your music library might store media files in several locations. If you consolidate your files, you can correct permissions and simplify syncing.

- [Consolidate your files](https://support.apple.com/guide/itunes/move-your-itunes-library-to-another-computer-itns3230/windows) to store your content in one location.

- Sync again.

## Check for issues with third-party security software

Some third-party software, including security software, can interfere with the sync process between your computer and your device.

- [Check if your security software might be causing an issue](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111760) .

- Sync again.

## If you still see the error

In some cases, there might be an issue with specific songs that you're trying to sync.

- Check if the error message names the songs causing the issue.

- Sync only a few songs at a time. Add more until the issue happens again.

If you bought a song from the iTunes Store, [delete it and redownload it](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118294) . You can also get songs directly on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch.

If you copied songs from a CD or didn't buy them from the iTunes Store, delete the songs. [Import them again from the original source.](https://support.apple.com/guide/itunes/import-items-already-on-your-computer-itns3081/windows)
