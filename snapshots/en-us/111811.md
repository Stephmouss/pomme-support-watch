# Use Apple USB-A camera adapters with USB-A devices

Learn how to use Apple USB-A Camera Adapters with USB-A devices.

## Use USB-A Adapters to connect your devices

You can use Apple [USB-A Camera Adapters](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MD821AM/A/lightning-to-usb-camera-adapter) to import photos from your digital camera or connect other USB-A devices to your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch.

You can use these devices with Apple USB-A Camera Adapters:

- Audio/MIDI interfaces and devices

- Hubs

- Ethernet adapters

- SD card readers

- Bar code scanners

[Image: Apple Lightning to USB Camera Adapter, used for connecting USB-A devices to an iPhone or iPad.]

## Use USB 3 Adapter for more power

Some devices need more power than your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch can provide. If you connect one of these devices, you might see an alert that says “attached accessory uses too much power." Try one of these steps:

- Connect the device to a powered USB-A hub or secondary power source.

- Use a [Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MK0W2AM/A/lightning-to-usb-3-camera-adapter) , which has a built-in Lightning port. Use the Lightning port to connect the adapter to the USB power adapter that came with your device. This provides power to your iOS device and your connected device.

[Image: Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter, used to provide power to USB devices connected to an iPhone or iPad.]

### Supported ethernet adapters

These are some of the USB-A ethernet adapters that you can use with Apple USB 3 Camera Adapter:

- Apple USB Ethernet Adapter

- Buffalo LUA-U2-GT

- Linksys USB200M

- Linksys USB1000

- IO-Data ETG-US2

- Belkin F5D5055

To use the Apple USB 3 Camera Adapter, you need an iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch with iOS 9.3 or later.

## Get help

If you can't connect a supported device, try these steps:

- Disconnect the Apple USB Camera Adapter from your iOS device.

- Disconnect any devices connected to the Apple USB Camera Adapter.

- Restart your iOS device.

- Plug in the Apple USB Camera Adapter and try to connect the USB device again.
