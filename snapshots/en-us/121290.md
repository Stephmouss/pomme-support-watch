# How to cancel an iCloud+ subscription without an Apple device

If you no longer have your Apple device but you need to cancel your iCloud+ subscription, learn what to do.

## Cancel an iCloud+ subscription online

- Sign in to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) with the email address or phone number and password that you used for your Apple Account. [Forgot your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/102656) ?

- Tap or click "I'd like to," then choose "Request a refund."

- Choose the reason why you want a refund or to cancel, then choose Next.

- Choose the most recent charge for your iCloud+ subscription, or the iCloud subscription at the top. Then choose Submit.

Refund decisions can take 24 to 48 hours.
