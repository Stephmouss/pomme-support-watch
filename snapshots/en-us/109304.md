# Set up Apple Cash

When you set up Apple Cash, you can send and receive money with people you know. You can also make purchases when you use Apple Cash with Apple Pay or use your virtual card number anywhere Visa is accepted.

## What you need

- Be at least 18 years old and live in the United States.

- [A compatible device](https://support.apple.com/102896) with the latest iOS, iPadOS, watchOS, or visionOS.

- [Two-factor authentication set up for your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/102660) .

- [Be signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/111001) on any device you want to use to send or receive money.

## How to set up Apple Cash

- Open the Settings app on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro.

- Scroll down and tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Turn Apple Cash 1 on.

- Under Payment Cards, tap Apple Cash.

- Tap Continue, then follow the instructions on your screen.

When someone sends you money, it's automatically and securely kept in your Apple Cash balance. To find your Apple Cash balance, go to the Wallet app and tap the Apple Cash card, or open Settings and tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

If you turn off Apple Cash on a device, you can still use Apple Cash on other devices where you’re signed in to your Apple Account.

If you want to set up Apple Cash for a family member under 18 years old, [learn how to set up Apple Cash Family](https://support.apple.com/105010) . If you're under 18, your Family Sharing organizer can set up Apple Cash for you with Apple Cash Family.

## What you can do with Apple Cash

- [Send money to someone](https://support.apple.com/105013#sendmoneyinmessages) nearby in Messages or Wallet. 2

- [Use Tap to Cash](https://support.apple.com/120544) to send money to someone nearby without sharing your phone number or email.

- [Add money](https://support.apple.com/108370) to Apple Cash.

- [Make purchases](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102626) using Apple Pay or your [Apple Cash virtual card number](https://support.apple.com/119943) in most places where Visa is accepted.

- [Transfer money from Apple Cash](https://support.apple.com/120401) to your bank account.

- [Use your Apple Cash to make payments on your Apple Card balance](https://support.apple.com/102534#applecash) .

- [Receive Daily Cash from Apple Card purchases](https://support.apple.com/119575#dailycash) .

- [Set up and use Apple Cash Family](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105010) .

## If you can't turn on Apple Cash

In rare cases, you might not be able to set up Apple Cash if your Apple Account is flagged for security reasons. [Learn what this means and how to request a review](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126755) .
