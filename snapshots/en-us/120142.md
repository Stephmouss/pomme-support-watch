# Track your overnight and daytime vitals with Apple Watch

Review your health data with the Vitals app on Apple Watch to get a better understanding of your daily health status.

## About the Vitals app

The Vitals app shows you what your typical overnight and daytime metrics are so you can assess them at a glance. 1 When you wear your Apple Watch during the day and to bed, the Vitals app estimates certain overnight and daytime health metrics and establishes a typical range for each.

The Vitals app shows these overnight metrics:

- Heart rate

- Respiratory rate

- Blood oxygen 2

- Wrist temperature 3

- Sleep duration

If you have Apple Watch Series 12 or Apple Watch Ultra 4, heart rate variability (HRV) replaces sleep duration and the Vitals app also shows a daytime view with these metrics:

- Resting heart rate 4

- HRV 4

Factors such as your medications, elevation, alcohol intake, or even illness can impact your metrics. If multiple metrics are outside of your typical range, you get a notification the next morning which tells you possible factors that may have caused the outliers.

## Here's what you need

- An iPhone with the latest version of iOS.

- An Apple Watch with the latest version of watchOS.

- [Sleep must be set up](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108906) with Track Sleep with Apple Watch and Sleep Focus enabled.

- Wear your Apple Watch to sleep for 7 days to establish your typical range.

- Make sure that you're wearing your Apple Watch at a comfortable fit. If your watch is too loose, the sensors might not capture accurate readings.

## View a history of your overnight and daytime vitals

You can view a history of your overnight vitals on your Apple Watch, iPhone, or iPad. If you have Apple Watch Series 12 or Apple Watch Ultra 4, you can also view your daytime vitals which include your resting heart rate and HRV.

### On Apple Watch

- Open the Vitals app. Your overnight vitals are shown by default.

[Image: An Apple Watch showing overnight vitals within a favorable range in the Vitals app.]

- Turn the Digital Crown to view details on your overnight vitals, then tap a metric to learn more about it.

- If you have Apple Watch Series 12 or Apple Watch Ultra 4, tap the Sleep button in the upper-right corner to see your daytime vitals.

[Image: An Apple Watch showing daytime vitals within a typical range in the Vitals app.]

- Turn the Digital Crown to view details on your daytime vitals, then tap a metric to learn more about it.

- To view your overnight or daytime vitals for the last 7 days, tap the calendar button in the upper corner.

You can also tap More Info to learn about what results you may see, what it means when your vitals are outside of your typical range, and the lifestyle factors that may impact your results.

### On iPhone or iPad

- Open the Health app.

- If you're on your iPhone, tap the Browse tab, then tap Vitals. If you're on your iPad, tap the sidebar button , then tap Vitals.

By default, the bar graph is set to D for daily. Tap W at the top of the chart to view your history for the past week, M for the past month, or 6M for the past 6 months. Tap Show More Vitals Data below the graph and select a category to review additional details.

[Image: An iPhone showing overnight health metrics in a typical range for a single day.]

- Metrics: View each health metric, as well as the number of outliers that occurred for the metric during the selected timeframe.

- States: View the number of days your vitals were within a High, Typical, or Low range during the selected timeframe.

- Vitals app measurements are not intended for medical use. Please consult your healthcare provider prior to making any decisions related to your health.

- The Blood Oxygen app is not supported on Apple Watch SE or Apple Watch For Your Kids. For models of Apple Watch purchased in the United States on or after January 18, 2024 with part numbers ending in LW/A or LS/A, the Blood Oxygen data analysis is performed on iPhone, and results can be viewed in the Health app. Blood Oxygen data is not available in the Vitals app for LW/A or LS/A models. Learn [how to identify your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108056) .

- Wrist Temperature is available with Apple Watch Series 8 or later and any model of Apple Watch Ultra.

- Daytime vitals are available only with Apple Watch Series 12 or Apple Watch Ultra 4.
