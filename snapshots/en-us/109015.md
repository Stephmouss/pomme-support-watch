# Set up your Apple Watch

Pair your iPhone and Apple Watch to set up your watch, customize its settings, and learn more about its features.

[Image: Play the video]

## What you need

To set up Apple Watch Ultra 3, Apple Watch Series 11, and Apple Watch SE 3, you need an iPhone 11 or later with iOS 26 or later.* Learn how to [identify your iPhone model](https://support.apple.com/108044) .

*It is possible to set up an older Apple Watch using different iPhone models with earlier operating systems.

## Before you begin

- [Update your iPhone to the latest version of iOS](https://support.apple.com/118575) .

- Make sure your iPhone has Bluetooth turned on, and that it's connected to Wi-Fi or a cellular network.

If you already set up your Apple Watch but want to use it with a different iPhone, you can [transfer your Apple Watch and its content to your new iPhone](https://support.apple.com/104956) .

## Turn on your Apple Watch and put it on

To turn on your Apple Watch, press and hold the side button until the Apple logo appears. Then put the watch on your wrist.

[Image: Apple Watch showing the side button]

## Hold your Apple Watch close to your unlocked iPhone

Make sure your iPhone is unlocked. Wait for the "Use your iPhone to set up this Apple Watch" message to appear on your iPhone, then tap Continue. If this message doesn't appear, open the Apple Watch app on your iPhone, tap All Watches, then tap Add Watch.

If this is your Apple Watch, tap Set Up for Myself. Or tap Set Up for a Family Member, then [follow the steps to set up the watch for someone in your family](https://support.apple.com/109036) .

Keep your Apple Watch and iPhone close together until you complete these steps.

[Image: Apple Watch and iPhone pairing screens]

## Hold your iPhone over the animation on your Apple Watch

Center the watch face in the viewfinder on your iPhone. Wait for a message to say that your Apple Watch is paired.

If you can't use the camera, or the pairing animation doesn't appear or your iPhone can't read it, tap Pair Apple Watch Manually, then follow the prompts.

[Image: iPhone aligned with Apple Watch during pairing]

## Set up your Apple Watch as new or restore from a backup

If this is your first Apple Watch, tap Set Up as New Apple Watch. You might have to update your watch to the latest version of watchOS before you can continue.

If you've set up another Apple Watch with your current iPhone, a screen appears that says Make This Your New Apple Watch. Tap Apps & Data and Settings to see how Express Setup will configure your watch, then tap Continue.

If you want to choose how your watch is set up, tap Customize Settings, then choose a backup from another previous Apple Watch to restore. Or tap Set Up as New Apple Watch if you want to fully customize your watch settings.

Your Apple Watch might also require an iOS update on your iPhone before you can set up the watch. If your iPhone says that a software update is available, tap Update Now and wait for the update process to finish.

Choose which wrist you wear your Apple Watch on, then tap Continue. Read the Terms and Conditions and tap Agree.

## Sign in to your Apple Account

If asked, enter your Apple Account password. If you aren’t asked, you can sign in later from the Apple Watch app on your iPhone. Tap General, then tap Apple Account, then sign in. Certain features that require a cellular phone number won’t work on cellular models of Apple Watch unless you sign in to iCloud.

If Find My isn't set up on your iPhone, you'll be asked to turn on Activation Lock. If an Activation Lock screen appears, your Apple Watch is already linked to an Apple Account. You need to enter the email address and password for that Apple Account to continue set up. If your Apple Watch was previously owned, you might need to contact the previous owner to remove Activation Lock.

## Create a passcode

You can skip creating a passcode, but you need one for features like Apple Pay. On your iPhone, tap Create a Passcode or Add a Long Passcode, then switch to your Apple Watch to enter your new code. To skip, tap Don't Add Passcode.

[Image: Apple Watch Passcode setup screen]

## Customize your settings

Select your preferred text size and whether to use bold text.

If you didn't use Express Setup, your Apple Watch shows you which settings it shares with your iPhone. If you turned on features such as Find My, Location Services, Wi-Fi Calling, and Diagnostics for your iPhone, these settings automatically turn on for your Apple Watch.

Next, add or update details such as your date of birth to make sure that your Fitness and Health data are accurate. You can also turn on health notifications such as noise levels, and details about Emergency SOS and Fall Detection appear.

## Learn about cellular, gestures, Apple Watch Ultra features, and Apple Pay

On cellular models of Apple Watch, you can [set up cellular](https://support.apple.com/119601) .

If you have an Apple Watch model that supports gestures, you'll see animations demonstrating the wrist flick and double tap gestures. You can also choose options for the Depth app, and if you have Apple Watch Ultra you can choose options for the Action button.

Finally, you can set up Apple Pay.

## Keep your devices close as they sync

Your Apple Watch shows the watch face when the pairing procedure is finished and your watch is ready to use. Keep your Apple Watch and iPhone close together so the devices can continue syncing information in the background.

## Get help

If you're having trouble, see if your issue is listed below. If you still need help, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .

### If your Apple Watch won't turn on

You might need to [charge your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/108927) .

### If you're asked for a passcode

Your Apple Watch is still paired with another iPhone. If you don't remember the passcode, you need to [erase your Apple Watch, then set it up again](https://support.apple.com/108348) .

### If an Activation Lock screen appears

Your Apple Watch is linked to an Apple Account. You need to enter the email address and password for that Apple Account to continue set up. If your Apple Watch was previously owned, you might need to [contact the previous owner to remove Activation Lock](https://support.apple.com/102573) .

### If you can't set up your Apple Watch or you get an error message

- [Make sure that your iPhone has the latest version of iOS](https://support.apple.com/118575) .

- [Turn off your iPhone and turn it back on](https://support.apple.com/118259) .

- [Turn off your Apple Watch and turn it back on](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apd521a8a902) .
