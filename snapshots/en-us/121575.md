# European Union countries and regions where some features function differently

Users based in the European Union have the ability to set a broader range of default apps, delete some built-in apps, and can use alternative app marketplaces.

## The Digital Markets Act in the European Union

Some features might function differently due to the Digital Markets Act in countries and regions of the European Union. This includes the ability to [use alternative app marketplaces](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118110) , [set a broader range of default apps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121430) on your device, and the ability to [delete some built-in apps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121327) .
