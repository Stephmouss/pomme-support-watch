# Set up your document in Pages on iPhone, iPad, or Mac

To get started on a document in Pages, you can choose what kind of document you want to create, change margins, change how your text looks, and add page numbers and other content to headers and footers.

## Choose a document type

Your Pages document can be either a word-processing document or a page layout document. Decide which type of document you want to create based on the type of content you’re creating.

[Learn how to choose a document type](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT212941)

## Change page size, page orientation, and margins

After you choose a document type, you can choose a page size, choose portrait or landscape orientation, and set the margins for your document.

[Learn how to choose a page size and orientation, and how to set margins for your document](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT212943)

## Change how text looks

Change the color, the alignment, and the spacing of the text in your document.

[Learn how to adjust text in your document](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT212947)

## Add page numbers and other content to headers and footers

You can add content like page numbers and text to headers and footers, and change how the text in headers and the content looks.

[Learn how to work with headers and footers](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT212949)
