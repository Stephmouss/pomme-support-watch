# Get an Apple Music family subscription

With an Apple Music family subscription, up to six people can enjoy unlimited access to Apple Music on their devices.

Each member of an Apple Music family subscription can stream millions of songs from the Apple Music catalog. Each family member gets their own personal music library and music recommendations based on what they love to listen to.

## Set up Family Sharing

[Set up Family Sharing on your device and invite family members to join your family group](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380)

If you already set up Family Sharing, everyone in your family group will automatically have access to Apple Music after you subscribe.

[Learn more about Apple Music pricing](https://www.apple.com/apple-music/)

## Get an Apple Music family subscription

- Open the Apple Music app.

- Go to Home.

- Tap or click the trial offer*, then tap or click See More Plans.

- Choose Family, then tap or click Start Trial.

- Sign in to the Apple Account that you use to make purchases. If you don’t have an Apple Account, you can follow the steps on the screen to create a new one.

- Confirm your billing information and [add a valid payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118429) .

- Tap or click Join.

[Learn how to accept or decline an invitation to a family](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380#join)

### If you already have an Apple Music individual or student subscription

If you're the family organizer, you can [change your subscription to a family subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118448) .

If someone in the family group already has an individual or student subscription to Apple Music, renewal of that subscription is automatically canceled when the family organizer subscribes to a family subscription.

*One trial per person or family.
