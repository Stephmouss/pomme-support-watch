# How to turn a Live Photo into a video on your iPhone

You can select a Live Photo in your Albums and turn it into a video to save or share.

- In the Photos app, swipe up to find Media Types, then tap Live Photos.

- Tap to select a Live Photo.

- Tap the More button in the upper-right corner, then select Save as Video.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

You can find your new video in the Recently Saved album. The Live Photo will remain accessible in the Live Photos album.

You can [use iMovie to combine multiple videos](https://support.apple.com/guide/imovie-iphone/welcome/ios) that were created from Live Photos.
