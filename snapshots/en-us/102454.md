# About fast charge on Apple Watch

Learn about Apple Watch models that support fast charge, and how to make sure your watch charges as quickly as possible.

Fast charging is supported on Apple Watch Series 7 and later, Apple Watch SE 3, and Apple Watch Ultra and later.

## Charge for 15 minutes to get hours of use

- Apple Watch Series 12 : 15 minutes of charging gives you up to 12 additional hours of battery life.

- Apple Watch Ultra 4 : 15 minutes of charging gives you up to 18 additional hours of battery life.

- Apple Watch SE 3 : 15 minutes of charging gives you up to 8 additional hours of battery life.

## Charge from 0 percent to 80 percent

- Apple Watch Series 10 and later charge from 0 percent to 80 percent in about 30 minutes.

- Apple Watch Series 7, Series 8, and Series 9 charge from 0 percent to 80 percent in about 45 minutes.

- Apple Watch SE 3 charges from 0 percent to 80 percent in about 45 minutes.

- Apple Watch Ultra 3 and later charge from 0 percent to 80 percent in about 45 minutes.

- Apple Watch Ultra and Apple Watch Ultra 2 charge from 0 percent to 80 percent in about an hour.

## What you need

Fast charging requires an Apple USB-C Magnetic Fast Charging Cable. You can identify this cable from other chargers by the USB-C connector and the aluminum around the magnetic charger.

[Image: Apple Watch Magnetic Fast Charger to USB-C Cable]

In countries with WPT regulation, Apple Watch is sold with a WPT-compliant charging cable when required. If your charger has a "WPT" label on the aluminum puck, then your charger is WPT-compliant. WPT-compliant chargers don't support fast charging. [Learn more about WPT-compliant Apple Watch charging](https://support.apple.com/120920) .

You also need one of these power adapters:

- Apple 18W, 20W, 29W, 30W, 35W, 61W, 87W, 96W, or 140W USB-C Power Adapter

- A comparable third-party USB-C power adapter that supports USB Power Delivery (USB-PD) of 5W or greater

If you're not sure which Apple Power Adapter you have, check the wattage on the top or bottom of the adapter:

[Image: Apple Power Adapter showing the wattage]

## Tips for getting the fastest charging speed

- Use the charger that came in the box with your Apple Watch, since that charger is guaranteed to provide the fastest charging speeds for your particular Apple Watch model. For example, the charger that comes with Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 charges those models faster than older chargers. To distinguish the charger that came with your Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4, look for model number A3277 printed on the part of the USB-C plug that you insert into your computer or power adapter.

- Avoid extreme temperatures when charging your Apple Watch. Fast charging might not work in conditions that are too cold or too hot.

- Don't use your Apple Watch while it's charging. Charging takes longer if you keep the display on, install software updates, or transfer media when charging. Charging can also take longer if it's the first time you fast charge Apple Watch after pairing with iPhone.

- If you set up cellular on your Apple Watch, keep your paired iPhone within range of your Apple Watch when charging.
