# Use dual-network debit cards with Apple Pay in Australia

In Australia, you can add eligible dual-network debit cards to use Apple Pay on eligible devices.

A dual-network debit card allows payments to be processed through the eftpos network or one of the international debit networks, Mastercard Debit or Visa Debit.

When you make a transaction in-store, the merchant chooses whether the transaction made by a dual-network debit card will be processed through the eftpos network or the international network.

Dual-network debit cards aren't available from all banks. Contact your bank for more information.

## Determine if your card is a dual-network debit card

You can determine if your debit card in Wallet supports Mastercard, Visa, and eftpos networks on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

### On your iPhone

[Image: Card number details in the Wallet app. The last four digits of the physical card number, Mastercard network card number, and eftpos card number are listed separately.]

- Open the Wallet app.

- Tap your card.

- Tap the More button .

- Tap Card Number.

### On your Apple Watch

[Image: Card number details in the Wallet app on Apple Watch. The Mastercard and eftpos network card numbers are listed under "Device Account Number".]

- Open the Wallet app.

- Select your dual-network debit card.

- Tap Card Details.

After following the steps above, if you see eftpos and an international debit network, then the card is a dual-network debit card.
