# Use Siri to play music and podcasts

Discover all the ways to use Siri to play the music and podcasts that you love to listen to. Learn tips for using Siri, and how to control whatʼs playing throughout your home with just your voice.

## How to use Siri

To activate Siri on your Apple device, use your voice or press a button. You can also [type your request to Siri](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphaff1d606/ios) .

[Learn how to use Siri on your Apple device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105020) .

## Use Siri with Apple Music

If you have an individual, family, or student subscription to Apple Music, you can ask Siri to play anything from the Apple Music catalog, find songs by lyrics, add music to your library, and more.

[Image: siri-control-play-song-icon]

Play a song, album, or artist

- "Hey Siri, play BIRDS OF A FEATHER by Billie Eilish."

- "Siri, play Dua Lipa's album Radical Optimism."

- "Hey Siri, play music by Lainey Wilson."

[Image: siri-music-play-hits-icon]

Play hits from any decade or genre

- "Siri, play some 90s alternative music."

- "Hey Siri, play some popular hip-hop songs."

- "Siri, play some 80s music."

Tip: Siri takes the work out of choosing what to play next. Just ask Siri to play a song, and similar songs will play automatically.

[Image: siri-music-mood-icon]

Play music that fits your mood

- "Hey Siri, play some chill music."

- "Siri, play music for a rainy day."

- "Hey Siri, play something happy."

[Image: siri-music-activity-icon]

Play music for an activity

- "Siri, play music to study."

- "Hey Siri, play some dinner party music."

- "Siri, play some workout music."

[Image: siri-music-like-icon]

Tell Siri what you like and don't like

- "Hey Siri, I like this song."

- "Siri, I don't like this song."

- "Hey Siri, play more music like this."

[Image: siri-music-favorites-icon]

Play music picked just for you

- "Siri, play some music I like."

- "Hey Siri, play my favorites mix."

- "Siri, play my personal station."

Tip: Did you know that Siri can replay your top songs of the year? Just say "Hey Siri, play Replay playlist."

[Image: siri-music-playlist-icon]

Find out what's playing

- "Hey Siri, who sings this song?"

- "Siri, what album is this on?"

- "Hey Siri, what's the name of this song?"

[Image: siri-music-library-icon]

Add music to your library or playlist

- "Siri, add this song to my library."

- "Hey Siri, add this album to my library."

- "Siri, add this song to my workout playlist."

Tip: Did you know that Siri can find a song just by the lyrics? Say "Hey Siri, play the song with the lyrics," then say the lyrics.

[Image: siri-music-radio-icon]

Listen to live and on-demand radio

- "Hey Siri, play R&B Now Radio."

- "Siri, play Apple Music 1."

- "Hey Siri, play Rap Life Radio."

[Image: siri-music-control-play-icon]

Control what's playing

- "Siri, pause."

- "Hey Siri, skip this song."

- "Siri, repeat this song."

- "Hey Siri, turn up the volume."

## Use Siri with Apple Podcasts

Listen to your favorite podcasts, follow shows, and control playback—all with Siri.

Here are some things you can do.

[Image: siri-podcast-listen]

Play and follow podcasts

- "Siri, play the BrainStuff podcast."

- "Hey Siri, play the latest episode of Apple News in Conversation."

[Image: siri-podscast-control-play-icon]

Control playback

- "Siri, pause."

- "Hey Siri, rewind 30 seconds."

- "Siri, play this twice as fast."

- "Hey Siri, skip ahead 10 minutes."

## Use Siri to control audio throughout your home

Ask Siri to play music in one room and stream a podcast to another. Or ask Siri to play the same audio everywhere in your home. And control what's playing with just your voice.

To get started, just [set up your AirPlay-compatible speakers in the Home app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104998) .

[Image: siri-home-play-anywhere-icon]

Play it anywhere or everywhere

- "Siri, play Bon Jovi in the kitchen."

- "Hey Siri, play this podcast in the bedroom."

- "Siri, play Bon Jovi in the office and kitchen."

- "Hey Siri, play this everywhere."

[Image: siri-home-control-play-icon]

Control where it’s playing

- "Siri, stop playing music everywhere."

- "Hey Siri, set the volume in the living room to 20 percent."

- "Siri, move this music to the bedroom."

- "Hey Siri, stop playing music in the bedroom."

Siri availability, features, and commands vary by language, and country and region. Learn [which Siri features are supported in your country or region](https://www.apple.com/ios/feature-availability/) .

Apple Music and Apple Podcasts availability might vary by country or region. [Learn what's available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
