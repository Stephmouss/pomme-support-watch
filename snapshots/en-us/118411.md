## On Apple TV, smart TV, or streaming device

Turn on subtitles or audio languages using the remote for your Apple TV, smart TV, or streaming device.

### Subtitles on Apple TV, smart TV, or streaming device

- On a Siri Remote or Apple TV Remote, press the Play/Pause button while watching a video to bring up the playback controls. Or on a remote for a smart TV, streaming device, or game console, press the button that brings up the video timeline.

- Use your Siri Remote or Apple TV Remote to swipe up and select the Subtitles button . Or use your smart TV, streaming device, or game console remote to navigate to the Subtitles button .

- Turn subtitles on.

- To change the language, tap Languages and choose the one you want.

- To change the look of your subtitles, tap Style, then choose the one you want.

To change the default subtitles language in Settings, go to Settings > Video and Audio > Audio > Subtitle Language, then choose the language that you want. If you don't want automatic subtitles and you're using an Apple TV, go to Settings > Video and Audio, then turn off Automatic Subtitles. If you're using a smart TV or streaming device, go to Settings > General, then turn off Automatic Subtitles.

[Learn how to turn on CC or SDH by default](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118411#default)

On Apple TV (3rd generation), hold down Select on your remote for three seconds while a video is playing to see the Subtitles tab.

### Audio languages on Apple TV, smart TV, or streaming device

- On a Siri Remote or Apple TV Remote, press the Play/Pause button while watching a video to bring up the playback controls. Or on a remote for a smart TV, streaming device, or game console, press the button that brings up the video timeline.

- Use your Siri Remote or Apple TV Remote to swipe up and select the Audio button . Or use your smart TV, streaming device, or game console remote to navigate to the Audio button .

- Choose the audio language that you want.

To change the default audio language in Settings, go to Settings > Video and Audio > Audio > Audio Language, then choose the language that you want.

## On iPhone, iPad, or Android mobile device

Turn on subtitles or audio languages on your iPhone, iPad, or Android mobile device.

### Subtitles on your iPhone, iPad, or Android mobile device

- While watching a video, tap the screen to open the playback controls.

- Tap the Subtitles button , then turn subtitles on.

[Image: A video is paused on an iPhone. In the bottom right corner, the Subtitles button is highlighted.]

- To change the language, tap Languages and choose the one you want.

- To change the look of your subtitles, tap Style, then choose the one you want.

[Learn how to turn on CC or SDH by default](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118411#default)

### Audio languages on your iPhone, iPad, or Android mobile device

- While watching a video, tap the screen to open the playback controls.

- Tap the Languages button .

[Image: A video is paused on an iPhone. In the bottom right corner, the Languages button is highlighted.]

- Choose the audio language that you want.

On your iPhone or iPad, to choose the audio languages that are downloaded with your videos, open the Settings app, then tap Apps. Scroll down and tap TV, then tap Languages. Tap Add Language and choose the languages that you want to add.

## On Apple Vision Pro

- While watching a video, tap the screen to open the playback controls.

- Tap the More Controls button .

- Choose Subtitles or Languages.

- Pick the subtitles or audio language that you want.

- [Learn how to turn on CC or SDH by default](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118411#default)

## If you don't see subtitles or language options

If you don't see subtitles or language options, they might not be available for that show or movie. Check the show or movie descriptions page in the Apple TV app to find out what subtitles or languages are available.

## Turn on CC or SDH by default

If you don't see an option for SDH, you can turn on CC or SDH by default. You can also look in the [Apple TV app to see whether movies and TV shows have accessibility features.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118409)

### On iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro smart TV, or streaming device

- Go to Settings and choose Accessibility.

- Under Hearing, choose Subtitles and Captioning.

- Choose "Closed Captions + SDH" to turn it on.

- To customize captions and subtitles, choose Style, then select an option.

### On Mac

- Choose Apple menu  > System Settings (or System Preferences), then click Accessibility.

- Click Captions.

- Select "Prefer closed captions and SDH" to turn it on.

To customize captions and subtitles, choose from the options on this screen.

Movies and TV shows aren't available in all countries and regions. See [what’s available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) . Languages and accessibility features supported by a movie or TV show vary by country and region.
