# Take a screenshot on your iPod touch

Save what's on your screen.

## Take a screenshot on iPod touch

- Press the top button and the Home button at the same time.

[Image: Press the top and Home buttons simultaneously on an iPod touch to take a screenshot.]

- Quickly release both buttons.

- Tap the screenshot in the lower-left corner.

- Tap Done.

- Choose Save to Photos, Save to Files, or Delete Screenshot.

## Find your screenshots

If you choose Save to Photos, you can view the saved item in the Screenshots album in the Photos app, or in the All Photos album if iCloud Photos is turned on.
