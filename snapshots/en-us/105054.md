# Close your Apple Cash account or turn it off on a device

You can choose which devices can use Apple Cash, or you can close your account altogether.

## Turn off Apple Cash on a device

After you set up Apple Cash * on one device, you can use it on any eligible device where you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account. If you don't want to use Apple Cash on a certain device, you can turn it off for just that device.

- On iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro, open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then turn off Apple Cash.

- For Apple Watch, open the Apple Watch app on your iPhone, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then turn off Apple Cash.

To turn Apple Cash back on, enter your Apple Account password.

Turning off Apple Cash in Settings doesn't close your account and doesn't stop recurring payments that you've set up.

## Close your Apple Cash account

Before you close your account, make sure that your balance is $0 and that you don't have [pending transactions](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT212811#transactions) . To check your balance, [go to your card info](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT212811#cardinfo) . You can [transfer money to your bank](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120401) , spend it, or [send it to someone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105013) .

When your balance is $0, [contact an Apple Cash Specialist at Green Dot Bank](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) to close your account.

- Go to your card info:

- On iPhone, open the Wallet app, tap your Apple Cash card, tap the More button , then tap Card Details .

Closing your Apple Cash account stops recurring payments that you've set up.

### If you're part of Apple Cash Family

If you're the family organizer, any Apple Cash Family accounts that you set up for family members will be closed. First, their balances will be transferred to your account, then their accounts can be closed, then your account will be closed when your balance is $0. Learn [what happens if you disband the Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201081#stopsharing) .

If you're part of Apple Cash Family, the organizer will need to close your account. If you're less than 18 years old and you're removed from Apple Cash Family by the organizer, or you remove yourself, your balance is transferred to the organizer, and your account is closed.

If you're over 18 years old and [take ownership of your Apple Cash account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102039) , you keep your balance. Refunds and credits to your account that were initiated before you took ownership will transfer to the organizer's account.

### What happens to Daily Cash you receive from Apple Card

When you close your Apple Cash account, your Daily Cash continues to accumulate and can be applied toward your Apple Card balance.
