# Use third-party cloud apps in the Files app

You can add third-party cloud services like Google Drive, Box, Dropbox, and Microsoft OneDrive to the Files app. Then you can access all your files on all your devices.

## Add third-party cloud storage apps to the Files app

- Download and set up the third-party cloud app, such as Dropbox, Microsoft OneDrive, or Google Drive.

- Open the Files app, then tap Browse.

- Tap the More button , then tap Edit.

[Image: An iPhone with iOS 26 shows the Browse tab of iCloud Drive after you tap the More button. A menu opens showing Edit as one of the options.]

- Turn on the third-party apps that you want to use in the Files app. Tap the Done button .

[Image: An iPhone with iOS 26 shows the Browse tab of iCloud Drive after you tap Edit. Under Locations, iCloud Drive, On My iPhone, Box, and Dropbox are listed.]

If you don't see one of your third-party cloud services in the Files app, check with your cloud provider.

## Move files from a third-party cloud app in the Files app

- In the Files app, find the third-party file that you want to move.

- Touch and hold on the file until a menu appears, then tap Move.

- Choose the folder that you want to move your file to.

- In iOS 26, tap the Done button . In iOS 18 or earlier, tap Move again.
