# If some third-party Apple Watch apps disappear after updating to watchOS 27

With watchOS 27, all apps are required to include 64-bit support to ensure the best performance. If a third-party app hasn't been updated for 64-bit support, it won't appear on your Apple Watch after you update to watchOS 27.

## Keep your apps updated

When the app developer updates their app to include 64-bit support, the app will again appear on your Apple Watch. Check for available updates:

### Using your iPhone

- Open the App Store.

- Tap the My Account button or your photo at the top of the screen.

- Tap App Updates, then tap Update next to an app to update only that app, or tap Update All.

### On your Apple Watch

- Open the App Store and scroll down to the bottom.

- Tap Account, then tap Updates.

- Tap Update next to an app to update only that app, or tap Update All.
