# Use iMessage apps on your iPhone and iPad

With iMessage apps, you can send messages later, use Image Playground, collaborate with others in a conversation, decorate messages with stickers, and more — without leaving Messages.

## Get started

- [Download the latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/118575) for your iPhone or iPad.

- Make sure that your device is [signed in to the iTunes & App Store with your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/105023) .

## Find iMessage apps

- Open Messages.

- Tap the Compose button to start a new message. Or go to an existing conversation.

- Tap the Plus button find the app that you want to use.

[Image: To access your iMessage apps, tap the + button in the Messages app when you create a message.]

- Swipe up to see more of your apps.

## Download iMessage apps

You can find and download apps to use with iMessage in the App Store for iMessage. Here's how:

- Open Messages.

- Start a new message or open a conversation, then tap the Plus button .

- Tap the Store icon .

- Tap the Get button or the price icon next to the app that you want to download, then tap Install. You might need to enter your Apple Account password to complete the purchase.

[Image: the Get button]

- Swipe down or tap the Close button to return to your message.

[Image: the Close button]

Some apps let you make additional purchases within the app. If you [set up Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/105062) , you can [use Ask to Buy](https://support.apple.com/105055) to help control app downloads and in-app purchases. Learn more about [in-app purchases](https://support.apple.com/108813) .

## Rearrange iMessage apps

You can add apps to your Favorites so you have quick access to them in the app drawer. You can also reorder apps in the app drawer, remove the ones you don’t want, and more.

- Open Messages.

- Start a new message or open a conversation, then tap the Plus button .

- Touch and hold any app icon in the list until it shrinks a little, then drag it to your chosen location.

[Image: List of iMessage apps including Camera, Photos, Stickers, Apple Cash, and Polls.]

## Turn iMessage apps on or off

- In Settings, tap Apps.

- Tap Messages.

- Tap iMessage Apps.

[Image: Screenshot of iPhone settings showing how to turn iMessage apps on or off by navigating to Settings > Apps > Messages > iMessage Apps.]

- Tap the app to turn it on or off.

You can also [delete an app](https://support.apple.com/101550) or [remove an app from the Home Screen](https://support.apple.com/108324) .

If you change your mind, you can [redownload the apps](https://support.apple.com/102417) from the App Store.
