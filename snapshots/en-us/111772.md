# Use Back Tap on your iPhone

With Back Tap in iOS 14 or later, a quick double or triple tap on the back of your iPhone can open Control Center, take a screenshot, trigger accessibility-specific actions, and more.

## Turn on Back Tap

- Check that you have the [latest version of iOS](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT204204) on your iPhone 8 or later.

- Go to Settings > Accessibility > Touch, and tap Back Tap.

- Tap Double Tap or Triple Tap and choose an action.

- Double or triple tap on the back of your iPhone to trigger the action you set.

You can even set a double or triple tap to [trigger an Accessibility Shortcut](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT204390) to quickly access features like AssistiveTouch, Siri Shortcuts, Magnifier, Reachability, and VoiceOver.

## Learn more

- [Turn on AssistiveTouch](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT202658) to adjust volume, lock your screen, use multi-finger gestures, restart your device, or replace pressing buttons with just a tap.

- Ask Siri to quickly do everyday tasks with the apps you use the most by [using Siri Shortcuts](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT209055) .

- [Use Magnifier](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT209517) to turn your iPhone or iPad into a magnifying glass so you can zoom in on objects near you.

- Learn more [about the accessibility features on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/accessibility) .
