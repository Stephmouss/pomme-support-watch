# How many displays can be connected to Mac mini

The number of external displays that you can use at the same time depends on your Mac model and the resolution and refresh rate of each display.

[Identify your Mac mini model](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102852) , then learn how many displays can be connected to that model, and at which resolutions and refresh rates:

- [Mac mini with M6 chip](https://support.apple.com/en-us/148370)

- [Mac mini with M5 Pro chip](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149070)

- [Mac mini (2024) with M4 chip](https://support.apple.com/en-us/148271)

- [Mac mini (2024) with M4 Pro chip](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149071)

- [Mac mini (2023) with M2 chip](https://support.apple.com/en-us/148273)

- [Mac mini (2023) with M2 Pro chip](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149072)

- Earlier models: [Check the technical specifications](https://support.apple.com/docs) for that model for a display support overview.
