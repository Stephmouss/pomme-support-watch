# Beats: Power your Executives

Two AAA batteries in the left ear cup of your Executives give you roughly 25 hours of [Active Noise Cancelation (ANC)](https://support.apple.com/kb/ht201129) .

Here’s how to swap them out:

- Pull back the battery cover on the left ear cup

- Swap the old batteries for a new set

- Replace the magnetic cover, so it snaps into place
