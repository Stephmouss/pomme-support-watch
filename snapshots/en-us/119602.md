# Redownload TV shows and movies that you purchased

Learn how to redownload TV shows or movies that you purchased from the Apple TV app.

## How to redownload TV shows and movies on your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Apple TV app.

- Tap the Library tab.

- Tap the movie or show that you want to redownload. If you use Family Sharing, tap a Family Sharing, then choose a family member’s name to download their purchases.

- Tap the Download button .

[Image: Download button]

[Don't see the movie or TV show that you purchased?](https://support.apple.com/108105)

## How to redownload TV shows and movies on your Mac

- Open the Apple TV app.

- Under the Library tab, click Movies or TV shows. If you use Family Sharing, click Family Sharing and then choose a family member's name to download their purchases.

- Hold the pointer over a movie or TV show, then click the Download button .

[Image: Download button]

[Don't see the movie or TV show that you purchased?](https://support.apple.com/108105)

## How to redownload TV shows and movies on your PC

- Open the Apple TV app.

- Click the Library tab , then click Movies or TV Shows.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Hold the pointer over a movie or TV show, then click the More button .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Click Download.

[Don't see the movie or TV show that you purchased?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108105)

## How to redownload TV shows and movies on Apple TV 4K, Apple TV HD, and other devices

You can't download movies and TV shows on Apple TV 4K, Apple TV HD, smart TVs, and other streaming devices with the Apple TV app. You can stream movies and TV shows that you purchased. Just open the Apple TV app and go to your library.

[Don't see the movie or TV show that you purchased?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108105)

## How to download movies and TV shows from your Apple TV subscription or Apple TV channels

TV shows and movies that are included with your Apple TV channels or Apple TV subscription won't appear in your list of purchases.

[Learn how to download movies and TV shows from Apple TV and Apple TV channels](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118239)

Any digital media you buy from Apple can be permanently downloaded on to an iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Windows PC. [Learn more](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121877) .
