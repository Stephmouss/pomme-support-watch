# About MLS Season Pass subscriptions

In 2026, MLS matches and programming will be part of an Apple TV subscription*, and the standalone MLS Season Pass subscription will end. Learn what you need to do if you're subscribed to MLS Season Pass.

## What happens to my current MLS Season Pass subscription?

Starting in the 2026 season, all MLS matches and exclusive programming will be part of Apple TV and can be accessed with an Apple TV subscription at no additional cost. At the end of the 2025 season, the standalone MLS Season Pass service will no longer be offered as a separate subscription.

[Subscribe to Apple TV to watch 2026 MLS Matches and exclusive programming](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118404)

### If you're subscribed to Apple TV and MLS Season Pass

If you have a current seasonal or monthly subscription to MLS Season Pass and an Apple TV or an Apple One subscription, you will no longer be billed for MLS Season Pass after the 2025 MLS season and auto-renew will be turned off. All MLS matches and exclusive programming will be part of your Apple TV subscription.

### If you're only subscribed to MLS Season Pass

If you have an eligible renewing seasonal or monthly subscription to MLS Season Pass, your subscription will automatically convert to an Apple TV subscription in early 2026. The new price of your Apple TV subscription will be the same as or less than the price of your current MLS Season Pass subscription. If eligible, you’ll receive an email before the billing change with more information.

If you don't have a subscription that's eligible for conversion, your MLS Season Pass subscription will expire before the start of the 2026 MLS season. When the 2025 season ends, auto-renew will be turned off and you will no longer be billed for your MLS Season Pass subscription. [Subscribe to Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118404) to continue watching MLS matches and exclusive programming.

### If you have an MLS Season Pass subscription from another provider

If you have access to MLS Season Pass through another provider, reach out to the provider for more guidance.

### If you're only subscribed to Apple TV or Apple One

If you're only subscribed to Apple TV or Apple One, you'll get access to all MLS matches and exclusive programming starting in the 2026 season.

### If you want to change or cancel your subscription

[Learn how to change your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118448)

[Learn how to cancel your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428)

*Apple TV subscription does not include third-party branded subscription services or content available for rental or purchase in the Apple TV app.

The Apple TV app and access to MLS matches and programming might vary by country and region. Learn [what's available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
