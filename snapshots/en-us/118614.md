# If your iPad is unable to scan QR codes after updating to iPadOS 17.4

After updating to iPadOS 17.4, the iPad models listed below may be unable to scan QR codes using the Camera app or third-party apps that use the camera. [Identify your iPad model](https://support.apple.com/108043) .

- iPad (7th Generation)

- iPad (6th Generation)

- iPad Pro 12.9-inch (2nd Generation)

- iPad Pro 10.5-inch

If you are unable to use the Camera app to scan a QR code, [update your iPad](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT204204) to iPadOS 17.4.1.
