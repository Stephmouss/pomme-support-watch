# Install Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 15.1 or later for Mac

To get the latest features in Keynote, Pages, and Numbers, download the new 15.1 versions directly from the App Store on your Mac.

Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 15.1 on Mac are available as new apps rather than as updates to the existing apps.

## Check your versions of Keynote, Pages, and Numbers

- In each app, click the app name in the menu bar, then click About [ app name ].

- Your version appears under the app name in the window. Do one of the following depending on the version you have:

- If you have Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 14.5 installed already, [download Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 15.1](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126151#update_to_15.1) from the App Store.

- If you have Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 14.4 or earlier and you have saved passwords for protected documents, [update to versions 14.5](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126151#update_to_14.5) before updating to versions 15.1.

## Update to Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 14.5

If you currently have Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 14.4 or earlier and you have saved passwords for protected documents that you'd like to make sure stay saved, update the apps to versions 14.5 manually:

- Go to the App Store app on your Mac.

- Click Updates in the sidebar, then click Update beside each app that you want to update, or click Update All.

- Open Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 14.5 at least once before or after downloading and opening version 15.1.

After you've updated to versions 14.5, you can [download Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 15.1](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126151#update_to_15.1) .

## Download Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 15.1

- Open the App Store app on your Mac.

- Enter "Keynote, Pages, Numbers" in the search field at the top of the App Store window, then press the Return key.

- Click the download button next to each app.

- Once you’ve downloaded the 15.1 versions of the apps, you’ll see the new versions—identifiable by updated icons—in the Applications folder on your Mac.* To preserve passwords for any password-protected documents, make sure you open Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 14.5 at least once before or after downloading and opening version 15.1.

## Manage features and settings after updating to Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 15.1

If you're an existing user of Keynote, Pages, and Numbers, here's how to manage these features and settings after updating.

Password-protected documents : To make sure saved passwords for password-protected documents transfer automatically to 15.1, open version 14.5 of each app at least once before or after downloading the 15.1 versions.

Recent documents : Recent documents created in versions 14.4 or earlier don't appear when you choose Open Recent from the File menu or when you Control-click the app icon in the Dock. To see recent documents, choose Open from the File menu, then click Recents in the sidebar. As older files are reopened, more documents will become available in Recents.

Templates and shapes : If you have iCloud enabled for the apps, custom shapes and templates that you've saved migrate to Keynote, Pages, and Numbers 15.1 or later.

Mail Merge in Pages : If you added a link to a spreadsheet in Mail Merge in Pages 14.4 or earlier, that link won't transfer when opened in Pages 15.1. You'll have to add the link again manually after opening in Pages 15.1.

Notes in Pages : Opening notes from the Notes app in Pages 15.1 requires macOS Tahoe 26 or later.

VoiceOver : Use the latest version of macOS for the best experience.

* If you want to save storage space on your Mac, you can drag the 14.5 versions to the Trash, then empty the Trash. If you retain the older versions of the apps along with 15.1, you may receive activity notifications for collaborative documents twice. You can [turn off notifications](https://support.apple.com/105052) in the older versions to stop these notifications.
