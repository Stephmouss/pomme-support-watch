# Turn on audio descriptions on your iPhone or iPad

On iPhone or iPad, you can turn on audio descriptions to have scenes in videos described to you.

## Use audio descriptions in Settings

- Go to Settings > Accessibility.

- Tap Audio Descriptions.

- Turn on Audio Descriptions.

[Image: The Audio Descriptions button in Settings on iPhone]

## Turn on audio descriptions in the Apple TV app

- Open the Apple TV app.

- While watching a video, tap the screen to reveal the playback controls.

- Choose the audio-described track that you want to use.

Find out [if a movie or TV show has audio descriptions](https://support.apple.com/HT205742) .

## If you don't see audio descriptions

- Not all videos include audio description tracks. Check the documentation for the video you're using to see if it includes these features.

- Some third-party websites and apps provide custom audio controls for videos. If you don't see audio controls when viewing video from a website, check the documentation, or contact the website owner or app developer for more information.

- Find out [if a movie or TV show has audio-described content](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT205742) .

If you've turned off audio descriptions but still hear menu descriptions, VoiceOver might be turned on. Find out [how to turn VoiceOver off](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/turn-on-and-practice-voiceover-iph3e2e415f/ios) .

Learn how to turn on audio descriptions on a different device:

- [Turn on audio descriptions on Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT205800) .

- [Turn on audio descriptions on Windows PC](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT205798) .

- [Turn on audio descriptions on Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT205799) .

The Apple TV app isn't available in all countries or regions. Find out [where the Apple TV app is available](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT208083) .
