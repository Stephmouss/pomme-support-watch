# Add or remove a device from your AppleCare One plan

After you start an AppleCare One plan, you can easily manage the devices you want to cover.

- [Add a device to your AppleCare One plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122224#add)

- [Understand AppleCare One eligibility](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122224#eligibility)

- [Remove a device from your AppleCare One plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122224#remove)

[Image: Play the video]

With AppleCare One, you can protect all eligible devices associated with your Apple Account in one AppleCare plan. 1

- For AppleCare One Individual, you start with coverage for three eligible devices, and you can swap covered devices, add more devices, or remove devices from your plan. 2

- For AppleCare One Family, all of your eligible devices will be automatically added to your plan Other devices may require additional steps when you add them manually. 2

- For iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, Theft and Loss coverage is included with AppleCare One.

Learn [how to get AppleCare One](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127207) , or learn more [about AppleCare coverage benefits](https://www.apple.com/applecare/) .

## Add a device to your AppleCare One plan

To add your device to your AppleCare One plan, it needs to be associated with your Apple Account. For AppleCare One Family, the device needs to be associated with an Apple Account in the Family Sharing group.

For AppleCare One Individual, you need to manually choose the devices you want to cover, and you can swap covered devices or buy Add-on Subscriptions to cover more devices. For Family plans, eligible devices will automatically be added to your plan. Some devices might require device checks, and others might not be eligible to add to your plan.

### Add a device to AppleCare One Individual

When purchasing a product from Apple, you can choose to add your new device to your AppleCare One Individual plan. If your plan is already covering three or more devices, you can swap covered devices or add more devices to your plan at an additional cost.

[Add devices to your AppleCare One Individual plan online](https://checkcoverage.apple.com) , or use a device associated with your Apple Account that meets these requirements:

- iPhone with iOS 18.5 or later

- iPad with iPadOS 18.5 or later

- Mac with macOS 15.5 or later

- Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 2.5 or later

Add devices or swap covered devices in your AppleCare & Warranty settings.

- [Open Settings, then General, then AppleCare & Warranty on your device](https://coverage.apple.com/cc/acW4820)

- In the AppleCare One Devices section, select Add Device.

- Next, choose the device you want to add.

- If the device appears under Ready to Add, follow the onscreen prompts to add it to your plan. 3

- If the device appears under Action Needed, you might need to [complete a device check](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127874) before it can be added to your plan.

- If you don't see one of your devices, tap or click "Don't see one of your devices?" to learn more. If you still don't see your device in the "Not Eligible for Plan" section, you might need to [sign in to your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/111001) on that device or pair it with one of your other Apple devices associated with your account.

### Add a device to AppleCare One Family

If you want to add a device to your AppleCare One Family plan, make sure you are signed in to your Apple Account on the device, or you pair it with a device associated with your Apple Account. To cover an eligible display, connect it to your Mac and add it in System Settings. Eligible devices will be automatically added to your Family plan, but some devices might require a [device check](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127874) before being added.

## Understand AppleCare One eligibility

Device eligibility for AppleCare One coverage might be impacted by several circumstances.

- The age of your device

- A failed device check 4

- The country or region of device purchase

- Active service in progress

- Marked as lost and Lost Mode is turned on

- Coverage by another AppleCare product 5

- Device is not associated with your Apple Account 6

If you are unable to add your device to your plan, refer to the message on your device for more information.

## Remove a device from your AppleCare One plan

When you remove a device from your AppleCare One plan, that device will immediately lose coverage. You might be able to add the device back to your AppleCare One plan or get new AppleCare coverage later if you change your mind. For AppleCare One Individual, if you have Add-on Subscriptions, removing any device from your AppleCare One plan will cancel an Add-on Subscription.

If you sign out of your Apple Account on a device that is covered by an AppleCare One plan, and another Apple Account signs in to the device, the device will be removed from your AppleCare One plan after 24 hours.

### Remove a device

You can sign in to your Apple Account and [remove a device from your plan online](https://checkcoverage.apple.com/) . Or, you can remove a device in the AppleCare & Warranty settings on your device.

- [Open Settings, then General, then AppleCare & Warranty on your device](https://coverage.apple.com/cc/acW4820)

- Select the device you want to remove from AppleCare One.

- Select Remove Device.

- On the next window, select Remove Device to confirm that you want to remove the device from your AppleCare One plan.

### Remove all devices

If you remove all devices from your AppleCare One plan, your plan will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle. During this time, you can still add devices to continue your plan.

If you want to immediately cancel AppleCare One coverage for all of your devices, or understand refund expectations when coverage is canceled, [learn more about canceling AppleCare coverage](https://support.apple.com/118218) .

- AppleCare One Individual is available in Australia, France, Germany, the United Kingdom, and the United States. AppleCare One Family is available only in the United States.

- Only one Apple Vision Pro can be covered with AppleCare One Individual, and only six Apple Vision Pro devices can be covered with AppleCare One Family. You can swap covered devices or purchase a single AppleCare+ plan for additional Apple Vision Pro devices.

- If you add a device that has AppleCare+ or AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss coverage, it will be covered by AppleCare One and [your previous AppleCare+ plan will be canceled](https://support.apple.com/118218) . You will receive a refund based on the percentage of unexpired AppleCare plan coverage. If the original plan was paid upfront in full, this will be sent as an Apple Gift Card.

- Some devices might be eligible for coverage after an Apple-certified repair.

- If your device is covered by an AppleCare Services plan, you need to contact your AppleCare Services vendor for options before being able to add it to your AppleCare One plan.

- To add a device to your AppleCare One plan, it must be associated with your Apple Account.
