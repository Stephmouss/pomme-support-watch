## Add a device to your AppleCare One plan

After you start your AppleCare One plan, you need to add devices to the plan in order to cover them with AppleCare One.

When purchasing a product from Apple, you can select AppleCare One to add your new device to your AppleCare One plan. If your plan is already covering three or more devices, you can swap coverage with one of your other devices, or you can purchase an Add-On Subscription to cover an additional device.

To add a device to your plan, you need to sign in to the device using the Apple Account used to start your AppleCare One plan.

You can [add devices to your AppleCare One plan online](https://checkcoverage.apple.com) , or use any device associated with your Apple Account that meets these requirements:

- iPhone with iOS 18.5 or later

- iPad with iPadOS 18.5 or later

- Mac with macOS 15.5 or later

- Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 2.5 or later

To add a device to your AppleCare One plan, follow these steps:

- [Open AppleCare & Warranty settings on your device](https://coverage.apple.com/cc/acW4820) , or open the Settings app, select General, then select AppleCare & Warranty.

- In the AppleCare One Devices section, select Add Device.

- Next, choose the device you want to add.

- If the device appears under Ready to Add, follow the onscreen prompts to add it to your plan. 1

- If the device appears under Action Needed, you might need to [complete a device check](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122224#devicecheck) before it can be added to your plan.

- If you don't see one of your devices, tap or click "Don't see one of your devices?" to learn more. If you still don't see your device in the "Not Eligible for Plan" section, you might need to [sign in to your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/111001) on that device or pair it with one of your other Apple devices associated with your account.

## Complete a device check for your device

After selecting the device you want to add to your AppleCare One plan, you might need to confirm that the device is in good condition before it can be added. These steps can include a remote device check, taking photos of your device, or visiting an Apple Store for further inspection.

If your device check is successful, follow the onscreen instructions to add the device. If further inspection is needed, you can also schedule an appointment at an Apple Store to help determine if the device can be added to your plan.

If the device is not in good condition, it might be eligible to add to your plan after an [Apple-certified repair](https://support.apple.com/repair) .

### For iPhone or iPad

Start the device check process on the iPhone or iPad you want to add.

If you are required to take photos of the iPhone or iPad you want to add, you will need a separate iPhone or iPad to complete this part of the process. Or, you can have your device checked at an Apple Store by following the link on your device.

#### Guidelines for the separate iPhone or iPad used to take photos:

- You need an iPhone with iOS 18.5 or later or iPad with iPadOS 18.5 or later. [Find the software version of your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/109065)

- Wi-Fi should be turned on for both devices used in this process.

- The iPhone or iPad you use to capture images needs to have a 12 MP camera. [Identify the model of your Apple device](https://support.apple.com/102604)

- You don't need to be signed in to your Apple Account on the device used for capturing images.

#### Tips for successfully taking photos of the device you want to add:

- Choose a flat area with bright, indirect light.

- Remove your case, stickers, and screen protector, if damaged.

- [Clean your device](https://support.apple.com/103258) to remove any smudges.

- [Adjust the screen brightness](https://support.apple.com/109351) of your device to maximum brightness.

### For Mac

For the Mac you want to add, start the device check process on an iPhone or iPad associated with your Apple Account. You need to be connected to Wi-Fi, and your Mac should be charged over 20% or is currently charging.

The results of the device check will be shown on the iPhone or iPad you used.

### For Apple Watch

To complete the remote device check for the Apple Watch you want to add, use the iPhone that your Apple Watch is paired with to start and complete the process.

## Understand AppleCare One device eligibility

Device eligibility for AppleCare One coverage might be impacted by several factors, including the following conditions:

- The age of your device

- A failed device check

- The country or region of device purchase

- Active service in progress

- Marked as lost and Lost Mode is turned on

- Coverage by another AppleCare product 2

- Device is not associated with your Apple Account 3

If you are unable to add your product to your plan, refer to the message on your device for more information.

## Remove a device from your AppleCare One plan

When you remove a device from your AppleCare One plan, that device will immediately lose coverage. If you cancel AppleCare coverage for your device, you might be able to get new AppleCare coverage later if you change your mind. If you have Add-on Subscriptions, removing any device from your AppleCare One plan will cancel an Add-on Subscription.

If you sign out of your Apple Account on a device that is covered by AppleCare One, the device will be removed from your AppleCare One plan after 24 hours.

### Remove a device

You can sign in to your Apple Account and [remove a device from your plan online](https://checkcoverage.apple.com) , or you can use your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro to remove a device from your plan.

- [Open AppleCare & Warranty settings on this device](https://coverage.apple.com/cc/acW4820) , or open the Settings app, select General, then select AppleCare & Warranty.

- In the AppleCare One Devices section, select the device you want to remove.

- Select Remove Device.

- On the next window, select Remove Device to confirm that you want to remove the device from your AppleCare One plan.

### Removing all devices

If you remove all devices from your AppleCare One plan, or if you only have one device on your AppleCare One plan, your plan will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle. During this time, you can still add devices to continue your plan.

If you want to immediately cancel AppleCare One coverage for all of your devices, or understand refund expectations when coverage is canceled, [learn more about canceling AppleCare coverage](https://support.apple.com/118218) .

- If you add a device that has AppleCare+ or AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss coverage, it will be covered by AppleCare One and [your previous AppleCare+ plan will be canceled](https://support.apple.com/118218) . You will receive a refund based on the percentage of unexpired AppleCare plan coverage. If the original plan was paid upfront in full, this may be sent as an Apple Gift Card.

- If your device is covered by an AppleCare Services plan, you need to contact your AppleCare Services vendor for options before being able to add it to your AppleCare One plan.

- To add a device to your AppleCare One plan, it must be associated with your Apple Account.
