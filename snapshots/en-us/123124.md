# Save your Apple Vision Pro setup to an iPhone or iPad

Learn how to save your Apple Vision Pro eye setup, hand setup, and ZEISS Optical Insert information to your iPhone or iPad. Then you can use this saved data for added convenience when you use someone else's Apple Vision Pro as a Guest User, or when you restore or set up your own Apple Vision Pro.

## Before you start

Make sure your devices are up to date. Saving your Apple Vision Pro setup requires visionOS 26 or later. You also need iOS 26 or later on your iPhone, or iPadOS 26 or later on your iPad.

## How to save your Apple Vision Pro setup as a guest

- Put on an Apple Vision Pro that someone has let you use, and begin a [Guest User session](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117742) .

- Bring your iPhone or iPad nearby. If you haven't saved your Apple Vision Pro setup before, a window appears on your iPhone or iPad that says No Saved Setup.

- Tap Start Setup. A window appears that says Look Here with Apple Vision Pro.

- Look at the code on your iPhone or iPad. When Apple Vision Pro recognizes your iPhone or iPad, a checkmark appears and a brief video introduces you to Apple Vision Pro.

- Go through the eye and hand setup process, then begin using Apple Vision Pro as a guest.

Your setup data, including eye and hand setup and ZEISS Optical Insert information, is saved on your iPhone or iPad for use next time you start a Guest User session. Your setup is also synced in iCloud so that you can use it across your Apple devices.

### Save your Apple Vision Pro setup after you've started a Guest User session

If you begin a Guest User session without saving your setup data, you can save it to your iPhone or iPad while you're using Apple Vision Pro.

- [Open Control Center](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/open-control-center-tand3233e05b/visionos) in Apple Vision Pro.

- Tap Guest User on the right.

- Tap Save Setup to iPhone or iPad.

- Bring your iPhone or iPad nearby, then follow the onscreen instructions.

## How to use your saved setup when you use Apple Vision Pro as a guest

- Put on an Apple Vision Pro that someone has let you use, and begin a [Guest User session](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117742) .

- If you see the option to Use Recent Guest Setup, and you weren't the most recent guest user, use the Digital Crown to select Set Up as New Guest.

- Bring your iPhone or iPad nearby. An Apple Vision Pro window appears on your device.

- Tap Use Saved Setup.

- Look at the code on your iPhone or iPad. When Apple Vision Pro recognizes your iPhone or iPad, a checkmark appears.

- Begin using Apple Vision Pro as a guest.

## Use your saved setup when setting up Apple Vision Pro

If you've set up an Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 26 or later, your setup information is saved in iCloud. These settings are applied automatically when you [set up Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/turn-on-and-set-up-devd5d9e3a52/visionos) using the Quick Start option.

## Delete your saved Apple Vision Pro setup data

You can delete your saved setup data during a Guest User session. You can also delete it from iCloud and your iPhone or iPad.

### How to delete your setup data during a Guest User session

- [Open Control Center](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/open-control-center-tand3233e05b/visionos) in Apple Vision Pro.

- Tap Guest User on the right.

- Tap Delete Eye & Hand Setup. Your setup information will be deleted when you take off Apple Vision Pro.

### Delete your setup data from iCloud

If you've saved your Apple Vision Pro setup on your iPhone or iPad, this data is also stored in iCloud. You can delete your setup in iCloud settings:

- Open Settings on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro.

- Tap your name.

- Tap iCloud.

- Tap Saved to iCloud.

- Tap Apple Vision Pro Setup.

- Tap Manage Data in iCloud.

- Tap Delete Data from iCloud, then tap Disable & Delete to confirm.

This deletes your Apple Vision Pro setup from iCloud, as well as any of your iPhone or iPad devices that have this information synced in iCloud.

### Delete your setup data from your iPhone or iPad

If you've saved your Apple Vision Pro setup on your iPhone or iPad and don't have syncing turned on in iCloud settings, you can delete the setup information from your device's local storage:

- Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad.

- Tap General.

- Tap iPhone Storage or iPad Storage.

- Tap the search bar and search for Apple Vision Pro Setup.

- Tap Apple Vision Pro setup in the search results.

- Tap Delete Data, then tap Delete to confirm.
