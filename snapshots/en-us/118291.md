# Remove a payment method from your Apple Account

If you can't remove a payment method, you might need to cancel subscriptions, turn off purchase sharing, or pay an unpaid balance.

## Remove a payment method

If you have subscriptions like iCloud+, have an unpaid balance, or are a Family Sharing organizer and use purchase sharing, you must keep at least one payment method on file. Learn [what to do if you can't remove a payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118291#troubleshooting) .

## Remove a payment method on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- In Settings, tap your name.

- Tap Payment & Shipping.

- Tap Edit.

- Tap the Delete button next to the payment method.

- Tap Remove to confirm.

[Image: An iPhone showing the Payment & Shipping settings.]

## Remove a payment method on your Mac

- In the App Store, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Or, click the Sign In button and sign in to your Apple Account.

- On the page that appears, click your name. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Payment Methods.

- Click Edit.

- Click the Delete button next to the payment method.

- Click Remove to confirm.

[Image: The Edit Payment Methods page in the App Store on Mac shows payment options.]

## Remove a payment method on your Windows PC

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app. Learn [how to get Apple's apps for Windows](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290) .

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Or, click Sign In and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click View My Account. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account.

[Image: A screen showing the Apple Music app on Windows.]

- Next to Payment Information, click Manage Payments.

- Click Edit.

- Click Remove Payment Method.

[Image: A screen showing the Edit Payment Information settings in the Apple Music app on Windows.]

### Remove your payment method on an older version of iTunes for Windows

- If your Windows PC isn't compatible with the Apple Music app or Apple TV app, open iTunes.

- In the menu bar at the top of the iTunes window, choose Account, then choose View My Account.

- Choose Manage Payments.

- Choose Edit.

- Click Remove Payment Method.

## Remove a payment method online

You can also remove a payment method after signing in to [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/account/manage/section/payment) . In some cases, you might need to add a new payment method in order to remove the existing payment method.

## If you can't remove a payment method

### If you have a subscription

If you have subscriptions like iCloud+, you must keep at least one payment method on file. You can [cancel your subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) . When your subscription period is over, remove your payment method.

### If you're a Family Sharing organizer and use purchase sharing

The family organizer must have a payment method on file. If you're the family organizer, [turn off purchase sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108774) , then remove the payment method.

### If you have an unpaid balance

You might not be able to remove a payment method. Instead, [change your payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118293) .

#### If you still can't remove a payment method

If you still can't remove a payment method, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
