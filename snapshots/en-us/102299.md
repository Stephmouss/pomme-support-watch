# If you see duplicate contacts after setting up iCloud Contacts

If you set up iCloud Contacts on your Mac or PC and then see duplicate contacts, follow these steps.

## Before you begin

- If you see duplicates in Contacts on [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/) , visit [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/icloud) for help.

- If you see duplicate contacts only on your computer and not on [iCloud.com](https://iCloud.com) , follow the steps below.

## Remove duplicate contacts

Follow the steps for your operating system.

### On your Mac

- [Make a copy of your contacts](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT204055) .

- Open Contacts.

- From the Card menu, select Card > Look for Duplicates.

- When asked, click Merge.

- Repeat steps 2 and 3 until no duplicates are found.

- [Make another copy of your iCloud Contacts](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT204055) .

### On your Windows PC

Visit these Microsoft articles for help:

- [Outlook 2007](http://office.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook-help/resolve-or-delete-duplicate-contacts-HP010098541.aspx)

- [Outlook 2010](http://office.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook-help/resolve-or-delete-duplicate-contacts-HP010355041.aspx)

- [Outlook 2013 and 2016](http://office.microsoft.com/en-us/outlook-help/delete-duplicate-contacts-HA102919681.aspx)
