# Subscribe to Apple TV

With an Apple TV subscription, you can watch hundreds of exclusive movies and TV shows, MLS matches, Formula 1, Friday Night Baseball, and more on your compatible devices with the Apple TV app.

## How to subscribe to Apple TV

Apple TV+ is now Apple TV. Subscribe to Apple TV to stream hundreds of Apple Originals with new releases weekly.

You can subscribe to Apple TV 1 at [tv.apple.com](https://tv.apple.com) , or in the Apple TV app on an Apple device, PC, smart TV, Android mobile device 2 , or compatible streaming device.

[Learn which streaming devices are compatible](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvplus/welcome/web)

If you have an [Apple Music student subscription,](https://support.apple.com/en-us/106008) you can enjoy free access to Apple TV content for a limited time.

[Learn more about Apple TV subscription pricing](https://www.apple.com/apple-tv-plus/)

- Open the Apple TV app. If you're using an eligible Apple device, a message might ask you to [redeem your Apple TV promotion](https://www.apple.com/apple-tv-plus/) .

- [If you can't redeem your promotion](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118222) , make sure that you're eligible.

- Select the Apple TV tab.

- Select Sign In or Sign Up. You might see a Get Apple TV button or Start Free Trial button to select.

- Sign in to the Apple Account that you use to make purchases, or [create a new one](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108647) . If you can't subscribe to Apple TV, you might need to [get your Apple Account ready for purchases](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102251) .

- If asked, confirm your billing information and add a valid payment method. Then select Confirm. If asked, agree to the terms and conditions.

## Share your Apple TV subscription with your family

You can share your Apple TV subscription with up to 5 family members.

[Learn how to set up Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380)

2 The Apple TV app and Apple TV subscription in the Google Play Store might vary by country and region.
