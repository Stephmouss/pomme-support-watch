# How to back up your iPhone or iPad with your Mac

After you back up your iPhone or iPad, you have a copy of your information to use in case your device is ever replaced, lost, or damaged.

[Back up with the Finder (for macOS Catalina 10.15 or later)](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108796#with-the-finder)

[Back up with iTunes (for macOS Mojave or earlier)](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108796#with-itunes)

## Back up with the Finder (for macOS Catalina 10.15 or later)

- Connect your iPhone or iPad to your Mac with a cable.

- If asked, click Allow to [allow the accessory to connect](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102282) .

- Open the Finder.

- In the Finder sidebar, select your iPhone or iPad. [Don’t see your device?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108643)

- If asked, click Trust to [trust this computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109054) .

- Click Trust on your iPhone or iPad.

- If asked, enter your iPhone or iPad passcode. [Forgot your passcode?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118430)

- If you want to save Health and Activity data from your device or Apple Watch, select "Encrypt local backup" and create a password. You can't recover your backup without this password.

- Enter the password again for verification.

- Click Set Password.

- Click Back Up Now.

[Image: iPhone selected in the sidebar of a Finder window on a Mac, displaying its backup options and the Back Up Now button to initiate a backup.]

Your iPhone or iPad is currently backing up.

Your backup status updates until it completes.

After a successful backup, the date and time of your last backup appear.

[Image: Finder window showing an iPhone's backup settings and status, including the date and time of the last backup.]

## Back up with iTunes (for macOS Mojave or earlier)

iTunes automatically backs up your device when you connect it to your computer. But you can also back up your device manually at any time:

- Connect your iPhone or iPad to your Mac.

- If asked, click Allow to allow the accessory to connect.

- Open iTunes.

- Select your device from the upper right corner of the iTunes window. [Don’t see your device?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108643)

- If asked, click Trust to [trust this computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109054) .

- On your iPhone or iPad, click Trust.

- If asked, enter your iPhone or iPad passcode. [Forgot your passcode?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118430)

- If you want to save Health and Activity data from your device or Apple Watch, select "Encrypt local backup" and create a password. You can't recover your backup without this password.

- Enter the password again for verification.

- Click Set Password.

- Click Back Up Now.

Your device is backing up.

Your backup status updates until it completes.

After a successful backup, the date and time of your last backup appear.
