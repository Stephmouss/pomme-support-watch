# Take a screenshot on your iPad

Quickly save what's on your screen.

## How to take a screenshot on an iPad without a Home button

- Press the top button and either volume button at the same time.*

- Quickly release both buttons.

- After you take a screenshot, a thumbnail temporarily appears in the lower-left corner of your screen. Tap the thumbnail to open it or swipe left to dismiss it. [Play](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102500) [Pause](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102500) [Replay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102500)

## How to take a screenshot on an iPad with a Home button

- Press the top button and the Home button at the same time.*

- Quickly release both buttons.

- After you take a screenshot, a thumbnail temporarily appears in the lower-left corner of your screen. Tap the thumbnail to open it or swipe left to dismiss it.

* If you're not sure, [find out which button is which on your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102354) .

## Where to find screenshots

- Open Photos, then tap Collections.

- Swipe up, tap Media Types, then tap Screenshots.
