# Pay with installments using Apple Pay

Pay for your purchase with installments when you check out with Apple Pay and select the pay later option that works for you.

To be eligible to pay with installments using Apple Pay, you need:

- A [compatible](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphone-models-compatible-with-ios-18-iphe3fa5df43/ios) iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later for purchases online and in apps. You can make in store purchases on an iPhone with [iOS 26 or later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- To [set up Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108398) .

- An installment option from a [debit or credit card issuer or installment provider](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109524#paylater) .

All installment plans are provided by the card issuer and are subject to the card issuer's eligibility requirements and approval. For issues or questions about installments, making payments, and managing your installment plan, please contact the card issuer or installment provider.

## How it works

Buy now, pay later (BNPL) and monthly installment plans allow you to make purchases without the need to make the full payment up front. These payment plans allow you to finance a purchase and pay it back in fixed installments over time. You can now make purchases with installments when you check out with Apple Pay on your iPhone or iPad and select the pay later option that works for you. 1 Installment offers are available on supported debit and credit cards [from banks and installment providers](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109524#paylater) .

If your card supports installments, you'll see a Pay Later hint below your card when you check out with Apple Pay online and in apps. When you check out in store, you'll see Pay Later options after you select your payment method. Some banks and installment providers may require you to complete an application which is subject to eligibility and approval.

## Make a purchase using installments online and in apps

- Tap the Apple Pay button or choose Apple Pay as your payment method.

- To see if your default card supports pay later, look for the Pay Later hint under the card. To use a different card, tap Change Payment Method or Other Cards & Pay Later Options.

[Image: "Pay Later" hint below an eligible credit card selected while checking out with Apple Pay.]

- Tap Pay Later on the Payment Options screen.

- If approved, you'll see the installment payment plans available for your purchase from your card issuer.

- Select the payment plan that you want to use. Review the details of the plan, including any terms and conditions.

- Tap Agree & Continue.

- Confirm the payment using Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

Purchases made using installments will be labeled in [your transaction history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104954) in Wallet. You can tap a transaction to see more details about your purchase and to visit the card provider's app or website to manage your installment plan.

## Make a purchase using installments in store

- Double-click the side button and tap the credit or debit card that you want to use.

- If your card is eligible, you'll see the option to tap Pay Later.

[Image: "Pay In Full" and "Pay Later" buttons on an eligible card during in-store checkout with Apple Pay.]

- You'll see the option to tap Choose a Plan.

- To set up an installment plan before your purchase, enter your Planned Purchase Amount and follow the instructions on-screen.

- To finalize checkout, hold the top of your iPhone near the card reader until you see Done or a checkmark on the screen.

For eligible cards, you can also set up your installment plan after your purchase. To do this, you'll tap Set Up After Purchase and then complete your in store transaction as normal. When you receive a notification, follow the prompts to choose a plan. 2

## Apply for and use additional pay later options

When available, you can apply for an installment plan for your Apple Pay purchase from a supported installment provider at checkout or directly in the Wallet app.

- Tap the Apple Pay button or choose Apple Pay as your payment method when you check out online and in apps.

- Tap Other Cards & Pay Later Options. If options are available, they'll be listed under Additional Pay Later options.

- Tap Set Up next to the pay later option you want to use.

[Image: Pay Later Options available for an Apple Pay transaction.]

- Information about the installment provider will be shown. Tap Continue to [Provider Name] to be transferred to the installment provider and follow the instructions to complete your installment plan application.

- If your application is approved and you select an installment offer for your purchase, a card will be added to the Wallet app to complete the purchase using Apple Pay.

- Once the card has been set up for Apple Pay, confirm the payment using Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

You'll have a card from the installment provider in the Wallet app for you to use for future eligible Apple Pay purchases.

To apply for and use additional pay later options in the Wallet app, open the Wallet app, tap the Add Card button , then tap Pay Later Options and follow the prompts to apply. If approved, you'll add a card from the installment provider in the Wallet app to complete your purchase.

If you don't see pay later options, please contact the card issuer for more support. In rare cases, you might not be able to add a card for pay later [due to security reasons](http://support.apple.com/126755) .

## How to handle order adjustments, returns, or refunds

When you pay with installments using Apple Pay, you can make changes to your order in the same way that you do for other card purchases you make using Apple Pay. If you have any questions about order adjustments, returns, or refunds, contact your card issuer.
