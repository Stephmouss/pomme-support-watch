# How to subscribe to Apple Fitness+

Subscribe to Apple Fitness+ to access video and audio workouts and meditations.

[Subscribe on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102233#iphone)

[Subscribe on your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102233#ipad)

[Subscribe on your Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102233#tv)

## Here's what you need to subscribe

Make sure that you [have compatible devices with the most recent versions of the software](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118210) . You'll also need to have your Apple Account email address and password ready. To subscribe to Fitness+ on your iPad or Apple TV, make sure that you're signed in to the same account that's signed in on your iPhone.

## How to subscribe to Apple Fitness+ on your iPhone

- Open the Fitness app.

- Tap Fitness+ at the bottom of your screen.

- If you're eligible, tap the offer, or follow the onscreen instructions to subscribe.

- If you're prompted to, sign in to your Apple Account with your email and password. [Forgot your Apple Account email address or phone number?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102627)

- Verify your billing information and [add a valid payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111741) .

- Tap Subscribe.

## How to subscribe to Apple Fitness+ on your iPad

To subscribe to Apple Fitness+ on your iPad, you need to be signed in to the same Apple Account that's signed in on your iPhone.

- Download the Fitness app from the App Store.

- Open the Fitness app.

- If you're eligible, tap the offer, or follow the onscreen instructions to subscribe.

- If you're prompted to, sign in to your Apple Account with your email and password. [Forgot your Apple Account email address or phone number?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102627)

- Verify your billing information and [add a valid payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111741) .

- Tap Subscribe.

## How to subscribe to Apple Fitness+ on your Apple TV

To subscribe to Apple Fitness+ on your Apple TV, you need to be signed in to the same Apple Account that's signed in on your iPhone.

- Open the Fitness app.

- If you're eligible, tap the offer, or follow the onscreen instructions to subscribe.

- If you're prompted to, sign in to your Apple Account with your email and password. [Forgot your Apple Account email address or phone number?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102627)

- Verify your billing information and [add a valid payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111741) .

- Select Subscribe.

If you don't see the Fitness app, you might need to [update to the latest version of tvOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108414) . You should also [check if your device is compatible](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118210) with Apple Fitness+.

## After you subscribe, here's what you can do

- [Find and start workouts in Apple Fitness+](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108761) .

- If you use Family Sharing and subscribe to Apple Fitness+, [you can share your subscription with up to five family members](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380) .

- With Apple One, you can bundle Apple subscriptions like Apple Fitness+ with Apple Music, Apple TV, iCloud storage, and more for a lower monthly price. [Learn how to sign up for Apple One](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111766) .

Apple Fitness+ availability might vary by country or region. Learn [what's available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
