# If custom motion templates don’t appear in a Final Cut Pro library

When you open a Final Cut Pro library in which you've saved Motion templates, the Motion templates might be missing from the browsers in Final Cut Pro.

You can close the library, then open the library again to make the Motion Templates appear. You’ll need to do this with each affected library.
