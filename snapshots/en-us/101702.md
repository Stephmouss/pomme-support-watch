# GarageBand: Left corner of multi-take regions can't be adjusted

When you are working with a multi-take region, it is not possible to adjust the length using the left corner of the region.

This is normal for multi-take regions. The left corner of a multi-take region won't adjust the length. The right corner can be adjusted, and both the left and right corners can be adjusted for single-take regions.
