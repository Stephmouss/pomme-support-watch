# iPhone Mirroring: Use your iPhone from your Mac

With iPhone Mirroring, you can wirelessly interact with your iPhone and its apps and notifications from your Mac. Your iPhone stays locked, so no one else can access it or use it to see what you’re doing.

## iPhone Mirroring system requirements

iPhone Mirroring is currently unavailable in the European Union. To simply view but not interact with your iPhone screen or its streamed video on an AirPlay-compatible device, you can [use AirPlay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102661) instead.

iPhone Mirroring can be used with only one Mac and one iPhone at a time. Your devices must meet these requirements:

- Your Mac is using macOS Sequoia 15 or later and is a [Mac with Apple silicon](https://support.apple.com/en-us/116943) or [Mac with the Apple T2 Security Chip](https://support.apple.com/en-us/103265) . Live Activities on Mac requires macOS Tahoe 26 or later.

- Your iPhone is using iOS 18 or later and is [set up to use a passcode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119586) .

- Your iPhone and Mac are [signed in to the same Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) using [two-factor authentication](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102660) .

- Your iPhone and Mac have Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on.

- Your iPhone is locked and near your Mac. It can also be charging and [using StandBy](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph878d77632/ios) .

- Your Mac is not [sharing its internet connection](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1540/mac) or using [AirPlay](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchld7e543a0/mac) or [Sidecar](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102597) .

## Start or stop iPhone Mirroring

- On your Mac, click iPhone Mirroring in the Dock. Or open it from the Applications folder or Spotlight . It's included with macOS Sequoia 15 or later.

[Image: iPhone Mirroring app in the Dock.]

- If your Mac asks you to unlock your iPhone, use your iPhone to enter your iPhone passcode.

- If your Mac asks whether to allow notifications from your iPhone, click Allow or Don’t Allow. You can change this later in [Notifications settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120421#notifications) on your Mac.

- If your Mac asks whether to require Mac login to access your iPhone, choose whether to be asked every time or to authenticate automatically. You can change this later in [iPhone Mirroring settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120421#settings) .

- When iPhone Mirroring shows your iPhone screen, it’s connected to your iPhone and you can use it to interact with your iPhone. If iPhone Mirroring can’t connect to your iPhone, review the [troubleshooting steps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120421#help) later in this article.

- To stop mirroring your iPhone, quit the iPhone Mirroring app or unlock your iPhone.

If you have more than one iPhone signed in to your Apple Account and nearby, you can [choose the iPhone that your Mac uses](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120421#choose-iphone) for mirroring and iPhone notifications.

[Image: iPhone Mirroring window after connecting to iPhone.]

Privacy & Security: iPhone Mirroring automatically pauses the connection after periods of inactivity. While mirroring, your iPhone says that it's in use and identifies the Mac using it. When unlocked after mirroring, it says that it was used from a Mac and links to Settings > General > AirPlay & Continuity > iPhone Mirroring, which identifies the Mac that used your iPhone, including when and for how long.

## Interact with iPhone using iPhone Mirroring

Tap, swipe, and type | Use your Mac mouse or trackpad to click wherever you would tap on your iPhone. Or click and hold wherever you would touch and hold, such as to edit the Home Screen or open a quick actions menu . You can scroll or swipe with your mouse or trackpad, and use your Mac keyboard to type and use keyboard shortcuts. To swipe left or right, you can also press and hold the Shift key while scrolling. To use a wireless game controller with iPhone apps that support it, use Bluetooth to connect the controller to your iPhone instead of your Mac.

Go to the Home Screen | Move the pointer to the top of the iPhone Mirroring window, then click the Home Screen button in the corner. Or click the bar at the bottom of the iPhone Mirroring window. Or press Command-1 on your keyboard.

Open the App Switcher | Move the pointer to the top of the iPhone Mirroring window, then click the App Switcher button in the corner. Or press Command-2 on your keyboard.

Open Spotlight | Use your mouse or trackpad to scroll or swipe up from the Home Screen. Or press Command-3 on your keyboard.

Play audio or video | iPhone audio plays through your Mac, and your Mac controls the volume, unless you're listening through headphones connected to your iPhone. iPhone video plays back in the iPhone Mirroring window. Streaming content providers might have playback restriction policies that affect whether their premium video content can be mirrored.

Access to the iPhone camera and mic is not available in iPhone Mirroring, including for Face ID or phone calls. You can [make and receive phone calls on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102405) instead.

## Move or resize the iPhone Mirroring window

To reposition the iPhone Mirroring window, move the pointer to the top of the window, then drag the window by the toolbar that appears.

To change the size of the iPhone Mirroring window, use the View menu in the iPhone Mirroring menu bar:

- Choose Larger, or press Command–Plus sign (+).

- Choose Actual Size, or press Command-0.

- Choose Smaller, or press Command–Minus sign (–).

iPhone Mirroring automatically switches to and from landscape view when required by the app that you’re using.

## Manage iPhone notifications

After iPhone Mirroring connects your Mac to your iPhone, your Mac automatically receives notifications and Live Activities from your iPhone. It receives them whether or not you’re using iPhone Mirroring or your iPhone is nearby, as long as your iPhone is turned on. You can manage these notifications from either device.

[Manage iPhone notifications and Live Activities on Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120684)

## Copy items between devices

Use any of these methods to copy items between devices:

- Drag and drop. For example, drag a video from the Photos app on iPhone and drop it onto the desktop on Mac, or drag a photo from Safari on Mac and drop it into a conversation in Messages or Mail on iPhone. You can also drag files to and from the [Files app on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102570) or [iCloud Drive](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118443) on your Mac.

- [Universal Clipboard](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102430)

- [AirDrop](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102538)

## Change iPhone Mirroring settings

- Open the iPhone Mirroring app on your Mac.

- From the iPhone Mirroring menu in the menu bar, choose Settings.

- Ask every time: iPhone Mirroring always asks you to authenticate before mirroring. You can authenticate using your Mac login password, [using Touch ID on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchl16fbf90a/mac) , or [using your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102442) .

- Authenticate automatically: After entering your Mac login password to select this setting, you are no longer asked to authenticate before mirroring. While this setting is selected, the "Require password after screen saver begins or display is turned off" setting in [Lock Screen settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh11784/mac) remains set to Immediately.

- Revoke Access to [name of iPhone]: Reset the iPhone Mirroring setup for this iPhone and remove "Allow notifications from iPhone" from Notifications settings on your Mac. To mirror your iPhone and receive its notifications again, [set up iPhone mirroring](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120421#start) .

### Choose the iPhone that your Mac uses

If you have more than one iPhone that is both signed in to your Apple Account and nearby, you can choose the one that your Mac uses for mirroring, iPhone notifications, and Live Activities:

#### macOS Tahoe 26.4 or later

- Choose System Settings from the Apple menu , then click General in the sidebar.

- Click AirDrop & Continuity on the right, then choose your iPhone from the iPhone pop-up menu. You can choose a different iPhone only when your Mac detects more than one iPhone that can be used for mirroring.

#### Earlier versions of macOS

- Choose System Settings from the Apple menu , then click Desktop & Dock in the sidebar.

- Choose your iPhone from the iPhone pop-up menu on the right. This menu appears just below the iPhone Widgets setting. It appears only when your Mac detects more than one iPhone that can be used for mirroring.

### Use Spotlight to find and open iPhone apps

In macOS Tahoe 26 or later, Spotlight can find and open the apps installed on your iPhone:

- To turn on this feature, make sure that you’ve set up iPhone Mirroring, then take either of these steps:

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings, click Spotlight in the sidebar, then scroll down on the right and turn on iPhone Apps.

- Or open Spotlight and click the Apps button (Command-1). Click the More button in the corner of the Apps window, then choose Show iPhone Apps from the pop-up menu.

- When you open an iPhone app from Spotlight, it opens in iPhone Mirroring.

## If you need help

If iPhone Mirroring doesn’t find your iPhone, doesn’t connect to it, or the connection is interrupted or times out, follow any instruction shown in the iPhone Mirroring window. If necessary, try these additional steps.

- Make sure that your devices meet the [iPhone Mirroring system requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120421#requirements) .

- Install the latest [software updates for Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) and [software updates for iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) and restart both devices.

- If either device is using Bluetooth or Wi-Fi intensively for another purpose, such as to stream audio or video to another device, discontinue that use and try again. It might also help to [check for wireless interference](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102319) .

- [Check for VPN and other third-party security software](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102281) on both devices. Similarly, the [firewall settings on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh11783/mac) should not be set to block all incoming connections.

- Revoke access to your iPhone in [iPhone Mirroring settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120421#settings) , then set up iPhone Mirroring again.

- If no other solutions work, [sign out of your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104958) on each device, restart, then sign back in.
