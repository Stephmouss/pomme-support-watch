# Allow USB and other accessories to connect to your iPhone or iPad

Connect accessories by unlocking your device, or learn how to adjust this security setting.

## Unlock your device

By default, you need to first unlock your iPhone or iPad to connect to an accessory or computer — like USB or Thunderbolt devices, Smart Connector accessories for iPad, or a Mac or PC. After you unlock your device, your accessory remains connected, even if your device is locked again.

- If you don't unlock your device, it won't communicate with the accessory and it might not charge. However, your device will charge as usual when connected to a USB power adapter, even if you don't unlock it.

- If the accessory is still not recognized after you unlock your device: Disconnect it from your device, unlock your device, then reconnect the accessory.

## Adjust the setting for allowing accessories to connect

- Open the Settings app, then tap Privacy & Security

- Scroll down to Security, then tap Wired Accessories.

[Image: Wired Accessories in Privacy and Security settings on iPhone.]

- Depending on the device model, choose your setting:

- iPhone or iPad with USB-C connector:

- Always Ask: Manually approve every accessory, every time.

- Ask for New Accessories: Manually approve new accessories the first time they connect.

- Automatically Allow When Unlocked: Automatically approve accessories when they connect to your unlocked iPhone or iPad (default setting).

- Always Allow: Automatically approve all accessories when they connect.

- iPhone or iPad with Lightning connector:

- Automatically Allow When Unlocked: Automatically approve accessories when they connect to your unlocked iPhone or iPad (default setting).

- Always Allow: Automatically approve all accessories when they connect.

If you're connecting to a storage accessory, you'll always need to unlock your iPhone or iPad before using it.

Administrators can [control the Accessories setting](https://support.apple.com/guide/deployment/dep6b5ae23e9/) on supervised devices using Apple Configurator or mobile device management.
