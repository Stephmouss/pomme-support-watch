# Use Amplify mode and Stereo mode on your Beats Pill and your Beats Pill+

You can use two Beats Pill speakers or two Beats Pill+ speakers to increase volume or get wider stereo separation.

## Use Amplify mode

In Amplify mode, both Beats Pill or Beats Pill+ speakers play the same stereo music.

### Use Amplify mode with your Beats Pill

- Make sure that one Beats Pill is turned on and connected.

- Make sure that the second Beats Pill is turned on.

- Hold the center button on each speaker for five seconds, until the light next to the power button on each speaker turns blue.

### Use Amplify mode with your Beats Pill+

- [Download and open the Beats Pill+ app](https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/beats-pill/id1005829608) on your iPhone or iPad, or on the Beats app on your Android device.

- Make sure that one Beats Pill+ is turned on and connected.

- Make sure that the second Beats Pill+ is turned on and discoverable.

- On your iPhone, iPad, or Android device, tap Amplify.

- To create the Amplify group, tap the name of the second speaker.

- Tap Play on your iPhone, iPad, or Android device.

## Use Stereo mode

In Stereo mode, one Beats Pill or Beats Pill+ speaker plays the left audio channel, and the other Beats Pill or Beats Pill+ plays the right audio channel.

### Use Stereo mode with your Beats Pill

- Make sure that both Beats Pill speakers are joined together in Amplify mode.

- On the speaker that you’d like to be the left channel, press and hold the center button and volume up button until the light next to the power button turns blue.

### Use Stereo mode with your Beats Pill+

- Open the Beats Pill+ app on your iPhone or iPad, or on the Beats app on your Android device.

- Make sure that one Beats Pill+ is turned on and connected.

- Make sure that the second Beats Pill+ is turned on and discoverable.

- On your iPhone, iPad, or Android device, tap Stereo.

- To create the Stereo group, tap the name of the second speaker.

- Tap Play on your iPhone, iPad, or Android device.

## Switch between Amplify mode and Stereo mode on your Beats Pill

To switch between Amplify mode and Stereo mode on your Beats Pill speakers, press and hold the center button and volume up button on either Beats Pill speaker.
