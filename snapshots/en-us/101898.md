# Clean and store your Beats Pill and your Beats Pill+ speakers

Learn how to clean, store, and care for your Beats Pill and your Beats Pill+.

[Image: Beats Pill speaker]

## Clean your Beats Pill and your Beats Pill+

- Use a dry cleaning cloth to wipe away dust and oil buildup.

- Don't use aerosol sprays, solvents, or abrasives.

- Keep moisture away from the grill and speaker.

- If any liquids spill on your Beats Pill or your Beats Pill+, use a dry cleaning cloth to dry it immediately.

## Take care of your Beats Pill and your Beats Pill+

- Avoid exposing your Beats Pill and your Beats Pill+ to liquids and high humidity. Learn about the [water resistance and dust resistance of Beats Pill](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119981) .

- To disconnect your Beats Pill or your Beats Pill+, grasp the plug and pull. Never pull on the cable itself.

## Store your Beats Pill and your Beats Pill+

Unplug your Beats Pill and your Beats Pill+ during lightning storms or when you’re away for extended periods of time.

## Care for other Beats products

Find out how to [clean and store your Beats earphones or headphones](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102136) .
