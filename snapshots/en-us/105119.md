# Which iCloud.com features are available in the browser on your device

Learn about which iCloud.com features you can use from your browser on your iPad or computer, and which are available on your iPhone.

## On your iPad or computer

When you sign in to [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) on your iPad, Mac, or Windows PC, you gain access to these features:

- Photos

- iCloud Drive

- Notes

- Mail

- Calendars

- Contacts

- Reminders

- Pages, Numbers, or Keynote 1

- Find My

- Account details

- Apple Invites

If you have iCloud+, you can also access Hide My Email and Custom Email Domain. 2

[Learn more about iCloud+](https://support.apple.com/108047)

## On your iPhone

When you sign in to [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) on an iPhone with your [Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/111001) , you can view or manage Photos, Notes, and Reminders, as well as some of your account settings. You can also locate or lock devices with Find My.

If you sign in to [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) on your iPhone, you can access these features:

- Photos

- iCloud Drive

- Notes

- Mail

- Reminders

- Pages, Numbers, or Keynote 1

- Find My

- Account details

- Apple Invites

If you have iCloud+, you can also access Hide My Email and Custom Email Domain. 2

[Learn more about iCloud+](https://support.apple.com/108047)

## Need more help?

- [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) works best on Safari for the latest versions of iOS, iPadOS, and macOS, or on the latest versions of Firefox, Google Chrome, Edge, or Opera for Windows.

- If you don't have an iPhone, iPad, or Mac, you can still use features included with [web-only access to iCloud](https://support.apple.com/102447) by visiting [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) on a computer or tablet.

1. Pages, Numbers, and Keynote features are only available on desktop. Documents, spreadsheets, and presentations are view-only in iOS and iPadOS.

2. Not all features are available in all countries or regions.
