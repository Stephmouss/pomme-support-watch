# Region-specific rules for managing an Apple Account

Some Apple Account settings and feature requirements for children, teens, and adults will vary depending on your state, country, or region.

## Apple Account age requirements

Where required by law, these age confirmation requirements apply to Apple Accounts in Singapore, South Korea, the UK, and the United States (Texas):

- Adults will have to confirm their age to use certain services or features, take certain actions on their account, or when they create a new Apple Account.

- You can confirm your age with a credit card that belongs to you or by scanning your passport (available in Singapore, the UK, and Texas in the United States), driver's license, or government-issued ID. Debit cards and gift cards aren't supported.

- If you already have an Apple Account, Apple will check if you have a credit card on file or other eligible methods available to confirm that you're 18 or older.

- If you choose not to confirm your age, you can continue to use your device — but you might need to confirm before using certain services or features or taking certain actions on your account.

## What is the age for a Child Account in my country or region?

In most countries and regions, a child under 13 can't create an Apple Account without help and consent from a parent or guardian. They must be part of a [Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/105062) . The age for these Child Accounts varies in some states, countries, and regions. These states, countries, and regions might also have additional requirements.

- Under 14 in Austria, Bulgaria, China mainland, Cyprus, Israel, Italy, Lithuania, South Korea, and Spain

- Under 15 in Czechia, France, Greece, Peru, and Slovenia

- Under 16 in Croatia, Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Kosovo, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malaysia, Netherlands, Philippines, Poland, Romania, Singapore, and Slovakia

- Under 18 in Brazil and the United States (Texas)

- Under 13 in all other countries and regions

[Learn how to update the birthday for a Child Account](https://support.apple.com/102473)
