# Change the name of your Apple Watch

Name your Apple Watch to make it easier to identify.

## How to change the name of your Apple Watch

- On your iOS device, open the Apple Watch app and tap the My Watch tab.

- Go to General > About.

- Tap the first line, which shows the name of your device.

- Rename your device, then tap Done on the keyboard.
