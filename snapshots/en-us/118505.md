# Liquid damage to Apple Vision Pro and accessories not covered by warranty

Learn about liquid damage to Apple Vision Pro and its accessories that isn't covered by warranty.

Damage to Apple Vision Pro due to liquid exposure is not covered by the [Apple One (1) Year Limited Warranty](http://www.apple.com/legal/warranty/) or the AppleCare Protection Plan. Similarly, liquid damage to Apple Vision Pro accessories is not covered.

Apple Vision Pro has Liquid Contact Indicators (LCI) to help determine if it has been exposed to liquid.

[AppleCare+ for Apple Vision Pro](https://www.apple.com/support/products/apple-vision-pro/) adds unlimited incidents of accidental damage protection for things like drops and spills for the device and the included accessories. Each incident is subject to a service fee plus applicable tax. Exclusions apply. Please see the [AppleCare+ for Apple Vision Pro Terms and Conditions](https://www.apple.com/legal/sales-support/applecare/applecareplus/us/apple-vision/) for details.

Benefits under the manufacturer's warranty are in addition to rights provided by consumer law. You can learn more at [Apple Products and Consumer Law Rights](http://www.apple.com/legal/warranty/statutoryrights.html) .
