# How to use Apple Music Sing on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV

With your Apple Music subscription, view perfectly timed lyrics and adjust the vocals so that you can sing along. Learn how to use Apple Music Sing on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV.

### Before you get started

Make sure you're using a supported device that's signed in with the Apple Account you use for your Apple Music subscription.

Apple Music Sing is supported on the following devices:

- iPhone 11 or later or iPhone SE (3rd generation) [updated to the latest version of iOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575)

- iPad Pro (5th generation or later), iPad Air (4th generation or later), iPad mini (6th generation or later), or iPad (9th generation or later) [updated to the latest version of iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575)

- Apple TV 4K (3rd generation or later) [updated to the latest version of tvOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108414)

### How to use Apple Music Sing on your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Apple Music app.

- Play a song from the Apple Music catalog.

- Tap the song that’s playing.

- At the bottom of your screen, tap the Lyrics button .

- Tap the Mic . [Don’t see the Mic button?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127940#mic)

- To adjust the vocals, drag the slider up or down.

### How to use Apple Music Sing on your Apple TV 4K (3rd generation or later)

- Open the Apple Music app.

- Play a song from the Apple Music catalog.

- If you don't see time-synced lyrics automatically, select Lyrics at the bottom of the screen.

- On your Apple TV Remote, press the Back button, then select the Mic . [Don’t see the Mic button?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127940#mic)

- To adjust the vocals, swipe the clickpad on your Apple TV Remote up or down.

### How to use your iPhone as a microphone with Apple Music Sing

If you have an Apple TV 4K (3rd generation or later), you can use your iPhone as a microphone to sing along to your favorite songs.

[Learn how to use your iPhone as a microphone with your Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/atvbd965bc0d)

### If you don't see the Mic button

If you're using a supported device and don't see the Mic button, Apple Music Sing might not be available for that song.

### Report a concern with lyrics

If you need to report a concern with lyrics, you can [report them on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105076#report) , or by going to [www.apple.com/feedback/apple-music.html](https://www.apple.com/feedback/apple-music.html) .
