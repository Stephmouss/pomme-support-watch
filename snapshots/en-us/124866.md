# Connect your third-party app accounts to your Apple Account

With Automatic Sign-in, use supported third-party apps on new or existing devices, without needing to reenter usernames or passwords.

## Before you start

- To connect a third-party app account to your Apple Account using Automatic Sign-in on your iPhone or iPad, your device must be using iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later. [Learn how to update your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575)

- To connect a third-party app account to your Apple Account on Apple TV, your device must be using tvOS 26 or later. [Learn how to update your Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108414)

Automatic Sign-in is available for supported third-party apps.

## How to link your third-party app accounts with Automatic Sign-in

- Open a supported third-party app on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple TV.

- Tap or select Connect Accounts when asked to link your third-party app account to your Apple Account. You might need to sign out of the third-party app and sign back in to see the prompt.

- If previously you tapped or selected Not Now but want to connect your account, sign out of the third-party app and sign back in. Then, tap or select Connect Accounts when asked.

This feature is available for supported third-party apps. If you don't see a prompt to connect your account, the app may not support Automatic Sign-In.

## See and manage connected accounts on iPhone or iPad

- In Settings, tap your Name.

- Tap Media & Purchases, then tap View Account. You might be asked to sign in with your Apple Account.

- Tap Connected Accounts to view third-party app accounts linked to your Apple Account.

- Tap the connected account name to see the last device used with an account.

- To disconnect a linked account, tap Disconnect. Disconnecting a linked account does not automatically sign you out of the third-party app on your devices.

## See and manage connected accounts on the web

- Go to [tv.apple.com](https://www.tv.apple.com) and sign in with your Apple Account.

- Click your profile picture or the Apple Account button .

- Click Settings.

- Scroll down to Account Access, then click Manage Connected Accounts to view third-party app accounts linked to your Apple Account.

- To disconnect a linked account, click Disconnect and then click Confirm. Disconnecting a linked account does not automatically sign you out of the third-party app on your devices.
