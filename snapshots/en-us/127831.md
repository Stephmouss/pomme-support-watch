# View episode transcripts in Apple Podcasts on iPhone and iPad

With Apple Podcasts on iPhone and iPad, you can follow along with time-synched transcripts, find links to relevant content mentioned in the show, and more.

## Before you get started

[Update your iPhone or iPad to latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575)

## How to view podcast transcripts

- Open the Podcasts app and start listening to an episode.

- Tap the mini player at the bottom of the screen to expand the full screen player.

- Tap the Transcript button . If the Transcript button is grayed out, transcripts are not available for that episode.

Your auto lock settings are disabled while viewing time-synced transcripts. Make sure you lock your device if you're not actively viewing time-synced transcripts.

In the transcript view you can:

- Follow along with your favorite shows while reading time-synced transcripts.

- Scroll forwards and backwards through the podcast and tap to play the section you want to listen to.

- Tap and hold a section to select the text or share it.

- Tap Search to find specific words and go directly to those sections.

- View show chapters and tap them to go to that section of the podcast.

- Find time-synced links to music and other content mentioned in the show, or follow mentioned podcasts.

- Report an issue with the transcript by press and holding the section, tap Report a Concern, then follow the onscreen steps.

Transcripts are not available in Belarus and Russia.
