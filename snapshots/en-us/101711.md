# Apple FIPS iOS 8 Cryptographic Modules v5.0

The iOS Cryptographic Modules, Apple iOS CoreCrypto Module v5.0, and Apple iOS CoreCrypto Kernel Module v5.0, require no setup or configuration to be in “FIPS Mode” for FIPS 140-2 compliance on devices using iOS 8.

For instructions on using the modules, refer to the [Crypto Officer Role Guide for FIPS 140-2 Compliance: iOS 8](http://km.support.apple.com/library/APPLE/APPLECARE_ALLGEOS/HT204924/APPLEFIPS_GUIDE_CO_iOS8.0.b3-01.pdf) .
