# Download the Toy Box sound collection for GarageBand for macOS

To download the animal, transportation, and voice sound effects included in the Toy Box sound collection, download the complete collection of sounds for GarageBand.

To download the Toy Box sound collection, make sure your Mac with GarageBand is connected to the internet.

- Open GarageBand, then choose GarageBand> Sound Library > Download All Available Sounds.

- Click Continue.

- When the new sounds are downloaded, you can access them in the Loop Browser. To open the Loop Browser, click the Loop Browser button in the control bar. Click the Loop Packs pop-up menu, then choose Toy Box from the menu.
