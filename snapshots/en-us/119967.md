# Transfer data from your previous iOS or iPadOS device to your new iPhone or iPad

Set up your new iOS or iPadOS device from a backup of your previous iPhone or iPad.

## Before you begin

- Make sure that you have a [backup of your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118426) .

- Have your [Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/apple-account) ready.

- Transfer your SIM card from your [iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109357) , [iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102392) , or [eSIM](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118669) . If your new device needs a different SIM card than what you have, [contact your carrier](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109526) .

- Make sure that you have a backup of your previous device. If you purchased a new iPhone or iPad, you may be eligible for [temporary iCloud storage](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104980) .

## Select a method

Next, select one of these transfer methods.

[Image: Use Quick Start to transfer data to a new iPhone or iPad.]

[Quick Start](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102659) : Use your iPhone or iPad to automatically set up a new device.

[Image: Use iCloud to transfer data from your previous iOS or iPadOS device to your new iPhone or iPad.]

[iCloud](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108344) : Transfer your data and purchased content to your new device from your previous device's iCloud backup.

[Image: Use the Finder, Apple Devices, or iTunes to transfer data from your previous iOS device to your new iPhone or iPad.]

[Apple Devices app, iTunes, or the Finde](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120001) [r](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT210218) : Transfer your data and purchased content to your new device from a backup you made with the Apple Devices app, iTunes, or the Finder.
