# Add or remove your phone number in Messages or FaceTime

You can use the phone number of your iPhone with Messages and FaceTime on your Mac or iPad.

- [Set up or remove your phone number](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#add)

- [Turn your number on or off for Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#Mac)

- [Turn your number on or off for iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#iOS)

- [Get help](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#help)

## Set up your phone number

On your iPhone:

- Go to Settings. Tap Apps, then select Messages. Make sure that iMessage is on. You might need to wait a moment for it to activate.

- Tap Send & Receive. If you see "Use your Apple Account for iMessage," tap it and sign in with the same [Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/apple-account) that you use on your Mac or iPad.

[Image: iPhone displaying the iMessage settings screen. At the bottom, there's a Use your Apple Account for iMessage button that you can tap to use your Apple Account to send and receive iMessages.]

- In the "You can receive iMessages to and Reply From" section, make sure that your phone number and Apple Account are selected.

- Go to Settings. Tap Apps, then select FaceTime. Make sure that your phone number and Apple Account are selected in the "You can be reached by FaceTime at" section.

On your other devices, you can now turn on the phone number that you just set up:

- [Turn on your phone number for your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#Mac)

- [Turn on your phone number for your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#iOS)

If you use this feature and share your Apple Account with someone, you can't prevent them from seeing your conversations. Instead of using the same Apple Account as someone else, [set up Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380) .

To set up a phone number to use with Messages or FaceTime, you need an active SIM or eSIM associated with that number.

## Remove your phone number

If you don't want calls or messages for your iPhone on your iPad or Mac, you can remove your phone number. There are two ways to do this:

- On your iPhone, go to Settings, then tap Apps. Select Messages, then tap Send & Receive. Select your Apple Account at the bottom of the screen and tap Sign Out. Then go to Settings, tap Apps. Select FaceTime. Tap your Apple Account, then choose Sign Out.

- If the phone number that you want to remove belongs to an iPhone that you can't access, [change your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101567) . This removes all phone numbers from your iPad and Mac. Calls or messages for the removed numbers won't appear on those devices anymore.

## Turn your phone number on or off for your Mac

After you [set up your phone number](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#add) , you can turn it on or off for Messages and FaceTime on your Mac.

- Open Messages or FaceTime. If asked, sign in with the Apple Account that you're using with Messages and FaceTime on your iPhone.

- For Messages, choose Messages, select Settings, then click iMessage. For FaceTime, choose FaceTime, select Settings, then click General.

- Turn your phone number on or off:

- To turn on your phone number, select it and your Apple Account in the list. If you don't see your phone number, [get help with your settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#help) . If you see a number that isn't yours, [change your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101567) .

- To turn off your phone number, deselect it in the list.

- If you want recipients to see your phone number and not your Apple Account, select your phone number from the "Start new conversations from" or "Start new calls from" pop-up menu.

[Image: iMessage settings screen on a Mac with options to select which phone number or email address you'd like to use to receive iMessages and start new conversations.]

Learn how to [make messages that you send and receive on your iPhone appear on your Mac and iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102545) .

## Turn your phone number on or off for your iPad

After you [set up your phone number](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#add) , you can turn it on or off for Messages and FaceTime on your iPad.

- For Messages, go to Settings, then tap Apps. Select Messages and make sure that iMessage is on. If asked, sign in with the Apple Account you're using with Messages and FaceTime on your iPhone. Then tap Send & Receive.

- For FaceTime, go to Settings, then tap Apps. Select FaceTime. If asked, sign in with the Apple Account that you're using with Messages and FaceTime on your iPhone.

- Turn your phone number on or off:

- To turn on your phone number, select it and your Apple Account in the “You can be reached” section. If you don't see your phone number, [get help with your settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#help) . If you see a number that isn't yours, [change your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101567) .

- To turn off your phone number, uncheck it.

[Image: iPad displaying the iMessage settings screen with options to select which phone number or email address you'd like to use to receive iMessages, reply to iMessages, and start new conversations.]

Learn how to [make messages that you send and receive on your iPhone also appear on your Mac and iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102545) .

## If you can't see or select your number in Messages or FaceTime

- Make sure that you're using the [latest software for your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) or [iOS device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- Make sure that you correctly followed the iPhone steps to [set up your phone number](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108758#add) . You should be using the same Apple Account on all of your devices.

- In the "You can be reached" section of Messages and FaceTime settings, make sure that there are checkmarks beside your phone number and Apple Account:

[Image: iPhone displaying the iMessage settings screen with options to select which phone number or email address you'd like to use to receive iMessages, reply to iMessages, and start new conversations.]

If your phone number is unchecked and you can't select it, or you have some other issue, follow these steps:

- On your Mac:

- In Messages, choose Messages, select Settings, then click iMessage. Click Sign Out.

- In FaceTime, choose FaceTime, select Settings, then click General. Click Sign Out.

- On your iPhone and iPad:

- Go to Settings, then tap Apps. Select Messages, then tap Send & Receive. Tap your Apple Account, then tap Sign Out.

- Go to Settings. Tap Apps, then select FaceTime. Tap your Apple Account, then select Sign Out.

- On your iPhone:

- Go back to Settings. Tap Apps, then select Messages. Make sure that iMessage is turned on.

- Tap Send & Receive, then choose "Use your Apple Account for iMessage" and sign in with your Apple Account.

- Using the same Apple Account, sign back in to Messages and FaceTime on your other devices.

If you still need help, [change your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101567) and repeat the setup steps. If that doesn't work, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
