# Turn the Mac startup sound on or off

Use Sound settings to turn the startup sound (chime) on or off.

Depending on which Mac and macOS you're using, you might hear a startup sound shortly after pressing the power button to turn on your Mac. With [macOS Big Sur 11 or later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033) , you can control whether your Mac plays this sound.

## macOS Ventura 13 or later

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings.

- Click Sound in the sidebar.

- Turn "Play sound on startup" on or off.

## macOS Monterey 12 or macOS Big Sur 11

- From the Apple menu , choose System Preferences.

- Click Sound.

- In the Sound Effects pane, use the "Play sound on startup" setting to turn the startup sound on or off.

## Earlier versions of macOS

Earlier versions of macOS do not offer a setting to control whether your Mac plays a sound on startup.
