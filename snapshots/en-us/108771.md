# Backup methods for iPhone or iPad

A backup allows you to copy and save the information from your iPhone or iPad. If you replace your device, you can use a backup to transfer your information to a new device.

[Choose the best backup method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108771#decide)

[iCloud backups](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108771#icloud)

[Backups from your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108771#computer)

[Can I use my device's backup for another kind of device?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108771#other-device)

## Choose the best backup method

You can back up your device using iCloud, your computer, or both. If one method isn't available, you can still use the other.

### Back up to iCloud

[Image: Cloud icon for iCloud backup.]

- Stores backups in iCloud

- [You can store up to 12 TB](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108047) . With an Apple One Premier plan, this increases to 14 TB.

- Always encrypts your backups

- Lets you create and use backups from anywhere with Wi-Fi

### Back up to your computer

[Image: Laptop and phone icon for computer backup.]

- Stores backups on your Mac or PC

- Storage depends on your Mac's or PC's available space

- Offers encrypted backups (off by default)

- Lets you create and use backups from your Mac or PC

## Manage iCloud backups

With a Wi-Fi network connection, you can back up your device using iCloud. You don't need to plug your device into a computer to back up with iCloud.

iCloud backups include the information and settings stored on your device that don't already sync to iCloud. iCloud backups don't include:

- Notes that you already store and sync in iCloud

- Photos, if you turned on iCloud Photos

- Messages (iMessage, SMS, and MMS), if you sync messages with iCloud

- Data stored in other cloud services, like Gmail and Exchange mail

- Apple Mail data

- Apple Pay information and settings

- Face ID or Touch ID settings

- iCloud Music Library and App Store content

- Offline maps

[Learn more about what iCloud backs up](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770)

## Back up from your computer

A computer backup includes almost all of your device's data and settings, which is [different from syncing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108311) .

A backup from a computer doesn't include:

- iTunes Store, App Store, and Apple Books content

- Content synced from your Mac or PC, like imported MP3s or CDs, videos, books, and photos

- Data already synced and stored in iCloud, like iCloud Photos, iMessages, and SMS and MMS messages

- Face ID or Touch ID settings

- Apple Pay information and settings

- Apple Mail data

- Activity, Health, and Keychain data (to back up this content, you'll need to create an encrypted backup)

- Offline maps

## Use a backup for a different device

You can [restore a device from a backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118105) of a different device type. For example, you can use an iPad backup to set up an iPhone. But certain content types won't transfer:

- Photos, unless you use iCloud Photos

- Messages and attachments in Messages, unless you sync messages with iCloud

- Voice Memos

- Apps that aren't compatible with the device that you’re setting up (for example, apps compatible only with iPad won't transfer to your iPhone)
