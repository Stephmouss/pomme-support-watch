# Learn how to get 3% Daily Cash back when you use Apple Card with Apple Pay

When you purchase something from Apple using your Apple Card or select merchants using Apple Card with Apple Pay, you get 3% Daily Cash back.

## What is Daily Cash?

Daily Cash 1 is the cash earned by using Apple Card. When you make a purchase with Apple Card, a percentage of the amount spent is returned as Daily Cash. [You can find your Daily Cash in the Wallet app](https://support.apple.com/119575#find) .

There’s no limit to how much Daily Cash you can receive, and you choose [where it goes](https://support.apple.com/119575#where) . Save it by [opening a Savings account](https://support.apple.com/102676) 2 through Apple Card and automatically depositing your Daily Cash into the account, where it will grow with interest. Or send it to Apple Cash 3 so you can spend it or send it to people you know.
