# If you can't see your shared album

Learn what to do if you can't see your shared album, your shared album isn't syncing, or you can't interact with your shared album.

## If you can't see your shared album or your shared album isn't syncing

- Make sure that your device meets the [minimum system requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118308) for Shared Albums. If possible, upgrade to version 27 or later of iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, or visionOS on your devices.

- Check the internet connection on all your devices by going to [apple.com](https://www.apple.com/) or [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/) .

- Go to Settings, tap Apps, then tap Photos. Scroll down and make sure Shared Albums is turned on.

[Image: iPhone Photos settings screen showing Shared Albums, Shared Library, and Share iCloud Image options.]

- Check whether the album or content is accessible on a different device, on [icloud.com/photos](https://www.icloud.com/photos/) , or by another subscriber of the shared album.

- Make sure that the owner of the shared album hasn't exceeded their iCloud storage limit. If they have, they can [manage their iCloud storage](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922) or [upgrade their iCloud+ plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108349) .

- In version 26 or earlier of iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or visionOS only, [back up the photos in your shared album](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111762) then turn Shared Albums off and back on again: Go to Settings, tap Apps, then tap Photos. Then turn off Shared Albums. Wait 10 minutes. Turn Shared Albums back on again.

## If you can't interact with your shared album

When you create a new shared album in iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macO S27, and visionOS 27 or later, or a shared album owner [upgrades their album to version 27 or later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108916) , any shared album participants on earlier OS versions see the shared album as read-only. To regain uploading, liking, and commenting abilities, upgrade to version 27 or later on all of your devices or view the album on [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/) .
