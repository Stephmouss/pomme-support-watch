# How to back up your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch with Windows

If you back up your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch, you'll have a copy of your information to use in case your device is ever replaced, lost, or damaged.

- Switch to the Finder on your Mac, or [open the Apple Devices app on your PC](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) . If your PC doesn't have the Apple Devices app, or your Mac is using macOS Mojave or earlier, [open iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290) instead.

- Connect your device to your PC using the cable that came with your device. [Find cables for your device](https://www.apple.com/shop/accessories/all/charging-essentials) .

- If asked for your device passcode or to Trust This Computer, follow the onscreen steps. [If you forgot your passcode, get help](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118430) .

- [Select your device in iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108392) .

[Image: iPhone icon in the iTunes toolbar, used to access iPhone backup settings.]

- Click General in the Apple Devices app or Summary in iTunes for Windows.

- If you want to save Health and Activity data from your device or Apple Watch, you need to encrypt your backup:

- Select the "Encrypt local backup" checkbox and create a password.

- Write down your password and store it somewhere safe, because there's no way to recover your encrypted backups without this password.

- Click Back Up Now.

[Image: Cursor hovering over the Back Up Now button in the iTunes for Windows Summary section.]

When the process ends, the date and time of your last backup appears in the iTunes window.

[Image: The date and time of a connected device's most recent local backup in iTunes for Windows.]
