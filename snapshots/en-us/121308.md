# If spatial photos appear as regular photos on Apple Vision Pro

Spatial photos that you take on iPhone might appear flat or two-dimensional when you view them on Apple Vision Pro with a previous version of visionOS. Update your Vision Pro to make sure your spatial photos appear in 3D.

## Update your Apple Vision Pro

- [Back up](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/tan1f03417ed/) your Vision Pro.

- Go to Settings > General > Software Update. The screen shows the currently installed version of visionOS and whether an update is available.

- If an update is available, tap Download and Install or Install Now. You might need to enter your Vision Pro passcode.

- When the update is ready to install, an alert asks you to take off your Vision Pro. You can see the progress of the update on the front of your device with EyeSight.

## Check the spatial button

After you update to visionOS 2 or later, photos that you took in spatial format should open in 3D in the Photos app. If a spatial photo still appears two-dimensional, toggle the photo's spatial button . When the button is white, the spatial photo appears in 3D.

## Share spatial photos via AirDrop

If you’re unable to update to visionOS 2 or later right away, a temporary workaround is to share spatial photos from your iPhone to your Vision Pro [using AirDrop](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119857) .
