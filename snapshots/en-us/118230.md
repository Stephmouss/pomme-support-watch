# About your @icloud.com, @me.com, and @mac.com email addresses

Depending on when you created your iCloud account, your iCloud email addresses and aliases may end with @icloud.com, @me.com, or @mac.com domains. Here's how they're granted.

Review these scenarios to see which one applies to you:

- If you created an iCloud account on or after September 19, 2012, your email address ends with @icloud.com. Learn more about [@icloud.com mail addresses](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mail-on-icloudcom-overview-mm6b1a17e3/icloud) .

- If you created an iCloud account before September 19, 2012, or moved to iCloud with an active MobileMe account before August 1, 2012, you have both @me.com and @icloud.com email addresses.

- If you had a working @mac.com email address as of July 9, 2008, kept your MobileMe account active, and moved to iCloud before August 1, 2012, you can use @icloud.com, @me.com, and @mac.com email addresses with your iCloud account.

In each case, you can sign in with your @icloud.com email address, email addresses associated with your Apple Account, or your phone number.

If you created one or more email aliases for your account, remember:

- You can [change or delete an email alias](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/add-and-manage-email-aliases-mm6b1a490a/icloud) , but if you delete an @mac.com or @me.com email alias, you can't add it back to your iCloud account.

- You can't sign in to iCloud.com with an email alias. Sign in with your @icloud.com email address or the [Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mm864f5e6593) that you use with your iCloud account.
