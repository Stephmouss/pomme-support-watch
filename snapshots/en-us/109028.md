# Delete apps from your Apple Watch

You can manage your Apple Watch apps directly on your watch, or using the Watch app on your iPhone.

## How to delete apps from your Apple Watch

- [Press the Digital Crown](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105063) to see a selection of your frequently and recently used apps. Then swipe up to see all of your apps.

- Touch and hold the display until the apps jiggle.

[Image: An Apple Watch face is shown. Some apps have the Delete button shown, which can be used to remove an app on your Apple Watch.]

- Tap the Delete button on the app that you want to remove, then tap Delete App to confirm. Not all built-in apps can be removed. Apps that you can't delete don't show the delete button.

If you remove an app from your iPhone, that app is also deleted from your Apple Watch. If you want to restore an app, including built-in Apple apps, you can download it from the App Store on your iPhone or Apple Watch.

## How to use your iPhone to manage your Apple Watch apps

- Open the Watch app on your iPhone, then tap the My Watch tab.

- Tap Apps, then tap the app that you want to remove.

- Turn off Show App on Apple Watch. For apps that are only available on watchOS, tap Delete app on Apple Watch, then tap Delete to confirm.

[Image: iPhone screen showing Keynote app settings in the Watch app, with the Show App on Apple Watch toggle turned on.]
