# If you can't send or accept an invitation in the Home app

If you don't have the option to invite someone to your home, or otherwise can't share control of your smart home accessories, learn what to do.

- Update your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to the latest software.

- [Sign in to iCloud](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) on iPhone, iPad, or Mac. In iCloud settings, turn on Home.

- Turn on [two-factor authentication for Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102660) and [iCloud for Passwords and Keychain](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109016) .

- [Update Apple Home](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102287) .

- [Set up a home hub](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102557) . A home hub is required to:

- Share control of your home on the latest version of Apple Home.

- Invite people to control your home while you're away. Without a home hub, you can only invite people when you're at your home and connected to your home Wi-Fi network.

- Manage remote access and edit permissions for people that you invite to control your home. Without a home hub, shared users can only control your accessories while they're at your home and connected to your home Wi-Fi network.

## Get more help

- If the invitee didn't receive an invitation notification, try accessing the invitation from the Home app. Open the Home app on the invitee's device, select the More button , then select Home Settings .

- Make sure that you and the people that you invite haven't blocked each other's phone number or Apple Account. Learn how to [manage who you've blocked on iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111104) and [Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/messages/block-messages-icht1019/mac) .

- [Check VPN and third-party security software](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102281) and resolve any conflicts on the home owner's and invitee's devices. Then, [forget the Wi-Fi network](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102480) on both devices and reconnect to the Wi-Fi network that the smart home accessories are on.

If you still need help, tell us more about what's happening, and we’ll suggest what you can do next.
