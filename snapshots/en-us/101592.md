# If the side button isn't working on your Apple Watch

If the side button isn't working on your Apple Watch, here are a few steps that might fix the issue. If they don't, you'll need to set up a repair.

- Remove any protective films or cases that might block the side button.

- Check for debris or dirt around the side button and [clean your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/108893) .

- If the side button still isn't working, is damaged, or works intermittently, you can [set up an Apple Watch repair](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
