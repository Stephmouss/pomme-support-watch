# How to use Ready for Repair or Trade In

You can use Ready for Repair or Trade In on a device with version 27 or later of iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, or visionOS to keep it secure and findable in the Find My app during eligible repairs or trade ins.

## What happens when you request a repair or trade in for your device

When you request a repair or trade in, Apple or the Authorized Service Provider will provide guidance on whether you can use Ready for Repair or Trade In to initiate the repair for your iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro. For eligible repairs or trade ins, using Ready for Repair or Trade In keeps Find My on throughout the repair or trade in process.

Here's what the process looks like:

- You request an authorized repair or trade in for your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, or Apple Vision Pro.

- You're given steps to prepare your device for service or trade in. If that device is updated to version 27 or later of iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, or visionOS, you turn on Ready for Repair or Trade In as part of the process to prepare your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, or Apple Vision Pro for in-person service or prepare your Mac for in-person or mail-in service. (For an Apple Watch or Apple Vision Pro, you can do this on the web or on another device.)

- You bring your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, or Apple Vision Pro to a service center or send in your Mac. In the Find My app on a device also updated to version 27 or later of iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or watchOS, or on [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find/) , you can check if the repair or trade in is in progress.*

- A technician completes the repair or trade in. They mark that it's complete and Ready for Repair or Trade In automatically turns off.

If you decide to cancel the repair or trade in, you can turn off Ready for Repair or Trade In before you send in your device.

* Apple Vision Pro location isn't available in the Find My app.

## Turn on Ready for Repair or Trade In on your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Find My app.

- Select the device that needs repair, then tap the More button .

- Under Settings, scroll down to Repair or Trade In. Tap Repair or Trade In.

[Image: iPhone screen shows Find My app with device list, including options for "Repair or Trade In."]

- Tap Enable.

- To authenticate, enter your Apple Account password.

To turn off Ready for Repair or Trade In, tap Turn Off next to Repair or Trade In. Then tap Turn Off again to confirm.

## Turn on Ready for Repair or Trade In on your Mac

- Open the Find My app.

- Select the device that needs repair.

- Under Settings, scroll down to Repair or Trade In. Click Repair or Trade In.

[Image: Find My app interface showing a device selected with options for Repair or Trade In.]

- Click Enable.

- To authenticate, enter your Apple Account password.

To turn off Ready for Repair or Trade In, click Turn Off next to Repair or Trade In. Then click Turn Off again to confirm.

## Turn on Ready for Repair or Trade In on the web

- Sign in to [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find/) .

- Select the device that needs repair.

- Click Repair or Trade In.

- Click Enable.

- To authenticate, enter your Apple Account password.

To turn off Ready for Repair or Trade In, click Turn Off next to Repair or Trade In. Then click Turn Off again to confirm.
