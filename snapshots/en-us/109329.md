# Subscribe to Apple Music

Subscribe to Apple Music to start listening to millions of songs. Discover new music every day, listen to your favorite songs with incredible quality, and access your music library across your devices.

You can subscribe to Apple Music at [music.apple.com](https://music.apple.com) . Or subscribe in the Apple Music app on an Apple device, PC, smart TV, Android mobile device, or streaming device.

## How to subscribe to Apple Music

- Open the Apple Music app.

- Go to Home.

- Tap or click the trial offer*, then tap or click See More Plans.

- Choose an individual subscription, [family subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109339) to share with up to six people, or [student subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/106008) .

- Sign in to the Apple Account that you use to make purchases. If you don’t have an Apple Account, you can follow the steps on the screen to create a new one.

- Confirm your billing information and add a valid payment method.

- Tap or click Join.

You can also go to [music.apple.com](https://music.apple.com) to subscribe. Go to Home and click the trial offer to get started.

## How to listen to Apple Music on all of your devices

After you subscribe, sign in with your Apple Account on your other devices to access Apple Music. If you want to access your music library across your devices, [learn how to turn on Sync Library](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285) .

### On your iPhone, iPad, or Android mobile device

- Open the Apple Music app and tap Home.

- Tap the My Account button , your photo, or initials.

- Sign in to your Apple Account.

### On your Mac

- Open the Apple Music app.

- In the menu bar at the top of your screen, choose Account > Sign In.

- Sign in to your Apple Account.

### On your PC

- Open the Apple Music app.

- At the bottom of the sidebar, click Sign In.

- Sign in to your Apple Account.

### On your Apple TV

- Go to Settings > Users and Accounts.

- Select Sign In. If there's more than one user, select Add User.

- Sign in to your Apple Account.

### On your Google Nest or Amazon Alexa devices

[Set up Apple Music on your Google Nest device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285)

[Set up Apple Music on your Amazon Alexa device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119922)

*One trial per person or family.

Apple Music isn't a back up service. Make sure to [back up your iPhone, iPad](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT203977) , [Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201250) , or PC, so that you have a copy of your music and other information if your device is ever replaced, lost, or damaged.
