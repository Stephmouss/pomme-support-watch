# Manage your iCloud storage on your Apple device

Need more iCloud storage? You can easily manage your storage to make space or upgrade to iCloud+ on your Apple device.

When you set up iCloud, you automatically get 5 GB of storage for free. You can use that space for your iCloud backups, to keep your photos and videos stored in iCloud Photos, to keep your files up to date in iCloud Drive, and more. You can [check how much iCloud storage you're using](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mm039c13d410) in Settings on your device or on [iCloud.com](https://icloud.com/) . And if you're running low on iCloud storage, you can manage your storage to make more space or upgrade to iCloud+.

Not sure about the difference between the storage capacity that comes with your device and iCloud storage? [Learn more about device storage and iCloud storage](https://support.apple.com/102670) .

[Learn how to upgrade to iCloud+](https://support.apple.com/108349)

## Make more space available in iCloud

If you run out of iCloud storage, your device won’t back up to iCloud, new photos and videos won't upload to iCloud Photos, and iCloud Drive and other iCloud apps won't stay up to date across your devices. And you won't be able to send or receive emails with your iCloud email address.

You can free up storage in iCloud by deleting content that you're not using — such as duplicate photos, files, and texts and attachments in Messages. Before you delete anything, you might want to [archive or make copies of the information that you store in iCloud](https://support.apple.com/108306) .

To remove content that you're not using, select a category below and follow the steps.

- [Check Recommended for You on your iPhone and iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#recommended)

- [Reduce the size of your iCloud Backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#backups)

- [Delete photos in iCloud Photos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#iCloud_photo_library)

- [Delete folders or files in iCloud Drive](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#iCloud_Drive)

- [Delete texts and attachments in Messages](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#Messages)

- [Delete messages and manage Mail](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#Mail)

- [Delete posters and photos for Contacts](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#delete-posters-photos)

- [Delete voice memos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#Voice-memos)

- [Delete FaceTime data from iCloud](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#facetime)

- [Delete Apple Invites data from iCloud](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#deleteevent)

You can also [manage iCloud storage on your Windows PC](https://support.apple.com/121314) .

## Check Recommended for You

If you're getting low on storage on your iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, go to Recommended for You in iCloud settings. From here, you can delete certain things that you might not need anymore, like photos, large files, or old backups.

- Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud.

- Tap Recommended for You.

- Tap one of the options and follow the onscreen instructions to free up storage.

[Image: iPhone screen showing "Recommended For You" with a suggestion to "Sync Photos to iCloud."]

If you don't see what you're looking for under Recommended For You, you can still manually delete content from iCloud.

## Reduce the size of your iCloud Backup

When you [back up your iPhone or iPad to iCloud](https://support.apple.com/118426) , the most important information on your device is backed up automatically. Many apps automatically back up to iCloud after you install them. To decrease the size of your iCloud Backup and free up space in iCloud, you can stop backing up apps that you don't use, or delete old iCloud backups.

[Learn more about what iCloud backs up](https://support.apple.com/108770)

### Choose which apps to back up on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud.

- Tap Storage or tap Manage Account Storage.

- Tap Backups, then tap the name of the device that you're using.

- Turn off any apps that you don't want to back up.

- Tap Turn Off to confirm.

When you confirm that you want to Turn Off, iCloud Backup turns off for that app and removes all of its information from iCloud.

Some apps are always backed up and you can't turn them off.

### Delete backups and turn off iCloud Backup for your device

You can delete old iCloud backups for the device you're currently using or older devices that you might not use anymore. iCloud backups are available for 180 days after you disable or stop using iCloud Backup. Backups that are currently being used to restore a device can't be deleted.

#### iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud.

- Tap Storage or tap Manage Account Storage, then tap Backups.

- Tap the name of the device with the backup you'd like to delete.

- Tap Turn Off and Delete from iCloud. Tap Turn Off and Delete to confirm.

When you delete a backup, it also turns off iCloud Backup for that device. You can turn iCloud Backup back on in iCloud settings on your device.

#### Mac

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings.

- Click your name, then click iCloud.

- Click Manage, then select Backups.

- Select one of the backups, then click the remove button (–) to delete. If you want to turn off backups and remove all backups for that device from iCloud, choose Delete when you're asked to confirm.

## Delete photos in iCloud Photos

iCloud Photos uses your iCloud storage to keep all of your photos and videos up to date across your devices. You can make more space in iCloud when you delete photos and videos that you no longer need from the Photos app.

- When you use iCloud Photos and delete a photo or video on one device, it also deletes on all other devices where you're signed in with the same Apple Account. Before you delete anything, make sure that you [back up the photos and videos that you still want](https://support.apple.com/108306) .

- If you're part of an iCloud Shared Photo Library, photos added to the Shared Library only count towards the storage of the person who created the Shared Library. To delete items that count towards your iCloud storage, [make sure that you're viewing your Personal Library](https://support.apple.com/118229) .

- In version 27 or later of iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and visionOS, photos added to a shared album count towards the storage of the person who created the shared album. If you delete a shared album, participants lose access to all items not saved to their personal library. Photos deleted from a shared album delete immediately — save a copy to your personal library to retain access.

If you don't use iCloud Photos, you can [delete photos from your Photos Library instead](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#camera_roll) .

You can recover photos and videos that you delete from your Recently Deleted album for 30 days. Items in Recently Deleted don't count towards your iCloud storage. If you exceed your iCloud storage limit, your device immediately removes any photos and videos you delete and they won't be available for recovery in your Recently Deleted album.

### Delete photos on iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Photos app.

- Go to your Library.

- Tap Select, then choose the photos or videos that you want to delete.

- Tap the Delete button , and then tap Delete Photo or Delete Video.

[Image: Delete button]

#### If you don't use iCloud Photos

If you don't use iCloud Photos, your Photos Library is part of your iCloud Backup. To check how much storage your Photos Library is using in iCloud Backup and reduce its size:

- Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud.

- Tap Storage or tap Manage Account Storage, then tap Backups.

- Tap the name of the device you're using, then look at Photo Library.

- To reduce the size of your Photos backup:

- [Save your photos and videos to your computer](https://support.apple.com/120267) , then [manually back up your device](https://support.apple.com/118426) .

- If you want to keep your photos and videos on your device, you can [turn off Photos in Backup](https://support.apple.com/108922#selectapps) or [upgrade to iCloud+](https://support.apple.com/108349) for more storage.

### Delete photos on Mac

- Open the Photos app.

- Select the photos and videos that you want to delete.

- Press the delete key on your keyboard, then click Delete to confirm.

### Delete photos on iCloud.com

- From your iPhone, iPad, Apple Vision Pro, or computer, sign in to [icloud.com/photos](https://www.icloud.com/photos/) .

- Select the photos or videos that you want to delete.

- To select multiple photos or videos in iOS, iPadOS, or visionOS, tap Select. In macOS, press and hold Command while you select the items. In Windows, press and hold Control while you select the items.

- To select all in iOS, iPadOS, or visionOS, tap Select All. In macOS, press Command-A. In Windows, press Control-A.

- Tap or click the Delete button , then tap or click Delete.

[Image: Delete button]

### Delete shared albums

Photos deleted from a shared album are deleted immediately. Be sure to save a copy to your personal library to retain access.

[Learn how to delete a shared album in iOS, iPadOS, or visionOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108314#delete-album-ios)

[Learn how to delete a shared album in macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/124160#delete-album-macos)

## Delete folders or files in iCloud Drive

You can manage and delete folders or files you store in iCloud Drive from your iPhone, iPad, Apple Vision Pro, or Mac. If you're a participant in someone else's shared folder, it doesn't count towards your iCloud storage.

### iPhone

- Go to the Files app and tap Browse.

- Under Locations, tap iCloud Drive.

- To delete files or folders, tap the More button , then tap Select and choose the files or folders that you want to delete. If the file is inside a folder, tap the folder first to open it.

- Tap the Delete button.

- Then tap Delete to remove the file or folder from iCloud Drive on all of your devices.

### iPad or Apple Vision Pro

- Go to the Files app and open the sidebar.

- Under Locations, tap iCloud Drive.

- To delete files or folders, tap Select, then choose the files or folders that you want to remove. If the file is inside a folder, tap the folder first to open it.

- Tap Delete.

- Tap Delete to remove the file or folder from iCloud Drive on all of your devices.

### Mac

- Open a Finder window and go to the iCloud Drive folder in the sidebar.

- Drag an item to the Trash, or move it to a different folder on your Mac. If you move it to the Trash, you can find it in Trash for 30 days.

### iCloud.com

- Sign in to [icloud.com/drive](https://www.icloud.com/drive) .

- Select the files or folders that you want to delete, then tap or click the Delete button . You can find it in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

## Delete texts and attachments in Messages

When you use [Messages in iCloud](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mm0de0d4528d/) , the text messages and the attachments that you send and receive use your iCloud storage. You can make more space available when you delete texts and attachments like photos, videos, and files that you don't need anymore.

### Delete attachments on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- In a message conversation, tap the contact's name at the top of the screen.

- In iOS 26 or later, tap Photos. In iOS 18 or earlier, in the Photos section, tap See All.

- Tap Edit, then tap Select Photos. In iOS 18 or earlier, tap Select.

[Image: iPhone screen showing photos of food from a conversation, with Select Photos selected at the top of the screen above Edit Contact Info and Edit Background.]

- Tap the images you'd like to delete.

- Tap Delete, then tap Delete Attachment to confirm.

You can also [automatically delete old messages](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph2c9c4bfcb) .

#### Delete an entire conversation

- Swipe left over the conversation that you want to delete.

- Tap the Delete button .

[Image: Delete button]

- Tap Delete again to confirm.

### Delete attachments on your Mac

- Open a message conversation.

- Click the Info button , then scroll to the Photos section.

[Image: Info button]

- Click on an attachment, then hold the Command key to select multiple attachments.

- Control-click or right-click, then choose Delete.

- Click Delete Attachment to confirm.

#### Delete an entire conversation

- Control-click a conversation.

- Choose Delete or Delete Conversation.

- Click Delete to confirm.

## Delete email messages and manage Mail

You can free up iCloud space when you delete email messages from your iCloud email account. Messages with attachments take up more space than emails with just text.

To easily delete old promotions, updates, or transactions, you can use iCloud Mail Cleanup, or you can delete messages manually.

### Set up iCloud Mail Cleanup on iPhone or iPad

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the Mail app and go to your iCloud Mail inbox.

- Tap the More button in the upper corner, then tap Cleanup Recommendations.

- Tap iCloud Mail Cleanup. Choose which type of messages to clean up, such as subscriptions, promotions, or transactions.

[Image: iPhone screen showing iCloud Mail Cleanup with a suggestion to Clean Up Promotions.]

- Follow the onscreen steps to identify and delete those messages.

iCloud Mail Cleanup continues to clean up this type of email automatically until you turn the feature off.

[You can also use iCloud Mail Cleanup on iCloud.com](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mma3d15ee93e/)

### Delete messages on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Swipe left across any message to delete it.

- Go to your Mailboxes and choose your Trash folder.

- To delete messages one at a time, swipe left on each message. Or tap Select, select the messages that you want to delete, then tap Delete.

You can also [move email messages](https://support.apple.com/100627) from your iPhone or iPad to your Mac, where they no longer count against your iCloud storage.

### Delete messages on your Mac

- Open the Mail app.

- Select the messages that you want to delete, then click the Delete button.

- From the Mailbox menu in the menu bar, choose Erase Deleted Items, then select your iCloud email account from the menu.

- Click Erase to confirm.

### Delete messages on iCloud.com

- Sign in to [icloud.com/mail](https://www.icloud.com/mail/) .

- Select the messages that you want to delete, then click the Delete button.

- Choose your Trash folder.

- To delete messages one at a time, select the message you want to delete, then select the Trash button . To delete multiple messages, select the messages you want to delete then select Delete.

[Image: Trash button]

## Delete posters and photos for Contacts

Starting in iOS 26, iPadOS 26, visionOS 26, and macOS Tahoe 26, when you save a contact photo and poster, those images count toward your iCloud storage. You can delete these contact images to help free up iCloud storage. Deleting the images doesn't delete the contact.

### iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud.

- Tap Storage or Manage Account Storage.

- Tap Contact Images, then tap Delete Data from iCloud.

### Mac

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings.

- Click your name, then click iCloud.

- Click Manage, then click Contact Images.

- Click Delete from iCloud.

## Delete voice memos

With [Voice Memos in iCloud](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph4d2a39a3b) , your voice recordings are stored in iCloud. You can delete them if you need more space.

### iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Voice Memos app, then tap All Recordings.

- Select the voice memos that you want to delete, then tap the Delete button .

[Image: Delete button]

- Tap Recently Deleted.

- Tap Select. Tap Delete All, or select the voice memo, then tap Delete.

- Tap Delete Forever to confirm.

### Mac

- Select the voice memos you want to delete.

- Click the Delete button .

[Image: Delete button]

- Open Recently Deleted, then click Delete All or select the voice memo, then click Delete.

- Click Delete Forever to confirm.

## Delete FaceTime data from iCloud

When you delete FaceTime from iCloud, it removes all of your data from the app and certain data from the Phone app, including phone voicemails and FaceTime video and audio messages. You won’t be able to recover the data after you delete FaceTime from iCloud. Depending on your software version, you can [save and export video messages from FaceTime](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108306) before you delete FaceTime from iCloud, so they save to your Photos app.

### iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud.

- Tap Storage or Manage Account Storage.

- Tap FaceTime, then tap Delete Data from iCloud.

### Mac

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings.

- Click your name, then click iCloud.

- Click Manage, then click FaceTime.

- Click Delete from iCloud.

## Delete Apple Invites data from iCloud

When you create an event in Apple Invites, the background image and any images added when a guest replies to an RSVP count toward the event creator's iCloud storage. You can delete all events or individual events that you’ve created to free up iCloud storage. When you delete an event, participants lose access to the event and most information associated with it. If participants joined a shared album or shared playlist, they still have access to those in the Photos app and Music app.

If you delete all Apple Invites data, all of the events you've created are removed, but events that you're attending won't be removed. You need to leave each of the events in order for your data to be deleted.

### Delete all Apple Invites data on iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap iCloud.

- Tap Storage or Manage Account Storage.

- Tap Apple Invites, then tap Delete Data from iCloud.

### Delete individual events on iPhone or iPad

- Open the Apple Invites app, then select the event.

- Tap the More button in the upper-right corner, then tap Event Settings.

- Tap Delete Event, then tap again to confirm.

### Delete individual events on iCloud.com

- Go to [icloud.com/invites](https://icloud.com/invites) , then sign in to your [Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-invites/aside/dev56fb72aaa/) (if necessary).

- Select the event.

- Select the Event Settings button .

- Tap or click Delete Event, then tap or click again to confirm.
