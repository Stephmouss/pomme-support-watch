# How to use Hide My Email with Sign in with Apple

Sign in with Apple allows you to use Hide My Email to keep your personal email private when you use Sign in with Apple to create a new account with an app or website.

## How Hide My Email works

Hide My Email generates unique, random email addresses that automatically forward to your personal email inbox. Each address is unique to you. You can read and respond directly to emails sent to these addresses and your personal email address is kept private.

If you create an account with an app or website that supports Sign in with Apple, you can choose to share your email address if you're familiar with the app or website, or hide your email address if you prefer more privacy. If you choose the Hide My Email option, only the app or website you created the account with can use this unique email address to communicate with you. When you select this option in Sign in with Apple, the Hide My Email addresses created use the address domain @privaterelay.appleid.com. This means that only emails sent from designated email addresses by the app or website will be automatically forwarded to the verified email address set on your Apple Account.

Apple doesn't read or process any of the content in the email messages that pass through Hide My Email, except to perform standard spam filtering that's required to maintain our status as a trusted email provider. All email messages are deleted from our relay servers after they're delivered to you, usually within seconds.

[Learn more about Sign in with Apple](https://support.apple.com/102609)

## View your Hide My Email addresses

Here's how to view your Hide My Email addresses that are used with Sign in With Apple.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap your name.

- Tap Sign in with Apple. From here, you can see the apps and websites that are using Sign in with Apple. To see if you used Hide My Email for a particular app or website, choose the app or website from the list. If you used Hide My Email, you should see the @privaterelay.appleid.com for that app or website.

### On your Mac

- Choose Apple menu , then click System Settings.

- Click your name, then click Sign in With Apple.

- Choose the app or website.

## Manage the Hide My Email addresses

If you have an iCloud+ subscription, you can change the email address used to forward emails to the verified email address that's on your Apple Account.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap your name, then tap iCloud.

- Tap Hide My Email in the iCloud+ Features section.

- Scroll to the bottom, tap Forward To, then choose the address that you want to use.

### On your Mac

- Choose Apple menu , then click System Settings.

- Click your name, then click iCloud.

- Choose Hide My Email in the iCloud+ Features section.

- Look for the Forward To menu and choose the email you want to use.
