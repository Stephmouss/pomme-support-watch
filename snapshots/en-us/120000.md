# If you see SOS, No Service, or Searching on your iPhone or iPad

If you see "SOS" or "SOS only" in the status bar, your device can still make emergency calls.

If you see "No Service" or "Searching" in the status bar on your iPhone or iPad (Wi-Fi + Cellular), your device isn't connected to a cellular network.

On iPhone models running iOS 18 or later, you can [download the latest version of the Apple Support app](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-support/id1130498044?ppid=5e7c213d-dd8a-485d-acc0-e724f97419e6) to run additional diagnostics that might help determine the source of your device's issue.

## If you see "SOS" or "SOS" only

If you see "SOS" or "SOS only" in the status bar, your device isn't connected to your cellular network, but you can still make emergency calls through other carrier networks. This feature is available in Australia, Canada, and the United States.

[Image: Top part of iPhone screen showing "SOS only" in status bar.]

[Learn about using Emergency SOS on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/104992)

## Try to reconnect to your cellular network

If your cellular network is available, you might be able to reconnect by turning Airplane Mode on and off.

- Open the Settings app.

- [Turn on Airplane Mode](https://support.apple.com/108785) for at least 15 seconds.

- Turn off Airplane Mode.

## Restart your iPhone or iPad

Restart your device. If you're not sure how to do this, follow these steps to [restart your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/118259) or [restart your iPad](https://support.apple.com/101603) .

## Contact your carrier

Issues with your carrier or account might affect your service. Contact your carrier to:

- Verify that your account is active and in good standing.

- Make sure that you're in an area with cellular network coverage and there are no outages in your area.

- Check that your device isn't blocked or barred from receiving cellular services and is set up with the right data plan.

- Ask if your local government requires IMEI registration to use an iPhone that you originally purchased outside your country or region.

Only your wireless carrier can access and manage details about your account:

- [iPhone wireless carriers](https://support.apple.com/109526)

- [iPad wireless carriers](https://support.apple.com/118626)

## Update your iPhone or iPad

Update your iPhone or iPad to the [latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/118575) .

## Check for a Carrier Settings update

Manually check for and install a [carrier settings update](https://support.apple.com/109324) .

- Make sure that your device is connected to Wi-Fi.

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap General.

- Tap About. If an update is available, you'll see an option to update your carrier settings.

- To see the version of carrier settings on your device, look next to Carrier under eSIM.

If you insert a new SIM card into your iPhone or iPad, you need to download the carrier settings for your new carrier.

## If you have an iPhone with Dual SIM

If you have an iPhone with Dual SIM, check your cellular lines and eSIM/SIM.

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Cellular.

- Tap the cellular line you'd like to review.

- Check to see if it's turned on.

- If the cellular line is off, tap Turn On This Line. Then, check to see if you have service.

If you don't see your cellular line, contact your carrier to set up an eSIM or insert a physical SIM card. If you're using a physical SIM card, remove the SIM card and put it back in.

If your SIM card is damaged, doesn't fit in the SIM tray, or you transferred your physical SIM card from another device, ask your carrier for a new SIM card.

Learn more about removing your [iPhone SIM card](https://support.apple.com/109357) or [iPad SIM card](https://support.apple.com/102392) .

## If you are traveling internationally

If you're traveling internationally, make sure that your device is set up for data roaming.

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Cellular or Cellular Data.

- If your device has a single SIM card or eSIM, turn on Cellular Data, then tap Cellular Data Options.

- If your device uses Dual SIM or Dual eSIM, turn on Cellular Data, tap the number you want to modify, then tap Cellular Data Options.

- Turn on Data Roaming.

[Image: iPhone Cellular Data Options screen with Data Roaming toggled on]

## If your device uses a 3G network

If you see "No Service" in the status bar of your device and you have an iPhone 5s, iPhone 5c, or earlier or an iPad 2 (Wi-Fi + Cellular) or earlier, contact your carrier to discuss your options.

If you have an iPhone 6 or later or an iPad (3rd generation) or later, check your Cellular Data Options settings.

- Update your iPhone or iPad to the [latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/118575) .

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Cellular.

- For iPhone, tap Cellular Data Options, then turn on Enable LTE.

- For iPad, turn on LTE.

If you still see No Service after following these steps, contact your carrier.

iPhone and iPad devices that support 5G networks are not affected by the phasing out of 3G networks.

## Get help

- If you see an alert next to "No Service", [learn what you can do](https://support.apple.com/101582) .

- If your carrier confirms that there's no issue with your account or network, and a hardware issue is suspected, you might need service. [Get service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
