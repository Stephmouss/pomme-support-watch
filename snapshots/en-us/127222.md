# If your iPhone doesn't vibrate in Silent Mode

Check your settings to make sure vibration is enabled for Silent Mode.

## Check your Haptics settings

- In Settings, scroll down and tap Sounds & Haptics.

- Tap Haptics.

- Select Play in Silent Mode.

## Check your Do Not Disturb settings

- Open Control Center.

- Tap Focus.

- If Do Not Disturb is highlighted, tap to turn it off.
