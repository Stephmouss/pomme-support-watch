# If you can't set up an eSIM on your iPhone

Learn what to do if you can't set up an eSIM on your iPhone.

[Learn more about eSIM](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118669)

## What you need

- Your new iPhone must be connected to a Wi-Fi network or hotspot. In some countries and regions, iPhone models that are eSIM-only can activate without a Wi-Fi network.

- An active cellular plan and eSIM on your iPhone from a [carrier that supports eSIM](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101569)

- [Your iPhone or iPhone devices need the latest version of iOS that they can use](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

## Follow these steps

After following each step below, open Control Center by swiping down from the upper-right corner of the screen. Check if your carrier appears in the status bar. If not, go to the next step.

- [Turn Airplane Mode on and off](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108785) .

- Open the Settings app and tap Cellular. Check if the number you're trying to activate is shown. If it is, turn off the line and then turn it back on again.

- Restart your device.

On iPhone models running iOS 18 or later, you can [download the latest version of the Apple Support app](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-support/id1130498044?ppid=5e7c213d-dd8a-485d-acc0-e724f97419e6) to run additional diagnostics that might help determine the source of your device's issue.

## Check the carrier settings

Check the carrier settings on your device.

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap General

- Tap About.

- Scroll down to the eSIM section. Your iPhone's carrier name and a version number is next to Carrier. Tap the version number. If an update is available, you'll see a prompt to select OK or Update.

## Contact your carrier

If you still can't set up an eSIM on your iPhone, [contact your carrier](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101569) .

Gathering the following information ahead of time might be helpful:

- Note the error message you are seeing on your iPhone.

- Collect your iPhone and carrier account identifiers:

- Your phone number

- Your carrier account password and/or PIN

- Your iPhone's IMEI or EID. To find them open the Settings app. Tap General and then tap About.
