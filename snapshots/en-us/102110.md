# Manage emails that you receive from Apple

You can update your Apple email subscription preferences by clicking unsubscribe in the email footer, or on the Apple Account page.

- Sign in to [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/) .

- Go to Privacy, then select Messages from Apple.

- Check the corresponding boxes of the emails that you want to receive from Apple.
