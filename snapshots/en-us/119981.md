# About the water and dust resistance of Beats Pill

Learn about the water and dust resistance of your Beats Pill speaker. And learn what to do if your Beats Pill gets wet accidentally.

## Is my Beats Pill speaker waterproof?

Your Beats Pill is water- and dust- resistant*, but not waterproof or dustproof. If your Beats Pill comes into contact with liquid, wipe it down with a soft, dry, lint-free cloth.

Water resistance and dust resistance are not permanent conditions. Resistance might decrease as a result of normal wear.

Here's how to prevent damage to your Beats Pill due to contact with liquid:

- Don't expose your Beats Pill to pressurized water or high velocity water, such as when showering.

- Don't expose your Beats Pill to soap, detergent, acids or acidic foods, or any liquids—for example, salt water, soapy water, pool water, perfume, insect repellent, lotion, sunscreen, oil, adhesive remover, hair dye, and solvents.

- Don't use your Beats Pill in a sauna or steam room.

- Don't intentionally submerge your Beats Pill in water.

- Don't operate your Beats Pill in extremely humid conditions.

- Don't drop your Beats Pill or subject it to other impacts.

- Don't disassemble your Beats Pill.

* Beats Pill has a rating of IP67 under IEC standard 60529.

## What should I do if my Beats Pill speaker gets wet or dusty?

If your Beats Pill has been exposed to liquid, don't charge it until it’s completely dry. Charging when wet may damage your Beats Pill.

If your Beats Pill comes into contact with liquid or dust, [here's how to clean it](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101898) .
