# If users can't sign in to iCloud or Google on a managed iOS device

Learn what to do if users on managed iOS devices can't sign in to iCloud or Google.

This article is for system administrators.

If you restrict which apps can be used on managed iOS devices, users might not be able to sign in to iCloud or Google. To let users sign in to their accounts without modifying restrictions, add the following bundle ID to the list of allowed apps:

com.apple.CoreCDPUI.localSecretPrompt
