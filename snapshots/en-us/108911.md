# If you can't find your family's shared apps and content

If members of your Family Sharing group can’t find shared content, check your purchase sharing settings, find out whether the content is shareable, and more.

## Check your Family Sharing settings

Make sure that you're using the same Apple Account for Family Sharing and Media & Purchases. And make sure that purchase sharing is turned on. Then ask your family members to check their settings too. In order to share purchases with your family group, you and the people you're trying to share with need to turn on purchase sharing.

[Learn how to turn on purchase sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108774)

## On your iPhone or iPad with iOS 16 or later

- Open the Settings app, then tap Family.

- Tap your name.

- Make a note of which Apple Account is listed.

[Image: On an iPhone, the Settings app is open, and Family Sharing settings for Danny Rico, with options for Personal Information, Parent/Guardian, Subscriptions, and Purchases, is shown.]

- Tap Purchases.

- Tap Apple Account for Purchases and make sure that you're signed in with the same Apple Account that you use for Family Sharing. If not, tap Share using secondary Apple Account and enter the email and password for that Apple Account.

[Image: On an iPhone, the Settings app is open, and Purchases settings, including options for Share My Purchases and Apple Account for Purchases, is shown.]

## On your Mac with macOS Ventura or later

- Choose Apple menu , click System Settings, then click Family.

- Click your name and make a note of which Apple Account is listed.

- Click Purchases and make sure that you're signed in with the same Apple Account that you use for Family Sharing. If not, click Personal Information in the sidebar, then click Share using secondary Apple Account. Enter the email address or phone number for that Apple Account, then enter your password.

[Image: On a Mac, System Settings is open and Family Sharing settings, including Purchase Sharing, is shown.]

- Open the App Store.

- In the menu bar, choose Store, then choose Account.

- Click your name at the top of the screen, then make sure that you're using the same Apple Account that you use for Family Sharing.

## If you still can't find shared content

- [Sign out of Media & Purchases](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108911#signout)

- [Learn what type of content is shareable](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108911#shareable)

- [Find out if the purchased app is hidden](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108911#hidden)

- [Make sure that your device meets the system requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108911#systemrequirements)

- [Learn more about in-app purchases, app subscriptions, and non-consumable in-app purchases](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102383)

### Sign out of Media & Purchases

Make sure that you [sign in to all Apple services](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) using the same Apple Account that you use for sharing content with your family group. You don't need to sign out of any other services that use your Apple Account, such as iCloud or iMessage.

On your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap your name.

- Tap Media & Purchases.

- Tap Sign Out, then tap Confirm.

- Tap Media & Purchases, then tap Not [your name]? and sign in with the Apple Account that you use for Family Sharing.

On your Mac

- Open the App Store.

- In the menu bar, choose Store, then click Sign Out.

- In the menu bar, choose Store, then click Sign In.

- Sign in with the same Apple Account that you use for Family Sharing.

On Apple TV

- Go to Settings, click Profiles & Accounts, then click [your name].

- Select Store.

- Select Sign Out.

- Select Sign In.

- Sign in with the same Apple Account that you use for Family Sharing.

### Learn what type of content is shareable

Some subscriptions, such as individual Apple Music and Apple One plans and subscriptions and in-app purchases from non-participating apps, can't be shared through Family Sharing.

[Learn what types of content you can share with your family](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111114)

[Learn how to add a family member to your shared subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108107)

### Ask your family member whether the purchased app is hidden

If a family member has hidden one of their purchased apps, no one in their Family Sharing group can see it. When you hide a purchased app, it also hides in-app purchases and subscriptions within the app.

[Learn how to hide or unhide purchases from the App Store](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108091)

### Make sure that your device meets the system requirements

To access your family's shared content, you need an iPhone or iPad using iOS 9 or later, or a Mac using OS X El Capitan 10.11 or later. If you're using an older device, you won’t be able to see your family’s shared content.
