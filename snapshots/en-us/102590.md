## How to get apps

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the App Store.

- Browse through the Today, Games, Apps, or [Arcade](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102206) tabs to find apps you like. Or tap the Search tab to look for something specific.

[Image: On an iPhone, the App Store is open and the Search tab at the bottom of the screen is shown.]

- Tap or click the price or Get button. If you see the Open button instead of a price or Get button, you already bought or downloaded that app.

- Double-click the side button on the right side of your iPhone—or the top button on your iPad— to complete your purchase. If you use Face ID or Touch ID to unlock your device, you'll glance at your device or touch the Home button to authenticate the download. You might need to enter your Apple Account password to complete the download.

In the App Store, if an app has a Get button instead of a price, the app is free. You won't be charged for downloading a free app. Some free apps offer in-app purchases and subscriptions that you can buy. Subscriptions and in-app purchases give you access to additional features, content, and more.

[Learn more about in-app purchases and subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102383)

## How to find apps that you bought

[Swipe down from the middle of the Home Screen to use Spotlight Search](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118232) and enter the app you're looking for. You can also [find your apps in the App Library](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108324) .

## If you can't find the App Store

If the App Store is missing on your device, you might have Content & Privacy Restrictions turned on. [Adjust your iTunes & App Store Purchases settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105121#prevent-purchases) and make sure that you choose "Allow" for the Installing Apps setting. The App Store should reappear on your device.

If you still can't find the App Store, swipe down to search for it with Spotlight.
