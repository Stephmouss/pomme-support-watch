# Use Screen Time to turn off in-app purchases on your iPhone or iPad

You can use Screen Time to prevent unintentional or unauthorized purchases from the App Store and other Apple services. You also can require a password for purchases, prevent certain types of purchases, or turn off purchasing completely.

To prevent in-app purchases, you need to turn on Screen Time first. [Learn how to turn on and use Screen Time](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108806) .

## How to turn off in-app purchases on your iPhone or iPad

- When Screen Time is enabled, open the Settings app, then tap Screen Time.

- Tap Content & Privacy Restrictions. If asked, enter your passcode, then turn on Content & Privacy Restrictions. You'll only be asked to enter a passcode if you already created one in Screen Time. Learn [how to create a Screen Time passcode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108806#lock-screen-time) .

[Image: An iPhone screen showing the settings for Content & Privacy Restrictions.]

- Tap iTunes & App Store Purchases.

- Tap In-app Purchases and select Don't Allow. *

[Image: An iPhone screen showing the settings to disable iTunes & App Store purchases.]

* From here, you can also require a password for future purchases from iTunes, Books, or App Store.

## Other ways to control purchases on an Apple device

- If children in your Family Sharing group have their own devices, [use Ask to Buy to screen and approve their purchase requests](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105055) .

- If you let children use your device, you can [require a password for every purchase](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119848) .
