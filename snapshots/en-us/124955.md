# How to attach and adjust Crossbody Strap or Wrist Strap

Learn how to attach, adjust, and care for Crossbody Strap and Wrist Strap. Select Apple iPhone cases are compatible with Crossbody Strap and Wrist Strap. Also, you can attach Wrist Strap to AirPods Pro 2 and later.

## Attach Crossbody Strap to your iPhone case

[Image: Close-up of a strap connector being threaded through a case opening with a blue arrow indicating movement.]

Start with your case off of your iPhone. Look for small openings on the bottom corners of your iPhone case where you can attach the Crossbody Strap connectors.

Thread the cord loop end of the connector from the outside of the case, through one of the openings in the case. Continue threading the cord loop through the second hole, to the outside of the case. Slip the metal ring through the cord loop, and secure the loop by gently pulling on the metal ring.

[Image: A cord loop threaded through an iPhone case opening. A blue arrow indicates the direction to thread one end of Crossbody Strap through the metal ring.]

Connect the second connector on the other corner of your iPhone case. Make sure the connectors are securely attached. Put the case back onto your iPhone.

Use the snaps on Crossbody Strap to attach and detach it from the connectors on your iPhone case. Unsnap one end of Crossbody Strap, loop through the metal ring on the connector, and snap it closed. Repeat on other end of Crossbody Strap with the other connector to attach both sides securely.

## Adjust the length of Crossbody Strap

Remove Crossbody Strap from your body before adjusting its length. Pull one stainless steel mechanism up or down. Then slide the second stainless steel mechanism until the two straps unify and lay flat against each other.

## Attach Wrist Strap to your iPhone case or AirPods Pro 2 and later

[Image: Close-up of a strap connector being threaded through a case opening with a blue arrow indicating movement.]

Attach a single connector to one corner of your iPhone case or AirPods Pro case. Look for small openings on the bottom corners of your iPhone or side of your AirPods Pro case where you can attach the Wrist Strap connector.

Thread the cord loop end of the connector from the outside of the case, through one of the openings in the case, similar to attaching Crossbody Strap. Continue threading the cord loop through the second hole, to the outside of the case. Slip the metal ring through the cord loop, and secure the loop by gently pulling on the metal ring.

[Image: A cord loop threaded through an iPhone case opening. One end of Wrist Strap is threaded through the metal ring and ready to snap onto the end of the strap.]

Loop the short end of the Wrist Strap through the metal ring on the connector. Place the long end inside the snap closure, then align the short end on top. Press the layers together to secure.

## Replace the connectors

Contact Apple to [order a replacement](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .

## Learn more

Crossbody Strap and Wrist Strap contain magnets that may interfere with medical devices. Read [important safety information in the iPhone User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/important-safety-information-iph301fc905/18.0/ios/18.0) .

You can clean the material on your [Crossbody Strap or Wrist Strap](https://support.apple.com/en-us/103258#iPhonecase) with soap and a damp cloth.
