# Buy music from the iTunes Store on your iPhone or iPad

On your iPhone or iPad, learn how to buy music in the iTunes Store app.

## Are you on a Mac or PC?

- [Learn how to buy music from the iTunes Store on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109337)

- [Learn how to buy music from the iTunes Store on your PC](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118286)

## How to buy music from the iTunes Store on your iPhone or iPad

- Open the iTunes Store app.

- At the bottom of the screen, tap Music.

- Find the song or album that you want to buy.

- Tap the price next to a song or album.

[Image: The Buy screen for Forever by Bon Jovi in the iTunes Store. The Buy button is on the right of the screen. It's the price of the album in a blue box.]

- Sign in with your Apple Account to complete your purchase. [Need help?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102627)

After you buy music, you can find it in your library in the Apple Music app.

## Want to redownload music that you purchased?

[Learn how to redownload music that you purchased from the iTunes Store on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118294)

iTunes Store availability might vary by country and region. [Learn what's available in your country or region.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205)

Any digital media you buy from Apple can be permanently downloaded on to an iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Windows PC. [Learn more](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121877) .
