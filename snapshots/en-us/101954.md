# Use the Beat Sequencer in GarageBand on iPhone or iPad

Build grooves on your iPhone or iPad with an interface inspired by classic drum machines, then add the groove to your song.

[Image: Beat Sequencer screen]

To get started using Beat Sequencer, either create a new song or open an existing one. If you've created a new song, the sound browser opens automatically.

If you want to add Beat Sequencer to an existing song, tap the Sound Browser button , swipe until you see Drums, then tap Beat Sequencer.

[Image: the Sound Browser button]

## Quickly add a beat to your song

In Beat Sequencer, tap the Patterns button , then choose the pre-designed pattern that best describes the style you want. The pattern plays back at the tempo you’ve set for your song, and loops depending on the length of the pattern. Each pre-designed pattern has unique settings, including the pattern length, [which you can change](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101954#settings) .

[Image: the Patterns button]

To stop and start to the pattern, tap the Play Beat button . If you want to add the pattern to your song, [record the pattern](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101954#record) .

[Image: the Play Beat button]

You can change the pattern by turning steps on or off. Each row corresponds to an individual instrument in the drum kit, which is shown along the left of the grid. To turn off a step in the pattern, tap a lit step in the grid. To turn a step on, tap an unlit step.

You can also change the sounds in the pattern. To change the entire kit, tap button at the bottom of the screen that shows the currently selected kit (such as Trap Door or Hacienda). In the Drums window, select the style of sounds from the left column and the individual kit from the right column. To download additional sounds, tap "Get more drum kits," then [select the sounds you’re interested in from the Sound Library](https://support.apple.com/KB/HT208195) . When you’ve found the kit you want, tap Done.

## Build your own beat

If you want to build your own beat from scratch, tap the Patterns button , then choose New Pattern. Tap Step/On Off to add and remove steps. You can add and remove steps while Beat Sequencer is playing back or idle.

[Image: the Patterns button]

After you’ve added steps, you can edit each individual step:

- To change the volume of a step, tap Velocity. Slide your finger down on the step to decrease the volume, and slide up to increase the volume.

- To slice an individual step into multiple steps, tap Note Repeat. Slide your finger upwards to increase the number of slices, and down to decrease the number of slices.

- To add human-like variation to a step, tap Chance. Then, slide your finger down to increase the variation of the step.

If you want to edit an entire row’s settings, tap an instrument along the left side of the grid.

- To change an individual kit piece, tap Kit Piece in the Row Settings window, then tap the piece you want assigned to that row. That row now plays back the newly assigned kit piece.

- To change the length of each step in the row, tap Step Length, then select a length.

- To change the direction Beat Sequencer plays back that instrument in the pattern, tap Playback Mode, then choose an option.

When you've created your beat, you can save it as a pattern. Tap the Patterns button , then tap Save. Enter a name for the pattern, then tap Done. You can recall that pattern and add it to different songs.

[Image: the Patterns button]

When you're ready to add the pattern to a song, [record the pattern](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101954#record) .

## Record the pattern

To record the pattern to your song, tap the Record button in the control bar. Beat Sequencer starts automatically. The pattern plays back in a loop until you stop recording. When you’re finished recording the pattern, tap the Tracks button to view the recorded track, then tap the Play button in the control bar to hear the pattern in the context of your other tracks.

[Image: Record button]

[Image: the Tracks button]

[Image: the Play button]

After you’ve recorded the pattern, you can edit and adjust the track as you would any other instrument track.

## View and Change Pattern Settings

Beat Sequencer uses steps to determine the length of a particular pattern. If your song is in 4/4 time, you can set the pattern length between 16 and 64 steps. If your song’s in 3/4 or 6/8 time, you can set the pattern length between 12 and 48 steps. You can also manually change the loop length of each individual kit piece by tapping the Loop Start/End button, then dragging the handle each row.

To view information and change settings for the current pattern, tap the Inspector button .

[Image: the Inspector button]

- Set length of pattern (16, 32, 48 or 64 steps in 4/4 time, and 12, 24, and 48 steps in 3/4 and 6/8 time)

- Set the step length (1/8, 1/8t, 1/16, 1/6t, 1/32)

- Set the Playback Mode (Forward, Reverse, Ping Pong, Random)

- Set the amount of swing in the pattern

- Reset the pattern. If you started with a blank pattern, tapping Reset clears the grid.
