# About Final Cut Pro

Learn more about Final Cut Pro for Mac.

Final Cut Pro is a revolutionary app for creating, editing, and producing the highest-quality video. Final Cut Pro combines high-performance digital editing and native support for virtually any video format with easy-to-use and time-saving features that let you focus on storytelling.

## How to get Final Cut Pro, Motion, and Compressor

You can purchase and download one-time purchase versions of these apps from the Mac App Store:

- [Final Cut Pro on the Mac App Store](http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/final-cut-pro/id424389933)

- [Motion on the Mac App Store](http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/motion/id434290957)

- [Compressor on the Mac App Store](http://itunes.apple.com/us/app/compressor/id424390742)

You can also get a subscription to [Apple Creator Studio](https://www.apple.com/apple-creator-studio/) , which includes Final Cut Pro, Motion, and Compressor.
