# About ECG app version availability on Apple Watch

Features in the ECG app can differ based on your country or region.

## Find your version of the ECG app

Due to regulations in your country or region, ECG version 1 or ECG version 2 might not be available. Here's how you can verify which version of the ECG app you have on your Apple Watch or iPhone.

### On your Apple Watch

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Heart, then tap ECG.

Your ECG app version number is displayed.

### On your iPhone

- Open the Health app.

- Tap Browse, then tap Heart.

- Tap ECG, then tap About.

Your ECG app version number is listed next to Feature Version.
