# If the microphones on your iPhone aren't working

If the sound is unclear during phone calls, during FaceTime calls, in video recordings, or in apps, learn what to do.

## Clear the microphones

- Remove any screen protectors, films, or cases from your iPhone.

- Clear any debris from the openings of your iPhone.

## If people can't hear you on phone or FaceTime calls

- Open the Voice Memos app.

- Tap the Record button , speak at the bottom of your phone, then stop the recording.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- When you play back the memo, you should hear your voice clearly.

## If people can't hear you when you're using speakerphone

- Open the Camera app.

- Record a video while speaking at the top of your iPhone.

- Tap the camera chooser to switch to the other camera. Then record another video while speaking at the top of your iPhone.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- When you play back the videos, you should hear your voice clearly.

## If the microphone doesn't work in a specific app

- Go to Settings.

- Tap Privacy & Security.

- Tap Microphone, then make sure that the app is enabled.

- If the app has permission to access your microphone, or if it isn't listed, [contact the app developer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102435) .

## Get service

If you still sound unclear during calls, recordings, or apps, you might need service. [Get service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .

FaceTime is not available in all countries or regions.
