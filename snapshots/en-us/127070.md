# Turn Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation on or off

Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation are optional settings that help Apple develop and improve Siri and foundation models. Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence is available for users of Siri AI, and others have the option to use Improve Siri & Dictation. When you turn these on, you agree to let Apple store and review a sample of your audio recordings and transcripts.

## Set up Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation

When you set up a new device, or turn on or use Siri, Dictation, or Translate for the first time on a device, you're asked whether to opt in to Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation. The process to opt in to Improve Siri & Dictation varies by device. For example:

- iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro : You're asked whether to opt in to Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation during device setup. If you don't turn on Siri during setup, you're asked whether to opt in the first time you turn on or use Siri, Dictation, or Translate on the device.

- Apple Watch: The Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation setting on Apple Watch matches the setting from the iPhone or iPad that you used to set it up. If you set up an Apple Watch for a family member, the Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation setting is off by default and can't be adjusted.

- HomePod : The Improve Siri & Dictation setting on your HomePod matches the setting from the iPhone or iPad that you used to set it up.

- Apple TV: If you use your iPhone or iPad to set up Apple TV, your Improve Siri & Dictation setting on your Apple TV matches the setting on that iPhone or iPad, unless you choose to customize your settings. If you set up Apple TV manually, you're asked whether to opt in during device setup.

## Adjust your Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation setting

You can adjust this setting at any time.

### On iPhone or iPad

- In the Settings app, tap Privacy & Security.

- Scroll down and tap Analytics & Improvements.

- Turn Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation on or off.

### On Mac

- Choose the Apple menu, then System Settings.

- From the sidebar, click Privacy & Security.

- Click Analytics & Improvements, then turn Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation on or off.

### On Apple Watch

- On your Apple Watch, open the Settings app.

- Tap General, then tap Analytics & Improvements.

- Turn Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation on or off.

### On HomePod

If you're the Home Organizer, you can adjust this setting from the Home app on your iPhone or iPad.

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the Home app.

- Touch and hold your HomePod, then tap Accessory Settings.

- Tap Analytics & Improvements, then turn Improve Siri & Dictation on or off.

### On Apple TV

- On your Apple TV, open the Settings app.

- Choose General, then Privacy.

- Choose Analytics & Improvements, then turn Improve Siri & Dictation on or off.

### On Apple Vision Pro

- On your Apple Vision Pro, open the Settings app.

- Select Privacy & Security, then select Analytics & Improvements.

- Turn Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation on or off.

## About Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence and Improve Siri & Dictation

When you opt in to Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence or Improve Siri & Dictation, you agree to let Apple store and review samples of your Siri, Dictation, and Translate interactions. This includes audio, transcripts, and related request data. Apple uses this information to develop and improve Siri, Dictation, and limited other language-processing features like Translate or Voice Control. If you opt in to Improve Siri & Apple Intelligence, Apple can also use this data to train foundation models.

This information is associated with a random, device-generated identifier, not with your Apple Account or email address. Only Apple employees review audio recordings. A subset of non-audio data samples may be reviewed by graders.

Your request history — which includes your audio data, transcripts, and related request data — is not used to build a marketing profile, and is never sold to anyone.
