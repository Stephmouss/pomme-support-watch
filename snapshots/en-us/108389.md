# Reset HomePod or HomePod mini

Learn how to reset your HomePod or HomePod mini to its factory settings before sending it in for service, selling it, or giving it away.

- [Before you begin](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108389#before)

- [Reset HomePod or HomePod mini to factory settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108389#reset)

- [Restore HomePod mini with a Mac or PC](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108389#restore)

## Before you begin

- If your HomePod speaker isn’t responding, first try to [restart HomePod in the Home app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105031#restart) .

- If you don't see an option to restart, or HomePod doesn't respond after restarting, unplug HomePod from power, then plug it back in. If HomePod still doesn't respond, reset it to factory settings.

- If you have two HomePod speakers set up as a stereo pair, you must ungroup the stereo pair before you can reset HomePod. Open the Home app, select the paired HomePod speakers, select the Settings button , then select Ungroup Accessories.

## Reset HomePod or HomePod mini to factory settings

You can reset HomePod in the Home app, or reset HomePod manually. Try resetting HomePod in the Home app first.

### Reset HomePod in the Home app

- Open the Home app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac that's signed in with the Apple Account that you used to set up HomePod.

- Tap or click HomePod, then tap or click the Settings button .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap or click Reset HomePod, then tap or click Remove Accessory.

[Image: Reset HomePod appears at the bottom of the HomePod settings screen.]

- If a white spinning light appears on the top of HomePod, wait until the light disappears indicating that the reset is complete. If a white spinning light does not appear, reset HomePod manually.

HomePod will chime and a white pulsing light will appear on the top of HomePod indicating that the reset is complete. Then you can unlock your iPhone and hold it next to HomePod to [set up HomePod](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111110) again.

### Reset HomePod manually

If you can't reset HomePod in the Home app:

- Unplug HomePod, or unplug the power adapter for HomePod mini

- Wait at least 10 seconds, then plug HomePod back into power.

- Wait 10 seconds, then touch and hold your finger to the top of HomePod. A white spinning light will appear.

- Continue to hold your finger to the top of HomePod. The white spinning light will turn red, and Siri will announce that your HomePod is about to reset.

- After you hear three beeps, lift your finger. HomePod will reset.

HomePod will chime and a white pulsing light will appear on the top of HomePod indicating that the reset is complete. Then you can unlock your iPhone and hold it next to HomePod to [set up HomePod](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111110) again.

## Restore HomePod mini with a Mac or PC

If you can't reset HomePod mini, you can restore it. Restoring your HomePod mini resets it and updates it to the latest software.

- Plug HomePod mini into your computer using the USB-C cable that came with it.

- Wait a few seconds. If you're using a Mac, open a Finder window. If you're using a PC, open the [latest version of the Apple Devices app or iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118406) .

- [Select your HomePod](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108392) when it appears on your computer.

- Click Restore HomePod.

[Image: The Restore HomePod button appears in the Finder on Mac.]

A notification appears on your Mac or PC when HomePod mini is done restoring. After you restore HomePod mini, you can [set up HomePod mini](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111110) again.
