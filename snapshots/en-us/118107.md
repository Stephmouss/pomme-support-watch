# Restore your iPhone, iPad, or iPod to factory settings using a computer

Erase your device and install the latest software using a computer. This factory restore removes all information and settings from your iPhone, iPad, or iPod.

[Image: Play the video]

## Prepare your device

- Update your Mac or PC.

- [Create a backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118426) .

- Go to Settings.

- Tap [Your Name].

- Tap Find My.

- Tap Find My [device].

- Turn Find My [device] off.

- Enter your Apple Account password.

- Tap the done button on your keyboard.

## Restore your device to factory settings

- Connect your device to your computer.

- If you see the "Allow accessory to connect?" alert on your computer, click Allow.

- Open the Finder on Mac or the [Apple Devices app](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) on a PC. [Find out which macOS your Mac is using](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033) .

- [Select your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108392) when it appears on your computer.

- If a message asks for your device passcode or to Trust This Computer, follow the onscreen steps. [If you forgot your passcode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118430) , get help.

- Click Restore [device] on your computer*. [Ensure that you have turned off Find My, as described above](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118107#prepare) .

- Click Restore to confirm. Your computer erases your device and installs the latest iOS, iPadOS, or iPod software.

[Image: Confirmation dialog in the Finder asking, "Are you sure you want to restore the iPhone 'iPhone' to factory settings?", after clicking Restore iPhone.]

Once the restore process finishes, the Hello screen will appear on your device.

* Restoring an iPhone doesn't delete your eSIM. [If you need to erase your eSIM, learn how to erase it](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102421) .
