# Restore your iPhone, iPad, or iPod to factory settings using a computer

Erase your iPhone, iPad, or iPod and install the latest iOS, iPadOS, or iPod software using a computer. This factory restore removes all information and settings from your device.

If your device works, [erase and restore it without a computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108931) .

[Image: Play the video.]

## Prepare your device

- Update your Mac or PC. If you're using iTunes, [make sure that you have the latest version](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290) .

- [Create a backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118426) .

- Go to Settings.

- Tap [Your Name].

- Tap Find My.

- Tap Find My [device].

- Turn Find My [device] off.

- Enter your Apple Account password.

- Tap the done button on your keyboard.

## Restore your device to factory settings

- Connect your device to your computer.

- Click Allow if the " [Allow accessory to connect?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102282) " alert appears on your computer.

- Open the Finder on Mac or the [Apple Devices app on a PC](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) . If you're not sure which Finder features apply, [check which macOS you're using](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033) .

- [Select your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108392) when it appears in the sidebar of the Finder window or the sidebar of the Apple Devices app.

- Click Trust to allow the computer to access your device.

- Tap Trust on your device.

- Enter your device passcode.

- On your computer, click “Restore [device],” then follow the onscreen instructions. 1 2

- Review the terms and conditions and click Agree.

Once the restore process finishes, the Hello screen appears on your device.

- Restoring an iPhone doesn't delete your eSIM. [Learn how to erase your eSIM](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102421) .

- If asked to create a backup, click "Back Up" or "Don't Back Up," then click "Restore" to confirm.
