# How to force restart your iPod shuffle

If your iPod shuffle doesn't respond when you press the buttons, you can force restart your device.

## Force restart your iPod shuffle

- Unplug your iPod shuffle from its charger and from your computer.

- Use the power switch to turn off your iPod shuffle. If you see green by the switch, your device is on. ( [Can't find the power switch?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102062#switch) )

- Wait for ten seconds. Then turn on your iPod shuffle again.
