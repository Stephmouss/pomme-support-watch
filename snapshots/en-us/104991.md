# Using eSIM with your iPad in China mainland

Learn which iPad models support eSIM in China mainland and how to set up cellular service on your device.

## Supported iPad models

- iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A3362

- iPad Pro 13-inch (M4) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A3007

- iPad Pro 11-inch (M5) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A3359

- iPad Pro 11-inch (M4) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A3006

- iPad Air 13-inch (M4) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A3464

- iPad Air 13-inch (M3) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A3271

- iPad Air 13-inch (M2) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A2900

- iPad Air 11-inch (M4) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A3463

- iPad Air 11-inch (M3) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A3270

- iPad Air 11-inch (M2) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A2904

- iPad (A16) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A3356

- iPad (10th generation) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A3162

- iPad mini (A17 Pro) Wi-Fi + Cellular, Model Number A2996

All other iPad models, including those purchased outside of China mainland, are unable to install an eSIM profile from carriers in China mainland.

## How to identify your iPad model

Use the model number to find out which model iPad you have. You can find the model number in the following ways:

- Look on the back of your iPad retail packaging for tech specs related to your iPad model. Some packaging might have a QR code that you can scan to show tech specs.

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap General.

- Tap About. To the right of Model Number, you'll find the part number. To find the model number, tap the part number.

[Learn more about identifying your iPad model](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108043)

## About eSIM for iPad in China mainland

- China Unicom is the only supported eSIM carrier for iPad. [Contact China Unicom for more details](https://mall.10010.com/static/homepage/subjectpage/5980000010023.html) .

- To activate cellular data with eSIM for iPad in China mainland, you must have an iPad model that supports eSIM in China mainland.

## Setting up an eSIM with your iPad in China mainland

### What you need

- An iPad model that supports eSIM in China mainland

- A [China Unicom eSIM supported plan](http://mall.10010.com/static/homepage/subjectpage/5980000010023.html)

- A wireless internet connection or hotspot

### Learn how to set up an eSIM on your iPad in China mainland

To activate an eSIM on your iPad:

- Download your carrier’s app from the App Store.

- Follow the onscreen instructions.

To use your iPhone to activate an eSIM on your iPad:

- Download your carrier’s app on your iPhone from the App Store.

- Follow the onscreen instructions.

## Traveling with your iPad outside of China mainland

When traveling outside of China mainland, your iPad model that supports eSIM in China mainland is able to install eSIM cellular data plans from carriers outside of China. You must have location services enabled on your device to install non-China mainland eSIMs while traveling outside of China mainland.

To learn more about roaming with your China Unicom rate plan, [contact China Unicom](http://mall.10010.com/static/homepage/subjectpage/5980000010023.html) .
