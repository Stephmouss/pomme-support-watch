# If your Mac starts up to a circle with a line through it

A prohibitory symbol, which looks like a circle with a line or slash through it, means that your startup disk contains a Mac operating system, but it's not a version or build of macOS that your Mac can use.

[Image: Circle with line through it screen]

- [Start up from macOS Recovery](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102518) .

- While in Recovery, [use Disk Utility to repair your Mac startup disk](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102611) .

- If Disk Utility finds no errors or repairs all errors, [reinstall macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102655) from Recovery.
