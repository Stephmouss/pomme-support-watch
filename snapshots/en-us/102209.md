# Subscribe to Apple News+ on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac

With an Apple News+ subscription, you get access to magazines, newspapers, audio stories, premium sports coverage, recipes, puzzles, and more.

Apple News+ is available only in Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Learn [what's available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .

## How to subscribe in the News app

- Open the News app.

- Do one of the following:

- On your iPhone, tap News+ at the bottom of the screen.

- On your iPad, tap News+ in the sidebar or tab bar.

- On your Mac, click News+ in the sidebar. If you don’t see the sidebar, choose View > Show Sidebar.

- Tap or click the trial or subscription offer.

- If asked, sign in to the Apple Account that you use for App Store purchases. If you don't have an Apple Account, follow the prompts to create one.

- If asked, confirm your billing information. You might need to add a valid payment method.

- If asked, agree to the terms and conditions.

If you want to share your Apple News+ subscription with your family, [you can set up Family Sharing in Settings on your iPhone or iPad or in System Settings on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108107) .

Feature availability within the News app and News+ subscription varies by country and region.
