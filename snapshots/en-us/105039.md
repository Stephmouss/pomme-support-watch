# If you forget your new passcode, use your old passcode to unlock your iPhone or iPad within 72 hours

Passcode Reset allows you to temporarily use your old password to regain access to your iPhone or iPad—without the need to use a computer or to reset your device.

## About Passcode Reset

When you change your passcode in iOS 17, iPadOS 17, and later, Passcode Reset gives you 72 hours during which you can still access your iPhone or iPad using your previous passcode. If you unlock your device this way, you’ll be prompted to change your passcode again immediately.

If you forget your passcode, try Passcode Reset first. If you can't use Passcode Reset, you can then [reset your iPhone or iPad without a computer](https://support.apple.com/105090) to regain access to your Apple device. Finally, if you can't use Passcode Reset and can't reset your iPhone or iPad on your device, you can [reset your iPhone or iPad with a computer](https://support.apple.com/118430) .

## How to use Passcode Reset

- On your Lock Screen after five incorrect passcode attempts, the "[Device] Unavailable" screen appears. When prompted, tap Forgot Passcode? in the bottom corner of the screen.

[Image: The "iPhone Unavailable" screen with the option to tap "Forgot Passcode?" to start the reset process.]

- Tap Enter Previous Passcode.

[Image: iPhone screen displaying "Forgot Passcode?" with a "Try Passcode Reset" button, allowing temporary use of the old passcode after changing it in iOS 17 or later.]

- Enter your old passcode, then follow the onscreen instructions to set a new passcode.

If you use iOS 17, iPadOS 17, or later, the Forgot Passcode? option appears when you are locked out, connected to a cellular or Wi-Fi network, signed into your Apple Account, and have Find My turned on.

If you use iOS 15.2, iPadOS 15.2, iOS 16, or iPadOS 16, an Erase [Device] option appears when you are locked out, connected to a cellular or Wi-Fi network, signed into your Apple Account, and have Find My turned on.

[Expire an old passcode sooner than 72 hours](https://support.apple.com/127950)
