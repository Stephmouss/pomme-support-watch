# If you can't activate your iPhone or iPad (Wi-Fi + Cellular)

If your iPhone or iPad (Wi-Fi + Cellular) can't be activated or an alert says the activation server is unavailable or the SIM card or eSIM is unsupported, learn what to do.

## Before you begin

- Learn what to do [if you get an error message that says "No SIM" or "Invalid SIM"](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108914) .

- [Check the list on the System Status page](https://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/) . If the box by iOS Device Activation isn't green, try to activate your iPhone or iPad later.

- If asked to enter your password as you activate your iPhone or iPad, enter the password. ( [A password prompt means that Activation Lock might be enabled](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108794) in Find My iPhone.)

## Follow these steps

- [Restart your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118259) or [iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101603) .

- If you're using a cellular-data connection, [connect to a reliable Wi-Fi network](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111107) instead.

- If you get an error message on your iPhone or iPad that says the activation server is temporarily unavailable or cannot be reached when trying to activate, wait a few minutes, then repeat the previous steps.

If you still can't activate your iPhone or iPad, try again using your computer instead:

- Make sure that you have the [latest version of macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) or [the latest version of iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290) .

- Check that your computer has an internet connection.

- With the cable that came with your iPhone or iPad, connect your iPhone or iPad to your computer. [Locate your device on your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108392) .

- Wait while your computer detects and activates your iPhone or iPad:

- If an alert appears that says "Set up as New" or "Restore from Backup," it means your iPhone or iPad is activated.

- If you get an error message on your computer that says that the SIM card or eSIM isn't compatible or is invalid, [contact your carrier](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109526) .

- If you get an error message that says the "activation information was invalid" or "activation information could not be obtained from the device," [use recovery mode to restore your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118106) .
