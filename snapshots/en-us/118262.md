# Charge your AirPods Max

Learn how to charge your AirPods Max and how to check the charge status.

## How to charge your AirPods Max

To charge your AirPods Max, plug the USB-C or the Lightning cable that came with your AirPods Max into the bottom-right earphone, then plug the other end of the cable into a USB charger or port.

You can use these types of cables to charge your AirPods Max, depending on which model you have:

- For AirPods Max equipped with a USB-C port, you can use a USB-C to USB-C cable or a USB-C to USB cable.

- For AirPods Max equipped with a Lightning port, you can use a USB-C to Lightning Cable or a USB to Lightning Cable.

[Image: AirPods Max charging while stored inside of Smart Case.]

To charge your AirPods Max inside the Smart Case, align the cutout on the case with the Lightning port or USB-C port on the headphones. When you're not using your AirPods Max, keep them in the Smart Case to put them into an ultralow power mode to preserve battery charge.

## Check the charge status

- When the battery charge of your AirPods Max is low, you get a notification on the screen of your iPhone or iPad. You get notifications when the charge is at 20, 10, and 5 percent remaining.

- A tone chimes when the battery charge is low. You hear the tone one time when the battery charge is at 10 percent, and a second time right before AirPods Max turn off.

- The status light on the right earphone shows the charge status of your AirPods Max.

- To check the charge status when your AirPods Max are connected to power, press the listening mode button. The status light on your AirPods Max 1 turns green if the charge has more than or equal to 95 percent remaining, or amber if the charge has less than 95 percent remaining. On your AirPods Max 2, the status light turns green if the charge has more than or equal to 95 percent remaining, and briefly pulses amber if the charge has less than 95 percent remaining.

- To check the charge status even when your AirPods Max aren't connected to power, press the listening mode button. The status light on your AirPods Max 1 turns green if the charge has more than or equal to 15 percent remaining, or amber if the charge has less than 15 percent remaining. On your AirPods Max 2, the status light turns green if the charge has more than or equal to 15 percent remaining, and briefly flashes amber if the charge has less than 15 percent remaining.

You can also check the charge status of your AirPods Max with your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

### On your iPhone or iPad

[Image: AirPods Max battery level in the Settings app on iPhone.]

- Take your AirPods Max out of the Smart Case.

- Hold your AirPods Max close to your iPhone or iPad.

- Go to Bluetooth Settings, then tap the Info button next to your AirPods Max.

### On your Mac

[Image: Control Center in macOS showing AirPods Max battery level.]

- Take your AirPods Max out of the Smart Case.

- Hold your AirPods Max close to your Mac.

- Click Control Center in the menu bar.

- Click Sound, then choose your AirPods from the list.

## Enable charging notifications

You can use your iPhone or iPad to turn on reminders to charge your AirPods Max 2 when the battery is low, and to notify you when charging is complete. Your iPhone or iPad must be on iOS or iPadOS 26 or later.

- [Connect your AirPods Max 2](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108364) to your iPhone or iPad.

- On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings. Choose your AirPods, then tap Battery.

- Turn on Charging Notifications.
