# Transfer your library and playlists from music services to Apple Music

Learn how to transfer your saved music and playlists that you've added in other music services to your Apple Music library.

[What you need](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118249#whatyouneed)

[Transfer to Apple Music on your iPhone, iPad, or Android](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118249#ios)

[Transfer to Apple Music on the web](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118249#web)

[What to do if some of your music wasn't transferred](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118249#musicnottransferred)

## What you need

- A subscription to Apple Music

- An iPhone or iPad updated to the latest version of iOS or iPadOS, Android device with the latest version of the Apple Music app, or a device that can access [music.apple.com](https://music.apple.com)

- Your username and password to the other music service

- [Turn on Sync Library on all of your devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285)

## Transfer music and playlists from other music services to Apple Music

You can start your transfer in the Apple Music app on your iPhone, iPad, Android device, or on the web. You can transfer your songs, albums, and playlists from other music services to Apple Music. Songs that you have liked or loved in other services will transfer over to your Favorite Songs playlist. What you can transfer varies and is determined by the service. You can still access all of your saved content in the other service. Transferring music to Apple Music is provided by a third-party.

### Transfer to Apple Music on your iPhone, iPad, or Android

- Go to Music Settings:

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the Apple Music app, then tap the My Account button , or your photo or monogram.

- On an Android device, open the Apple Music app, tap the More button , then tap Settings.

[Image: tap the More button]

- Tap Transfer Music from Other Music Services.

- Choose from the list of supported music services. Then sign in to the music service.

- Choose what you want to transfer.

- Tap Add to Library.

- Wait for Apple Music to find matches for your music in the Apple Music catalog. Once this process is complete, you'll see one of the following messages:

- Transfer Complete: Apple Music found matches for the music in the Apple Music catalog and added the music to your library.

- [Some Music Needs Review:](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118249#review) Apple Music didn't find an exact match but found similar alternate versions for the music. Tap Review Now, choose an alternate version, then tap Save to add the music to your library.

### Transfer to Apple Music on the web

- On your web browser, go to [music.apple.com](https://music.apple.com) .

- Click Sign In and sign in with the Apple Account you use for Apple Music.

- Click your photo or monogram in the top-right corner, then select Transfer Music.

- Choose what you want to transfer.

- Tap Add to Library.

- Wait for Apple Music to find matches for your music in the Apple Music catalog. Once this process is complete, you'll see one of the following messages in the bottom of the sidebar:

- Transfer Complete: Apple Music found matches for the music in the Apple Music catalog and added the music to your library.

- [Some Music Needs Review:](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118249#review) Apple Music didn't find an exact match but found similar alternate versions for the music. Tap Review Now, choose an alternate version, then tap Save to add the music to your library.

## If some of your music wasn't transferred to Apple Music

Some content might not be available or have an exact match in the Apple Music catalog. If you have [explicit content turned off](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105121) on your Apple device, explicit tracks will be grayed out.

### If you see Some Music Needs Review or Review Alternates

You have 30 days from the initial transfer to review unmatched music and choose from alternate versions to add to your music library. You can't start a new transfer on that device if you have music that needs review.

To review alternate versions, make sure you're on the the device that initiated the transfer:

- On an iPhone or iPad, go to Settings > Apps > Music, then tap Review Current Transfer.

- On an Android device, open the Apple Music app, tap the More button , tap Settings, then tap Review Current Transfer.

[Image: tap the More button]

- On the web, click your photo or monogram in the top-right corner, then select Review.

### If you don't see some of your playlists

You can transfer only playlists that you created. Playlists created by the music service won't transfer.

### If you see Something Went Wrong

Make sure that your device is connected to the internet, then tap Try Again.

Transferring your library and playlists to Apple Music is not available in China Mainland, Myanmar, and Russia. Apple Music availability might vary by country or region. [Learn what's available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
