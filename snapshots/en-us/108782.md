# Set up and use iCloud Photos

iCloud Photos works with the Photos app to keep your photos and videos securely stored in iCloud, and up to date across your devices.

## How iCloud Photos works

iCloud Photos keeps every photo and video you take in iCloud, so you can access your library from any device, anytime — just open the Photos app. Changes you make on one device change on your other devices too.

Photos and videos are stored in iCloud in their original formats at full resolution (HEIF, JPEG, RAW, PNG, GIF, TIFF, HEVC, and MP4), as well as special formats you capture with your iPhone or iPad, like slo-mo, time-lapse, 4K videos, and Live Photos. Depending on your internet speed, the time it takes to see your photos and videos across your devices and [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) can vary.

## Turn on iCloud Photos

Make sure that you set up iCloud and sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on all of your devices.

### Turn on iCloud Photos on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Go to Settings. Tap your name, then select iCloud.

- Tap Photos.

- Turn on Sync this device.

### Turn on iCloud Photos on your Mac

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings or Preferences.

- Click your name, then click iCloud.

- Under Saved to iCloud, click Photos. In macOS Sonoma or earlier, under Apps Using iCloud, click Photos.

- Turn on Sync this Mac.

### Turn on iCloud Photos on your Apple TV 4K and Apple TV HD

- Go to Settings, then select Users and Accounts.

- Select iCloud.

- Turn on iCloud Photos.

### Turn on iCloud Photos on your Windows PC

[Learn how to set up and use iCloud Photos on your PC](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108994)

## What happens when you edit or delete photos

When you edit or delete photos or videos, changes reflect across all of your devices.

### Edits show up on all of your devices

When you [make edits in the Photos app on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphb08064d57) or [on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/photos/pht304c2ace6/) , the photo automatically updates across your devices. So when you crop or enhance a photo on your iPhone, you see the changes on your Mac. iCloud stores your original photos and videos, so you can revert any changes you made, any time.

### Photos delete from all of your devices

When you [delete photos and videos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104967) on one device, they're deleted everywhere that you use iCloud Photos. If you delete something accidentally, you can recover it in the Recently Deleted folder for 30 days. After that, it's deleted permanently and you can't get it back.

## Download copies of your photos and videos

When you turn on iCloud Photos, your photos and videos automatically upload to iCloud. They're not duplicated in your iCloud backup, so keep [backup copies of your library](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111762) .

## Manage your on-device storage with Optimize Storage

Photos and videos that you keep in iCloud Photos use [your iCloud storage and your device storage](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102670) . To save space on your device, you can turn on Optimize Storage.

When you turn on Optimize Storage, your device keeps space-saving versions of your photos and videos, while iCloud Photos stores your photos and videos in their original, high-resolution version. When you need more device storage, iCloud Photos optimizes your library, starting with photos and videos you access least. You can download originals over Wi-Fi or cellular.

### Turn on Optimize Storage on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Go to Settings. Tap your name, then select iCloud.

- Under Saved to iCloud, tap Photos.

- Tap iPhone Storage, then choose Optimize Storage. In iOS 26 or earlier, just choose Optimize Storage.

### Turn on Optimize Storage on your Mac

- In the Photos app, from the Photos menu in the menu bar, choose Settings (Preferences).

- Click the iCloud tab, then choose a storage setting.

To keep your original, full-size photos and videos in iCloud and on your Mac, select Download Originals to this Mac.

## Pause library uploads to iCloud

When you turn on iCloud Photos, the time it takes for photos and videos to upload to iCloud depends on the size of your collection and your internet speed. Larger content collections might take more time. You can [check the status](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101559) and pause the upload for one day. Depending on your library status, pause might not be available.

### On iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- In iOS 26 or later, open the Photos app, then tap Collections. Tap the profile button, then tap Pause.

- In iOS 18, open the Photos app, tap the profile button and tap Pause.

- In iOS 17 or earlier, open the Photos app. Tap Library. Select All Photos, then scroll down and tap Pause.

### On Mac

- In the Photos app, in the sidebar, select Library.

- Click All Photos.

- Scroll down and click Pause.

## Prioritize library uploads to iCloud

To prioritize syncing your iCloud Photos library, in version 27 or later of iOS, iPadOS, and visionOS, you can turn on Sync Immediately. If you allow Photos to [use cellular data](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109323) , this could lead to higher data usage.

- Go to Settings. Tap your name, then tap iCloud.

- Under Saved to iCloud, tap Photos.

- Tap Sync Immediately.

- Choose For Today.
