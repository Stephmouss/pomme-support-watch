# Block and unblock channels and topics in Apple News

You can block specific channels or topics you don't want to read.

## Block or unblock a channel or topic in the Apple News app on your iPhone

To block a channel or topic:

- Tap the Search tab .

- Search for the channel or topic you want to block, then tap it.

- Tap the More Actions button .

- Choose Block Channel or Block Topic.

To unblock a channel or topic:

- Tap the Following tab .

- Scroll down to Manage and tap Blocked.

- Tap the Remove button next to the channel or topic you want to unblock, then tap Unblock.

## Block or unblock a channel or topic in the Apple News app on your iPad

To block a channel or topic:

- Tap the Search tab in the tab bar.

- Search for the channel or topic you want to block, then tap it.

- Tap the More Actions button .

- Choose Block Channel or Block Topic.

To unblock a channel or topic:

- If the sidebar is closed, tap the Sidebar button to open it.

- Scroll down to Manage and tap Blocked.

- Tap the Remove button next to the channel or topic you want to unblock, then tap Unblock.

## Block or unblock a channel or topic in the Apple News app on your Mac

To block a channel or topic:

- Click Search in the sidebar. If the sidebar isn't shown, choose View > Show Sidebar in the menu bar.

- Search for the channel or topic you want to block, then click it.

- Click the More Actions button .

- Choose Block Channel or Block Topic, then click Block.

To unblock a channel or topic:

- Choose File in the menu bar.

- Choose Manage Blocked Channels & Topics.

- Click the Remove button next to the channel or topic you want to unblock, then click Unblock.

## If something you've blocked appears in Top Stories

Top Stories appear the same for everyone. If a story from a channel you blocked is included in Top Stories, you see the headline, but the channel is still blocked in the rest of your feed.
