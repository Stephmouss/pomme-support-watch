# Installing apps through alternative app distribution

If alternative app distribution is available in your region, you can install apps from sources other than the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.

Alternative app distribution is available in Brazil, Japan, and the countries or regions of the European Union. The [features of alternative app distribution might vary](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118110#availability) .

## About installing alternative app marketplaces and apps offered through alternative app distribution

Only users based in Brazil, Japan, or the European Union are able to install apps through alternative app distribution. The country or region of your Apple Account must be set to one of those countries or regions, and you must physically be located there. How alternative app distribution works can vary by country or region.

All apps available through alternative app distribution are submitted to a Notarization process with Apple. Notarization is a baseline review that applies to all apps, regardless of their distribution channel, focused on platform policies for security and privacy and to maintain device integrity. Through a combination of automated checks and human review, Notarization helps ensure apps are free of known malware, viruses, or other security threats, function as promised, and don’t expose users to egregious fraud. Apple does not enforce the App Store’s high standards for business practices and content on apps distributed through alternative app distribution.

## How to install alternative app marketplaces

### In Japan or Brazil

- Use a web browser on a device with the latest version of iOS to find an alternative app marketplace that you want to install.

- Tap the link to install.

- In the message that appears, tap Settings. Then tap Manage App Installation.

- Tap Allow next to the app marketplace.

- Read the information about what installing an alternative app marketplace means for your iPhone, then tap Allow.

- Follow the onscreen steps to finish installing the app marketplace.

### In the European Union

- Use a web browser on a device with the latest version of iOS or iPadOS to find an alternative app marketplace that you want to install.

- Tap the link to install.

- Read the information about what installing an alternative app marketplace means for your iPhone or iPad, then tap Allow.

- Follow the onscreen steps to finish installing the app marketplace.

## How to install apps from a developer’s website in the European Union

- Use a web browser on a device with the latest version of iOS or iPadOS to find an app you want to install from web distribution.

- Tap the link to install.

- If this is your first time installing an app from this developer’s website, you’ll have to allow app installations from the web for that developer. Tap Settings, then tap Continue on the message in the Settings app. Read the information about what installing from the web for this developer means, then tap Allow.

- Read the information about what installing an app from a developer website means for your iPhone or iPad, then tap Install App.

- Follow the onscreen steps to finish installing the app.

## If you need to ask a parent or guardian for an app exception

A child can request an exception for an alternative app marketplace or alternatively distributed app that exceeds the app age restrictions set for them. When installing an app, they might see a message to ask a parent or guardian for permission. To approve an exception:

- In the notification or message with the child’s request for an exception, tap Approve or Deny.

- On the child’s device, tap “Approve in Person”, then enter the Screen Time passcode.

With app exceptions, you can view the apps you granted permission for and remove access at any time using Screen Time. Use your iPhone or iPad to open Settings, tap Family, and then tap [your child]. Go to Screen Time and choose Content & Privacy Restrictions. Tap App Store, Media, Web, & Games, and then tap Apps.

## Purchases from apps installed through alternative app distribution

Any purchases you make outside of the App Store or Apple In-App Purchase aren’t supported by Apple. You can [check where you downloaded an app from in Settings and get support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118111) .

For help with purchases of apps, purchases within apps, and subscriptions, contact the alternative app marketplace developer or the app developer.

## About updates to apps from alternative app distribution

If you have Automatic Downloads turned on for App Updates on your iPhone or iPad, your device will automatically check for the latest updates and install them. You can check if you have this turned on or off in Settings. Go to Apps, then choose App Installation.

If you have Automatic Downloads turned off, you can check for updates for each app or marketplace by going to Settings, tapping Apps, and then tapping the name of the app.

If you delete an alternative app marketplace or it ceases operations, you will no longer receive updates for the apps you installed from the alternative app marketplace.

## If you want to continue getting apps that are approved under Apple’s App Review Guidelines, use the App Store

If you prefer using apps that have met all of Apple’s App Review Guidelines, including Apple’s standards for privacy, security, and quality, you can use the App Store. Your iPhone or iPad won’t install alternative app marketplaces or apps from outside the App Store without your permission. If you want to safeguard your device or a family member’s device from being able to install alternative app marketplaces or apps through web distribution, you can [change the parental control settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118128) .

You can also contact Apple Support for help when you download apps from the App Store. This includes support for purchases of apps, Apple In-App Purchase, sharing your purchases with your Family Sharing group, and more.
