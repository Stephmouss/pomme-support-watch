# If Dual SIM with eSIM isn't working on your iPhone

Learn how to fix Dual SIM issues on iPhone XS, iPhone XS Max, iPhone XR, or later. These models use a physical nano-SIM and an eSIM, or two eSIMs.

## Check your plans

- [Your iPhone must be unlocked](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109316) , or both cellular plans must be from the same carrier.

- Both plans must be set up and ready to use.

- Your carrier or carriers must support Dual SIM with eSIM.

- If a CDMA carrier provides your first SIM, your second SIM won't support CDMA. Contact your carrier for more information.

In China mainland, you can activate iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and iPhone 17e using either an eSIM or a physical nano-SIM, while iPhone Air activates by eSIM only.

In Hong Kong and Macao, the following support eSIM: iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, iPhone 17e, iPhone 17, iPhone Air, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone 16e, iPhone SE (2nd or 3rd generation), iPhone 13 mini, iPhone 12 mini, and iPhone XS. Learn about [fixing issues with Dual SIM when using two nano-SIM cards](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109318) in China mainland, Hong Kong, and Macao.

## If you can't set up your eSIM

- [Check with your carrier](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101569) to see if your iPhone plan is eligible for Dual SIM service.

- If you have an enterprise or corporate cellular service plan, check with your company or carrier to see if they support this feature.

- Make sure that your carriers support coverage in your area and that there are no service outages.

- Make sure that you set up your cellular plans so they're ready to use and don’t have a billing-related blockage.

If you still can't set up your eSIM, contact your carriers for support.

## View your cellular plans

To view your cellular plans, open the Settings app and tap Cellular or tap Mobile Data.

### If you don't see your eSIM plan

- Follow the steps to [set up your cellular plan with eSIM](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317) .

- [Open Control Center](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108330) and make sure that both of your carriers appear in the status bar.

[Image: iPhone Control Center displaying dual sims with Carrier 1 on LTE and Carrier 2 on Wifi.]

### If you don't see your nano-SIM card plan

Ensure your nano-SIM card is correctly placed within the SIM tray:

- [Remove the SIM tray](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109357) and inspect the SIM cards for signs of damage or corrosion.

- Remove your SIM card from the SIM tray, then put the SIM card back.

- Make sure that you close the SIM tray completely, so that it isn’t loose.

- [Open Control Center](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108330) and make sure that both of your carriers appear in the status bar.

If the nano-SIM card doesn't work, doesn't fit, or you bent it, contact your carrier for a new one. Also contact them if you see "Card is not compatible with LTE" on your iPhone.

## If you're on a call and see No Service on your other line

Your iPhone can use one cellular data network at a time. When you're using one line to make a call, your other line shows No Service. If your carrier supports Wi-Fi Calling on your other phone number and you have Wi-Fi Calling turned on, you can answer incoming calls on that number. When you're on a call that isn't your default data line, you need to turn on Allow Cellular Data Switching.

## Still need help?

If you still can't set up or use the Dual SIM features of your iPhone, [contact your carrier](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101569) .
