# Move from your current iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch to a lower capacity device

Learn how to optimize your content and reduce your usage so that you can move to a device with less storage.

## Check your storage

Before you move to a new device, [check how much storage your current device uses](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429) . If all your data fits on the new device, you can [transfer your data to the new device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119967) without removing anything.

If you're moving to a lower-capacity device, you might need to optimize or remove some of your existing content. Find out what takes up the most space on your device:

- Go to Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap [Device] Storage.

[Learn more about data usage on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429) .

## Manage your storage

There are a few ways to manage or reduce the storage on your device.

### Audio, movies, series, and books

Learn how to [delete music](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102344) , [movies, TV shows](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118410) , and [books](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/organize-books-iphab219b91/ios) from your device.

### Photos and videos

To reduce the space that your photos and videos use, you can [import photos and videos from your camera roll to your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120267) . To delete photos from your camera roll after you import them:

- Tap Select.

- Select the photos that you want to delete.

- Tap the Trash button .

If you want to view the photos on your device after removing them from your camera roll, [sync them from your photo library](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108311) . When you sync your photo library to your computer, it optimizes synced photos, saving space. You can also sync a smaller selection of your photo library to save space.

### Apps

To reduce the storage space that your apps use:

- If the app stores data on the device, see if it can store its data in the cloud. Check the app's documentation or contact the app developer for more information.

- [Delete some of the app's content](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429) .

- [Delete apps you don't use anymore](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101550) .
