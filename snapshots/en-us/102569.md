# If your Siri Remote or Apple TV Remote isn't working

If your remote isn't working as expected, first try pairing your remote to your Apple TV. If your remote still isn't working as expected, trying restarting your remote, or check your remote signal strength.

## Pair your remote

- Point your remote at your Apple TV. Make sure that the remote is about three inches from your Apple TV.

- Press and hold the Back button (or the Menu button) and the Volume Up button for five seconds.

[Image: Apple TV remotes with Back (or Menu) and Volume Up buttons highlighted in blue.]

- If asked, place your remote on top of Apple TV to complete pairing.

- If your remote doesn't pair, unplug your Apple TV, wait six seconds, and plug your Apple TV back into power. Then try pairing your remote again.

## Restart your remote

If you can't pair your remote, try restarting it.

- Press and hold the TV/Control Center button and the Volume Down button at the same time. Hold the buttons down for about 5 seconds, or until the status light on your Apple TV turns off and on again.

[Image: Apple TV remotes with TV/Control Center button and Volume Down button highlighted in blue.]

- Release the buttons, then wait 5 to 10 seconds.

- Press any button on your remote. If a Remote Connected notification appears, you can use your remote. If a Remote Connected notification doesn't appear, try the steps to pair your remote again.

## Check your remote signal strength

If your remote pairs to your Apple TV but your remote is still not working as expected, check your remote signal strength.

- On your Apple TV using tvOS 18.0 or later, go to Settings and tap Remotes and Devices, then select Remote.

- Check the remote signal strength indicator (Bluetooth RSSI) on the screen.

[Image: Bluetooth RSSI in Apple TV Settings shows Bluetooth signal strength.]

- If the indicator says your signal is weak, or if you don't have an indicator, move to within 20 feet (about 6 meters) of your Apple TV. Move anything that blocks the path between your remote and the front of your Apple TV, television, receiver, and sound bar.

## Get more help

- [Charge your remote for 30 minutes](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/charge-the-siri-remote-atvb7d4de50d/tvos) using a USB-C to USB or Lightning to USB cable and a USB wall charger.

- [Update your Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108414) to the latest version of tvOS. You can [use the Apple TV Remote on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/atvb701cadc1) to navigate your Apple TV and check for updates.

- If the volume buttons on your Apple TV remote stop working, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108769) .

- If your Apple Remote (aluminum or white) isn't working, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120036) .
