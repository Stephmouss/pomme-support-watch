# Split the bill with Apple Cash

Learn how to request or send Apple Cash using a receipt.

## What you need

- [Apple Cash 1 set up on your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109304)

- An iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch the latest version of [iOS, iPadOS,](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) or [watchOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108926)

- To scan a receipt, you need an iPhone or iPad with [Apple Intelligence](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115) and Siri enabled on your device. 2

## Request payment from a friend or group

- Open Messages, Wallet, Camera, Photos, or take a screenshot:

- On iPhone or iPad, open a conversation in Messages, tap the + button, tap Apple Cash, then tap Split Bill. Position a receipt in view of the camera, then tap the Take Photo button , or tap the image chooser and select a receipt.

[Image: Conversation in Messages with Apple Cash start screen showing button for Split Bill]

- On iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap your Apple Cash card, then tap Send or Request. Select a person or add people, then tap the Split Bill button. Position a receipt in view of the camera, then tap the Take Picture button , or tap the image chooser and select a receipt.

- On iPhone, open the Camera app and tap Siri at the bottom or press and hold Camera Control. Position a receipt in view of the camera, then tap Split Bill with Apple Cash when it appears.

- On iPhone, open the Photos app, then open a photo of a receipt. Swipe up on the photo, then tap Split Bill with Apple Cash when it appears.

- On iPhone, [take a screenshot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102616) with a receipt onscreen, then tap Split Bill with Apple Cash when it appears.

- Tap Request in the menu that appears.

- Select a conversation or add people if you haven't already.

- Review the request and make adjustments if necessary:

- Swipe left on an item and tap the Edit button or Delete button.

- Tap the item quantity and tap the - or + buttons.

[Image: Receipt scanned showing transcribed items and amounts]

- If a tip is needed, tap Add Tip and select an option.

- Choose how you want to split the bill:

- To split the bill by item, tap Request from [Name or Number] or Share with Group.

- To split the bill evenly, select Split Evenly, use the - and + buttons to choose how many people to split the bill with, then tap Request [amount] from [Name or Number] or Request [amount] per Person.

- Tap the Send button.

## Pay your share of the bill from a request

If another person paid the bill, they can request payment from you in a message.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Messages app on your iPhone or iPad, open the conversation that contains the request, then tap Pay [name].

- If the bill is split by item, select the items that you want to pay for. You can tap the - button next to an item to split the cost in half or more for shared items, or tap the + button to increase the quantity.

[Image: Pay request showing selected items, tip, and button to pay.]

- If the bill is split evenly, the requested amount appears. If you want to change the amount, use the - and + buttons.

- Tap Pay [Name] [Amount], then tap the Send button.

- To pay, double-click the side button, then confirm your payment with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

### On your Apple Watch

You can pay from your Apple Watch whenever you split a bill evenly. For all other requests, you can select your items and complete the request on your iPhone or iPad.

- Open the Messages app.

- Open the conversation that contains the Apple Cash request.

- Tap Pay [Name] [Amount], then tap the Send button.

- To pay, double-click the side button to confirm your payment.

## Pay your share of the bill from a receipt

If you want to pay your share of a bill without waiting for someone to request it, you can scan a physical receipt using the camera on your device.

- Open Messages, Wallet, Camera, Photos, or take a screenshot:

- On iPhone or iPad, open the Messages app, tap the conversation with the person you want to pay, tap the + button, tap Apple Cash, then tap Split Bill. Position a receipt in view of the camera, then tap the Take Picture button.

- On iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap your Apple Cash card, then tap Send or Request. Select a person, then tap the Split Bill button. Position a receipt in view of the camera, then tap the Take Picture button , or tap the image chooser and select a receipt.

- On iPhone, open the Camera app and tap Siri at the bottom or press and hold Camera Control. Position a receipt in view of the camera, then tap Split Bill with Apple Cash when it appears.

- On iPhone, open the Photos app, then open a photo of a receipt. Swipe up on the photo, then tap Split Bill with Apple Cash when it appears.

- On iPhone, [take a screenshot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102616) with a receipt onscreen, then tap Split Bill with Apple Cash when it appears.

- Tap Pay Your Share, then select a conversation or add people if you haven’t already.

- Select the items that you want to pay for. You can tap the - button next to an item to split the cost in half or more for shared items, or tap the + button to increase the quantity.

- If you want to add a tip, tap Add Tip and select an option.

- Tap Pay [Name] [Amount], then tap the Send button. If you're in a group message, tap Pay [Amount], tap the recipient you want to pay, then tap the Send button.

- To pay, double-click the side button, then confirm your payment with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

## View your open requests

Your requests stay open for up to 30 days, until fully paid, or marked complete.

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap your Apple Cash card.

- In the Requests section, tap a conversation to see:

- Who made payments

- How much Apple Cash you’ve received

- If you no longer want to track the request, tap Mark as Complete.

You can also see payments you've received in the Latest Transactions section. A group icon appears below the amount if the request is from a group conversation. To view past requests on iPad, go to Settings, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, tap Apple Cash, then tap Transactions.
