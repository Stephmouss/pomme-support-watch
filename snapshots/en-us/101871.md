# If you can't add images from your iCloud Photo Library to a Clips video

If you can't add photos or videos from your iCloud Photo Library to a video in the Clips app, or thumbnails in Library tab appear grey, update your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch to iOS 11.

[Learn how to update](https://support.apple.com/KB/HT204204) your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch to the latest version of iOS—wirelessly or using iTunes.
