# If your payment method is declined in the App Store or iTunes Store

Add a different payment method, then remove the old one and try your purchase again. Make sure your billing details are entered correctly.

## Before you start

Check that your name, billing address, and other information are spelled correctly. Your billing details must match the information on file with your financial institution.

## Retry your purchase with a new payment method

Add a new payment method on your device. Then remove the old payment method and try your purchase again.

### On iPhone or iPad

- In Settings, tap your name.

- Tap Payment & Shipping. If you're asked, sign in to your Apple Account.

- Tap Add Payment Method.

- Enter the new payment method, then tap Done.

- Tap Edit, then tap the red delete button next to the old payment method and tap Remove.

- Try your purchase again.

[If you still can't make a purchase](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108095#troubleshooting)

### On Mac

- In the App Store, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Or, click the Sign In button and sign in to your Apple Account.

- On the page that appears, click your name. If you're asked, sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Payment Methods.

- Click Add Payment Method, then enter the new payment method. Click Done.

- Click Edit, then click Remove Payment Method to remove the old payment method.

- Try your purchase again.

## If you still can't make a purchase

If you still can't make a purchase after verifying your billing details and updating your payment method:

### Pay any unpaid orders

If you have an unpaid order, you might not be able to make purchases or download free apps until your balance is paid. [Pay an unpaid order](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118284) .

### Check your Apple Account location

Make sure that you're using a [payment method you can use with your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111741) in your country or region. If you're using an accepted payment method but your Apple Account is set to a different country or region, [change your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118283) .

In some countries and regions, your payment method might be declined because of changes to local law. Learn [about billing and purchases in Australia, Brazil, Europe, and Japan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108103) .

### Contact your financial institution

You might need to ask your financial institution to allow international purchases on your debit or credit card.

Some payment methods require verification through your financial institution's app, text message, or other means. If you can't verify, contact your financial institution for help.

If you want to know why a debit card, credit card, or other payment method was declined, speak with your financial institution. Apple doesn't have information about why the payment method was declined.

## If you see a message that says "Purchase Not Completed"

If Apple detects suspicious purchase activity, you might need to submit a review request for your purchase. You can [submit a request for Apple Support to review your purchase](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .

To request a review of your purchase:

- Go back to the item that you tried to buy.

- Try to buy the item again.

- When you see the "Purchase Not Completed" message, select Continue.

- Follow the onscreen instructions to submit a purchase review.

Apple Support can't submit a review on your behalf or expedite the review process.
