# Mac Pro (2023) status indicator light behavior

Learn about the different status indicator light patterns you might see on your Mac Pro.

## Turned off

[Image: Mac Pro (2023) front and side views, highlighting the location of the status indicator lights.]

When Mac Pro is turned off, the status indicator light does not illuminate.

## Turned on or in sleep mode

[Image: Front and side view of the Mac Pro (2023), with an arrow indicating the location of the status indicator light.]

When Mac Pro is on or sleeping, the status indicator light is solid white and remains on until the computer turns off.

## Housing unlocked or top cover removed while computer is on

[Image: Front and side view of the Mac Pro (2023) tower, showing the location of the status indicator light.]

If the computer is on and you unlock the housing on tower enclosure models, or open the top cover or access door on rack enclosure models, the status indicator light flashes amber steadily until the housing is locked or the top cover or access door is reinstalled.

## Firmware recovery mode

[Image: Mac Pro (2023) tower model, front view showing power button and status indicator light, and top view showing handle and ventilation holes.]

If Mac Pro is in firmware recovery mode, the status indicator light displays an amber pattern of three rapid flashes, three longer flashes, then three rapid flashes. You might need to [revive the firmware on your Mac Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT213662) . If you revive the firmware, the status indicator light is solid white once the revive begins. If you still need help, [contact an Apple Authorized Service Provider](https://locate.apple.com/) .

## Locate the status indicator lights

Mac Pro (2023) and Mac Pro (Rack, 2023) have a status indicator light next to the power button and on the back near the power port.
