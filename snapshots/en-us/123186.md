# Find your version of the Hearing Aid feature for your AirPods

Due to regulations in your country or region, version 1 or version 2 of the Hearing Aid feature might not be available for your AirPods Pro 2 or your AirPods Pro 3. You can verify which version you have on your iPhone or your Mac.

## On iPhone

- Go to Settings > your AirPods Pro.

- To view the feature version, go to Hearing Assistance > About Hearing Aid.

## On Mac

- Choose Apple menu > System Settings > your AirPods Pro.

- To view the feature version, go to Hearing Health > Hearing Aid, and click About Hearing Aid.
