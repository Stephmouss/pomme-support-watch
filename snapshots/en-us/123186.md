# Find your version of the Hearing Aid feature for your AirPods

Due to regulations in your country or region, version 1 or version 2 of the Hearing Aid feature might not be available for your AirPods Pro 2 or your AirPods Pro 3. You can use your iPhone or your Mac to verify which version you have.

## On iPhone

- Go to the Settings app, then tap the name of your AirPods Pro.

- Tap Hearing Health, then tap Hearing Assistance. The feature version is displayed under About Hearing Aid.

## On Mac

- Choose Apple menu , click System Settings, then click the name of your AirPods Pro.

- Click Hearing Health, then click Hearing Assistance. The feature version is displayed under About Hearing Aid.
