# If you can’t download or update apps on your iPhone or iPad

Try these tips if you have an issue when you update or download apps from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.

## Manually update the app

[Learn how to manually update an app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102629)

## Add a payment method

You might need to have a valid payment method on file, even if the app that you want to download is free.

[Add a payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118429)

## Resolve a problem with your payment method

[If a message says 'billing problem with previous purchase' or 'verification required'](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118284)

## Prioritize the download

From the Home Screen, touch and hold the app. From the menu that appears, choose Prioritize Download.

[Image: iPhone screen showing an app download menu. Prioritize Download is an option in the menu.]

## Restart your device

Restart your iPhone or iPad, then check to see if you can download or update apps.

[Restart your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118259)

[Restart your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101603)

## Confirm your age

In some countries or regions, you might have to confirm that you’re an adult before you can download certain apps.

[Learn how to confirm you're an adult](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125662)

## If you still can't download or update apps

[Contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase&PGF=PGF63003&category_id=SC0078)
