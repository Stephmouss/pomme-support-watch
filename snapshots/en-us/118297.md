# Add money to your Apple Account balance

Use a payment method, such as a credit or debit card, to add money to your Apple Account balance. Then use your Apple Account balance to buy apps, games, music, iCloud+, and more.

If you want to add a payment method to your Apple Account, not add money to your Apple Account balance, [learn how to add a payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118429) .

## Add money to your Apple Account balance in Wallet on iPhone

In some countries and regions, you can add money to your Apple Account balance in the Wallet app. To do this, you must [add your Apple Account card to Wallet](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/apple-account-iph5f869c0af/ios) .

- Open the Wallet app.

- Tap your Apple Account card.

- Tap Add Money.

[Image: An iPhone 17 Pro shows the Apple Account card in the Wallet App. To add money to your Apple Account, tap Add Money.]

- To add money using your selected Apple Pay payment method in Wallet, your iPhone must be using [iOS 18.1 or later](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/update-ios-iph3e504502/ios) .

- Follow the onscreen instructions to complete the purchase. Some payment methods might prompt you to verify your purchase with a text message or other method.

Adding money to your Apple Account balance in Wallet requires a payment method that is accepted in Wallet. To add money using a non-Wallet-based payment method, [use the App Store on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118297#iOS) .

## Add money to your Apple Account balance in the App Store on iPhone

- Open the App Store app.

- Tap your photo, initials, or the sign-in button at the top of the screen.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Add Money to Account.

[Image: An iPhone 17 Pro showing the Account screen in the App Store app. To add money your Apple Account, tap Add Money to Account.]

- To add funds, tap the amount that you want to add. Or to automatically add to your balance in the future, tap Set Up Auto Reload. [What's auto reload](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118297#topup) ?

- Follow the onscreen instructions to complete the purchase. Some payment methods might prompt you to verify your purchase with a text message or other method.

## Add money to your Apple Account balance on Mac or PC

- On your Mac, open the App Store. On a PC, open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app. If you're not signed in, sign in to your Apple Account.

- Open the Account Information page:

- On a Mac, click your name or the account button in the sidebar. Then click Account Settings at the top of the screen.

- On a PC, click your name or the account button in the sidebar. Then click View My Account.

- On the Account Information page, click Add Money.

[Image: A Mac showing the Account Settings screen in the App Store on macOS Tahoe 26. To add money to your Apple Account, click Add Money to Account.]

- Click the amount that you want to add. Or to automatically add to your balance in the future, click Set Up Auto Reload. [What's auto reload](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118297#topup) ?

- Click Next, then confirm your selection. Some payment methods might prompt you to verify your purchase with a text message or other method.

## Automatically add money to your Apple Account balance

If you want to automatically add to your Apple Account balance, set up auto reload. You can add money to your balance weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Or you can automatically top up your balance when it's low.

If you want to turn on auto reload on your iPhone, [visit the Add Money to Account page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118297#iOS) , then tap Set Up Auto Reload. To turn off auto reload on your iPhone, [visit the Add Money to Account page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118297#iOS) , then tap Turn Off Auto Reload.

To turn off auto reload on your computer, [visit the Add Money page](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118297#computer) , and click Turn Off Auto Reload.

In some countries and regions, these options to automatically add money might not be available.
