# If you get an alert from NTRA in Egypt that your IMEI isn't registered

Learn what to do when you see a message on your iPhone from NTRA that your IMEI isn't registered. NTRA is the telecommunications regulations authority in Egypt.

## If you buy a device or receive a service part replacement outside of Egypt

If you start using your iPhone on a mobile network in Egypt, you might get an alert from the NTRA that your IMEI isn't registered.

Register your IMEI using the Egyptian Customs Authority Telephony app from App Store. There is a 90-day grace period from the activation date of the Egyptian SIM card on the network. If you don't register your iPhone within 90 days, the status bar on your iPhone will show "No Service". The 90-day registration requirement applies to Egyptian SIM cards only and not to iPhone models using roaming.

## If you buy an iPhone or receive a replacement device in Egypt through an Apple-authorized channel

- Apple will register the IMEI of your iPhone on your behalf.

- If you buy an iPhone from an authorized seller, the VAT will prepay the tax. The receipt will say that the VAT paid the tax.

- If you buy an iPhone within Egypt, make sure that you receive an e-invoice to protect your consumer rights.

Learn more at [Telephony: National Telecom Regulatory Authority](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6739450752) , email contactus@telephony.gov.eg, or call 15380.
