# Turn Stage Manager on or off on your iPad

Use Stage Manager to resize windows to look the way you want, see multiple overlapping windows in a single view, tap to switch between apps, and more.

## Turn Stage Manager on or off

- Swipe down from the top-right corner of your screen to open Control Center.

- Tap the Stage Manager button , or tap and hold the Stage Manager button or Windowed Apps button to switch between Stage Manager or Windowed Apps.

You can also turn Stage Manager on or off from the Settings app:

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Multitasking & Gestures.

- To turn Stage Manager on, select Stage Manager. To turn it off, select Full Screen Apps (or Off), or select Windowed Apps to [multitask another way](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/work-with-multiple-apps-at-once-ipad08c9970c/26/ipados/26) .

## iPad models that support Stage Manager

With iPadOS 26, you can use Stage Manager on these iPad models:

- iPad (A16)

- iPad Air 13-inch (M3)

- iPad Air 11-inch (M3)

- iPad mini (A17 Pro)

- iPad Pro 13-inch (M4)

- iPad Pro 11-inch (M4)

- iPad Air 13-inch (M2)

- iPad Air 11-inch (M2)

- iPad (8th generation and later)

- iPad Pro 12.9-inch (3rd generation and later)

- iPad Pro 11-inch (1st generation and later)

- iPad Air (3rd generation and later)

- iPad mini (5th generation and later)

With iPadOS 26, you can use Stage Manager to [move apps and windows to an external display](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/connect-to-a-display-with-a-cable-ipadf1276cde/26/ipados/26#iPad3b881f32) from these iPad models:

- iPad Air 13-inch (M3)

- iPad Air 11-inch (M3)

- iPad Pro 13-inch (M4)

- iPad Pro 11-inch (M4)

- iPad Air 13-inch (M2)

- iPad Air 11-inch (M2)

- iPad Pro 12.9-inch (5th generation and later)

- iPad Pro 11-inch (3rd generation and later)

- iPad Air (5th generation)
