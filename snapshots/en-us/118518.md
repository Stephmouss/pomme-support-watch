# What to do before you sell or give away your Apple Vision Pro

Remove personal information from your Apple Vision Pro and prepare it for a new owner.

## Cancel or transfer AppleCare coverage

If your Apple Vision Pro is covered by an AppleCare plan, you can cancel that plan or transfer it to the new owner.

[Cancel your AppleCare plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118218)

[Transfer your AppleCare plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111801)

## Back up your Apple Vision Pro

iCloud Backup includes all the information and settings stored on your device that don't already sync to iCloud.

- In Settings, tap your name, tap iCloud, then tap iCloud Backup.

- Turn on iCloud Backup.

- To perform a manual backup, tap Backup Now.

[Learn more about iCloud Backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770)

## Reset your Apple Vision Pro to factory settings

To get your Apple Vision Pro ready for a new owner, remove Activation Lock and erase your personal information.

### Remove Activation Lock

To remove Activation Lock, sign out of your Apple Account:

- In Settings, tap your name.

- Scroll down and tap Sign Out.

- Enter your Apple Account password, then tap Turn Off.

### Erase your Apple Vision Pro

- In Settings, tap General, then tap Transfer or Reset This Apple Vision Pro.

- To transfer your content and settings to a new Vision Pro, tap Get Started and follow the onscreen instructions. Then return to Settings, tap General, then tap Transfer or Reset This Apple Vision Pro.

- Tap Erase All Content and Settings.

## Clean your Apple Vision Pro

[Learn how to clean your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119574)

## If you no longer have your Apple Vision Pro

- Ask the new owner to follow the steps to erase your Apple Vision Pro.

- [Learn how to remove Activation Lock on the web](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108934)

If you can't follow either of these steps:

- [Change your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101567) . Your personal information won’t be erased from the device, but the new owner won't be able to delete your information from iCloud.

- [Remove the device from your list of trusted devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102649)
