# Edit a calendar event with Siri AI on iPhone

Make changes to an event on your calendar, without opening the Calendar app.

## How to edit a calendar event

- Update your device to the [latest software](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , and make sure that you're using an Apple Intelligence-enabled device with Siri AI turned on. If you need help, learn [how to get Siri AI](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893) .

- [Activate Siri](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/aside/summon_siri/27/ios/27) , then tell Siri what you want to change within an [existing calendar event](https://supportb.corp.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-and-edit-events-in-calendar-iph3d110f84/27/ios/27) . For example, you might say, "Move my family video call to tomorrow." If more than one matching event appears, Siri can ask to clarify. Let Siri know which event you want to edit.

[Image: iPhone screen shows three "Family video call" event suggestions from Siri AI, each with an identical event title on different dates, and Siri asks which family video call should be moved.]

- Tap Change. If you want to make additional edits, open the Calendar app and tap the event.

This feature is available in [iOS 27 and iPadOS 27](https://www.apple.com/ios/feature-availability/) , and also in [macOS 27 Golden Gate](https://www.apple.com/macos/feature-availability/) . Siri AI is available in beta when your [device language](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109358) and [Siri language](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120499) are set to the same supported language. Siri AI will not be initially available in the EU on iOS and iPadOS. Learn more about [Siri AI requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/148218) and [how to get Siri AI](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893) .
