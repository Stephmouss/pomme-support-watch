# Subscribe to Apple Music on Android

Subscribe to Apple Music on your Android mobile device to stream millions of songs. Get exclusive playlists and recommendations for music you’ll love and listen across your other devices with the Apple Music app.

## Get the Apple Music app

To subscribe to Apple Music, you need an Android phone or tablet, or a Chromebook that supports Android apps. Make sure you update your device to the most recent version of the Android operating system. Then, [download the Apple Music app on the Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.apple.android.music) .

If you're in China mainland, you can download the Apple Music app from [Tencent](https://sj.qq.com/appdetail/com.apple.android.music) or [Xiaomi](https://app.mi.com/details?id=com.apple.android.music) .

## Then follow these steps to subscribe

- Open the Apple Music app.

- The first time you open the app, you'll see a "Welcome to Apple Music" message. If you don't see this message, tap Home at the bottom of your screen. [Do you already subscribe to Apple Music?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109340#already_member)

- Tap the trial offer (one trial per person or family).

- Tap Use Existing Apple Account to sign in to your Apple Account, or Create New Apple Account to create a new one.

- Choose an individual subscription, [family subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109339) to share with up to five people, or [student subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109339) .

- Confirm your billing information and add a valid payment method.

- Tap Subscribe.

After you subscribe to Apple Music, [you can change your subscription to a family, student, individual, or annual plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118448) .

## If you already subscribe to Apple Music

- On your Android device, [download the Apple Music app from the Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.apple.android.music) .

- Open the Apple Music app.

- If you see an offer for a trial, tap Sign In in the upper corner, or tap the More button > Settings, then tap Sign In.

- Sign in to the Apple Account that you use with Apple Music.

[Sign in to Apple Music on your other devices with the Apple Music app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109329#signin)

[View or cancel your Apple Music subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428)
