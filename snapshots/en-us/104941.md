# Get AppleCare coverage for your Apple device

Learn about your options for AppleCare coverage for your Apple device.

AppleCare plans available for your device and how to buy AppleCare might differ based on your country or region and the device you want to cover. [Find information about AppleCare pricing and benefits in your country or region](https://www.apple.com/applecare/)

## Available AppleCare plans

### AppleCare Protection Plan

AppleCare Protection Plan coverage is available to buy when you purchase your device and within 12 months after purchase.

## Available AppleCare plans

## Get AppleCare coverage

You can get AppleCare coverage in Settings on an iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro associated with your Apple Account. [Open AppleCare & Warranty settings on your device](https://coverage.apple.com/cc/acW4820) , or follow these steps:

- Open Settings on your device.

- Select General.

- Select AppleCare & Warranty.

- Select the device to which you want to add AppleCare coverage, and follow the onscreen steps to add coverage.

You can also [check your AppleCare coverage options online](https://checkcoverage.apple.com) .

Your option for AppleCare coverage includes AppleCare+. If your AppleCare plan for your Apple device expires, you might be able to continue coverage within 30 days of the end date of your coverage.

- If you paid upfront for 12, 24, or 36 months of coverage for your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, or Apple Vision Pro, you might be able to purchase coverage on an annual basis when the service period is over.

- If you pay annually, your annual plan will renew each year until canceled.

## Available AppleCare plans

Your AppleCare coverage options include AppleCare+, AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss, and AppleCare One. If your AppleCare plan for your Apple device expires, you might be able to purchase new coverage within 45 days after your coverage ends. Your new plan will have recurring payments.

### AppleCare+

Four days after your Apple device is shipped or picked up from Apple, and within 60 days of your purchase, you can buy AppleCare+ for your device.

### AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss

Four days after your Apple device is shipped or picked up from Apple, and within 60 days of your purchase, you can buy AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss for your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch.

### AppleCare One

Four days after your Apple device is shipped or picked up from Apple, and within 60 days of your purchase, you can buy an AppleCare One plan. You'll start with coverage for up to three devices, and you can swap devices and add more devices to your AppleCare One plan. For iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, Theft and Loss coverage is included in your AppleCare One plan.

[Learn how to start an AppleCare One plan](https://support.apple.com/127207)

### AppleCare+

### AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss

### AppleCare One

#### AppleCare One Individual

When you purchase a new product from Apple, you can [get an AppleCare One plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127207) . If you don't buy AppleCare One when you purchase your new device, you have 60 days after purchase to buy an AppleCare One plan using your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro. You'll start with coverage for up to three devices, and you can [add more devices to your AppleCare One plan](https://support.apple.com/122224) anytime. For iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, Theft and Loss coverage is included in your AppleCare One plan.

#### AppleCare One Family

With AppleCare One Family, you can cover all eligible devices for you and your Family Sharing group. For iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, Theft and Loss coverage is included with AppleCare One Family.

[Learn about AppleCare One for your Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/en-us/148275)
