# Set a Live Photo as your Lock Screen wallpaper on your iPhone

In iOS 17 or later, you can choose a Live Photo for your Lock Screen wallpaper so that your image plays whenever you wake up your device.

- In the Settings app, tap Wallpaper.

- Tap Add New Wallpaper.

- Tap Photos.

- Tap Live Photo.

- Tap the Live Photo that you want to use on your Lock Screen.

- Customize your Live Photo wallpaper. If you want your Live Photo wallpaper to play automatically when you wake your phone, make sure the play button is on.

- Tap Add.

- Tap Set as Wallpaper Pair to use your new wallpaper on the Lock Screen and Home Screen. If you want a separate Home Screen wallpaper, tap Customize Home Screen and set up a second wallpaper.

You can also touch and hold your Lock Screen to open the wallpaper gallery and add a new wallpaper.
