## How to use Siri on your iPhone

Here are a few ways to activate Siri.

### Use your voice

Just say Siri* or Hey Siri, then immediately say what you need.

Adjust voice activation for Siri: open Settings, tap Apple Intelligence & Siri (or Siri), then tap Talk & Type to Siri (or Talk to Siri).

### Press and release the button

[Image: iPhone with arrow pointing to the Side button to activate Siri.]

- If your iPhone doesn't have a Home button, press the Side button, then make your request.

- If your iPhone has a Home button, press the Home button, then make your request.

- To make a longer request, press and hold the Side or Home button until you're finished with your request.

Turn Side button activation for Siri on or off: open Settings, tap Apple Intelligence & Siri (or Siri), then tap Talk & Type to Siri (or Talk to Siri).

### Type instead of speaking to Siri

If you have both [Type to Siri](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-siri-accessibility-settings-iphaff1d606/ios) and Apple Intelligence turned on, double-tap the bottom of the screen and type your request. If you have Type to Siri turned on but not Apple Intelligence, press the button to activate Siri and type your request.

## How to use Siri on your iPad

Here are a few ways to activate Siri.

### Use your voice

Just say Siri* or Hey Siri, then immediately say what you need.

Adjust voice activation for Siri: open Settings, tap Apple Intelligence & Siri (or Siri), then tap Talk & Type to Siri or (Talk to Siri).

### Press and release the button

[Image: iPad with arrow pointing to the Top button to activate Siri.]

- If your iPad doesn't have a Home button, press and hold the Top button, then make your request.

- If your iPad has a Home button, press and hold the Home button, then make your request.

- To make a longer request, press and hold the Home or Top button until you're finished with your request.

Turn Top button activation for Siri on or off: open Settings, tap Apple Intelligence & Siri (or Siri), then tap Talk & Type to Siri (or Talk to Siri).

### Type instead of speaking to Siri

[Turn on Type to Siri](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/siri-ipadd58b8673/ipados) . Then press the button to activate Siri and type your request.

## How to use Siri on your Mac

Here are a few ways to activate Siri.

### Use your voice

On [Mac computers with Apple silicon](https://support.apple.com/en-us/116943) , say Siri* or Hey Siri, then make your request.

Adjust voice activation for Siri: choose System Settings (or System Preferences) from the Apple m enu , choose Apple Intelligence & Siri (or Siri), then click Listen For (or Enable Ask Siri).

### Use the Siri button

[Image: The Siri bar appears after you press the Siri button in the top-right corner of a Mac screen.]

- In the menu bar or Dock, click the Siri button , then say what you need.

- On a Mac with a Touch Bar, tap the Siri button , then make your request.

- On an Apple keyboard with function keys, press and hold the Dictation key , then make your request.

[Image: Dictation Key]

- To make a longer request, hold the Siri button until you're finished with your request.

### Type instead of speaking to Siri

[If Apple Intelligence is turned on](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/intro-to-apple-intelligence-mchl46361784/mac) , you can automatically Type to Siri by clicking the Siri button. If Apple Intelligence is not turned on, [turn on Type to Siri](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchl5715a9f2) , then click the Siri button.

## How to use Siri on your Apple Watch

Here are a few ways to activate Siri.

### Use your voice

Look at your Apple Watch or tap its screen. Then say Siri* or Hey Siri, followed by your request.

Adjust voice activation for Siri: open Settings, tap Siri, then tap Listen for.

### Raise to speak

On your Apple Watch Series 3 or later, raise your wrist so your Apple Watch is near your mouth, then say what you need.

### Press the Digital Crown

[Image: Apple Watch with arrow pointing to the Digital Crown to activate Siri.]

- Press and hold the Digital Crown, then say what you need.

- To make a longer request, press and hold the Digital Crown until you're finished making your request.

Turn Digital Crown activation for Siri on or off: open Settings, tap Siri, then tap Press Digital Crown.

### Type instead of speaking to Siri

- Open Settings.

- Tap Accessibility.

- Tap Siri.

- Tap Type to Siri.

- Turn on Type to Siri.

- Press and hold the Digital Crown and type your request.

## How to use Siri on your HomePod

Here are a few ways to activate Siri.

### Use your voice

Just say Siri* or Hey Siri, then immediately say what you need.

### Touch the top of HomePod

[Image: HomePod pictured from above with multicolored lights illuminated.]

- Touch and hold the top of HomePod, then make your request.

- To make a longer request, touch and hold the top of HomePod until you're finished making your request.

HomePod isn't available in all countries and regions.

## How to use Siri on your Apple TV

[Image: Apple TV Remote with blue arrow pointing to the Siri button to activate Siri.]

Hold down the Siri button on your Siri Remote, make your request, then release the Siri button.

[Learn more about using Siri on your Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105019)

## How to use Siri with CarPlay

If your [vehicle supports CarPlay or Siri Eyes Free](https://www.apple.com/ios/carplay/available-models/) , hold down the voice-command button on your steering wheel while you make your request.
