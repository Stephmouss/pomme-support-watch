# Transfer money from Savings to Apple Cash or your external bank account

You can transfer money from Savings to a linked external bank account or to Apple Cash.

## Transfer money from Savings to a linked external bank account

To transfer money from your Savings 1 account to your bank account, you need to [link your bank account to Savings](https://support.apple.com/102163#bankaccount) . 2

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap Savings account, then tap Withdraw.

- Enter the amount to withdraw, tap the Done button , then tap Continue.

- If the bank account you want use is listed in the Transfer To field, tap Next.

- If you don't see your preferred bank account or you see Apple Cash 3 listed, tap Transfer To. Then tap your preferred bank account.

- Double-click the side button to transfer, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

If you transfer money from Savings to a linked external bank account, it typically takes 1-3 business days for the transfer to complete. Transfers are subject to review. Check with your external bank for funds availability.

## Transfer money from Savings to Apple Cash

To transfer money from Savings to Apple Cash, you need to [set up Apple Cash](https://support.apple.com/109304) and [verify your identity](https://support.apple.com/109312) . Transfers you make from Savings to Apple Cash are subject to transfer limits.

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap Savings account, then tap Withdraw.

- Enter the amount to withdraw, tap the Done button , then tap Continue.

- If Apple Cash is listed in the Transfer To field, tap Next.

- If you don't see Apple Cash listed, tap Transfer To. Then tap Apple Cash.

- Double-click the side button to transfer, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

If you transfer money from Savings to Apple Cash, the funds are typically available instantly. Transfers are subject to review.
