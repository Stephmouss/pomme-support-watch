# About Apple's Power Adapter Replacement Program --- US Only

Apple's Power Adapter Replacement Program ended on January 31, 2014.

If you think you have an Apple power adapter with strain relief, [contact Apple](https://www.apple.com/support/contact/) or take your power adapter to an [Apple Authorized Service Provider or Apple Store](https://locate.apple.com/) . They will help you determine which service and support options are available. If you plan to visit an Apple Retail Store, [make a reservation](http://www.apple.com/retail/geniusbar) for the Genius Bar (only available in some countries and regions).
