# Remove a recovery contact from an Apple Account

If you don’t want to be someone’s recovery contact, or if you don’t want someone to be your recovery contact, you can remove them.

## Remove yourself as a recovery contact from someone else’s Apple Account

If you don’t want to be someone’s recovery contact, use your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to remove yourself. You might need to update your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to the latest software.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Go to Settings, then tap [your name].

- Tap Sign In & Security, then tap Recovery Contacts.

- Scroll to Account Recovery For and tap the contact’s name.

- Tap Remove. The contact receives a message that you’re no longer their account recovery contact.

[Image: Settings showing someone that you’re an account recovery for with the option to Remove yourself.]

### On your Mac

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings, then click [your name].

- Click Sign In & Security, then click Recovery Contacts.

- Scroll to Account Recovery For and click your contact’s name.

- Click Remove. The contact receives a message that you’re no longer their account recovery contact.

[Image: Settings showing someone that you’re an account recovery for with the option to Remove yourself.]

## Remove a recovery contact from your own Apple Account

If you don’t want someone listed as your recovery contact anymore, use your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to remove them. You might need to update your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to the latest software.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Go to Settings, then tap [your name].

- Tap Sign In & Security, then tap Recovery Contacts.

- Tap the contact’s name.

- Tap Remove Contact. The contact receives a message that they’re no longer your account recovery contact.

[Image: Settings showing an account recovery contact with the option to Remove Contact.]

### On your Mac

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings, then click [your name].

- Click Sign In & Security, then click Recovery Contacts.

- Click the contact’s name.

- Click Remove Contact. The contact receives a message that they’re no longer your account recovery contact.

[Image: Settings showing an account recovery contact with the option to Remove Contact.]
