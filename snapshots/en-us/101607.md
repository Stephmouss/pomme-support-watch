# Identify status lights on HomePod and HomePod mini

Lights appear on the top of your HomePod (2nd generation) and HomePod mini, and change colors depending on what you're doing on your HomePod speaker. Here's what you might see.

- [Static white light](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101607#static_white)

- [White flashing light](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101607#white_flashing)

- [White pulsing light](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101607#white_pulsing)

- [White spinning light](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101607#white_spinning)

- [Multicolored light](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101607#multicolored)

- [Orange flashing light](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101607#orange_flashing)

- [Green pulsing light](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101607#green_pulsing)

- [Red spinning light](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101607#red_spinning)

- [Red wiggling light](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101607#wiggle)

## Static white light

[Image: A continuous white light on top of a HomePod speaker, indicating audio is playing.]

If a continuous white light appears on the top of your HomePod or HomePod mini, then [your HomePod speaker is playing audio](https://support.apple.com/guide/homepod/play-audio-using-your-ios-or-ipados-device-apdfb81a72e4/homepod) .

If you don't hear audio, the volume may be too low.

## White flashing light

If a white light shines on and off repeatedly on the top of your HomePod or HomePod mini, unplug your HomePod speaker from power. Wait 15 seconds, then plug it back in.

If the white flashing light continues, [reset your HomePod speaker](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108389) .

If the white flashing light continues after resetting your HomePod speaker, you might need service. [Get service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .

## White pulsing light

If a continuous white light pulses on the top of your HomePod or HomePod mini, then your HomePod speaker is ready for setup.

Unlock your iPhone or iPad and hold it next to your HomePod speaker to [continue setup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111110) .

## White spinning light

If a white light moves in a circle around the top of your HomePod or HomePod mini, then your HomePod speaker is turning on or [updating software](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105005) .

Keep your HomePod speaker plugged in while it's updating.

## Multicolored light

If a multicolored light appears on the top of your HomePod or HomePod mini, Siri is listening, thinking, or responding. A multicolored light will also play when Sound Glow is active on compatible HomePod models.

## Orange flashing light

If an orange light shines on and off repeatedly on the top of HomePod mini, then you connected HomePod mini to a computer, or you plugged it into a power adapter that isn’t rated 20W (9V ⎓ 2.22A).

Plug your HomePod mini into power using a [20W (9V ⎓ 2.22A) adapter](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MWVV3AM/A/20w-usb-c-power-adapter) .

## Green pulsing light

If a continuous green light pulses on the top of your HomePod speaker, you [transferred a phone call to HomePod.](https://support.apple.com/guide/homepod/use-for-phone-calls-apdeaa15a6c3/homepod)

## Red spinning light

If a red light moves in a circle around the top of your HomePod or HomePod mini, then you're resetting your HomePod speaker.

Wait for the reset to finish, then [set up your HomePod speaker again](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111110) .

## Red wiggling light

If a red light moves back and forth on the top of your HomePod or HomePod mini, then the touch controls for the device are locked.

[Learn how to lock or unlock the touch controls](https://support.apple.com/guide/homepod/apd99ee29027/1.0/homepod/1.0#a78350e2-bdb0-4df7-8996-34298b390827)
