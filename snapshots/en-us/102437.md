# If the built-in camera isn’t working on your Mac

Learn what to do if the built-in camera won’t activate or appear in an app on your Mac

## Update your software

[Make sure your software is up to date](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) .

## Check Screen Time settings

If your Mac is using macOS Catalina or later and you use [Screen Time](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/set-up-screen-time-for-yourself-mchle4e2edfe/14.0/mac/14.0) , make sure your camera is turned on and apps that use the camera have time available.

- Choose Apple menu  and select System Settings (or System Preferences). Click Screen Time.

- Click Content & Privacy, then click App Restrictions (or Apps).

- Make sure the checkbox for Camera is selected.

- Click App Limits. If you need to use your camera with an app in the list, make sure the app is off or not selected.

## Give apps permission

If your Mac is using macOS Mojave or later, choose which apps can use your built-in camera:

- Choose Apple menu  and select System Settings (or System Preferences). Then click Security & Privacy.

- Click Privacy & Security (or Privacy), then click Camera.

- Turn on any apps that you want to use your camera in. You might be prompted to quit and reopen an app before it can use your camera.

## Restart your Mac or reset the SMC

If you have a Mac with [Apple silicon](https://support.apple.com/en-us/116943) , restart your computer. If you have an Intel-based Mac, [reset the SMC](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102605) .

## Get more help

- If you still can't use the built-in camera on your Mac, or the camera indicator light next to the camera flashes green, you might need service. [Get service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .

- If you can't connect to FaceTime, learn what to do [if FaceTime isn't working on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102203) .
