# Pay with rewards using Apple Pay

Use rewards to make purchases online, in apps, and in store on your iPhone or iPad.

To pay with rewards using Apple Pay, you need:

- A [compatible](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphone-models-compatible-with-ios-18-iphe3fa5df43/ios) iPhone or iPad with iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later for purchases online and in apps. You can make in store purchases on an iPhone with [iOS 26 or later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- To [set up Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108398) .

- A debit or credit card from [a participating card issuer in the Wallet app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204916#rewards) .

All reward offers are provided directly by your card issuer and are subject to the card issuer's terms and conditions. For issues or questions about your rewards or purchases made using rewards, contact the card issuer.

## How it works

You can use eligible debit or credit card rewards to make purchases when you check out with Apple Pay on your iPhone or iPad. 1 Whatever type of rewards you have — cash back, points, or miles — your card issuer will convert them into a dollar amount so that you can apply them to your purchase when you check out with Apple Pay.

When you use rewards for a purchase, the full purchase amount is charged when you check out. Your debit or credit card issuer will provide a statement credit equal to the value of the rewards used for the purchase after the purchase is completed.

## Use rewards to make purchases online and in apps

To pay with rewards using your card:

- Tap the Apple Pay button or choose Apple Pay as your payment method.

- If your card supports this feature you'll see a Use Rewards hint under your card. You can also see your available rewards balance. 2 To use a different card, tap Change Payment Method or Other Cards & Pay Later Options.

[Image: "Use Rewards" hint below an eligible credit card selected while checking out with Apple Pay.]

- Tap Use Rewards.

- Enter the amount of rewards that you want to apply to your purchase. You can apply rewards for the full purchase amount or you can apply rewards to a part of the purchase amount, depending on your available balance.

- Tap Apply Rewards.

- Confirm the payment using Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

## Use rewards to make purchases in store

- Double-click the side button and tap the credit or debit card that you want to use. If the card that you want to use is eligible, you'll see Use Rewards beneath the card.

- To apply your rewards balance to your transaction, tap Use Rewards. Then tap Choose Amount to enter a specific amount of rewards that you want to use.

[Image: "Pay In Full" and "Use Rewards" buttons below an eligible card.]

- To check out with your rewards applied, hold the top of your iPhone near the card reader until you see Done or a checkmark on the screen.

If your card is supported but you don't see the option to use rewards, contact your card issuer for more information. Some card issuers might require you to opt in through a bank app or website before using rewards with Apple Pay.

## View your rewards balance and redemption details

Purchases made using rewards will be labeled in [your latest transactions history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104954) in Wallet.

- To see your rewards balance, tap on your supported card in the Wallet app. Your rewards balance is below your card. You can also see your rewards balance when you're making a purchase.

- To see your latest redemptions all in one place, tap on the rewards balance on the front of your card.
