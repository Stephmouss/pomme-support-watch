# If you can't update or restore your iPad

You can put your iPad in recovery mode, then restore your iPad using your computer.

In these situations, you might need to use recovery mode to restore your iPad:

- Your computer doesn't recognize your iPad or says it's in recovery mode.

- If your screen shows the Apple logo for several minutes with no progress bar.

- You see the recovery mode screen.

- Your device repeatedly starts up to Recovery Assistant, which isn't able to recover your device.

[Learn what to do if you see the progress bar on your screen for several minutes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102317) .

## Put your iPad in recovery mode and set it up again

If you tried updating your device using a computer and it still wont start up:

- Make sure that your [Mac is up to date](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) . If you're using a PC, make sure that you have the latest version of the [Apple Devices app](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) or [iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290) .

- Connect your iPad to your computer using a USB cable.

- Open the Finder on your Mac, or open the Apple Devices app on your PC.

- Keep your iPad connected and wait until the recovery mode screen appears.

- If your iPad doesn't have a Home button:

- Press and quickly release the volume button closest to the top button.

- Press and quickly release the volume button farthest from the top button.

- Press and hold the top button.

[Image: Animation demonstrating how to put an iPad into recovery mode by quickly pressing the Volume Up button, then the Volume Down button, and then pressing and holding the top button.]

- Hold the top button until you see the recovery mode screen.

[Image: An iPad showing the recovery mode screen.]

- If your iPad has a Home button:

- Press and hold both the Home and the top button at the same time.

[Image: Animation of an iPad with arrows pointing to the Home button and the top button, indicating the buttons used to put the iPad into recovery mode.]

- When your iPad turns off, release the top button, but keep holding the Home button until you see the recovery mode screen.

[Image: An iPad showing the recovery mode screen.]

- [Locate your iPad on your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108392) .

- When you see the option to Restore or Update, choose Restore. Restore reinstalls iPadOS and erases all of your data. If you are troubleshooting an issue and haven't tried updating yet, try updating first.

- Wait while your computer downloads the software for your iPad. If the download takes more than 15 minutes and your iPad exits the recovery mode screen, let the download finish, then repeat step 3.

- After the Restore process completes, [set up your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105132) .
