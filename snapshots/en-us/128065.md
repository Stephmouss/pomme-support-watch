# If you see Error 110 when restoring your iPhone or iPad

Learn what causes Error 110 when you restore your iPhone or iPad, and how to resolve it.

When you restore your iPhone or iPad using a computer, your device contacts Apple. This verifies that the software is genuine and authorized. If verification is successful, the restore completes and your device restarts normally. You can then set it up using the Finder or iTunes.

If Apple can't verify that iOS or iPadOS restored correctly, an error occurs.

If an error occurs, [try restoring your device again](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/restore-all-content-from-a-backup-iph1624229a/26/ios/26) .

## Get more help

If you tried the steps for your issue and you still need help, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
