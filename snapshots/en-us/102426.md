# Use Handoff to continue tasks on your other Apple devices

With Handoff, you can start work on one Apple device, then switch to another nearby device and pick up where you left off.

[Image: iPhone and Mac showing the same document after handoff to Mac]

## Set up Handoff

- Make sure that your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch have Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on.

- Make sure that each device is [signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) .

- On each device, make sure that Handoff is turned on. It’s turned on by default.

- Mac with macOS Ventura 13 or later: From the Apple menu , choose System Settings. Click General in the sidebar, then click AirDrop & Continuity (or AirDrop & Handoff) on the right. Turn on "Allow Handoff between this Mac and your iCloud devices."

- Mac with earlier versions of macOS: From the Apple menu , choose System Preferences. Click General, then select "Allow Handoff between this Mac and your iCloud devices."

- iPhone or iPad: Open Settings, tap General, tap AirPlay & Continuity (or AirPlay & Handoff), then turn on Handoff.

- Apple Watch: In the Apple Watch app on your iPhone, tap General, then turn on Enable Handoff. Apple Watch supports handing off from your watch to your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

If your older device doesn’t have Handoff, make sure that it meets the [Handoff system requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108046#handoff) .

## Start a task on one device

- On your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch, open an app that works with Handoff and is also installed on your other device. Apps that work with Handoff include Calendar, Clock, Contacts, FaceTime, Freeform, Keynote, Mail, Maps, News, Notes, Numbers, Pages, Reminders, Safari, Stocks, and many third-party apps.

- Then use the app to start a task, such as viewing a webpage or writing an email or document.

- Keep the app open and in front, then continue the task on your other device.

## Continue the task on your other device

Make sure that the device handing off the task is unlocked, near your other device, and shows the task in progress. Then follow these steps to continue the task on your other device.

### Continue the task on Mac

On your Mac, click the app’s Handoff icon in the Dock. The icon includes a small symbol of the device that is handing off the task.

[Image: Handoff icon in the Dock on Mac.]

Or press Command (⌘)-Tab on your keyboard and choose the app’s Handoff icon from the App Switcher.

[Image: Handoff icon at the beginning of the App Switcher on Mac.]

For example, this video explains how to use your Mac to continue a task you’ve started on your iPhone:

[Image: Play the video]

### Continue the task on iPhone

- On your iPhone, [open the App Switcher](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118408) by swiping up from the bottom of the screen.

- Tap the app banner at the bottom of the screen.

[Image: App banner at the bottom of the App Switcher on iPhone.]

### Continue the task on iPad

On your iPad, tap the app’s Handoff icon in the Dock. The icon includes a small symbol of the device that is handing off the task.

[Image: Handoff icon in the Dock on iPad.]
