# Connect Apple subscriptions from your wireless carrier or other provider to your Apple Account

If your wireless carrier plan or other provider includes a subscription to an Apple service, connect your subscription to your Apple Account to access your service.

## How to connect an Apple subscription to your Apple Account

- Your carrier or other provider might send you a text message, email, or another link to connect your subscription. [Learn what to do if you don’t receive a link from your wireless carrier.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108099#notext)

- Open the link.

- Follow the onscreen steps to sign in to your Apple Account and connect your subscription. If you see a message that says, “This subscription is already being used with a different Apple Account,” [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) to unlink your Apple Account.

- If you have a Dual SIM phone, you can connect your Apple subscription on your default line.

### If you subscribe to Apple TV through Amazon Prime

- Go to [link.apple.com/account/partner/YW1hem9u/login](https://link.apple.com/account/partner/YW1hem9u/login) .

- Follow the onscreen steps to sign in to your Amazon Account and connect your subscription.

Once connected, you can access your Apple TV subscription [through the Apple TV app on your devices](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvplus/welcome/web) . To disconnect your Apple Account, manage your account settings through Amazon Prime.

Connecting an Apple TV subscription purchased through Amazon Prime to your Apple Account is only supported in certain countries and regions. Apple Accounts for children under 13 cannot be connected to Apple TV subscriptions purchased through Amazon Prime.

### If you subscribe to the Apple TV and Peacock bundle through Peacock billing

Follow the onscreen steps to subscribe to Apple TV and connect your subscription to your Apple Account. Once connected, you can access Peacock content through the Peacock app and Apple TV content [through the Apple TV app on your devices](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvplus/welcome/web) .

To unsubscribe, manage your bundled subscription through Peacock.

#### Change the Apple Account connected to your bundled subscription

- To connect a different Apple Account to your Apple TV and Peacock subscription bundle, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) to unlink your Apple Account.

- Log out of the Peacock app, then follow the onscreen instructions to log in with the preferred Apple Account.

The Apple TV and Peacock bundled subscription is only available in the United States.

## If you don’t receive a link from your wireless carrier or other provider

Your wireless carrier or other provider might have a different way of helping you connect your subscription to your Apple Account. First, try opening the app the subscription is for. If you’re still unsure how to connect your subscription, contact your wireless carrier or other provider for support.

### If your subscription is from a wireless carrier

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app.

- Tap your name.

- Tap Subscriptions.

- Scroll until you see your carrier’s name, then tap it.

If you still can’t find a link to connect your subscription, contact your wireless carrier.

## If you already have a subscription from Apple

If you subscribe to an Apple service billed through Apple and then receive the same subscription from your wireless carrier or other provider, cancel one to avoid duplicate charges. You can [cancel your subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) or cancel your subscription through your wireless carrier or other provider.

Subscriptions to Apple services are available only in certain countries and regions through some wireless carriers and providers. Contact your wireless carrier or other provider for more information.
