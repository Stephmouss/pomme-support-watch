# If your iPad won't turn on or is frozen

If your iPad has a frozen screen, doesn't respond when you touch it, or becomes stuck when you turn it on, learn what to do.

[iPad screen is black or frozen](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102642#block-frozen)

[iPad is stuck on the Apple logo](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102642#startup)

## If your screen is black or frozen

Follow the steps for your device.

### On your iPad without a Home button

- Press and quickly release the volume button closest to the top button.

- Press and quickly release the volume button farthest from the top button.

- Press and hold the top button until you see the Apple logo.

If your iPad doesn't turn on, [follow these steps to check your hardware and charge your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102612) . You might need to charge for up to an hour.

If your iPad still doesn't turn on, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102642#getsupport) .

### On your iPad with a Home button

Press and hold both the Home button and the top button until you see the Apple logo.

If your iPad doesn't turn on, [follow these steps to check your hardware and charge your device](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT211203#slow) . You might need to charge for up to an hour.

If your iPad still doesn't turn on, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102642#getsupport) .

## If your iPad turns on but gets stuck during startup

If you see the Apple logo or a red or blue screen during startup, try these steps:

### On your iPad without a Home button

- Connect your iPad to a computer. On a Mac with macOS Catalina 10.15 or later, open the Finder. On a Mac with macOS Mojave or earlier, open iTunes. If you're using a PC, use the latest version of the [Apple Devices](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) app or [iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290) .

- [Locate your device on your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108392) .

- While your device is connected:

- Press and quickly release the volume button closest to the top button.

- Press and quickly release the volume button farthest from the top button.

- Press and hold the top button until you see the Apple logo. Keep holding the top button.

- Keep holding the top button until you see the recovery-mode screen. [Play](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102642) [Pause](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102642) [Replay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102642)

- When you get the option to restore or update, choose Update.

[Image: Message showing update and restore options]

- Your computer will download the software for your device. If it takes more than 15 minutes, your iPad will exit recovery mode, and you'll need to repeat steps 2–4.

### On your iPad with a Home button

- On a Mac with macOS Catalina 10.15 or later, open the Finder. On a Mac with macOS Mojave or earlier, open iTunes. If you're using a PC, use the latest version of the [Apple Devices](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) app or [iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290) .

- [Locate your device on your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108392) .

- While your device is connected, press and hold both the Home button and the top button. Keep holding the buttons.

- Keep holding the top button until you see the recovery-mode screen. [Play](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102642) [Pause](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102642) [Replay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102642)

- When you get the option to restore or update, choose Update.

[Image: Message showing update and restore options]

- Your computer will download the software for your device. If it takes more than 15 minutes, your iPad will exit recovery mode, and you'll need to repeat steps 2–4.
