# Find your Apple TV model number

You can find the model number of your Apple TV in three places.

[Check Settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109027#settings)

[Check the bottom of your Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109027#appletv)

[Check the box](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109027#box)

## Check Settings

From the Apple TV Home screen, choose Settings > General > About and check the Model.

[Image: Model number appears near the top of the About screen]

## Check the bottom of your Apple TV

To see the model number, check the information label on the bottom of your Apple TV.

[Image: Model number appears on the bottom of the Apple TV device]

## Check the box

To find the model number, check the back of the box that your Apple TV came in.
