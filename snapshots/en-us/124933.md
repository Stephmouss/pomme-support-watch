# Update your Apple Pay information

Update your billing and contact information that Apple Pay uses for in-app and online purchases.

When you enter billing, shipping, and contact information for a payment card in Apple Wallet, Apple Pay stores the information and uses it when you make purchases. Depending on the merchant, you can update your billing, shipping, and contact information when you check out online and within apps.

## Update your Apple Pay billing information on your iPhone

- In the Wallet app, tap a card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Card Details. *

- Tap Billing Address.

- Tap the billing address you want to set as your default address or tap Enter New Billing Address to add a new billing address.

- Tap Edit to change the details of the address or to remove it.

### Update your Apple Pay billing information on your other devices

- Apple Watch: In the Apple Watch app on your iPhone, tap Wallet & Apple Pay. If it's not already selected, tap the Info tab and then tap the card whose address you want to change. Then tap Billing Address.

- iPad and Vision Pro: In Settings, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap the card whose address you want to change. Then tap Billing Address.

- Mac with Touch ID: In System Settings (or System Preferences), click Wallet & Apple Pay, click the card whose address you want to change. Then click the Billing Address menu.

* In iOS 15 and iPadOS 15 and earlier, you won't see the option to tap Card Details.

## How to update your Apple Pay shipping and contact information

- iPhone: Go to Settings and tap Wallet & Apple Pay. Scroll down to Transaction Defaults and update your shipping address, email, and phone number.

- Apple Watch: In the Apple Watch app on your iPhone, tap Wallet & Apple Pay. Scroll down to Transaction Defaults and update your shipping address, email, and phone number.

- iPad and Vision Pro: Go to Settings and tap Wallet & Apple Pay. Scroll down to Transaction Defaults and update your shipping address, email, and phone number.

- Mac with Touch ID: In System Settings (or System Preferences), go to Wallet & Apple Pay. Click Contact and Shipping, and update your shipping address, email, and phone number.

To use Apple Pay you need a supported card from a participating card issuer. To check if your card is compatible with Apple Pay, contact your card issuer.
