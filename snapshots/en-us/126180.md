# Organize smart home accessories in the Home app

Organize accessories by group, room, and zone to help you keep track of where your accessories are and to control your accessories with Siri.

[Organize accessories into a group](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126180#group)

[Organize accessories by room](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126180#organize-by-room)

[Organize rooms into a zone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126180#organize-by-zone)

[Learn more](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126180#learn-more)

## Organize accessories into a group

- Open the Home app, then tap or click an accessory.

- Tap or click the Settings button .

- Select Group with Other Accessories.

- Name your group and select accessories to add to your group.

- Tap or click the Done button .

## Organize accessories by room

- Open the Home app, then tap or click the Add button .

- Select Add Room.

- Give your room a name, then tap or click the Done button .

- Tap or click the Back button to return to the Home tab.

- Tap or click the accessory you want to add to the room, then tap or click the Settings button .

- Tap or click Room, then choose a room.

## Organize rooms into a zone

- Open the Home app, then tap or click the room name to open the room.

- Tap or click the More button and select Room Settings.

- Tap or click Zone.

- Select a suggested zone, or choose Create New, then name the zone.

- Tap or click the Done button .
