# Set up a recovery contact for your Apple Account

A recovery contact can help you regain access to your account, even if you forgot your Apple Account password or device passcode. Learn how to set up a recovery contact on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

## What’s a recovery contact?

An account recovery contact is someone who can verify your identity and help you regain access to your Apple Account. During the account recovery process, they can get a recovery code for you. When you enter this recovery code, you can reset your Apple Account password.

Your recovery contacts won’t have any access to your account, only the ability to give you a code. To protect your privacy, Apple doesn’t know who your recovery contacts are. It’s important for you to remember who your recovery contacts are.

If you don’t have a recovery contact, you can still go through the full [account recovery](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118574) process. This can take longer if you don’t have a recovery contact to help.

## Before you set up a recovery contact

Your recovery contact should be a person that you know and trust, like a family member or a close friend. You can add up to five recovery contacts for your account. You might need to do a few things before you can add a recovery contact:

- Update your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to the latest software. All of your Apple devices must be running iOS 15, iPadOS 15, or macOS Monterey 12 or later. You might be prompted to remove any devices with older software from your account that you aren’t using.

- Your recovery contact must have an Apple device running iOS 15, iPadOS 15, or macOS Monterey 12 or later.

- You and your recovery contact must have iMessage turned on.

- You and your recovery contact must be over the age of 13 ( [age varies by country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125666) ).

- If prompted, [turn on two-factor authentication](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102660) for your Apple Account. Most accounts already use two-factor authentication.

## Set up an account recovery contact

You can add an account recovery contact on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Go to Settings, then tap [your name].

- Tap Sign-In & Security, then tap Recovery Contacts.

[Image: iPhone showing Apple Account settings and how to add a Recovery Contact.]

- Tap Add Recovery Contact, then follow the onscreen steps.

- Choose one of the recommended contacts. Or tap Choose Someone Else to add a different person.

[Image: iPhone showing contacts you can add as a Recovery Contact.]

- If you select someone in your Family Sharing group, they’re added automatically. If you choose someone else, they need to accept the invitation in the Messages app.

- After they accept your request, you’ll see a message that they’ve been added as your account recovery contact.

If your friend or family member declines or removes themself as your recovery contact, you’ll receive a notification. You can select a new account recovery contact.

### On your Mac

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings, then click [your name].

- Click Sign-In & Security, then click Recovery Contacts.

[Image: Mac showing System Settings and how to add a Recovery Contact.]

- Click Add Recovery Contact, then follow the onscreen steps.

- Choose one of the recommended contacts. Or you can click Choose Someone Else to add a different person. Then click Continue.

[Image: Mac showing contacts you can add as a Recovery Contact.]

- If you select someone in your Family Sharing group, they’re added automatically. If you choose someone else, they need to accept the invitation in the Messages app.

- After they accept your request, you’ll see a message that they’ve been added as your account recovery contact.

If your friend or family member declines or removes themself as your recovery contact, you’ll receive a notification. You can select a new account recovery contact.

## How to get a recovery code from your recovery contact

- If you forget your Apple Account password and can’t reset it, [start the account recovery process](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118574) .

- After verifying some information for account recovery, you’ll need to reach out to your recovery contact in person or on the phone.

- Then your recovery contact can [get a six-digit recovery code for you](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102608) . After you enter that code on your own device, you can reset your Apple Account password.
