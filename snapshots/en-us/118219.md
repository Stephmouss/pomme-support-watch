# Change or remove the payment cards that you use with Apple Pay

Manage your payment cards by changing your default card used for Apple Pay in-app and online purchases, or removing cards from Apple Wallet entirely.

## Change your default card

The first payment card that you add to Apple Wallet on a device becomes your default card for that device. You can also change your default card when you [add more cards](https://support.apple.com/108398) .

### How to change the default card for Apple Pay on your iPhone

- Open the Wallet app.

- Touch and hold the payment card that you want to set as your default card.

- Drag the card in front of your other cards.

### How to change the default card when making a purchase

- After tapping the Apple Pay button at checkout, tap Change Payment Method or Other Cards & Pay Later Options.

- On the Payment Options screen, tap the Info button next to the card you want to set as default.

- Tap Set as Default Card.

You can also change your default card by tapping the card hub at checkout and following the steps above.

### How to change the default card for Apple Pay on your Apple Watch

- On your iPhone, open the Apple Watch app.

- In the My Watch tab, tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Tap Apple Pay Defaults, then tap Default Card.

- Tap a card to set it as your default card.

### How to change the default card for Apple Pay on your Mac, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- On your device, open Wallet & Apple Pay settings.

- On your Mac with Touch ID, go to System Settings (or System Preferences), then select Wallet & Apple Pay.

- On your iPad or Vision Pro, go to Settings, then tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Select a new default card.

- On your Mac with Touch ID, choose a card from the menu.

- On your iPad or Vision Pro, scroll down to Transaction Defaults, then tap Default Card and choose a card.

## Remove a card from your device

You can remove a debit or credit card from your device. If you need help removing a card, you can also contact your card issuer.

### How to remove a payment card on your iPhone

- In the Wallet app, tap the card that you want to remove.

- Tap the More button , then tap Card Details . *

[Image: the card details icon]

- Scroll down and tap Remove Card.

* In iOS 15 and earlier, you don't see the option to tap Card Details.

### How to remove a payment card on your iPad or Apple Vision Pro

- Go to Settings, then tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Tap the card that you want to remove.

- Tap Remove Card.

### How to remove a payment card on your Apple Watch

- On your Apple Watch, tap the Wallet app.

- Tap the card that you want to remove.

- Scroll down and tap Remove.

### How to remove a payment card on your Mac with Touch ID

- Go to System Settings (or System Preferences), then go to Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Click the card that you want to remove.

- Click the Remove button .

If you need to remove a card and your device is lost or stolen, you can [mark your device as lost](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/use-lost-mode-mmfc0f0165/icloud) , which automatically turns off Apple Pay on your device. For support with your payment cards, contact your card issuer.

To use Apple Pay you need a supported card from a participating card issuer. To check if your card is compatible with Apple Pay, contact your card issuer.
