# How to be removed from the InSight feature in the Apple TV app

Email us if you want to request a correction or opt out of the InSight feature.

If you are an individual appearing in content on the Apple TV app and do not want your name and image to appear dynamically as a part of the InSight feature during video playback, or otherwise would like to request a correction to your name or image in the InSight feature, please email [InSightRequest@apple.com](mailto:InSightRequest@apple.com) .
