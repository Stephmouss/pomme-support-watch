# About the Apple USB-C Digital AV Multiport Adapter

Connect a Mac that has a Thunderbolt 4, Thunderbolt 3, or USB-C port to an HDMI display, while also charging your Mac and connecting to a USB-A device. iPad models that have a Thunderbolt or USB-C port can also use this adapter.

[Image: Apple digital AV multiport adapter top view and ports]

The [USB-C Digital AV Multiport Adapter](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MUF82AM/A/usb-c-digital-av-multiport-adapter) connects the following devices to a display that uses an HDMI cable:

- [Mac that has a Thunderbolt 4, Thunderbolt 3, or USB-C port](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201736)

- [iPad that has a Thunderbolt or USB-C port](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201471)

This adapter also connects your Mac or iPad to USB-A devices, and it includes a USB-C port for charging your Mac laptop or iPad. All three ports can be used simultaneously.

## Connect to a display with HDMI

[Image: HDMI port]

Use the HDMI port of this adapter with a display, TV, or projector that connects using an HDMI cable. It's HDCP compliant and supports the specifications below, depending on the model of the adapter.

Model A2119 is the newest model of this adapter. It supports these resolutions and refresh rates when used with macOS Mojave 10.14.6 or later or iOS 12.4 or later.

3840 x 2160 at 60Hz with these devices:

- 13-inch [MacBook Pro](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201300) with Apple silicon introduced in 2020 or later

- 13-inch [MacBook Pro](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201300) with four Thunderbolt 3 ports introduced in 2020

- [MacBook Air](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201862) introduced in 2020 or later

- Retina [iMac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201634) introduced in 2017 or later

- iMac Pro

- [Mac mini](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201894) introduced in 2018 or later

- [Mac Studio](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT213073) introduced in 2022 or later

3840 x 2160 at 30Hz with these devices:

Up to 1080p at 60Hz

Model A1621 supports these resolutions and refresh rates when used with macOS Sierra 10.12 or later or iOS 12 or later:

- 3840 x 2160 at 30Hz

- Up to 1080p at 60Hz

After connecting your display, TV, or projector, video from your Mac or iPad should appear within 10 seconds.

## How to identify your adapter

- The model number is printed on the retail box and the side of the adapter, near the ports.

- When the adapter is connected to a Mac, you can also check the USB section of [System Information](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT203001) . The Product Identifier for A2119 is 0x1463. For A1621, it's 0x1460.

## Learn more

If you have issues with the adapter, unplug it, then make sure that you've plugged all cables into the adapter before plugging the adapter into your Mac or iPad.
