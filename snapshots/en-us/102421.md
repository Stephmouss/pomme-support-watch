# How to erase an eSIM on your iPhone or iPad

If you transferred an eSIM's data plan or phone number to another device, erase the eSIM. Also, if you are going to sell, trade in, or give away your device, erase the eSIM.

- Don't erase an eSIM as a troubleshooting step unless directed to by your carrier.

- If you erase an eSIM, you'll need to contact your carrier to get a new one. Then [set up the new eSIM](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119606) .

- If you want to cancel your cellular plan, contact your carrier.

## Erase an eSIM on your iPhone

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Cellular.

- If you have a single plan, tap Delete eSIM.

- If you have multiple plans, tap the plan that you want to erase. Then tap Delete Plan.

## Erase an eSIM on your iPad

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Cellular Data.

- If you have a single plan, tap Delete eSIM.

- If you have multiple plans, tap the plan you want to erase. Then tap Delete Plan.

If you [erase content and settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108931) from your iPhone or iPad, you can choose to erase your eSIM or keep it.

## If you erase an eSIM on a device using iPhone Handoff

If you erase an eSIM that is set up on a device using iPhone Handoff, you must contact your carrier to turn off iPhone Handoff for that eSIM. Your carrier may continue to charge you until you request them to turn off the service.
