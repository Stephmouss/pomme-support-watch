# Locate and manage backups of your iPhone, iPad, and iPod touch

Find where your Mac or PC stores iPhone and iPad backups. Then delete or archive a backup in the Finder or Apple Devices app.

[Locate backups stored on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108809#locate-mac)

[Locate backups stored on your PC](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108809#locate-pc)

[Manage your backups](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108809#manage-backups)

## Locate backups stored on your Mac

- From the Go menu in the Finder menu bar, choose Go to Folder.

- Paste or type ~/Library/Application Support/MobileSync/Backup/ in the text field, then press Return.

## Locate backups stored on your PC

- Press the Windows key + R.

- Type %USERPROFILE%\Apple\MobileSync\Backup , then press Enter.

- If the folder doesn't open, type %AppData%\Apple Computer\MobileSync\Backup instead.

## Manage your backups

After you delete a backup, you can't recover it. If you want to keep a backup, archive it so that later backups don't replace it.

- Open the Finder or the [Apple Devices](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) app.

- Connect your device to your computer with a USB cable.

- If your device isn't selected, click it.

- If prompted, tap Trust on your device and enter your passcode.

- Click the General tab, then click Manage Backups.

- Right-click the backup that you want.

- Choose Delete, Archive, or Show In Finder.

[Image: The Device backups dialog in the Finder with a backup selected and its shortcut menu open.]

- After you finish, click OK.

If your device doesn't appear, see [Fix a device that doesn't appear](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108643) .

On a PC, the third option is Show in Explorer.
