# How to clean your nano-texture Apple display

Learn how to clean dust and smudges from your nano-texture display.

## Find out which devices have a nano-texture display

iPhone Duo comes with a nano-texture cover layer on the inner display. These devices can have a nano-texture display as an option:

- Apple Studio Display

- Apple Pro Display XDR

- iMac

- MacBook Pro

- iPad Pro (M4)

## Clean the nano-texture display

The nano-texture display differs for each Apple product, but the cleaning methods are the same. You can remove dust or smudges from the nano-texture display with any of these:

- A clean Apple Polishing Cloth

- A different microfiber cloth made for electronic displays

- A fabric made from a blend of cotton and Elastane or SPANDEX.

While some Apple products come with a microfiber polishing cloth, you can also buy an [Apple Polishing Cloth](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/mlh74am/a/polishing-cloth) or a third-party microfiber polishing cloth.

For infrequent cleaning of hard-to-remove smudges, you can moisten the cloth with a 70-percent isopropyl alcohol (IPA) solution.

[Learn more about cleaning iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108765)

## Clean your polishing cloth

- Hand-wash your polishing cloth with dish soap and water.

- Rinse thoroughly.

- Allow the polishing cloth to air dry for at least 24 hours.
