# Switch to a different subscription plan

You can switch your Apple subscription to a different type, such as a monthly plan, annual plan, or student plan.

## Switch your subscription plan

## Switch your subscription plan on the web

- Go to [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/account/manage/section/subscriptions) and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Select the subscription that you want to change.

- Select See All Plans. If the option to manage the subscription on the web isn't available, select Continue on Device.

- Follow the onscreen instructions to complete your purchase.

You can also [cancel your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) . If your new subscription includes Family Sharing, learn how to [add a family member to your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108107) .

## Switch your subscription plan on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- In Settings, tap your name.

- Tap Subscriptions.

- Tap the subscription that you want to change.

- Tap See All Plans, then follow the onscreen instructions to complete your purchase.

[Image: An iPhone 17 Pro showing an Apple One subscription, with the option to See All Plans.]

You can also [cancel your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) . If your new subscription includes Family Sharing, learn how to [add a family member to your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108107) .

## Switch your subscription plan on your Mac

- In the App Store, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Or, click the Sign In button and sign in to your Apple Account.

- On the page that appears, click Subscriptions.

[Image: The Apple Account page in the App Store on Mac showing the Subscriptions option.]

- Click the subscription that you want to change.

- Click See All Plans. Then, follow the instructions on the screen to complete your purchase.

You can also [cancel your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) . If your new subscription includes Family Sharing, learn how to [add a family member to your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108107) .

## Switch your subscription plan on a Windows PC

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Or, click Sign In and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click View My Account. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account first.

- Scroll to Subscriptions, then click Manage.

[Image: The Windows OS showing the Apple Music app with the option to manage subscriptions highlighted.]

- Next to the subscription that you want to change, click Edit.

- Choose a different subscription plan, then follow the onscreen instructions.

You can also [cancel your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) . If your new subscription includes Family Sharing, learn how to [add a family member to your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108107) .
