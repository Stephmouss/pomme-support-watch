# Get your Mac ready for service

To save time and protect your information, complete as many of these steps as you can before your Mac is serviced.

## 1. Make a backup

[Back up your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102307)

Having a backup is important in case it becomes necessary to erase or replace your computer's startup disk during service.

## 2. Arrange for service

[Get service for your Mac](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase)

Depending on availability in your area, you can make a reservation at an Apple Store, take your Mac to an Apple Authorized Service Provider, or set up mail-in service. If you're participating in an [Apple Service Program](https://support.apple.com/service-programs) , you should also follow any additional steps required by that program.

## 3. Check your settings

Complete as many of these steps as possible to help ensure that the service technician can perform any necessary diagnostic testing. If you can’t complete these steps, the technician might need to discuss options with you.

- [Turn off Find My Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/remove-devices-and-items-from-find-my-mmdc23b125f6/icloud) . If you can’t use your Mac to turn off Find My, use another device to remove your Mac from iCloud on [iCloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find) .

- If your [Mac with Apple silicon](https://support.apple.com/en-us/116943) and [macOS Tahoe 26 or later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033) was previously repaired, or you’re not sure, [use Repair Assistant to check for an unfinished repair](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123128) .

- If your Intel-based Mac has been set up with a firmware password, [turn off the firmware password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102384) , if possible. This does not apply to Mac computers with Apple silicon.

- If you arranged for mail-in service, follow any additional instructions that you receive from Apple.

If you're concerned about the security of your data during service, either [turn on FileVault](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/protect-data-on-your-mac-with-filevault-mh11785/mac) or remove your data, such as by [erasing all content and settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102664) .

## 4. Gather your information

If you're carrying your Mac to a service location:

- Bring the [sales receipt](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102264) with you, if you have it. You might also need your Apple Account password and a form of personal identification, such as a driver license, passport, or other government-issued ID.

- Make sure that you know the name and password of your macOS user account, in case you need to log in to your Mac at the service location.

## 5. Gather your equipment

- If you're carrying your Mac to a service location, also bring its power cable and any accessories that you need help with.

- If you arranged for mail-in service, follow the packaging and mailing instructions you received.
