# About Photomator

Learn more about Photomator for Mac and iPad.

## Photomator subscription or license

Photomator for macOS, iOS, iPadOS, or visionOS is available exclusively [from the App Store](https://www.pixelmator.com/photomator/buy/index.html) . You can sign up for a monthly or yearly subscription or you can purchase a lifetime license.

[Learn how to change, restore, cancel, and get a refund for your Photomator subscription](https://support.pixelmator.com/photomator-user-guide/manage-your-photomator-subscription)

## Photomator Trial

To begin your free, full-featured 7-day trial, download Photomator from the App Store, then sign up for a monthly or yearly subscription using your Apple Account. You can cancel your subscription at any time.

If you downloaded Photomator for free during a brief promotional period, you have to [subscribe to or purchase a license for Photomator](https://www.pixelmator.com/photomator/buy/index.html) to continue using the app.

## If you already purchased Photomator

If you already purchased Photomator but you can't download the app, you might be signed in to the App Store with a different Apple Account or your App Store region may have changed. To verify this, you can [check your App Store purchase history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212) .

## Family Sharing with Photomator

When you enroll in Family Sharing, family members can download, install, and Photomator on their personal devices.

- To share a Photomator monthly or yearly subscription, make sure the app appears under Family Sharing > Subscription Sharing and is active.

- To share a Photomator lifetime license, make sure that the app appears under Family Sharing > Purchase sharing and is active.

[Learn how to share apps and purchases with your family](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108774)

## Features and system requirements

Photomator for Mac requires macOS Ventura 13 or later and is fully optimized for Apple silicon. Photomator for iPhone or iPad requires iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later. Photomator for Apple Vision Pro requires visionOS 1.0 or later.

## Photomator compared to Pixelmator Pro

For fast photo-editing workflows with the ability for batch editing, use Photomator. To create designs from scratch by combining images, shapes, or text, use Pixelmator Pro.

Photomator and Pixelmator Pro have been designed to work together. Both apps have been designed for seamless edit-syncing through iCloud, which makes it easy to round-trip workflows between apps. For example, edit a photo in Photomator, save it to iCloud, then open the file in Pixelmator Pro to add text, shapes, captions, and more.

Layered PXD files (the non-destructive [Pixelmator Pro file format](https://support.pixelmator.com/pixelmator-pro-user-guide/create-open-and-save-images/about-the-pixelmator-pro-file-format) ) open flattened in Photomator.
