# If you get an "iCloud Data Not Syncing" message

If your iCloud data isn't syncing, check whether you have any badges in Settings. Then follow the instructions on your device.

- Open Settings on your device.

- Check if there are any badges.

- Tap or click the badge, then follow the onscreen instructions.

- If prompted, authenticate your account.

If the "iCloud Data Not Syncing" message still appears after authenticating your account, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
