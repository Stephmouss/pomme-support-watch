# Finish setting up your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch

When you move your content to a new device, you might need to complete a few steps to finish the process.

## Check Mail, Contacts, and Calendar settings

[Image: Mail app icon on iOS]

Mail: Launch the Mail app and wait for your email to download to your device. If prompted, enter the password for your email again. If you need to set up your email, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102556) .

[Image: Contacts app icon on iOS]

Contacts: Open the Contacts app and make sure that your contacts transferred. If you're missing any contacts, [learn how to set up and manage your Contacts](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111793) .

[Image: iOS Calendar app icon]

Calendar: Open the Calendar app and make sure that your calendar events transferred. You can also [use iCloud to keep your Calendar up to date](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/what-you-can-do-with-icloud-and-calendar-mm15eb200ab4/1.0/icloud/1.0) .

## Turn on notifications for your apps

You might need to open some of your apps to enable the notifications for those apps. Open each app and if prompted, tap Allow Notifications. For some apps, you might need to turn on your notifications by going to Settings and tapping Notifications.

[Learn more about Notifications](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108781) .

## Download content and restore in-app purchases

You might need to [redownload the content](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102417) , such as music, movies, TV shows, or podcasts that you had on your previous device:

- Go to Settings.

- Tap your name.

- Tap Media & Purchases.

- Tap View Account. Make sure that you're [signed in with the same Apple Account that you use to make purchases.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001)

If you're missing music, movies, TV shows, or podcasts, [learn how to redownload the missing content](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102417) .

If you previously made in-app purchases, [you need to restore them](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108096) .

## Pair your Bluetooth accessories

After you set up your new device, you need to pair your Bluetooth accessories, like headphones and speakers, and connect your device to your car stereo:

- Go to Settings.

- Tap Bluetooth and make sure that Bluetooth is turned on.

- Put your accessory in Discovery Mode and wait for it to show up on your device.

- Tap the accessory name to pair the accessory. You might need to enter a PIN or passcode. Learn more about pairing Bluetooth accessories.

Learn how to [pair your AirPods](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104989) .

## Add your cards to Apple Pay

You can use Apple Pay as soon as you add your credit, debit, or prepaid card to Wallet. Remember to add your cards to any device that you want to use with Apple Pay.

- Go to Settings.

- Tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Add your card and wait for your bank to verify your information. If your bank needs more information to verify your card, they'll ask for it.

Learn how to [set up and use Apple Pay.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108398)

## Manage your subscriptions

Some apps and services offer subscriptions to access their content. These subscriptions will automatically renew until you choose to cancel the subscription. If you have any subscriptions, [learn how to view, change, or cancel them](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) .
