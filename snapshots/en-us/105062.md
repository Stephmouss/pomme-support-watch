# How Family Sharing works

With Family Sharing, you and up to five other family members can share access to Apple services like iCloud+, Apple Music, Apple TV, and more. You can even help locate each other’s missing devices, share your location, and manage a child's device with Screen Time and parental controls.

[Image: Photos of family members and Apple service icons demonstrate Family Sharing connections.]

## How to get started with Family Sharing

To set up Family Sharing, one adult — the family organizer — invites the other family members. When members join, they get access to the Family Sharing group’s subscriptions and content, as long as it's eligible for sharing. Each member uses their own Apple Account, so everyone’s experience is personalized and private.

[Learn how to set up Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/108380)

## How subscription and purchase sharing work

When you set up Family Sharing, eligible subscriptions can be shared with the entire family group, and some subscriptions are shared automatically. However, some subscriptions — like iCloud+ — require additional steps in order to share with your family members.

[Learn how to share iCloud+ with your Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/108783)

When you share other subscriptions like Apple Music, Apple Fitness+, or Apple TV, each person sees their own preferences and recommendations. If you want to keep certain purchases private, you can [hide individual items](https://support.apple.com/108091) .

[Learn how to add a family member to your subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/108107)

When you turn on Purchase Sharing, everyone in the Family Sharing group gets access to the apps, music, movies, TV shows, and books that family members buy. The content should automatically appear on the Purchased page in the App Store, iTunes Store, Apple Books, or Apple TV app. Some items can't be shared.

[Learn how to turn on Purchase Sharing](https://support.apple.com/108774)

## How to manage a child's device with Family Sharing and parental controls

The Family Sharing organizer or a parent/guardian, can create a Child Account for children in their Family Sharing group. With their own Child Account, a child can use their own device — and even make their own purchasing decisions. With Screen Time and parental controls, the organizer or parent/guardian can easily manage the child's device to help keep their experience safe, private, and age appropriate. A Child Account is required for children under 13 and available for children up to 18. *

[Learn how to create a Child Account for your child](https://support.apple.com/102617)

### Set up a device for your child

With Family Sharing, as the organizer, you can use Quick Start to set up a new iPhone or iPad for your child and customize Screen Time and parental controls. You can also set up a device for your child without using Quick Start.

[Learn how to set up your child's iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/118660)

### Update your child's birthday and how they share their age with apps

As the Family Sharing organizer, you can update the birthday that's associated with your child's Apple Account. You can also allow them to share their age range with certain apps to help provide an age-appropriate experience within the app.

- [Learn how to update the birthday associated with your child's Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/102473)

- [Learn how to turn on Share Age Range with Apps](https://support.apple.com/122770)

### Use Screen Time and parental controls to approve requests and manage settings

With parental controls, you can manage the downloads and purchases your child makes, who they communicate with, the apps and content they access, and more. With Screen Time, there are additional settings and parental controls you can use to ensure your child's device is safe and age appropriate.

[Learn more about using parental controls to manage your child's devices](https://support.apple.com/105121)

### Manage Apple Cash for your child

With Apple Cash Family**, your child can send or receive money in Messages and Wallet and make purchases with Apple Pay. Parents can set up an allowance using recurring payments or send money directly to children. They can also see their child's transaction history and limit their spending. Family members that are under 18 can also participate in Apple Cash Family.

[Learn how to set up up Apple Cash Family](https://support.apple.com/105010)

## How to share locations and find devices

To help you stay coordinated, people in your Family Sharing group can share locations. And you can help other family members find their devices if they're missing.

### Keep track of where everybody is

With Family Sharing, you can use the Find My app to share your location automatically with your family. If you need privacy, you can temporarily stop sharing your location.

[Learn how to share your location with your family](https://support.apple.com/105107)

### Find a missing device

Your family members' devices appear in the Find My app. This allows you to locate it, mark it as lost to protect the device and personal information, or erase it if you believe it's permanently lost or stolen.

[Learn how to locate a missing device or item with Find My](https://support.apple.com/104978)
