# How Family Sharing works

With Family Sharing, you and up to five other family members can share access to Apple services like iCloud+, Apple Music, Apple TV, and more. You can even help locate each other’s missing devices, share your location, and manage a child's device with Screen Time and parental controls.

[Image: Five smiling family members are shown with icons of iCloud, photos, Apple TV, and other Apple products and services.]

## How to get started with Family Sharing

To set up Family Sharing, one adult in your household — the family organizer — invites the other family members. When members join, they get access to the Family Sharing group’s subscriptions and content, as long as it's eligible for sharing. Each member uses their own Apple Account, so everyone’s experience is personalized and private.

[Learn how to set up Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/108380)

## How subscription and purchase sharing work

When you set up Family Sharing, eligible subscriptions can be shared with the entire Family Sharing group, and some subscriptions are shared automatically. However, some subscriptions — like iCloud+ — require additional steps in order to share with your family members.

[Learn how to share iCloud+ with your Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/108783)

When you share other subscriptions like Apple Music, Apple Fitness+, or Apple TV, each person sees their own preferences and recommendations. If you want to keep certain purchases private, you can [hide individual items](https://support.apple.com/108091) .

[Learn how to add a family member to your subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/108107)

When you turn on purchase sharing, everyone in the Family Sharing group gets access to the apps, music, movies, TV shows, and books that family members buy. The content should automatically appear on the Purchased page in the App Store, iTunes Store, Apple Books, or Apple TV app. Some items can't be shared.

[Learn how to turn on purchase sharing](https://support.apple.com/108774)

## How to manage a child's device with Family Sharing

Parents and guardians can create an Apple Account for children in their Family Sharing group that are under 13.* With their own Apple Account, a child can use their own device — and even make their own purchasing decisions. With Screen Time and parental controls, a parent or guardian can easily manage the child's device to help keep their experience safe, private, and age appropriate.

[Learn how to create an Apple Account for your child](https://support.apple.com/102617)

### Set up a device for your child

With Family Sharing, as the organizer, you can use Quick Start to set up a new iPhone or iPad for your child and customize Screen Time and parental controls. You can also set up a device for your child without using Quick Start.

[Learn how to set up your child's iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/118660)

### Approve what your child buys or downloads

With Ask to Buy, you can see and approve what your child purchases and downloads. When a child asks to buy apps, movies, or other content, the family organizer receives a notification and can approve or decline it right from their own device.

[Learn how to set up Ask to Buy](https://support.apple.com/105055)

### Limit how and how often your child uses their device

With Screen Time and parental controls, you can monitor how much time your child is on their device using apps, visiting websites, and more. You can review Screen Time reports, and set limits and restrictions right from your own device.

[Learn how to set up Screen Time for your child's iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-screen-time-iphbfa595995/ios)

[Learn how to use parental controls on your child's iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/105121)

### Update your child's birth date and how they share their age with apps

As the family organizer, you can update the birth date that's associated with your child's Apple Account. You can also allow them to share their age range with certain apps to help provide an age-appropriate experience within the app.

[Learn how to update your child's birth date associated with their Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/102473)

[Learn how to turn on Share Age Range with Apps](https://support.apple.com/122770)

### Manage Apple Cash for your child

With Apple Cash Family**, your child or teen can send or receive money in Messages and Wallet and make purchases with Apple Pay. Parents can set up an allowance using recurring payments or send money directly to children. They can also see their child's transaction history and limit their spending. Family members that are under 18 can also participate in Apple Cash Family.

[Learn how to set up up Apple Cash Family](https://support.apple.com/105010)

## How to share locations and find devices

To help you stay coordinated, people in your Family Sharing group can share locations. And you can help other family members find their devices if they're missing.

### Keep track of where everybody is

With Family Sharing, you can use the Find My app to share your location automatically with your family. If you need privacy, you can temporarily stop sharing your location.

[Learn how to share your location with your family](https://support.apple.com/105107)

### Find a missing device

Your family members' devices appear in the Find My app. This allows you to locate it, mark it as lost to protect the device and personal information, or erase it if you believe it's permanently lost or stolen.

[Learn how to locate a missing device or item with Find My](https://support.apple.com/104978)
