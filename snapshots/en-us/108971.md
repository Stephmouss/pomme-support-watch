# Share analytics, diagnostics, and usage information with Apple

Apple asks customers to help improve iOS by occasionally providing analytics, diagnostic, and usage information. Apple collects this information anonymously.

## iOS 10 and later

If you have iOS 10.3 or later, go to Settings, tap Privacy & Security, then select Analytics or Analytics & Improvements. Then tap Share iPhone & Watch Analytics.

[Image: iPhone screen showing the Analytics & Improvements page.]

If you have iOS 10 to iOS 10.2, go to Settings, tap Privacy, tap Diagnostics & Usage, then select Automatically Send or Don't Send.

[Learn how your data is managed](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/device-analytics/)

## iOS 8 and iOS 9

Go to Settings, tap Privacy, tap Diagnostics & Usage, then select Automatically Send or Don't Send.

When you turn on Automatically Send, you can also turn on Share With App Developers. This helps developers improve their apps by allowing Apple to share data, as well as statistics about how you use their apps.

## iOS 5, iOS 6, and iOS 7

Go to Settings, tap General, tap About, tap Diagnostics & Usage, then select Automatically Send or Don't Send.
