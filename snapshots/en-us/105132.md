# Set up your iPhone or iPad

Learn how to set up your iPhone or iPad.

If you already set up your new device but want to start over, [learn how to erase your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/108931) .

## Turn on your iPhone or iPad

Press and hold the device's power button until you see the Apple logo. You'll then see "Hello" in many languages. Follow the onscreen instructions to get started.

If you're blind or have low vision, you can [turn on VoiceOver or Zoom from the Hello screen](https://support.apple.com/111780) . To enable VoiceOver during setup, press the side button or top button three times. Then wait for the announcement that VoiceOver is on.

To enable Zoom, tap the screen two times with three fingers.

[Image: iPhone or iPad displaying the "hello" screen with a wavy abstract background.]

## Choose the size of text and icons on your phone

Select your preferred size for text and icons: Default, Medium, or Large. As you choose between those sizes, the text and images on this Appearance screen will change so you can preview the selected option.

When ready, select your preferred size and tap Continue.

[Image: iPhone screen showing appearance options with a wallpaper preview and text size controls.]

## Use Quick Start or set up manually

If you have another iOS device, you can use it to [automatically set up your new device with Quick Start](https://support.apple.com/102659) .

Otherwise, tap Set Up Without Another Device. Then choose whether you want to use languages commonly used in your region for your keyboard and dictation or whether you want to select different written and spoken languages.

If you want to transfer from an Android device and don't have another iOS device, tap Set Up Without Another Device. On the Transfer Apps & Data screen, you can select the option to [move data from your Android device to your new iOS device](https://support.apple.com/118670) .

Here you can also [set up Accessibility Options](https://support.apple.com/111780) .

[Image: An iPhone setup screen shows the Quick Start option in progress.]

## Activate your iPhone or iPad

You need to connect to a Wi-Fi network or a cellular network to activate and continue setting up your device.

Tap the Wi-Fi network that you want to use or, if available, tap Continue Without Wi-Fi to use your device's cellular network. If you're setting up an iPhone or iPad (Wi-Fi + Cellular), you might need to insert your [iPhone SIM card](https://support.apple.com/109357) or [iPad SIM card](https://support.apple.com/102392) first. You might also be asked to [activate your eSIM](https://support.apple.com/118669) .

Get help if you [can't connect to Wi-Fi](https://support.apple.com/111786) or [if you can't activate your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/109326) .

## Set up for you or for a child

Choose whether to set up your iPhone for you or for a child. Learn how to [create a new Apple Account for a child under 13](https://support.apple.com/102617) , how to [set up Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/108380) , and how to [use parental controls on a child's iPhone](https://support.apple.com/105121) .

[Image: iPhone setup screen asking to select the primary user's age range.]

## Set up Face ID or Touch ID and create a passcode

Follow the onscreen instructions to set up [Face ID](https://support.apple.com/108411) or [Touch ID](https://support.apple.com/102528) to use face recognition or your fingerprint to unlock your device and make purchases.

Next, set a six-digit passcode to help protect your data. You need a passcode to use features like Face ID, Touch ID, and [Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/108398) . If you'd like a four-digit passcode, custom passcode, or no passcode, tap Passcode Options.

## Restore or transfer your data and apps

Choose how you want to restore or transfer your data from your old device to your new device. You can [use an iCloud backup or a backup made from your computer](https://support.apple.com/119967) , or [move data from an Android device](https://support.apple.com/118670) .

If you don't have a backup or another device, select Don't Transfer Anything.

[Image: Transfer Your Apps & Data screen shows options to restore from iCloud, Mac or PC, or Android.]

## Sign in with your Apple Account or another Apple device

Enter the email address or phone number that you use with your Apple Account, then enter your password. Or tap "Forgot password or don't have an Apple Account?" From there, you can recover your Apple Account or password, create an Apple Account, or set it up later.

If you use more than one Apple Account, tap Other Sign-In Options, then tap Use Multiple Accounts.

When you sign in with your Apple Account, you might be prompted for a verification code from your previous device.

[Learn what to do if you forgot your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/102656)

[Image: iPhone screen prompts users to sign in to their Apple Account for iCloud setup.]

## Turn on automatic updates and set up other features

Follow the onscreen instructions to allow iOS or iPadOS to update automatically and set up other features, like cellular service and a phone number or [Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/108398) :

- You'll be asked to set up or enable services and features, like Siri. On some devices, you'll be asked to speak a few phrases so that Siri can get to know your voice.

- Tap on Light or Dark to see a preview of how your iPhone adjusts. Tap Auto to have your iPhone automatically switch based on the time of day. When ready, tap Continue.

- Follow the onscreen instructions to set up [Screen Time](https://support.apple.com/108806) , which gives you insight into how much time you and your kids spend on your devices. It also lets you set time limits for daily app use.

- Learn about the data you can share with Apple and choose whether to share data with app developers.

- Adapt the appearance of Liquid Glass to match your preference.

- If your iPhone is an Apple Intelligence capable device, you can set up Apple Intelligence and [learn about features like Visual Intelligence in iOS 26](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-visual-intelligence-iph12eb1545e/26/ios/26) .

- If you set up an iPhone 14 or later (all models), learn about safety features like [Emergency SOS](https://support.apple.com/guide/personal-safety/emergency-call-text-iphone-apple-watch-ips4f0cd709b/web) , [Crash Detection](https://support.apple.com/104959) , and [Emergency SOS via Satellite](https://support.apple.com/105097) in select countries.
