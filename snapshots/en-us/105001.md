# Turn the flashlight on or off on your iPhone or iPad Pro

The LED flash on your iPhone or iPad Pro doubles as a flashlight, so you can get extra light when you need it.

There are a few ways you can turn your flashlight on or off:

- You can [ask Siri](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105001#siri) .

- You can [use Control Center](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105001#control-center) .

- You can [use the Action button](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105001#action-button) .

## Ask Siri

Here are a few examples of how you can use Siri to turn on your flashlight:

- "Hey Siri, turn on my flashlight."

- "Hey Siri, will you turn on my flashlight?"

- "Hey Siri, turn off my flashlight."

## Use Control Center to turn the flashlight off or on

- Swipe down from the upper-right corner to [open Control Center on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/108330) or [iPad](https://support.apple.com/111790) . On an iPhone with a Home button, swipe up from the bottom to open Control Center.

- Tap the Flashlight button .

[Image: Flashlight icon]

[Image: Access the flashlight in Control Center on iOS 26.]

- To turn off the flashlight, tap the Flashlight button again.

[Image: Flashlight icon]

You can also turn on the flashlight from the Lock screen: Press and hold the Flashlight button , in the bottom-left corner.

If you don't see the Flashlight button when you open Control Center, you can change your settings to add it. Go to Settings > Control Center, then tap Flashlight in the list of available controls. Or, tap and hold the screen when Control Center is open to [customize the available controls](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-and-customize-control-center-iph59095ec58/ios) .

[Image: Flashlight icon]

## Use the Action button to turn the flashlight on or off

- In the Settings app, tap Action Button.

- Swipe to the Flashlight icon.

[Image: On an iPhone with the Action button, you can assign the flashlight to that button.]

- Press and hold the Action button to turn the flashlight on or off.

The Action button is available on iPhone 15 Pro and iPhone 15 Pro Max and later models.

[Learn more about the Action button](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphe89d61d66/ios)

## Change the brightness of the flashlight

- On an iPhone with Face ID or an iPad, swipe down from the upper-right corner to open Control Center. Or on an iPhone with a Home button, swipe up from the bottom edge of the screen to open Control Center.

- Press and hold the Flashlight button .

[Image: Flashlight icon]

- Drag the slider up or down.

## Change the size of your flashlight beam with Dynamic Island

- Turn on your flashlight.

- Tap the Dynamic Island.

- Touch and hold the Light icon, then drag it to change the width and length of your flashlight beam.

[Image: With the Dynamic Island, you can widen the beam of your flashlight in iOS 26.]

[The Dynamic Island](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-the-dynamic-island-iph28f50d10d/ios) is available on iPhone 14 Pro and later Pro models.

[Learn what to do if the flashlight isn't working on your device](https://support.apple.com/102514)
