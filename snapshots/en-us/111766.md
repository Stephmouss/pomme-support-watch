# Bundle Apple subscriptions with Apple One

With Apple One, bundle Apple Music, Apple TV, Apple Arcade, iCloud+, and more into one plan. With a Family plan or Premier plan, you can share it all with your family.

## How to subscribe to Apple One on your iPhone or iPad

- In Settings, tap your name.

- Tap Subscriptions.

- Tap Apple One.

- Choose a plan.

### If you don't see the option to subscribe to Apple One on your iPhone or iPad

[Update your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , then go to Settings and look for the option again.

## How to subscribe to Apple One on your Mac

- Go to System Preferences.

- Click Apple Account.

- Click Media & Purchases.

- Next to Subscriptions, click Manage.

## If you already subscribe to an Apple service included with Apple One

- You won’t lose your data or customized content if you switch from your individual service subscriptions to Apple One.

- Existing subscriptions to services included in your Apple One plan (except iCloud+) will be automatically canceled when you're billed for Apple One.

- You’ll receive a prorated refund for any individual services you’re already subscribed to (except iCloud+). If you receive a trial of Apple One, this happens at the end of your trial.

- If Apple One costs less than your existing Apple service subscriptions combined, you will be transitioned to the Apple One subscription without a free trial.

### If you already have an iCloud storage plan

If you have an existing iCloud+ plan that has more storage than your Apple One plan, you’ll be charged separately and your storage will be combined. Learn [what happens to your iCloud+ plan when you join Apple One](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108104) .

## What's included with Apple One

Apple One includes a free 1-month trial for services that you don’t already have. If you have existing subscriptions to services included with Apple One, they will not be included in your trial.

After your free trial, services that you previously subscribed to are automatically included in Apple One. If you recently canceled a service, you might not be eligible for the free trial. Your plan automatically renews after the trial.

[See prices](https://www.apple.com/apple-one/)

### Individual

- Apple Music

- Apple TV

- Apple Arcade

- iCloud+ with 50GB of storage

Apple Music can't be shared with members in your Family Sharing group using this plan. You can always share Apple TV, iCloud+, and Apple Arcade with family.

### Family

- Apple Music

- Apple TV

- Apple Arcade

- iCloud+ with 200GB of storage

Share them all with up to five other family members.

### Premier

- Apple Music

- Apple TV

- Apple Arcade

- Apple News+ (if available in your region)

- Apple Fitness+ (if available and included with Apple One in your region)

- iCloud+ with 2TB of storage

Share them all with up to five other family members.

[See if Apple One is available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205)
