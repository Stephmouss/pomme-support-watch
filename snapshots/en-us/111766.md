# How to subscribe to Apple One

With Apple One, bundle Apple Music, Apple TV, Apple Arcade, iCloud+, and more into one plan. Choose a Family or Premier plan to share services with up to five people.

[If you already subscribe to an Apple service included with Apple One](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111766#users)

[What's included with Apple One](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111766#bundle)

## Subscribe to Apple One on your device

You can subscribe to Apple One on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

### Subscribe to Apple One on your iPhone or iPad

- In Settings, tap your name.

- Tap Subscriptions.

- Tap Apple One, then choose a plan.

#### If you don't see the option to subscribe to Apple One

[Update your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , then go to Settings and look for the option again.

### Subscribe to Apple One on your Mac

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings.

- Click your name. Or, click Sign In and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Media & Purchases.

- Next to Subscriptions, click Manage.

- Click Apple One, then choose a plan.

## If you already subscribe to an Apple service included with Apple One

- You won’t lose your data or customized content if you switch from your individual service subscriptions to Apple One.

- Existing subscriptions to services included in your Apple One plan (except iCloud+) will be automatically canceled when you're billed for Apple One.

- You’ll receive a prorated refund for any individual services you’re already subscribed to (except iCloud+). If you receive a trial of Apple One, this happens at the end of your trial.

- If Apple One costs less than your existing Apple service subscriptions combined, you will be transitioned to the Apple One subscription without a trial.

### If you already have an iCloud storage plan

If you have an existing iCloud+ plan that has more storage than your Apple One plan, you’ll be charged separately and your storage will be combined. Learn [what happens to your iCloud+ plan when you join Apple One](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108104) .

## What's included with Apple One

Apple One availability and features vary by country or region. Check [Apple One availability and features in your country or region](https://www.apple.com/apple-one/) .

Apple One includes a 1-month trial for services that you don’t already have. If you have existing subscriptions to services included with Apple One, they won't be included in your trial.

After your trial, services that you previously subscribed to are automatically included in Apple One. If you recently canceled a service, you might not be eligible for the trial. Your plan automatically renews after the trial.

[See Apple One prices](https://www.apple.com/apple-one/)

If Apple One isn’t available to new subscribers in your country or region, some Apple services might be included with an iCloud+ subscription. See [what Apple services are included with iCloud+ in your country or region](https://apple.co/icloud-plus-tv-arcade) .

### Individual

- Apple Music

- Apple TV

- Apple Arcade

- iCloud+ with 50GB of storage

Apple Music can't be shared with members in your Family Sharing group using this plan. You can always share Apple TV, iCloud+, and Apple Arcade with family.

### Family

- Apple Music

- Apple TV

- Apple Arcade

- iCloud+ with 200GB of storage

Share them all with up to five other family members.

### Premier

- Apple Music

- Apple TV

- Apple Arcade

- Apple News+ (if available in your region)

- Apple Fitness+ (if available and included with Apple One in your region)

- iCloud+ with 2TB of storage

Share them all with up to five other family members.
