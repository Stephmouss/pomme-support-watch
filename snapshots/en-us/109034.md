# Use audio descriptions in iTunes for Windows

If the video you're watching includes audio descriptions (AD), you can turn them on to have scenes in videos described to you.

## Turn on audio descriptions in iTunes for Windows

- Move your pointer over your video.

- Click the speech bubble icon .

[Image: speech bubble icon]

- Choose the audio-described track that you want to use.

[Image: windows-10-itunes-12-player-audio-subtitles-options]

## Turn on audio descriptions by default

- Open iTunes for Windows.

- From the menu bar, click Edit, then choose Preferences.

- Click Playback.

- Select Play Audio Descriptions When Available.

## Learn more

- Turn on audio descriptions [on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT205796) , or [your Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT205800) or [Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT205799) .

- [Find audio-described content](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT205742) in the Apple TV app or iTunes Store. Not all videos include audio description tracks.
