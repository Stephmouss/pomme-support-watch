# About purchasing movies, TV shows, music, books, and ringtones from Apple

Any digital media you buy from Apple can be permanently downloaded on to an iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Windows PC.

Content you purchase from Apple will generally remain available for you to redownload or otherwise access from Apple. Though it is unlikely, content might be removed from Apple Media Services and become unavailable for further download or access (for instance, because Apple loses its right from the content provider to make it available). To ensure your ability to continue enjoying your movies, TV shows, music, books, and ringtones, download all purchased content to a compatible device in your possession.

You can permanently download purchased content to your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Windows PC. Once downloaded, you can access content without an internet connection, and Apple can’t remove it from your device.

Learn how to redownload your purchased content on an iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Windows PC:

- [Redownload TV shows and movies that you purchased](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119602)

- [Redownload music from the iTunes Store on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118294)

- [Redownload music from the iTunes Store on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120552)

- [Redownload music from the iTunes Store on your Windows PC](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120553)

- [Redownload books and audiobooks](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118416)

- [Use tones and ringtones with your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118402)

Store availability and features might vary by country or region. Learn [what you can buy in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
