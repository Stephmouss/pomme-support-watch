# Use multiple displays with your Mac Pro (2019)

Learn how to connect multiple displays (such as 4K, 5K, and 6K displays) to your Mac Pro (2019) using Thunderbolt 3 and HDMI.

You can connect up to 12 displays to your Mac Pro, depending on the graphics cards installed. To find out which ports to use to connect your displays, choose your graphics card:

- [Radeon Pro 580X MPX Module](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT210319)

- [Radeon Pro W5500X MPX Module](https://support.apple.com/kb/ht210957)

- [Radeon Pro W5700X MPX Module](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT210968)

- [Radeon Pro W6600X MPX Module](https://support.apple.com/kb/ht213098)

- [Radeon Pro W6800X MPX Module](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT212547)

- [Radeon Pro W6900X MPX Module](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT212579)

- [Radeon Pro W6800X Duo MPX Module](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT212546)

- [Radeon Pro Vega II MPX Module](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT210316)

- [Radeon Pro Vega II Duo MPX Module](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT210392)

## Connect displays to the Thunderbolt 3 ports on your Mac Pro

You can connect displays to the HDMI and Thunderbolt 3 ports on your Mac Pro and Radeon Pro MPX Module. Learn about [adapters for the Thunderbolt 3 ports on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT207443) .

To use the Thunderbolt 3 ports on the top* and back of your Mac Pro to connect displays, you must have at least one Radeon Pro MPX Module installed. If a Radeon Pro MPX Module isn't installed, the Thunderbolt 3 ports on your Mac Pro are used only for data and power.

## Starting up your Mac Pro

When you start up your Mac Pro, only one connected display illuminates at first. Any additional displays illuminate after your Mac is finished starting up. If one or more displays don't illuminate after startup completes, make sure your displays and any display adapters are connected properly.

If you use [Boot Camp](https://support.apple.com/boot-camp) and install a third-party graphics card from AMD, you might need to [use different AMD drivers in Windows](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT210560) .
