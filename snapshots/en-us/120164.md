# Get help with charges from apple.com/bill

A charge on your financial statement from apple.com/bill could be for apps, subscriptions, music and movie purchases, or more from Apple.

## View charges or request a refund

Sign in to view what was purchased with your Apple Account, request a refund, and more.

[Learn more](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108092)

If you use Family Sharing and have Purchase Sharing turned on, you can see what you were charged for as the Family organizer. To see family members' purchases, sign in and then tap or click the Apple Account button to choose a family member.

## Cancel subscriptions

If your charge is for a subscription, you can cancel that subscription to prevent future charges.

[Learn more](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428)
