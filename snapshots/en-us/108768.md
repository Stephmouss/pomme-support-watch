# If you can’t send or receive messages on your Apple Watch

If you see an alert or red exclamation point, or you can’t get messages, learn what to do.

## If you see a red exclamation point

If you see a red exclamation point or "not delivered" next to a message, tap the alert to try sending the message again. If the message still won't send, try the steps below.

[Image: exclamation point]

## If you can't send or receive messages

After each step, try to send or receive a message on your Apple Watch again.

## Check Control Center

If you see the [Do Not Disturb or Airplane Mode](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/silence-notifications-for-extended-periods-apd41eadfc95/26/watchos/26#apdad2a285bc) icons on your watch face, you need to turn the settings off in Control Center.

To open Control Center, press the side button. Tap the highlighted Do Not Disturb and Airplane Mode icons to turn them off.

[Image: Apple Watch showing Do Not Disturb and Airplane Mode turned on in Control Center]

## Check your connection

Make sure that your Apple Watch can [connect to your iPhone, cellular, or Wi-Fi](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109319) . To send and receive SMS/MMS from cellular models of Apple Watch, your paired iPhone must be powered on and connected to Wi-Fi or cellular, but your iPhone doesn’t need to be nearby.

[Image: Apple Watch showing Wi-Fi button in Control Center]

Even if your iPhone isn’t connected or powered on, you can send and receive iMessages on your Apple Watch when your Apple Watch is connected to Wi-Fi or cellular.

[Learn how your Apple Watch makes connections](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108300) and [get help if your devices disconnect](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108360) .

## Try sending a message from your iPhone

If your iPhone can't send or receive messages, try to [fix messages on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118433) .

## Make sure that you signed in

On your iPhone, open the Apple Watch app. Tap the My Watch tab, tap General, then tap Apple Account.

[Image: iPhone showing Apple Account screen indicating that the user is signed in to Apple Account]

## Check your iMessage settings

On your iPhone, open the Settings app. Tap Apps, then tap Messages and make sure that iMessage is turned on.

Then tap Send & Receive and make sure that you're using the same Apple Account that your Apple Watch is using.

If you aren't signed in, [sign in to iMessage with your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) .

## Sign out of iMessage

On your iPhone, open the Settings app. Tap Apps, then tap Messages, then tap Send & Receive. Tap your Apple Account, then tap Sign Out.

[Image: iPhone showing iMessage settings screen]

Next, in the Settings app, go back to Apps, then tap Messages. Turn iMessage off, then turn it back on.

## Sign in to iMessage again

On your iPhone, open the Settings app. Tap Apps, then tap Messages, then tap Send & Receive. Tap Use your Apple Account for iMessage, then sign in.

## Contact us

Before you contact us, [unpair your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108372) , then [pair it again](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109015) . If you still can't send or receive messages, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/) .
