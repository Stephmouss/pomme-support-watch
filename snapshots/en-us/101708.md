# GarageBand for iOS (v2.0): Issues playing or recording with Audiobus

Refer to this article if you are unable to play or record from Audiobus in GarageBand for iOS (v2.0).

[Update your iOS device](http://support.apple.com/kb/HT4623) to iOS 7.0.3 or later.
