# If you can't set up your Apple TV 4K or Apple TV HD

Learn what to do if you your Apple TV stops responding during setup, or if you can't get past a screen during setup.

## If you want to use your iPhone to set up your Apple TV

Do these things first on your iPhone:

- Update [to the latest version of iOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- Sign in to iCloud with your Apple Account and turn on [two-factor authentication for your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102660) .

- [Turn on iCloud Keychain in iCloud Settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109016) .

- Turn on Bluetooth and [connect to your home Wi-Fi network](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111107) .

## If your Apple TV stops responding during setup

Unplug your Apple TV from power, then plug it back in and try setting up Apple TV again.

## If you can't get past a screen during setup

Try connecting your Apple TV to a different Wi-Fi network, like a personal hotspot from your phone. After setup is complete, go to Settings on your Apple TV and connect to your home Wi-Fi network.
