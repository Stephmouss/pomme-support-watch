# Availability of two-factor authentication for Apple Account

Learn if you can use two-factor authentication in your country or region.

## About two-factor authentication for your Apple Account

Two-factor authentication is an additional layer of security for your Apple Account, designed to make sure that you’re the only one who can access your account — even if someone else knows your password. Learn more [about two-factor authentication for your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102660) .

While you can turn on two-factor authentication for most accounts, certain account types might be ineligible at Apple’s discretion.
