# Delete your Safari history, cache, and cookies on iPhone

In your iPhone settings, you can choose to delete your Safari history, cookies, cache, or a specific website from your history. You can also turn on content blockers.

- [Delete history, cache, and cookies](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105082#deletehistory)

- [Clear cookies and cache, but keep your history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105082#keephistory)

- [Delete a website from your history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105082#deletewebsite)

- [Block cookies](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105082#blockcookies)

- [Use content blockers](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105082#contentblockers)

## Delete history, cache, and cookies

- Go to Settings, tap Apps, then select Safari.

- Scroll down and tap Clear History and Website Data.

[Image: iPhone screen showing Clear History and Website Data option in Safari Settings.]

Confirm the timeframe that you want to clear, then tap Clear History. This doesn't change your AutoFill information. If this button is gray, there is either no data to clear, or you might need to check [your web content restrictions in Screen Time](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105121) .

[Image: iPhone screen showing All history selected, and option to tap Clear History button.]

## Clear cookies and cache, but keep your history

- Go to Settings, tap Apps, then select Safari. Tap Advanced, then choose Website Data.

- Tap Remove All Website Data. If this button is gray, there is either no data to clear, or you might need to check [your web content restrictions in Screen Time](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105121) .

- Tap Remove Now. This clears data that's used for tracking, and by websites to save login information for faster browsing.

## Delete a website from your history

- Open the Safari app, then tap the More button .

[Image: iPhone screen showing the More button magnified in Safari.]

- Tap Bookmarks.

[Image: iPhone screen showing the Bookmarks button in Safari.]

- Tap the History button , then tap the More button again.

[Image: History button]

[Image: iPhone screen showing the More button magnified in Safari.]

- Tap Select Websites, then select one or more websites to delete from your history.

[Image: iPhone screen showing the History tab selected with a website selected to delete.]

- Tap the Trash button .

## Block cookies

- If you want to block cookies (data a site places on your device to remember you), go to Settings, tap Apps, choose Safari, then tap Advanced.

- Turn on Block All Cookies.

- Tap Block All. This removes all existing cookies and website data. Safari quits, and your tabs are reloaded.

If you block cookies, some webpages might not work. Here are some examples:

- You will likely not be able to sign in to a site, even when using your correct username and password.

- You might see a message that cookies are required or that your browser's cookies are off.

- Some features on a site might not work.

## Use content blockers

- Go to the App Store and download a content blocking app (third-party apps and extensions that let Safari block cookies, images, resources, pop-ups, and other content).

- Then tap Settings, tap Apps, choose Safari, then tap Extensions.

- Tap to turn on a listed content blocker.

You can use more than one content blocker. If you need help, [contact the app developer](https://support.apple.com/102435) .
