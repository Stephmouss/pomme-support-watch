# If you can't see email messages in Mail on Mac

If messages seem to be missing because Mail doesn't show them, try these solutions.

- [Select your mailbox in the sidebar](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117379#sidebar)

- [Check for message filtering](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117379#filtering)

- [Check the email categories setting](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117379#categories)

- [Check the Junk mailbox](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117379#junk)

- [Check for blocked senders](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117379#blocked)

- [Check for mail rules](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117379#rules)

- [Search for messages](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117379#search)

- [Update macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117379#update)

- [Make sure that Mail is receiving messages](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117379#receiving)

## Select your mailbox in the sidebar

To see the messages in a mailbox, select that mailbox in the sidebar of the Mail window.

If you don't see the sidebar:

- From the View menu, choose Show Sidebar.

- Or press Control-Command-S.

If you see the sidebar but don't see your mailbox in the sidebar:

- Make sure that the email account is enabled: From the Mail menu in the menu bar, choose Settings (or Preferences), then click Accounts. Select your account on the left, then confirm that “Enable this account” is selected.

- Make sure that your mailbox isn't hidden in a section of the sidebar. Move your pointer over the name of a section to see a disclosure arrow next to the name. Then click the arrow to hide or show items in that section. In this example, items in the Favorites section of the sidebar are hidden in the left image and shown in the right image:

## Check for message filtering

If you use message filtering, Mail can hide certain messages, such as messages that are already read or aren’t addressed to you. If filtering is turned on, you can turn it off:

- From the View menu, choose Filter > Disable Message Filter.

- Or press Command-L.

- Or click the Filter button in the toolbar:

## Check the email categories setting

Mail in [macOS Sequoia 15.4 or later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033) offers additional filtering based on categories. At the top of your list of messages, if the mail window shows a row of buttons named Primary, Transactions, Updates, and Promotions, this feature is turned on.

To view all of your messages instead of those in the selected category:

- Click the selected category button (such as Primary) to turn off filtering for that category. The All Mail button then appears, indicating that Mail is showing all messages in your inbox.

- Or turn off the categories feature: Click the View Options button in the Mail toolbar, then deselect Show Mail Categories. This setting is also available from the View menu in the menu bar.

[Image: View Options menu with "Show Mail Categories" selected.]

## Check the Junk mailbox

Select the Junk mailbox in the sidebar. If your missing messages are there, you can move them to your inbox or mark them as not junk. Learn more about [managing junk mail](https://support.apple.com/guide/mail/reduce-junk-mail-mlhlp1065/mac) .

## Check for blocked senders

If you blocked the sender of a message, Mail might have moved the message to the Trash. You can select Trash in the sidebar to see the messages it contains. Learn more about [blocking and unblocking senders](https://support.apple.com/guide/mail/mlhlp1065/mac) and [changing Blocked settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mail/mlhle407d4a3/mac) .

## Check for mail rules

Mail rules can affect whether messages are shown in a mailbox. If you [created Mail rules](https://support.apple.com/guide/mail/cpmlprefrules/mac) , you can edit or disable them in Mail settings: From the Mail menu, choose Settings, then click Rules.

## Search for messages

If you don't know which mailbox contains your messages, use the Search field in Mail to [search for your messages](https://support.apple.com/guide/mail/search-for-emails-mlhlp1003/mac) . You can then select a mailbox in the sidebar to limit your search to that mailbox.

If search isn't working as you expect, rebuild the mailbox: Select the mailbox in the sidebar, then choose Rebuild from the Mailbox menu in the menu bar.

## Update macOS

[Update macOS on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) , then check for your email messages again.

## Make sure that Mail is receiving messages

If you don't know that you ever received the missing messages, make sure that Mail is receiving messages. [Learn what to do if you're not receiving email](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102422) .
