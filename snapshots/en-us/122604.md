# Get help with installing and updating Pro Apps on your Mac

Whether you’re purchasing, installing, reinstalling, or updating a Pro App on your Mac, make sure to have your Apple Account information nearby.

Pro Apps from Apple include Final Cut Pro, Logic Pro, Pixelmator Pro, Photomator, MainStage, Compressor, and Motion.

[Image: Apple Pro Display, and two MacBook Pro computers showing Pro Apps and the App Store]

## Purchase and download Pro Apps from the App Store

When you purchase a Pro App from the Mac App Store, make sure that you're signed in to the Apple Account that you want associated with the app. That way, you get all the benefits that come with your purchase, including updates, the ability to install the app on other personal devices, and the ability to share the app with family members.

[Purchase and download Pro Apps from the App Store](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122605)

## Update Pro Apps

To update a Pro App, sign into the Mac App Store with the Apple Account you used when you originally purchased the app.

[Update Pro Apps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122606)

## Install a previously purchased Pro App

If you bought a new Mac, had to erase your existing Mac, or had to remove a Pro App for troubleshooting purposes, you can download and install your previously purchased Pro App from the Mac App Store. Make sure you're signed in to your Mac with the Apple Account that you used to purchase the app.

[Install a previously purchased Pro App](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122694)

## Install a Pro App on another Mac

You can install a Pro App on your other Mac computers, as long as you’re signed in to the same Apple Account that was used to purchase the app. You can also [use Family Sharing to install Pro apps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102599) on other Mac computers.

## Redeem the Pro Apps Bundle for Education

When you purchase the Pro Apps Bundle for Education, redeem the five content redemption codes in the emails that Apple sends you. When you redeem your bundle, you apps download and install on your Mac.

[Redeem the Pro Apps Bundle for education](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118586)

## Adopt your preinstalled Pro Apps

When you purchase Logic Pro, Final Cut Pro, or the Pro Apps Bundle for Education preinstalled on your new Mac, your Mac arrives with the apps installed and ready to use. You can find the apps in the Applications folder.

To make sure you get all the features included with Pro Apps—including updates, the ability to install the app on your other personal devices, and more—make sure to adopt the apps in the App Store as soon as you get your new Mac.

[Adopt your preinstalled Pro Apps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101869)
