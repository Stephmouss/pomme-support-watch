# Restart your iPad

Learn how to turn your iPad off, then back on.

## How to restart an iPad without a Home button

- Press and hold either volume button and the top button until the power off slider appears.

[Image: Pressing and holding the volume and top buttons on an iPad without a Home button until the power off slider appears.]

- Drag the slider, then wait 30 seconds for your device to turn off. If your device is frozen or unresponsive, [force restart your device](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/restart-ipad-ipad63d30b5a/14.0/ipados/14.0#iPad715e3eec) .

- To turn your device back on, press and hold the top button until you see the Apple logo.

## How to restart an iPad with a Home button

- Press and hold the top button until the power off slider appears.

[Image: Illustration of pressing the top button on an iPad without a home button to initiate the restart process.]

- Drag the slider, then wait for 30 seconds for your device to turn off. If your device is frozen or unresponsive, [force restart your device](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/restart-ipad-ipad63d30b5a/14.0/ipados/14.0#iPad715e3eec) .

- To turn your device back on, press and hold the top button until you see the Apple logo.

## How to restart an iPad in Control Center.

In iOS 18 and later, you can turn off your device from Control Center.

- Swipe down from the top-right corner of your screen to open Control Center.

- Press and hold the Power button in the top-right corner of your screen, then let go.

[Image: Control center open on iPad with the power button icon highlighted.]

- Drag the slider, then wait for 30 seconds for your device to turn off.

- To turn your device back on, press and hold the top button until you see the Apple logo.
