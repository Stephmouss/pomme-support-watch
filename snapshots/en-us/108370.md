# Add money to Apple Cash

With just a few taps, you can add money to your Apple Cash balance.

After you [set up Apple Cash](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109304) 1 , you can add money to your balance using the debit or prepaid cards 2 that you already use with Apple Pay in Wallet.

If you don’t already have one of these cards in Wallet, [you need to add one](https://support.apple.com//108398) before you can add money.

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app, tap your Apple Cash card, tap the More button , then tap Add Money.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- iPad: Open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, tap your Apple Cash card, then tap Add Money.

- Apple Watch: Open the Wallet app, tap your Apple Cash card, tap Card Balance, then tap Add Money.

- Apple Vision Pro: Open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, tap your Apple Cash card, then tap Add Money.

- Enter an amount. The minimum is $10.

- Tap Add.

- Authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID, or your passcode.

- [Face ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108411) : Double-click the side button, then glance at your iPhone or iPad to approve.

- [Touch ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102528) : Lightly place your finger on the Touch ID sensor to approve.

- The sensor is either in the Home button or—on iPad Air (4th generation) and later and iPad mini (6th generation)—the top button.

- [Passcode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119586) : Enter your passcode to approve.

- [Optic ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118483) : Double-click the top button, then look at the Optic ID icon to approve.

## Set up auto reload on your iPhone or iPad

Auto reload adds money to your Apple Cash card when the balance is running low, so you always have money for Apple Cash purchases.

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app.

- Tap your Apple Cash card, tap the More button , then tap Card Details . Tap Set Up Auto Reload. On iPad: Open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, tap your Apple Cash card, then tap Set Up Auto Reload.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Select the amount to automatically be added to your Apple Cash card any time your balance falls below your Minimum Balance.

- Tap Minimum Balance to specify the balance below which funds will automatically be added to your Apple Cash.

- Tap Set Up.

- Double-click and authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode to complete setup. You should see a Setup Complete screen.

[You can edit or cancel auto reload at any time.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108370#cancel)

### Cancel auto reload

#### On your iPhone

- Open the Wallet app.

- Tap your Apple Cash card, tap the More button , then tap Card Details .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Auto Reload, then tap Cancel Auto Reload.

#### On your iPad

- Open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap your Apple Cash card.

- Tap Auto Reload. Then tap Cancel Auto Reload.

### Edit auto reload

#### On your iPhone

- Open the Wallet app.

- Tap your Apple Cash card, tap the More button , then tap Card Details .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Auto Reload, then from the top right, tap Edit. You can change the amount to automatically be added, the minimum balance, and your payment method.

#### On your iPad

- Open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap your Apple Cash card.

- Tap Auto Reload. Then tap Edit. You can change the amount to automatically be added, the minimum balance, and your payment method.

## Get Daily Cash with Apple Card

If you have an [Apple Card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104952) and an Apple Cash account in good standing, you can get Daily Cash back on every purchase that you make with it.

- When you pay with Apple Card, you [get back a percentage of each purchase as Daily Cash](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119575) .

- [Daily Cash is automatically added to your Apple Cash balance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119575#dailycashapc) after the payment settles.

## View your balance

- iPhone: Open the Wallet app, then tap your Apple Cash card.

- iPad: Open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap your Apple Cash card.

- Apple Watch: Double-click the side button and swipe to your Apple Cash card.

- Apple Vision Pro: Open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap your Apple Cash card.

To see your up-to-date balance, be sure you have a cellular or Wi-Fi connection.

You can also check your Apple Cash balance on your [Apple Account page](http://account.apple.com/) .
