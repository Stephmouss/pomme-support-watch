# Check battery usage on your iPhone

Learn how to check the amount of battery each app uses on your iPhone.

## Check battery usage

- Open Settings.

- Tap Battery.

iPhone shows when the battery was last fully charged and when you disconnected it from the charger. While charging, iPhone displays estimated times to reach 80% and 100%. See [Charge the iPhone battery](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/charge-the-battery-iph63eecc618/ios) to learn more about charging.

The Daily Usage graph shows your battery level throughout the day.

- Open Settings.

- Tap Battery.

- Tap View All Battery Usage to view the battery usage percentage over the last eight days.

[Image: An iPhone screen showing View All Battery Usage as the selected option.]

## Find insights and suggestions about battery life

Your iPhone may show insights about conditions or usage patterns that affect battery life and suggest ways to reduce energy consumption.

- Go to Settings.

- Tap Battery. If there are any insights or suggestions, you'll find them just above Daily Usage.

[Image: An iPhone screen showing insights and suggestions about recent iPhone usage.]

- Tap a suggestion to go to the related setting.
