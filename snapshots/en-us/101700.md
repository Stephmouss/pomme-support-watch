# About the transition to Compressor 4.1 white paper

Learn about the transition to Compressor 4.1 white paper.

Compressor 4.1 introduces a new, improved interface and various workflow enhancements that differ from workflows in previous versions of Compressor. The [transition to Compressor 4.1](http://images.apple.com/final-cut-pro/docs/Transitioning_to_Compressor.pdf) white paper, structured according to the major parts of a transcoding workflow, uses the Compressor 4.0.7 application for comparison to discuss how to complete important tasks in Compressor 4.1.
