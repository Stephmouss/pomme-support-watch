# Download and stream shows, movies, sports, and events from your Apple TV subscription and Apple TV channels

Learn how to download or stream content with your Apple TV subscription and Apple TV channels.

## Download a show, movie, or event on your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Apple TV app.

- Browse or search for a TV show, movie, or event you'd like to download.

- Choose what you'd like to download, then tap the Download button . If you're downloading a TV show, choose the season you'd like to download, then download the entire season or individual episodes.

- To find and play your downloads, go to the Library tab, then choose Downloaded.

Can't download a show or movie? You might have reached the [limits for downloads](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118239#downloadlimits) , or the channel might not allow downloads.

## Download a show, movie, or event on your Mac or PC

- Open the Apple TV app.

- Browse or search for a TV show, movie, or event you'd like to download.

- Choose what you'd like to download. If it's a movie or event, click the Download button on your Mac, or the Download button on your PC. If it's a TV show, find the episode, click the More button , then click Download.

- To find and play your downloads, go to the Library tab, then choose Downloaded. If Downloaded isn't in your Library tab on your PC, click the More button , click Edit Library Sidebar, then turn Downloaded on.

Can't download a show or movie? You might have reached the [limits for downloads](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118239#downloadlimits) , or the channel might not allow downloads.

## Download a show, movie, or event on Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Apple TV app.

- Browse or search for a show, movie, or event.

- Pinch and hold the item you'd like to download, then tap Download.

- To find and play your downloads, open the sidebar, tap Library, then tap Downloaded.

Can't download a show or movie? You might have reached the [limits for downloads](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118239#downloadlimits) , or the channel might not allow downloads.

## Limits for downloads

You can't download content from the Apple TV app on Apple TV 4K, Apple TV HD, smart TVs, streaming devices, and on [tv.apple.com](http://tv.apple.com) . Instead, you can only stream content on these devices.

For most Apple TV channels, downloads are available for up to 30 days before they expire. For a few channels, downloads might expire earlier. Some titles you download from your Apple TV subscription will expire 48 hours after you begin playing them, or 30 days after you download them, whichever comes first.

Some Apple TV channels might have limits for how many titles can be downloaded simultaneously and the number of devices you can download content on. The number of titles you can download with your Apple TV subscription at one time might also be limited. If you reach the limit, the Apple TV app alerts you. If you want to download more titles, delete TV episodes or movies that you already downloaded from one of your (or your family's) devices.

## Limits for streaming on multiple devices

- Apple TV subscription: For most content, one subscription allows you to enjoy 6 simultaneous streams. Some titles might be available to stream on fewer devices at the same time.

- Apple Music student subscription with Apple TV access: One subscription allows you to stream Apple TV on one device at a time.

- Apple TV channels: One subscription allows you to enjoy up to 3 simultaneous streams for most channels. Some channels might allow more or less than 3 simultaneous streams.

The Apple TV app, Apple TV channels, Apple TV subscription, and store availability might vary by country or region. See [what’s available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) . Apple TV subscription does not include third-party branded subscription services or content available for rental or purchase in the Apple TV app.
