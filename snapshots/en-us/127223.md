# How to clean your Beats 360

Regular cleaning can help maintain your Beats 360.

Is it OK to use a disinfectant on my Beats 360?

Using a 70 percent isopropyl alcohol wipe, 75 percent ethyl alcohol wipe, or Clorox Disinfecting Wipes, you may gently wipe the exterior surfaces of your Beats 360. Don’t use on the knit headband and ear cushions of your Beats 360. Don't use products containing bleach or hydrogen peroxide. Avoid getting moisture in any openings, and don’t submerge your Beats 360 in any cleaning agents.

## Clean your Beats 360

- Don’t run your Beats 360 under water.

- Use a soft, dry, lint-free cloth.

- If your Beats 360 are exposed to anything that might cause stains or other damage—for example, soaps, shampoos, conditioners, lotions, perfumes, solvents, detergent, acids or acidic foods, insect repellent, sunscreen, oil, or hair dye:

- Wipe them clean with a cloth slightly dampened with fresh water and dry with a soft, dry, lint-free cloth.

- Don't attempt to use them until they're completely dry.

- Make sure not to get any liquid in the openings.

- Don't use sharp objects or abrasive materials to clean your Beats 360.

## Clean the cushions and headband of your Beats 360

- In a clean container, mix 1 tsp. (5 mL) of liquid laundry detergent into 1 cup (250 mL) of water.

- Remove the cushions from the ear cups, and remove the headband.

- Dip a lint-free cloth into the soapy water solution, wring it out slightly, and rub the cloth on the cushions and headband gently for 1 minute each.

- Wipe the cushions and headband clean with a separate cloth that's slightly dampened with fresh water.

- Dry the cushions and headband with a soft, dry, lint-free cloth, making sure to remove any excess moisture.

## Tips to avoid skin irritation

Here’s how to avoid skin irritation, especially if you have allergies or skin sensitivities:

- After workouts with Beats 360, or after your device is exposed to liquids such as sweat, soap, shampoo, makeup, sunscreen, and lotions that can potentially cause skin irritations, clean and dry your device. Keeping your Beats 360—as well as your skin—clean and dry will maximize comfort and prevent long-term damage to your device.

- If you have known allergies or sensitivities to certain substances, [check the materials in Beats](https://support.apple.com/101598) .

- Learn about [Beats 360 sweat and water resistance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149159) .
