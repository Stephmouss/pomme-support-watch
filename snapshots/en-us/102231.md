# Identify your Mac Studio model

Use this information to find out which Mac Studio you have, and where it fits in the history of Mac Studio.

## Find the name of your Mac model

The easiest way to find the name of your Mac model is to choose About This Mac from the Apple menu  in the corner of your screen. Learn more about how to [find your Mac model name or serial number](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102767) , even when you don’t have your Mac or it doesn’t start up all the way.
