# How to turn AutoMix or Crossfade on or off in the Apple Music app

Use AutoMix to seamlessly transition from one song to the next. You can also use Crossfade to fade in and out between songs. Learn how to turn AutoMix and Crossfade on or off.

## Choose your transition style

You can choose how your music transitions between songs.

If you [subscribe to Apple Music](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109329) , you can use AutoMix, which dynamically transitions between Apple Music subscription content for a seamless listening experience.

Crossfade uses a set amount of seconds to fade songs in and out of your songs. You can adjust the amount of time it takes to transition between them. If you're not an Apple Music subscriber, Crossfade is the only transition option in your Music settings and in the Apple Music app.*

You can also choose to have no transition between songs by turning Song Transitions off.

AutoMix and Crossfade are not available with hi-res lossless. [Change your audio quality settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118295) to use AutoMix or Crossfade on your device.

### Choose your transition style on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Apps, Music, then Song Transitions.

- Turn Song Transitions on and select AutoMix or Crossfade. If you don't want songs to transition, you can turn Song Transitions off. If you're not an Apple Music subscriber, AutoMix will be grayed out.

- If you select Crossfade, you can drag the slider to set the amount of time it takes for the current song to fade out and the new song to fade in.

[Image: iPhone Settings app showing Song Transitions options with Crossfade selected. There is a slider at the bottom for Crossfade Duration from 1 to 12 seconds.]

### Choose your transition style on your Mac

- Open the Apple Music app.

- From menu bar, choose Music, then Settings.

- Click the Playback tab and select Song Transitions.

- Under Transition Style, choose AutoMix or Crossfade. If you don't want songs to transition, you can turn Song Transitions off. If you're not an Apple Music subscriber, AutoMix will be grayed out.

- If you select Crossfade, you can drag the slider to set the amount of time it takes for the current song to fade out and the new song to fade in.

AutoMix is only available on a Mac with Apple silicon.

## Use AutoMix on your Apple Device

If you [subscribe to Apple Music](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109329) , you can turn AutoMix on or off in the Apple Music app.

### Turn AutoMix on or off on your iPhone

- Open the Apple Music app.

- Tap the MiniPlayer at the bottom of the screen.

- Tap the Up Next button .

- Tap the AutoMix button to turn it on or off.

[Image: Apple Music app Up Next screen on an iPhone, with the AutoMix button highlighted.]

### Turn AutoMix on or off on your Mac, iPad, Apple TV 4K (2nd gen or later), or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Apple Music app.

- In the playback controls, click the Up Next button .

- Click or tap the AutoMix button to turn it on or off.

AutoMix is only available on a Mac with Apple silicon.

### If your music isn't dynamically transitioning with AutoMix

AutoMix might not dynamically transition between songs if:

- Your album is played in sequential order.

- The genres or tempos are incompatible.

- Your content is uploaded from your music library.

- Your songs are purchased and downloaded from the iTunes Store.

## Use crossfade on your Apple Device

Turn Crossfade on or off in the Apple Music app.

### Turn crossfade on or off on your iPhone

- Open the Apple Music app.

- Tap the MiniPlayer at the bottom of the screen.

- Tap Up Next .

- Tap the Crossfade button to turn it on or off.

[Image: Apple Music app Up Next screen on an iPhone, with the Crossfade button highlighted.]

### Turn crossfade on or off on your Mac, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Apple Music app.

- In the playback controls, click the Up Next button .

- Click or tap the Crossfade button to turn it on or off.

* AutoMix is not available on your Intel-Based Mac, Apple Watch, Android, or the Apple Music app for Windows.

Apple Music availability might vary by country or region. Learn [what's available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
