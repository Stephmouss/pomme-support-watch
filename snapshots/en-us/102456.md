# If Safari isn't loading websites or quits on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch

If you can't load a website or webpage, or Safari quits unexpectedly, follow these steps.

## Connect to a different network

Try to load a website, like [www.apple.com](https://www.apple.com) , using cellular data. If you don't have cellular data, [connect to a different Wi-Fi network](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111107) , then load the website.

If you're using a VPN (Virtual Private Network), [check your VPN settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102281) . If you have a VPN turned on, some apps or websites might block content from loading.

## Restart your device

Turn off your device and turn it on again:

- [Restart your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118259)

- [Restart your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101603)

- [Restart your iPod touch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102137)

## Clear website data

You can clear website data occasionally to improve Safari performance:

- Go to Settings, then tap Apps.

- Tap Safari.

- Tap Clear History and Website Data.

- Tap Clear History to confirm.

## Turn on JavaScript

Turn on JavaScript if it's not already on:

- Go to Settings, then tap Apps.

- Tap Safari.

- Tap Advanced, then turn on JavaScript.

## Get more help

If the issue continues and only affects a certain website or webpage, check if you have Private Relay turned on. You can [temporarily turn off Private Relay in iCloud Settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102022) . If Safari still doesn't load websites and you tried all of these steps, contact the website developer for more help.
