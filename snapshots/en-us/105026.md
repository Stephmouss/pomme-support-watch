# If Siri isn't working on your Apple Watch

Learn what to do if Siri doesn't respond or asks you to try again.

After each step, see if Siri responds:

- [Check the Internet connection](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109319) on your Apple Watch.

- On your iPhone, see if Siri is turned on. Tap Settings, tap Siri or Apple Intelligence & Siri, then turn on Listen for "Hey Siri". Or tap Listen for and turn on Siri.

- On your Apple Watch, see if Siri is turned on. Tap Settings, then tap Siri.

- [Restart your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apd521a8a902) .

- On your iPhone, go to Settings, tap Siri or Apple Intelligence & Siri, choose Talk & Type to Siri, then tap "Off". Then tap to turn on "Siri" or "Hey Siri" again.

- See if the microphone or speaker on your Apple Watch is blocked. For example, if your device has a protective case, remove it.

- If Siri doesn't speak on Apple Watch Series 3 or later, you might need to download its voice. The download happens when you’re on Wi-Fi and your Apple Watch is charging. To give the download time to complete, try charging your Apple Watch overnight. To check the status of the download on your Apple Watch, tap Settings, tap Siri, then scroll to Siri Voice. [Learn which languages Siri speaks](https://www.apple.com/watchos/feature-availability/#siri-speaks) .
