# Use Apple Music with your Android devices

Subscribe to Apple Music to stream millions of songs. Stream music to your TV with Chromecast, listen to your favorite music in your car with Android Auto, and so much more — all with Apple Music on Android.

## Get the Apple Music app

To download the Apple Music app, you need an Android phone or tablet with Android 5.0 Lollipop or later, or a Chromebook that supports Android apps. [Go to the Google Play Store to download the Apple Music app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.apple.android.music) .

## Subscribe to Apple Music

Stream millions of songs, get exclusive curated playlists, browse recommendations for music you’ll love, and listen on any device with the Apple Music app.

[Learn how to join Apple Music on your Android device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109340)

### If you already subscribe, just sign in to your Apple Account

- Open the Apple Music app.

- If you see a trial offer, tap Sign In in the upper-right corner. Or tap the More button , then tap Sign In.

[Image: the More button]

- Sign in to the Apple Account that you use with Apple Music.

### Share an Apple Music family subscription

On your Android device, you can use Family Sharing to share and manage an Apple Music family subscription with a family group. Or you can join someone else's family group to share their subscription.

[Learn how to use Family Sharing with Apple Music on your Android device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109333)

## Stream music from Apple Music to your Chromecast device

- Make sure that your Android device and Chromecast device are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

- Open the Apple Music app, then start playing a song.

- Tap the song at the bottom of the screen to open Now Playing. At the bottom of your screen, tap the Cast button .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap your Chromecast device.

- Play music.

If you need help with your Chromecast, [visit Google Support](https://support.google.com/chromecast) .

Not all content types in Apple Music support Casting, and not all Chromecast devices are supported.

## Use Apple Music with Android Auto

With Android Auto, you can listen to Apple Music in your car and control what's playing from your car's display panel.

- On your Android device, download Android Auto from the Google Play Store.

- Connect your Android device to your car.

- From your car's display panel, open Android Auto.

- In Android Auto, open the Apple Music app and play music.

Need help with Android Auto? [Visit Google Support](https://support.google.com/androidauto) .

Android Auto requires Apple Music for Android 2.6 or later.

Apple Music availability might vary by country or region. Learn more about [what's available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
