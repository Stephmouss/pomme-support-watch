# How to block my Caller ID on iPhone

In Settings, you can choose to hide your phone number from outgoing calls.

## Hide your Caller ID in your iPhone settings

- On your iPhone, open the Settings app.

- Tap Apps, then tap Phone.

- Tap Show My Caller ID, then turn it off. When you make a call, the recipient won't see your phone number.

If Show My Caller ID is dimmed or doesn't appear, contact your carrier.
