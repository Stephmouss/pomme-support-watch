# Redownload apps from Apple

Sign in to your Apple Account in the App Store and redownload apps on all your devices.

## How to redownload apps on iPhone or iPad

- Open the App Store app.

- Tap the My Account button or your photo at the top of the screen.

- Tap Apps & Purchase History, then tap Your Apps. If you use Family Sharing, you can tap a family member's name to see their purchases.

- Tap "Not on this [device]."

- Find the app that you want to download, then tap the Redownload button .

[Image: the Redownload button]

Learn what to do [if you can't redownload the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108105) . If you can't find the app on your device, [use the App Library to find the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108324) .

## How to redownload apps on Mac

- Open the App Store on your Mac.

- Click your name in the sidebar. If you don't see your name in the sidebar, click Sign In, sign in to your Apple Account, then click your photo in the sidebar.

- Click Apps & Purchase History, then click Your Apps. If you're part of a Family Sharing group, click a family member’s name to see their purchases.

- Find the app you want to download, then click the Redownload button .

[Image: the Redownload button]

[Learn what to do if you can't redownload the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108105)

## How to redownload apps on Apple Vision Pro

- Open the App Store.

- Tap the My Account button or your photo at the top of the screen.

- Tap Apps & Purchase History, then tap Your Apps. If you use Family Sharing, you can tap a family member's name to see their purchases.

- Choose Apple Vision or iPhone & iPad.

- Find the app you want to download, then click the Redownload button .

[Learn what to do if you can't redownload the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108105)

## How to redownload apps on Apple TV 4K and Apple TV HD

- From the Home Screen, choose App Store.

- Choose Purchased at the top of the screen. If you use Family Sharing, select Family Sharing, then select a family member's name to see their purchases.

- Select Not on This TV.

- Find and select the app that you want to redownload.

- Select the Redownload button .

[Learn what to do if you can't redownload the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108105)

You can't redownload apps on Apple TV (3rd generation or earlier).

## How to redownload apps on Apple Watch

- Open the App Store on your Apple Watch.

- Scroll all the way to the bottom, tap Account, then tap Purchased. If you use Family Sharing, you can tap a family member's name to see their purchases.

- Find the app that you want to download, then tap the Redownload button .

[Learn what to do if you can't redownload the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108105)

## Learn more

- Some apps aren't available on all devices. For example, an app that you bought on your iPhone might not have a version for Mac.

- On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, you can also [redownload games in the Apple Games app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123712) .

- Games that you play with an Apple Arcade subscription don't appear in your purchases. To redownload a game from Apple Arcade, search for the game and download it again.

- You can't redownload in-app purchases, but you can [restore some in-app purchases](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108096) if you used Apple In-App Purchase to buy them.
