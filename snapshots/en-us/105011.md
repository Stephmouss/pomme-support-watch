# View smart home accessory activity in the Home app

View up to 30 days of activity for smart home accessories like door locks, alarm systems, and Adaptive Temperature-supported thermostats using Activity in the Home app.

- [What you need](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105011#what-you-need)

## About Activity

- If you have an Apple TV or HomePod using tvOS 17 or later, and you're using the latest version of Apple Home, Activity is turned on automatically.

- If Activity is turned on, and you have smart home accessories like door locks and alarm systems in your home, activities like locking and unlocking your front door are available to view in the Home app.

- If Activity is turned on, and [Adaptive Temperature](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphdf6fde3d8) is enabled, heating and cooling activities associated with Adaptive Temperature-supported thermostats are available to view in the Home app.

- Activity doesn't include recorded camera activity. Learn how to [set up HomeKit Secure Video](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mm7c90d21583) to record and view video.

Activity cannot guarantee that events are captured in all circumstances. Network or power outages may result in lost events. When the connected home hub is not reachable due to power outage, events may not be logged successfully.

## Who can view Activity

- Home Owners and Residents can view Activity. Home Guests can't view Activity. Learn how to [invite others to control accessories in your home](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/invite-others-to-control-accessories-iphcbaf7e8f3/ios) .

- Apple cannot read the contents of Activity because it's end-to-end encrypted. Activity is permanently deleted after 30 days. [Learn how Apple uses end-to-end encryption](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102651) to help protect your data.

[Remote access](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102386#allow-remote-access-and-edit-permissions) to the home is required to view Activity while you are outside the home.

## What you need

- You need an Apple TV or HomePod with tvOS 17 or later in a home on [the latest version of Apple Home](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102287) .

- Your home must include smart home accessories like Adaptive Temperature-supported thermostats, door locks, garage doors, alarm systems, contact sensors, smoke detectors, doors, and windows. Learn how to [add smart home accessories in the Home app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104998) .

## View Activity

- Open the Home app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

- Select the Climate or Security category.

[Image: The Climate and Security categories appear at the top of the Home Screen in the Home app.]

- Select Activity.

[Image: Activity appears chronologically with the most recent activity first.]

Some climate activity is based on home Owner and Resident device location. Learn [how to turn Location Services on and off](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102647) .

## Turn Activity on and off

Only the home Owner or a Resident with [permission to add and edit accessories](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102386#allow-remote-access-and-edit-permissions) can turn Activity on and off. When you turn off Activity, all previous activity for the home is permanently deleted.

- Open the Home app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

- Select the More button , select Home Settings , then select Activity.

- Choose One Month to turn Activity on, or choose Off.

[Image: Activity is turned on for One Month.]
