# Find your Exchange Device ID

If you need help using your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch, Exchange administrators might need your Exchange Device ID.

## Find your Exchange Device ID in iOS 11 and later

- Go to Settings > Passwords & Accounts.

- Select Add Account.

- Choose Microsoft Exchange from the list. Your Exchange Device ID is at the bottom of your screen.

[Image: Screenshot of iPhone settings showing the Exchange Device ID location]

If you restore your device to factory settings or move to a new device, you receive a new Exchange Device ID.
