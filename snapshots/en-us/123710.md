# How to add or remove a bank account for your Apple Card

You can add or remove a bank account in the Wallet app or online at [card.apple.com](https://card.apple.com) .

## Manage your bank accounts in the Wallet app

To add a bank account, you need your bank account and routing numbers.

- Open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details.

- Scroll down and tap Bank Accounts:

- To add a bank account: Tap Add Bank Account or Add Another Bank Account, then enter the routing number of your bank and your bank account number. Click Next, enter your routing and account number again to confirm, then click Save. You can add multiple bank accounts to make payments. The first one you add is your default bank account.

- To remove a bank account: Tap Edit, tap the minus sign next to the bank account you want to remove, then tap Delete. Before deleting a bank account, be sure to cancel any scheduled payments.

## Manage your bank accounts at card.apple.com

- Go to [card.apple.com](https://card.apple.com) and sign in with your Apple Account and password.

- Click Settings in the sidebar.

- Below Bank Accounts, do one of the following:

- To add a bank account : Click Add Bank Account or Add Another Bank Account, then enter the routing number of your bank and your bank account number. Click Next, enter your routing and account number again to confirm, then click Save.

- To remove a bank account: Click the More button next to the bank account you want to remove, click Remove Bank, then click Remove.
