# Use Apple Intelligence to suggest reminders from any app in iOS 26

Get suggested reminders even when you don't have the Reminders app open. Share text or a website to Reminders, and Apple Intelligence can suggest reminders — like key action items from emails or ingredients from a recipe.

- Open a website in Safari, a message in Mail, or another app with text.

- Find then tap the Share button . If there is no Share button at first, like in Mail, highlight the text then tap the Share button.

- Tap Reminders.

- Under Suggestions, tap the Add button next to the Apple Intelligence suggestions that you want to add as reminders or tap Include All.

- Choose the List you want to add your reminders to, then tap the checkmark .

[Image: With suggested reminders, Apple Intelligence can help turn important details into reminders — like a travel prep list based on an email.]

[Apple Intelligence is available](https://support.apple.com/121115) in beta on iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, all iPhone 16 and later models, iPad mini (A17 Pro), and iPad models with M1 and later, with Siri and device language set to the same [supported language](https://support.apple.com/121115#supportedlanguages) .

Apple Intelligence uses generative models and outputs may vary. Check important information for accuracy.
