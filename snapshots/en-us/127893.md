# How to get Siri AI

Siri AI (Beta) is an entirely new version of Siri powered by the next generation of Apple Intelligence, and deeply integrated across your Apple devices. A more capable and conversational assistant, Siri AI can answer questions about almost anything and help you find what you need in the moment.

Siri AI understands personal context across your messages, emails, photos, and more, so you can find what you need just by asking. It can answer questions about what's on your screen, or go out to the web for up-to-date information to generate a helpful answer. And with even more systemwide app actions, you can get more done across your apps.

A dedicated Siri app uses iCloud to privately sync your conversation history across your devices, so you can start a conversation on iPhone and continue it on Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, or Apple Vision Pro.

Siri AI is available in beta for iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27 Golden Gate, visionOS 27, and watchOS 27 when your [device language](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109358) and [Siri language](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120499) are set to the same supported language and your Apple Account is in an eligible region.

## Turn on Siri AI

After you update your device, go to Settings, tap or click Siri, then tap or click Try Siri AI (Beta). If Siri is turned off, tap or click Turn On Siri first.

Follow the onscreen instructions to pick a voice, complete voice enrollment, and review your privacy settings. This will add you to a waitlist, and wait times can vary. Siri AI will begin to download automatically when your wait is complete.

## Siri AI requirements

To use Siri AI, you need an Apple Intelligence–capable iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, or Apple Vision Pro with [device language](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109358) and [Siri language](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120499) set to a supported language:

- iOS 27 on iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 16 models or later, and iPhone Air

- iPadOS 27 on iPad mini (A17 Pro), and iPad models with M1 or later

- macOS 27 Golden Gate on MacBook Neo (A18 Pro) and Mac with M1 or later

- watchOS 27 on Apple Watch Series 9 or later, Apple Watch Ultra 2 or later, and Apple Watch SE 3, when paired with an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone nearby

- visionOS 27 on any Apple Vision Pro

## Supported languages and regions

Siri AI is currently available in English. Availability varies by region and platform. After you turn on Siri AI, it will continue to work when you travel to other regions.

### For European Union residents

Siri AI is not available for iOS, iPadOS, or watchOS in the EU. It is available for macOS 27 Golden Gate and visionOS 27 if your [Apple Account region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118283) is in the EU and your device language and Siri language are both set to English.

### For China mainland residents

Siri AI will not currently work if your [Apple Account region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118283) is in mainland China.

## Siri AI features

- Broad world knowledge: get answers to almost any question with broad world knowledge.

- Personal context understanding: find what you need across Messages, Mail, Calendar, Notes, Reminders, and more with personal context understanding.

- Onscreen awareness: ask about and act on what's on your screen.

- App actions: Siri can get things done across your apps to send messages, create events, and more.

- Siri app: pick up where you left off with the Siri app, which keeps your conversation history synced with iCloud across your Apple devices.

- Write with Siri: describe what you need, and Siri can generate a draft from scratch, edit what you've written, or give you feedback—virtually anywhere you type.

- Visual Intelligence: learn more about what's in front of you with Visual Intelligence. Use Siri mode in the Camera app (iPhone only), the screenshot gesture on iPhone and iPad, or keyboard shortcuts on Mac. On Apple Vision Pro, you can search, ask questions, and take action by looking at your surroundings and asking Siri.

- Expressive voices: customize pace and expressiveness. Requires a device with the AFM Core Advanced on-device model. AFM Core Advanced is available on iPhone Air, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPad (M4), or later with at least 12GB of unified memory, and Mac (M3) or later with at least 12GB of unified memory, and Apple Vision Pro (M5).
