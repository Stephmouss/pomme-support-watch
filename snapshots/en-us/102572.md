# Show the battery percentage on your iPhone or iPad

Depending on your device, battery percentage appears in Control Center or in the status bar.

## Find battery percentage on iPhone 14 and other iPhone models with Face ID

On iPhone 14 and other [iPhone models with Face ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102854) , find the battery percentage in Control Center. Swipe down from the top-right corner of the device's display to access Control Center.

[Image: iPhone screen with Control Center showing battery percentage at 100%.]

To see iPhone battery percentage in the status bar:

- Open Settings.

- Tap Battery.

- Turn on Battery Percentage.

## Find battery percentage on other iPhone models and iPad

Battery percentage typically appears in the status bar on these devices:

- iPhone SE (2nd and 3rd generation)

- iPhone 8 or earlier

- iPad (all models)

If battery percentage doesn't show:

- Open Settings.

- Tap Battery.

- Turn on Battery Percentage.

## Use the Batteries widget

The Batteries widget shows your device's battery percentage on the Home Screen or Today View.
