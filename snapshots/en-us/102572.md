# Show the battery percentage on your iPhone or iPad

Depending on your device, battery percentage appears in Control Center or in the status bar.

## Find battery percentage on iPhone 14 and other models with Face ID

On iPhone 14 and other [iPhone models with Face ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102854) (iPhone X and later), the battery percentage appears in Control Center. Just swipe down from the top-right corner of the device's display.

[Image: iPhone screen with Control Center showing battery percentage at 100%.]

With iOS 16 and later, you can turn on the battery percentage so it appears in your status bar:

- Go to Settings.

- Tap Battery.

- Turn on Battery Percentage.

## Find battery percentage on other iPhone models and iPad

Battery percentage is always visible in the status bar on these devices:*

- iPhone SE (2nd and 3rd generation)

- iPhone 8 or earlier

- iPad (all models)

* If battery percentage doesn't show, go to Settings, tap Battery, and turn on Battery Percentage.

## Use the Batteries widget

Another way to check your battery percentage is to add the Batteries widget to your device's Home Screen or Today View. Learn how to [use widgets on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118610) or [widgets on your iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/add-edit-and-remove-widgets-ipadb0de8630/ipados) .
