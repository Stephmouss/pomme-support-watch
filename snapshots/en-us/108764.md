# Change the settings of your AirPods and AirPods Pro

Change the name of your AirPods, change what happens when you double tap your AirPods or press-and-hold AirPods Pro, and more.

## Name your AirPods

- Open your charging case.

- On your iPhone or iPad, go to the Settings app, then tap Bluetooth.

- Tap the name of your AirPods.

- Tap the More Info button next to your AirPods.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap the name of your AirPods.

- Enter a new name for your AirPods.

- Tap Done.

## Change the press-and-hold action on AirPods Pro, AirPods 4 (ANC), and AirPods 5 (both models)

AirPods Pro, AirPods 4 (ANC), and AirPods 5 (both models) have [three listening modes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108918) : Active Noise Cancellation, Transparency mode, and [Adaptive Audio](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104979) . You can also turn Listening Mode off. By default, when you press and hold the force sensor on the stem of your left or right AirPod, your AirPods switch between Active Noise Cancellation and Transparency mode. You can change which listening modes the press-and-hold action uses:

- Open your charging case.

- On your iPhone or iPad, go to the Settings app.

- Tap the name of your AirPods.

- Under Press and Hold AirPods, tap Left or Right, then make sure that Listening Mode is selected. If Press and Hold AirPods isn't displayed, first tap Controls & Gestures.

- Select the two or three listening modes that you want to use with the press-and-hold action. If both AirPods are set to change listening modes, a change to the press-and-hold action settings on one AirPod applies to both.

- To set the press-and-hold action to use Siri, tap Left or Right, then select Siri. You can have one AirPod set for Siri and the other set to change listening modes.

[Image: Press and Hold settings for an AirPods earbud.]

You can also switch between listening modes in Control Center.

If you have Camera Remote set to use the press-and-hold action and have a supported camera app open, press-and-hold doesn't change listening modes. Learn how to [use AirPods with supported camera apps](https://support.apple.com/guide/airpods/dev8e2a2ba2e) on your iPhone or iPad.

## Change the double-tap action on AirPods 1 and 2

You can choose what you want to happen when you double-tap one of your AirPods 1 or 2:

- Open your charging case.

- With your AirPods in your ears and connected to your iPhone or iPad, go to the Settings app.

- Tap the More Info button next to the name of your AirPods.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Select the left or right AirPod to choose which AirPod you'd like to double-tap for Siri, playing or pausing audio content, or skipping between tracks.

## Turn Automatic Ear Detection on or off

By default, AirPods sense when they're in your ear and receive the audio from your device. AirPods also pause and resume playback when you remove one of your AirPods, or stop playback—and won't resume—when you remove both. When you have Automatic Ear Detection on and you're not wearing your AirPods, audio plays through your device's speakers. To turn Automatic Ear Detection on or off, wear your AirPods, and make sure they’re connected to your device. Then:

- On your iPhone or iPad, go to the Settings app, tap the name of your AirPods, then turn Automatic Ear Detection on or off. If Automatic Ear Detection isn't displayed, first tap Audio & Routing.

- On your Mac, choose Apple menu , click System Settings, then click the name of your AirPods. If Automatic Ear Detection isn't displayed, first click Audio & Routing.

When you turn off Automatic Ear Detection, these features are off and all audio plays to your AirPods whether or not you're wearing them.

## Set Microphone to left, right, or automatic

There's a microphone in each AirPod, so you can make phone calls and use Siri. By default, Microphone is set to Automatic, so that either of your AirPods can act as the microphone. If you're using only one AirPod, that AirPod will be the microphone. To set Microphone to left or right, wear your AirPods, and make sure they’re connected to your device. Then:

- On your iPhone or iPad, go to the Settings app, tap the name of your AirPods, then tap Microphone. If Microphone isn't displayed, first tap Audio & Routing. Then, choose between Automatically Switch AirPods, Always Left AirPod, or Always Right AirPod.

- On your Mac, choose Apple menu , then click System Settings. Click the name of your AirPods, then click Microphone. If Microphone isn't displayed, first click Audio & Routing. Then, choose between Automatically Switch AirPods, Always Left AirPod, or Always Right AirPod.

If you set Microphone to Always Left or Always Right, either the right AirPod or the left AirPod uses the microphone even if you remove it from your ear or put it in the case.

## Pause media when you fall asleep

If you have AirPods 4 or later or AirPods Pro 2 or later, you can choose to pause media automatically when you fall asleep while listening to audio using your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple TV. To turn on Pause Media When Falling Asleep, wear your AirPods, and make sure they’re connected to your device. Then:

- On your iPhone or iPad, go to the Settings app, tap the name of your AirPods, then turn on Pause Media While Falling Asleep. If Pause Media While Falling Asleep isn't displayed, first tap Audio & Routing.

- On your Mac, choose Apple menu , then click System Settings. Click the name of your AirPods, then turn on Pause Media While Falling Asleep. If Pause Media While Falling Asleep isn't displayed, first click Audio & Routing.

- On your Apple TV, open Control Center, select your AirPods, then turn on Pause Media While Falling Asleep.

## Use Custom EQ with your AirPods

You can [use a three-band equalizer](https://support.apple.com/guide/airpods/te819024i19r/web) on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to personalize the sound signature of your AirPods 4 or later, your AirPods Pro 2 or later, or your AirPods Max 2.
