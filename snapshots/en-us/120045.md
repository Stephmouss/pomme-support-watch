# Use Screen Time on Apple Vision Pro

With Screen Time in visionOS, you can turn on Content & Privacy Restrictions to manage content, apps, and settings. You can also restrict explicit content, purchases, and changes to privacy settings.

You can change or turn off these settings for yourself, or for a child in your Family Sharing group, at any time.

- In your Apple Vision Pro, open Settings. Tap Screen Time, then tap Content & Privacy Restrictions.

- Turn on Content & Privacy Restrictions, then set the controls that you want:

- iTunes & App Store Purchases — Restrict or block App Store purchases and require a password for iTunes, Book, and App Store purchases.

- Allowed Apps & Features — Choose apps and features that you want to be available on the Home Screen. Turning off an app or feature doesn't remove it from Apple Vision Pro.

- App Store, Media, Web, & Games — Set restrictions for what kind of content is available from Apple Media Services, on the web, and in Game Center.

- Intelligence & Siri — Choose the Apple Intelligence and Siri features that you want to allow on Apple Vision Pro.

- To restrict changes, tap Lock Screen Time Settings and set a passcode that will be required before changing settings.

If you have Screen Time settings for a child in your family group,* some settings for limiting usage or communication — such as App Limits, Downtime, and Communication Safety — aren't available on Apple Vision Pro and won’t carry over to the child account being used on Apple Vision Pro.

* Age varies by country or region.
