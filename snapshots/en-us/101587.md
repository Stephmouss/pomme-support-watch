# Use Maps on your iPhone to find your parked car's location

On an iPhone 6 or later with iOS 10 or later, Maps can drop a parked car marker when you disconnect your iPhone from your car’s Bluetooth or CarPlay system.

## Before you begin

- [Turn on Location Services](https://support.apple.com/102647) : In the Settings app, tap Privacy & Security. Then tap Location Services, and turn on Location Services.

- Turn on Significant Locations: In the Settings app, tap Privacy & Security. Next tap Location Services, tap System Services, and then turn on Significant Locations or Significant Locations & Routes.

## Turn on Show Parked Location in Maps

- In the Settings app, tap Apps.

- Tap Maps.

- Scroll to Show Parked Location and turn the feature on.

[Image: Turn on Show Parked Location in the Maps page of the Settings app.]

## Receive notifications about your parked car

- In the Settings app, tap Notifications.

- Tap Maps.

- Turn on Allow Notifications and select the type of alerts that you want to receive.

## Get directions to your parked car

- [In Maps, search for Parked Car](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-directions-to-your-parked-car-ipha13ef1c2e/26/ios/26) . Choose Parked Car from the results to view the location, edit the location, or add notes. Tap Directions to then route to your car.

- On your Home Screen, tap the Search field. Tap Parked Car below the Siri Suggestions to get directions to your car.

You need to turn on Bluetooth and use Maps on an iPhone 6 or later with iOS 10 or later to use Show Parked Location. Maps drops a parked car marker when you disconnect your iPhone from your car’s Bluetooth or CarPlay system and exit your vehicle.
