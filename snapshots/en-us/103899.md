# If Final Cut Pro won't detect certain duplicate clips

If duplicate clip detection in Final Cut Pro 10.6.2 doesn’t work for certain clips, speed changes might have been applied to those clips.

[Duplicate clip detection](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro/vercefac2468/mac) , introduced in Final Cut Pro 10.6.2, won't detect [retimed clips](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro/ver15f86b6a/mac) .

Apple will update this article when more information is available.
