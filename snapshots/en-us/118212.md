# View your purchase history for the App Store and other Apple media services

Use the web or your device to find your purchase history, including a list of apps, subscriptions, music, and other purchased content.

## See your purchase history online

- Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/?s=6) .

- Sign in to your Apple Account to see a list of your purchases.

- If you're not sure what you were charged for but know the amount, search for the amount.

Learn what to do [if you can't find the item that you’re looking for](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212#cannotfind) or [if you find unexpected charges](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212#unexpected) .

## See your purchase history on your device

If you can't find an item in your purchase history at reportaproblem.apple.com, you can check your purchase history on your device.

### On your iPhone

- In the App Store, tap your photo or initials. Or, tap the My Account button and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Tap Apps & Purchase History. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Tap Purchase History.

- If you're not sure what you were charged for but know the amount, search for the amount.

- To view more purchases, tap "Last 90 days" at the top of Purchase History and choose another way to filter your purchases.

[Image: The Purchase History screen on an iPhone, displaying a list of recent transactions with dates and amounts.]

### On your iPad

- In the App Store, tap your photo or initials. Or, tap the My Account button and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Tap Apps & Purchase History. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Tap Purchase History.

- If you're not sure what you were charged for but know the amount, search for the amount.

- To view more purchases, tap "Last 90 days" at the top of Purchase History and choose another way to filter your purchases.

### On your Mac

- In the App Store, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Or, click the Sign In button and sign in to your Apple Account.

- On the page that appears, click Apps & Purchase History. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Purchase History to see your purchases.

- To view more purchases, click "Last 90 Days" at the top of Purchase History and choose another way to filter your purchases.

### On your Windows PC

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Or, click Sign In and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click View My Account. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Scroll to the Purchase History section. Next to Most Recent Purchase, click See All. It might take a moment for your Purchase History to appear.

- To view more purchases, click "Last 90 Days" at the top of Purchase History and choose another way to filter your purchases.

## If you can't find an item in your purchase history

If an item isn't in your purchase history, a few reasons might apply.

### If you have more than one Apple Account

You might have used a different Apple Account to buy an item. Sign in to the other Apple Account and recheck your purchase history.

### If you received a suspicious email notification about a purchase

The email might not be from Apple or reflect actual charges to your account. [Learn how to identify legitimate App Store or iTunes Store emails](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102406) .

## If you find purchases that you don't recognize or unexpected charges

Unfamiliar charges might be from multiple purchases grouped into one bill or items bought by a family member.

### Check with your family members

- If you find an item in your purchase history that you don't remember buying, ask your family members if they bought the item. If family members have access to your device, you might want to [require a password for every purchase](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119848) .

- If someone else is using your Apple Account and password, [change your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101567) .

### Check for grouped purchases

Apple might group multiple purchases and subscriptions into one bill, based on [how Apple bills apps, content, and subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118244) .

### Manage the purchase or subscription

- If you purchased a subscription that you no longer want, you can [cancel the subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) .

- If a purchase is eligible, you can [request a refund](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223) .

### If you don't recognize a charge on your billing statement

Learn what to do [if you don't recognize a charge from Apple on your billing statement](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108092) from your bank or financial institution.
