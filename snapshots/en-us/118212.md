# View your purchase history for the App Store and other Apple media services

Find your purchase history online, including a list of apps, subscriptions, music, and other purchased content.

## How to see your purchase history online

- Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/?s=6) .

- Sign in with your Apple Account and password to see a list of your purchases.

- If you're not sure what you were charged for but know the amount, search for the amount.

Learn what to do [if you can't find the item that you’re looking for](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212?device-type=windows-pc#cannotfind) or [if you find unexpected charges](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212?device-type=windows-pc#unexpected) .

## How to see your purchase history on your device

If you can't find an item in your purchase history at reportaproblem.apple.com, you can check your purchase history on your device.

### On your iPhone

- Open the App Store app.

- Tap the Sign-In button or your photo at the top of the screen.

[Image: the Sign-In button]

- Tap Purchase History to see your purchases. You might be asked to sign in.

- If you're not sure what you were charged for but know the amount, search for the amount.

- To view more purchases, tap "Last 90 days" at the top of Purchase History and choose another way to filter your purchases.

[Image: The Purchase History screen on an iPhone, displaying a list of recent transactions with dates and amounts.]

### On your iPad

- Open the App Store app.

- Tap the Sign-In button or your photo at the top of the screen.

[Image: the Sign-In button]

- Tap Apps & Purchase History.

- Tap Purchase History to view your purchases. You might be asked to sign in.

- If you're not sure what you were charged for but know the amount, search for the amount.

- To view more purchases, tap "Last 90 days" at the top of Purchase History and choose another way to filter your purchases.

### On your Mac

- Open the App Store app.

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar.

- Click Account Settings. You might be asked to sign in.

- Click Purchase History to see your purchases.

- To view more purchases, Click "Last 90 Days" at the top of Purchase History and choose another way to filter your purchases.

### On your Windows PC

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then choose View My Account. You might need to sign in with your Apple Account.

- Scroll to the Purchase History section. Next to Most Recent Purchase, click See All. It might take a moment for your Purchase History to appear.

- To view more purchases, click "Last 90 days" at the top of Purchase History and choose another way to filter your purchases.

## If you can't find an item in your purchase history

If you have more than one Apple Account, you might have used a different Apple Account to buy an item. Sign in with the other Apple Account and recheck your purchase history.

If you received a suspicious email notification about a purchase, the email might not be from Apple or reflect actual charges to your account. [Learn how to identify legitimate App Store or iTunes Store emails](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102406) .

## If you find purchases that you don't recognize or unexpected charges

- If a purchase is eligible, you can [request a refund](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223) .

- If you purchased a subscription that you no longer want, you can [cancel the subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) .

- If you find an item in your purchase history that you don't remember buying, ask your family members if they bought the item. If family members have access to your device, you might want to [require a password for every purchase](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119848) . If someone else is using your Apple Account and password, [change your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101567) .

- Multiple purchases and subscriptions might be grouped into one bill, based on [how apps, content, and subscriptions from Apple are billed](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118244) .

- Learn what to do [if you don't recognize a charge from Apple on your billing statement](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108092) from your bank or financial institution.
