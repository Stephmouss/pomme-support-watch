# If you need to change or update your Apple Account payment method

Payment information that you use for Apple media purchases and subscriptions might be automatically updated by your financial institution. Learn how to edit your payment method manually.

## Update your payment method

## Update your payment method on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- In Settings, tap your name.

- Tap Payment & Shipping. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- If you need to update the billing information of an existing card, tap the payment method, then tap Edit.

- Enter your new billing information, then tap the Done button .

### If there's an issue, change your payment method

- In Settings, tap your name.

- Tap Payment & Shipping. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Tap Add Payment Method, enter the new payment method, then tap Done.

- Tap Edit.

- Tap the red delete button next to the old payment method, then tap Remove.

[Image: An iPhone screen showing Payment & Shipping, list of Payment Methods.]

- If you want, you can then re-add the old payment method with updated billing information.

## Update your payment method on your Mac

- In the App Store, click your name in the sidebar. Or, click the sign-in button and sign in to your Apple Account, then click your name in the sidebar.

- Click Account Settings. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Payment Methods.

- If you need to update the billing information of an existing card, click the payment method, then click Edit.

- Enter your new billing information, then click Done.

### If there's an issue, change your payment method

- In the App Store, click your name in the sidebar. Or, click the sign-in button and sign in to your Apple Account, then click your name in the sidebar.

- Click Account Settings. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Payment Methods.

[Image: macOS screen showing the Manage Payments screen.]

- Click Add Payment Method, then enter the new payment method. Click Done.

- Click Edit.

- Click the red delete button next to the old payment method, then click Remove.

- If you want, you can then re-add the old payment method with updated billing information.

## Update your payment method on your Windows PC

- On your Windows PC, open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app. Learn [how to get Apple's apps for Windows](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT210384) .

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click View My Account. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account first. (If you have iTunes for Windows, use the menu bar to choose Account, then select View My Account.)

[Image: Windows screen in Apple Music or Apple TV app showing View Profile and View My Account options.]

- Next to Payment Information, click Manage Payments.

- If you need to update the billing information of an existing card, click Edit next to the payment method.

- Enter your new billing information, then click Done.

### If there's an issue, change your payment method

- On your Windows PC, open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app. Learn [how to get Apple's apps for Windows.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290)

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click View My Account. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account first. (If you have iTunes for Windows, use the menu bar to choose Account, then select View My Account.)

- Next to Payment Information, click Manage Payments.

- Click Add Payment, then enter the new payment method. Click Done.

- Click Edit next to the old payment method, then click Remove Payment Method.

[Image: Windows screen: Edit Payment Information settings, indicating Apple uses encryption to protect personal info.]

- If you want, you can then re-add the old payment method with updated billing information.

## More ways to manage your payment methods

- If you need to add a new payment method or change the order in which your payment methods are billed, [learn how to add a payment method to your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118429) .

- If you are moving to a new country or region, [learn how to change the country or region for your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118283) .

- If you need to edit Apple Pay information, [change or remove the payment cards that you use with Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118219) .

- [Learn what to do if you can't remove a payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118291) .
