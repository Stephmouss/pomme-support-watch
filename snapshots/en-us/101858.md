# Replace the Apple I/O card in your Mac Pro (2019 or later)

Learn how to remove and replace the Apple I/O card in your Mac Pro.

## Check required tools

- Phillips #1 screwdriver

If you need to order a replacement Apple I/O card, you can purchase it through the [Self Service Repair process](https://support.apple.com/self-service-repair) .

## Remove the housing or top cover

Follow the steps to remove the housing or top cover on your Mac Pro.

### Mac Pro (2019 or later)

- [Shut down your Mac Pro](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201150#turnoff) .

- Wait approximately 5-10 minutes for the computer to cool down.

- Unplug all cables except the power cord from your Mac Pro.

- Touch the metal housing on the outside of the Mac Pro to discharge any static electricity, then unplug the power cord. Always discharge static before you touch parts or install components inside your Mac Pro. To avoid generating static, don’t move around the room until you finish installing the Apple I/O card and slide the housing back onto the computer.

- Flip the top latch up, then twist left to unlock the housing.

[Image: Flipping the top latch to unlock the housing on a Mac Pro (2019 or later).]

- Lift the housing straight up and off of the computer. Carefully set it aside. Mac Pro won't turn on when its cover is removed.

[Image: Mac Pro (2019 or later) tower with arrow indicating how to lift and remove the housing]

### Mac Pro (Rack, 2019 or later)

- [Shut down your Mac Pro](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201150#turnoff) .

- Wait approximately 5-10 minutes for the computer to cool down.

- Unplug all cables except the power cord from your Mac Pro.

- Touch the metal housing on the outside of the Mac Pro to discharge any static electricity, then unplug the power cord. Always discharge static before you touch parts or install components inside your Mac Pro. To avoid generating static, don’t move around the room until you finish installing the Apple I/O card and slide the top cover back onto the computer.

- Slide the top cover release latches to the unlocked position, lift the top cover and pull it away from the front plate, then set it aside.

[Image: Mac Pro (2019) housing being unlocked and removed, with arrows indicating the direction of movement.]

## Remove and install the Apple I/O card

- Unscrew the two Phillips #1 screws on the clamp plate that covers slots 5-8, then remove the plate. The screws are captive and stay attached to the clamp plate.

[Image: Unscrew the two Phillips #1 screws on the clamp plate that covers slots 5-8, then remove the plate.]

- Slide the PCI retention latch to the unlocked position.

[Image: Slide the PCI retention latch to the unlocked position.]

- Hold the edges of the Apple I/O card and pull it straight out of the slot depending on your computer:

- Mac Pro (2023): Slot 7

- Mac Pro (2019): Slot 8

[Image: Hold the edges of the Apple I/O card and pull it straight out of slot 7 for Mac 2023, or slot 8 for Mac 2019.]

- Insert the new Apple I/O card and push firmly and evenly until the card is fully seated. Use the same slot as before depending on your computer.

- Mac Pro (2023): Slot 7

- Mac Pro (2019): Slot 8

[Image: Insert the new Apple I/O card into the slot inside the Mac Pro and push firmly and evenly until the card is fully seated.]

- Slide the PCI retention latch to the locked position.

[Image: Inside the Mac Pro, slide the PCI retention latch to the locked position]

- Place the clamp plate back over slots 5-8 and fully tighten two Phillips #1 screws.

[Image: Place the clamp plate back over slots 5-8 and fully tighten two Phillips #1 screws.]

## Reinstall the housing or top cover

Follow the steps to reinstall the housing on Mac Pro (2019) or the top cover on Mac Pro (Rack, 2019).

### Mac Pro (2019 or later)

- Carefully lower the housing over the Mac Pro, being careful not to touch any of the circuit boards.

[Image: Mac Pro (2019 or later) tower with arrow indicating how to lower and reinstall the housing]

- After the housing is fully seated, twist the top latch right and flip it down to lock it.

[Image: Flipping the top latch to lock the housing on a Mac Pro (2019 or later).]

- Connect the power cord, display, and any other peripherals.

### Mac Pro (Rack, 2019 or later)

- Align the edge of the top cover with the slot in the front plate, then firmly press near the release latches and listen for them to click into place.

[Image: Removing the housing of a Mac Pro (2019) by twisting and flipping the top latch, as indicated by arrows.]

- Connect the power cord, display, and any other peripherals.

## Send your original I/O card back

If you received a replacement part from Apple, follow the steps for [Mac Pro (2023)](https://support.apple.com/kb/ht213660#sendback) or [Mac Pro (2019)](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT210556#sendback) to send your original part back to Apple.
