# Beats: Executive functions

Using your Executives is as easy as the slide of a switch and press of a button—literally.

## On again, off again

- Slide the power switch to turn on the headphone and [Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)](http://support.apple.com/kb/HT201129)

- Slide it back to turn off your headphones.—look at you, saving batteries

## ANC and audio

- Insert the straight end of the cable into the ear cup.

- Insert the L-shaped end of the cable into your device.

- Turn the headphones on.

The LED light will glow green if you have enough power or red if you need new batteries.

## Mute

- To mute audio and noise cancelation press and hold the b button on the right ear cup

- Release to resume audio and/or noise cancellation
