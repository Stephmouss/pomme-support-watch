# About third-party video plug-in compatibility with Final Cut Pro on Mac computers with Apple silicon

Update third-party video plug-ins to FxPlug4 to use them with Final Cut Pro on Mac computers with Apple silicon.

Click the links on this page to see which video plug-ins from these popular developers are compatible with Mac computers with Apple silicon. Check this page and the manufacturers' websites periodically for the most up-to-date information on FxPlug4 compatibility.

- [FxFactory](https://fxfactory.com/products/applesilicon/)

- [MotionVFX](https://www.motionvfx.com/applesilicon)

- [CoreMelt](https://coremelt.com/pages/coremelt-plugins-support-for-fxplug4)

- [Color Finale](https://colorfinale.com/apple-silicon)

- [Digital Anarchy](https://digitalanarchy.com/beautyVID/FCP-fxplug4.html)
