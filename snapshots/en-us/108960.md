# See lyrics in Apple Music on your Mac or PC

With your Apple Music subscription, you can follow along as you listen to your favorite songs with perfectly timed lyrics.

## How to see lyrics on your Mac or PC

- Open the Apple Music app.

- Play a song from the Apple Music catalog.

- At the top of your screen, click Lyrics to show or hide time-synced lyrics.

[Image: the Lyrics button]

- If available, time-synced lyrics will automatically appear on your screen.

- If time-synced lyrics aren’t available, then you might see the full lyrics for the song instead.

## If you don't see lyrics on your Mac or PC

If the Lyrics button is grayed out, you’re playing a song that's not from the Apple Music catalog, lyrics aren’t available for that song, or you're not connected to the internet.

## If lyrics are missing or incorrect

You can report missing or incorrect lyrics by going to [www.apple.com/feedback/apple-music.html](https://www.apple.com/feedback/apple-music.html) .

Apple Music availability might vary by country or region. Learn [what's available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
