# Use your iPad after service

Here's how to use your iPad after service.

Your serviced part or product is covered by a 90-day service warranty, or the remainder of your original product warranty or AppleCare plan, whichever is longer. Consumer law will also apply to your serviced or replaced product where available.

- If your iPad Wi-Fi + Cellular uses a SIM card, [insert the SIM card into your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT212767) .

- [Restore your iPad from an iCloud or iTunes backup](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT204184) .

- [Check your Apple ID device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT205064) list to confirm that you're signed in.
