# If you can’t open workflow extensions in Final Cut Pro

If you can’t open workflow extensions in Final Cut Pro on a Mac with Apple silicon, open the extension from the Applications folder and then open Final Cut Pro.

Close Final Cut Pro, then open the extension from the Applications folder, which [will install Rosetta](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT211861) . Then you can open Final Cut Pro and open the workflow extension.
