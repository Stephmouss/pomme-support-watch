# GarageBand for iOS 2.0: Restoring purchased instruments and sounds

If you update to GarageBand for iOS 2.0 via iTunes on your Mac or PC, you may need to restore the instruments and sounds that you previously purchased from Apple.

To restore your purchases, tap Already Purchased when you’re asked if you want to purchase additional instruments or sounds.
