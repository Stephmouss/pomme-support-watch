# See your Apple Account balance transaction history in Wallet

View purchases made with your Apple Account balance in Wallet on iPhone or Apple Watch.

## Check your recent Apple Account balance transactions in Wallet

If you make a [purchase with your Apple Account balance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118245) , you can see your recent transaction history in the Wallet app on iPhone or Apple Watch * .

### Before you begin

- If you haven't already, [add your Apple Account card to Wallet](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/apple-account-iph5f869c0af/ios) .

- [Update your iPhone to iOS 18 or later](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/update-ios-iph3e504502/ios) or [update your Apple Watch to watchOS 11 or later](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/update-apple-watch-software-apd105341ac3/watchos) .

### On iPhone

- Open the Wallet app.

- Tap your Apple Account card.

- Your recent purchase history appears under Latest Transactions. To view the details of a transaction, tap it.

[Image: An iPhone 16 Pro shows the Wallet app with the Latest Transactions for Apple Account.]

### On Apple Watch

- Open the Wallet app.

- Tap your Apple Account card.

- Turn the Digital Crown to scroll and view your recent transactions. Your recent purchase history up to 90 days appears under Latest Transactions. To view the details of a transaction, tap it.

[Image: An Apple Watch shows the Wallet app with the Latest Transactions for Apple Account.]

## If you can't find a purchase made with your Apple Account balance in your transaction history in Wallet

If you can't find an item purchased with your Apple Account balance in your recent transaction history in Wallet, you can also [view your purchase history online](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212) .

Learn what to do [if you can't find the item that you’re looking for](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212#cannotfind) or [if you find unexpected charges](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212#unexpected) .

*In some countries and regions, Apple Account balances are not supported. [Check if Apple Account balance in your country or region is supported](https://support.apple.com/HT202631) .
