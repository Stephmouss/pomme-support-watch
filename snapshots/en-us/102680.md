# Scan a QR code with your iPhone or iPad

Use your Camera app or Code Scanner to quickly access websites, apps, tickets, and more.

## How to scan a QR code with the Camera app or Code Scanner

- Open the Camera app or Code Scanner on your device:

- Open the Camera app from the Home Screen, Lock Screen, or Control Center; or [from the Camera Control on supported models](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-the-camera-control-iph0c397b154/ios) .

- Open Control Center and tap the [Scan Code](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102680#codescanner) button .

Hold your device so that the QR code appears, then tap the link to open the content.

[Image: iPhone shown in Camera app with a tappable QR code link.]

## Add Code Scanner to Control Center

- [Open Control Center](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/aside/iph7c412b4db/18.0/ios/18.0) .

- Touch and hold the background of the screen, then tap the Add a Control button.

- In the Control Center gallery, tap the Scan Code control, then tap anywhere on the screen to return to Control Center.
