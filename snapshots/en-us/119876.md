# If an app on your iPhone or iPad stops responding, closes unexpectedly, or won’t open

If an app on your iPhone or iPad doesn't work as expected, try this.

## Close and reopen the app

[Force the app to close](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109359) . Then open the app to see if it works as expected.

## Restart your device

[Restart your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118259) or [restart your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101603) . Then open the app to see if it works as expected.

## Check for updates

To see if updates are available for the app, open the App Store, tap your profile icon, then tap App Updates. If updates aren't available for the app, [update the software on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

## Delete the app, then redownload it

[Delete the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101550) . When you do this, you might lose data that was stored in the app. Then [redownload the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102417) . Then check to see if the problem with the app is fixed.

## If the app is still not working as expected

- If you found the app on the App Store, [contact the app developer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102435) .

- If you bought the app recently and you no longer want it, you can also [request a refund](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223) .
