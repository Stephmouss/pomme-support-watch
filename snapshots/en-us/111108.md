# If Active Noise Cancellation isn't working as expected on your AirPods

If you're having issues with Active Noise Cancellation on your AirPods Pro, AirPods Pro 2, AirPods Pro 3, or AirPods 4 (ANC), these solutions might help.

## Before you start

- Make sure that you have the latest software on your connected [iPhone, iPad,](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) or [Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) .

- Check that your AirPods Pro, AirPods Pro 2, AirPods Pro 3, or AirPods 4 (ANC) are on the latest [firmware version](https://support.apple.com/en-us/106340) .

- For AirPods Pro and AirPods 4 (ANC), avoid blocking or covering the external microphones. Blocked or covered microphones may cause temporary high-pitched squeals, chirps, feedback, or other audio performance issues.

## Check your AirPods settings

- With your AirPods Pro, AirPods Pro 2, AirPods Pro 3, or AirPods 4 (ANC) in your ears and connected to your device, make sure that [Active Noise Cancellation](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108918) is turned on.

- [Make sure that Adaptive Audio isn't enabled](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104979) . Adaptive Audio adjusts noise-control settings based on your surroundings.

- Play audio and remove one AirPod from your ear. If the audio doesn’t pause automatically, or if you can’t enable Active Noise Cancellation from Settings or Control Center, [reset your AirPods](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118531) .

## Use the Ear Tip Fit Test or the Acoustic Seal Test

Your AirPods ear tips should make a good seal with your ear canal for the best sound quality and noise cancellation. You can check the seal of your AirPods Pro 1 and AirPods Pro 2 with the Ear Tip Fit Test, or check the seal of your AirPods Pro 3 with the Acoustic Seal Test:

- With your AirPods Pro in your ears and connected to your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings > Bluetooth.

- Tap the Info button next to your AirPods in the list of devices.

- Tap Ear Tip Fit Test, or tap Acoustic Seal Test.

- Tap Continue, then tap the Play button .

Learn how to [choose the right ear tip size](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119849) for a good seal and optimal sound performance.

## Clean your AirPods

If you experience a loss of bass sound or an increase in background sounds when you enable Active Noise Cancellation, you might need to [clean your AirPods](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102672) or [clean your AirPods Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120409) .

## Reset your AirPods

If Active Noise Cancellation still isn't working as expected, [reset your AirPods](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118531) . If Active Noise Cancellation still isn’t working after you've reset your AirPods, [you might need service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
