# Delete apps on your iPhone or iPad

Learn how to delete apps on your iPhone or iPad.

## Before you get started

- [Cancel any in-app subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) that you don't need. If you delete an app, it doesn’t automatically cancel in-app subscriptions.

- Find the app you want to delete. If you can't find the app, [use Spotlight to search for it](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118232) . You can delete apps from Spotlight.

## How to delete an app

- Touch and hold the app.

- Tap Remove App .

[Image: Remove App icon]

[Image: An iPhone screen showing the menu that appears when you touch and hold an app. Remove App is the last option in the menu.]

- Tap Delete App, then tap Delete to confirm.

If you touch and hold an app from the Home Screen and the apps start to jiggle:

- Tap the Remove icon in the upper-left corner of the app.

[Image: An iPhone screen showing an app with the Remove icon in the upper-left corner of the app.]

- Tap Delete App, then tap Delete to confirm.

- Tap Done.

[Learn how to delete app data from your iCloud backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922#backups)

## If you don't see the option to delete an app

Some built-in Apple apps can't be deleted from your iPhone or iPad. Here’s a list of built-in [Apple apps you can delete from your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118582) . If you can't delete an app that's not made by Apple, [check your parental control settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105121#prevent-purchases) . Make sure Deleting Apps is set to Allow, then try deleting the app again.
