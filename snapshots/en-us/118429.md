# Add a payment method to your Apple Account

Add a payment method that you and your family can use for the App Store, iCloud+, Apple Music, and more. If you can't add a payment method, learn what to do.

## Add a payment method

## Add a payment method on your device

You can use your device to add a payment method to your Apple Account.

### Add a payment method on your iPhone

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap your name.

- Tap Payment & Shipping. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Tap Add Payment Method.

[Image: An iPhone screen showing the Payment & Shipping settings for Apple Account. To add a payment method, tap Add Payment Method.]

- Enter the payment method details, then tap Done.

#### Reorder payment methods on your iPhone

- On the Payment & Shipping screen, tap Edit.

- Touch and hold a payment method to drag it further up or down your list of payment methods. Apple will attempt to charge your payment methods in the order that they appear.

- Tap Done.

If you already have a payment method on file, learn how to [change or update your payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118293) .

### Add a payment method on your Mac

- Open the App Store.

- Click your name. If your name doesn't appear, click the Sign In button, sign in to your Apple Account, then click your name.

- Click Account Settings. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Next to Payment Information, click Manage Payments.

- Click Add Payment.

- Enter the payment method details, then click Done.

[Image: A Mac screen showing the Manage Payment settings.]

#### Reorder payment methods on your Mac

On the Payment Information screen, use the arrows next to each payment method to move it further up or down your list of payment methods. Apple will attempt to charge your payment methods in the order that they appear.

If you already have a payment method on file, learn how to [change or update your payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118293) .

### Add a payment method on your Windows PC

- On your Windows PC, open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then choose View My Account. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account first.

[Image: A Windows screen showing the Apple Music app. To manage your payment method, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click View My Account.]

- Next to Payment Information, click Manage Payments.

- Click Add Payment.

[Image: A Windows screen showing the Manage Payments settings in the Apple Music app. To add a payment method, click Add Payment.]

- Enter the payment method details, then click Done.

#### Reorder payment methods on your Windows PC

On the Payment Information screen, use the arrows next to each payment method to move it further up or down your list of payment methods. Apple will attempt to charge your payment methods in the order that they appear.

If you already have a payment method on file, learn how to [change or update your payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118293) .

## Add a payment method online

You can also add a payment method after signing in to [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com) .

In some countries and regions, when you edit payment information online at account.apple.com, additional payment methods might be removed from your account automatically.

## If you can't add a payment method

- Check what [payment methods you can use with your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111741) in your country or region.

- If you're using an accepted payment method but your Apple Account is set to a different country or region, [change your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118283) .

- Double-check that your name, billing address, and other information is spelled correctly and matches what you have on file with your financial institution.

- Some payment methods require that you verify through your financial institution's app, text message, or other means. Contact your financial institution for help if you're unable to verify.
