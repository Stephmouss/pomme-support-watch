# Restore an iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch that needs a newer version of iOS or iPadOS

If you're restoring your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch from an iCloud backup or a computer, your device might need a newer version of iOS or iPadOS.

## Restoring from an iCloud backup

If you restore an iCloud backup that requires a newer version of iOS or iPadOS, the system might ask you to update your software. If you choose to download and install the update, your device will automatically update your software and then restore your backup.

## Restoring from a computer

If you restore your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch backup from a computer needing a newer iOS or iPadOS, an error might appear saying that you can't restore the backup. Follow these steps to update your device and restore the backup.

## Set up, update, and erase your device

- [From the Transfer Your Apps & Data screen](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105132) on your device, tap Don't Transfer Apps & Data. If you tapped Restore from iCloud Backup, tap Back to return to the Set Up screen.

[Image: Options for transferring apps and data to a new iPhone during setup.]

- Follow the remaining steps. For now, you can skip the steps for Apple Account, Touch ID or Face ID, and passcode.

- Once the setup is complete, update your device to the [latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- Let the update finish, and wait for your device to restart.

- Go to Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap Transfer or Reset [Device].

- Tap Erase All Content and Settings.

After you erase your device, your device restarts, and you'll see the Hello screen in the Setup Assistant.

You are now able to set up your device again, and select a backup to restore from.
