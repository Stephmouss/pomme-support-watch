# Take a screenshot on your iPhone

Quickly save what's on your screen.

After you take a screenshot in iOS 26, your device automatically displays the screenshot with quick tools available. You can [draw on the image with Markup](https://support.apple.com/119875) , crop your image, send the screenshot to other apps or contacts, or tap the save button to choose where to save your screenshot on your device.

[Image: In iOS 26, you can quickly edit, save, and share a screenshot right after you capture it.]

In iOS 18 and earlier, a thumbnail temporarily appears in the lower-left corner of your screen. Tap the thumbnail to open it or swipe left to dismiss it.

## How to take a screenshot on an iPhone with Face ID

- Press the side button and the volume up button at the same time. [Play](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102616) [Pause](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102616) [Replay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102616)

- Quickly release both buttons.

- After you take a screenshot, edit, share, or save it. In iOS 18 or earlier, a thumbnail temporarily appears in the lower-left corner of your screen. Tap the thumbnail to open it or swipe left to dismiss it.

## How to take a screenshot on iPhone models with Touch ID and side button

- Press the side button and the Home button at the same time.

- Quickly release both buttons.

- After you take a screenshot, edit, share, or save it. In iOS 18 or earlier, a thumbnail temporarily appears in the lower-left corner of your screen. Tap the thumbnail to open it or swipe left to dismiss it.

## How to take a screenshot on iPhone models with Touch ID and top button

- Press the top button and the Home button at the same time.

- Quickly release both buttons.

- After you take a screenshot, edit, share, or save it. In iOS 18 or earlier, a thumbnail temporarily appears in the lower-left corner of your screen. Tap the thumbnail to open it or swipe left to dismiss it.

## Where to find screenshots

Open Photos, then go to Collections and tap Media Types. Tap Screenshots.
