# See your content at a glance in the Apple Support Community

Manage and streamline your view of the threads, people, and communities you follow in the Apple Support Community.

With the improved filtering options, you can manage Subscriptions content by Community or Threads. You can filter threads to find the answers that you’re looking for, discover new threads that interest you, or find questions that other members still need help with. When you’re signed in to [the Apple Support Community](https://discussions.apple.com/welcome) , you can access your Subscriptions view in your profile or from the navigation bar in the top-right corner.

When you view another member’s profile, you can see what threads, communities, and members they follow in the Subscriptions tab. If you want to always see the content that another member shares in the community, click Follow, and their posts will appear in your Subscriptions view.
