# Restore your iPhone or iPad with a nearby device

During a wireless software update, your iPhone or iPad might ask you to move it closer to another device. If you see two devices moving together, you can use a second iPhone or iPad to restore yours.

## Check device compatibility

This feature works on these devices.

### iPhone

- iPhone Duo

- iPhone 18 Pro Max

- iPhone 18 Pro

- iPhone 17 models

- iPhone Air

- iPhone 16e

- iPhone 16 models

- iPhone 15 Pro models

### iPad Pro

- iPad Pro (M4 and M5) models

- iPad Pro 12.9-inch (5th–6th generation)

- iPad Pro 11-inch (3rd–4th generation)

### iPad Air

- iPad Air (M2, M3, and M4) models

- iPad Air (5th generation)

### iPad mini and iPad

- iPad mini (A17 Pro)

- iPad (A16)

The other device needs iOS 18 or iPadOS 18, or later.

## Restore your device

- Plug the recovery-mode device into a power source.

- Place the recovery-mode device near an unlocked device connected to Wi-Fi. 1 2

- Tap Continue and wait for the devices to connect.

[Image: An iPhone showing the Restore Nearby iPhone message.]

- Note the six-digit code on the recovery-mode device.

- Enter this code on the other device to connect to Wi-Fi.

- When you see the "Restoring [device]" message, tap OK.

If you want to view the restore progress, bring the devices next to each other again.

If you see a restore screen, or prefer to use a computer, you can [restore with a computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108969) .

- Make sure that you unlock the second device.

- Captive networks and networks that require 802.1X authentication (networks that require a username and password) aren't supported.
