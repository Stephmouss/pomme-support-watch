# Restore your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch from a backup

Learn how to restore your device from a backup in iCloud or on your computer.

- [Restore your device from an iCloud backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118105#icloud)

- [Restore your device from a backup on your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118105#computer)

- [Restore your device after a service appointment](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118105#service)

## Restore your device from an iCloud backup

- Turn on your device. You should see a Hello Screen. If you already set up your device, you need to [erase all of its content](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108931) before you can use these steps to restore from your backup.

- Follow the onscreen setup steps until you reach the Transfer Your Apps & Data screen, then tap From iCloud Backup.

[Image: An iPhone showing From iCloud Backup as the first option.]

- Sign in to your Apple Account.

- Choose a backup. Look at the date and size of each and pick the most relevant. After you choose a backup, the transfer starts. If a message says that you need a newer version of software, follow the onscreen steps to update. [Learn what to do if you don't see onscreen steps to help you update your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108811) .

- When asked, sign in to your Apple Account to restore your apps and purchases. If you've purchased iTunes or App Store content using multiple accounts, you'll be asked to sign in to each account. If you can't remember your password, you can tap Skip this Step and sign in later. Until you sign in to your Apple Account, you will not be able to use the apps.

- Stay connected to Wi-Fi and wait for a progress bar to appear and complete. Depending on the size of the backup and the network speed, the progress bar might need a few minutes to an hour to complete. If you disconnect from Wi-Fi too soon, the progress will pause until you reconnect.

[Image: An iPhone showing the status of restoring your device from an iCloud backup.]

- Finish setting up your device. Content like your apps, photos, music, and other information will continue to restore in the background. This may take several hours or days, depending on the amount of information on your device. Try to connect often to Wi-Fi and power to let the process complete.

## Restore your device from a backup on your computer

- Open the Finder or the [Apple Devices app](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) on Windows. Open [iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290) on Windows without the Apple Devices app or macOS Mojave or earlier. [Find out which macOS your Mac is using](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033) .

- Connect your device to your computer with a USB cable. If a message asks for your device passcode or to Trust This Computer, follow the onscreen steps. Get help [if you forgot your passcode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118430) .

- [Select your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108392) when it appears in the Finder window, the Apple Devices app sidebar, or the iTunes window. Learn what to do [if your device doesn't appear](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108643) .

[Image: A Finder window showing iPhone selected in the sidebar.]

- Select Restore Backup. Learn what to do [if a message says that your device's software is too old](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108811) .

- Look at the date of each backup and pick the most relevant.

- Click Restore and wait for the restore to finish. If asked, enter the password for your encrypted backup.

[Image: A Finder window showing a prompt to choose a backup and enter your password.]

- Keep your device connected after it restarts and wait for it to sync with your computer. You can disconnect after the sync finishes.

## Restore your device after a service appointment

When you get your iPhone or iPad back from an Apple Store or Apple Authorized Service Provider, keep in mind you might need to restore your device. If so, you need to [restore it from an existing iCloud backup or a backup on your computer](https://support.apple.com/118105) , or set it up as a new device.

If you choose to set up your iPhone or iPad as a new device, then decide to restore the device to an existing iCloud backup at a later time, you need to [erase your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108931) and restore it from a previously saved iCloud backup as soon as possible. If you don't erase your device and restore from a previous iCloud backup, the new iCloud backups created when you set up the device as a new device, will overwrite your previously saved iCloud backups.
