# If you can’t send or receive messages using Messages on Mac

If you can’t send or receive messages using the Messages app on Mac, try these solutions.

- Check our [System Status page](http://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/) to learn whether iMessage is experiencing a service outage.

- [Make sure that your Mac is connected to the internet](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101589) . You should be able to load webpages and receive email, for example.

- Restart your Mac.

- [Install the latest macOS updates](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) .

- [Check Date & Time settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/change-date-time-settings-mchlp1124/mac) to make sure that the date and time are correct. [Can't change the time or time zone?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101619)

- Make sure that you're addressing the message to the correct phone number or email address.

- If Messages shows an alert stating that the person is not registered with iMessage, or you can't continue SMS/MMS conversations that you send and receive on your iPhone, make sure that your iPhone is turned on and you have [set up Text Message Forwarding](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102545) correctly.

- Learn what to do [if your Mac says that it couldn't sign in to iMessage](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108791) , or that it couldn't contact the iMessage server.

If you still can't send or receive messages, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
