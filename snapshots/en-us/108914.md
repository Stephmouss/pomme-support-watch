# If you see 'invalid SIM' or 'no SIM' on your iPhone or iPad

If you get an alert that says Invalid SIM or No SIM Card installed, follow these steps.

- Make sure that you have an active plan with your wireless carrier.

- If your iPhone is locked to a carrier, it can be used only by that carrier. To check if your iPhone is unlocked:

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap General

- Tap About. If your iPhone is unlocked, "No SIM restrictions" appears next to Carrier Lock. [Learn how to unlock iPhone for use with a different carrier](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109316) .

- Restart your [iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118259) or [iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101603) .

- [Check for a carrier settings update](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109324) .

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap General.

- Tap About. If an update is available, you'll see a prompt to select OK or Update.

- If your device has an eSIM, [contact your carrier](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101569) .

- If your device has a physical SIM card, remove your [iPhone SIM card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109357) or [iPad SIM card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102392) from the SIM card tray and then put the SIM card back. Make sure that the SIM tray closes completely and isn’t loose. If you use a SIM tray from a different model iPhone, iPad, or from another phone manufacturer, it might not fit properly.

- If your device has a physical SIM card and you've completed the previous step, try using another SIM card. If you don't have one, go to your carrier's retail store and ask them to test with another SIM card. You might need to replace your SIM card.

If you still see an alert about your SIM card, you might need service. [Get service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
