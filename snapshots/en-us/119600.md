# If Final Cut Pro for iPad isn't working

Learn what to do if Final Cut Pro for iPad won't open, won't play your project, or isn't operating as expected in other ways.

Before proceeding, make sure to [back up your Final Cut Pro for iPad projects and media](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro-ipad/dev49e6feccf/ipados) .

The information in this article is for troubleshooting and resolving technical issues.

[Learn more about managing your subscription](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro-ipad/dev1d3962929/ipados)

## Try these steps in order

After trying each step in this list, test Final Cut Pro for iPad again to see if you've fixed your issue.

- [Restart your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#Restart)

- [Disconnect external devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#External)

- [Check system requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#System)

- [Make sure the app is working as expected](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#Guide)

- [Update Final Cut Pro for iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#Update)

- [Check device compatibility](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#Device)

- [Check media compatibility](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#Media)

- [Test with the demo project](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#Demo)

- [Remove and reinstall Final Cut Pro for iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#reinstall)

## Restart your iPad

To restart your iPad, [turn it off, then turn it back on](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/ipad63d30b5a/ipados) .

[Back to top](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#top)

## Disconnect external devices

If an external device like a camera or storage device is connected to your iPad, disconnect it:

- [Turn off your iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/ipad63d30b5a/ipados) .

- Disconnect all external devices.

- [Turn on your iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/ipad63d30b5a/ipados) .

If the issue only occurs when a particular external device is connected, you might need to update or service the device. Contact the manufacturer for support.

[Back to top](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#top)

## Check system requirements

Make sure your iPad and version of iPadOS meet the [Final Cut Pro for iPad system requirements](https://www.apple.com/final-cut-pro-for-ipad/#footnote-7) .

[Back to top](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#top)

## Make sure the app is working as expected

To make sure the app is performing as you expect, check the [Final Cut Pro for iPad User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro-ipad/welcome/ipados) . You can search the User Guide for key terms shown in alerts, alert icons, or error messages.

[Back to top](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#top)

## Update Final Cut Pro for iPad

- Try to complete all active projects.

- If you haven't already, [back up your projects and media](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro-ipad/dev49e6feccf/ipados) .

- [Update Final Cut Pro for iPad](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT202180) .

[Back to top](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#top)

## Check device compatibility

Check that you're using a [supported camera](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT213668) . Contact the manufacturer of the device for support. If a software update for the device is available, follow the manufacturer's instructions to update firmware for devices like cameras and external storage devices.

[Back to top](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#top)

## Check media compatibility

Check that you're using a [supported media format](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro-ipad/dev3f1bb94c2/ipados) .

[Back to top](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#top)

## Test with the demo project

[Download a new copy of the demo project included with Final Cut Pro for iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro-ipad/deve71a8fd41/ipados#deva5073eff7) . Then try to recreate the issue you were having using the demo project and the included media.

[Back to top](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#top)

## Remove and reinstall Final Cut Pro for iPad

When you delete the Final Cut Pro for iPad app from your iPad, all related data — including projects, timelines, and imported media — are also deleted. Back up your projects before deleting the app from your iPad.

- [Back up your projects and timelines](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro-ipad/dev49e6feccf/ipados) .

- [Remove the Final Cut Pro app from your iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/remove-apps-ipad0aed1df8/ipados) .

- [Reinstall Final Cut Pro for iPad](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/ipadee10c6e7/ipados#iPadc5d51bbd) .

- [Test with the demo project](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119600#Test) to see if the issue is resolved. If the issue no longer occurs, [reimport your backed-up projects](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro-ipad/import-final-cut-pro-for-ipad-projects-deva6238c512/ipados) .
