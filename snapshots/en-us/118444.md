# How to set and change alarms on your iPhone

Set, edit, or delete an alarm with the Clock app. You can also ask Siri to set an alarm for you.

## How to set an alarm

- Open the Clock app, then tap the Alarms tab.

- Tap the Add button .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

[Image: Set an alarm on iPhone in the Clock app.]

- Set a time for the alarm. You can also choose one of these options:

- Repeat: Tap to set up a recurring alarm.

- Label: Tap to name your alarm.

- Sound: Tap to pick a sound that plays when the alarm sounds.

- Snooze: Turn on to see a Snooze option when the alarm sounds and set a Snooze Duration.

- Tap the Done button .

## How to change the alarm's volume

- In the Settings app, tap Sounds & Haptics.

- Under Ringtone and Alerts, drag the slider left or right to set the volume. As you drag, an alert will play, so you can hear how the volume changes.

- Turn on Change with Buttons to use the volume buttons on your device to change the alarm volume.

Learn what to do [if the alarm volume is too low or quiet](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118444#gethelp) .

## How to edit an alarm

- Open the Clock app and tap the Alarms tab.

- Tap Edit in the upper-left corner.

- Tap the alarm, make your changes, then tap Save.

[Image: Edit an alarm on iPhone in the Clock app.]

## How to delete an alarm

- Swipe left over the alarm and tap Delete.

- Tap Edit, tap the Delete button , then tap Delete.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Edit, tap the alarm, then tap Delete Alarm.

## If the alarm volume is too low or quiet

Do Not Disturb, the Ring/Silent switch, and Silent mode don't affect the alarm sound. If you use the Ring/Silent switch or Action button to turn on Silent mode or turn on [Do Not Disturb](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105112) , the alarm still sounds.

If you have an alarm that doesn't sound or is too quiet, or if your iPhone only vibrates, check the following:

- Set the volume on your iPhone. If your alarm volume is too low or too loud, press the volume up or volume down button to adjust it. In the Settings app, you can also tap Sounds & Haptics and drag the slider under Ringtone and Alerts.

- If your alarm only vibrates, make sure that your alarm sound isn't set to None. Open the Clock app, tap the Alarms tab, then tap Edit. Tap the alarm, then tap Sound and choose a Sound.

- If you connect headphones to your iPhone, the alarm plays at a set volume through the built-in speakers on your iPhone as well as wired and wireless headphones.

- When you use StandBy, haptics for your alarms are disabled. To make sure that your alarm sound isn't set to None: open the Clock app, tap the Alarms tab, then tap Edit. Tap the alarm, then tap Sound and choose a Sound.

[Manually adjusting the time on your iPhone in Settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101619) to change app behavior, like game timers, isn't recommended and can affect alarms.
