# What happens after you choose Sign Out But Don't Erase

Learn what information stays on your device and what happens after you sign back in to your Apple Account when you choose Sign Out But Don't Erase from your iPhone, iPad, Apple Vision Pro, or Mac.

- [What data stays on your device when you sign out but don’t erase](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122682#data_on_device)

- [How to keep iCloud data that hasn't synced](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122682#keep_unsynced_data)

- [What happens when you merge iCloud data](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122682#merge_icloud_data)

## What data stays on your device when you sign out but don’t erase

If you want to sign out of your Apple Account on your iPhone, iPad, Apple Vision Pro, or Mac but plan to keep the device for yourself, you can choose Sign Out But Don't Erase. This lets you sign out of your Apple Account without erasing your device so that later you can sign back in faster.

When you choose this option, most data stays on your device. This includes:

- Health

- Passwords

- Contacts

- Photos

- Documents

- Apps

- Safari and bookmarks

- Wallet passes

- Freeform

- Reminders

- Notes

- Calendars

- Data that hasn't yet synced with iCloud

To remove all of this data from your device when you sign out of your Apple Account, choose "Erase this [device]" instead.

To help prevent data loss on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro, make sure that you have an up-to-date [iCloud backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108366) before you sign out. You can also [archive or make copies of the information you store in iCloud](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108306) using your Apple devices. If you recently made changes to content synced with iCloud, wait before signing out, or check [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/) or another device to make sure that your changes sync.

[Learn more about what iCloud backs up](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770)

## How to keep iCloud data that hasn't synced

In some cases, you might get a warning that data that hasn't yet synced with iCloud will be removed from the device if you choose Sign Out But Don't Erase. If you receive this warning:

- Make sure that you have a good Wi-Fi connection.

- [Check that you haven't exceeded your iCloud storage quota](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mm039c13d410/) . If you've exceeded your iCloud storage, [learn how to make more space](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922) .

- Open the Settings app (or System Settings on Mac), tap your name, then tap iCloud.

- Check that the status icon next to Saved to iCloud is green. If it's yellow or red, check the status message, then follow the onscreen instructions to resume syncing.

- Check [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/) or another device to make sure that your changes sync.

Or, you can [archive or make copies of this iCloud data](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108306) before you sign out.

## What happens when you merge iCloud data

If the previous device owner chose to sign out and not erase and you sign in to that device with your Apple Account, data on the device merges with your iCloud data and then syncs to your other devices.

If you don't want to merge your iCloud data with the previous device owner's data, on the "Merge iCloud data from this [device]?" screen, tap Go back. To remove the data left behind from the prior device owner, on the screen labeled "Previous data will be removed from this [device]", tap Continue.
