# GarageBand for iOS: Audio may not switch outputs with Apogee Jam

If you are using an Apogee Jam and you connect or remove external speakers or headphones from the headphone jack, audio may not switch to play through the built in speaker, external speakers, or headphones.

[Restart the device](http://support.apple.com/kb/ht1430) to resolve this issue.
