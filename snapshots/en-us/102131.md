# Before you sell, give away, return, or recycle your AirPods

Learn how to get your AirPods ready for a new owner — including unpairing them from your Apple Account.

## Remove your AirPods from your Apple Account

AirPods can be paired with only one Apple Account at a time. You need to remove AirPods from your Apple Account before giving away or selling them. That way, the new owner can pair them to their Apple Account and take advantage of all the features — including Find My.

You can remove AirPods from your Apple Account on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. You only need to remove AirPods from your Apple Account on one device to unpair.

Your AirPods don't have to be connected via Bluetooth to your device — but the process might happen more quickly if your AirPods are Bluetooth-connected and within Bluetooth range of your device.

### Remove AirPods from your Apple Account on iPhone or iPad

- Open the Find My app.

- Select the Devices tab, tap your AirPods, then swipe up.

- Tap Remove This Device, then tap Remove to confirm.

### Remove AirPods from your Apple Account on Mac

- Open the Find My app

- Select the Devices tab, then select your AirPods.

- On the map, click the More Info button .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Click Remove This Device.
