# If the camera or flash on your iPhone or iPad isn't working

Get help with camera or flash issues. After troubleshooting, you might need to get service.

## Get help with these issues

- Photos are blurry, have visual artifacts or lens flare, or show poor visual quality

- The variable aperture on your iPhone 18 Pro or iPhone 18 Pro Max won't change

- The camera screen is black

- The camera flash keeps blinking or other issues with the flash

## Try these steps first

- Remove any case, film, or accessory (such as a polarizer, extender, or magnifier) that might block the camera or flash or that places a magnet near the camera.

- If photos are blurry, clean the front and back camera lenses with a microfiber cloth.

- Restart your [iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118259) or [iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101603) .

## Troubleshoot the camera

- [Update your iPhone or iPad to the latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- Test the camera by trying to take a photo or—with iPhone 18 Pro or iPhone 18 Pro Max—changing the variable aperture.

- If your device has a front and rear camera, check if one or both aren't working. To switch cameras, tap the rotate button .

- If you see motion blur in low-light situations and you use a lens converter, metallic case, or a magnetic lens mount, try removing the accessory.

- If the camera screen is black, use iOS Diagnostics to test the camera:

- Start Recovery Assistant by turning off your device and pressing and holding the side button when your device starts up.

- Tap Diagnostics Mode, then follow the on-screen instructions. When asked to choose a diagnostic, tap Mobile Resource Inspector, then tap Start Diagnostic.

- If Mobile Resource Inspector finds an issue, [get service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .

[Learn more about Recovery Assistant](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123114)

## Troubleshoot the flash

- [Try the flashlight](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105001) in [Control Center on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108330) or [Control Center on your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111790) . On an iPhone X or later or an iPad, swipe down from the upper-right corner of the screen. On an iPhone 8 or earlier, swipe up from the bottom edge of any screen. Tap the Flashlight button .

- If the flash works only sometimes, your camera might be set to Flash Auto. To choose a different setting, open the Camera app and tap the flash button .

- Learn what to do [if the flash blinks repeatedly](https://support.apple.com/en-us/103256) .

## Get service

If the camera or flash still isn't working properly, you might need service. [Get service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
