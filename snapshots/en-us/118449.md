# Hide privacy indicators on external displays in macOS

In macOS Sonoma 14.4 or later, you can choose to hide the privacy indicators on a secondary external display while you're viewing an app in full screen. The privacy indicators will continue to appear on your primary (main) display.

## Turn off privacy indicators for external displays on your Mac

Requires macOS Sonoma 14.4 or later

The privacy indicator is the orange dot (microphone indicator) or green dot (camera indicator) that appears in the menu bar to show that your microphone or camera is in use. To hide the indicator on a secondary external display while viewing an app in full screen:

- [Start up from macOS Recovery](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102518) .

- From the Utilities menu in the menu bar in macOS Recovery, choose Terminal.

- Enter this command: system-override suppress-sw-camera-indication-on-external-displays=on

- Quit Terminal.

- From the Apple menu , choose Restart, then log in to your user account.

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings, then click Privacy & Security in the sidebar.

- Click Microphone. Or click Camera.

- Turn off Privacy Indicators.

If the external display is not also your primary (main) display, the privacy indicator no longer appears in the menu bar. (If the external display is the only display in use by your Mac, it's the primary display.)
