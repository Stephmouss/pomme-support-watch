# Collaborate on documents in Keynote, Pages, and Numbers

With built-in collaboration, work on presentations, documents, or spreadsheets with others in real time.

With collaboration in Keynote, Pages, and Numbers on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro, you can invite others to collaborate on a document, set permissions for the document, see who’s worked on the document and a summary of the changes they’ve made, and communicate with participants from within the app.

You can also collaborate using Keynote, Pages, and Numbers [online at iCloud.com](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105052#online) .

## Invite others to collaborate

When you invite people to collaborate on a presentation, document, or spreadsheet, you send a link to them. Up to 100 people can view and edit a shared document at the same time.

To start collaborating:

- [Sign in to iCloud on your device turn on iCloud Drive](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/set-up-icloud-on-your-devices-mmfc0f1e2a/icloud) for Keynote, Pages, and Numbers.

- With your document open, click or tap the Share button in the toolbar, choose Collaborate from the pop-up menu, then choose how you want to invite others.

If your document is larger than 2 GB (4 GB if you're an Apple Creator Studio subscriber), you'll need to use Keynote, Pages, or Numbers to [reduce the document size before inviting others to collaborate](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119884) .

If you have a managed Apple Account, you can collaborate with others outside of your organization. Contact your administrator to turn on those settings.

## Set permissions

To set permissions for who can view, edit, and invite others to collaborate on your document, click or tap the pop-up menu below the Collaborate pop-up menu.

You can set the following options:

- To allow only specific participants to access the document, choose “Only people you invite.” To open the shared document, those participants must sign in to iCloud or [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/) with an Apple Account. If they don't have an Apple Account, they can create one after you share the document with them.

- To allow anyone who has the link to the shared document to be able to open it, choose “Anyone with the link.”

- To allow anyone who can access the document to be able to edit and print it, choose “Can make changes.”

- To allow anyone who can access the document to be able to view and print, but not edit it, choose “View only.”

- If you don't want participants to be able to invite others to collaborate, deselect “Anyone can add more people.”

## Collaborate on a shared document

After you open the document, you can see the edits made by others in real time and what they’ve done recently. Depending on your device, the number of people editing or viewing the document appears on the Collaboration button in the toolbar, which appears after you invite others. You can also view a list of participants by clicking or tapping the Collaboration button.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

When editing a shared document, you can use [all of the features of Keynote, Pages, and Numbers, with some exceptions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105052#limits) .

## See the latest activity

In Keynote, Pages, and Numbers on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, tap or click the Collaboration button in the toolbar to see what’s happened since the last time you visited the document. You can also start communication with participants from within the app and change activity settings.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Click or tap Show All Activity to see who’s worked on the document and a summary of the changes they’ve made. Click or tap the disclosure arrow to see more detailed changes, such as edits made by one participant in several different places. Or tap an activity to show where the change was made. Unread activities are highlighted, and tapping a highlighted activity will mark all unread activities as read.

- Click or tap a name in the participant list to jump to where they are currently working in the document.

- Click or tap Activity Settings to turn notifications on or off for when new people join, make edits, or make comments.

- Click or tap the message button to start a Messages conversation with participants, or tap or click the audio or video button to start a FaceTime call.

With Keynote on Apple Vision Pro, you can collaborate with others on presentations and participants can see your activity, but you can't see others' activities.

## Change a shared document’s settings

To change permissions after you’ve shared your document, click or tap the Collaboration button in the toolbar, then click or tap Manage Shared Document, Presentation, or Spreadsheet, depending on the iWork app you’re using. You can change access and permissions, as well as stop sharing.

## Features not supported with collaboration

If you need to use one of these features, stop sharing the document, make your changes, then share the document again. When you stop sharing, the document is removed from iCloud Drive for all participants. If you've restricted the document to “Only people you invite,” you will need to re-invite everyone after.

These features aren't currently available when you collaborate with other participants:

### Keynote, Pages, and Numbers

- Reduce File Size (File > Reduce File Size).

- Drag rows and columns between body and header areas in tables.

- Create, delete, or reorder styles.

- Edit with Ruby in Asian languages.

- Create or edit custom cell formats.

- Replace every instance of a font in a document.

* You can't reduce the size of a shared document, but you can reduce the size of a copy.

### Keynote

- Change slide size.

- Change theme.

- Record a slideshow or play a previously recorded slideshow.

- Add or edit a soundtrack.

- Clear a soundtrack or delete tracks.

- Clear an existing recording of a slideshow.

### Pages

- Adjust footnote settings.

- Insert, cut, copy, paste, delete, duplicate, reorder, or edit sections.

- Adjust EndNote settings.

- Add and edit section layout objects.

- Convert Word Processing documents to Page Layout documents and vice versa.

- Edit page number settings.

- Add or edit page templates.

- Reapply a page template to a page.

### Numbers

- Insert smart fields.

- Copy or paste table styles.

- Transpose tables.

### Keynote Pages, and Numbers for iCloud

You can use Keynote, Pages, and Numbers online at iCloud.com to invite others to collaborate on a document, set permissions for participants, and collaborate. But some features aren’t currently available when you collaborate from [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) :

- If you track changes in a Pages document, you can view the document and select text, but not edit it.

- If your Keynote, Pages, or Numbers document has any vertical text, you can view an image of the document on iCloud.com, but not edit it.

- Edit objects inside groups.

- You can jump to another participant's cursor in a collaborative document, but other collaboration features like the activity feed and tools to start communications aren't available in Keynote, Pages, and Numbers for iCloud. Changes made to a collaborative document from iCloud.com are still shown in the activity feed of participants who are using Keynote, Pages, and Numbers on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

You can still edit documents containing tracked changes and vertical text in Keynote, Pages, and Numbers on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## If you can't collaborate on a document

If you use Keynote, Pages, or Numbers and collaboration isn't available, you might need to update your apps. [Turn on automatic updates](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102629) so that your apps always stay up to date. If your device is too old to update your apps, you can still collaborate online at [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/) using a Mac or PC.

If you want to send a copy of the document, you can send it without collaborating. Click or tap the Share button, choose Send Copy from the pop-up menu, then choose how you want to send the document.

## Minimum system requirements and iCloud requirements for collaboration

To view or edit a shared document, and invite others to participate in the presentation, you and the people you share it with need any of the following:

- A Mac with macOS Sequoia 15.6 and Keynote, Pages, or Numbers 15.3 or later.

- An iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 18 and Keynote, Pages, or Numbers 15.3 or later.

- An Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 2 and Pages or Numbers 15.3 or later, or visionOS 2.4 and Keynote 15.3 or later.

Keynote, Pages, and Numbers for iCloud works with a Mac or PC using Safari 13.1 or later, Google Chrome, or Microsoft Edge.
