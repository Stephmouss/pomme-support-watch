# Change the language on your iPhone or iPad

You can change the language setting on your iPhone or iPad if it's incorrect or if you've accidentally changed the language to one you don't understand.

## Find the language options in the Settings app for iOS

- In the Settings app, tap General.

- Select Language & Region.

- Tap Add Language.

[Image: An iPhone showing the Language & Region menu, with Add Language highlighted.]

- Select your language.

- An alert will ask you which language you want to use as your primary language. Tap to select your primary language.

After your device updates the language, your device automatically displays the language you selected.
