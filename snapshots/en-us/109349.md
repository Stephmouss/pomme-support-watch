# Use AirPrint to print from your iPhone or iPad

Learn how to print from your iPhone or iPad to an AirPrint-enabled printer.

## Before you begin

- Confirm that your printer supports AirPrint, which requires Wi-Fi or wired network connections. Contact your printer's manufacturer for details.

- Make sure that your printer and device are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

## Print with AirPrint

- Open the app that you want to print from.

- To find the print option, tap the Share button or the Share button or the Actions button .

[Image: Share button]

[Image: Actions button]

- Scroll down and tap the Print button or Print. Not all apps support printing. If you can’t find the print option, check the app’s User Guide or Help section.

[Image: Print button]

[Image: iPhone showing the option to tap the Print button.]

- Tap No Printer Selected and choose an [AirPrint-enabled printer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102895) .

- Choose the number of copies or other options, like which pages you want to print.

- Tap Print in the upper-right corner.

AirPrint works with Wi-Fi or wired network connections. AirPrint devices connected to the USB port of an Apple AirPort Base Station or AirPort Time Capsule, or to your computer using Bluetooth connection, can’t use AirPrint.
