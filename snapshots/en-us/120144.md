# How to set up your Apple Vision Pro

Learn what comes in the box and how to get started using Apple Vision Pro.

## What's in the box

Your Apple Vision Pro comes in the box complete with:

- Enclosure and Audio Straps

- Light Seal

- Light Seal Cushion

- Dual Knit Band*

Also included in the box is a second Light Seal Cushion, battery with attached power cable, USB-C Charge Cable, USB-C Power Adapter, Apple Vision Pro Cover, and Apple Vision Pro Polishing Cloth.

You need to charge the battery, connect the power cable, and attach your ZEISS Optical Inserts, if needed.

*Apple Vision Pro (M2) came with the Solo Knit Band and Dual Loop Band.

## Before you set up your Apple Vision Pro

- Make sure that you have an Apple Account with [two-factor authentication](https://support.apple.com/102660) turned on.

- Check that you have a strong Wi-Fi connection. To set up Apple Vision Pro with a [captive Wi-Fi network](https://support.apple.com/102554) , you need visionOS 1.1 or later. Learn how to [update your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/118481) .

- Make sure that you're in a safe area free of obstacles. [Learn more about how to safely use your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/118507) .

- Learn how to [get the best fit with Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/117730) , including how to remove and attach the head bands, how to put it on, and how to protect Apple Vision Pro when not in use.

## Set up your Apple Vision Pro

- [Charge your Apple Vision Pro battery](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/tancf125362f) using the included power adapter and USB-C Charge Cable.

- On your Apple Vision Pro Audio Strap, insert the power cable with the power cable angled slightly upward. The dot on the power cable should line up with the white outlined dot on the Audio Strap.

[Image: Illustration showing the dot on an Apple Vision Pro power cable lining up with the dot on the device's audio strap.]

- Rotate the power cable clockwise until it locks in.

[Image: Illustration showing an Apple Vision Pro power cable rotating into a locked position on the device's audio strap.]

- When you see the battery light pulse on the power cable, the power cable is successfully connected and your Apple Vision Pro turns on.

- Remove the cover on the front of Apple Vision Pro.

- If you purchased prescription or reader ZEISS Optical Inserts for vision correction, you can [attach your ZEISS Optical Inserts](https://support.apple.com/117731) .

- When you hear a tone or see that the battery light on the power cable is solid, Apple Vision Pro is ready to wear.

- [Put on your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/117730) and make sure that you have a good fit.

- Follow the steps onscreen to begin visionOS setup.

After Apple Vision Pro is turned on and the battery stays connected, it will sleep when you take it off and wake when you put it on.
