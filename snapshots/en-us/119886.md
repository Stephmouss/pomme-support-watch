# Use Keynote Remote to control your Keynote presentation

You can control your Keynote presentation from an iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch using Keynote Remote.

- [Check your requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119886#req)

- [Link Keynote Remote to your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119886#linkmac)

- [Link Keynote Remote to another iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119886#linkios)

- [Use Keynote Remote](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119886#use)

## Check your requirements

Keynote Remote is included with Keynote for iPhone and iPad. To use Keynote Remote to control your presentation, here's what you need:

- An iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch with Keynote 2.1 or later for iPhone or iPad

- A Mac with Keynote for Mac 6.1 or later

- A remote device linked to your Mac or another iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch

## Link Keynote Remote to your Mac

You can play your presentation on your Mac and remotely control it with your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch.

- [Turn on Wi-Fi on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT202639) and [turn on Wi-Fi on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201974) .

- On your Mac, open Keynote.

- From the menu bar, choose Keynote > Settings (or Preferences), click Remotes, then select the "Enable" checkbox.

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the Keynote app, then tap the Keynote Remote button in the toolbar. If you don't see the Keynote Remote button, tap the Back button , then tap the Keynote Remote button.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- On your iPhone or iPad, tap Continue.

- On your Mac, next to the name of your iPhone or iPad, click Link.

- Make sure that the four-digit passcode that appears matches on both devices, then click Confirm on your Mac.

After you link your devices, learn how to [use Keynote Remote](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119886#use) .

## Link Keynote Remote to another iPhone or iPad

You can play your presentation on iPhone or iPad and remotely control it with another.

- [Turn on Wi-Fi on both devices](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT202639) .

- Open your Keynote presentation on the device with the presentation.

- Tap the More button in the toolbar, tap Keynote Remote, then turn on Enable Remote Control.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- On the device that you want to use as a remote, open the Keynote app.

- Tap the Keynote Remote button in the toolbar. If you don't see the Keynote Remote button, tap the Back button , then tap the Keynote Remote button.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- On your iPhone or iPad, tap Continue.

- On the presentation device, tap Link next to the remote device.

- Make sure that the four-digit passcode that appears matches the code on your device, then click Confirm.

After you link your devices, learn how to [use Keynote Remote](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119886#use) .

## Use Keynote Remote

- After you link Keynote Remote to your Mac, iPhone or iPad, follow these steps to control your slideshow.

- [Turn on Wi-Fi on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT202639) and [turn on Wi-Fi on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201974) .

- Open your Keynote presentation on the device with the presentation.

- On the Keynote Remote device, open the Keynote app, then tap the Keynote Remote button . If you don't see the Keynote Remote button, tap the Back button , then tap the Keynote Remote button.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

[Image: Back button]

- Tap Play, then choose what you want to do:

- To use a different layout, tap the Layout button , then select an option.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- To move to the next or previous slide, swipe in either direction.

- To highlight or point to parts of a slide during a presentation, tap Highlighter button , then choose an option. To close Highlighter, tap the Close button .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- To end the presentation, tap the Close button or swipe down with two fingers.

If your devices can't stay connected, connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network [on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201974) and [on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/kb/ht202639) .
