# Send and save GIFs on your iPhone or iPad

With Messages, you can find hundreds of trending GIFs in #images to add to your messages.

#images is available in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, France, Germany, Italy, India, Singapore, Philippines, and Japan.

## Add a GIF from #images

Use #images in the Messages app to find and share GIFs with your contacts. Here's how:

- Open Messages, tap the Compose button , and enter a contact or tap an existing conversation.

- Tap the add button , then tap the #images button

- To search for a specific GIF, tap Find images, enter a keyword like birthday, then tap search .

- Tap the GIF to add it to your message.

- Tap the Send button .

## Save a GIF

You can save a GIF that you receive in a message to the Photos app on your iPhone or iPad. Here's how:

- Open Messages.

- Open the message that has the previously sent GIF that you want to save.

- Tap and hold the GIF, then tap Save.

## Report a concern with an image

If you find an issue or have a concern with content found in #images, you can report it:

- Tap the #images button and search for the image that you want to report.

- When you find the image, touch and hold the image to see a larger preview.

- [Take a screenshot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102616) , then tap the thumbnail to open the preview.

- Tap the Share button , then tap Mail.

- Email the screenshot to [images-reports@apple.com](mailto:images-reports@apple.com) with a description of your concern.

If you have a copyright infringement claim or question, please [report it here](https://www.apple.com/legal/contact/#copyright-infringement ) .
