# Mac tips for Windows switchers

Learn how to perform some common tasks on your new Mac.

## Right click

Click the right corner of your Apple mouse, or click with two fingers on your Apple trackpad. You can change this in [Mouse settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1138/mac) and [Trackpad settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1226/mac) .

## Scroll, swipe, click

Settings for scroll direction, swipe gestures, and button assignments are also in Mouse and Trackpad settings.

## Close & resize windows

Buttons for [closing, minimizing, and maximizing a window](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp2469/mac) are in the upper-left corner of the window.

## Change volume

Use the volume control in the [menu bar](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1446/mac) or [Control Center](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchl50f94f8f/mac) , or use the volume keys on your Apple keyboard.

## Change Mac settings

Looking for the Control Panel? Use [System Settings (or System Preferences)](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh15217/mac) instead.

## Browse for files

Looking for Windows File Explorer? Learn about the [Finder](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp2605/mac) .

## Find files

Use [Spotlight](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1008/mac) to quickly find and open apps, documents, and other files.

## Open apps

To find and open your apps (programs), you can use [Spotlight](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1008/mac) or the [Dock](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh35859/mac) . You can also open apps from the Applications folder on Macintosh HD. To quickly access the Applications folder, click Applications in the sidebar of any Finder window, or use the Go menu in the Finder menu bar.

## Throw files away

Looking for the Recycle Bin? Use the Trash, which is in the [Dock](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh35859/mac) .

## Rename files

Click the file once to select it, then press the Return key and type a new name. Press Return when done.

## Preview files

[Preview most files on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh14119/mac) using Quick Look. Click the file once to select it, then press Space bar.

## Back up files

[Time Machine](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104984) keeps a copy of all your files, and it remembers how your system looked on any given day.
