# Connect a third-party Bluetooth accessory to your iPhone or iPad

Pair a Bluetooth accessory to your iPhone or iPad to stream music and videos, make calls, and more.

## Pair a Bluetooth accessory

- Go to Settings, then tap Bluetooth and turn on Bluetooth.

- Under My Devices, look for the accessory that you want to pair. If you don't see it listed, consult the accessory's manual or online instructions about how to make a device discoverable in Bluetooth.

- Tap the name of your accessory when it appears. You might need to enter your PIN or passcode. If you need help, consult the accessory's manual or online instructions again.

[Image: iPhone showing Bluetooth settings, with Bluetooth enabled and My Devices showing connected and unconnected devices.]

- Repeat these steps to pair multiple Bluetooth accessories. If you have multiple Bluetooth devices paired with your device, you can [choose which audio source to use](https://support.apple.com/105068) .

## Unpair a Bluetooth accessory

- Go to Settings, then tap Bluetooth.

- Tap the More Info button next to the device that you want to unpair. When you unpair an accessory, it unpairs on all devices signed in to your Apple Account.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Forget this Device. This removes the accessory from your list of available Bluetooth devices, but you can repeat the steps to add it any time.
