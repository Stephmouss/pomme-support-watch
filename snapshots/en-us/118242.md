# Redeem your Apple Gift Card, App Store Card, or App Store & iTunes Gift Card

Redeem your gift card in the App Store or the Apple Music app to use for Apple Account purchases, like apps, subscriptions, and more.

## Redeem your gift card

## Redeem a gift card on your device

You can redeem a gift card on your device to add funds to your Apple Account balance.

### Redeem a gift card in the App Store on your iPhone

- Find the 16-digit code on the back of the card. For some gift cards, you might need to peel or gently scratch off the label to see the code.

- In the App Store, tap your photo, initials, or the My Account button . You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Tap Redeem Code.

[Image: iPhone screen showing the Apple Account page in the App Store with a highlighted Redeem Code button.]

- Tap Use Camera and follow the instructions on the screen. If you have trouble redeeming the card, tap Enter Code Manually, then follow the instructions on the screen.

- Tap Redeem.

### Redeem a gift card in the Wallet app on iPhone

In some countries and regions, you can redeem your gift card in Wallet. To do this, you must first [add your Apple Account card to Wallet](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/apple-account-iph5f869c0af/ios) .

- Find the 16-digit code on the back of the card. For some gift cards, you might need to peel or gently scratch off the label to see the code.

- In the Wallet app, tap your Apple Account card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Redeem.

- Tap Use Camera and follow the instructions on the screen. If you have trouble redeeming the card, tap Enter Code Manually, then follow the instructions on the screen.

- Tap Redeem.

[Can't redeem your gift card?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108285)

### Redeem a gift card on your Mac

- Find the 16-digit code on the back of the card. For some gift cards, you might need to peel or gently scratch off the label to see the code.

- In the App Store, click your name in the sidebar. Or, click the Sign In button and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Redeem Code.

[Image: The Apple Account page in the App Store on Mac with the Redeem Code button highlighted.]

- Click Use Camera and follow the instructions on the screen. If you have trouble redeeming the card, click Enter Code Manually, then follow the instructions on screen.

[Can't redeem your gift card?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108285)

### Redeem a gift card on a Windows PC

- Find the 16-digit code on the back of the card. For some gift cards, you might need to peel or gently scratch off the label to see the code.

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click Redeem. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account first.

- Follow the instructions on screen, then click Redeem.

[Image: The Apple Music app on a PC showing Redeem selected in the sidebar to redeem a gift card.]

[Can't redeem your gift card?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108285)

## Redeem a gift card that someone emailed to you

- Open the gift card email.

- Tap or click the "Redeem Now" button.
