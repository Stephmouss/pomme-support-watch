# Set up Exchange ActiveSync on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

Exchange ActiveSync lets you sync your Exchange account Mail, Contacts, Calendars, Reminders, and Notes with your Apple device.

If you're not in a familiar location, you must first turn off Stolen Device Protection before you configure an Exchange account, and then re-enable it after configuration is complete. [Learn more about Stolen Device Protection](https://support.apple.com/120340)

## Add and configure your Exchange Account

You can add your Exchange account in the Mail app on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro.

### Enter your Exchange email address

- Go to Settings, then tap Apps.

- Tap Mail.

- On iPhone or iPad, tap Mail Accounts.

- On Apple Vision Pro, tap Accounts.

- Tap Add Account, then tap Microsoft Exchange.

- Enter your email address, then tap Next. You can also enter an account description.

[Image: iphone-xs-ios13-settings-account-add-exchange-steps-crop]

### Sign in to configure your Exchange account

After you enter your email address, you'll be prompted to sign in to configure your Exchange account either automatically or through manual configuration.

#### Sign in automatically

- Tap Sign In to automatically discover your Exchange account information.

- Enter your email password, then tap Next.

If your account uses [modern authentication](https://support.apple.com/guide/deployment/dep158966b23) , you'll be guided through a custom authentication workflow.

#### Configure your account manually

- Tap Configure Manually to set up your account with Basic authentication.

- Enter your email password, then tap Next.

You might also be prompted to enter additional server information, which you can get from your Exchange Server administrator.

[Image: iphone-xs-ios13-settings-account-add-exchange-server-steps-crop]

## Sync your content

After you configure your Exchange account, you can sync Mail, Contacts, Calendars, Reminders, and Notes. Turn on the data you want to sync, then tap Save.

[Image: iphone-xs-ios13-settings-account-add-exchange-save-steps-crop]

## Edit your Exchange account settings

- Go to Settings, then tap Apps.

- Tap Mail.

- On iPhone or iPad, tap Mail Accounts.

- On Apple Vision Pro, tap Accounts.

- Tap your Exchange account, then choose the settings you want to change.

- Turn on or off which apps you want to sync.

- Tap Mail Days to Sync to change the number of days Mail will sync with your Exchange mailbox.*

- Tap Automatic Reply to set up automatic out-of-office replies.

To find and change advanced settings such as [SSL and S/MIME](https://support.apple.com/102245) , tap your account name, then tap Advanced Settings.

*Depending on your server capacity and network performance, syncing a large Exchange mailbox might result in a longer syncing time. For faster syncing, you can limit the number of days (for example, by selecting "1 Month" or less) you want Mail to sync with your Exchange account.
