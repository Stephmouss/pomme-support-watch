# How to leave or remove a member from a Family Sharing group

Any adult can remove themselves from a Family Sharing group. If you're the Family Sharing organizer, you can remove members or disband the family group.

- [Remove yourself from a Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102652#leavegroup)

- [Remove someone from your Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102652#removesomeone)

- [Disband a Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102652#disbandgroup)

- [What happens when you stop sharing?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102652#stopsharing)

If there are members in the Family Sharing group between 13-17 * and Screen Time is enabled for their device, the Family Sharing organizer must turn off Screen Time before the member can leave or be removed from the family group. Depending on a child's age and their state, country, or region, they might have to stay connected to a family group until they turn 18 and [can confirm that they're an adult](https://support.apple.com/125662) .

If a child in the Family Sharing group is under 13 * , the Family Sharing organizer needs to [delete the child's Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102634) and let the deletion take effect before they can remove the child from the family group. Or, the Family Sharing organizer can move the Child Account to another family group.

*Age varies by state, country, or region. Where legally required, an Apple Account created for a child under 18 needs to be part of a Family Sharing group. [Learn how settings and features for kids vary by state, country, or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125666) .

## Remove yourself from a Family Sharing group

Any adult can remove themselves from a Family Sharing group.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap Family. If you don't see Family, tap your name, then tap Family.

- Tap your name, then tap Stop Using Family Sharing.

[Image: iPhone screen showing Family Sharing options: Personal, Subscriptions, Purchases, and a red button to Stop Using Family Sharing.]

When you leave a Family Sharing group, [you lose access to any shared purchases and services](https://support.apple.com/102652#stopsharing) shared by the family group. If you're the purchaser of any shared content and services, your family group will lose access to the content or services if you leave the family group.

### On your Mac

- Choose Apple menu , then click System Settings.

- Click Family, then click your name.

- Click Stop Using Family Sharing.

[Image: Family Sharing settings on macOS, showing options to manage members and purchases.]

When you leave a Family Sharing group, [you lose access to any shared purchases and services](https://support.apple.com/102652#stopsharing) shared by the family group. If you're the purchaser of any shared content and services, your family group will lose access to the content and services if you leave the family group.

### Remove your Apple Account from a Family Sharing group online

If your Apple Account is being used to share media purchases with a Family Sharing group that you no longer want to be part of, you can leave the family group and remove your Apple Account from the family group altogether.

- On your iPhone, iPad, or Mac, sign in to [account.apple.com](https://appleid.apple.com/#!&page=signin) with your Apple Account and password.

- On your iPhone, tap the downward-facing arrow at the top of the page, then tap Family Sharing. On your iPad or Mac, tap or click Family Sharing on the left side of the page.

- Tap or click Disconnect Account.

- Tap or click Disconnect.

After you leave the Family Sharing group, you can [set up your own family group](https://setup.iCloud.com/family/messages?aaaction=showFamilyInvite&inviteCode=organizer_create&campaign=kbArticle) or join another one.

## Remove someone from your Family Sharing group

The Family Sharing organizer can usually remove anyone 13 * or older from the Family Sharing group, but it varies depending on the state, country, or region of the user's Apple Account. The Family Sharing organizer can also [transfer a Child Account to another Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/102634) .

When you remove someone from your Family Sharing group, they [lose access to any shared purchases and services shared by the family group](https://support.apple.com/102652#stopsharing) .

### On the Family Sharing organizer's iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap Family. If you don't see Family, tap your name, then tap Family.

- Tap the name of the family member that you want to remove.

- Tap Remove [name] from Sharing Group.

[Image: iPhone screen showing Family Sharing settings with options to manage members and stop sharing.]

### On the Family Sharing organizer's Mac

- Choose Apple menu , click System Settings, click your name, then click Family.

- Click the name of the family member that you want to remove.

- Click Remove [name] from Sharing Group.

[Image: Mac settings screen showing a Family Sharing group with a member selected and options to remove them.]

- Click Remove [your family member's name] to confirm.

## Disband a Family Sharing group

The Family Sharing organizer can typically turn off Family Sharing if all members are 13* and older. However, this may vary depending on the state, country, or region, and if a child is in the family group. When Family Sharing is stopped, all members are removed from the family group at the same time. When you disband a family group, [everyone loses access to shared purchases and services](https://support.apple.com/102652#stopsharing) .

If the Family Sharing group has members under 13, the Family Sharing organizer needs to delete the child's Apple Account and let the deletion take effect before they can remove the child from the family group. Or, the Family Sharing organizer can move the Child Account to another family group. [Learn how move a child to another Family Sharing group.](https://support.apple.com/102634)

### On the Family Sharing organizer's iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap Family.

- Tap your name, then tap Stop Using Family Sharing.

[Image: iPhone Family Sharing screen for Organizer Danny Rico, showing options like Purchased Share, Subscriptions, Location Sharing, Screen Time, Apple Cash, and Apple Card.]

### On the Family Sharing organizer's Mac

- Choose Apple menu , then click System Settings.

- Click Family, then click your name.

- Click Stop to stop using Family Sharing.

[Image: Family Sharing settings showing account details and options to manage members.]

## What happens when you stop sharing?

- Your Apple Account is removed from the Family Sharing group and you won't have access to any services shared by your family, such as an Apple Music family subscription or a shared iCloud+ plan. If you're the purchaser of shared content and services, if you leave a family group, the family group will lose access to the shared content and services.

- You stop sharing locations with your family members and your devices are removed from the family's Find My list.

- If your family shares iTunes, Apple Books, and App Store purchases, you immediately stop sharing your purchases and lose access to the purchases made by your other family members. You keep any purchases you initiated while part of the family group. Other family members can't use content downloaded from your collection.

- Any content that your family shared with you isn't automatically removed from your device. You can purchase it again or remove it to free up space on your device. If you downloaded an app from a family member’s purchase history and made in-app purchases, you'll need to purchase the app yourself to access your in-app purchases.

- If your family organizer set up an Apple Cash account for you as part of [Apple Cash Family](https://support.apple.com/105010) , your account automatically closes and your balance transfers to the family organizer's Apple Cash account. If you're the organizer and you disband the family group, any Apple Cash Family accounts you set up for family members will close and their balances transfer to your account.

- If you have an Apple Watch that uses [Apple Watch For Your Kids](https://support.apple.com/109036) (formerly known as Family Setup), you won't be able to manage that Apple Watch.

- If you share a photo album, calendar, or reminders with family members, you won't share them anymore. If you want to keep using Family Sharing but not share certain features with your family, you can unsubscribe from them instead in the Photos, Calendars, or Reminders apps on your device or on [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/) .

- If you use Apple Card Family and you leave a Family Sharing group, you aren't automatically removed from the Apple Card Family account. [Learn how to remove yourself or a participant from a shared Apple Card account](https://support.apple.com/102240) .

*Age varies by state, country, or region. Where legally required, an Apple Account created for a child under 18 needs to be part of a Family Sharing group. [Learn how settings and features for kids vary by state, country, or region](https://support.apple.com/125666) .
