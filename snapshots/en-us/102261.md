# Update your device purchase date with your proof of purchase

Correct the purchase date for your device if the estimated expiration date of your Limited Warranty or AppleCare plan coverage is incorrect.

To request the correction of the purchase date for your product, you will need to send the original proof of purchase for your product to Apple.

The sales receipt you use should include the following details:

- A clear description of your device

- The date of purchase for the device

- An invoice or receipt number

- The price of the device

- The reseller’s contact information

- The serial number of the device

[Correct the purchase date for your device](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase)
