# Get your Apple Account ready for purchases or downloads on your Apple TV

If you see "No iTunes Store account" on your Apple TV or are unable to complete accepting the terms and conditions, follow the steps below. Then you can download apps, subscribe to streaming services, and make purchases with your Apple Account.

## Sign in on the web

- Go to [tv.apple.com](https://tv.apple.com) or [music.apple.com](https://music.apple.com) .

- Choose Sign In, then sign in to your Apple Account.

- Follow the prompts to complete setup.

If you're using the Apple TV app on a non-Apple device, you can also sign in on the web to set up your Apple Account.

## If you're still unable to make a purchase on your Apple TV

Try the steps again, then return to your Apple TV and sign in to your Apple Account. Accept the terms and conditions to complete setup.
