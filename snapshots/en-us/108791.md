# If you can’t turn on or sign in to iMessage or FaceTime on iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro

When you turn on or sign in to iMessage or FaceTime, your device might say that it couldn't sign in or couldn't contact the iMessage or FaceTime server.

If the issue is occurring on your iPhone, [follow the iPhone instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119859) instead.

## Check your internet connection

Make sure that your device is connected to Wi-Fi or your wireless carrier's cellular data network, and that you can access the internet.

- [Connect to Wi-Fi on iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111107)

- [Connect to Wi-Fi on Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101589)

- [Connect to Wi-Fi on Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/tan75f632320)

## Check for a service outage

Check our [System Status page](http://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/) to learn whether iMessage or FaceTime is experiencing a service outage.

## Check for software updates

- [Update the software on your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575)

- [Update the software on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382)

- [Update the software on your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118481)

## Check for the correct date, time, and time zone

- On iPad, open Settings, tap General, then tap Date & Time to check.

- On Mac, choose System Settings (or System Preferences) from the Apple menu , then [check Date & Time settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1124/mac) .

- On Apple Vision Pro, go to Settings, tap General, then tap Date & Time to check.

Learn what to do [if you can't change the time or time zone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101619) .

## Check your Apple Account

- Go to [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com) and sign in with the same Apple Account that you're using on your device. If you [forgot your account name](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102627) or [forgot your account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102656) , resolve that before continuing.

- If the issue is with iMessage:

- On iPad, open Settings, tap Apps, then tap Messages. If iMessage is turned on, turn it off.

- On Mac, open the Messages app. From the Messages menu in the menu bar, choose Settings (or Preferences), then click iMessage. If you're signed in to iMessage, click Sign Out.

- On Apple Vision Pro, go to Settings, tap Apps, then tap Messages. If iMessage is turned on, turn it off.

- If the issue is with FaceTime:

- On iPad, open Settings, tap Apps, then tap FaceTime. If FaceTime is turned on, turn it off.

- On Mac, open the FaceTime app. From the FaceTime menu in the menu bar, choose Settings (or Preferences), then click General. If you're signed in to FaceTime, click Sign Out.

- On Apple Vision Pro, go to Settings, then tap FaceTime. If FaceTime is turned on, turn it off.

- Restart your device:

- [Restart your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101603)

- [Restart your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp2522/mac)

- [Restart your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118504)

- Return to the same settings screen to turn on or sign in to iMessage or FaceTime. If you're signing in, use the same Apple Account that you used in step 1.

## Try to activate your iMessage or FaceTime account online

- Go to [apple.co/IMFT-mac](https://apple.co/IMFT-mac) .

- Under Enable Apple Account, tap or click Get Started.

- Sign in to your Apple Account and follow the onscreen instructions. If you use a phone number with FaceTime or iMessage, make sure to enter it.

You will receive an email if your account is ready for iMessage or FaceTime use.

## Check for VPN and security software

[Check for VPN and security software](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102281) . If you installed such software, it could be blocking [network ports used by iMessage and FaceTime](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102036) . If you're not sure, it might help to try again while connected to a different network.

## If you still need help

If you get a message that your email address isn't verified, sign in at [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/) and verify the email address. Learn what to do [if you can't verify because you aren't receiving your verification email](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102409) .

If you still need help, the issue might be unrelated to activation or sign-in. Other solutions:

- [If you can't send or receive messages on iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118433)

- [If you can’t send or receive messages on Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102200)

- [If FaceTime isn't working on Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102203)
