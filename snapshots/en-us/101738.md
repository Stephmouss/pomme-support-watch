# Import Sony FX9 media into Final Cut Pro

If you see a missing plug-in alert on Sony FX9 media in your libraries and projects or Sony FX9 media is dimmed when you import, update Final Cut Pro.

To fix this issue, [update to Final Cut Pro 10.4.10](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201541) .

[Contact the vendor](https://support.apple.com/103190) for additional information.
