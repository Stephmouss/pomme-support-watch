# Make a conference call from your iPhone

Set up a conference call on your iPhone with up to five people, if your carrier supports it.

The number of people on a conference call — and the ability to add, merge, or disconnect callers — depends on your carrier. Contact your carrier for more information.

## Start a conference call

- Dial the first person and wait for the call to connect.

- Tap the More button, then tap Add People.

[Image: iPhone showing an active phone call, with the option to tap the More button.]

- Select the second person, and wait for the call to connect.

- Tap Merge to combine calls into one conversation. If you don't see this, your carrier might not support it. Repeat steps 2 and 3 to add more people. Your carrier sets the number of people you can add.

[Image: iPhone showing a phone call on Hold, with the option to merge a second call.]

Only the person who started the conference call can see the names of all participants. But if a participant adds someone else, they can see the name of the person they added and the person who started the conference call.

## Add an incoming caller to the conversation

- If you're on a conference call and someone calls you, tap Accept to add the new caller to the conversation.

- After the call connects, tap Merge. If you don't see this option, your carrier might not support this.
