# Clean your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit

Learn how to clean and care for your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit.

## About cleaning your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit

Your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit earbuds are sweat and water resistant, but not sweatproof or waterproof. The charging case is not waterproof, sweatproof, sweat resistant, or water resistant, so be careful not to get moisture in any openings. Learn more about [water and sweat resistance](https://support.apple.com/ht209542) .

Clean your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit earbuds and charging case if they come in contact with anything that might cause stains or other damage, such as dirt or sand, makeup, ink, soap, detergent, acids or acidic foods, or if they come in contact with liquids, including those that might lead to skin irritation such as sweat, salt water, soapy water, pool water, perfume, insect repellent, lotions, sunscreen, oil, adhesive remover, hair dye, or solvents.

Avoid damaging your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit when you clean them:

- Make sure not to get any liquid in the openings.

- Don’t dry your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, Powerbeats Fit, or charging case using an external heat source (for example, a hair dryer).

- Don’t use cleaning products or compressed air.

- Don't use sharp objects or abrasive materials.

- Don’t insert a foreign object into the USB-C connector on the charging case (for example, a cotton swab or a paper towel).

## Clean your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit

- Remove your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, or Powerbeats Fit earbuds from the charging case.

- Disconnect the charging cable from both the charging case and your computer or power adapter.

- To clean the charging contacts on Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, or Powerbeats Fit, use a cotton swab slightly dampened with warm water. To clean the charging pins on the charging case, use a lint-free cloth slightly dampened with warm water. If necessary, you can slightly dampen the cotton swab or cloth with 70 percent isopropyl alcohol.

- Use a soft, dry, lint-free cloth to clean all other areas of your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, Powerbeats Fit, and charging case. Here's how to [clean your eartips](https://support.apple.com/ht201125) .

## Care for your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit

Clean and dry your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit earbuds and your skin after workouts or heavy sweating. Keep your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit earbuds and charging case clean and dry.

## Care for the USB-C cable

Discoloration of the USB-C connector after regular use is normal. Dirt, debris, and exposure to moisture may cause discoloration. If your USB-C cable or connector becomes warm during use or the charging case won’t charge, disconnect it from your computer or power adapter and clean the connector with a soft, dry, lint-free cloth. Do not use liquids or cleaning products when cleaning the connector.

## Tips to avoid skin irritation

To avoid skin irritation, especially if you have allergies or skin sensitivities:

- Wear your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, or Powerbeats Fit with the right fit. See [Wearing your AirPods, EarPods, and Beats devices](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT211158) .

- After workouts with Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, or Powerbeats Fit, or after your device is exposed to liquids such as sweat, soap, shampoo, makeup, sunscreen, and lotions that can potentially cause skin irritations, clean and dry your device. Keeping your Beats Studio Buds, Beats Studio Buds +, Beats Fit Pro, and Powerbeats Fit, as well as your skin, clean and dry will maximize comfort and prevent long-term damage to your device.

- If you have known allergies or sensitivities to certain substances, [check the materials in Beats headphones and earphones](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT211158) .
