# If you can't connect to your Mac in Apple Vision Pro

Learn what to do if Mac Virtual Display on Apple Vision Pro isn't working as expected.

## If Mac Virtual Display isn't working, try these tips

- Make sure your Mac meets the system requirements. You can use Mac Virtual Display with [any Mac that has macOS 14 Sonoma or later](https://support.apple.com/105113) . If you want to use a Wide or Ultrawide Mac Virtual Display screen, you need a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS Sequoia 15.2 or later, and your Apple Vision Pro needs visionOS 2.2 or later.

- Update Apple Vision Pro and your Mac with the latest software updates. Learn how to [find out which macOS your Mac is using](https://support.apple.com/109033) , and find out how to [update visionOS](https://support.apple.com/118481) and [update macOS](https://support.apple.com/108382) .

- Sign in to the same Apple Account [on your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/sign-apple-vision-pro-tanf9fa7d96e/visionos) and [on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/sign-in-or-out-of-your-apple-id-on-mac-mchla99dc8da/mac) .

- Turn on [iCloud Keychain](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/tana39a7fa0/) on both devices.

- On your Mac, go to System Settings, select [your name], then select iCloud. Select See All next to Saved to iCloud. Select Passwords & Keychain, then make sure Sync this Mac is turned on.

- On your Apple Vision Pro, go to Settings, tap [your name], then tap iCloud. Tap See All next to Saved to iCloud. Tap Passwords & Keychain, then make sure Sync this Apple Vision Pro is turned on.

- Turn on Wi-Fi [on your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/connect-apple-vision-pro-to-the-internet-tan75f632320/visionos) and [on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/change-wi-fi-settings-on-mac-mh11935/mac) .

- Turn on Bluetooth [on your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/connect-bluetooth-accessories-tanaa651a58d/1.0/visionos/1.0) and [on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/turn-bluetooth-on-or-off-blth1008/mac) .

- Make sure you're not using the [Guest User](https://support.apple.com/117742) feature on Apple Vision Pro. Mac Virtual Display doesn't work in this mode.

- Move closer to the Mac that you're trying to connect to.

- Restart [your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/118504) and [your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/shut-down-or-restart-your-mac-mchlp2522/mac) .

## If the Connect button doesn't appear, use Control Center

When you're wearing Apple Vision Pro and you look at your Mac laptop, a Connect button should appear above your computer.

If you have a Mac desktop computer — or if the Connect button doesn't appear above your Mac laptop — turn on Mac Virtual Display in Control Center:

- [Open Control Center](https://support.apple.com/117741#controlcenter) .

- On the right-hand side of Control Center, scroll to the Mac Virtual Display button , then tap it.

- Select your Mac from the list that appears.

## Try connecting from your Mac

- While wearing your Apple Vision Pro, open Control Center on your Mac.

- Click Screen Mirroring.

- Click Extend to Apple Vision Pro.

## Get support and learn more

- If Mac Virtual Display still isn't working, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com) .

- Find out more about [using Mac Virtual Display](http://support.apple.com/118521) .
