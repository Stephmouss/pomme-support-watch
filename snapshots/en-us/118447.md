# How to access and view iCloud Photos

When you store and sync your photos in iCloud, you can view your iCloud Photos in the Photos app on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, or Apple Vision Pro, and on iCloud.com.

## How iCloud Photos works

iCloud Photos keeps every photo and video you take in iCloud, so you can access your library from any device, anytime — just open the Photos app. Changes you make on one device change on your other devices too. If you take a photo on your iPhone, for example, you can see that same photo on your iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro, on [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com/) , or on a Windows PC.

## How to access iCloud Photos

- [Sign in to iCloud](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mmfc0f1e2a/icloud) with your Apple Account on all of your devices.

- [Turn on iCloud Photos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108782#turnon) on every device that you want to view your photos. [Need to set up iCloud Photos on your PC](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud-windows/icw9f0ba665f/icloud) ?

- On any device, open the Photos app.

- Scroll to view your library. Or tap or click Collections to view your Memories, Albums, and more.

Photos in your library with a Messages icon in the lower-left corner have been shared with you and aren't part of your iCloud Photos. To save them to iCloud Photos, tap the image, then tap Save Shared Photo.

[Learn what to do if you're missing photos or videos in the Photos app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118558)
