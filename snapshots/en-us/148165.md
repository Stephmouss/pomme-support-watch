# Wi-Fi 7 availability on Apple devices

These devices support Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be), which can operate across the 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz Wi-Fi bands. Operation on the 6 GHz band is not available in all regions.

## Apple devices that support Wi-Fi 7

[Apple devices that support Wi-Fi 6E or Wi-Fi 7 or later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102285) can join Wi-Fi 7 networks, but only these devices support all of the features of Wi-Fi 7:

- [MacBook Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108052) with M5 Pro or M5 Max chip or later

- [MacBook Air](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102869) with M5 chip or later

- [Mac mini](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102852) with M5 Pro chip or later

- [Mac Studio](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102231) with M5 Max chip or later

- [iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108044) 16 Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max, or later (excluding iPhone 16e, iPhone 17e)

- [iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108044) Air

- [iPad Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108043#ipadpro) with M5 chip or later

- [iPad Air](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108043#ipadair) with M4 chip or later

## Availability of Wi-Fi 7 on the 6 GHz band

Wi-Fi 6E and Wi-Fi 7 features that operate on the 6 GHz band are available only in regions that permit unlicensed Wi-Fi use in that spectrum. If regulatory approval of such operation is unavailable in your region, your device uses only the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands for Wi-Fi.
