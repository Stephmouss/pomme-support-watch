# How to find your lost iPhone or iPad

If you lost your iPhone or iPad, you can use Find My to try to locate it and mark it as lost to protect your personal information.

## Ways to find your iPhone or iPad

You can find a lost iPhone or iPad with the Find My app on another device or on the web at [icloud.com/find](https://icloud.com/find) . If you can't find your device right away, [mark it as lost to protect it](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101593#lost) .

[Learn what to do if your iPhone or iPad was stolen](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120837)

### Use the Find My app on another device

If you have another trusted Apple device that's signed in with your Apple Account, you can use Find My on that device to lock and find your iPhone or iPad.

[How to use Find My on another Apple device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104978)

### Use a family member's device

If you're part of a Family Sharing group, your devices can appear in Find My on your family members' devices.

[How to use a family member's iPhone to locate your lost device](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph6231f621a)

### Use Find Devices on icloud.com/find

When you sign in to [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find/) , you can use Find My to locate your devices. Once you’re in Find My, review the list of your devices under All Devices and tap the one you want to find. Then you can play a sound to help locate it, mark it as lost, or erase it.

## How to mark your iPhone or iPad as lost

When you mark your device as lost, your device is put in Lost Mode, which means your device is locked with your passcode, and payment cards and passes that you use with Apple Pay are suspended. You can also provide a phone number and message that allows others to contact you if they find your device.

Apple will never contact you to say that your iPhone or iPad has been found. Never share your device passcode, passwords, verification codes, or other account information with anyone who requests it.

Stay vigilant to avoid social engineering schemes, including phishing messages, phony support calls, and other scams.

[Learn how to help protect your personal data from social engineering](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102568)

[Image: Lost Mode settings on an iPhone, showing options to enable Lost Mode and preview your customized message and phone number to be displayed on the lost device.]

When you mark a device as lost:

- It only appears online and in Lost Mode if the device has been turned on and is connected to Wi-Fi or cellular.

- If you use [Stolen Device Protection for iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120340) , Face ID or Touch ID is required to turn off Lost Mode — so even if a person has your iPhone and knows your passcode, they're still locked out of your device.

- If you have AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss, don't remove your iPhone from Find My or your Apple Account until your claim has been fully approved.

If Find My wasn't turned on for your device before it was lost, it won't appear at [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find/) or in the Find My app and you can't mark it as lost or remotely erase it.

### Use Find Devices on icloud.com/find

To sign in to [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find/) , you don't need a verification code — so you can mark your device as lost, even if it's your trusted device that's lost. If you're asked for a verification code, select the Find Devices button instead.

[Mark your device as lost on icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find/)

[Learn more about using Lost Mode on iCloud.com](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mmfc0f0165/icloud)

### Use the Find My app on another device or a Family Sharing group member's device

- In the Find My app, go to the Devices tab.

- Scroll down and select your missing iPhone or iPad. On a Family Sharing group member's device, your iPhone or iPad appears underneath their devices.

- Tap the More button .

- Scroll down to Lost [Device], then turn on Lost Mode.

- Follow the onscreen steps if you want to display your contact information on your missing device.

- Tap Activate.

## If your iPhone or iPad is lost permanently

If your iPhone or iPad is lost and you're unable to find it, follow these additional steps to protect your accounts and personal information, and replace your device (if applicable).

### File a claim with AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss

- If your iPhone is covered by AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss, [file a claim for an iPhone replacement](https://support.apple.com/iphone/theft-loss-claims) .

- Don't remove your iPhone from Find My or your Apple Account until your claim is fully approved.

AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss is not available in all countries and regions.

### Report your missing iPhone or iPad

- Report it to local law enforcement. They might request the serial number of your iPhone or iPad, which you can find even if you don't have the device.

- Contact your wireless carrier to report your missing iPhone, ask them to disable your account, and file a claim if your iPhone or iPad is covered under your wireless carrier plan.

[Learn how to find the serial number of your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108037#help)

### Remotely erase your iPhone or iPad

You can remotely erase your device using the Find My app on another device or at [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find/) . But make sure that you've tried everything else to find it, because erasing your device can't be undone.

Don't remove the device from Find My, even if you remotely erase it. Removing the device from your Find My list removes Activation Lock, which will make it easier for the thief to unlock and resell your device.

If you filed a claim with AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss, don't remove the device from Find My or your Apple Account, even if you remotely erase it.

If your device is offline, the remote erase process begins the next time it's online.

[Remotely erase your iPhone or iPad on icloud.com/find](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mmfc0ef36f/icloud)

[Remotely erase a device in the Find My app on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph21a030ae3/ios)

### Review your Apple Account information

Make sure that your Apple Account is secure by reviewing your Apple Account information. If you find changes that you don't recognize or didn't make, update that information and change your Apple Account password.

#### Review your account on another trusted device

Use your other trusted device (like an iPad or Mac) to review your account.

- On an iPhone or iPad, go to Settings, then tap your name. On a Mac, from the Apple menu , choose System Settings, then tap your name.

- To review your security settings, tap Sign-In & Security.

#### Review your account on a new Apple device

After you sign in with your Apple Account, you can review your account information. There may be a waiting period before you can make changes to your security settings.

#### Review your account on the web

- You can review your account information at [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/) .

- If you have a trusted device, you will be directed to make any changes on that device.
