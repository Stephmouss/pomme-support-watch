# Get the Apple Music app on your smart TV, game console, or streaming device

Learn how to download and set up the Apple Music app on your smart TV, game console, or streaming device.

## Here's what you need

- [A smart TV, game console, or streaming device that supports the Apple Music app](https://www.apple.com/apple-music/features/)

- [A subscription to Apple Music](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109329)

## Set up the Apple Music app

- Go to your device's app store and download the Apple Music app. If you don't see the Apple Music app, then your device might not be supported or the Apple Music app isn't available in your country or region.

- Open the Apple Music app.

- Follow the onscreen steps to sign in to your Apple Account and complete setup:

- Sign in using your mobile device: Use the camera on your phone to scan the QR code on your screen. Then follow the instructions on your mobile device to sign in to your Apple Account and finish setup.

- Manually sign in: Use your remote or controller to sign in to your Apple Account in the Apple Music app or web browser and finish setup.

Some content in your music library might not be available in the Apple Music app on your smart TV, game console, or streaming device.

## Sign out of the Apple Music app

- Open the Apple Music app.

- Go to Settings > Account.

- Choose Sign Out.

## Reset the Apple Music app to its original settings

Before you sell or give away your device or uninstall the Apple Music app, you should reset the Apple Music app. Resetting the Apple Music app signs you out of your account, removes your personal information, and restores the app to its original settings.

If you reset the Apple Music app on a smart TV, game console, or streaming device that's also signed in to the Apple TV app, then the Apple TV app will also be restored to their original settings.

To reset the Apple Music app:

- Open the Apple Music app.

- Go to Settings.

- On your smart TV or streaming device, choose Reset Apple TV and Apple Music.

- On your game console, choose Reset Apple Music.

## If you're signed in to the Apple Music app on a device you don't have access to

If you're signed in to the Apple Music app on a smart TV, game console, or streaming device that you can't access, [remove the device from your Apple Account device list](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102649#remove) .

The Apple Music app availability might vary by country and region. [Learn what’s available in your country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) . The Apple Music app features and supported devices also might vary by country and region.
