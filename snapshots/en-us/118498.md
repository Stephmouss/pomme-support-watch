# Factory reset your Apple Vision Pro

Follow these steps to securely erase information from your Apple Vision Pro and restore your device to factory settings.

- [Back up your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/tan1f03417ed/) so that you can restore your data later.

- After you've backed up your data, open Settings, tap General, then tap Transfer or Reset Apple Vision Pro.

- Tap Erase All Content and Settings.

- If asked, enter your device passcode or Apple Account password. Forgot your [Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/102656) ?

- Confirm that you want to erase your device.

- Wait for your device to erase. It might take a few minutes to completely erase your data.
