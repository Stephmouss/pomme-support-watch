# How to avoid or lower Apple Card interest charges

When you [pay your monthly balance in full](https://support.apple.com/102534) every month on or before the due date, you won't be charged interest.

If you don't pay your monthly balance in full by your due date, interest begins to accrue. Interest is charged on the unpaid portion of your monthly balance and begins to accrue on new purchases until you pay the monthly balance in full. You can find out if you have any accrued interest charges by looking at your monthly statements in the Wallet app.

Interest never accrues on Apple Card Monthly Installment balances.

## If you see interest charges after you pay your monthly balance

If you see interest charges even after you pay your monthly balance in full, it's likely because you paid your prior monthly balance after the due date. You can [set up automatic payments](https://support.apple.com/102534#autopay) to make sure that you always pay your monthly balance by the due date.

## How interest is estimated

If you're unable to pay your monthly balance by the statement due date, Apple Card makes it easy to see how much you need to pay to lower your interest charges. 1 Estimated interest charges are automatically calculated and presented to you any time that you [make a one-time payment in the Wallet app](https://support.apple.com/102534#onetimepayment) .

- Interest charge estimates are based on the selected payment amount, taking into account your remaining balance from the previous month and new purchases in the current month.

- Estimates don't include pending transactions, credits received from disputes on purchases made during the current month, or any other purchases or payments that you make before the end of the billing period.

- The actual interest charge you're responsible for paying appears on your monthly statement.

- Apple purchases made with Apple Card Monthly Installments are interest-free. 2
