# Use FaceTime on your iPhone or iPad

With FaceTime, you can make and answer calls, translate different languages, share your screen, watch movies together, and more—all in real time.

- [Make a FaceTime call](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105088#make-call)

- [Answer a FaceTime call](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105088#audio)

- [Discover more ways to interact on a FaceTime call](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105088#options)

- [Find your missed calls, voicemails, and more in FaceTime](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105088#Find)

## Make a FaceTime call

- In Settings, [turn on FaceTime](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/get-started-with-facetime-iphc4774d8d8/ios) .

- Then, in the FaceTime app (not Settings), tap the New Call button. If you have the person's phone number or email address saved in your Contacts, 1 start typing their name and tap the name when it appears.

- Tap the Audio button or the FaceTime button to start the call.

[Image: In FaceTime on iPhone, a name is entered in the To field for a new FaceTime call.]

You can use FaceTime over Wi-Fi 2 or over cellular on supported iOS or iPadOS devices. 3 Data rates may apply. FaceTime, Group FaceTime, or FaceTime audio may not be available in all countries or regions.

## Answer a FaceTime call

- If you see an incoming FaceTime call, tap the Accept button to answer.

[Image: iPhone home screen showing a FaceTime Video call with the option to accept or decline.]

- If you're already on FaceTime, and you get another incoming call, tap one of these options:

- End & Accept (ends your current call and accepts the incoming call)

- Hold & Accept (holds your current call and accepts the new call)

- Decline (declines the new call)

## Discover more ways to interact on a FaceTime call

- During a FaceTime call, tap the More button .

- Tap any of these options:

- Add People (adds more people to the current call)

- Contact Card (opens the other caller's Contact card)

- Live Captions (shows real-time captions on the current call)

- Live Translation (translates between languages in real-time)

- Hold Assist (get notified to pick up when you're no longer on hold)

- Screen Sharing (shares your screen with the other caller)

- SharePlay (lets you share movies, workouts, and more on the current call)

## Find your missed calls, voicemails, and more in FaceTime

- In FaceTime, tap the Filter button in the upper right corner.

[Image: In FaceTime on iPhone, the Filter button is magnified, which can be tapped for in-call options.]

- Tap any of the options from the list:

- Calls (your recent FaceTime video and audio calls)

- Missed (FaceTime video and audio calls that were not answered)

- Video (video messages from other callers)

- Voicemail (voicemails from other callers)

- Unknown Callers (calls from unsaved Contacts)

- Spam (calls identified as spam or fraud)

- Manage Filtering (your settings to screen, silence, and filter calls)
