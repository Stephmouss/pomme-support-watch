# Watch MLS matches and programming with your Apple TV subscription

If you subscribe to Apple TV, you can watch live Major League Soccer matches and replays, and check when a team is playing.

## Where can I watch MLS matches and programming?

All MLS content is included with your Apple TV subscription* at no additional cost. [Subscribe to Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118404) to start watching MLS matches, Apple Originals, and more.

You can watch MLS content on the Apple TV app on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV 4K, Apple TV HD, Apple Vision Pro, Windows PC, Android mobile device, smart TV, streaming device, game console, or cable set-top box. You can also watch MLS content on the web at [tv.apple.com/sports](https://tv.apple.com/sports) , including on Chrome OS devices.

## How to watch a live match

- Open the Apple TV app or go to [tv.apple.com/sports](https://tv.apple.com/sports) .

- If prompted, sign in to your Apple Account.

- Go to Search, then search for MLS.

- Select MLS.

- Select a match, then select Watch.

[Download the Sports app for iPhone to see live sports scores and more](https://support.apple.com/en-us/116979)

## How to watch an MLS match recap or full replay

After a match ends, you can watch a short recap or a full replay.

- Open the Apple TV app or go to [tv.apple.com/sports](https://tv.apple.com/sports) .

- Go to Search, then search for MLS.

- Select MLS.

- Scroll down to Past Matches.

- Select a match, then choose Recap to watch the highlights, or Watch to watch the full replay.

You can also use Search to look for a match. After you start a replay, you can change the audio to Spanish. Some replays are also available to watch in French.

## How to find out when a team is playing

- Open the Apple TV app or go to [tv.apple.com/sports](https://tv.apple.com/sports) .

- Go to Search, then search for a team and select it.

- Scroll down to find the schedule.

[View the full MLS match schedule](https://www.mlssoccer.com/schedule/)

You can also follow your favorite teams in the Apple TV app to see a schedule of upcoming matches.

*Apple TV and MLS features, content, and availability might vary by country or region. Learn [what's available in your country or region.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205)
