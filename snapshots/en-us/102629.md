# How to manually update apps from the App Store

You can turn off automatic updates and update apps manually. Or you can turn on automatic updates so that you don't need to manually update each app.

## Update your apps manually

On your iPhone and iPad, apps that you download from the App Store automatically update by default. But if there's an issue, you can update an app manually.

### How to manually update apps on your iPhone or iPad

- Open the App Store.

- Tap the My Account button or your photo at the top of the screen.

- Tap App Updates, then tap Update next to an app to update only that app, or tap Update All.

[Learn what to do if you can't update an app on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102632)

### How to manually update apps on your Mac

- Open the App Store.

- In the sidebar, click Updates.

- Click Update next to an app to update only that app, or click Update All.

If you didn’t get the app from the App Store on your Mac, contact the app developer to get help with updates.

[Learn what to do if you can't update an app on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102331)

### How to manually update apps on your Apple Watch

- Open the App Store and scroll down to the bottom.

- Tap Account, then tap Updates.

- Tap Update next to an app to update only that app, or tap Update All.

## Turn automatic app updates on or off

If you prefer to manually update all your apps, turn off automatic updates. When you do this, you receive notifications from the App Store each time an update for one of your apps is released.

### How to turn automatic app updates on or off on your iPhone or iPad

- Open Settings, then tap Apps.

- Tap App Store.

- Turn App Updates on or off.

### How to turn automatic app updates on or off on your Mac

- Open the App Store.

- From the menu bar at the top of the screen, open App Store and choose Settings or Preferences.

- Select or deselect Automatic Updates.

### How to turn automatic app updates on or off on your Apple Watch

- Open Settings, then tap App Store.

- Turn on or turn off Automatic Updates.

You can also open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to App Store and tap it, then turn on or turn off Automatic Updates.

### How to turn automatic app updates on or off on your Apple TV HD or 4K

- Open Settings, then select Apps.

- Turn on or turn off Automatically Update Apps.

### How turn automatic app updates on or off on your Apple Vision Pro

- Open Settings, then tap Apps. Tap App Store.

- Turn App Updates on or off.
