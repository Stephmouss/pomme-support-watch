# Reply to specific messages on your iPhone and iPad

With iOS 18 and iPadOS 18 and later, you can reply directly to a specific message and use mentions to call attention to certain messages and people.

## How to reply to a specific message

- Open a Messages conversation.

- Touch and hold a message bubble, then tap the Reply button .

[Image: An iPhone showing the inline reply menu after you touch and hold a message bubble to send an inline reply]

- Type your message, then tap the Send button .

You can reply to a specific message in conversations with one person and in groups.

## How to use mentions

- Open a Messages conversation.

- Type a contact's name, then tap the name when it appears. Or type @ followed by their name.

[Image: An iPhone showing how to send a mention in a text message]

Depending on your contact's settings, a mention can notify them even if they've muted the conversation. To change this notification setting:

- In the Settings app, tap Apps.

- Tap Messages.

- Scroll down to Mentions, and turn Notify Me on or off.
