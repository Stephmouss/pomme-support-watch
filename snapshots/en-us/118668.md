## Before you get started

[Update your iPhone or iPad to latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575)

## How to follow podcasts on iPhone or iPad

When you follow a podcast, new episodes automatically download to your device. You can also get notified when new episodes are released.

- Open the Podcasts app.

- Search for the show you'd like to follow. [Need help finding the shows that you like?](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/find-podcasts-iph19bb8e705/ios)

- Tap the show to see its information page.

- Tap the Follow button .

To find the podcasts that you follow or the ones that you've manually added through a URL, go to the Library tab. Then tap Shows.

Want to listen to premium podcast content? [Learn how to purchase Apple Podcasts Subscriptions.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108378)

## How to unfollow podcasts on iPhone or iPad

- Open the Podcasts app.

- On your iPhone, tap the tab bar, then tap the Library tab. On your iPad, go to Library in the sidebar.

- Tap Shows, then tap a show to see its information page.

- Tap the More button for the show.

- Tap the Unfollow button .

- If you have episodes downloaded, you can choose to keep all your downloaded episodes, or remove them from your Library. Tap the show page to keep your downloaded episodes. If you keep them, new episodes won't download, but episodes that are already in your Library won't be deleted.

If you unfollow a show with premium content, your subscription is still active.

[Learn how to cancel a subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428)

## Edit your queue

- Open the Podcasts app.

- Search for the show you'd like to add to your queue.

- Tap the show to see its information page and find the episode you'd like to add.

- Tap the More button next to the episode.

- Tap Play Next to play the episode after your current episode ends, or tap Add to Queue to put the episode at the end of your queue.

- To change the order of your For You queue or your custom queue, tap the mini player at the bottom of the screen, then tap the Queue button .

- To move the episode, press and drag the Reorder button .

## Watch video podcasts

Some podcasts might have video versions of the show that you can watch in the Podcasts app.

### How to watch a video podcast

- Open the Podcasts app.

- Search for a show with video and play it.

- Tap the mini player at the bottom of the screen.

- Under the track position bar, tap Turn Video On to play the video version of the podcast. Tap Turn Video Off to play the audio version.

[Image: The mini player is open in the Podcasts app on iPhone. The Turn Video On button is circled, showing its position under the track position bar.]

You can [use Picture in Picture](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/multitask-with-picture-in-picture-iphcc3587b5d) to continue playing the video while you use other apps. If you close the Picture in Picture, the audio version of the show will continue playing. Pause the show to stop playback.

### Change your video podcasts preferences

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Apps, then tap Podcasts.

- Stream video over only Wi-Fi: go to Video Streaming preferences and turn Allow Over Cellular off.

- Automatically download video versions of a show: go to Automatic Downloads and turn Download Video on. You can choose if you want to download over cellular data by tapping Allow Over Cellular, then selecting Audio Only or Video.

## How to speed up or slow down a podcast episode

- Open the Podcasts app and start listening to an episode.

- Tap the mini player at the bottom of the screen to expand the audio controls.

- Tap the Playback Speed button , then choose a speed from the menu that appears. You can also press and drag the speed menu to get a specific playback speed.

## How to view podcast transcripts

- Open the Podcasts app and start listening to an episode.

- Tap the mini player at the bottom of the screen to expand the full screen player.

- Tap the Transcript button . If the Transcript button is grayed out, transcripts are not available for that episode.

In the transcript view you can:

- Follow along with your favorite shows while reading time-synced transcripts.

- Scroll forwards and backwards through the podcast and tap to play the section you want to listen to.

- Tap and hold a section to select the text or share it.

- Tap Search to find specific words and go directly to those sections.

- View show chapters and tap them to go to that section of the podcast.

- Find links to music and other content mentioned in the show, or follow mentioned podcasts.

- Report an issue with the transcript by press and holding the section, tap Report a Concern, then follow the onscreen steps.

Transcripts are not available in Belarus and Russia.
