# Leave Apple Cash Family

If you're at least 18 years old, you can leave Apple Cash Family and get your own Apple Cash account. Then, only you can view and manage your activity.

Before you start, make sure that [the birth date associated with your Apple Account](https://appleid.apple.com/) is correct.

## How to leave Apple Cash Family

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap your Apple Cash card. 1

- Tap the More button , then tap Card Details .

[Image: the more button]

[Image: the card details button]

- Scroll down and tap Stop Sharing Activity.

- Tap Continue, then agree to the Terms and Conditions.

- Follow the instructions on your screen to provide your information and verify your identity. 2

- Then, tap Next.

Learn about [Apple Cash and person to person payment limits](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT207884) , and see what happens [when you leave a Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201081) .

## What happens when you leave Apple Cash Family

- Your Apple Cash balance remains the same. 3

- The members of your Family Sharing group can no longer see your activity.

- Your maximum allowed balance, and the amount of money that you can send and receive in Messages or Wallet, increases.
