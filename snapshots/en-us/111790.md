# Access Control Center on your iPad

Control Center gives you instant access to the things you do most. You can use Control Center to quickly take a picture, turn on Wi-Fi, control your Apple TV, and more.

## How to open and close Control Center

- To open Control Center, swipe down from the top-right corner of your screen.

- To close Control Center, swipe up from the bottom of the screen, or just tap the screen. [Play](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111790) [Pause](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111790) [Replay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111790)

Learn how to open and close [Control Center on iPhone and iPod touch](https://support.apple.com/108330) .

## How to customize your settings

Need to quickly adjust the brightness of your display or change the volume? You can [customize your Control Center settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/ipad/use-and-customize-control-center-ipade572ca56/ipados) to get to your apps, settings, and features with just a few taps.
