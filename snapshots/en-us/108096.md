# If you can't find or use your Apple In-App Purchases

If you can’t use or find an Apple In-App Purchase that you bought, try to restore your purchase in the app you used to buy the item.

Some in-app purchases, such as game currency or hints, are consumable, which means that they run out as you use them. You can’t restore consumable purchases. [Learn about different types of in-app purchases](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108813) .

You might be able to restore a non-consumable purchase made through the App Store or Apple In-App Purchase, such as an upgrade to a pro version, an extra feature in a game, or a subscription.

## Restore your Apple In-App Purchases

- Open the app that you used to buy the item.

- Look for an option to restore. You might find it in the app’s store, main menu, Settings menu, or Options menu.

## If you try to restore and nothing happens or you get an error

- Make sure that you’re signed in to the same Apple Account that you used to make the purchase.

- [Make sure you allow Apple In-App Purchases on your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102470) .

- Restart your device.

- If you were charged for the purchase and you still can’t restore it, [contact the app developer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102435) for support. You can also [report a problem to Apple](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/?s=6) .
