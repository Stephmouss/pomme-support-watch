# If your AirPods or AirPods Pro won't connect

If your AirPods or AirPods Pro won't connect to your iPhone, iPad, or Mac, try to pair your AirPods to your device again.

- [Update your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) to the latest version of iOS or iPadOS, or [update your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) to the latest version of macOS.

- Put both AirPods in the charging case and [make sure that both AirPods are charging](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119912) .

- Make sure that Bluetooth is on:

- On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings, then tap Bluetooth.

- On Mac, choose Apple menu , then click System Settings. Click Bluetooth.

- Take your AirPods out of the charging case and put them in your ears.

- Open Control Center on your device, then tap or click the AirPlay button :

- If your AirPods appear in the list of devices, select them, then test them by playing audio from your device. Make sure the volume on your device is turned up.

- If your AirPods don't appear in the list of devices, or they do appear but won't connect, go to the next step.

- Put both AirPods back in the charging case and close the lid. Wait 15 seconds, then open the lid.

- If you're using a Mac, [pair your AirPods with your Mac manually](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108786#manual) . If you're using an iPhone or iPad, do the following, depending on which AirPods you have:

- For AirPods 4 (both models) and AirPods 5 (both models), double-tap the front of the case until the status light flashes white.

[Image: AirPods 4 in their case with the lid open.]

- For AirPods Pro 3, double-tap the front of the case until the status light flashes white.

[Image: AirPods Pro 3 in their case with the lid open.]

- For all other models, press and hold the setup button on the back of the case until the status light flashes white.

[Image: AirPods Pro in their case with the lid open and the back button held down.]

- Hold the charging case, with your AirPods inside and the lid open, next to your iPhone or iPad, and follow the steps on the screen.

- If you still can't connect, [reset your AirPods](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118531) .
