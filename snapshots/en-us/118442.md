# Use Notes on your iPhone, iPad, and iPod touch

With Notes, you can capture a quick thought, create checklists, sketch ideas, and more. And with iCloud, you can keep your notes updated across all your devices.

Start a new note.

To use the latest Notes features, make sure that you [set up Notes with iCloud](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/set-up-notes-mm8685520792/icloud) or have notes saved on your device. Many Notes features won't work with other email providers.

## Do more with notes

With Notes, you can pin your top notes, draw, create checklists, scan and sign documents, and add attachments — like photos, videos, or web links.

### Pin a note

Pin your favorite or most important notes to make them easier to find. To pin a note:

- Swipe right over the note in the Notes list.

- Tap the Pin button .

You can also pin a note from within a note. Tap the More button , then tap the Pin button .

[Image: Pin button]

### Format a note

When you're in a note, tap the table button or the formatting button to add a table, title, heading, or bulleted list. You can also touch and hold to quickly open a list of formatting options.

[Image: Formatting icon for a table.]

[Image: Formatting icon for titles and headings in the Notes app.]

[Image: In Notes in iOS 18 or later, highlight text then tap the Formatting button to reveal options like italics or Heading styles.]

In iOS 18, when you format text in Notes as a Title, Heading, or Subheading, you can tap the v button next to the text to collapse any text, images, or media in the section below.

### Undo changes to a note

If you want to reverse a change you made in a note, tap the Undo button at the top of the screen. You can undo any changes that you made while you have the current note open.

### Add an attachment

To add something from another app, like a location or website, tap the Share button in the app that you want to share from. Tap the Notes app, then select the note that you want to add an attachment to, then tap Save.

[Image: When you add an attachment to a note in iOS 18 or later, you can choose which note to add the attachment to.]

### Add a file, photo, or video

To add a file, photo, or video, tap in a note, then tap the Attachment button . Tap Take Photo or Video to create a new photo or video, or Choose Photo or Video to add an existing one. Tap Use Photo or Use Video, or tap Add to add an existing one. You can also [scan documents](https://support.apple.com/108963) and add them to your notes.

[Image: When you add an attachment to a note, you can choose which note to add the attachment to.]

### Record and transcribe audio

In iOS 18 and iPadOS 18, you can [record and transcribe audio within a note](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/record-and-transcribe-audio-iphbe11247b5/ios) . Tap the Attachment button , then tap Record Audio to get started.

On supported iPhones, you can then tap the Transcript button to view the generated text. You can add the transcript text to a note, search the transcript, or copy transcript text to other documents. [If you have Apple Intelligence turned on, you can summarize the text](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-writing-tools-iph6f08da1d2/ios) , too.

[Learn how to record a call in FaceTime or Phone and get a transcript in Notes on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-sg/guide/iphone/iph57c6590e9/ios)

### Link to a note

In iOS 17 and later, you can link to other notes within a note:

- In a note, touch and hold blank space. Let go to bring up the options menu.

- Swipe or tap the Forward button to find Add Link, then tap Add Link.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

[Image: To add a link in Notes, touch and hold blank space or highlight text to bring up the options menu. Then, tap Add Link.]

- Enter the title of the note that you want to link to and select it from the list.

- Turn Use Note Title as Name on or off. Enter different link text if needed.

- Tap the Done button .

You can also type ">>" in the body of your note, enter the title of the note that you want to link to, and select it from the list.

## Create folders

- If you're in the Notes list, tap the back arrow icon to see your Folders list.

- In the Folders list, tap the New Folder button .

[Image: In Notes, you can view all your tags and folders simultaneously.]

- Choose where to add the folder. To create a subfolder, drag the folder that you want to be a subfolder to the main folder. It will move below the main folder and be indented.

- Name your folder, then tap Save.

## Move a note to a different folder

- If you're in a note, tap the back arrow icon to go back to your Notes list.

- In the Notes list, tap the More button , then tap Select Notes.

- Tap the notes that you want to move.

[Image: To move notes to different folders, go to your Notes list and tap the notes that you want to move.]

- Tap Move, then select the folder you want to move them to.

## See your notes in Gallery view

Gallery view gives you a new way to see your notes — making it easier than ever to find the note you're looking for. Open a folder or list of notes, tap the More button , then tap View as Gallery.

[Image: The Gallery view in Notes on iOS 26.]

To sort your notes inside of folders:

- Go to the folder that you want to sort.

- Tap the More button , then tap Sort Notes By.

- Choose how you want to sort your notes.

To sort all of your notes automatically, go to Settings > Apps > Notes, and tap Sort Notes By. Then choose how you want to sort your notes.

## Search for a note or attachment

To search for a specific note, just tap the Search field, then enter what you’re looking for. You can search for typed and handwritten notes. Search can recognize what's in the images inside of your notes. For example, if you search for "bike," search will show you all images that you have with a bike. Search can also find specific text inside scanned documents or images, like receipts or bills. Learn [what to do if you can't find a note](https://support.apple.com/102476) .

To search for something in a specific note, select the note, tap the More button , then tap Find in Note and type what you're searching for.

You can also search for attachments. While you're in a Notes list, tap the More button , then tap View Attachments. To go to the note with the attachment, touch and hold the thumbnail of the attachment, then tap Show in Note.

## Delete a note

- Swipe left over the note in the Notes list.

- Tap the Trash button .

[Image: Trash button]

[Image: Swipe left over a note in the Notes list, then tap the Trash button to delete a note.]

You can also open the note, tap the More button , then tap Delete.

[Learn what to do if you're missing notes](https://support.apple.com/102476)

## Recover a deleted note

- In the Notes app, go to the Folders list.

- Tap Recently Deleted.

- Tap the note that you want to keep.

- Tap anywhere in the note, then tap Recover.

[Image: In the Folders list in Notes, you can view recently deleted notes then choose to recover them.]

## Proofread, summarize, and more with Writing Tools and Apple Intelligence in Notes

On Apple Intelligence capable devices with iOS 18.1 and iPadOS 18.1, you can [use Writing Tools to proofread, rewrite, summarize, or change the tone of text](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-writing-tools-iph6f08da1d2/ios) . Select the text you want to change, tap the Forward button >, then tap Writing Tools to get started.

To use Apple Intelligence features on your iPhone, you need an iPhone 16, iPhone 16 Plus, iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max, iPhone 15 Pro, or iPhone 15 Pro Max. Apple Intelligence is available in beta starting with iOS 18.1, iPadOS 18.1, and macOS Sequoia 15.1 when your device language and Siri language are set to the same [supported language](https://support.apple.com/121115#supportedlanguages) .

Apple Intelligence uses generative models and outputs may vary. Check important information for accuracy.
