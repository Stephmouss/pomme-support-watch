# Share iCloud+ with your Family Sharing group

With Family Sharing, you can share an iCloud+ subscription with up to five other family members. This includes up to 12 TB of iCloud storage, as well as features like iCloud Private Relay, Hide My Email, and HomeKit Secure Video support.

When you share an iCloud+ subscription, everyone in the Family Sharing group uses their own Apple Account on their device. That way your photos, documents, and other information stay private, even though you're sharing access to the service. You simply share the features and space available in iCloud+ with your family members, so you only have one plan to manage. The Family Sharing organizer doesn't have to be the one who shares the iCloud+ subscription with the family group. You can allow another member to take over sharing iCloud+ with the entire family group.

## Share iCloud+ with five members of your family

Before you can share iCloud+ with your family, you need to [set up Family Sharing on your device.](https://setup.iCloud.com/family/messages?aaaction=showFamilyInvite&inviteCode=organizer_create&campaign=kbArticle)

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap Family.

- Tap Subscriptions, then tap iCloud+.

- Follow the steps to share your existing plan.

### On your Mac

- Choose Apple menu , click System Settings, then click Family.

- Click Subscriptions, then choose iCloud+.

- Click Share with Family.

- Follow the onscreen instructions.

[Learn what to do if a family member already has iCloud+](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108783#switch)

## Check how much iCloud storage your Family Sharing group is using

If your Family Sharing group is low on iCloud storage, all members get a notification. The family organizer or family member that purchased the plan can buy more at any time — up to 12 TB.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap Family.

- Tap Subscriptions, then tap iCloud+.

- Tap Manage This Subscription, then follow the steps to purchase more iCloud+ storage.

## Switch from your personal iCloud+ plan to your shared family plan

When you share iCloud+, any family members using the free 5 GB plan are added to your shared family plan automatically.

If a family member is already paying for their own iCloud+ plan, they can choose to switch to your plan or keep their own and still remain part of the Family Sharing group. When they switch to the iCloud+ plan shared with your family, they get a refund for the remainder of their personal iCloud+ plan. They can't keep their current plan and the shared family plan at the same time.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap Family.

- Tap Subscriptions, then tap iCloud+.

- Tap Use Family Storage.

### On your Mac

- Choose Apple menu , click System Settings, then click Family.

- Click Subscriptions, then choose iCloud+.

- Click Use Shared Plan.

## Ask family member to increase the iCloud+ storage plan

If you see a message that your iCloud storage is full or almost full — and you're part of an iCloud+ plan shared with your family — you can ask the family organizer or the member who purchased the iCloud+ plan to upgrade to a plan with more storage.

You can also manage the storage on your device to help make space. [Learn how to make more space available in iCloud](https://support.apple.com/108922) .

## Change which family member shares the iCloud+ plan with the group

You can change the member who shares an iCloud+ plan with the Family Sharing group. You may want to do this if another member has an iCloud+ plan with more storage, or wants to purchase a larger plan. To allow another member to take over sharing iCloud+ with the family group, follow these steps:

- Open the Settings app, then tap your name.

- Tap iCloud, then tap Storage.

- Tap Family Usage, then tap Stop Sharing with Family.

The member who plans to take over sharing the iCloud+ plan should follow these steps:

- [Buy an iCloud+ plan or upgrade an existing plan](https://support.apple.com/108349) , if you have one already.

- Open the Settings app, then tap your name.

- Tap iCloud, then tap Storage.

- Tap Share with Family and follow the onscreen instructions to invite the other members.

## If your family stops sharing iCloud+ or you leave Family Sharing

If you leave a Family Sharing group that shares an iCloud+ plan and you're using more than 5 GB of storage, you can buy your own plan to continue using iCloud storage.

[Learn more about what happens when you stop using Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/102652#stopsharing)

If you choose not to buy your own plan and the content that you’re storing in iCloud exceeds your available storage, new photos and videos won’t upload to iCloud Photos, files will no longer upload in iCloud Drive, and your iOS and iPadOS devices will stop backing up.

[Learn how to make more space available in iCloud](https://support.apple.com/108922)

Not all features are available in all countries or regions. Without access to your IP address, some websites may require extra steps to sign in or access content after you turn on iCloud Private Relay. HomeKit Secure Video requires a supported iCloud plan, compatible HomeKit-enabled security camera, and HomePod mini, HomePod, Apple TV 4K, Apple TV HD, or iPad running as a home hub. [Learn more about iCloud+](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/mmfc854d9604) .
