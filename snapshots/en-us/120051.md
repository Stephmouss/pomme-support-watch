# If your eyes are too close or too far from your Apple Vision Pro displays

To prevent eye injury during a fall or collision and to maintain input accuracy, use the recommended Light Seal Cushion so that the display lenses of your Apple Vision Pro remain a safe distance from your eyes.

## How to replace your Light Seal Cushion

- In your Apple Vision Pro packaging, identify the Light Seal Cushion recommended in your visionOS alert:

- If your eyes are too close to the display, select the thicker N+ or W+ Light Seal Cushion.

- If your eyes are too far from the display, select the thinner N or W Light Seal Cushion. This is the Light Seal Cushion found on your Light Seal when you first opened your packaging.

[Image: Apple Vision Pro light seal with size options: XS, S, M, L]

- To remove your current Light Seal Cushion, pull the Light Seal Cushion straight off the Light Seal.

[Image: Diagram of Apple Vision Pro, with an arrow indicating the location of the Light Seal Cushion.]

- To attach the recommended Light Seal Cushion, place it next to the Light Seal until it snaps magnetically into place.

[Image: Apple Vision Pro with an arrow pointing to the light seal cushion, which is being discussed for replacement in the article.]

It's important to always wear the correct size Light Seal and Light Seal Cushion for your face. If your eyes are too close to the display lenses of your Apple Vision Pro, it could cause eye injury in the case of a fall or collision. If your eyes are too far from the display lenses, it can reduce eye input accuracy and image quality. If you're using the incorrect size Light Seal Cushion, you also might not be able to [set up or use Optic ID](https://support.apple.com/HT213962) .

## Need more help?

If you continue to see this message after replacing your Light Seal Cushion:

- If you purchased your Apple Vision Pro online or on the phone, go to [https://secure.store.apple.com/shop/vision/fit/help](https://secure.store.apple.com/shop/vision/fit/help) or call 1-800-MY-APPLE.

- If you purchased your Apple Vision Pro at an Apple Store, visit your local Apple Store.
