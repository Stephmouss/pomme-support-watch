# About electronic Government Uniform Invoice (eGUI) consent in Taiwan

Learn how to verify or change the email address that you use to receive eGUI receipts.

When you make purchases from the App Store or other Apple media services in Taiwan, Apple must issue an electronic Government Uniform Invoice and send it to the Ministry of Finance.

When you make a purchase, you agree that the email address that you provide will be shared with the Ministry of Finance.

## Change your email address

Apple automatically sends your eGUI receipts to the primary email address that you have on file with your Apple Account. If you want your eGUI receipts to be sent to a different email address, [contact Apple support](http://www.apple.co/eGUI) .

To check which email address received the eGUI receipt for a purchase, [view your purchase history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212) .
