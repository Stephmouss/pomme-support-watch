# Watch baseball, soccer, and Formula 1 in the Apple TV app

With an Apple TV subscription, you can watch live Friday Night Baseball games, Major League Soccer matches, and Formula 1 races in the Apple TV app.

## Where can I watch Friday Night Baseball, MLS, and F1 events and coverage?

When you [subscribe to Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118404) *, you can watch games, matches, and races in the Apple TV app on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV 4K, Apple TV HD, Apple Vision Pro, Windows PC, Android mobile device, smart TVs, streaming devices, and cable set-top boxes. You can also watch on the web at [tv.apple.com/sports](https://tv.apple.com/sports) , including on Chrome OS devices.

[Learn how to watch sports, Apple Originals, and more on your Apple devices, Smart TV, and on the web](https://support.apple.com/guide/tvplus/welcome/web)

## Get started with the Apple TV app

To watch, make sure that you:

- [Subscribe to Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118404) .

- Update to the latest software on your Apple device, or update to the latest version of the Apple TV app.

- Are signed in to the Apple Account you use with your Apple TV subscription.

- [Set up the Apple TV app if you're using a streaming device or game console](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102472) .

## How to watch a live Friday Night Baseball, MLS, or F1 event

- When the sport is in season, open the Apple TV app or go to [tv.apple.com/sports](http://tv.apple.com/sports) .

- Go to Search.

- Search for Friday Night Baseball, MLS, or F1 and select the channel.

- Select a game, match, or race.

- Select Watch.

[Download the Sports app for iPhone to see live sports scores and more](https://support.apple.com/en-us/116979)

## How to watch a Friday Night Baseball, MLS, or F1 recap or full replay

After a game, match, or race ends, you can watch a short recap or a full replay.

- When the sport is in season, open the Apple TV app or go to [tv.apple.com/sports](https://tv.apple.com/sports) .

- Go to Search.

- Search for Friday Night Baseball, MLS, or F1 and select the channel.

- Scroll down to Past Games, Past Matches, or Past Races and select the event.

- Choose Recap to watch the highlights, or Watch to watch the full replay.

## How to find out when a team is playing

- When the sport is in season, open the Apple TV app or go to [tv.apple.com/sports](https://tv.apple.com/sports) .

- Go to Search.

- Search for Apple TV Friday Night Baseball, MLS, or F1 and select the channel you want to view the schedule for.

- Scroll down to find the schedule.

You can also follow your favorite teams in the Apple TV app to see a schedule of their upcoming games.
