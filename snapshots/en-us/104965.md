# Move content manually from your Android device to your iPhone or iPad

Transfer contacts, photos, music, documents, and more from your Android device to your new iPhone or iPad.

[Image: iOS logo, representing the destination device for content transfer from Android.]

Use the [Move to iOS app](https://support.apple.com/118670) to automatically transfer your Android content to your new iPhone or iPad. If you can't use the app, you can move your content manually instead.

## Mail, contacts, and calendars

The software on your iPhone or iPad works with email providers like Google, Microsoft Exchange, Yahoo, and more, so you can probably keep the email, contacts, and calendars that you have now. To get started, [add each of your email accounts](http://support.apple.com/102619) to your device.

[Image: An iPhone showing the settings for a connected Gmail account in the Settings app.]

## Photos and videos

To move photos and videos from your Android device to your iPhone or iPad using a computer:

- Connect your Android to your computer and find your photos and videos. On most devices, you can find these files in the DCIM folder.

- Choose the photos and videos that you want to move and drag them to a folder on your computer. You can also use an app like [Image Capture](https://support.apple.com/guide/image-capture/transfer-images-imgcp1003/mac) or [Photos](https://support.apple.com/guide/photos/import-from-a-camera-or-phone-pht6c803201/mac) on your Mac to import photos and videos from a connected device.

- Disconnect your Android and connect your iPhone or iPad to your computer.

- On a Mac with macOS Catalina, open Finder. On a Mac with macOS Mojave or earlier, open iTunes. On a PC, open the [Apple Devices app](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) . On your computer, [sync your photos to your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/102375) . You can find your photos and videos on your device in the Photos app.

## Music

If you use a streaming music app, go to the App Store, get the app, then sign in with your user name and password. If you use [Apple Music](https://support.apple.com/109329) , just sign in on your iPhone or iPad.

To move music from your Android device to your iPhone or iPad using a computer:

- Connect your Android device to your computer and find your music. On most devices, you can find these files in Music.

- Select the songs that you want to move and drag them to a folder on your computer, then disconnect your Android device.

- On a Mac with macOS Mojave or earlier, open iTunes and click Songs . On a Mac with macOS Catalina or later, open Music . On a PC, open the Apple Devices app.

- Open the folder where you put your songs and drag them to the Songs section in the Music app, the Music section in the Apple Devices app, or iTunes Library.

- Connect your iPhone or iPad. Then, in Finder, the Apple Devices app, or iTunes, [select your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/108392) and click Music. You can choose to sync your entire library or select only the songs or artists that you just added.

[Image: Finder window on macOS Monterey showing music sync options for a connected iPhone named "iPhone". The sidebar displays the connected device, and the main panel shows options to sync music.]

- Click Sync. You can find your music on your iPhone or iPad in the Music app.

## Books and PDFs

To move eBooks from your Android device, you can either import them to your iPhone or iPad, or access them through apps like Kindle, Nook, Google Play Books, and others. To access books from an app, go to the App Store, get the app, then sign in with your user name and password.

To move ePub books and PDFs from your Android to your iPhone or iPad using a computer:

- Connect your Android device to your computer and find your books and PDFs. On most devices, you can find these files in Documents.

- Select the books and PDFs that you want to move and drag them to a folder on your computer.

- Disconnect your Android device and connect your iPhone to your computer.

- On a Mac, drag the books and PDFs into the Books app. On a PC , drag the books and PDFs to the Apple Devices app.

- On a Mac with macOS Catalina or later, open Finder. On a Mac with macOS Mojave or earlier, open iTunes and sync your ePub books and PDFs. You can find your ePub books and PDFs on your iPhone or iPad in the Books app.

## Documents

If you store documents in the cloud or another service like Dropbox, Google Drive, or Microsoft OneDrive, you can download the app from the App Store, then sign in. You can also [bring all your files together with the Files app](https://support.apple.com/102570) .

If you don't use the cloud to transfer your documents, get the apps:

- Go to the App Store on your iPhone or iPad and install Pages, Numbers, and Keynote.

- Connect your Android to your computer and find your documents. On most devices, you can find these files in Documents.

- Select the documents that you want to move and drag them to a folder on your computer.

- On a Mac with macOS Catalina or later, open Finder. On a PC, open the Apple Devices app. On a Mac with macOS Mojave or earlier, open iTunes and sync your documents to your iPhone or iPad.

## Apps

Most apps that you use on your Android device are available in the [App Store](https://support.apple.com/102590) . Go to the App Store, search for the apps that you have now, and install them.

If you need help with third-party apps and data, [contact the app developer](https://support.apple.com/102435) .
