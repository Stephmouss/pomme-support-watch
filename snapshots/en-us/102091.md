# Transition from iBooks Author to Pages

With Pages, you can create and collaborate on books from your Mac, iPad, iPhone, and iCloud.com.

## About iBooks Author

iBooks Author is no longer updated or available. If you’ve previously downloaded the app, you can still access it from your App Store purchase history. You can continue to use iBooks Author on macOS Catalina or earlier. And you'll continue to have access to books you've previously published to Apple Books from iBooks Author. You can also open and edit iBooks Author files (IBA files) in Pages.

## About book creation in Pages

You can create books in Pages on your Mac, iPad, iPhone, and [iCloud.com](https://www.iCloud.com) .

### Create and share books

You can create, collaborate on, and publish books.

- [Import an iBooks Author book](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/open-an-ibooks-author-book-in-pages-tanee9d5036e/mac) into Pages on your Mac for further editing.

- [Create a book](https://support.apple.com/118122) by selecting a template, adding pages, and viewing and sending an EPUB.

- [Collaborate with others](https://support.apple.com/105052) on a shared book.

- [Publish to Apple Books](https://support.apple.com/108365) to offer your books for download or purchase.

### Design books

You can use many features in Pages to help you design your book.

- [Use drop caps](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/add-drop-caps-tan91b247fab/mac) to make a paragraph stand out with a large, decorative first letter.

- [Fill text with gradients or images](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/change-the-color-of-text-tan2484da646/mac) for added style.

- [Apply a color, gradient, or image to the background](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/change-the-page-background-tanc71e3248e/mac) of any page.

- [Add linked text boxes](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/link-text-boxes-tan6d9592dde/mac) to allow your text to flow from one text box to another automatically.

- [Create a new page template](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/page-templates-tan1959e234e/mac) to keep your design consistent across your book.

- [Use facing pages](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/set-up-facing-pages-tan026e4c95a/mac) to create two-page spreads.

- [Add vertical text](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/use-vertical-text-tanc8021e516/mac) for Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

### Add media to books

You can add video, image galleries, drawings, audio, and more to your book.

- [Add images](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/add-an-image-tanb3bc78786/mac) and [include video](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/add-existing-video-and-audio-tan785873ed3/10.0/mac/1.0) in your book.

- [Play YouTube and Vimeo videos](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/add-video-and-audio-tan785873ed3/mac#tanc86d152c9) right in your books.*

- [Insert an image gallery](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/add-an-image-gallery-tand1fd03aa2/mac) to view and move through a collection of photos on the same page.

- [Add drawings to your book](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT208459) with Apple Pencil or your finger and animate them.

- [Include captions and titles](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/create-a-caption-taneb1d1f4a5/mac) for images, videos, shapes, and other objects.

- [Record and play audio](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/record-audio-tana6bcd99e0/mac) directly on a page.

- [Add accessibility descriptions to media](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT210563) to help people using assistive technology.

- [Choose from over 700 editable shapes](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/add-and-edit-a-shape-tan9ad18c384/mac) to add to your book.

- [Use LaTeX or MathML notation](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT207569) to add an equation.

- [Place images, shapes, and equations inline](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/place-objects-with-text-tand6205cf6d/mac#tane97685f79) in text boxes, so they move with the text.

* This feature might not be available in all countries or regions.

### Organize books

You can give your book structure and form.

- [Create a table of contents](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/table-of-contents-tan5b8c588d6/mac) to make it easy to navigate your book.

- [Duplicate, move, or copy and paste](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/duplicate-pages-or-sections-tan8569ca593/mac) pages or sections of your book.

- [Add links](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/link-a-webpage-email-page-pages-mac-tand51db403#tanb12b1f7f4) to webpages or other pages in your book.

- [Create and save a custom template](https://support.apple.com/guide/pages/create-a-custom-template-tan526e83fd0/mac) to use as a model for new books.
