# Use Tap to Cash

With Tap to Cash, users can send or receive Apple Cash* by holding two iPhone or Apple Watch devices together. When money is transferred, it's private—your phone number and email aren't shared.

[Image: Two iPhone devices lie horizontally, side by side, with the top of the devices facing each other.]

## What you need

You and the person you're sending money to or receiving money from must have the following:

- A [compatible device](https://support.apple.com/102896) with [iOS 18 or later](https://support.apple.com/118575) or [watchOS 11 or later](https://support.apple.com/108926)

- [Apple Cash set up](https://support.apple.com/109304)

- A Wi-Fi or cellular connection

## Send money using Tap to Cash

### On your iPhone

- Open the Wallet app, tap your Apple Cash card, then tap Send or Request.

- Select Tap to Cash.

- Enter the amount, then add a note if you want to identify what the payment is for. Tap Next.

- Double-click the side button to confirm.

- Hold the top of your iPhone near another iPhone or Apple Watch.

### On your Apple Watch

- Open the Wallet app, tap your Apple Cash card, then scroll down and select Tap to Cash.

- Enter the amount you want to send, then tap Continue.

- Double-click the side button to confirm, or tap Apple Cash to change your payment method.

- Hold your Apple Watch near another Apple Watch or iPhone.

Within a 7-day period, [you can send or receive a maximum of $2,000](https://support.apple.com/109305) .

### Other ways to access Tap to Cash

- [Touch and hold the Wallet app](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/perform-quick-actions-iphcc8f419db/ios)

- [Add a widget to your Lock Screen](https://support.apple.com/118610)

- [Add a control to Control Center](https://support.apple.com/102351)

- [Customize the Action button](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/action-button-iphone-15-pro-max-iphe89d61d66/ios#:~:text=Go%20to%20Settings%20%3E%20Action%20Buttonname%20appears%20below%20the%20dots.)

Some shortcuts to Tap to Cash aren't available if Require Face ID for Wallet is enabled.

## Accept money using Tap to Cash

- On your iPhone or Apple Watch, tap the screen or press the side button to wake the device.

- Hold the top of your iPhone or Apple Watch near another Apple Watch or iPhone.

- Tap Accept or Decline. If you accept a payment and don't have Apple Cash set up, tap Set Up Now to set up Apple Cash on your device.

## Identify a Tap to Cash transaction

### On your iPhone

- Open the Wallet app, then tap your Apple Cash card.

- Under Latest Transactions, if it's a Tap to Cash transaction, you'll see the Tap to Cash icon .

### On your Apple Watch

- Open the Wallet app, then tap your Apple Cash card.

- Tap the transaction. If it's a Tap to Cash transaction, you'll see "via Tap to Cash."
