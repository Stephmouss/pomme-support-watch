# Forward text messages from your iPhone to other devices

With the Text Message Forwarding feature of your iPhone, the SMS, MMS, or RCS messages that you send and receive on your iPhone can also appear on your Mac, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro, so you can continue the conversation from the device you want.

[Image: An image of a MacBook showing a text message forwarded to an iPhone]

## Before you begin

- Make sure that each of your devices is [signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) .

- Make sure that your iPhone is turned on, connected to a Wi-Fi or cellular network, and can send and receive messages. Learn what to do [if your iPhone can’t send or receive messages](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118433) .

## Automatically forward messages

Set up either Messages in iCloud or Text Message Forwarding, as described below.

### Set up Messages in iCloud

When you set up Messages in iCloud on your devices, your entire messages history is kept up to date across those devices, even when you set up a new device. This includes messages sent or received with SMS, MMS, RCS, or iMessage. You don't need to set up Text Message Forwarding separately, because that feature is built into Messages in iCloud.

[Set up Messages in iCloud](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/set-up-messages-mm0de0d4528d/icloud)

### Or set up Text Message Forwarding separately

If your devices are using Messages in iCloud, you don't need to set up Text Message Forwarding separately.

- On your iPhone, open Settings, tap Apps, then tap Messages.

- Tap Text Message Forwarding. (If you don't see it, turn iMessage off and back on, then tap Send & Receive and sign in again.)

- Tap to turn on each device that you want to allow to send and receive messages from this iPhone. (Text Message Forwarding is always on for each of your devices that is using Messages in iCloud.)

[Image: An iPhone showing how to turn on Text Message Forwarding]

- If you select a device that isn't using [two-factor authentication for your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102660) , a verification code appears on each of your other devices: enter that code on your iPhone.

If you don't see one or more of your other devices in Text Message Forwarding settings:

- iPad: Open Settings, tap Apps, then tap Messages and make sure that iMessage is turned on. Tap Send & Receive, then make sure that the Apple Account shown at the bottom of this screen is the same as on your iPhone. If it isn't, tap it to sign out, then sign in with the correct Apple Account.

- Mac: Open the Messages app. From the Messages menu in the menu bar, choose Settings (or Preferences). Click iMessage and make sure that the Apple Account shown in this window is the same as on your iPhone. If it isn't, click Sign Out, then sign in with the correct Apple Account.

- Apple Vision Pro: Open Settings, tap Apps, then tap Messages and make sure that iMessage is turned on. Tap Send & Receive, then make sure that the Apple Account shown at the bottom of this screen is the same as on your iPhone. If it isn't, tap it to sign out, then sign in with the correct Apple Account.

## Manually forward messages

Whether or not you have Messages in iCloud or Text Message Forwarding turned on, you can manually forward selected messages to other devices.

[Forward and share messages on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/forward-and-share-messages-iph125628311/ios)
