# About Final Cut Pro for iPad

Final Cut Pro for iPad is a powerful app for shooting, editing, and sharing video at home, in the studio, or on the go.

Redesigned from the ground up for iPad, Final Cut Pro puts a pro video studio at your fingertips.

### Features and requirements

[Learn about Final Cut Pro for iPad features, system requirements, and more](https://www.apple.com/final-cut-pro-for-ipad/#footnote-9)

### Availability

[Download Final Cut Pro for iPad](https://apps.apple.com/app/final-cut-pro-for-ipad/id1631624924) from the App Store for iPad.

### Trial

To begin your free, full featured one-month trial, download the app, then sign up for a monthly or yearly subscription with your Apple Account. Cancel your subscription at any time.

### Subscription

Final Cut Pro for iPad requires a subscription and is a separate purchase from Final Cut Pro for Mac.

[Learn how to manage your subscription](https://support.apple.com/guide/final-cut-pro-ipad/dev1d3962929/ipados)
