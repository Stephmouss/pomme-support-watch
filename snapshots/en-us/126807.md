# If you can't delete an app on your iPhone

You might need to check these settings if you can't delete an app.

## Before you begin

- Update your device to [the latest version of iOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- Touch and hold the app you want to delete, then tap Remove App.

## Check your Screen Time settings

- Go to Settings, then tap Screen Time.

- Tap Apps & Websites.

- Tap Restrictions.

- Make sure Deleting Apps is set to Allowed.

## Check if your device is managed

- Go to Settings, then tap General.

- Tap VPN & Device Management.

- Look for a profile listed under Mobile Device Management. If one appears, your device is managed. Contact your organization's IT admin or employer to see if the app can be deleted.

## Check for third-party apps

- Swipe down from the middle of your Home Screen, and search for third-party apps that might restrict how you use apps. For example, apps like Screen Zen, Cape, Opal, Freedom, or Refocus.

- If you find one, open the app and check its settings to see if it's blocking app deletion.

- If you can't find a setting to allow app deletion, contact the app developer for help.

## Need more help?

Tell us more about what's happening, and we’ll suggest what you can do next.
