# How to unsend messages on your iPhone

In Messages on iOS 16, iPadOS 16.1, macOS Ventura, or later, you can unsend a message for up to two minutes after you send it.

## Unsend messages on your iPhone or iPad with iOS 16 or later

- In Messages, tap a conversation.

- Touch and hold the message you want to unsend.

- Tap Undo Send.

[Image: Tap and hold a message in iOS 16 or later to see the Undo Send option.]

The message disappears and a notification that says "You unsent a message" appears. You can unsend an iMessage for up to two minutes after you send it.

[Image: iPhone screen showing a Messages conversation with the notification "You unsent a message" displayed at the bottom.]

[Unsend a message on Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT202549)

To unsend messages, everyone must use iMessage with iOS 16, iPadOS 16.1, macOS Ventura, or later. If you unsend a message to someone on an older version of iOS, iPad OS, or macOS, your contact might still see the message.
