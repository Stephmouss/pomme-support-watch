# Using eSIM with your iPhone in China mainland

Learn which iPhone models support eSIM in China mainland and how to set up cellular service on your device.

## Supported iPhone models

The following iPhone models support eSIM in China mainland:

- iPhone 18 Pro, model A3715

- iPhone 18 Pro Max, model A3718

- iPhone Duo, model A3721

- iPhone 17e, model A3635

- iPhone Air, model A3518

Other iPhone models, including those purchased outside of China mainland, are unable to install an eSIM profile from carriers in China mainland.

## How to find your iPhone model

Use the model number to find out which model iPhone you have. You can find the model number in either of two ways:

- Look on the back of your iPhone retail packaging for tech specs related to your iPhone model. Some packaging might have a QR code that you can scan to show tech specs.

- Open the Settings app. Tap General and then tap About. To the right of Model Number, you'll find the part number. To find the model number, tap the part number.

[Learn more about identifying your iPhone model](https://support.apple.com/en-us/106343)

## About eSIM for iPhone in China mainland

- In China mainland, China Mobile, China Telecom, and China Unicom support eSIM.

- To activate an eSIM in China mainland, you must have an iPhone model that supports eSIM in China mainland and visit your carrier's store.

- You can activate and store up to two eSIMs on the same eSIM-capable iPhone purchased in China mainland.

- iPhone Duo and iPhone Air activate by eSIM only. iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and iPhone 17e activate by either an eSIM or a physical nano-SIM card.

## Setting up an eSIM with your iPhone in China mainland

To activate eSIM on your iPhone, visit your carrier's store. Store staff will help you with a required ID check and activating your eSIM.

### Before you go to a carrier store

- Set up your iPhone.

- Transfer your data from your current iPhone to your eSIM-supported iPhone. If you can't transfer your data before going to your carrier's store, back up your current iPhone with iCloud:

- On your current iPhone, open the Settings app.

- Tap your name.

- Tap iCloud.

- Tap iCloud Backup.

- Tap Back Up Now.

- Bring a government ID or passport, and, if applicable, your existing SIM card.

## Traveling with your iPhone outside of China mainland

When traveling outside of China mainland, you can activate an eSIM from a local carrier on your eSIM-supported iPhone that was purchased in China mainland. To do so, you must have location services enabled. eSIMs from non-China mainland carriers can't be installed while located in China mainland.

You can have up to two eSIMs on the same eSIM-supported iPhone purchased in China mainland. If you already have two eSIMs on your iPhone, you will have to delete one to activate an additional travel eSIM while abroad. To reactivate your deleted eSIM when returning home, you must visit your carrier store.

Learn more about [using an eSIM while traveling internationally with your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118227) .
