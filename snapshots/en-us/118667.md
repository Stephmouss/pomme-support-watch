# Use Switch Control on your device to control another Apple device

With Use Other Devices for Switch Control, you can control your other Apple devices remotely on the same Wi-Fi network without adjusting any switch connections. This enables you to navigate your Mac or Apple TV with the same switch setup that you use to control your iPhone.

## Use your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch to control another device

- Connect your devices to the same Wi-Fi network.

- Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices.

- If you want to control a Mac, choose System Settings from the Apple menu  on your Mac, click Accessibility in the sidebar, then click Switch Control on the right. Select "Allow platform switching to control your computer."

- [Turn on Switch Control on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119835) .

- Using your switch to navigate the Switch Control menu, select Device.

[Image: iPhone screen displaying the Switch Control menu, used to control other devices.]

- Select Use Other Device.

[Image: iPhone screen displaying the Switch Control menu with "Use Other Device" selected, illustrating how to control another Apple device.]

- Select the device that you want to control, then select Connect. Now you can use the switch connected to your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch to control the other device.

## Use your Mac to control another device

- Connect your devices to the same Wi-Fi network.

- Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices.

- [Turn on Switch Control on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh43607/mac) .

- Using your switch to navigate the Switch Control Home panel, select Devices.

[Image: Switch Control Home panel on Mac, showing icons for Mail, Cursor, Dock, Custom, Devices, Scanner, Location, and Restart, used to navigate Switch Control.]

- Select the device that you want to control, then select Connect. Now you can use the switch connected to your Mac to control the other device.

## Stop controlling another device

To stop controlling another device, press and hold your switch for ten seconds. Your switch will control the original device again. Or use the following steps.

### Stop controlling a Mac

- Using your switch to navigate the Switch Control Home panel, select Devices.

- Select Disconnect. Your switch will control the original device again.

### Stop controlling an iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch

- Using your switch to navigate the Switch Control menu, select Device.

- Select Stop Using Device. Your switch will control the original device again.

[Image: iPhone screen showing the Switch Control menu, highlighting the 'Device' option, a step to stop controlling another device.]

## Turn off Switch Control

If you'd like to turn off Switch Control entirely, follow these steps for your device:

### Turn off Switch Control on an iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch

Use your switch to open Settings, tap Accessibility, then tap Switch Control and turn the setting off. Or triple-click the Home button or the Side button on certain models at any time to exit Switch Control.

### Turn off Switch Control on a Mac

Use your switch to choose System Preferences from the Apple menu . Click Accessibility in the sidebar, then click Switch Control and turn off Switch Control.
