# If Siri isn't working on your iPhone or iPad

Check a few settings on your iPhone or iPad to make sure that Siri is set up to listen for your voice and respond to your requests.

## Turn on "Siri" or "Hey Siri"

- Go to Settings.

- Tap Apple Intelligence & Siri, or tap Siri. Then tap Talk & Type to Siri.

- Make sure "Siri" or "Hey Siri" is turned on. If it's already on, turn it off and back on again.

[Image: Talk & Type to Siri panel open, showing "Siri" or "Hey Siri" selected and the options turned on for Press Side Button for Siri and Type to Siri.]

Restart your device. If Apple Intelligence & Siri or Siri & Search doesn't appear in Settings after your device restarts, check your VPN settings. Some VPN profiles might not allow use of Siri.

## Set up Siri to recognize your voice

- Go to Settings.

- Tap Apple Intelligence & Siri, or tap Siri. Then tap Talk & Type to Siri.

- Turn off Listen for "Siri" or "Hey Siri," then turn it back on.

- When the Siri setup screen appears, tap Continue.

- Say each command that you see on your screen.

- Tap Done.

## If you can't hear Siri

- Go to Settings.

- Tap Apple Intelligence & Siri.

- Tap Siri Responses.

- Tap Prefer Spoken Responses to hear Siri speak responses, even when Silent mode is turned on.

[Image: Siri Responses screen with "Prefer Spoken Responses" selected and the option turned on for "Always Show Request".]

You can also [change Siri's speaking volume and speed](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105092) .

## Update your software

Siri requires iOS 7 or later. If your device is using an older operating system, [update the software](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) on your iPhone or iPad to continue using Siri.
