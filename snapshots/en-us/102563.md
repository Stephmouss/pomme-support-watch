# If you can't back up to iCloud

Learn what to do if you need help backing up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud Backup.

## If an alert says that there's not enough iCloud storage

- Open the Settings app, then tap your name.

- Tap iCloud. At the top, you can [check how much iCloud storage you've used](https://support.apple.com/102670#icloudstorage) .

- Tap Storage or Manage Account Storage.

- Tap Backups, then tap the device you're using. Next Backup Size should show you the size of the next backup. It might take a few minutes for your device to calculate Next Backup Size.

- If your backup needs more space than the amount you have available in iCloud, you can:

- [Delete information that you don't need](https://support.apple.com/108922#make_space) to reduce the size of your backup

- [Upgrade your iCloud+ plan](https://support.apple.com/108349) to get more space

## If a message says that your last backup couldn't be completed

- Open the Settings app, then tap Wi-Fi to make sure that you're connected to Wi-Fi.

- Open the Settings app, tap General, then tap Software Update. Download and install any available updates for your device.

- Try to back up on another Wi-Fi network.

- [Check to make sure that you have enough iCloud storage for the backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102563#alert) .

## If your iCloud backup is taking longer than expected to complete

- Open the Settings app, then tap Wi-Fi to make sure that you're connected to Wi-Fi.

- Check your upload speed.

- Leave your device connected to power and Wi-Fi for 24 hours. If your backup still isn't done, [archive important data](https://support.apple.com/108306) , then try these steps again.

## If the iCloud Backup option is grayed out

- Check if a restore is still in progress. Open the Settings app, tap your name, tap iCloud, then tap iCloud Backup. If your device is still being restored, you see “This [device] is currently being restored and will automatically back up when it is done.” You can create a new backup after your device finishes restoring.

- Check whether you installed a profile that restricts iCloud Backup. Open the Settings app, tap General, then tap VPN & Device Management to see a list of installed profiles.
