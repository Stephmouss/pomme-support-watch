# If you change an Apple Account to a Child Account

If the birthday associated with an account is incorrect, you can change it. You might be prompted to complete additional steps in order to change to a Child Account.

Parents can easily correct the age that is associated with their child’s Apple Account. By moving your child to a Child Account, you can [set parental controls](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105121) to help keep your child’s device usage safe, private, and age appropriate.

## Change the birthday for a child

You can update the birthday of the Apple Account your child or teen is using. Use Settings or System Settings on a device with the account signed in, or sign in with their account on account.apple.com.

[Learn how to update the birthday of an Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102473)

## About converting an Apple Account to a Child Account

If a child or teen is using an Apple Account with a birthday accidentally set to an adult age, they might get a prompt to convert to a Child Account.

In order to convert an existing account to a Child Account, that account:

- Can’t be the organizer or parent/guardian of a Family Sharing group

- Must [join a Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380) , if prompted

* [Age varies by country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125666) .

## If your account is accidentally converted to a Child Account

If your Apple Account is accidentally converted to a Child Account, you can undo the change. If you’re part of a Family Sharing group, you might need the family organizer to help if it’s been longer than 14 days since the change was made.

- Sign in to [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com) .

- Select Personal Information, then choose Birthday.

- Update your birthday and then click Save.

- On the message that appears, click either Revert Account or Request Update. If it’s been more than 14 days since the change was made, your only option is to Request Update.

- Revert Account: You can verify that you’re an adult with a payment method. Follow the onscreen steps to enter your credit card information. This is for verification purposes only. Your card won’t be charged.

- Request Update: Send a request to your Family Sharing organizer to review your account changes. The family organizer receives an email notification to approve.
