# Find the serial number of your MagSafe Battery Pack

Learn how to find the serial number of your MagSafe Battery Pack.

## Check on the MagSafe Battery Pack

The serial number is located below the magnetic ring.

[Image: Close-up of a MagSafe Battery Pack showing the location of the serial number below the magnetic ring.]

## Check in Settings

- Attach your MagSafe Battery Pack to the back of your MagSafe-compatible iPhone model.

- On your iPhone, go to Settings > General > About > MagSafe Battery Pack.
