# Review devices signed in to your Apple Account

Learn which devices appear in your Apple Account device list, how to find your device information, and how to remove a device if you’re no longer using it.

## What devices on your Apple Account can do

When you sign in with your Apple Account on a device, it’s added to your Apple Account device list. This includes Android mobile devices, Windows PCs, and streaming devices that you use with your Apple Account for Apple Music or Apple TV. Depending on the device type, devices signed in to your Apple Account could be used to:

- Change your password

- Receive two-factor authentication verification codes

- Edit personal information

- Sync with iCloud

## When to remove a device from the list

If you aren’t using your device anymore — or if you don’t recognize a listed device — you can remove it from the list. Devices that you remove from your account won’t have access to iCloud, Find My, or other Apple services, and won’t display verification codes for two-factor authentication. The device won’t reappear in the list unless you sign in on that device again.

If your Apple device is lost or you no longer want to use it, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102649#permanentlyremove) .

## View or remove devices in your list

You can sign out of your Apple Account remotely, if needed.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Open Settings.

- Tap [your name], then scroll down to see a list of devices.

[Image: iPhone settings showing a list of devices.]

- Tap any device name to view that device’s information.

- If you want to remove that device from your account by signing out remotely, tap Remove from Account. Review the message that appears, then confirm that you want to remove that device.

If your Apple device is lost or you no longer want to use it, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102649#permanentlyremove) .

### On your Mac

- Choose Apple menu , then choose System Settings.

- Click [your name], then scroll down to see a list of devices.

[Image: Mac System Settings showing a list of devices.]

- Click any device name to view that device’s information.

- If you want to remove that device from your account by signing out remotely, click Remove from Account. Review the message that appears, then confirm that you want to remove that device.

If your Apple device is lost or you no longer want to use it, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102649#permanentlyremove) .

### On the web

If you use a Windows PC, Android mobile device, or don’t have any Apple devices, you can sign in on the web to check your Apple Account device list.

- Sign in to your Apple Account at [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/) .

- Select Devices. If you don’t see your devices right away, click View Details to authenticate and view device details.

[Image: Apple Account website showing devices associated with an account.]

- Click any device name to view that device’s information.

- If you want to remove that device from your account by signing out remotely, click Remove from Account. Review the message that appears, then confirm that you want to remove that device.

If your Apple device is lost or you no longer want to use it, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102649#permanentlyremove) .

## If your Apple device is lost or you no longer want to use it

If you’re still signed in to your Apple Account on the device, it might reappear on your Apple Account screen the next time your device connects to the internet.

- To permanently remove an Apple device from your account, [go to Settings on that device and sign out](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104958) .

- If your Apple device is lost or missing, keep your account data safe. [Use Find My to turn on Lost Mode or erase your device remotely](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120837) .
