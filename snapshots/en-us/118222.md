# If you can't redeem your promotion for an Apple subscription

If you can't redeem your promotion or trial for Apple Arcade, Apple Fitness+, Apple Music, Apple News+, Apple TV, or Apple One, check your eligibility.

## Make sure that you're eligible

To make sure you're eligible to redeem your promotion, check each of the following:

### Check if the promotion is still available

To see if the promotion is still current, [check current Apple promotions](https://www.apple.com/promo/) . Most promotions are available for a limited time and apply to first-time subscribers only.

### Check if your device or subscription qualifies

Read the terms and conditions included with your promotion to confirm that your device or subscription qualifies.

### Check if you previously subscribed to or participated in an Apple Service through Family Sharing

If you subscribed to an Apple service before, or joined one through Family Sharing, you might not be eligible for the promotion.

### Check if you or a family member already redeemed the promotion

If you or a family member already redeemed the promotion, you can't redeem the promotion again.

### If your promotion isn't listed

The promotion might've come from another company. Contact that company to help you redeem the promotion. Learn how to [get help with a promotion from an app developer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/106011) .

## If you've confirmed your eligibility

If you've confirmed you're eligible, but still can't redeem your promotion:

- If you haven't already, download the app for the promotion you want to redeem.

- Update your device to the latest version of [iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , [macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) , [watchOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108926) , or [tvOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108414) . After updating, open the app and redeem the promotion again.

- [Sign in with your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) on the eligible device. After signing in, open the app and redeem the promotion again. If you just set up your Mac, you might need to wait at least 4 hours for the promotion to appear.
