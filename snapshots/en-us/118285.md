# Use Sync Library with your Apple Music subscription

Use Sync Library to stream your music library on any device that's signed in to the Apple Music app with the Apple Account that you use with your Apple Music subscription. Learn what you need to use Sync Library and how to turn it on.

Apple Music isn't a backup service. Make sure to back up your music library before you make any changes.

- [What you need to use Sync Library](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#whatyouneed)

- [Turn on Sync Library on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#mac)

- [Turn on Sync Library on your PC](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#pc)

- [Turn on Sync Library on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#iphoneipad)

- [Stream your music library on other devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#other)

## To use Sync Library, you need a subscription to Apple Music

Sync Library requires a subscription to Apple Music. You can also use Sync Library with an iTunes Match subscription. If you don’t see the option to turn on Sync Library, then it might be because of the following:

- You don’t have a subscription to Apple Music or iTunes Match.

- Your device isn't signed in to the Apple Music app with the same Apple Account that you use with your subscription.

If you don’t want a subscription to Apple Music or iTunes Match, [learn how to manually sync music](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108311) .

## How to turn on Sync Library on your Mac

- Open the Apple Music app.

- In the menu bar, choose Account and make sure that you're signed in with the same Apple Account that you use with your Apple Music subscription.

- Choose Music, then select Settings.

- Click the General tab.

- Select Sync Library. [Don't see Sync Library?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#whatyouneed)

[Image: Apple Music Settings showing Sync Library turned on.]

- Click Ok.

- Wait for your music library to sync. To check the status, go to the bottom of the sidebar and click Updating Cloud Library.

- [Turn on Sync Library on your other devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#turnon) .

## How to turn on Sync Library on your PC

Follow the steps below to turn on Sync Library in the Apple Music app or iTunes for Windows.

### How to turn on Sync Library in the Apple Music app for Windows

- Open the Apple Music app.

- At the bottom of the sidebar, click your name and make sure that you're signed in with the same Apple Account that you use with your Apple Music subscription.

- At the top of the sidebar, click Sidebar Actions .

[Image: sidebar actions button]

- Click Settings.

[Image: Apple Music app for Windows showing Settings in the menu that appears when you click sidebar actions.]

- Click General.

- Turn on Sync Library. [Don't see Sync Library?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#whatyouneed)

[Image: Apple Music app for Windows showing Sync Library turned on in Settings > General.]

- Wait for your music library to sync. To check the status, go to the bottom of the sidebar and click Updating Cloud Library.

- [Turn on Sync Library on your other devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#turnon) .

### How to turn on Sync Library in iTunes for Windows

In iTunes for Windows, Sync Library is called iCloud Music Library. [Learn how to turn on iCloud Music Library in iTunes for Windows](https://support.apple.com/guide/itunes/icloud-music-library-itnsa3dd5209/12.12/windows/10) .

## How to turn on Sync Library on your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Apple Music app and tap the photo icon . Tap Account Settings and make sure that you're signed in with the same Apple Account that you use with your Apple Music subscription.

[Image: Photo icon]

- Open the Settings app.

- Tap Music.

- Turn on Sync Library. [Don't see Sync Library?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#whatyouneed)

[Image: iPhone showing Sync Library turned on in Music Settings.]

- [Turn on Sync Library on your other devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118285#turnon) .

## Stream your music library on other devices

After you turn on Sync Library on your Mac, PC, iPhone, or iPad, you can stream your music library on any device that has the Apple Music app. Just make sure that your device is signed in with the same Apple Account that you use with your Apple Music subscription. You can also stream your music library on [music.apple.com](https://music.apple.com) .
