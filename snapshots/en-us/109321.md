# Use Instant Hotspot to connect to your Personal Hotspot

With the Instant Hotspot feature, you and members of your family group can connect to the Personal Hotspot of your iPhone or iPad without needing to enter its Wi-Fi password. And with Auto-Join Hotspot, the connection is made automatically when needed.

- [Make sure that you have a Personal Hotspot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109321#requirements)

- [Connect manually to your Personal Hotspot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109321#manually)

- [Connect automatically to your Personal Hotspot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109321#automatically)

- [Allow your family to connect to your Personal Hotspot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109321#family)

- [Allow others to connect to your Personal Hotspot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109321#others)

- [If Personal Hotspot isn’t working](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109321#issue)

## Make sure that you have a Personal Hotspot

To provide the Personal Hotspot, you need an iPhone or iPad (Wi-Fi + Cellular) with an activated carrier plan that includes Personal Hotspot service:

- In the Settings app, tap Personal Hotspot (or tap Cellular, then Personal Hotspot).

- If Personal Hotspot is dimmed or unavailable, open Settings, tap Cellular, and make sure that Cellular Data is turned on.

When using Instant Hotspot to connect to your Personal Hotspot, the "Allow Others to Join" setting doesn’t need to be turned on, and you and members of your family group can connect to your Personal Hotspot without being asked to enter its password.

## Connect manually to your Personal Hotspot

With Instant Hotspot, you can manually join your Personal Hotspot from another Apple device just as you would any other Wi-Fi network, except that you aren’t asked to enter the Wi-Fi password.

- Make sure that the devices are near each other and have Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on.

- Make sure that each device is [signed in with the same Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) . Or for family members, make sure that you’ve [set them up to connect to your Personal Hotspot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109321#family) .

- Join your Personal Hotspot from another Apple device:

- On Mac, click Wi-Fi in the menu bar or Control Center , then choose the name of the nearby iPhone or iPad providing your Personal Hotspot .

## Connect automatically to your Personal Hotspot

With Auto-Join Hotspot, your device or your family member’s device can automatically join or ask to join your Personal Hotspot whenever a Wi-Fi network isn't available and the device providing your Personal Hotspot is nearby.

- Make sure that the devices are near each other and have Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on.

- Make sure that each device is [signed in with the same Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) . Or for family members, make sure that you’ve [set them up to connect to your Personal Hotspot](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109321#family) .

- On each device that will join your Personal Hotspot, choose one of these settings as described in the next step:

- Ask to Join: When this device attempts to join your Personal Hotspot, it will show a message that your Personal Hotspot is available and ask whether to join it.

- Automatic. This device can automatically join your Personal Hotspot when needed.

- How to choose the setting:

- On Mac, click Wi-Fi in the menu bar or Control Center , then choose Wi-Fi Settings. Scroll down on the right to see "Ask to join hotspots," then choose Ask to Join or Automatic from the menu. (The Automatic setting is available in macOS Tahoe 26 or later).

[Image: Wi-Fi]

[Image: Control Center]

- On iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro, open Settings, tap Wi-Fi, tap Auto-Join Hotspot, then tap Ask to Join or Automatic.

- On Apple Watch, open Settings, then tap Wi-Fi. Scroll down to Auto Hotspot Settings, then tap Ask to Join or Automatic.

When connected to a Personal Hotspot, the Wi-Fi status icon on your device changes to the Personal Hotspot icon .

## Allow your family to connect to your Personal Hotspot

With Family Sharing, your family can also join your Personal Hotspot without needing to enter the password.

On the iPhone or iPad providing the Personal Hotspot:

- Open Settings, tap Personal Hotspot, then tap Family Sharing.

- Turn on Family Sharing. If you don't see this option, [set up Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108380) .

- Tap the name of a family member, then select a setting:

- Automatic: Allow their device to automatically join your Personal Hotspot when needed.

- Ask for Approval: Whenever their device attempts to join your Personal Hotspot, you’re prompted on your device to allow it.

## Allow others to connect to your Personal Hotspot

To connect to your Personal Hotspot from a device that isn’t signed in to the same Apple Account, isn't part of your family group, or is a Windows PC or other non-Apple device, follow the steps to [connect to Personal Hotspot via Wi-Fi, USB, or Bluetooth](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111785) . In that case, the "Allow Others to Join" setting must be turned on in Personal Hotspot settings.
