# If you forgot your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro passcode, reset your device without a computer

When you see the Security Lockout screen or a device unavailable message in iOS 15.2, iPadOS 15.2, or later, you can reset your iPhone or iPad with that device to regain access.

## What you need

- visionOS, or iOS 15.2 or iPadOS 15.2 or later

- Find My previously enabled on your device

- An active cellular or Wi-Fi connection

- Your Apple Account and password

If you forget your passcode, try [Passcode Reset](https://support.apple.com/105039) first. If you can't use Passcode Reset, you can then reset your iPhone or iPad without a computer to regain access to your Apple device. Finally, if you can't use Passcode Reset and can't reset your iPhone or iPad on your device, you can [reset your iPhone or iPad with a computer](https://support.apple.com/118430) .

## Reset your iPhone in iOS 17 or later

If you're using an eSIM on iOS 16 or earlier, contact your carrier before trying these steps and ask for a QR code to [set up your eSIM](https://support.apple.com/118669) again.

If you can't follow these steps, you can [use a computer to reset your device](https://support.apple.com/118430) .

- From your device's Lock Screen, try to enter your passcode until the "[Device] Unavailable" screen appears.

- When you're able to, tap Forgot Passcode? The Forgot Passcode? button only appears if you have an active cellular or Wi-Fi connection and have Find My turned on.

[Image: The iPhone Unavailable screen in iOS 17 or later includes a Forgot Passcode? option.]

- On the Security Lockout screen, tap Start [Device] Reset to confirm.

- Enter your Apple Account password to sign out of your Apple Account on your device.

- Tap Erase [Device] to permanently delete all of your data and settings. If you use an eSIM with iOS 17 or later, you can also choose to keep your eSIM and erase your data, or to delete your eSIM and erase your data.

[Image: When you go through the process to reset your passcode in iOS 17 and later, you can choose to keep your eSIM or delete your eSIM.]

6. When your device restarts, follow the onscreen instructions to set up your device again, [restore your data and setting from a backup](https://support.apple.com/118105) if you have one, and set a new passcode.

If you use iOS 17 or later, the Forgot Passcode? option appears when you are locked out, connected to a cellular or Wi-Fi network, signed into your Apple Account, and have Find My turned on.

If you use iOS 15.2 or iOS 16, an Erase [Device] option appears when you are locked out, connected to a cellular or Wi-Fi network, signed into your Apple Account, and have Find My turned on.

## Reset your iPad in iPadOS 17 or later

- From your device's Lock Screen, try to enter your passcode until the "[Device] Unavailable" screen appears.

- When you're able to, tap Forgot Passcode?

[Image: The Device Unavailable screen appears on an iPad after a failed passcode attempt.]

- On the Security Lockout screen, tap Start [Device] Reset to confirm.

- Enter your Apple Account password to sign out of your Apple Account on your device.

- Tap Erase [Device] to permanently delete all of your data and settings.

[Image: To reset your iPad when you forget your passcode, you need to erase the media, content, and settings as part of the process.]

- When your device restarts, follow the onscreen instructions to set up your device again, [restore your data and setting from a backup](https://support.apple.com/118105) if you have one, and set a new passcode.

If you can't follow these steps, you can [use a computer to reset your device](https://support.apple.com/119858) .

If you use iPadOS 17 or later, the Forgot Passcode? option appears when you are locked out, connected to a cellular or Wi-Fi network, signed into your Apple Account, and have Find My turned on.

If you use iPadOS 15.2 or iPadOS 16, an Erase [Device] option appears when you are locked out, connected to a cellular or Wi-Fi network, signed into your Apple Account, and have Find My turned on.
