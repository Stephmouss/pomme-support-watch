# Use iCloud on a virtual machine

On a virtual machine with macOS Sequoia or later, you can use many of your favorite iCloud features, but some features aren't available.

## iCloud features that you can use on a virtual machine

To sign in to your Apple Account on a virtual machine, your host machine must be updated to macOS Sequoia or later. When you sign in to your Apple Account on a virtual machine, you can use services like Messages, FaceTime, and most iCloud features, just as you would on an Apple device.

## iCloud features not available on a virtual machine

- iCloud Backup

- Find My

- Apple Media Services, such as apps, movies, music, books, and more

- Apple Wallet

If you need to make changes to your Apple Account, including updating payment information or attempting account recovery, or need to make a new Apple Account, you need to use an iPhone, iPad, Apple Vision Pro, Mac, or [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) .
