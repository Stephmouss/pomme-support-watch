# How to delete calendars and remove events on your iPhone

If you have unwanted events or calendars in Calendar on your iPhone, you can delete, unsubscribe, or hide them.

## Delete a calendar event

- In the Calendar app, tap the unwanted event.

- Tap Delete Event, then tap Delete Event again. Or if you want to report an event from an unknown contact, tap Report Junk, then tap Delete and Report Junk.

## Delete or unsubscribe from a calendar

- In the Calendar app, tap the Calendars button .

- Tap the info button next to the calendar.

- Tap Delete Calendar or Unsubscribe. If a calendar can't be removed (for example, the US Holidays calendar), you can hide it instead.

- To confirm, tap Delete Calendar or Unsubscribe again.

## Hide a calendar

- In the Calendar app, tap the Calendars button .

- Tap to deselect the checkmark next to the calendar.
