# How to add Apple Card to your Apple devices

You can add Apple Card to your iPhone, Apple Watch, iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro.

Sign in to your Apple Watch, iPad, Mac, or Vision Pro with the same Apple Account that was signed in to iCloud on the iPhone that you used to apply for Apple Card.

## Add Apple Card to your iPhone

- Update to [the latest version of iOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app.

- Tap the add button .

- Tap Previous Cards, make sure only Apple Card is selected, then tap Continue.

After you add Apple Card to your iPhone, you can [request a titanium Apple Card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111741) in the Wallet app.

## Add Apple Card to your Apple Watch

- Update to [the latest version of watchOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108926) .

- Open the Apple Watch app on your iPhone, then go to the My Watch tab. If you have multiple watches, choose one.

- Tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Under Other Cards On Your Phone, tap Add next to Apple Card.

- If you want to make Apple Card your default payment card for Apple Pay, tap Use as Default Card.

You can [make Apple Card your default card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118219) on your Apple Watch. This makes Apple Card your default card when you use Apple Pay on your Apple Watch.

## Add Apple Card to your iPad

- Update to [the latest version of iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118219) .

- Open the Settings app, then tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Under Other Cards on Your iPad, you see Apple Card.

- Tap Add.

You can [make Apple Card your default card on your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118219) . This makes Apple Card your default card when you use Apple Pay on your iPad.

## Add Apple Card to your Mac

- Update to [the latest version of macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118219) .

- Go to System Settings (or System Preferences), then select Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Click Add Card.

- Select Apple Card, then choose Next and follow the steps on your screen.

You can [make Apple Card your default card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118219) on your Mac. This makes Apple Card your default card when you use Apple pay on your Mac.

To add Apple Card to Wallet, you need a Mac model with Touch ID. If you have a Mac model without built-in Touch ID, you can turn on Allow Payments on Mac to complete your purchases on your eligible iPhone or Apple Watch:

On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then turn on Allow Payments on Mac.

## Add Apple Card to your Vision Pro

- Update to [the latest version of visionOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118481) .

- Open the Settings app, then tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Under Payment Cards, tap Add Card, then tap Cards Found For You.

- Make sure that only Apple Card is selected, tap Continue, then tap Continue to confirm.

You can [make Apple Card your default card on your Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118219) . This makes Apple Card your default card when you use Apple Pay on your iPad.

## If you have trouble adding Apple Card to a new device

- Make sure that you're signed into the device with the same Apple Account that you used to apply for Apple Card.

- Make sure your device has a good internet connection.

- Make sure the Settings app and Wallet app are enabled for cellular data:

- Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.

- Tap Cellular.

- Turn on Cellular Data.

Be sure to check [which devices are compatible with Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102896) .
