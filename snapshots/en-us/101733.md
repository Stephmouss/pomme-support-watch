# If your droplets won't open after you update Compressor

If your custom Compressor droplets won’t open after updating Compressor, update your droplets.

- Locate your droplet, then double-click the droplet to open it.

- In the alert that appears, click Open.

- If another alert appears telling you the droplet can't be opened, click Quit.

- Repeat the previous steps for each droplet.

- After you've tried to open each droplet, open Compressor, choose Compressor > Preferences, then click Advanced.

- Click Update Droplets, then click Update.
