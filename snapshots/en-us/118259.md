# Restart your iPhone

Learn how to turn your iPhone off, then back on.

## How to restart your iPhone X, 11, 12, 13, or later

- Press and hold either volume button and the side button until the power-off slider appears.

[Image: Pressing the volume button on the left and the side button on the right initiates the power-off sequence.]

- Drag the slider, then wait 30 seconds for your device to turn off. If your device is frozen or unresponsive, [force restart your device](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/force-restart-iphone-iph8903c3ee6/ios) .

- To turn your device back on, press and hold the side button (on the right side of your iPhone) until you see the Apple logo.

## How to restart your iPhone 6, 7, 8, or SE (2nd or 3rd generation)

- Press and hold the side button until the power-off slider appears.

[Image: Press and hold the side button on your iPhone as the first step to restart it.]

- Drag the slider, then wait 30 seconds for your device to turn off. If your device is frozen or unresponsive, [force restart your device](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/force-restart-iphone-iph8903c3ee6/ios) .

- To turn your device back on, press and hold the side button until you see the Apple logo.

## How to restart your iPhone SE (1st generation), 5, or earlier

- Press and hold the top button until the power-off slider appears.

[Image: The power button on top of iPhone SE (1st generation), 5, or earlier]

- Drag the slider, then wait 30 seconds for your device to turn off. If your device is frozen or unresponsive, [force restart your device](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/force-restart-iphone-iph8903c3ee6/ios) .

- To turn your device back on, press and hold the top button until you see the Apple logo.
