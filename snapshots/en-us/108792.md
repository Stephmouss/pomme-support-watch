# If you can't add a card to Apple Wallet to use with Apple Pay

Learn what steps to take if you can't add a card to the Wallet app to use with Apple Pay.

- Check that you're able to use Apple Pay:

- Contact your card issuer to see if they [support Apple Pay.](http://support.apple.com/kb/HT204916)

- Update to the latest version of [iOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , [iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , [watchOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108926) , [macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) , or [visionOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118481) .

- Confirm that you have [Face ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108411) , [Touch ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102528) , [Optic ID](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120145) , or a [passcode set on your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119586) .

- Make sure that you are in a [supported country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102775) .

- Check that your [device is compatible with Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102896) .

- Confirm that you have [turned on two-factor authentication for your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102660) .

- Check for [service outages or connection issues](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108093) .

- Contact your bank or card issuer if you see the following error messages while adding your card to Apple Wallet:

- Could Not Add Card

- Invalid Card

- Card Device Limit

Apple does not approve or decline cards for use with Apple Pay. If your card was declined, contact your bank or card issuer.

If you need help adding a card, learn how to [set up Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/108398) . For issues connecting your account in the Wallet app or viewing your account details, learn how to [connect your account to the Wallet app](http://support.apple.com/111117#unabletoconnect) .

To use Apple Pay you need a supported card from a participating card issuer. To check if your card is compatible with Apple Pay, contact your card issuer.

Apple Pay is not available in all markets. [View Apple Pay countries and regions.](https://www.apple.com/ios/feature-availability/#apple-pay)
