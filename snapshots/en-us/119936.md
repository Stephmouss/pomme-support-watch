# About 3D movie upgrade eligibility with Movies Anywhere and other services on Apple Vision Pro

Learn about which movies are eligible to be upgraded to 3D.

If you purchased an HD or 4K movie through the Store and the movie is available in 3D, it will be upgraded to the 3D version for free. You can find the 3D versions on your Vision Pro in TV App > Library > 3D.

### If you purchased your movie from Movies Anywhere or another service

Movies will not be upgraded to the 3D version if you:

- Purchase your movie from Movies Anywhere, Vudu, or other similar services.

- Redeemed the movie in SD using a digital redemption code or [a digital copy of a DVD or Blu-ray](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118413) .

You will still be able to watch your movies from Movies Anywhere or another service in 2D on your Vision Pro.
