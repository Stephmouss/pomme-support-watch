# How to set a Screen Time passcode on an iPhone or iPad

You can create a Screen Time passcode to ensure the settings you choose for your child's device or your own device remain in place. That means the passcode must be entered before you can change any Screen Time settings. When a Screen Time passcode is enabled for a child's device, you'll receive a notification any time the passcode is used on their device.

[Image: Play the video]

A Screen Time passcode isn’t the same as the passcode you use to unlock your device or a child's device. It’s important to keep track of your Screen Time passcode because you need it to change the Screen Time settings for your device or a child’s device. You can change or remove a Screen Time passcode at any time.

If you use [Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/108380) to manage a child’s Apple Account, the Family Sharing organizer or a parent/guardian needs to set the child's Screen Time passcode on their own device, not the child's device.

[Learn what to do if you need to change your Screen Time passcode](https://support.apple.com/102677)

## Set a Screen Time passcode for your child’s iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap Screen Time.

- Scroll down and tap your child’s name under Family. If you don’t see your child’s name under Family, make sure you’re both signed in with your own Apple Account and have Family Sharing set up.

- Tap Manage Screen Time, then tap Lock Screen Time Settings.

[Image: iPhone screen showing Manage Screen Time settings for child with Lock Screen Time Settings highlighted.]

- Enter a four-digit passcode, then enter the passcode again to confirm.

## Set a Screen Time passcode for your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap Screen Time.

- Scroll down, tap Manage Screen Time, then tap Lock Screen Time Settings.

[Image: iPhone screen showing Manage Screen Time settings with a button to Lock Screen Time.]

- Enter a four-digit passcode, then enter the passcode again to confirm.

- To add a Screen Time Passcode Recovery, enter the email address or phone number and password for your Apple Account.

[Image: iPhone screen showing Screen Time Passcode Recovery options, with fields for Apple ID and password.]

It’s recommended that you don’t skip adding a Screen Time Passcode Recovery. That way, if you forget your Screen Time passcode, you can use your Apple Account to reset it. If you have more than one Apple Account, you must use the same account you entered when you first set up the Screen Time Passcode Recovery.

It's important to remember the Apple Account that you used for your Screen Time Passcode Recovery because without it, you won't be able to remove or reset your Screen Time passcode, and Apple won't be able to provide that information.
