# How to create a Screen Time passcode on an iPhone or iPad

You can create a Screen Time passcode so the settings you choose for your child's device or your own device stay in place. That means the passcode must be entered before you can change any Screen Time settings. When a Screen Time passcode is enabled for a child's device, you'll receive a notification any time they use the passcode on their device.

- [How a Screen Time passcode works](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126533#how)

- [How to set a Screen Time passcode for your child's iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126533#child)

- [How to set a Screen Time passcode for your own iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126533#adult)

## How a Screen Time passcode works

A Screen Time passcode isn’t the same as the passcode you use to unlock your device. It’s important to keep track of your Screen Time passcode because you need it to change the Screen Time settings for your device or a child’s device.

To ensure that your Screen Time settings sync across all the devices in your Family Sharing group, update all the devices to the latest software version before turning on or changing your Screen Time settings. If you have Share Across Devices turned on, your Screen Time passcode automatically updates on those devices if you reset it.

If you use [Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/108380) to manage a child's Apple Account, the Family Sharing organizer or a parent/guardian needs to reset the Screen Time passcode on their own device, not the child's device.

[Learn what to do if you forgot your Screen Time passcode, or just need to to reset it](https://support.apple.com/102677)

## How to set a Screen Time passcode for your child’s device

These steps apply to iOS 27 and iPadOS 27 and later. Learn how to [set a Screen Time passcode for your child's device on iOS 26 and earlier](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-manage-track-a-screen-time-passcode-iph272b4c4bd/26/ios/26) .

- On the Family Sharing organizer or parent/guardian's iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app.

- Tap Family, then tap your child's name. If you don’t see your child’s name under Family, make sure you’re both signed in to your devices with your own Apple Account and that your child's account is part of the Family Sharing group.

- Tap Passcode & Management. If you don't see Passcode & Management, you may need to [set up usage and parental controls first](https://support.apple.com/108806) , then set up the Screen Time passcode.

- Tap Lock Screen Time Settings.

- Enter a four-digit passcode, then enter the passcode again to confirm.

## Set a Screen Time passcode for your own iPhone or iPad

These steps apply to iOS 27 and iPadOS 27 and later. Learn how to [set a Screen Time passcode on your iPhone with iOS 26](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/create-manage-track-a-screen-time-passcode-iph272b4c4bd/26/ios/26) .

- Open the Settings app, then scroll down and tap Screen Time.

- Scroll down, tap Passcode & Management.

- Tap Lock Screen Time Settings.

[Image: iPhone screen showing Manage Screen Time settings with a button to Lock Screen Time.]

- Enter a four-digit passcode, then enter the passcode again to confirm.

- To add a Screen Time Passcode Recovery, enter the email address or phone number and password for your Apple Account.

[Image: iPhone screen showing Screen Time Passcode Recovery options, with fields for Apple Account and password.]

It’s recommended that you don’t skip adding a Screen Time Passcode Recovery. That way, if you forget your Screen Time passcode, you can use your Apple Account to reset it. If you need to recover your Screen Time passcode, you must use the same account you entered when you first set up the Screen Time Passcode Recovery.

It's important to remember the Apple Account that you used for your Screen Time Passcode Recovery because without it, you won't be able to remove or reset your Screen Time passcode, and Apple may not be able to provide that information.
