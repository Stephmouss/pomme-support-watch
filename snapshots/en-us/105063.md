# Use buttons and gestures on your Apple Watch

Learn how to interact with your Apple Watch using the Digital Crown, side button, Action button, and gestures.

## Press or turn the Digital Crown

[Image: Apple Watch showing the Digital Crown]

- Press to see the watch face.

- From the watch face, press to browse the apps on the Home Screen.

- Double-click to [open the App Switcher](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105093) .

- Press and hold to [use Siri](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apd02f71f945/) .

- Turn while on the watch face to [use the Smart Stack](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apdecf142fb9) .

- Turn to zoom, scroll, or adjust what's on the screen.

- Press and hold to [unlock the screen after a water workout](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apd09135915f) .

The Digital Crown works differently if you're using an [earlier version of watchOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105063#previousOS) .

## Press the side button

[Image: Apple Watch showing the side button]

- Press to show or hide Control Center.

- Press and hold to [use Emergency SOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108374) .

- Double-click to [use Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102626) .

- Press and hold to turn your watch on or off.

The side button works differently if you're using an [earlier version of watchOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105063#previousOS) .

## Press the Action button on Apple Watch Ultra

If you have Apple Watch Ultra, you can [customize the Action button](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apda005904ef) on the left side of the watch to access your favorite features quickly. You can also press and hold the Action button to [activate the siren](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/siren-apd49c8baf38/11.0/watchos/11.0) or access your Medical ID, Compass Backtrack, and Emergency SOS Call.

## Use taps and gestures

You can use taps and gestures to interact with your Apple Watch.

### Tap

[Image: Person tapping their finger on Apple Watch screen]

Tap the screen to select a button or item. On Apple Watch models with [Always On](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105074) , a tap also brings the screen to full brightness.

### Tap and hold

[Image: Person holding their finger on Apple Watch screen]

Tap the screen and keep your finger in place to [change the watch face](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apda6559ad78) , see options in an app, and more.

### Drag

[Image: Person dragging their finger across Apple Watch screen]

Drag your finger across the screen to scroll or adjust a slider.

### Swipe

[Image: Person swiping their finger across Apple Watch screen]

Swipe up, down, left, or right to see different screens.

### Double tap gesture

[Image: Person using double tap gesture with Apple Watch]

Tap your index finger and thumb together twice to [perform common actions on Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apdabb7b275c) .

### Wrist flick gesture

[Image: Person using wrist flick gesture with Apple Watch]

Quickly turn your wrist away from you and back to [dismiss notifications, timers, calls, and more](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/quickly-mute-notifications-with-gestures-apd8bcbaa778/26/watchos/26) .

Double tap and wrist flick gestures are available for Apple Watch Series 9 and later, Apple Watch SE 3, and Apple Watch Ultra 2 and later.

## If you haven't updated to watchOS 10 or later

Here's how to interact with the Digital Crown and side button with watchOS 9 and earlier.

Press or turn the Digital Crown:

- Press to see the watch face or Home Screen.

- Double-click to return to the last app.

- Press and hold to [use Siri](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apd02f71f945/) .

- Turn to zoom, scroll, or adjust what's on the screen.

- Press and hold to [unlock the screen after a water workout](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apd09135915f) .

Press the side button:

- Press to show or hide the Dock.

- Press and hold to [use Emergency SOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108374) .

- Double-click to [use Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102626) .

- Press and hold to turn your watch on or off.
