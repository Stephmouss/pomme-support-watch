# Get unlimited Daily Cash with Apple Card

Learn how to use Apple Card to get a percentage of every purchase back as Daily Cash.

## What is Daily Cash?

When you make a purchase with Apple Card, you get a percentage of your purchase back in Daily Cash. [Apply for an Apple Card Savings account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102676) to have your Daily Cash automatically deposited into Savings, where it earns interest. 1 Set up Apple Cash to have Daily Cash automatically deposited into Apple Cash so that you can spend it with Apple Pay or send it to people you know. 2 There’s no limit to how much Daily Cash you can get, and you choose where it goes.

## How to find your Daily Cash

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap the More button .

[Image: the more button]

- Tap Rewards & Offers.

Here, you'll see:

- The lifetime Daily Cash that you've received

- How to earn Daily Cash

- Your Daily Cash Election

- Special offers

- Merchants that offer 3% Daily Cash when you use your Apple Card with Apple Pay

### See the Daily Cash that you've earned in a specific week, month, or year

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap your Apple Card.

- Tap Weekly Activity, Monthly Activity, or Yearly Activity.

- From the top of the screen, tap Week, Month, or Year to see the total amount of Daily Cash you received for each time period.

- To view a previous week, month, or year, swipe right.

### See the Daily Cash that you earned for a specific purchase

1. Open the Wallet app, then tap your Apple Card.

2. From the top-right of your screen, tap the Search icon . You can search by category, merchant, location, date, or transaction amount.

3. When you find the transaction, tap it to see the Daily Cash you earned.

You can also see the charge amount and a map showing where the purchase was made.

If you use Apple Card Family, you can see who made the purchase.

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

Here, you can download or open merchant apps, access merchant websites, and find store locations.

[Learn more about the merchants that offer 3% Daily Cash when you use Apple Card with Apple Pay](https://support.apple.com/120400)

## Choose where to send your Daily Cash

- Select Savings for your Daily Cash to be deposited into your Savings account. [Apply for a Savings account.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102676)

- Select Apple Cash for your Daily Cash to be deposited into your Apple Cash account. [Set up Apple Cash.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109304)

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Rewards & Offers.

- Under Daily Cash Election, select Savings or Apple Cash.

You can have one Daily Cash Election at a time.

When you open a Savings account with Apple Card, if you have any Daily Cash available to redeem, it will automatically be transferred to your new Savings account.

[Image: Apple Card Rewards and Offers on iPhone. Savings is selected under "Daily Cash Election".]

## If you don't set up Savings or Apple Cash

- If you don't set up Savings or Apple Cash as your Daily Cash Election, your Daily Cash from Apple Card purchases accumulates.

- The Daily Cash that accumulates can be redeemed as credit to your Apple Card account when you pay your bill.

- If you later make a Daily Cash Election, any accrued but unredeemed Daily Cash will be transferred to your Daily Cash Election.

## What happens to Daily Cash when you return a purchase

If you return a purchase made with Apple Card and are issued a credit for the purchase price, the Daily Cash you received when you made the purchase is charged back to your Apple Card.

## If you share an Apple Card or use Apple Card Family

- Each person in the family receives unlimited Daily Cash for their own purchases. 3

- Only owners and co-owners 4 can get a Savings account.

- Apple Card Family 5 participants 6 can direct their Daily Cash to Apple Cash, but not to Savings.

- If a participant in the family is under 18, the family organizer [must set up Apple Cash for their account](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/use-apple-cash-family-apddee18ec3d/watchos#:~:text=To%20set%20up%20Apple%20Cash,teen,%20then%20tap%20Apple%20Cash) before they can receive Daily Cash.
