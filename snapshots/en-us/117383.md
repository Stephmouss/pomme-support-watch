# Use a wired connection to transfer data to a new iPhone or iPad

You can migrate data from your current iPhone to a new iPhone — or from your current iPad to a new iPad — by connecting the devices directly with a cable.

Which type of device are you using?

### Transfer data from an iPhone 15 or later to another iPhone 15 or later

To migrate data from one iPhone 15 or later to another iPhone 15 or later using a wired connection, use a [USB-C Charge Cable](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MQKJ3AM/A/60w-usb-c-charge-cable-1-m) . Connect the USB-C Charge Cable to the charging ports of your previous iPhone and your new iPhone. Then:

- Turn on both devices and follow the onscreen instructions.

- Connect your new iPhone to Wi-Fi or your device's cellular network.

- You might be asked to [activate your cellular service](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118669) .

- Set up Face ID.

- You'll need to wait for the transfer to complete on both devices before you can use them. Transfer times can vary based on factors such as network conditions and the amount of data being transferred.

To migrate iPhone data to your new iPhone wirelessly, [use Quick Start to transfer data to a new iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102659) ﻿.

### Transfer data from an iPhone 14 or earlier to an iPhone 15 or later

To migrate data from an iPhone 14 or earlier to an iPhone 15 or later using a wired connection, use a [USB-C to Lightning Cable](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MUQ93AM/A/usb-c-to-lightning-cable-1-m) . Connect the USB-C to Lightning Cable to the charging ports of your previous iPhone and your new iPhone. Then:

- Turn on both devices and follow the onscreen instructions.

- Connect your new iPhone to Wi-Fi or your device's cellular network.

- You might be asked to [activate your cellular service](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118669) .

- Set up Face ID or Touch ID.

- You'll need to wait for the transfer to complete on both devices before you can use them. Transfer times can vary based on factors such as network conditions and the amount of data being transferred.

### Transfer data from an iPhone 14 or earlier to another iPhone 14 or earlier

To migrate data from an iPhone 14 or earlier to another iPhone 14 or earlier using a wired connection, use a [Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MX5J3AM/A/lightning-to-usb-3-camera-adapter) and a [Lightning to USB Cable](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MUQW3AM/A/lightning-to-usb-cable-1-m) . Then:

- Connect the Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter to power through its Lightning port. Make sure to use a [12W or higher power adapter](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MGN03AM/A/apple-12w-usb-power-adapter) .

- Connect the Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter to your current iPhone.

- Plug the Lightning to USB Cable into your new iPhone, then connect the other end to the USB 3 Camera Adapter.

- Turn on both devices and follow the onscreen instructions.

- Connect your new iPhone to Wi-Fi or your device's cellular network.

- You might be asked to [activate your cellular service](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118669) .

- Set up Face ID or Touch ID.

- You'll need to wait for the transfer to complete on both devices before you can use them. Transfer times can vary based on factors such as network conditions and the amount of data being transferred.
