# About the sweat and water resistance of AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, and AirPods 4

Learn about sweat and water resistance on your AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, and AirPods 4 (both models), and what to do if your AirPods get wet.

AirPods Pro 1 are sweat and water resistant for non-water sports and exercise, and have a rating of IPX4 under IEC standard 60529. The MagSafe Charging Case and Wireless Charging Case for AirPods Pro 1 are not water resistant or sweat resistant.

The AirPods and charging case for these models are sweat and water resistant for non-water sports and exercise, and have a rating of IPX4 under IEC standard 60529:

- AirPods Pro 2 with MagSafe Charging Case (Lightning)

- AirPods 3 with Lightning Charging Case

- AirPods 3 with MagSafe Charging Case

The AirPods and charging case for these models are dust, sweat, and water resistant for non-water sports and exercise, and have a rating of IP54 under IEC standard 60529:

- AirPods Pro 2 with MagSafe Charging Case (USB-C)

- AirPods 4 with Charging Case (USB-C)

- AirPods 4 (ANC) with Wireless Charging Case

AirPods Pro 3 and the MagSafe Charging Case (USB-C) for AirPods Pro 3 are dust, sweat, and water resistant for non-water sports and exercise, and have a rating of IP57 under IEC standard 60529 (maximum depth of 1 meter up to 30 minutes).

AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, and AirPods 4 (both models) are sweat and water resistant, but they are not waterproof or sweatproof. They aren't designed for use in water sports like swimming, or for showering. After workouts, heavy sweating, or water splashes and before putting AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, or AirPods 4 (both models) into its charging case, wipe down your AirPods with a soft, dry, lint-free cloth. Don't use heat or compressed air to dry your AirPods.

References to AirPods Pro below include AirPods Pro 1, AirPods Pro 2, AirPods Pro 3, the MagSafe Charging Case (Lightning) for AirPods Pro 2, the MagSafe Charging Case (USB-C) for AirPods Pro 2, and the MagSafe Charging Case (USB-C) for AirPods Pro 3. References to AirPods 3 below include the MagSafe Charging Case for AirPods 3 and the Lightning Charging Case for AirPods 3. References to AirPods 4 below include the AirPods 4 (ANC) and AirPods 4, and the AirPods 4 (ANC) Wireless Charging Case and the AirPods 4 Charging Case (USB-C).

## Keep your AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, and AirPods 4 safe

Sweat, water, and dust resistance aren't permanent conditions and can diminish over time. AirPods and charging cases can't be rechecked or resealed for sweat, water, or dust resistance.

Follow these tips to avoid damage to your AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, and AirPods 4:

- Don't place your AirPods under running water, such as a shower or faucet.

- Don't swim with your AirPods.

- Don't put AirPods in the washing machine or dryer.

- Don't wear AirPods in a sauna or steam room.

- Don't expose AirPods to high-velocity water, such as while water skiing.

- Avoid dropping AirPods or subjecting them to other impacts.

- Don't submerge your AirPods Pro 1, AirPods Pro 2, AirPods 3, or AirPods 4.

If your AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, or AirPods 4 are exposed to liquid through any of the scenarios above:

- Wipe them clean with a soft, dry, lint-free cloth.

- Allow them to dry completely — for at least two hours — before using them or placing them in the charging case.

Don't expose AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, and AirPods 4 to soaps, shampoos, conditioners, lotions, perfumes, solvents, detergent, acids or acidic foods, insect repellent, sunscreen, oil, or hair dye. All of these can negatively affect water seals and acoustic membranes. If your AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, or AirPods 4 are exposed to any of these, [learn how to clean your AirPods Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120409) or [AirPods 3 and AirPods 4](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102672) .

If your AirPods Pro, AirPods 3, or AirPods 4 are exposed to dust, wipe them clean with a soft, dry, lint-free cloth. Don't use cleaning products or compressed air.
