# About Apple Creator Studio

Everything you need to know about Apple Creator Studio—which apps are included, where to download, how to subscribe, system requirements, intelligence features, and more.

Apple Creator Studio is a collection of creativity and productivity apps from Apple that includes Final Cut Pro, Logic Pro, Pixelmator Pro, Motion, Compressor, and MainStage. The subscription also includes premium content and AI features in Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Freeform.

[Get an overview of Apple Creator Studio features](https://www.apple.com/apple-creator-studio)

## Subscribe to Apple Creator Studio

When you first open an Apple Creator Studio app, you can sign up for a free one-month trial with your Apple Account and choose a monthly or an annual subscription. You can’t use a Managed Apple Account to subscribe.

- Open one of the Apple Creator Studio apps, click or tap Continue, then follow any onscreen instructions. For Keynote, Pages, Numbers, or Freeform, you can subscribe from within the app when you interact with premium content or features. You can't subscribe within Keynote or Freeform on Apple Vision Pro.

- Click or tap Accept Free Trial (or another available option), then follow the onscreen instructions to subscribe using your Apple Account.

- To get started, create a new project or document or open an existing project or document in an Apple Creator Studio app.

If you're already subscribed to Final Cut Pro for iPad or Logic Pro for iPad, download and open another Apple Creator Studio app, then subscribe from within that app.

When you [purchase a new Mac or iPad](https://www.apple.com/promo/pdf/EN_US_Apple_Creator_Studio_Promo_TandCs.pdf) , you can get three months of Apple Creator Studio for free. To get the offer, make sure to subscribe with the Apple Account that you used to set up the new device.

## Manage your subscription

You can modify or cancel your Apple Creator Studio subscription from your Mac, iPad, or iPhone. If you cancel your subscription, you'll retain access to all included apps and features until the end of the billing period of your subscription.

After the end of the billing period, you'll still have access to Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Freeform but without the intelligence features included in the subscription. You'll also still retain access to the projects and files you created with the apps included in the subscription. That way, you can work on your projects if you decide to resubscribe later or you want to purchase one of the apps individually.

### Manage your subscription on Mac

- In one of the included apps, choose the app name from the menu bar, then choose Manage Subscriptions from the menu. The App Store opens and shows your subscriptions. If you don’t see your subscriptions, make sure you’re [signed in to the Apple Account in the App Store](https://support.apple.com/guide/app-store/sign-in-fir6253293d/mac) that you used to subscribe to Apple Creator Studio.

- In the Subscriptions list, click Apple Creator Studio, then modify or cancel your subscription:

- To modify your subscription plan, click a different subscription option, then follow the instructions.

- To cancel your subscription or free trial, click Cancel Subscription, then follow the instructions.

- To change or update your payment method, click your name in the lower-left corner, click Account Settings, click Payment Methods, then follow the instructions.

### Manage your subscription on iPad or iPhone

- From the Home screen, open the Settings app, then tap your Apple Account.

- Tap Subscriptions. If you don’t see your subscriptions, make sure you’re [signed in to the Apple Account in the App Store](https://support.apple.com/guide/app-store/sign-in-fir6253293d/mac) that you used to subscribe to Apple Creator Studio.

- In the Subscriptions list, tap Apple Creator Studio, then modify or cancel your subscription:

- To modify your subscription plan, tap See All Plans, tap a different subscription option, then follow the onscreen instructions.

- To cancel your subscription or free trial, tap Cancel Subscription, then follow the onscreen instructions.

- To change or update your payment method:

- Open the App Store, then tap the My Account button or your picture at the top right.

- Tap your name.

- Tap Manage Payments, then tap Payment Methods.

- Edit or remove the existing payment method or add a new payment method.

## Share Apple Creator Studio with your family

When you enroll in Family Sharing, you and up to five other family members can share access to an Apple Creator Studio subscription. To share an Apple Creator Studio monthly or annual subscription, make sure the subscription appears under Family Sharing > Subscription Sharing and is active.

[Learn more about sharing apps and purchases with your family](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108774)

Family sharing is not available for higher education student and educator subscriptions.

## Get an Apple Creator Studio college student or educator subscription

If you’re a verified college student enrolled in or a verified educator employed at a degree-granting college or university, you can [subscribe to Apple Creator Studio at a discounted monthly or yearly rate](https://support.apple.com/en-us/126312) .

For other students and educators, you can purchase the Pro Apps Bundle for Education at a special discounted price. The Pro Apps Bundle for Education includes the one-time purchase versions of Final Cut Pro, Motion, Compressor, Logic Pro, and MainStage. The bundle is available from the [Apple Store for Education Institution](https://ecommerce.apple.com/asb2bstorefront/fys?language=EN&country=US) .

## Apple Creator Studio system requirements

Full Apple Creator Studio functionality is available with macOS 26, iPadOS 26, and iOS 26. Apple Creator Studio is available in the App Store and requires an Apple Account. Features are subject to change, and some may require internet access. Additional fees and terms may apply.

Minimum system requirements for each Apple Creator Studio app are as follows:

- All Mac apps require macOS 15.6 or later, except for Pixelmator Pro, which requires macOS 26 or later.

- Final Cut Pro:

- Final Cut Pro on Mac requires macOS 15.6 or later.

- Final Cut Pro for iPad requires iPadOS 18.6 or later and an iPad, iPad Pro, or iPad Air with an Apple M1 chip or later, iPad (A16), or iPad mini (A17 Pro).

- Logic Pro:

- Logic Pro for Mac requires macOS 15.6 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

- Logic Pro for iPad requires iPadOS 26 or later and an iPad with Apple A12 Bionic chip or later. Some features require Apple A17 Pro chip or later.

- Pixelmator Pro:

- Pixelmator Pro for Mac requires macOS 26 or later.

- Pixelmator Pro for iPad requires iPadOS 26 or later and an iPad, iPad Pro, or iPad Air with an Apple M1 chip or later, iPad (A16), or iPad mini (A17 Pro).

- Keynote, Pages, and Numbers:

- Keynote, Pages, and Numbers on Mac require macOS 15.6 or later.

- Keynote, Pages, and Numbers for iPad and iPhone require iPadOS 18 or later and iOS 18 or later.

- For Apple Vision Pro, Pages and Numbers require visionOS 2 or later, and Keynote requires visionOS 2.4 or later.

- Some premium features require macOS 26, iPadOS 26, iOS 26, or visionOS 26 or later.

- Freeform:

- Freeform for Mac, iPad, iPhone, and Apple Vision Pro requires macOS 26.4, iPadOS 26.4, iOS 26.4, and visionOS 26.4 or later.

- Motion requires macOS 15.6 or later.

- Compressor requires macOS 15.6 or later. Some features require a Mac with Apple silicon.

- MainStage requires macOS 15.6 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon.

## Intelligence features

The following intelligence features are available exclusively to Apple Creator Studio subscribers:

- Image Generation 1 in Keynote, Pages, Numbers, Pixelmator Pro, and Freeform

- Shape Generation 1 in Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Pixelmator Pro

- Super Resolution and Auto Crop in Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Freeform

- Generate Presentation 2,3 , Generate Presenter Notes 1,3 , and Slide Clean Up 3 in Keynote

- Magic Fill in Numbers

### Intelligence features system requirements

The Generate Image, Generate Shape, Generate Presenter Notes, and Generate Presentation features require specific devices, iOS and macOS versions, and an Apple Account with iCloud enabled:

- An iPhone 15 Pro or an iPhone 15 Pro Max or later running iOS 26 or later.

- An iPad with an A17 Pro chip, or an iPad Pro or iPad Air with an M1 chip or later, running iPadOS 26 or later.

- A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 26 or later.

- An Apple Account [with iCloud set up and active](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/set-up-icloud-on-your-devices-mmfc0f1e2a/icloud) . Managed account configurations may affect the availability of intelligence features.

Some intelligence features are subject to age restrictions.

#### Turn on Apple Intelligence

[Enable Apple Intelligence on your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115) to use the Image and Shape Generation features with these apps and devices:

- Pixelmator Pro on Mac and iPad.

- Pages, Numbers, and Freeform on Apple Vision Pro.

### Usage limits of intelligence features

The Generate Image, Generate Shape, Generate Presenter Notes, and Generate Presentation features are subject to limitations:

- The Generate Presentation feature is powered by OpenAI and is available only in regions that [OpenAI supports](https://help.openai.com/articles/5347006-openai-api-supported-countries-and-territories) and in regions where [Apple Creator Studio is available](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .

- The Generate Image, Generate Shape, and Generate Presenter Notes features are powered by Google Cloud and are available in certain regions 1 where [Apple Creator Studio is available](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .

- Availability may be further limited by network restrictions or blocked by local restrictions, network providers, or government regulations.

- The exact number of images, slides, and presenter notes that you can generate varies based on the complexity of the queries, server availability, and network availability. At a minimum per month, if used exclusively, you can:

- Generate 50 images

- Generate 250 shapes

- Generate 50 presentations (with approximately 8–10 slides each)

- Generate presenter notes for 700 slides

- Usage limits reset each month.

- To view your usage status:

- On Mac, depending on the app you're using, choose Keynote, Pages, Numbers, or Freeform from the menu bar, choose Intelligence Features, then choose Show Usage Status.

- On iPhone or iPad, open Keynote, Pages, Numbers, or Freeform, tap the More button , tap Intelligence Features, then tap Show Usage Status.

- These features may lead to unexpected results. To report a concern with the results:

- For a generated presentation, click or tap "Report a concern" in the alert that appears when slides have finished generating.

- For generated presenter notes, click or tap the Report a concern button in the presenter notes after the notes have been generated.

- For a generated image or a generated shape, click or tap the More button on the created image, then click or tap "Report a concern" in the menu.

- These features do not integrate with personal ChatGPT and Google accounts.

### Intelligence features and privacy

The content that you send as part of these features will never be used to train intelligence models.
