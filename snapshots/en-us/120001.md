# Use the Finder, Apple Devices, or iTunes to transfer data from your previous iOS device to your new iPhone or iPad

Transfer your data and purchased content to your new device from a backup that you made in Apple Devices, the Finder, or iTunes.

If your [backup isn't encrypted](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108353) , your Health and Activity data from your iOS device or Apple Watch won't transfer.

## Get started

If you already set up your new device, you need to erase it before you can transfer your backup. Erasing your device removes all of its data. After you erase it, your device restarts and the "Hello" screen appears.

To erase your device running iOS or iPadOS 15 or later:

- Tap Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap Transfer or Reset [Device].

- Tap Erase All Content and Settings.

To erase your device running iOS or iPadOS 14 or earlier:

- Tap Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap Reset.

- Tap Erase All Content and Settings.

## Transfer your backup to your new device

- Turn on your new device. A "Hello" screen should appear.

- Follow the steps until you see the Apps & Data screen, then tap Restore from Mac or PC.

[Image: An iPhone showing the Transfer Your Apps & Data screen with "From Mac or PC" selected.]

- Connect your new device to the computer that you used to back up your previous device.

- Open an Apple Devices, iTunes, or Finder window on your computer and select your device. [The system might prompt you to trust your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109054) .

- Select Restore Backup.

- Choose a backup. Make sure the backup is the correct one by looking at the date and size of each.

- If you need to restore from an encrypted backup, enter your password when asked.

- Wait for the restore process to finish, then complete the rest of the setup steps on your iOS or iPadOS device. If possible, keep your device connected to Wi-Fi and plugged into power after setup. This allows content like your photos, music, and apps stored in iCloud to automatically download back to your device.

## Complete the transfer process

After you transfer your data and purchased content to your new iOS or iPadOS device, [you might need to complete a few more steps to finish the process](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102156) .
