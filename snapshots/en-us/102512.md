# Update your Apple Card account information

In the Wallet app, you can view or edit your Apple Card billing address and other personal information.

## Update your billing address

- Open the Wallet app on your iPhone and tap Apple Card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details.

- Scroll to Billing Address, then tap your address.

- Tap Update Billing Address, enter your new information, then tap the Done button .

## Update your annual income

- Open the Wallet app on your iPhone and tap Apple Card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details.

- Scroll down and tap Annual Income, use Face ID or Touch ID to authenticate, then tap Update Income.

- Tap the current annual income and enter your new annual income, then tap Confirm.

## Update your personal information

- If your name or phone number changes, [call or chat with an Apple Card Specialist](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase&PGF=PGF90002&category_id=SC0305&symptom_id=23386) at Goldman Sachs to update your account information.

- If you no longer use the email address that's associated with your Apple Account, you can also [update your email address](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109353) .

- If you want to update your legal name or select a chosen first name to be displayed on your Apple Card, call or chat with an Apple Card Specialist at Goldman Sachs. Once updated, you can [request a new Titanium Card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102517) in the Wallet app.

## Update your Apple Card information that's stored in Safari AutoFill

- Open the Settings app on your iPhone.

- Scroll down and tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap Apple Pay Defaults.

- Under Transaction Defaults, you can update your shipping address, email, and phone number.

If you [set up iCloud Keychain](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109016) , your card information is updated for all of your devices where you use Safari.

## If you believe your Apple Card was compromised

If your Apple Card account may have been compromised, you can [lock your Apple Card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102161) , [request a new virtual card number](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118544) , or [close your Apple Card account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119925) .

You can also [call or chat with an Apple Card Specialist](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase&PGF=PGF90002&category_id=SC0305&symptom_id=23386) at Goldman Sachs.
