# Connect to Wi-Fi on your iPhone or iPad

Connect your device to available Wi-Fi networks, including public, secure, and previously used networks.

## Connect to a Wi-Fi network

- From your Home screen, go to Settings, then tap Wi-Fi.

- Tap to turn on Wi-Fi. Your device will automatically search for available Wi-Fi networks.

- Tap the name of the Wi-Fi network that you want to join. You might be asked to enter the network's password first, or [agree to terms and conditions](https://support.apple.com/102554) . If you [don't know the password to the Wi-Fi network](https://support.apple.com/102511) , contact your network administrator.

- Look for the blue checkmark next to the network name, and the Wi-Fi icon in the upper corner of your screen. This means you're successfully connected.
