# Check your Apple Account balance

To see your Apple Account balance on an Apple device, use the App Store. Or, if you can't spend the last of your balance to make changes to your account, learn what to do.

[If you can’t spend the last of your balance to make changes to your account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119902#zerobalance)

## Check your Apple Account balance on your device

You can use your device to see your balance.

### Check your balance on your iPhone

- In the App Store, tap your photo, initials, or the My Account button . You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- If you have a balance, the amount appears below your name. If you don’t see an amount, you don’t have a balance.

[Image: The Account screen in the App Store on iOS, with the account balance displayed.]

### Check your balance in Wallet

You might be able to check your account balance in the Wallet app on iPhone in some countries or regions. Learn more about [checking your balance in Wallet on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/apple-account-iph5f869c0af/ios) .

### Check your balance on your iPad

- In the App Store, tap your photo, initials, or the My Account button . You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- If you have a balance, the amount appears below your name. If you don’t see an amount, you don’t have a balance.

### Check your balance on your Apple Vision Pro

- In the App Store, tap your photo, initials, or the My Account button . You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- If you have a balance, the amount appears below your name. If you don’t see an amount, you don’t have a balance.

### Check your balance on your Mac

- Open the App Store. If you see the Sign In Button at the bottom of the sidebar, click it and sign in to your Apple Account.

- If you have a balance, the amount appears below your name. If you don’t see an amount, you don’t have a balance.

[Image: In the App Store on macOS, the account balance is displayed at the bottom of the sidebar.]

### Check your balance on your Windows PC

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- Click your name or Sign In at the bottom of the sidebar. You might be asked to sign in to your Apple Account.

- If you have a balance, the amount appears below your name. If you don’t see an amount, you don’t have a balance.

[Image: The Account menu in Apple Music for Windows, with the account balance displayed.]

## If your Apple Account balance seems incorrect

Sign out of the App Store, Apple Music app, or Apple TV app. Then sign in and check the balance again.

If you still think that the balance is incorrect, [check your purchase history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212) to see whether you bought an item or a subscription renewed.

If you have an unredeemed Apple Gift Card or App Store Card, you can [check the balance of your gift card](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108111) .

## If you can’t spend the last of your Apple Account balance to make changes to your account

Some changes to your Apple Account require you to have no remaining Apple Account balance. To clear your balance:

- Wait for any pending refunds to process.

- Spend your remaining balance on items in the App Store or other Apple services.

- If your remaining balance is less than the cost of a single item, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .

#### If cash refunds are required by law

You can’t redeem or return Apple Gift Cards, App Store Gift Cards, or unused Apple Account balance for cash, except as required by law. If your jurisdiction allows for refund of redeemed gift balances, you can contact Apple Support to request a refund. When you contact Apple, include the primary email address that you use to sign in to your Apple Account.
