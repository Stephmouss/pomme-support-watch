# Download games with the Apple Games app

Use the Apple Games app to find and download games on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Before you begin

Make sure that you upgrade your device to the latest version of [iOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , [iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , or [macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) .

## How to get games

- Open the Apple Games app. If prompted, [sign in to your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) and [your Game Center account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105022) .

- Browse through the Home or [Arcade](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102206) tabs to find games you like. Or tap or click the Search tab to look for something specific.

- Tap or click the game you want, then tap or click the price or Get button to download it. If you see the Play button instead of a price or Get button, you've already bought or downloaded that game.

## How to find games that you bought on iPhone or iPad

Do one of the following:

- Open the Apple Games app and tap the Library tab. Games you've already purchased will be listed under Your Games.

- [Swipe down from the middle of the Home Screen to use Spotlight Search](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118232) and enter the app you're looking for.

- From your Home Screen, [swipe left until you see the App Library](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108324) .

## How to find games that you bought on Mac

Do one of the following:

- Open the Apple Games app and click the Library tab. Games you've already purchased will be listed under Your Games.

- Click the Spotlight icon in the menu bar and type what you’re looking for—results appear as you type.

## If you have an issue when you download games

- If you can't find a game that you've purchased on your device, [redownload the game](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123712) .

- If you can't download or update apps on your iPhone or iPad, [try these tips](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102632) .

- If you can't download or update apps on your Mac, [try these tips](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102331) .
