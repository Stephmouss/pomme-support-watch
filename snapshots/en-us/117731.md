# Attach and pair your ZEISS Optical Inserts

After you purchase a pair of ZEISS Optical Inserts, you need to attach them to your Apple Vision Pro and then pair them.

[Attach and pair your ZEISS Optical Inserts](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117731#attach)

[Pair or unpair your ZEISS Optical Inserts in Settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117731#pair)

[Find your pairing code or serial number](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117731#pairing_code)

[If you can't select your ZEISS Optical Inserts](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117731#troubleshoot)

## Attach and pair your ZEISS Optical Inserts

- Place your Apple Vision Pro inside its cover and set it on a level surface.

- Hold one ZEISS Optical Insert by its edges to avoid fingerprints. Each ZEISS Optical Insert is laser marked with an “L” or an “R” to indicate left or right. The white line on the top of each ZEISS Optical Insert indicates the top. Position the ZEISS Optical Insert with its “L” or “R” indicator pointing inwards, towards the nose bridge.

- Place the ZEISS Optical Insert over the display. It snaps into place magnetically when connected. Repeat for the second ZEISS Optical Insert. You need to attach both ZEISS Optical Inserts, even if you only need vision correction for one eye.

- Put your Vision Pro on, making sure to handle it by the frame, and follow the onscreen instructions to adjust the display alignment.

- As you begin eye setup, you're prompted to pair your ZEISS Optical Inserts. If you don't see the prompt, quadruple-click the top button, turn the Digital Crown to select Pair New Optical Inserts, and press the Digital Crown to begin.

- Look at the pairing code provided in your ZEISS Optical Inserts packaging, or in the Health app. [Can't find your pairing code](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117731#pairing_code) ? If your pairing code won't scan or doesn't work:

- Try increasing the lighting in the room you're in.

- Register your ZEISS Optical Inserts at [https://myzeissvision.zeiss.com/](https://myzeissvision.zeiss.com/) and try the code generated for you there.

## Pair or unpair your ZEISS Optical Inserts in Settings

Your Apple Vision Pro automatically prompts you to complete pairing after you attach your ZEISS Optical Inserts. You can also set up or pair your ZEISS Optical Inserts anytime in Settings. Make sure that you have the pairing code that came with your ZEISS Optical Inserts. [Can't find your pairing code](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117731#pairing_code) ?

If you have an iPhone or iPad associated with the same Apple Account as your Apple Vision Pro, a health record will automatically be added to the Health app, including your prescription, serial number, and pairing code of your ZEISS Optical Inserts. This gives you easy access to the pairing code directly from your iPhone or iPad.

### Pair your ZEISS Optical Inserts in Settings

- Attach your ZEISS Optical Inserts if they aren't already attached.

- Put on your device, and make sure that Eye and Hand Tracking are set up.

- Quadruple-click the top button, then use the Digital Crown to choose Pair New Optical Inserts.

- Or go to Settings, then tap Eyes & Hands. Under Displays & Optical Inserts, select Set Up New Optical Inserts.

- Complete pairing by scanning the pairing code that you received with the packaging. [Can't find your pairing code](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117731#pairing_code) ? If your pairing code won't scan or doesn't work:

- Try increasing the lighting in the room you're in.

- Register your ZEISS Optical Inserts at [https://myzeissvision.zeiss.com/](https://myzeissvision.zeiss.com/) and try the code generated for you there.

You can also use these steps to pair another set of ZEISS Optical Inserts with your Apple Vision Pro. Before you swap between different pairs of ZEISS Optical Inserts, if prompted, enter your passcode, then use the Digital Crown to select the correct set of ZEISS Optical Inserts.

### Remove a pairing

If you no longer need to use a particular pair of ZEISS Optical Inserts and want to remove them from your device:

- Go to Settings, then tap Eyes & Hands.

- Under Displays & Optical Inserts, select your ZEISS Optical Inserts.

- Select Forget These Optical Inserts.

## Find your pairing code or serial number

Your ZEISS Optical Inserts come with a pairing code that you need for set up. It's located in the box on a separate card. Make sure to keep this code. This code will help if you ever need support for your ZEISS Optical Inserts. If you can't find your pairing code:

- In the Health app, go to Browse, tap Body Measurements, then tap Vision Prescription.

- Check the packaging of your ZEISS Optical Inserts.

- You can also go to [https://myzeissvision.zeiss.com/](https://myzeissvision.zeiss.com/) to register your ZEISS Optical Inserts using their serial number. After you register your ZEISS Optical Inserts, you can look them up and find your pairing code.

The serial number is printed on the back of the frame of each ZEISS Optical Insert. Or if you added your prescription to the Health app, you can also find your serial number in the Vision Prescription section.

## If you can't select your ZEISS Optical Inserts

- If your ZEISS Optical Inserts or your optical module are dirty or damaged, you might not be able to enroll your ZEISS Optical Inserts or select them for use on your Vision Pro.

- Make sure both ZEISS Optical Inserts are properly attached to your Vision Pro.

- If you have multiple pairs of ZEISS Optical Inserts, make sure the pair that is attached is a matching pair.

- Make sure that you are not using any third-party accessories such as lens protectors.
