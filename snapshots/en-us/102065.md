# Vote Helpful Content in the Apple Support Community

Let other members in the Apple Support Community know if their reply or User Tip was helpful with an upvote. You can also select “Me too” if you have a similar question.

## Why should I vote?

Voting may help prioritize and surface helpful, high-quality content in [the Apple Support Community](https://discussions.apple.com/welcome) . In addition, when you upvote a helpful reply or User Tip, you add value not only to the content, but also to the community member who provided the helpful contribution, which helps to build their reputation in the community.

## Types of votes

### Upvote helpful content

Upvote helpful content to let the contributor know if their content — such as a reply to a question or a User Tip — helped. Reasons you should upvote helpful content:

- Helps to highlight and elevate valuable content for greater visibly

- Gives power to the community to decide what is helpful

- Rewards high-quality contributions and helps to build a member’s reputation in the community

### Me too

If you have the same issue, choose “Me too.” This creates traction on the question and lets the author know that they're not the only one experiencing the issue.
