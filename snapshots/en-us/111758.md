# Leave iCloud Shared Photo Library

If you created an iCloud Shared Photo Library, learn how to remove a participant or delete your Shared Library. If you're a participant, learn how to leave a Shared Library.

- [Leave a Shared Library](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111758#leave)

- [Remove a participant from your Shared Library](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111758#remove)

- [Delete your Shared Library](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111758#delete)

## Leave a Shared Library

If you're a participant, you can leave a Shared Library at any time. Just make sure that you stay signed in to iCloud with iCloud Photos turned on until the process is complete.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Go to Settings, tap Apps, then tap Photos. In iOS 17 or earlier, go to Settings, then tap Photos.

- Tap Shared Library.

- Choose Leave Shared Library.

[Image: An iPhone with iOS 26 or later showing the Shared Library menu. Leave Shared Library is located at the bottom of the screen in red text.]

- Choose to copy everything from the Shared Library into your Personal Library, or just the content that you contributed.

- Tap Leave Shared Library again to confirm.

### Mac

- In the Photos app, choose Settings from the Photos menu.

- Select the Shared Library tab.

[Image: A Mac with macOS 26 or later showing the Shared Library menu. Leave Shared Library is located at the bottom of the window.]

- Click Leave Shared Library.

- Choose to copy everything from the Shared Library into your Personal Library, or just the content that you contributed.

- Click Leave Shared Library again to confirm.

## Remove a participant

If you’re the library creator, you can remove participants from the Shared Library at any time.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Go to Settings, tap Apps, then tap Photos. In iOS 17 or earlier, go to Settings, then tap Photos.

- Tap Shared Library.

- Select a participant, then choose Remove from Shared Library.

[Image: An iPhone with iOS 26 or later showing a participant named Ashley Rico in the Shared Library menu. Remove from Shared Library is shown at the bottom of the screen.]

- Tap Remove from Shared Library again to confirm. The participant receives a notification that they've been removed from the Shared Library:

- If the participant has been in the Shared Library for more than seven days, all of the shared assets automatically copy to their Personal Library.

- If they've been in the Shared Library for less than seven days, only the photos and videos that they contributed copy to their Personal Library.

### On your Mac

- In the Photos app, choose Settings from the Photos menu.

- Select the Shared Library tab.

- Click the More button next to a participant's name, then choose Remove.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

[Image: A Mac with macOS 26 or later showing the Shared Library menu. Ashley Rico is selected. The option “Remove” is located below Show Contact Card.]

- Tap Remove from Shared Library again to confirm. The participant receives a notification that they've been removed from the Shared Library:

- If the participant has been in the Shared Library for more than seven days, all of the shared assets automatically copy to their Personal Library.

- If they've been in the Shared Library for less than seven days, only the photos and videos that they contributed copy to their Personal Library.

## Delete your Shared Library

If you’re the library creator, you can delete your Shared Library at any time.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Go to Settings, tap Apps, then tap Photos. In iOS 17 or earlier, go to Settings, then tap Photos.

- Tap Shared Library.

- Choose Delete Shared Library.

[Image: An iPhone with iOS 26 or later showing the Shared Library menu. Delete Shared Library is located at the bottom of the screen in red text.]

- Choose Keep Everything or Keep Only What I Contributed, then tap Delete Shared Library to confirm. All participants receive a notification:

- If the participant has been in the Shared Library for more than seven days, all of the shared assets automatically copy to their Personal Library.

- If they've been in the Shared Library for less than seven days, only the photos and videos that they contributed copy to their Personal Library.

### On your Mac

- In the Photos app, choose Settings from the Photos menu.

- Select the Shared Library tab.

[Image: A Mac with macOS 26 or later showing the Shared Library menu. Delete Shared Library is located at the bottom of the window.]

- Click Delete Shared Library.

- Choose Keep Everything or Keep Only What I Contributed, then click Delete Shared Library to confirm. All participants receive a notification:

- If the participant has been in the Shared Library for more than seven days, all of the shared assets automatically copy to their Personal Library.

- If they've been in the Shared Library for less than seven days, only the photos and videos that they contributed copy to their Personal Library.
