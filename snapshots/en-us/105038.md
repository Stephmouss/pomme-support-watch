# Feature availability and data sources in the Weather app

Learn more about regional availability and data sources for features in the Weather app.

The Weather app includes a number of features that provide information about current and forecasted weather. Some features aren't currently available in every country or region.

## 10-day forecast

A 10-day weather forecast is available for most countries and regions. Unless otherwise specified, the 10-day forecast data is provided by Apple Weather.

## Severe weather information

Severe weather information is available from national weather services for Australia, Brazil, Canada, Hong Kong, India, Japan, Mexico, New Zealand, Thailand, the United States, and most countries and regions in Europe.

Severe weather information is available from QWeather for China mainland.

## Next-hour precipitation

Next-hour precipitation forecasts and precipitation notifications are available for Australia, Ireland, Japan, the United Kingdom, and the United States. The information is provided by Apple using data from national weather services.

## Air quality

Air quality information is provided by BreezoMeter for Austria, Belgium, Canada, Czechia, Denmark, France, Germany, India, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Mexico, Netherlands, Singapore, South Korea, Spain, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Air quality information for China mainland is provided by QWeather and reflects data from air monitoring stations. If you use iOS 14.1 or earlier, you’ll see Air Quality information provided by The Weather Channel.

## Maps

Temperature maps, wind maps, and 12-hour precipitation maps are available for most countries and regions. Next-hour precipitation maps are available for Australia, Ireland, Japan, the United Kingdom, and the United States.

Temperature and precipitation map data is provided by Apple Weather. If you use iOS 15.2 or earlier, you might see some temperature and precipitation data that's provided by The Weather Channel.

Unless otherwise specified, temperature, precipitation, and wind map data is provided by Apple Weather. Data for air quality maps is provided by BreezoMeter and QWeather.
