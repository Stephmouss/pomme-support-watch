# If your iPhone freezes on the Apple logo

When restoring or migrating your iPhone with iOS 13 or later, the Apple logo and progress bar might appear for over an hour. Here's what to do.

- Make sure that the progress bar on your iPhone screen hasn't moved for at least one hour.

- If the progress bar hasn't moved for more than one hour, connect your device to a computer. Then put it into recovery mode:

- iPhone 8 and later:

- Press and release the Volume Up button.

- Press and release the Volume Down button.

- Press and hold the Side button. Keep holding the Side button until you see the recovery mode screen.

- iPhone 7 and iPhone 7 Plus:

- Press and hold both the Volume Down button and the Sleep/Wake button.

- Keep holding the Volume Down button until you see the recovery mode screen.

- iPhone 6s and earlier:

- Press and hold both the Sleep/Wake button and the Home button.

- Keep holding the Home button until you see the recovery mode screen.

- On a Mac with macOS Catalina 10.15 or later, open the Finder. On a Mac with macOS Mojave 10.14 or earlier, open iTunes. If you're using a PC, use the latest version of the [Apple Devices](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) app or [iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290) . When asked to update or restore, [choose Update](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) . This will update your device to the latest version of iOS. If you choose Restore, it will erase your iPhone.
