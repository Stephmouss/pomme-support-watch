# How to download iPadOS 27

Learn how to download iPadOS 27.

[Image: iPadOS 27 icon, featuring the number 27 in a gradient of beige and brown.]

Before you update, [back up your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118426) to keep a copy of your important information.

## Check compatibility

You'll see only updates compatible with your iPad. If your iPad is running an earlier version, [install that update first](https://support.apple.com/en-us/148850#update-manually) .

iPadOS 27 is compatible with iPad models:

- iPad Pro (4th generation and later, M1 and later)

- iPad Air (4th generation and later, M2 and later)

- iPad (9th generation and later, A16)

- iPad mini (6th generation, A17 Pro)

[Learn what's new in iPadOS 27.](https://www.apple.com/os/ipados/)

## Turn on automatic updates

You can set up your iPad to install updates automatically:

- Open Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap Software Update.

- Tap Automatic Updates.

- Choose Automatically Install or Automatically Download.

- Automatically Install: When an update is available, your iPad downloads and installs the update overnight while charging and connected to Wi-Fi. Your iPad notifies you before it installs the update.

- Automatically Download: Your iPad downloads updates over Wi-Fi while it's charging and locked. You can install them at your convenience. To see this option, first turn off Automatically Install.

## Update iPad manually

At any time, you can check for and install software updates.

- Open Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap Software Update.

- If an update is available, tap Download and Install.

- Follow the onscreen instructions.

## Install the software update

If an update is already downloaded but not installed:

- Open Settings.

- Tap Update to iPadOS 27.

- Tap Update Now or Update Tonight.

- Follow the onscreen instructions.

## View update information

To see the current version of iPadOS installed on your device (such as iPadOS 27):

- Open Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap About.
