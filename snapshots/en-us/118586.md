# Redeem the Pro Apps Bundle for Education

When you buy the Pro Apps Bundle for Education, redeem the five content redemption codes that Apple sends to you in emails, then download your apps.

The Pro Apps Bundle for Education includes the one-time purchase versions of Final Cut Pro, Motion, Compressor, Logic Pro, and MainStage. You can buy the bundle preinstalled when you buy a Mac from the [Apple Store for Education](https://www.apple.com/us-edu/store) or from the [Apple Store for Education Institution](https://ecommerce.apple.com/asb2bstorefront/fys?language=EN&country=US) .

Each app has its own redemption code, which you can find in the emails that Apple sends you after your purchase. It's important to redeem all five redemption codes. Redeeming all codes entitles you to future updates and allows you to install your apps on your other Mac computers that use the same Apple Account.

If you purchased the Pro Apps Bundle for Education pre-installed on a Mac, you won’t receive redemption codes. Instead, you will have to [adopt the apps.](https://support.apple.com/101869)

## Find the emails from Apple with the redemption codes

In one to three business days after purchasing the bundle, you'll receive two emails from Apple about the bundle:

- The first email contains a password that you use to open a PDF, which is attached to the second email.

- The PDF attached to the second email contains five content redemption codes — one for each app in the bundle. The PDF also has the license agreement for each app.

To find the emails, search your email inbox for the subject "Your Apple License Agreement."

## Redeem content codes in the App Store on your Mac

Once you've located the email with the codes, redeem your codes. After you successfully redeem a code for an app in the bundle, the latest version of each app automatically downloads from the Mac App Store.

- On your Mac, [go directly to the code redemption page of the App Store](https://www.apple.com/redeem) . Or you can:

- Open the App Store.

- Click your name or the sign-in button in the sidebar.

- Click Redeem Gift Card.

- If prompted, sign in to your Apple Account. Make sure to sign in to the Mac App Store using the Apple Account that you want to associate with the apps.

- Enter one of the 12-digit codes from the list of content redemption codes provided in the PDF you received in the email from Apple, then click Redeem. You can’t use the camera on your Mac to redeem these content redemption codes. You have to enter the codes manually.

- If prompted, sign in to your Apple Account again.

- While the app you just redeemed downloads to your Mac, click Redeem Another Code, then enter the next code.

- Continue until you've redeemed the codes for all the apps in the bundle.

Your Pro Apps are ready to use after you've downloaded them to your Mac. Make sure that you redeem all five codes so all the apps in the bundle are associated with your Apple Account. That way, you can update the apps in the future and install them on your other Mac computers.

## If you haven’t received the emails with the content redemption codes

If you haven’t received the content redemption codes for your order in one to three business days from when you placed the order, contact the Apple Online Store:

- To contact the Apple Online Store in the United States, call 1-800-MY–APPLE (1-800–692–7753).

- For other countries or regions, [go to the Contacting Apple webpage](https://www.apple.com/contact/) to find the number for your Apple Store.

You’ll need your order number, which begins with the letter W and is located at the top of the Order Acknowledgement email. That email includes ”We’re processing your order” in the email subject line in case you need to search for it.

## If you can't redeem a content code

- Verify that your Mac meets the minimum system requirement for the app whose code you want to redeem:

- [Final Cut Pro, Motion and Compressor](https://www.apple.com/final-cut-pro/specs/)

- [Logic Pro](https://www.apple.com/logic-pro/specs/)

- [MainStage](https://www.apple.com/mainstage/)

- Choose Restart from the Apple menu  to restart the Mac.

- Sign out, then sign back in to the App Store on your Mac:

- To sign out, choose Store from the App Store menu bar, then choose Sign Out.

- To sign back in, click Sign In at the bottom-left corner of the App Store window, then sign in to your Apple Account. Use the account that you want to associate with the app.

- Try to redeem the code again. When you enter the code, make sure to enter the correct letters and numbers. Some letters and numbers can look alike.

## If you need to install or reinstall the apps that you already redeemed

- In the App Store on your Mac, click your name in the bottom-left corner, or click Sign In if you’re not already. Sign in to the Apple Account you used to redeem the bundled apps.

- Locate the purchased app you want to install or reinstall, then click the Download button .
