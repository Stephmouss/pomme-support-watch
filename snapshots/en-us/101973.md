# If you can't sign out due to restrictions, or your Apple Account is grayed out in Settings

If you're unable to sign out of your Apple Account, or your Apple Account is grayed out in Settings on your iPhone or iPad, check if you have a Screen Time passcode and/or Screen Time restrictions enabled.

- [If you can't sign out due to restrictions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101973#restrictions)

- [If your Apple Account is grayed out in the Settings app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101973#account-gray)

- [If you're a child in a Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101973#child)

## If you can't sign out due to restrictions

If you can't sign out of your Apple Account on your iPhone or iPad because you see "Sign out is not available due to restrictions", you might have a Screen Time passcode enabled. Check for a Screen Time passcode, then turn it off to sign out of your Apple Account.

[Image: iPhone, MacBook Pro, and Apple Watch listed in settings, with a disabled Sign Out button.]

### Turn off your Screen Time passcode

- Open the Settings app, then tap Screen Time.

- Scroll down, then tap Manage Screen Time.

- Tap Change Screen Time Passcode.

- Tap Turn Off Screen Time Passcode, then enter your passcode to turn it off.

[Learn how to change your Screen Time passcode](https://support.apple.com/102677)

## If your Apple Account is grayed out in the Settings app

If your Apple Account is grayed out in the Settings app, you might have certain Screen Time restrictions enabled for your device.

[Image: iPhone Settings screen showing Apple ID, Airplane Mode, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Cellular options.]

### Check your Screen Time settings

- Open the Settings app, then tap Screen Time.

- Tap Content & Privacy Restrictions.

- If All Restrictions is turned on, scroll down to Privacy Restrictions.

- Tap Allow Changes to Settings.

[Image: iPhone screen showing Allow Changes to Settings with Accounts set to Don't Allow.]

- Tap Accounts, and set it to Allow. If asked, enter your Screen Time passcode.

- Go back to the Allow Changes to Settings screen, and check if any other settings are set to Don't Allow. If so, tap the setting, then choose Allow. If asked, enter your Screen Time passcode.

## If you're a child in a Family Sharing group

If you're a child in a Family Sharing group and your parent or guardian [set up parental controls to manage your device](https://support.apple.com/105121) , you need to ask them to enter the Screen Time passcode to allow changes to your device settings. Changes to a child's Screen Time passcode must be made on the family organizer or parent/guardian's device.
