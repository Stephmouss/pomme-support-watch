# About WPT-compliant Apple Watch charging in China mainland and Indonesia

Learn about WPT-compliant Apple Watch models and chargers sold by Apple in China mainland and Indonesia.

## WPT compliance

Wireless power transfer (WPT) regulations limit the frequencies that devices, including Apple Watch, can use to charge their batteries. In countries with WPT regulation, Apple Watch is sold with a WPT-compliant charging cable when required. WPT-compliant chargers do not support fast charging.

## Supported Apple Watch models

These models of Apple Watch sold in China mainland are WPT-compliant:

- Apple Watch Series 10 and later

- Apple Watch Ultra 3

- Apple Watch SE 3

- Apple Watch Ultra 2, Model 型号 MXXXX/CH/B

- Apple Watch SE 2, Model 型号 MXXXX/CH/B

Apple Watch Series 10 sold in Indonesia is also WPT-compliant.

Included in the box is a WPT-compliant Apple Watch Magnetic Charger to USB-C Cable (1 m). This charger does not work with models of Apple Watch other than those listed above. If you try to charge an incompatible Apple Watch with your charger, you receive a message: "Unable to charge. This is a country-specific charger. It is incompatible with this Apple Watch."

Learn how to [identify your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/108056) .
