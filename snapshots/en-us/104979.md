# Use Adaptive Audio with your AirPods

Features for AirPods Max 2, AirPods Pro 2, AirPods Pro 3, AirPods 4 (ANC), and AirPods 5 (both models) such as Adaptive Audio, Personalized Volume, and Conversation Awareness respond to your environment automatically to optimize your audio experience.

## Turn on Adaptive Audio features

You can turn on Adaptive Audio features on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. The settings carry over to your other devices when your AirPods switch devices.

### Turn on Adaptive Audio features on iPhone or iPad

- While you're wearing your AirPods and they're connected to your device, open Control Center.

- Touch and hold the volume control, then choose the features you want to turn on.

- To enable [Adaptive mode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104979#anc) , tap Listening Mode, then choose Adaptive.

- To turn on [Conversation Awareness](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104979#conversation) , tap Conversation Awareness.

- Go to the Settings app, then tap Bluetooth. Tap the More Info button next to your connected AirPods, then turn on Personalized Volume. If Personalized Volume isn’t displayed, first tap Audio & Routing.

### Turn on Adaptive Audio features on Mac

- While you're wearing your AirPods and they're connected to your device, open Control Center.

- Click Sound, then click your AirPods.

- Choose Adaptive to enable [Adaptive mode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104979#anc) .

- To turn on [Conversation Awareness](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104979#conversation) , click Conversation Awareness On.

### Use Siri to turn on Adaptive Audio features

[Get Siri's attention](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105020) , then say something like "Turn on Personalized Volume" or "Turn on Adaptive."

## About Adaptive mode

Adaptive mode blends Active Noise Cancellation and Transparency mode together to control the level of noise you hear in your headphones based on the changing noise conditions in your environment. You can customize Adaptive Audio to allow more or less noise.

### Customize Adaptive Audio on iPhone or iPad

- While you're wearing your AirPods and Adaptive Audio is turned on, go to the Settings app.

- Tap the name of your AirPods.

- Below Listening Mode, tap Adaptive.

- Move the slider to the left or right to adjust the amount of ambient noise allowed into your AirPods when in Adaptive mode.

You can also customize Adaptive Audio in Control Center on your iPhone or iPad:

- While you're wearing your AirPods and they're connected to your iPhone or iPad, open Control Center.

- Touch and hold the volume control.

- Tap Listening Mode, then tap Adaptive.

- Move the slider to the left or right to adjust the amount of ambient noise allowed into your AirPods when in Adaptive mode.

### Customize Adaptive Audio on Mac

- While you're wearing your AirPods and Adaptive Audio is turned on, choose Apple menu , then choose System Settings.

- Click the name of your AirPods in the sidebar.

- Below Listening Mode, click Adaptive.

- Move the slider to the left or right to adjust the amount of ambient noise allowed into your AirPods when in Adaptive mode.

You can also customize Adaptive Audio in Control Center on your Mac:

- While you're wearing your AirPods and they're connected to your Mac, open Control Center.

- Click Sound, click the name of your AirPods, then click Adaptive.

- Under Adaptive, move the slider to the left or right to adjust the amount of ambient noise allowed into your AirPods when in Adaptive mode.

## About Personalized Volume

Personalized Volume adjusts the media volume on your AirPods based on environmental conditions and your volume preferences. It learns your listening preferences over time to fine-tune your media volume in response to your surrounding environment.

## About Conversation Awareness

When you start talking while you're wearing your AirPods, Conversation Awareness seamlessly lowers your media volume and enhances voices in front of you. When your conversation ends, Conversation Awareness automatically turns up the volume and brings you back to your original Listening mode. You can also end a conversation manually by pressing and holding on the AirPods stem.
