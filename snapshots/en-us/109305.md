# Apple Cash transfer limits

Here are the limits associated with using Apple Cash and person-to-person payments.

## Apple Cash balance limit

The maximum Apple Cash 1 balance you're allowed to have after verifying your identity is $20,000. 2

If you're part of Apple Cash Family, the maximum Apple Cash balance you're allowed to have as a family member is $4,000.

## Limits for adding money to your Apple Cash balance from a debit card or prepaid card

You can add money only from a supported United States debit card or prepaid card that you use with Apple Pay in Wallet.

- Per transaction, you can add a minimum of $10.

- Per transaction, you can add a maximum of $10,000.

- Within a 7-day period, you can add a maximum of $10,000.

You might also be limited by the available balance on your debit or prepaid card or limits from your bank or card issuer. 3

### If you're an Apple Cash Family member

- Per transaction, you can add a minimum of $10.

- Per transaction, you can add a maximum of $2,000.

- Within a 7-day period, you can add a maximum of $2,000.

## General sending and receiving limits

- Per message, you can send or receive a minimum of $1.

- Per message, you can send or receive a maximum of $10,000.

- Within a 7-day period, you can send or receive a maximum of $10,000. 4

You might also be limited by the available balance on your debit or prepaid card or limits from your bank or card issuer. 3

### If you're an Apple Cash Family member or you're using Tap to Cash

- Per message, you can send or receive a minimum of $1.

- Per message, you can send or receive a maximum of $2,000.

- Within a 7-day period, you can send or receive a maximum of $2,000.

## Limits for transferring money to your debit card or bank account

- You can transfer a minimum of $1 to your debit card or bank account, or your full balance if it's under $1.

- You can transfer a maximum of $10,000 to your debit card or bank account in a single transfer.

- Within a 7-day period, you can transfer a maximum of $20,000 to your debit card or bank account.

- If you [use Instant Transfer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120401) , 5 a 1.7% fee (with a minimum fee of $0.25 and a maximum fee of $25) is deducted from the amount of each transfer. With Instant Transfer, the transfer limit doesn't include fees.

To transfer money to your debit card, you must have an eligible debit card in the Wallet app.

### If you're an Apple Cash Family member

- You can transfer a minimum of $1 to your debit card or bank account, or your full balance if it's under $1.

- You can transfer a maximum of $2,000 to your debit card or bank account in a single transfer.

- Within a 7-day period, you can transfer a maximum of $4,000 to your debit card or bank account.

To have an eligible United States debit card or prepaid card in the Wallet app, you must be at least 13 years old. To transfer money to or from an eligible debit card and use Instant Transfer, you must also be at least 13 years old.
