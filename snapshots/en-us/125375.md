# Identify your Apple Vision Pro model

Learn how to identify your Apple Vision Pro model by its model number and other details.

## Find the model or number

To find your Apple Vision Pro model name or number, go to Settings > General > About.

## Apple Vision Pro (M5)

[Image: Apple Vision Pro with a gray knitted band and a shiny black front display.]

Model number: A3416

Year introduced: 2025

Capacity: 256GB, 512GB, 1TB
