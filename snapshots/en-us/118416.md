# Redownload books and audiobooks

To find the books and audiobooks that you previously purchased in Apple Books, follow these steps to redownload them on your device.

## How to redownload books or audiobooks on your iPhone

- Open the Books app, then tap Home at the bottom of the screen.

- Tap the Account button or your photo in the top corner of the screen.

[Image: the Account button]

- Under My Purchases, tap either Books or Audiobooks. If you use Family Sharing, you can tap a family member's name to see their purchases.

- Tap "Not on this iPhone", then tap All Books or All Audiobooks.

- Find the book or audiobook that you want to download, then tap the Download button . [Can't find the item that you purchased?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT211842)

[Image: the Download button]

## How to redownload books or audiobooks on your iPad or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Books app, then tap the Show Sidebar button .

[Image: the Show Sidebar button]

- Tap Home, then tap the Account button or your photo in the top corner of the screen.

[Image: the Account button]

- Under My Purchases, tap either Books or Audiobooks. If you use Family Sharing, you can tap a family member's name to see their purchases.

- Tap "Not on this [device]", then tap All Books or All Audiobooks.

- Find the book or audiobook that you want to download, then tap the Download button . [Can't find the item that you purchased?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT211842)

[Image: the Download button]

## How to redownload books or audiobooks on your Mac

- Open Apple Books.

- Choose Sign In or your name in the bottom corner of the screen.

- Under My Purchases, choose Books or Audiobooks. If you use Family Sharing, you can tap a family member's name to see their purchases.

- Choose "Not on this Mac" to view purchased content that isn't on your device.

- Find the book or audiobook that you want to download, then click the Download button . [Can't find the item that you purchased?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT211842)

[Image: the Download button]

## How to redownload audiobooks on iTunes for Windows

- Open iTunes.

- From the menu bar at the top of the iTunes window, choose Account > Purchased or Family Purchases if you use Family Sharing.

- Choose Audiobooks.

- Find the audiobook that you want to redownload.

- If you use Family Sharing, you can click your name next to Purchased, then choose a family member's name to see their purchases.

- Click the Download button for the audiobook. [Can't find the item that you purchased?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT211842)

[Image: the Download button]

Any digital media you buy from Apple can be permanently downloaded on to an iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Windows PC. [Learn more](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121877) .
