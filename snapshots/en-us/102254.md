# About search results in Apple Books

You can use Search to find books and audiobooks in the Apple Books app.

## How to use Search in Apple Books

To find what you're looking for in the Books app, start typing a search term in the search field. Once you start typing, you can also tap one of the search suggestions that appears.

## How does Search work in Apple Books?

Search in Apple Books uses your search terms to find the best possible matches. The most relevant search results appear first. The main factors that Apple Books uses to order your search results are:

- Metadata from book publishers

- Customer engagement with Apple Books

- The popularity of books and audiobooks in Apple Books
