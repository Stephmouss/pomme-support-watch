# Use the Finder to share files between your Mac and your iPhone, iPad, iPod touch

With macOS Catalina or later, you can use the Finder to share files between your iOS and iPadOS devices and your Mac.

If you're using macOS Mojave or earlier or a Windows PC, [use iTunes on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/HT201301) or [use Apple Devices on your Windows PC](https://support.apple.com/HT214072) .

## Explore other ways to share

Learn about ways you can share files between your Mac and your devices. These features work wirelessly.

- Use [iCloud Drive](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/set-up-icloud-drive-mm203b05aec8/icloud) to securely access all of your documents from your iPhone, iPad, iPod touch, Mac, or PC. No matter which device you're using, you'll always have the up-to-date documents when and where you need them.

- Use [Handoff](https://support.apple.com/102426) to create a document, email, or message on one device and pick up where you left off on another device. Apps that work with Handoff include Safari, Mail, Maps, Reminders, Calendar, Contacts, Pages, Numbers, Keynote, FaceTime, and many third-party apps.

- Use [AirDrop](https://support.apple.com/119857) to share your photos, videos, documents, and more with other Apple devices that are nearby.

- Use iPhone Mirroring to [copy items](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120421#copy) between devices and to [drag and drop items](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102459#inputdevices) between devices.

## Use Finder to share files

To share the files using the Finder, you need:

- A Mac model on [macOS Catalina or later](https://support.apple.com/108382)

- An iPhone, iPad, iPod touch with the [latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575)

- An iOS or iPadOS app that works with File Sharing

## See apps that share files with your Mac

- Open a Finder window.

- Connect your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch to your computer with a USB cable.

- Select your device in the Finder. [Learn what to do if your computer doesn't recognize your device](https://support.apple.com/108643) .

- Click the Files tab to see a list of apps that can share files. If you don't see a Files section, your device doesn't have any apps that can share files.

- Click the triangle next to an app to see the files that you can share.

[Image: Files tab for a connected device in the Finder.]

### Copy from your Mac to your device

- Select the files that you want to copy. Select only files that work with the app. Check the app’s user guide to see which ones work.

- Drag the files to the app on your device. The Finder copies the files to your device.

### Copy from your device to your Mac

- Select the files that you want to copy.

- Drag the files to a folder on your computer. The Finder copies the files to your computer.

## Open shared files

On your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch, use the app that you chose in the Files tab to open files that you shared from your computer.

Depending on the file type, some apps can't open files in other apps or locations. You can [see the list of files](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119585#which) in the Files tab of the Finder when you connect your device to your computer.

## Back up shared files

When iCloud or your computer [backs up your device](https://support.apple.com/118426) , it backs up your shared files as well.

## Delete shared files from your device

- Open a Finder window on your Mac.

- Connect your device to your computer using a USB cable.

- Select your device in the Finder. [Learn what to do if your computer doesn't recognize your device](https://support.apple.com/108643) .

- Select the app from the list in the Files section.

- In the Documents list, select the file that you want to delete, then press the Delete key on your keyboard.

- When the Finder asks you to confirm, click Delete.

If you delete an app with shared files from your device, you delete those shared files from your device and your iCloud or computer backup. If you want to avoid losing shared files, copy the files to your computer before deleting the app from your device.

There might be other ways to delete files from an app. See the app's user guide to find out more.
