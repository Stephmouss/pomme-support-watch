# How to request or replace a titanium Apple Card

You can request a new titanium Apple Card or a replacement card in the Wallet app.

## How to get a titanium card

If you don't have an Apple Card, [learn how to apply](https://support.apple.com/104952) .

If you have an Apple Card, you can request a titanium card.

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details .

[Image: the More button]

[Image: the card details button]

- Scroll down and tap "Get a titanium Apple Card."

- Follow the instructions on your screen.

A notification is sent to your iPhone when your titanium card ships—the card ships to the Apple Card billing address in Wallet. If you want your card delivered to a different address, [update your billing address](https://support.apple.com/102512) in Wallet before you request your titanium card.

[Image: titanium Apple Card]

## Order a titanium card for a participant under 18

If you're the account owner or co-owner 1 of an Apple Card Family 2 account, you can order a titanium card for any participants 3 on your account who are under 18.

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details .

[Image: the More button]

[Image: the card details button]

- Tap the participant's name.

- Scroll down to Titanium Card and tap "Request."

- Follow the instructions on your screen.

## How to activate your Apple Card

You need a [compatible iPhone](https://support.apple.com/102896) with the [latest iOS version](https://support.apple.com/118575) to activate your titanium Apple Card. Your card is ready to use as soon as it's activated. If you're part of a shared Apple Card account, you can activate your own titanium card.

### If you have the Apple Card packaging

- Open the packaging that contained your titanium card.

- Follow the steps in the packaging. If you need help, [contact an Apple Card Specialist at Goldman Sachs](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase&PGF=PGF90002&category_id=SC0305&symptom_id=23386) .

### If you don't have the Apple Card packaging

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details .

[Image: the More button]

[Image: the card details button]

- Under Titanium Card, tap "Activate Your Card."

- Tap "Having trouble activating your card."

- Tap "Do Not Have Card Packaging," then follow the instructions on your screen.

## How to get a replacement card

If your card is lost or stolen, you should [lock the card](https://support.apple.com/102161) before you request a replacement card. 4 Additionally, you can [contact an Apple Card Specialist at Goldman Sachs](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase&PGF=PGF90002&category_id=SC0305&symptom_id=23386) .

You can also request a replacement card if your titanium card is damaged or expired.

### On iPhone

- Open the Wallet app.

- Tap Apple Card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details .

[Image: the More button]

[Image: Card Details]

- Scroll down to Titanium Card and tap "Request Replacement Card," then follow the instructions on your screen.

### On iPad

- Open the Settings App.

- Scroll down and tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Tap Apple Card.

- Under Titanium Card, tap "Request Replacement Card," then follow the instructions on your screen.

If you're a participant under 18 in an Apple Card Family account, ask the account owner or co-owner to request a replacement card for you.

### If both your iPhone and titanium card are lost or stolen

- Call Goldman Sachs at (877) 255-5923 to request a replacement Apple Card.

- Then, [learn how to protect your information](https://support.apple.com/101593) .
