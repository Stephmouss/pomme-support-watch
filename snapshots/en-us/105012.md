# Use multiple languages to speak to Siri in India

You can ask Siri to call someone, set timers and alarms, send messages, open apps, and more using English mixed with Bangla, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Punjabi, Tamil, or Telugu.

## Get started

Siri can recognize multiple languages in India on [all of your Apple devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105020) . To get started, just [set the language that Siri uses to English (India)](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120499) . Then ask Siri something.

[Image: Settings for Siri with English (India) language selected.]

You can use a mix of languages to ask Siri to do things, but the text you tell Siri to write, such as in a message or a note, must be in English.

You can also choose to have Siri respond in English Only, or in either English or Hindi depending on which language you make the request in. If you [turn on Siri AI](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893) with iOS 27, Siri will respond in English to spoken requests.

## Ask Siri

If your device supports using Siri hands-free, then say "Siri" or "Hey Siri." If it doesn't, then you can use Siri with just a press of a button. [Learn how to activate Siri on your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105020) .

[Image: Phone app icon]

Make a call

Want to FaceTime a friend, or check your missed calls? Siri can help. Say "Siri" or "Hey Siri," then say something like:

- "Mom ko call karo"

- "Ajay ge FaceTime maadu"

- "Missed calls choopinchu"

[Image: Maps app icon]

Find places

Want to know what time the grocery store closes? Or need to get home? Just ask Siri. Say "Siri" or "Hey Siri," then you can ask things like:

- "Amar kachhakachhi bakery dekhao"

- "Vadodara ketalu door che"

- "Mujhe ghar le chalo"

[Image: Music app icon]

Play music

Already know what you want to listen to? Then ask Siri to play it. Say "Siri" or "Hey Siri," then say something like:

- "Badshah de gaane bajao"

- "Volume jasti maadu"

- "Hey gaane skip kar"

[Image: Weather app icon]

Check the weather

Want to know if you should wear a coat or bring an umbrella? Just ask Siri. Say "Siri" or "Hey Siri," then say something like:

- "Ajj da mausam kiven da hai"

- "Enthanu thapanila"

- "Sunday ko weather kaisa rahega"

- "Coimbatore vaanilai enna"

Siri features might vary by country or region.
