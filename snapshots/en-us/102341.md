# Turn on three finger drag for your Mac trackpad

The ability to drag items using a three-finger gesture is an option for trackpads that support Force Touch.

The setting names vary based on the version of macOS you're using:

- From the Apple menu , choose System Settings (or System Preferences).

- Click Accessibility.

- Click Pointer Control (or Mouse & Trackpad).

- Click the Trackpad Options button.

- Turn on “Use trackpad for dragging” (or “Enable dragging”).

- From the pop-up menu, choose the Three Finger Drag dragging style.

- Click OK.

[Learn more about these trackpad options](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/unac899/mac)
