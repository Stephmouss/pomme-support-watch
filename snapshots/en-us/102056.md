# If your HomeKit or Matter accessory isn't responding in the Home app

If "No Response" appears next to a smart home accessory made by a manufacturer other than Apple, learn what to do.

[lf accessories from one manufacturer not responding](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102056#one-manufacturer)

[If accessories from several manufacturers not responding](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102056#several-manufacturers)

## If accessories from one manufacturer aren't responding

Check if your smart home accessories are responding in the Home app after each step.

- Turn on Bluetooth in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, or in System Settings on your Mac.

- Restart any accessories that aren't responding. You may have to take the batteries out of your accessory and put them back in.

- Check the manufacturer's app to see if accessories that aren't responding in the Home app need a firmware update. If the accessories aren't responding in the manufacturer's app, you may need to reset them.

- Restart any third-party bridges and all home hubs (HomePod, HomePod mini, and Apple TV) by unplugging then plugging them back into power.

- Disconnect third-party Thread accessories from power for 5 minutes by unplugging them or removing their batteries. Then reconnect third-party Thread accessories and wait 10 minutes for the Thread network to stabilize.

- Remove accessories that aren't responding from the Home app.

- Select an accessory, scroll down to accessory settings, and select Remove Accessory. If you don't see the option to remove the accessory in the Home app, remove it through the manufacturer's app.

- After you remove the accessories, follow the manufacturer's instructions to reset them and add them to the Home app again.

If your accessories still aren't responding, [contact the manufacturer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/103190) for support.

## If accessories from several manufacturers aren't responding

Check if your smart home accessories are responding in the Home app after each step.

- Update your [iPhone, iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , or [Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) to the latest software.

- Close the Home app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac and reopen it.

- Connect your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to the same Wi-Fi network as your accessories. To control your accessories remotely, you must [set up a home hub](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102557) .

- Turn on Bluetooth in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, or in System Settings on your Mac.

- Restart any accessories that aren't responding. You may have to take the batteries out of your accessory and put them back in.

- Check the manufacturer's app to see if accessories that aren't responding in the Home app need a firmware update. If the accessories aren't responding in the manufacturer's app, you may need to reset them.

- Disconnect third-party Thread accessories from power for 5 minutes by unplugging them or removing their batteries. Then reconnect third-party Thread accessories and wait 10 minutes for the Thread network to stabilize.

- If your accessories still aren't responding in the Home app, restart your devices:

- Unplug your modem and router, then plug them back into power.

- Unplug any third-party bridges and all home hubs (HomePod, HomePod mini, and Apple TV), then plug them back into power.

- Restart accessories that aren't responding. You may have to take the batteries out of your accessory and put them back in.

- [Restart your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118259) , [iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101603) , or [Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp2522) .

- [Check VPN and third-party security software](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102281) to resolve potential network connectivity issues.

If your accessories still aren't responding, [contact Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
