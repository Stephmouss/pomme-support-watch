# Find the software version on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch

Use your device or a computer to find the version of iOS on your iPhone or iPod touch or iPadOS on your iPad.

## Find software version on iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch

- Go to Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap About.

[Image: iOS Version listed on the About page in Settings.]

## Find software version using a computer

- On a Mac with [macOS Catalina or later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033#latest) , open the Finder. On a Mac with [macOS Mojave or earlier](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033#latest) , open iTunes. On a Windows PC, open iTunes or [the Apple Devices app](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) .

- Connect your device to the computer.

- Locate your device on the computer. [If the computer doesn't recognize your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108643) , learn what to do.

- Look under the General tab or the Summary tab to see the software version.

[Image: On a PC with the Apple Devices app, you can manually check for iOS updates when you connect your iPhone.]
