# Find the software version on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch

Use your device or a computer to find the version of iOS on your iPhone or iPod touch, or the version of iPadOS on your iPad.

## Find the software version on your device

- Open Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap About.

- Find the software version next to iOS Version or iPadOS Version.

[Image: The About screen in Settings on iPhone, with iOS Version showing 27.0.]

## Find the software version using a computer

- Open the app for your computer:

- The Finder on a Mac with [macOS Catalina or later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033#latest)

- iTunes on a Mac with [macOS Mojave or earlier](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033#latest)

- iTunes or the [Apple Devices app](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) on a Windows PC

- Connect your device to your computer.

- Select your device. If your device is missing, [get help connecting it to your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108643) .

- Find the software version on the General tab in the Finder or the Apple Devices app, or on the Summary tab in iTunes.

[Image: The General tab in the Finder for a connected iPhone, with Software: iOS 27.0 highlighted.]
