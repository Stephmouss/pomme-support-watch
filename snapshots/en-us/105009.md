# See your Apple Cash transactions

View your Apple Cash transactions and check your card details from the Wallet app.

## See your transactions

### On iPhone

- Open the Wallet app, then tap your Apple Cash * card.

- You see Latest Transactions. To see transactions by year, scroll down and tap Transactions in [year].

- Tap a specific transaction for more details.

### On Apple Watch

- Open the Wallet app, then choose your Apple Cash card.

- Scroll down to see Latest Transactions.

- Tap a specific transaction for more details.

### On iPad

- Open the Settings app, then tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Tap your Apple Cash card, then tap the Transactions tab.

- Tap a specific transaction for more details.

### On Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Settings app, then tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Tap your Apple Cash card, then tap the Transactions tab.

- Tap a specific transaction for more details.

If you have any recurring payment transactions (either sent or received), you'll see an icon with two circling arrows underneath the dollar amount.

## Find your Apple Cash card details

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app, tap your Apple Cash card, tap the More button More button then tap Card Details .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- On your iPad, open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap your Apple Cash card.

- For Apple Watch, open the Apple Watch app on your iPhone, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap your Apple Cash card.

- On your Apple Vision Pro, open the Settings app, tap Wallet & Apple Pay, then tap your Apple Cash card.

[Image: Apple Cash card details on iPhone.]

From here, you can see your balance, add money, [set up auto reload](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108370#auto) , update your bank account, and more.

You need a cellular or Wi-Fi connection to see your up-to-date balance.

### If you're asked for a PIN

Apple Cash doesn't require a PIN because every payment is authenticated by Face ID, Touch ID, or a secure passcode. There is also no card number, security code, or expiration date for your Apple Cash card.

If you're prompted by a terminal to enter a PIN to complete a debit transaction, enter a four-digit code, like 0000.

## Get a PDF of your activity

On iPhone

- Open the Wallet app, then tap your Apple Cash card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Card Details .

[Image: the more button]

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Scroll down and tap Request Transaction Statement.

- Confirm your email address, then tap Send.

On iPad

- Open the Settings app, then scroll down and tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Tap your Apple Cash card, then tap the Transactions tab.

- Tap Request Transaction Statement, then tap Send.

On Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Settings app, then scroll down and tap Wallet & Apple Pay.

- Tap your Apple Cash card, then tap the Transactions tab.

- Tap Request Transaction Statement, then tap Send.

A PDF of your transaction history from the last 12 months is sent to [the same email address that you use as your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/109353) . You can also see information regarding any fees that have been applied to your Apple Cash card.
