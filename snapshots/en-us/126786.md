# Age requirements for managing an Apple Account in Singapore

Some Apple Account settings and feature requirements for children, teens, and adults will vary depending on your country or region.

These age confirmation requirements apply to Apple Accounts in Singapore:

- To download or purchase apps with an age rating of 18+, you need to confirm that you're 18 or older.

- You can confirm that you're an adult with a credit card that belongs to you, by scanning your passport, driving license, National Registration Identity Card, or Foreign Identification Number (FIN) card, or by taking a selfie. Debit cards and gift cards aren't supported.

- If you already have an Apple Account, Apple will check if you have a credit card on file or other eligible methods available to confirm that you're 18 or older.

- If you choose not to confirm that you're an adult, you can continue to use your device — you need to confirm before you can download apps with an age rating of 18+.

[Learn how to confirm that you're an adult](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125662)

[Confirm that you're an adult with a selfie](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149095)

## What is the age for a Child Account in my country or region?

In Singapore, a child under 16 can't create an Apple Account without help and consent from a parent or guardian. They must be part of a [Family Sharing group](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105062) .

[Learn how to update the birthday for a Child Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102473)
