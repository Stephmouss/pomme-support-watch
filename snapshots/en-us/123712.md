# Redownload games with the Apple Games app

In the Apple Games app, you can access your entire game library, including new and past downloads, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Before you begin

Make sure that you upgrade your device to the latest version of [iOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , [iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , or [macOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) .

## How to redownload games

- Open the Apple Games app. If prompted, [sign in to your Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111001) and [your Game Center account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105022) .

- Do one of the following to find the game you want to redownload:

- Tap or click Library, then scroll to Your Games to see a list of all of the games you have played. If you don't see the game you're looking for, check your filters and make sure none of them are selected.

- Tap or click the Search tab , type the game you're looking for in the search field, then tap or click the Search button . Select Library at the top of your screen to only see search results from your game library.

- Tap or click the game that you would like to redownload, then tap or click the Redownload button .

You can also see a list of apps that you've purchased, but are not downloaded on your device in the App Store. [Learn how to redownload apps and games from the App Store](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102417) .

## If there’s an issue when you redownload games

- If you're seeing a price or the Get button for a game that you've previously purchased, make sure you're signed in to the Apple Account that you used to purchase the game.

- If you still can't redownload a game, [try these tips](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108105) .
