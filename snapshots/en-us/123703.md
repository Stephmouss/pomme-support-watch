# Pay your Apple Card bill with Apple Cash or a bank account

You can use the Wallet app on your iPhone to pay your Apple Card bill with Apple Cash or a bank account.

## Pay using Apple Cash

If you don't have an Apple Cash* account, [learn how to set one up](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109304) .

- Open the Wallet app on your iPhone and tap Apple Card.

- Tap the area to the right of Card Balance.

- Tap Pay [amount]. You can also tap Other Amount.

- If you see your Apple Cash card, double-click the side button to pay, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. Tap done.

- If you see your bank account, tap Change Bank Account, then turn the Apple Cash slider on. Double-click the side button to pay, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. Tap Done.

If the balance of your Apple Cash account is less than the amount of your payment, the remaining balance is paid from your default bank account.

## Pay using your bank account

If you haven't set up a bank account in the Wallet app to pay your Apple Card bill, [learn how to add one](https://support.apple.com/en-us/123710) .

- Open the Wallet app on your iPhone and tap your Apple Card.

- Tap the area to the right of Card Balance.

- Tap Pay [amount]. You can also tap Schedule or Other Amount.

- If you see your bank account, double-click the side button to pay, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. Tap Done.

[Image: Apple Card payment sheet with a selected bank account and the Change Bank Account button.]

- If you want to pay with a different bank account, tap Change Bank Account, then tap Add Bank Account and follow the onscreen instructions.

- If you have an Apple Cash account, and see it instead of your bank account, tap Change Bank Account, then turn the Apple Cash slider off. Double-click the side button to pay, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode. Tap Done.
