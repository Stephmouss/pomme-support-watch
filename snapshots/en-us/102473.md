# Update the birthday associated with an Apple Account

It’s important to have your correct birthday on file with your Apple Account. It’s used in features like Family Sharing and helps provide age-appropriate experiences.

## Edit the birthday on an Apple Account

You can edit your birthday in your Apple Account settings or on the web.

You can edit a child’s birthday by signing in to their account on the web. If you’re changing a birthday from an adult to a child, [learn more about converting to a Child Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102473#birthdateforchild) .

### Edit your birthday on an iPhone or iPad

- Open Settings, then tap [your name].

- Tap Personal Information. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Tap your birthday to edit it.

[Image: Apple Account settings on iPhone showing the Change Birthday option.]

- Update your birthday, then tap the Done button . If you’re changing a birthday from an adult to a child, [learn more about converting to a Child Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102473#birthdateforchild) .

### Edit your birthday on a Mac

- Open System Settings, then click [your name].

- Click Personal Information. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click your birthday to edit it.

[Image: Apple Account settings on Mac showing the Edit Birthday option.]

- Update your birthday. If you’re trying to change a birthday from an adult to a child, use an iPhone, iPad, or the web instead.

### Edit your birthday or a child’s birthday on the web

- Sign in to [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com) with the account you want to edit.

- Select Personal Information, then choose Birthday.

[Image: The Apple Account page showing Personal Information and Birthday]

- Update the birthday and click Save. If you’re changing a birthday from an adult to a child, [learn more about converting to a Child Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102473#birthdateforchild) .

Depending on how you created your Apple Account, you might be required to provide additional information or change your Apple Account primary user name from a mobile phone number to an email address.

## Change the birthday for a Child Account

To change a birthday to any age below 13 ( [age varies by country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125666) ), the account must be a member of a Family Sharing group, and the Family Sharing organizer must approve the change.
