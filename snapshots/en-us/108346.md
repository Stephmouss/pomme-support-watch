# If you can't back up your iOS or iPadOS device to a computer or restore it from a backup

If you can't back up your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch to a computer, or if you can't restore your device from a computer backup, learn what to do.

## Try again after each step

- Make sure your [Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) or PC is up-to-date. Make sure that you have the [latest version of the Apple Devices app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118406) or the [latest version of iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118290) .

- Connect your device to your computer with a USB cable, and [locate your device on your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108392) . If your device doesn't appear in Finder, iTunes, or the Apple Devices app, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108643) . If you forgot your passcode or your device is disabled, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118430) .

- Restart all your devices.

- If you use a Mac, skip this step. If you use a Windows PC and iTunes won't respond or your backup never finishes, you might need to [update, change, disable, or uninstall your security software](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111760) .

- If you own more than one computer, try to make a backup on your other Mac or PC. Or, [use iCloud to back up your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118426) and [restore from your iCloud backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118105) .

- If you see an alert that says [an error occurred](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108346#linkdown) , your [backup is corrupt or incompatible](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108346#corrupt) , or [there's not enough disk space](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108346#space) , find your error message below to learn what to do next.

### If an alert says that an error occurred

If a message says that your computer can't back up your device because an error occurred, check that you have [the latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

### If an alert says there's not enough disk space

If a message says there’s not enough disk space when you try to back up your device, free some space on your Mac or PC.

### If an alert says that your backup is corrupt or incompatible

If your iOS or iPadOS device can't restore from a backup because the backup is corrupt or incompatible, make sure that your computer is updated. If you see an error that says your iOS or iPadOS software is too old, find out how to [update your device to restore the backup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108811) . If you still can't restore the backup, you might not be able to use that backup. Try to use an alternate backup or an iCloud backup.
