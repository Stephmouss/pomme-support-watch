# How to factory reset your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch

Learn how to erase information from your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch and restore your device to factory settings.

If you also need to securely erase your Apple Watch, [unpair your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108372) .

[Image: Play the video]

## Create a backup

[Back up your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118426) so you can restore your content, settings, and apps to a new or erased iPhone.

## Erase and reset your device

- Go to Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap Transfer or Reset [device].

- Tap Erase All Content and Settings.

[Image: Transfer or Reset iPhone screen with the Erase All Content and Settings button.]

- Tap Continue.

[Image: Erase This iPhone screen with a list of items that will be removed and a Continue button.]

- Choose to [keep or delete your eSIM](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102421) . If you erase your eSIM, you need to [contact your carrier to reactivate your cellular plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109526) .

[Image: An alert asking "Are you sure you want to erase all data?" with buttons to Cancel, Keep eSIM and Erase Data, and Delete eSIM and Erase Data listed after the alert text.]

- Enter your device passcode.

- Wait for your backup to finish.

- Enter your Apple Account password.

- Tap the done button on your keyboard.

- Wait for your device to reset.

When the process is complete, the Hello screen appears.

## Fix an iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch that won't erase

- If you don't remember your passcode, or your device is disabled, [find out what to do.](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118430)

- Learn what to do [if you forgot your Apple Account password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102656) .

- [Reset your Screen Time passcode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102677) . If you restore your device from a backup, it won't remove your Screen Time passcode.
