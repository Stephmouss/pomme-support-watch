# Sync your photos manually using the Finder

If you don't use iCloud Photos, you can manually sync your photos and videos from your Mac to your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch using the Finder.

## Turn on Finder syncing

You can use the Finder on your Mac to sync photos to your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch from the Photos app, or from a folder on your computer. Each time that you sync your iOS device with your computer, the photos and videos on your iOS device update to match the albums on your computer. If you have photos on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch that aren't on your computer, [learn how to import them](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120267) .

[Image: Photos sync settings for a connected iPhone in the Finder.]

- Open a Finder window and connect your device to your computer using a USB cable.

- Unlock your device.

- Your device appears in the sidebar of the Finder window. Click the device to select it.

- If prompted, [trust your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109054) .

- Click the Photos tab below your device's information.

- Select the checkbox next to Sync photos to your device from, then select Photos or a folder on your Mac.

- Choose between syncing all your photos and albums or only selected albums. You can also choose to sync only favorites, and whether to include videos.

- Click the Apply button in the lower-right corner of the window. If syncing doesn't start automatically, click the Sync button.

Import Slo-Mo and Time-Lapse videos through the Photos app or Image Capture in OS X Yosemite or later. This ensures the Finder syncs their effect. When you use iCloud Photos, Slo-Mo and Time-Lapse videos keep their effects.

[Live Photos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104966) keep their effect when you import them to Photos in OS X El Capitan and later, but if you sync them back to your device using your computer, Live Photos lose their effect. Use iCloud Photos so that Live Photos keep their effect, no matter what device you use.

You can sync your device only with the [System Photo Library](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104946) . If you have more than one photo library on your Mac, be sure that you designate the correct one as the System Photo Library.

## Turn off Finder syncing

If you don't want the synced photos on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch anymore, turn off syncing in the Finder and your device will remove them. You can turn off syncing for some of your photo albums or all of them at once.

### Stop syncing some albums

- Open a Finder window and connect your device to your computer using a USB cable.

- Unlock your device.

- Your device appears in the sidebar of the Finder window. Click the device to select it.

- If prompted, [trust your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109054) .

- Click the Photos tab below your device's information.

- Choose "Selected albums" and deselect the albums or collections that you want to stop syncing.

- Click Apply.

### Stop syncing all photos

- Open a Finder window and connect your device to your computer using a USB cable.

- Unlock your device.

- Your device appears in the sidebar of the Finder window. Click the device to select it.

- If prompted, [trust your device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109054) .

- Click the Photos tab below your device's information.

- Deselect "Sync Photos" then click "Remove photos."

- Click Apply.

## Update your photos with iCloud

If you use iCloud Photos, you can keep all your photos and videos stored in iCloud. They sync across all of your devices so you can access your library from your Mac, Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, and even your PC. Your newest shots and edits upload when you're connected to the internet.

[Learn how to set up and use iCloud Photos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108782) .

## Learn more

- iCloud Photos doesn't upload photos and videos that you add to your device from your computer. If you're missing photos after you turn on iCloud Photos, [follow these steps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101559) .

- You can always [make a copy of your photos and videos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108306) and keep them on your computer.

- Learn how to [import photos and videos from your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch to your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120267) .
