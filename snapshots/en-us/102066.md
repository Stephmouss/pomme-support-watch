# Use your iPhone after service

Here's information on how to use your iPhone after service.

- Record the serial number of your replacement iPhone. Your serviced part or product is covered by a 90-day service warranty, or the remainder of your original product warranty or AppleCare plan, whichever is longer. Consumer law may also apply to your serviced or replaced product where applicable.

- Charge your iPhone.

- [Insert the SIM card into your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109357) , if your iPhone uses a SIM card. If your iPhone uses an eSIM, contact your wireless service provider to set up your new eSIM.

- [Restore your iPhone from an iCloud backup or a backup on your computer](https://support.apple.com/HT204184) .

- [Check your Apple ID device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT205064) list to confirm that you're signed in.
