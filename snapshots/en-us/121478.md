# If you're asked to buy a movie or TV show again on your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

Learn what to do if you're asked to buy a movie or TV show that you've already purchased in the Apple TV app.

## How to watch a purchased item if you're being asked to buy it again

In iOS and iPadOS 18.0 and visionOS 2.0, you might be asked to buy a movie or TV show in your Library that you've already purchased. If you see the Buy button, you can play your movie or TV show without purchasing it again.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Apple TV app.

- Tap Library.

- Find the movie or TV show you'd like to watch.

- Tap the More button .

- Tap Play.

### On your Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Apple TV app.

- In the sidebar, tap Library.

- Find the movie or TV show you'd like to watch.

- Pinch and hold the item to see more options, then tap Play.
