# Record the screen on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch

In Control Center, you can start a screen recording and capture sound on your iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch.

[Image: iPhone screen recording indicator shows 00:01 and a red record button.]

## How to record your screen

- Swipe down from the top-right corner of your screen.

- Tap the gray Record button , then wait for the three-second countdown. If you don't see the Record button, [add it to your Control Center](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-and-customize-control-center-iph59095ec58/ios) .

[Image: gray record icon]

- Exit Control Center to record your screen.

- To stop recording, tap the Screen Recording button at the top of your screen, then tap the Stop button . Or open Control Center and tap the red Record button .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

[Image: red record icon]

To find your recording, open the Photos app and select your screen recording in your Library.

[Learn how to take a screenshot on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/102616)

Some apps might not allow you to record audio or video. You can't record your screen and use screen mirroring at the same time.

## Record your screen with audio

- Swipe down from the top-right corner of your screen.

- Tap and hold the gray Record button . If you don't see the Record button, [add it to your Control Center](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-and-customize-control-center-iph59095ec58/ios) .

- Tap the Microphone button to turn it on.

- Tap the gray Record button , then wait for the three-second countdown.

- Exit Control Center to record your screen.

- To stop recording, tap the Screen Recording button at the top of your screen, then tap the Stop button . Or open Control Center and tap the red Record button .
