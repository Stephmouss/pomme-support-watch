# Authorize or deauthorize your computer for Apple Account purchases

You can authorize your computer to play your purchases, deauthorize a computer from accessing purchases, and check how many authorized computers you have.

[What you need to know about authorizing your computer](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118292#aboutauthorizing)

[Authorize or deauthorize your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118292#authorizemac)

[Authorize or deauthorize your Windows PC](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118292#authorizepc)

## What you need to know about authorizing your computer

- You can authorize up to five computers at a time to play your Apple Account purchases.

- Each computer that you authorize counts as one authorization — even if you authorize the same computer multiple times.

- If you have five authorized computers and want to authorize a new computer, you have to deauthorize one of your computers.

- You can only authorize a computer that you have access to. You can’t authorize a computer using another computer or an iPhone or iPad.

- After you authorize your computer in any Apple app, you can access all of your Apple Account purchases in other Apple apps.

- After you authorize your computer, your computer is associated with your Apple Account for purchases. Other devices — like iPhone and iPad — don't count toward your authorized computer limit. However, these are associated with your Apple Account for purchases. Learn how to [view your associated devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118412) .

## Manage authorizations on Mac

Use the Apple Music or Apple TV app to authorize or deauthorize your computer, and to check your authorized computer count.

### Authorize or deauthorize your Mac

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- From the Account menu in the menu bar, choose Authorize This Computer or Deauthorize This Computer.

[Image: Apple Music app on Mac, with the Account menu open and Authorizations selected, showing the "Authorize This Computer" or "Deauthorize This Computer" options.]

- Sign in to your Apple Account. [Forgot the email address or phone number that you use with Apple Account?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102627)

- Click Authorize or Deauthorize.

### Check how many computers are authorized

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- From the Account menu in the menu bar, choose Account Settings.

- Sign in to your Apple Account. [Forgot the email address or phone number that you use with Apple Account?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102627)

- Scroll down, then look next to Authorized Devices to see how many computers are authorized.

### Deauthorize a computer that you don't have access to

You have to deauthorize all of your computers, then authorize each computer that you have again. You can deauthorize all computers only once a year.

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- From the Account menu in the menu bar, choose Account Settings.

- Sign in to your Apple Account. [Forgot the email address or phone number that you use with Apple Account?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102627)

- Scroll down, then click Authorized Devices.

[Image: Apple Music app on Mac showing the Account Settings page and the Authorize Devices option.]

- Click Deauthorize All. If you don't see this option, then this is your only authorized computer.

- [Authorize each computer that you use again](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118292#authorizemac) .

## Manage authorizations on Windows PC

Use the Apple Music or Apple TV app to authorize or deauthorize your computer, and to check your authorized computer count.

### Authorize or deauthorize your Windows PC

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Or, click Sign-in and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Authorize Machine or Deauthorize Machine.

[Image: Apple Music app on Windows PC showing Authorize Machine and Deauthorize Machine.]

- Sign in to your Apple Account. [Forgot the email address or phone number that you use with Apple Account?](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102627)

- Click Authorize or Deauthorize.

### Check how many computers are authorized

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Or, click Sign-in and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click View My Account. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account.

- On the Account Settings page, look next to Computer Authorizations to see how many computers are authorized.

[Image: Apple Music app for Windows PC showing the Account Settings page with the 'Computer Authorizations' section, displaying the number of authorized computers and a 'Deauthorize All...' button.]

### Deauthorize a computer that you don't have access to

You have to deauthorize all of your computers, then authorize each computer that you have again. You can deauthorize all computers only once a year.

- Open the Apple Music app or Apple TV app.

- Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Or, click Sign-in and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click View My Account. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account.

- On the Account Settings page, look next to Computer Authorizations, then click Deauthorize All. If you don't see this option, then this is your only authorized computer.

- [Authorize each computer that you use again](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118292#authorizepc) .
