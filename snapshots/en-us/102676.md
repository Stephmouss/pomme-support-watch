# How to apply for a Savings account

When you open a Savings account, earned Daily Cash will be automatically deposited to your account. You can manage your Savings account in the Wallet app, where you can easily track your account balance and interest earned over time.

## How Savings works

When you set up Savings 1 , you agree to have future Daily Cash automatically deposited into your account — this allows you to earn interest on the Daily Cash that you get back from Apple Card purchases. You also earn interest on deposits you make to Savings from a [linked external bank account](https://support.apple.com/102163#bankaccount) 2 or Apple Cash. 3 There are no fees or minimum balance requirements. 4

You can change where your Daily Cash is sent so that it's [automatically deposited to your Apple Cash account](https://support.apple.com/119575#dailycash) at any time.

To access your Savings funds for spending or making purchases, [transfer money from Savings to Apple Cash or a linked external bank account](https://support.apple.com/108334) . You can use Savings to pay your Apple Card balance, but Savings funds can't be directly accessed through Apple Pay.

## What you need to open and maintain an account

- Be an active Apple Card account owner or co-owner 5 and [add Apple Card to your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/109301) .

- [Update to the latest version of iOS](https://support.apple.com/118575) .

- Be at least 18 years or older.

- A social security number or individual taxpayer identification number.

- Be a U.S. resident with a valid, physical U.S. address. 6

- [Two-factor authentication set up for your Apple Account](http://support.apple.com/102660) .

## How to apply for Savings

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap the More button , then tap Rewards & Offers.

- Next to Savings, tap Set Up, then follow the onscreen instructions.

When you open Savings, you'll be asked if you want to transfer funds from your Apple Cash balance to your new account to start earning interest on that money. If you don't want to transfer your Apple Cash balance, tap the Close button . If you want to transfer funds, tap Transfer Your Apple Cash and follow the onscreen instructions.

[Image: the Close button]

If you have any Daily Cash available to redeem as an Apple Card statement credit, it will be transferred to your new Savings account when you set up Savings.

If your Apple Card account is restricted or locked, you may be unable to open a Savings account.

## Deposit money into Savings

When you open Savings, you agree to automatically deposit [Daily Cash](https://support.apple.com/119575) that you earn into your Savings account. You can also add money to Savings from a linked external bank account or Apple Cash.

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap Savings account, then tap Add Money.

[Image: Savings balance screen with the “Add Money” button selected.]

- Enter the amount that you want to add, then tap Next.

- If the payment method next to Transfer From is the one you want to use, tap Add.

- To transfer money from your Apple Cash account instead, tap Transfer From.

- Tap your Apple Cash card.

- Double-click the side button to transfer, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

The money you deposit into Savings starts earning interest when the transfer is initiated from the Wallet app.

If you transfer money from Apple Cash, the funds are typically available instantly. If you transfer money from a linked external bank account, it will generally take a few days for funds to be available for withdrawal.

## View your Savings account balance and interest earned

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap Savings Account. From here, you can see your Current Balance. The Current Balance may include pending transactions or deposits, so the entire amount may not be available to withdraw immediately.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details to see your Available Balance. Your Available Balance is the amount that you can withdraw immediately. You can also see any interest you've earned this year and your current Annual Percentage Yield (APY). 7

Your interest is compounded daily and credited to your Savings account monthly. You can also view your Savings balance and interest earned at [card.apple.com/savings](https://card.apple.com/savings) .
