# About Pixelmator Pro

Learn more about Pixelmator Pro for Mac.

## Where to purchase and download Pixelmator Pro

You can [purchase and download Pixelmator Pro](https://www.pixelmator.com/pro/buy/index.html) from the Mac App Store. You can install Pixelmator Pro on up to six Mac computers if you're signed into the same Apple Account on all of them.

If you see a trial screen when opening Pixelmator Pro, you have the Pixelmator Pro trial, which is no longer available. To delete the Pixelmator Pro trial, locate the app, drag the app to the trash, then choose Finder > Empty Trash.

## If you already purchased Pixelmator Pro

If you already purchased Pixelmator Pro but you can't download the app, you might be signed in to the App Store with a different Apple Account or your App Store region may have changed. To verify this, you can [check your App Store purchase history](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212) .

## Family Sharing with Pixelmator Pro

When you enroll in Family Sharing, family members can download, install, and use Pixelmator Pro on their personal devices.

[Learn how to share apps and purchases with your family](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108774)

## Features and system requirements

Pixelmator Pro requires macOS 12 Monterey or later and is fully optimized for Apple silicon.

## Pixelmator Pro compared to Photomator

To create designs from scratch by combining images, shapes, or text, use Pixelmator Pro. For fast photo-editing workflows with the ability for batch editing, use Photomator.

Pixelmator Pro and Photomator have been designed to work together. Both apps have been designed for edit-syncing through iCloud, which makes it easy to round-trip workflows between apps.* For example, edit a photo in Photomator on Mac, save it to iCloud, then open the file in Pixelmator Pro to add text, shapes, captions, and more.

Layered PXD files (the non-destructive [Pixelmator Pro file format](https://support.pixelmator.com/pixelmator-pro-user-guide/create-open-and-save-images/about-the-pixelmator-pro-file-format) ) open flattened in Photomator.
