# "Cellular Settings Updated" message

If you see the "Cellular Settings Updated" message on your device, an update was made to the cellular modem configurations in your cellular settings.

The cellular modem keeps your device connected to 2G, 3G, 4G (LTE), and 5G cellular networks and updates automatically.

Cellular modem configuration updates ensure that you receive high quality cellular service and maintain compliance. Your data plan or carrier settings are not affected by cellular modem updates. There is no action required by you.
