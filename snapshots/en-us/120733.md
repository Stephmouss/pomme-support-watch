# Delete a wallpaper on your iPhone

On your iPhone with iOS 16 or later, open the wallpaper gallery from your Lock Screen to delete a wallpaper option.

## Delete a wallpaper on your iPhone

- On your iPhone with iOS 16 or later, press [the side button](https://support.apple.com/105103) to go to the Lock Screen.

- Touch and hold your Lock Screen, then use your Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode to open the wallpaper gallery.

- Swipe up on the wallpaper that you want to delete.

[Image: When viewing your wallpapers, swipe up to reveal the delete button.]

- Tap the trash icon .

- Tap Delete This Wallpaper.

In iOS 15 and earlier, you can [set a new wallpaper in the Settings app](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-the-wallpaper-iph3d267104/15.0/ios/15.0) . To delete an image that you set as a wallpaper in iOS 15 and earlier, go to the Photos app and [delete the image](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/delete-and-hide-photos-and-videos-iphb4defbde9/15.0/ios/15.0) .
