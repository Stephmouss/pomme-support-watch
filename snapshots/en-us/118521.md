# Use your Mac with Apple Vision Pro

Learn how to use Apple Vision Pro as a private, portable 5K display for your Mac with Mac Virtual Display.

[Image: Play the video]

## Before you begin

Make sure your Mac meets the requirements:

- Mac Virtual Display works with [any Mac that has macOS 14 Sonoma or later](https://support.apple.com/105113) .

- To [use a Wide or Ultrawide Mac Virtual Display screen](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118521#aspect) , you need a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS Sequoia 15.2 or later, and your Apple Vision Pro needs visionOS 2.2 or later.

Next, check these things on your Mac and your Apple Vision Pro:

- Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on both devices.

- Sign in to the same Apple Account with [two-factor authentication](https://support.apple.com/102660) on both devices.

- Turn on [iCloud Keychain](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/tana39a7fa0/) on both devices.

- On your Mac, go to System Settings, select [your name], then select iCloud. Select See All next to Saved to iCloud. Select Passwords & Keychain, then make sure Sync this Mac is turned on.

- On your Apple Vision Pro, go to Settings, tap [your name], then tap iCloud. Tap See All next to Saved to iCloud. Tap Passwords & Keychain, then make sure Sync this Apple Vision Pro is turned on.

## Start a Mac Virtual Display session

If you have a compatible Mac laptop, you can begin a Mac Virtual Display session by looking at it. If you have a Mac desktop, start by opening Control Center.

### If you have a Mac laptop

- Make sure that your Mac laptop is open and its display is active.

- While wearing Apple Vision Pro, look at your Mac.

- Tap the Connect button that appears above your Mac.

If you don't see the Connect button, follow the steps in the next section.

### If you have a Mac desktop computer

- [Open Control Center](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/open-control-center-tand3233e05b/visionos) .

- In the center section of Control Center, tap the Mac Virtual Display button .

- Select your Mac from the list that appears.

Mac Virtual Display connects your Apple Vision Pro to only one Mac at a time.

## Adjust and use the Mac Virtual Display window

When your Mac connects, its display appears as a window in Apple Vision Pro. Pinch and drag the window bar that appears at the bottom of the window to move your Mac Virtual Display and arrange it closer or farther away in space.

You can also resize Mac Virtual Display by pinching and dragging the window bar that appears at either bottom corner of the display, [and change the window’s aspect ratio and resolution](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118521#aspect) .

Use your Mac computer's built-in keyboard and trackpad — or its connected [Bluetooth keyboard, trackpad, or mouse](https://support.apple.com/118516) — to control your Mac within Apple Vision Pro.

## Route audio from Mac to Apple Vision Pro

If your Mac has Apple silicon and you’ve updated your devices to macOS Sequoia 15.2 or later and visionOS 2.2 or later, audio from your Mac (such as music and videos) plays through the audio pods on your Apple Vision Pro. System sounds like notifications still play through your Mac.

If you're using a Mac app with sound input, such as videoconferencing, the sound is routed through the microphone that's built in or connected to your Mac.

## Change the Mac Virtual Display aspect ratio and resolution

With macOS Sequoia 15.2 or later and visionOS 2.2 or later, you can choose among Standard, Wide, and Ultrawide aspect ratios:

- Connect to your Mac in your Apple Vision Pro.

- Tap your computer's name above the Mac Virtual Display window.

- Choose Standard, Wide, or Ultrawide.

Standard is a 16:9 aspect ratio, Wide is 21:9, and Ultrawide is 32:9. The displays are curved, and can wrap around your workspace as you increase their size.

### Adjust the screen resolution

Open System Settings on your Mac, click Displays, then choose from the resolutions. To see all available sizes, turn on Show all resolutions.

If you have a [Mac with Apple silicon](https://support.apple.com/116943) , it can appear in Apple Vision Pro in the Standard aspect ratio at resolutions up to 5K (5120 x 2880). With an Intel processor, your Mac can appear in the Standard aspect ratio at resolutions up to 3K.

If you have a Mac with Apple silicon updated to macOS Sequoia 15.2 or later, it can appear in the Wide aspect ratio at resolutions up to 3360 x 1440 points (6720 x 2880 pixels), and in Ultrawide at resolutions up to 5120 x 1440 points (10240 x 2880 pixels).

When your Mac has multiple displays connected to it, Mac Virtual Display shows only the one that you've set as the main display.

## Use your Mac input devices to control visionOS

When you're in a Mac Virtual Display session, you can use your Mac computer's built-in keyboard and trackpad, or its connected input devices, to control visionOS apps and content.

- Look at another visionOS window.

- Touch your Mac trackpad or move its connected mouse. A circle-shaped pointer appears where you're looking.

- Use the pointer to scroll, select items, and move into text fields where you can type with your Mac keyboard.

## Adjust the Mac Virtual Display window for the best experience

- Increase the size of the Mac Virtual Display window.

- Move the Mac Virtual Display farther away.

- Change the resolution of your Mac display within Apple Vision Pro.

- Try [using an Environment](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/use-environments-tanb58c3cfaf/visionos) to see if removing distractions through full or partial immersion helps. If you're using an Environment with visionOS 2 or later, your Mac keyboard is revealed when you look at it.

## Stay connected to your Mac when you take off Apple Vision Pro

- While in a Mac Virtual Display session, tap your computer's name above the Mac Virtual Display window.

- Tap Settings.

- Turn on Stay Connected.

Now Mac Virtual Display will stay active even if you take off Apple Vision Pro.

## End a Mac Virtual Display session

When you're finished, tap the close button at the bottom of the Mac display to disconnect mirroring from your computer.

## If Mac Virtual Display isn't working

[Learn what to do if Mac Virtual Display isn't working on your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119942)
