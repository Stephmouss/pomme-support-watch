# If your iPhone or iPad is running slow

If your iPhone or iPad freezes or has performance issues, try these tips.

## Check your network conditions

Apps often need an internet connection, and slow performance can occur due to network congestion or when a device reconnects to cell towers while moving. Even with a strong signal, you might need to wait, try a different location, or use Wi-Fi. If cellular data issues persist across multiple locations, contact your carrier.

[Troubleshoot cellular data issues](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120000) .

## Close an app that's not responding

- Swipe up from the bottom of the screen (or double-click the Home button on older device models).

- Find the app.

- Swipe up on its preview to close the app.

Force closing apps unnecessarily can slow down your device. Reopening a closed app takes longer because it has to reload all its data.

[Fix an unresponsive app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/119876) .

## Check your device's available storage

iOS and iPadOS automatically manage storage during app installs and updates. They remove items that you can download again or no longer need. To avoid slowdowns, maintain at least 1 GB of free space.

- Open Settings.

- Tap General.

- Tap [device] Storage.

## Offload or delete apps to free up storage

iOS and iPadOS list your apps on the [device] Storage screen along with the amount of space that they use. iOS and iPadOS offer storage-saving recommendations on the [device] Storage screen.

You can enable these recommendations or review apps to free up more space. If you don't see any recommendations, or if you need to free up even more space, review the apps on your device.

Tap an app, then choose from the options 1 :

- Offload the app, which frees up storage used by the app, but keeps its documents and data.

- Delete the app, which removes the app and its related data.

[Free up more storage](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429) .

## Turn off Low Power Mode

Low Power Mode is a feature that helps extend battery life by reducing how much power your device uses. When enabled, Low Power Mode disables certain features or makes them work slower, and the battery icon turns yellow.

Low Power Mode can be turned on by going to Settings and tapping Battery. If you need your iPhone or iPad to function without low-power restrictions, consider leaving Low Power Mode off.

[Manage Low Power Mode](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101604) .

## Keep your device from getting too hot or cold

When your iOS or iPadOS device gets too hot or cold, it might run slower. To fix this, move it to a more moderate temperature.

[Check operating temperature limits](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118431) .

## Check your battery health

All rechargeable batteries are parts that wear out over time. Over time, your iPhone or iPad battery loses capacity and peak performance. Eventually, you'll need to replace it.

If a battery needs to be replaced, iOS and iPadOS can show battery health and recommend replacement.

- Open Settings.

- Tap Battery.

- Tap Battery Health.

## Get more help

- A touch screen that responds to taps incorrectly can make your device seem slow. If you think that [your touch screen isn't responding correctly](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102567) , try restarting your [iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118259) , [iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101603) , or [iPod touch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102137) .

- If your iPhone or iPad touch screen still isn't responding, you might need service. [Get service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .

- Depending on the app, you might be able to delete some of its documents and data.
