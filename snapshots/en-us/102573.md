# About Activation Lock on your Apple Watch

Find My includes a feature called Activation Lock that's designed to prevent anyone else from using your Apple Watch if it's ever lost or stolen.

## What is Activation Lock?

Find My and Activation Lock for your Apple Watch turn on automatically when you [set up Find My](https://support.apple.com/102648) on your paired iPhone. With Activation Lock, your Apple Account email address or phone number and password are required before anyone can:

- Unpair your Apple Watch from your iPhone.

- Pair and use your Apple Watch with a new iPhone.

- Turn off Find My on your device.

Activation Lock can help you keep your device secure, even if it's in the wrong hands, and can improve your chances of recovering your Apple Watch. Even if you erase your device remotely, Activation Lock can continue to keep anyone from reactivating your device without your permission. All you need to do is keep Find My turned on, and remember your Apple Account email address or phone number and password.

- Tap the My Watch tab, then tap All Watches at the top of the screen.

## If your Apple Watch goes missing

If your Apple Watch is lost or stolen, you can use Find My to help you locate it and protect your information. Just open the Find My app on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac or sign in to [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find/) to locate your missing watch on a map. You can also play a sound to help you find it, use Lost Mode to lock it, or remotely erase all of your personal information.

[Learn what to do if your Apple Watch is lost or stolen](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101558)

## Turn off Activation Lock before you send your Apple Watch in for service, sell it, or give it away

Before you [send your Apple Watch in for service](https://support.apple.com/102868) , sell it, or give it away, you should turn off Activation Lock. Just unpair your Apple Watch from your iPhone:

- Keep your Apple Watch and iPhone close together.

- On your iPhone, open the Apple Watch app.

- Tap the My Watch tab, then tap All Watches at the top of the screen.

[Image: Default screen of the Apple Watch app]

- Tap the More Info button next to your Apple Watch.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

[Image: All Watches screen where the More Info button can be found]

- Tap Unpair Apple Watch. To confirm, tap Unpair [watch name]. For cellular models of Apple Watch, tap Remove Cellular Plan. If you aren't going to pair with a different Apple Watch or iPhone, you might need to contact your carrier to cancel your cellular subscription.*

[Image: Option to unpair Apple Watch]

- Enter your Apple Account email address or phone number and password. If you forgot your Apple Account password, [you can reset it](https://support.apple.com/102656) .

- Tap again to confirm.

* Carrier fees may apply. Contact your carrier for details.

## Turn off Activation Lock on the web

If you can't access your iPhone or Apple Watch, or if your Apple Watch isn't paired with your iPhone and you need to turn off Activation Lock:

- Go to [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find) and sign in with your Apple Account.

- Look for your Apple Watch under All Devices, then click your watch name.

- Click Erase.

- Click Next. You might need to enter your Apple Account password again.

- Add a phone number if you'd like, then click Next.

- Click Erase.

- Click the Remove button that appears under your Apple Watch after it's erased.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Click Continue.

- Click Deactivate & Remove if you have Theft and Loss coverage provided by AppleCare+.
