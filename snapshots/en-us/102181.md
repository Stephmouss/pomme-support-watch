# How to connect the Apple USB SuperDrive

Find out which Mac computers can use the Apple USB SuperDrive and how to connect it.

[Image: usb-superdrive-straight-front]

## Check compatibility

The [Apple USB SuperDrive](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MD564LL/A/apple-usb-superdrive) is compatible with Mac computers introduced in 2008 or later that don't have a built-in optical drive.

## Connect your SuperDrive

To use your SuperDrive, connect it to a [USB-A port on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201736) * , then insert a disc. Make sure that the aluminum enclosure of the SuperDrive is facing up.

To connect your SuperDrive to a [Thunderbolt 4, Thunderbolt 3, or USB-C port on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201736) , you can use one of these adapters:

- [USB-C Digital AV Multiport Adapter](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT207806)

- [USB-C VGA Multiport Adapter](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT207807)

- [USB-C to USB Adapter](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT207816)

## Eject a disc

To eject a disc, click Finder in the Dock, then choose File > Eject. Or, if your keyboard has an eject key, press and hold the eject key .

[Image: key]

* If your SuperDrive doesn't respond when connected to an external display or USB hub, connect your SuperDrive to a USB port directly on your Mac.
