# Sync your iPhone, iPad, or iPod with your computer

Sync music, movies, and more with the Finder, iTunes, or the Apple Devices app. The app you need depends on your Mac or PC.

Before you sync, consider keeping your content online with iCloud, Apple Music, or a similar service. Then you can access your music, photos, and more when you're away from your computer.

## Sync on a Mac with macOS Catalina or later

You can [sync or remove content in the Finder](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102471) .

## Sync on a Mac with macOS Mojave or earlier

You can [sync music, movies, and more in iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108347) .

## Sync on a PC

You can [sync with iTunes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108347) or [sync with the Apple Devices app](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) .
