# Change your Apple Account password

Learn how to use a trusted device to change your Apple Account password or a child’s Apple Account password.

Apple ID is now Apple Account. You can still sign in with the same email address or phone number and password.

## Use a trusted Apple device

The easiest and most secure way to change your Apple Account password is to use your iPhone or another trusted device — one on which you’ve already signed in to your Apple Account using two-factor authentication, so that we know it’s yours.

A parent or guardian can also use their trusted device to [change the Apple Account password for a Child Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101567#childaccount) in their Family Sharing group.

## Change your Apple Account password on your iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, or Apple Vision Pro

- Open the Settings app, then tap [your name].

- Tap Sign-In & Security.

- Tap Change Password.

[Image: iPhone showing Change Password button in settings.]

- Follow the onscreen instructions to change your Apple Account password.

- If you have multiple devices signed in to your Apple Account, you’ll have the option to remain signed in or not. Tap Remove Other Devices to require signing in again on those devices, or tap Keep All Devices Signed In.

You might be required to wait one hour before changing your Apple Account password. Learn more [about Stolen Device Protection](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120340) and when a security delay might be required.

## Change your Apple Account password on your Mac

- Choose Apple menu , then System Settings.

- Click [your name], then click Sign-In & Security.

- Click Change Password.

- Enter the password used to unlock your Mac. Then follow the onscreen instructions to change your Apple Account password.

[Image: Mac showing Apple Account sign-in and security settings.]

You might be required to wait one hour before changing your Apple Account password. Learn more [about Stolen Device Protection](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120340) and when a security delay might be required.

## Change your Apple Account password on the web

The Apple Account website redirects you to a trusted device to manage your Apple Account, when possible. But if you don't have a trusted device, you might be able to change your Apple Account password on the web.

Go to [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com) and sign in to your Apple Account. Then go to Sign-In and Security and choose Password.

## Change your child’s Apple Account password using your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Vision Pro

- Open Settings, then tap Family. Choose your child’s account.

- Tap Change [child’s Name] Password. If the Child Account has Screen Time turned on, you might need to choose Personal Information and then tap Change [child’s Name] Password.

- Follow the onscreen instructions to change the Apple Account password of the Child Account.

You can also change a child’s Apple Account password in Settings on their own device.

## Can’t change your password?

If you forgot your password, [learn how to regain access to your account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102656) .
