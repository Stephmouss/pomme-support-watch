# About the Apple Education Community

Learn about the Apple Education Community and how to sign up.

The Apple Education Community is an online, professional learning hub designed for educators using Apple technology to make engaging learning experiences for their students. The Community features two main spaces, the Learning Center (formerly Apple Teacher Learning Center) and the Forum —– a secure, collaborative space for educators to connect and learn from one another.

The Learning Center offers skill‑building tutorials, lesson ideas, and inspiration from Apple. With resources available in 17 languages, educators can build strong foundational skills for using iPad and Mac, and deepen student learning with Pages, Keynote, Numbers, GarageBand, and iMovie. As educators learn, they can gain recognition through the [Apple Teacher Program](https://education.apple.com/learning-center/T006360A) and access further learning resources.

The Forum* gives educators the opportunity to connect peer‑to‑peer and to receive coaching and mentoring from Apple experts. Educators can ask questions, discuss relevant topics, and share stories and resources that impact learning and more. Community members with Forum profiles can follow other members to stay connected and inspired. You can learn more about using the Forum in the [Forum Help Resources](https://education.apple.com/forum/forum-help-resources) .

*Content approved for public viewing in the Forum is available as read-only for all users. Some collaboration features and areas of the Forum may not be available in all languages or in all countries or regions.

## Sign up using your Apple Account

Membership to the Apple Education Community is free and provides unlimited access to an ever-growing collection of resources. When you become a member, you gain access to the Apple Teacher professional learning program and all of its benefits. You can use your personal Apple Account (recommended) to [sign up for the Apple Education Community](https://education.apple.com/#/auth/sign-up) , or you can use a [Managed Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/guide/deployment/service-access-with-managed-apple-ids-depdc4ba8d82/web) assigned by your school if your role is an instructor, manager, or administrator. Managed Apple Accounts with a student role are restricted from accessing the Apple Education Community.

If you don’t have a personal Apple Account, [learn how to create one](https://support.apple.com/108647) .

## Get email updates

As a member of the Apple Education Community, you may sign up to receive emails and stay up to date on inspiring stories, practical tips, and fresh ideas to help you integrate Apple products and apps into your lessons. Emails are sent to the address associated with your Apple Account. Confirm this email address and update your Apple Account settings at [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/) .
