# See your Apple Card spending history

View your transactions and track how much you spend on entertainment, food, shopping, and more. You can also compare your spending from previous weeks, months, or years.

## See your purchases

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Scroll down to Latest Card Transactions to see your purchases from the last month. To see older transactions, scroll down further to see previous months and years.

- To see details about a specific purchase, tap the transaction, then tap it again.

If you use Apple Card Family,* you can see who made a purchase. If you don't recognize a purchase or need help with the transaction, you can [report a transaction issue](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102335) .

## Find a transaction

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap your Apple Card.

- Tap the Search button . Here you can search by category, merchant, or location.

[Image: Search button in Apple Card overview in the Wallet app.]

### Search by category, merchant, or location

- Open the Wallet app, tap the Search button , then scroll down.

- Tap the category, merchant, or location for the transaction you’re looking for.

- If there are multiple transactions, tap See All next to Transactions to show more.

### Search by date

- Open the Wallet app, tap Apple Card, then tap the Search button .

- In the search field, enter a date such as 8/31/25, then tap the Search button on the keyboard.

[Image: Searching for transactions by date in the Wallet app.]

- If there are multiple transactions, tap See All next to Transactions to show more.

### Search by time period

- In the Wallet app, tap Apple Card, then tap the Search button .

- In the search field, enter the time period, such as 2/15/25-6/4/25.

- If there are multiple transactions, tap See All next to Transactions to show more.

### Search by transaction amount

- In the Wallet app, tap Apple Card, then tap the Search button .

- Enter an amount, for example, 8.

[Image: Searching for transactions by amount in the Wallet app.]

- Choose from the three options shown at the top of the screen:

- Transactions equal to $8.00

- Transactions over $8.00

- Transactions under $8.00

Look for the purchase under Transactions. If you don't see it, tap See All.

## Change the category of a transaction

If a transaction belongs in a different category, you can change it for one or multiple transactions.

- Tap a transaction, then tap it again.

- Next to Category, tap the current category, then tap the category you want to change the transaction to.

[Image: Category options for an Apple Card transaction.]

- If there are multiple transactions, tap Select Multiple Transactions, select each transaction you want to change or tap Select All, then tap the Change button .

## View your weekly, monthly, or yearly spending

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Under Card Balance, tap the Monthly Activity spending summary button.

- Tap Week, Month, or Year to switch between each view.

[Image: Monthly spending highlights for Apple Card in the Wallet app.]

- Swipe right to see spending activity from previous periods.

Under your Total Spending amount, you'll see a message letting you know how much more or less you spent compared to the previous period.
