# Customize headphone audio levels on your iPhone or iPad

Amplify soft sounds and adjust certain frequencies for your hearing needs with Headphone Accommodations in iOS and iPadOS 14 and later.

Headphone Accommodations works with these headphones:

- AirPods 2 and later

- AirPods Max (all models)

- AirPods Pro (all models)

- Apple EarPods (with 3.5 mm Headphone Plug or Lightning Connector)

- Beats Fit Pro

- Beats Solo Pro

- Powerbeats

- Powerbeats Fit

- Powerbeats Pro

- Powerbeats Pro 2

When [using automatic device switching](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104988) with your headphones, your custom audio settings will transfer between your iPhone, your iPad, your paired Apple Watch (on watchOS 7 or later), your Mac running macOS Sequoia or later (depending on your model of AirPods), and your Apple Vision Pro.

If you [enable Hearing Assistance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120992) with your AirPods Pro 2 or AirPods Pro 3 on iOS 18.1 or later, your Headphone Accommodations settings will be overridden.

## Set up Headphone Accommodations

- Check that you have the [latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- Go to the Settings app , tap Accessibility, then select Audio & Visual. Tap Headphone Accommodations, then turn on Headphone Accommodations.

- Choose how you want to apply your custom audio settings:

- Turn on Phone to apply these settings to phone calls, FaceTime calls, and any third-party video call apps.

- Turn on Media to apply these settings to music, movies, podcasts, audiobooks, Siri, voicemail, and [Live Listen](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102479) .

- Customize your headphone audio settings:

- [Use Customize Audio](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102663#customize-audio) . You’ll listen to audio samples and choose which ones sound better to you. Then you can apply the custom settings suggested based on your choices.

- [Manually adjust audio settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102663#manually-adjust) .

### Use Customize Audio

- Reset any current audio configurations to their default settings:

- Go to the Settings app. Tap Apps, then select Music. Tap EQ, then choose Off to turn off Music EQ.

- Go to the Settings app. Tap Accessibility, then select Audio & Visual. Adjust the Balance slider. Set the audio volume balance to the center.

- Connect your AirPods. Go to the Settings app, then tap Bluetooth. Tap the Info button next to your AirPods, then tap Audio & Routing. Tap Equalizer, and set it to Recommended.

- Connect your headphones. Make sure that you're in a quiet environment.

- Go to the Settings app. Tap Accessibility, choose Audio & Visual, then tap Headphone Accommodations.

- Tap Customize Audio.

- Follow the instructions on your screen. When finished, tap Use Custom Settings to apply the custom settings suggested based on your choices. You can switch back to standard settings at any time.

### Manually adjust audio settings

From the Headphone Audio screen, you can also adjust tone and amplification settings manually. Tap Play Sample to hear an audio sample with your current settings. You can change these settings while listening to the sample to compare them.

You can also adjust the audio volume balance between left and right channels:

- Go to the Settings app. Tap Accessibility, then choose Audio & Visual.

- Adjust the Balance slider.

## Customize Transparency mode on your AirPods Pro

Headphone Accommodations supports Transparency mode on AirPods Pro, making quiet voices more audible and tuning the sounds of your environment to your hearing needs.

[When Transparency mode is turned on](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108918) , your Headphone Accommodations settings might change how you hear your own voice or increase your environment's noise level. You might need to adjust these settings to find a setup that's most comfortable for you. You can start from a milder setting by adjusting the sliders for Amplification, Tone, Ambient Noise Reduction, and Own Voice Amplification. Then gradually increase to the setting that's most helpful for hearing people’s voices or natural sound around you.

To customize Transparency mode settings:

- Check that you have the [latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- Put your AirPods Pro in your ears and connect them to your iPhone or iPad.

- Go to the Settings app. Tap Accessibility, then choose Audio & Visual.

- Tap Headphone Accommodations, then turn on Transparency.

- Tap Customize Transparency.

To adjust the audio-volume balance between left and right channels, adjust the Transparency Balance slider. You can also adjust Transparency mode settings by tapping the Hearing button in Control Center.

## Use Conversation Boost on your AirPods Pro

To automatically detect if a conversation is happening in front of you and focus your AirPods Pro on the person speaking to you, turn on Conversation Boost.

- Check that you have the [latest version of iOS or iPadOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- Put your AirPods Pro in your ears and connect them to your iPhone or iPad.

- Go to the Settings app, then tap Accessibility. Select Audio & Visual, then tap Headphone Accommodations.

- Make sure that Headphone Accommodations is turned on.

- Make sure that Transparency is turned on.

- Tap Customize Transparency.

- Turn on Conversation Boost.

You can also turn on Conversation Boost by tapping the Hearing button in Control Center.

## Use Custom EQ with your AirPods

You can use a three-band equalizer on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac to [personalize the sound signature](https://support.apple.com/guide/airpods/te819024i19r/web) of your AirPods 4 or later, your AirPods Pro 2 or later, or your AirPods Max 2.
