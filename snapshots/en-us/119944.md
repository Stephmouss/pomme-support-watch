# Mirror your Apple Vision Pro to another device

Learn how to mirror what you see on Apple Vision Pro to an iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, or AirPlay-compatible smart TV. You can also use Apple Vision Pro as an AirPlay Receiver to view another device's screen.

## Before you begin

- To mirror your view from Apple Vision Pro, you need an iPhone with iOS 17.2 or later, an iPad with iPadOS 17.2 or later, [a supported Mac with macOS Monterey or later](https://support.apple.com/108046#airplaytomac) , an Apple TV 4K (2nd generation or later), or an AirPlay-compatible smart TV.

- To use AirPlay Receiver on your Vision Pro, you need [visionOS 2 or later](https://support.apple.com/118481) .

- Make sure on your other device that AirPlay Receiver (or AirPlay) is turned on as described in the next section.

- Make sure that both of your devices have Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on.

## Mirror your view from your Apple Vision Pro

- Connect your Vision Pro to the same Wi-Fi network as your other device.

- Turn on AirPlay Receiver or AirPlay on your other device:

- On your Mac: From the Apple menu , choose System Settings (or System Preferences). Click General in the sidebar, click AirDrop & Handoff on the right, then turn on AirPlay Receiver. If you have macOS Monterey, [learn how to turn on AirPlay Receiver](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/set-up-your-mac-to-be-an-airplay-receiver-mchleee00ec8/12.0/mac/12.0) .

- On your iPhone or iPad: Open the Apple Vision Pro app, then tap the Vision Pro tab. Tap AirPlay Receiver, then turn on AirPlay Receiver.

- On your Apple TV: Open Settings, select AirPlay and HomeKit, then select AirPlay to turn it on.

- On your AirPlay-compatible smart TV: Check with your TV manufacturer for instructions on how to turn on AirPlay.

- [Open Control Center](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/open-control-center-tand3233e05b/visionos) .

- In the center section of Control Center, tap the Mirror My View button .

- Select your other device.* Depending on the settings of your other device, you may need to tap Accept, enter a passcode, or enter a password to begin mirroring.

- To stop mirroring, open Control Center, tap the Mirror My View button , then tap Stop Mirroring.

Mirroring shares your view in up to 1080p resolution to your AirPlay-enabled device. When you mirror your view, turns green to let you know mirroring is active. A pulsing white light on EyeSight tells others you are sharing your view.

If you play a protected video such as a movie, TV show, or music video purchased from Apple, the video displays a black screen. Vision Pro doesn’t support mirroring these types of videos to other devices.

*If you're in the European Union, the Apple Vision Pro app must be open and in the foreground on your iPhone or iPad for it to appear in the Apple Vision Pro Control Center.

### Change audio output

- Open Control Center.

- In the center section of Control Center, tap the Mirror My View button .

- Select a device in the Audio Routing area.

## Allow other devices to mirror to your Apple Vision Pro

You can turn on AirPlay Receiver on your Vision Pro so other devices can use AirPlay to mirror their screen or view to your device while you're wearing it.

- Open Settings, then tap AirPlay & Continuity.

- Turn on AirPlay Receiver.

To change who can mirror to your Vision Pro, tap Allow AirPlay For, then tap Current User or Everyone. To require a password before anyone can mirror to your Vision Pro, turn on Require Password, then set the password.

## If you can't connect

- Make sure that your Vision Pro and your other device have Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on.

- Make sure that your Vision Pro and your other device are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

- If "Allow AirPlay for" AirPlay settings on your other device is set to Current User, make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account.

## Recording your view while mirroring

To [capture a recording of your view](https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-vision-pro/take-a-capture-or-recording-of-your-view-tan1527c9e00/visionos) using the high-quality setting, start recording first, and then begin mirroring. When you use the high-quality setting, the recording and the mirroring will stop after three minutes.

To record longer while mirroring, use the standard-quality setting. Open Settings, tap General, tap Captures & Recordings, then select Standard Quality.
