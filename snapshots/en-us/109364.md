# Join a FaceTime call from an Android or Windows device

Got an Android or Windows device? With FaceTime links in iOS 15, macOS Monterey, and later, anyone can join a FaceTime call from their web browser.

## What you need

- An Android or Windows device

- A strong Wi-Fi or cellular internet connection

- The latest version of Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge

## How to join a FaceTime call on the web

- If you receive a link to a FaceTime call, open the link.

- Enter your name, then select Continue. You might need to allow FaceTime to use your microphone and camera.

- Select Join. Then wait for the call's host to let you in.

- Select Leave to leave the call.

## What you can do in a FaceTime call on the web

When you're in a FaceTime call on the web, you can do many of the things you can do in the FaceTime app.

SharePlay and screen sharing aren't available in FaceTime on the web.

[Image: Smartphone screen showing a web-based FaceTime call with a person's video, plus mic, camera, and end call controls.]

### Mute or unmute your microphone

Use the Microphone button to mute your mic or turn it back on.

[Image: the Microphone button]

### Turn your camera on or off

Use the Camera button to turn your camera on or off.

[Image: the Camera button]

### Use full-screen mode

If you see the Full Screen button , use it to make your FaceTime call take up the whole screen.

[Image: the Full Screen button]

### Switch camera view

If you see the Flip Camera button , use it to switch to your front or rear-facing camera.

[Image: the Flip Camera button]

### View participants in a grid layout

You can see people in your Group FaceTime calls in tiles arranged in a grid. The speaker's tile highlights automatically, so it's easy to know who's speaking.

- Select the Participant List and Settings Menu button .

[Image: the Participant List and Settings Menu button]

- Select the Grid Layout button . To turn Grid Layout off, select it again.

[Image: the Grid Layout button]

### Share the link with another person

- Click or tap the More button .

[Image: the More button]

- Choose Copy Link or Share Link, then choose how you want to share the link.

[Image: Screenshot of a mobile phone displaying the FaceTime call join screen. It shows a FaceTime Call with 2 active participants, and a red Join button, along with Share Link, Camera, and Microphone options.]

## Learn more

Anyone can [create a link to a FaceTime call with an iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphfc9b58aaa/ios) using iOS 15 or later or [with a Mac](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT208176#facetimelink) using macOS Monterey or later.

FaceTime links aren't supported in China mainland.

Creating a link to a FaceTime call isn't supported on iPhone models purchased in China mainland.
