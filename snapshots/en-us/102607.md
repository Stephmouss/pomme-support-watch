# Find information about your AppleCare plan

Check if your device is covered by an AppleCare plan and find details about your AppleCare coverage.

## Check if your device is covered by an AppleCare plan

To find AppleCare coverage information, you might be able to [open AppleCare & Warranty settings on your device](https://coverage.apple.com/cc/acW4820) . Or, open Settings, select General, then select AppleCare & Warranty. You can also find your plan when you [check your coverage online](https://checkcoverage.apple.com/) .

[Learn what to do if you can't find your AppleCare plan](https://support.apple.com/102190)

Not all AppleCare products are available in all countries or regions.

## Find your AppleCare coverage expiration date

- On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings, then tap General. Tap AppleCare & Warranty, then tap your device.

- On your Mac, click the Apple menu in the corner of your screen. Choose System Settings, then click General. Click AppleCare & Warranty, then select your device.

- Go to [mysupport.apple.com](https://mysupport.apple.com/) and sign in to your Apple Account. Then choose your device to see details about the support that you're eligible for — including hardware repairs and technical support.

## Get the agreement number or proof of coverage

- Go to [mysupport.apple.com](https://mysupport.apple.com/) .

- Sign in to your Apple Account.

- Choose your device.

- Click View Proof of Coverage. If you don't see your proof of coverage, make sure that you [set up your Apple Account with two-factor authentication](https://support.apple.com/102660) .
