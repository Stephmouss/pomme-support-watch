# How to redo eye setup on Apple Vision Pro

You can redo your eye setup on Apple Vision Pro any time.

## Redo eye setup on Apple Vision Pro

Put on your Apple Vision Pro, then:

- Quadruple-press the top button to enter eye and hand setup. You complete hand setup first, then eye setup.

- Or go to Settings, tap Eyes & Hands, then tap Redo Eye Setup.

If you use ZEISS Optical Inserts, you're prompted to [pair your optical inserts](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117731#pair) before you complete eye setup. Make sure that you have the [pairing code](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117731#pairing_code) that came with your optical inserts.

## Share Apple Vision Pro without redoing your settings

When you use Guest User to share your device, the guest does their own hand and eye setup at the start of the session. After they finish, Apple Vision Pro returns to your hand and eye settings.

[Learn how to let another person use your Apple Vision Pro with Guest User](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117742)

## If you can't redo eye setup

If you're not able to successfully complete eye setup on Apple Vision Pro, check the fit of your device, make sure it's clean, and make sure you're using only supported accessories, such as ZEISS Optical Inserts. Certain vision conditions might also impact the visual experience.

[Learn what to do if you need help with eye setup on Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118513)
