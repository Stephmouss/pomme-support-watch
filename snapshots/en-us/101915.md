# If you can’t access licenses in Apple Configurator

Learn what to do if you can't access your licenses after you've migrated to Apps and Books in Apple School Manager.

## Revoke all assigned licenses

- Open Apple Configurator.

- Choose Account.

- Select Sign In, then sign in to the App Store using your managed Apple Account.

- Choose Window, then select VPP Assignments.

- Click the triangle next to an app to view the serial number of the devices the app is assigned to.

- Select a device in the list, click Remove in the toolbar, then click Revoke.

- Repeat steps 4 and 5 to revoke licenses from any other apps.

## Transfer your legacy licenses

After you've revoked all assigned licenses, follow these steps.

- Sign in to Apple School Manager.

- Click Settings, then click Apps and Books.

- Transfer any legacy licenses.

## Create a new location for Apple Configurator

After you've transferred your legacy licenses, follow these steps.

- Click Locations.

- Click Add New Location.*

- Enter the new location information, then click Save. Only Apple Configurator will use this location.

## Create a new Content Manager

After you've created a new location, follow these steps.

- Click Accounts.

- Click Add New Account.*

- Enter the account details, select Content Manager as the manager role, then select the new location for Apple Configurator.

- Click Save Person.

- Click Create Sign-In, gather the name and password, then sign out.

## Purchase free licenses on the new location

After you've created a new Content Manager, follow these steps.

- Sign in to Apple School Manager using the new Content Manager account.

- Click Apps and Books.

- Select Get Started and transfer licenses to the new location if prompted.

- Search for a free app that you want to deploy.

- Select the app from the search results.

- Choose the Apple Configurator location you created.

- Enter a quantity, then click Get.

- After the licenses show available, sign in to Apple Configurator.

## Sign in to Apple Configurator

After you get the free licenses on the new location, follow these steps:

- Open Apple Configurator.

- Choose Account.

- Select Sign In, then sign in using the new Content Manager account.

- Choose Window.

- Select Volume Purchasing Assignments, then verify the licenses are listed.

After you complete these steps, you can transfer licenses to the new Apple Configurator location while signed in using a Content Manager account.
