# Add and log medications with iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch

You can add your medications in the Health app to help track and manage the medications, vitamins, and supplements you take.

## Add a medication

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the Health app.

- If you're on your iPhone, tap Search . If you're on your iPad, tap sidebar to open the sidebar.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Medications, then tap Add a Medication. If you are using [Health Records on iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105016) , you'll see suggestions from your health records.

- Enter the name of your medication in the Search field.

- If you're in the United States, you can also use your iPhone camera to add a medication. Tap the camera icon, then tap Next and follow the onscreen instructions.

- If you're outside of the United States, or if the medication is unlisted, tap Add, then tap Next.

- Choose the Medication Type, then tap Next.

- Choose the Medication Strength. You can also tap Add Custom to add a custom medication if the strength is not available. If the medication is unlisted, enter the Strength of the medication.

- Tap Next.

- Tap Change and select when you will take the medication, then tap Done. Depending on which schedule option you selected, you can also select specific days of the week, the time of day, and the cycle for when you will take the medication.

- Tap Edit under Duration to select the Start Date and End Date of the medication, then tap Done .

- Tap Next. If you receive a warning that the schedule will not appear on your device, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105064#unavailable) .

- Choose the shape of your medication and tap Next, or you can tap Skip.

- Select the color of the Medication and Background to customize it.

- Review your Medication details and enter any Optional Details.

- If you're in the United States, tap Next to [view the drug interactions between your medications](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iph2fcefa8d6) . If you're outside of the United States, tap Done.

After adding your medications, you can scroll to the More section of the Medications screen and tap Export PDF to save or share a copy of your current medications. You can also tap on a medication to review how frequently you took or skipped the medication.

If you share your health data with family and friends, you can also include which medications you're taking. Learn more about how to [share and view health data in the Health app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108323) .

## Log a medication

After you add a medication, you can log it from the Health app on your iPhone or iPad or from the Medications app on your Apple Watch.

### On your iPhone or iPad

- Open the Health app.

- If you're on your iPhone, tap Search . If you're on your iPad, tap sidebar to open the sidebar.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Medications.

- Tap the plus sign next to the medication. For As Needed medications, you can tap the medication.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Skipped or Taken, then tap Done.

### On your Apple Watch

- Open the Medications app .

[Image: The app icon for the Medications app on Apple Watch]

- Tap a medication.

- Tap Log as Taken to log the medication. You can also tap Skipped then Done if you did not take the medication.

If the medication is one you take as needed, you can scroll to the medication on the Your Medications list, tap it, then tap Log.

## Log medications from a reminder

Your iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch can also alert you when it's time to take the medications on your schedule. From the Health app on your iPhone, tap Search , then tap Medications. Scroll down and tap Options, then turn on Dose Reminders. You can also turn on follow up reminders which will alert you if you haven't logged a medication 30 minutes after the initial notification.

When you receive a reminder:

- On your iPhone or iPad, tap the notification, then tap Skipped or Taken.

- On your Apple Watch, tap the notification, then tap Skipped, Taken, or Remind Me in 10 Minutes.

If you change time zones, you'll also receive a notification that your time zone has changed. Tap the notification and confirm the details of your medication schedule.

## Remove a medication

If you're no longer taking a medication, you can archive it to remove it from your list of medications. If a medication was added in error, you can delete it.

- Archive a medication to keep the medication details and log history for your records.

- Delete a medication to remove all information and history of the medication.

### Archive a medication

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the Health app.

- If you're on your iPhone, tap Search . If you're on your iPad, tap sidebar to open the sidebar.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Medications, then scroll to Your Medications.

- Swipe left on a medication, then tap Archive.

The medication appears in your Archived Medications list.

### Delete a medication

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the Health app.

- If you're on your iPhone, tap Search . If you're on your iPad, tap sidebar to open the sidebar.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Medications.

- Scroll to Your Medications, then tap the medication you want to delete.

- Scroll to Options, then tap Delete Medication.

- Tap Delete Medication.

## If you can't view a medication schedule or receive reminders

If a medication shows Schedule Unavailable, it might have an unsupported schedule type assigned. The On a Cyclical Schedule and On Specific Days of the Week schedule types are available only with iOS 18 or later, iPadOS 18 or later, and watchOS 11 or later. Make sure that your iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch are on the latest version of iOS, iPadOS, or watchOS.

- If you're unable to update your iPhone or iPad to iOS 18 or later or iPadOS 18 or later, tap the medication that shows Schedule unavailable. Under Schedule, tap Reset Schedule and reschedule the medication with a different schedule type.

- If you're unable to update your Apple Watch to watchOS 11 or later, open the Health app on your paired iPhone or iPad, then tap the medication with the schedule you'd like to change. Under Schedule, tap Reset Schedule and reschedule the medication with a different schedule type. If you choose to keep the existing schedule, you can log medications and receive reminders only on your iPhone or iPad.

Unlisted medications are not included in potential drug interactions. Drug interactions are available only in the United States.

The Medications feature should not be used as a substitute for professional medical judgment. Additional information is available on the labels of your medications, but please consult your healthcare provider prior to making any decisions related to your health.

The Medications feature is not available with Apple Watch For Your Kids.
