# Switch your AirPods to another device

You can switch from listening to one device with your AirPods to listening to another.

## Switch automatically

These AirPods can switch automatically 1 between your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro:

- AirPods 2, 3, and 4 (both models)

- AirPods Pro (all models)

- AirPods Max (all models)

To switch between devices automatically, update all your devices to the latest versions of their operating systems and sign in to your devices with the same Apple Account using [two-factor authentication](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102660#setup) .

For example, when you're signed in with the same Apple Account on your devices, your AirPods will switch automatically from listening to music on your Mac to listening to a podcast on your iPhone. Or if you get a phone call on your iPhone that you answer on your Watch, the phone call audio automatically routes to your AirPods.

In macOS Sequoia and later, compatible AirPods can switch automatically between your Mac and your other Apple devices. In macOS Sonoma, a notification about your AirPods appears on your Mac—click Connect to confirm that you want your AirPods to switch to your Mac.

## Switch manually

You can manually switch your AirPods to another device. Make sure you are wearing your AirPods near the device you want to switch to when trying to switch devices.

### On iPhone or iPad

Tap the AirPlay button on the Now Playing screen, the Lock Screen, or in Control Center [on your iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108330) or [on your iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111790) , then choose the device that you want from the list. 2

### On Mac

Click Control Center in the menu bar, click Bluetooth, then choose your AirPods from the list.

### On Apple Watch

- Press the side button to open Control Center.

- Tap the Playback Destination button , then choose your AirPods.

### On Apple TV

- Press and hold the TV/Control Center button on on the Siri Remote to [open Control Center](https://support.apple.com/guide/tv/aside/atvb503c335f/tvos) .

- Select the General Settings tab.

- Select the Audio Controls button , then choose your AirPods.

### On Apple Vision Pro

- [Open Control Center](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117741#controlcenter) .

- Tap the Control Center button to see more options.

- Tap the Now Playing control.

- In the Now Playing window that appears, select your AirPods.

## Turn off automatic switching

If you don't want your AirPods to switch automatically, you can turn off automatic switching on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro, or other playback devices.

### On iPhone or iPad

- With your AirPods in your ears and connected to your iPhone or iPad, open the Settings app, then tap Bluetooth. Alternatively, you can open the Settings app, then choose your AirPods.

- Tap the More Info button next to your AirPods in the list of devices.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Connect to This iPhone [or iPad].

- Tap When Last Connected to This [Device].

- To turn on this feature again, tap Automatically.

### On Mac

- With your AirPods in your ears and connected to your Mac, choose Apple menu . Click System Settings, then click Bluetooth.

- Click the Options button next to your AirPods in the list of devices.

- From the Connect to This Mac menu, choose When Last Connected to This Mac.

### On Apple TV

- With your AirPods in your ears and connected to your Apple TV, go to Settings. Select Remotes and Devices, then select Bluetooth.

- Turn off Suggest Nearby AirPods.

### On Apple Vision Pro

- With your AirPods in your ears, go to Settings, then tap Bluetooth.

- Turn off Automatic Ear Detection.

### On other playback devices

To keep audio in your AirPods when your iPhone connects to a vehicle or speakers:

- On your iPhone or iPad, go to the Settings app, then tap General.

- Tap AirPlay & Continuity, then turn on “Keep Audio with Headphones.”

When Keep Audio with Headphones is turned on and a car or speaker is available, a banner appears at the top of your iPhone screen. Tap the AirPlay button to transfer audio to the car or speakers.

When Keep Audio with Headphones is turned off, tap the Undo button to move the audio back to AirPods.
