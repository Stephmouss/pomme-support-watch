# Recharge the built-in battery in your Apple keyboard, mouse, or trackpad

Learn about charging the built-in battery of your Magic Keyboard, Magic Mouse, and Magic Trackpad.

## Charge your device

Depending on your device, it may charge with a USB-C or Lightning port. To charge your device's battery, connect a USB-C or Lightning cable to its charging port, then connect the other end of the cable to your Mac or a power adapter. For the fastest battery charging performance, make sure your device is on while charging.

Magic Keyboard and Magic Trackpad can be used while charging. Magic Mouse is unavailable for use while charging.

## Check battery status

To check your device's charge level, click the Control Centre Icon in the menu bar, then click Bluetooth. Or click the Bluetooth menu in the menu bar. Your device itself does not have an indicator of its charge level.
