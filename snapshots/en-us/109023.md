# Download apps on your Apple Watch

Find apps you'll love on your Apple Watch.

- [How to get apps for your Apple Watch](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109023#get)

- [If you can't find an app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109023#find)

## How to get apps

Use the iPhone that's paired with the watch, or use the App Store on the watch.

### Get apps from the App Store on your Apple Watch

- [Press the Digital Crown](https://support.apple.com/guide/watch/apda1bf1a95b/watchos) to see a selection of your frequently and recently used apps. Then swipe up to see all of your apps.

- Tap the App Store, then tap Search to find apps using Scribble, Dictation, or the onscreen keyboard, or scroll down to discover featured apps and curated app collections.

- Tap an app to see its description, ratings and reviews, screenshots, release notes, and more.

- Tap the price or tap Get.

[Image: On an Apple Watch, the App Store is open. The screen displays an app's detail page with a Get button.]

- When prompted, double-click the side button under the Digital Crown to download and install the app.

In the App Store, if an app has a Get button instead of a price, the app is free. You won't be charged for downloading a free app. Some free apps offer in-app purchases and subscriptions that you can buy. Learn more [about in-app purchases and subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102383) .

### Get apps with the iPhone that's paired with the Apple Watch

- On your iPhone, open the Watch app.

- Tap the My Watch tab.

- Tap Apps, then scroll to the app that you want to add. Apps that you can install appear in the Available Apps section.

[Image: On an iPhone, the Watch app is shown. The Apple Store app is listed under Available Apps, with an Install button shown.]

- To add an app to your watch, tap Install.

## If you can't find an app

- [Press the Digital Crown](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105063) to see a selection of your frequently and recently used apps. Then swipe up to see all of your apps.

- If you don't see the app you're looking for, you can [see a list of apps that you bought and redownload them](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102417) . When you redownload an app, you don't have to pay for it again.

- Some watchOS apps are not currently available in every region or language. If you can't find the app in the App Store or on your Apple Watch, [see if the app is supported in your region](https://www.apple.com/watchos/feature-availability/) .
