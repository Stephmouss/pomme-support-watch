# Get additional payment options with a subscription commitment

With monthly subscriptions with a 12-month commitment, a more affordable payment option might be available for some subscriptions.

Some service providers offer monthly subscriptions with a 12-month commitment. Your rate might be more affordable than the subscription cost without a commitment. Your rate stays the same for your commitment length.

Monthly subscriptions with a 12-month commitment aren't available in the United States or Singapore.

Monthly subscriptions with a 12-month commitment are available for select third-party subscription services.

## How it works

When you subscribe to a monthly subscription with a 12-month commitment, you agree to complete all payments until your commitment is fulfilled.

Monthly subscriptions with a 12-month commitment are available on [iOS 26.4](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , [iPadOS 26.4](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) , [macOS Tahoe 26.4](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382) , [tvOS 26.4](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108414) , and [visionOS 26.4](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118481) , or later.

## Manage billing and cancellation

Your subscription automatically renews at the end of your commitment. To prevent the subscription from renewing, [cancel your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) at least one day before the renewal date.

### Canceling your monthly subscription with a 12-month commitment

At any time during your commitment, you can [cancel your subscription](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) . Canceling before your commitment ends doesn't end your payment commitment.

If you cancel your subscription before your commitment ends:

- You’ll continue to be billed for remaining payments until your commitment is fulfilled.

- You’ll keep access to the service for the remainder of your commitment.

- Your commitment won't automatically renew.

### If the payment method on file can’t be charged

You might lose service access until payment is received. To avoid interruptions to your subscriptions and purchases, [update your Apple Account payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118293) .

### Upgrading your subscription plan

If a higher-tier plan upgrade is available, you can [upgrade to a different plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118448) during your 12-month commitment.

If you upgrade your plan during your 12-month commitment:

- You'll immediately switch to the new plan.

- You'll receive a prorated refund for any unused time in your current billing period.

- Your commitment terms adjust to reflect your new plan.
