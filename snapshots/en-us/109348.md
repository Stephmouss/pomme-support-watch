# Add your vision prescription in the Health app

You can add and store your vision prescription in the Health app to easily access information about your vision or corrective eyewear.

## Add your vision prescription

If you have an image or PDF of your prescription saved on your iPhone, you can upload it to the Health app. If you don't have a saved copy, you can also manually add your vision prescription information.

### Add your vision prescription from an image

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the Health app.

- If you're on your iPhone, tap Search . If you're on your iPad, tap sidebar to open the sidebar.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Body Measurements, then tap Vision Prescription.

- Tap Add a Prescription .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

[Image: An iPhone that shows the Add a Prescription option as well as information about vision prescriptions.]

- Tap Date Issued to enter the start date of your prescription.

- Tap Add an Image .

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

[Image: An iPhone that shows the options to Scan a Document, Choose Photo, or Add from Files when adding a vision prescription.]

- Tap Scan a Document if you have a physical copy of your prescription to scan. Tap Choose Photo if your prescription is saved in the Photos app. Tap Add from Files if your prescription is saved in the Files app.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Scan or locate your prescription, then tap Done .

- Tap Done if you don't need to enter your prescription details. If prescription details are required in your country or region, tap Required or Add next to a field to add the information.

- Tap Done in the upper corner.

### Manually add your vision prescription

Vision prescriptions can vary, and not all countries or regions will require the same fields or measurements. If you manually add your vision prescription, you only need to add the information specific to the prescription in your country or region.

- On your iPhone or iPad, open the Health app.

- If you're on your iPhone, tap Search . If you're on your iPad, tap sidebar to open the sidebar.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

- Tap Body Measurements, then tap Vision Prescription.

- Tap Add a Prescription.

- Tap a field or tap Add next to the field to edit it.

[Image: An iPhone that shows manually adding the Right (OD) Sphere value of a vision prescription.]

- Once you've added your prescription details, tap Done in the upper corner.
