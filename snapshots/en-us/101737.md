# Replace the power supply in your Mac Pro (2023)

Learn how to remove and replace the power supply in your Mac Pro.

## Check required tools

- Adjustable torx screwdriver with 8 IP x 89MM 1/4" bit

- Phillips #1 screwdriver

If you need to order a replacement power supply, you can purchase it through the [Self Service Repair process](https://support.apple.com/self-service-repair) .

## Remove the housing or top cover

Follow the steps to remove the housing or the top cover on your Mac Pro.

### Mac Pro (2023)

- [Shut down your Mac Pro](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201150#turnoff) .

- Wait approximately 5-10 minutes for the computer to cool down.

- Unplug all cables except the power cord from your Mac Pro.

- Touch the metal housing on the outside of the Mac Pro to discharge any static electricity, then unplug the power cord.

- Always discharge static before you touch parts or install components inside your Mac Pro. To avoid generating static, don’t move around the room until you finish installing the power supply and slide the housing back onto the computer.

- Flip the top latch up, then twist left to unlock the housing.

[Image: Mac Pro (Rack, 2023) housing being unlocked and lifted for power supply replacement.]

- Lift the housing straight up and off of Mac Pro. Carefully set it aside.

[Image: Lifting the housing off of the Mac Pro (2023)]

### Mac Pro (Rack, 2023)

- [Shut down your Mac Pro](https://support.apple.com/kb/HT201150#turnoff) .

- Wait approximately 5-10 minutes for the computer to cool down.

- Unplug all cables except the power cord from your Mac Pro.

- Touch the metal housing on the outside of the Mac Pro to discharge any static electricity, then unplug the power cord.

- Always discharge static before you touch parts or install components inside your Mac Pro. To avoid generating static, don’t move around the room until you finish installing the power supply and slide the top cover back onto the computer.

- Slide the top cover release latches to the unlocked position, lift the top cover and pull it away from the front plate, then set it aside.

[Image: Mac Pro (Rack, 2023) top cover release latches being slid to unlock the top cover for removal.]

## Remove the power supply

- Unscrew all six Phillips #1 screws from clamp plate #3, then remove it.

- The screws are captive and stay attached to the clamp plate.

[Image: Power supply unit inside the Mac Pro (Rack, 2023)]

- Remove one 8 IP screw from the power supply grounding bracket.

[Image: Diagram of Mac Pro (Rack, 2023) showing location of 8 IP screw to remove for power supply replacement.]

- Grip both sides of the power supply and pull it out.

[Image: Diagram of Mac Pro (Rack, 2023) internal components, showing power supply location.]

## Install the new power supply

- Insert the new power supply in the bottom slot.

[Image: Location of the power supply inside the Mac Pro (Rack, 2023)]

- Push firmly and evenly until the power supply is fully seated.

- Reinstall the 8 IP screw back into the power supply grounding bracket.

[Image: Installing the new power supply in the Mac Pro (Rack, 2023).]

- Reattach clamp plate #3, then fully tighten the six Phillips #1 screws.

[Image: Diagram of the Mac Pro (Rack, 2023) showing the location of the power supply.]

## Reinstall the housing or top cover

Follow the steps to reinstall the housing or top cover on your Mac Pro.

### Mac Pro (2023)

- Carefully lower the housing over the Mac Pro, being careful not to touch any of the circuit boards.

[Image: Mac Pro (2023) case with arrow pointing to the top latch, which must be unlocked to open the case.]

- After the housing is fully seated, twist the top latch right and flip it down to lock it.

[Image: Twisting the top latch to lock the housing on the Mac Pro (Rack, 2023).]

- Connect the power cord, display, and any other peripherals.

### Mac Pro (Rack, 2023)

- Align the edge of the top cover with the slot in the front plate, then firmly press near the release latches and listen for them to click into place.

[Image: Mac Pro (Rack, 2023) top latch opening procedure]

- Connect the power cord, display, and any other peripherals.

## Send your original power supply back

If you received a replacement part from Apple, follow the steps to [send your original part back to Apple](https://support.apple.com/kb/ht213660#sendback) .
