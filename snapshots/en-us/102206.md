# Join Apple Arcade

Subscribe to Apple Arcade to access a curated collection of premium games on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro.

## How to join Apple Arcade

Apple Arcade is a subscription service that gives you and up to five family members unlimited access to 200+ premium, ad-free games that you can play across all of your devices — online or offline.

- To subscribe to Apple Arcade, do one of the following:

- On your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro, go to the Arcade tab of the App Store and look for the subscription offer.

- On your Apple TV, open the Arcade app and look for the subscription offer.

- If you don't see the subscription offer, try to download an Apple Arcade game, and you'll be prompted to subscribe.

- Choose the trial subscription offer. One trial per Apple Account.

- Sign in to the Apple Account that you use for Media & Purchases, if prompted. If you don't have an Apple Account, follow the prompts to create one. Not sure [if you have an Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102627) ?

- Confirm your billing information, if prompted. You might need to add a [valid payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111741) .

- Agree to the terms and conditions, if prompted.

With [Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108107) , you can get access to Apple Arcade for yourself and up to five family members. If you're using Family Sharing and someone in your family group is already a subscriber, you'll see a message that you have access to the subscription when you try to subscribe.

To use all of the features with Apple Arcade, make sure that your device is updated to the latest software.

[Update your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575)

[Update your Mac](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108382)

[Update your Apple TV](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108414)

[Update your Apple Vision Pro](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118481)

## What's included with Apple Arcade?

Unlimited access to 200+ premium, ad-free games is included with an Apple Arcade subscription. New game releases and updates are added all the time. To find out which games are included with Apple Arcade:

- Open the App Store.

- Go to the Arcade tab.

- Tap or click the All Games button, or scroll to the bottom and tap or click See All Games.

Learn more about [Apple Arcade and Apple Arcade pricing](https://www.apple.com/apple-arcade/) .
