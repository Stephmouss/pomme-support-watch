# View and update your Savings account information in Apple Wallet

Update your address and account notifications, manage your linked external bank accounts, and get tax forms and account statements — all from the Wallet app.

## Change your Savings email address

The email address used for your Savings account 1 is the email address that's associated with your Apple Account. You can [change your Apple Account email address](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109353) at any time.

## Change your Savings mailing address

If you update your mailing address for Savings, your Apple Card billing address is also updated automatically. 2

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap Savings account.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details.

- Scroll down and tap Account Information.

- Tap Update Address.

## Add or delete an external bank account for Savings

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap Savings account.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details.

- Scroll down and tap Bank Accounts.

- To add an external bank account: Tap Add Another Bank Account, then follow the onscreen instructions.

- To delete an external bank account: Tap Edit, tap the Remove button next to the bank account that you want to delete, then tap Done.

### External bank account eligibility

Your external bank account will need to meet the following criteria in order to be linked:

- Be a checking, savings, or other deposit account.

- Be a personal account and not a business or trust account.

- Be a financial institution based in the U.S. or U.S Territories.

- Be an account owned by you.

When you set up Savings, any eligible 3 bank account you use for your Apple Card account will automatically be added to your new Savings account. If you delete your linked account from Savings, it will also be deleted from Apple Card as a bill payment option.

## Download or export your Savings statements

Your monthly Savings statement is typically available on the 1st of each month, and shows your account activity for the statement period.

### On your iPhone

- Open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap Savings account.

- Tap the More button , then tap Documents.

- Tap Statements, then tap the month that you want to download or export.

- To download a PDF: Tap View PDF Statement, then tap the Share button to save, print, or share a copy of your statement.

- To export your transactions: Tap Export Transactions, then choose the file format that you prefer.

### At card.apple.com/savings

- Go to [card.apple.com/savings](https://card.apple.com/savings) .

- Sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Documents, click Statements, then click the download button next to the month that you want to download.

## Download a PDF of your Savings tax documents

Tax documents are available by January 31 for customers that have reportable interest.

### On your iPhone

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap Savings account.

- Tap the More button , then tap Documents.

- Tap Tax Documents. You may also have to tap the year that you want to download.

- Tap the Share button to save, print, or share a copy of your tax documents.

[Image: No alt supplied for Image]

### At card.apple.com/savings

- Go to [card.apple.com/savings](https://card.apple.com/savings) .

- Sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Documents, click Tax Documents, then click the download button next to the year that you want to download.

## Add or delete a beneficiary for your Savings account

- [Update to the latest version of iOS](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118575) .

- Open the Wallet app and tap your Apple Card, then tap Savings Account.

- Tap the More button , then tap Account Details.

- Tap Beneficiaries.

## Change your device notifications for Savings

You can opt in to receive notifications such as when your Savings rate changes, and when you earn interest on your account balance.

- On your iPhone, open the Wallet app and tap Apple Card.

- Tap Savings account.

- Tap the More button , then tap Notifications. From here you can turn notification on or off for APY Updates 4 and Interest Paid.
