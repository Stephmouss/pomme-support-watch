# Use a different Apple Account to share purchases with Family Sharing

If you have more than one Apple Account and you want to use a different account to share purchases with your Family Sharing group, you can change the account that you use for Purchase Sharing.

## Check if you can migrate purchases to your primary Apple Account

You might be able to migrate purchases that you've made from your secondary Apple Account — like apps, music, and other content — to your primary Apple Account.

[Learn more about migrating Apple Account purchases between accounts](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117267)

## Change the Apple Account that you use for Purchase Sharing on your iPhone or iPad

You can change the Apple Account that you use to share purchases with your Family Sharing group once every 90 days.

- Open the Settings app, then tap Family.

- Tap your name.

- Tap Personal Information.

[Image: On an iPhone, the Settings app is open, and a screen showing Danny Rico's personal information, including options for Personal Information, Parent/Guardian, and Subscriptions is shown.]

- In the note under your Apple Account, tap Share using secondary Apple Account.

[Image: On an iPhone, the Settings app is open. A Personal Information screen is shown, and the "Share using secondary Apple Account" link is highlighted.]

- Enter the email address or phone number and password that you use for your secondary Apple Account.

- Tap Use this Apple Account.

## Change the Apple Account that you use for Purchase Sharing on your Mac

You can change the Apple Account that you use to share with your Family Sharing group once every 90 days.

### In macOS Ventura or later

- Choose Apple menu , then click System Settings.

- Click Family, then click your name.

[Image: On a Mac, System Settings is open, and Family Sharing options, including a button to change the Apple Account used for purchase sharing, is shown.]

- In the note under your Apple Account, click Share using secondary Apple Account.

- Click Use Different Apple Account.

- Enter the email address or phone number and password that you use for your secondary Apple Account.

- Follow the onscreen instructions to complete.

## What happens when you change the Apple Account that you use to share media purchases and subscriptions

After you change the Apple Account that you use for Family Sharing, your family members can access or download purchases from that account. However, they'll no longer be able to access purchases or subscriptions from your previous account. This includes DRM-protected music, movies, TV shows, books, apps, or in-app purchases that they downloaded from your previous account, as well as any media subscriptions tied to that account.
