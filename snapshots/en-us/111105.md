# Download apps from the App Store on your Mac

Use the App Store to discover apps for your Mac that you'll love.

## How to download apps for Mac

- Open the App Store app.

- Browse or search for the app that you want to download.

- Click the price or Get button. If you see the Open or Update button instead of a price or Get button, you already bought or downloaded that app.

[Image: The GarageBand app is shown in an App Store window.]

In the App Store, if an app has a Get button instead of a price, the app is free. You won't be charged for downloading a free app. Some free apps offer in-app purchases and subscriptions that you can buy. Subscriptions and in-app purchases give you access to more features, content, and more. [Learn more about in-app purchases and subscriptions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102383) .

## How to find apps that you bought

[Use Spotlight to search for apps on your Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp1008/mac) or [use the Finder](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mchlp2605/mac) to see a full list of your applications. Some apps might ask you to [download Rosetta before you open the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102527) .

- If the app that you want doesn't appear in Spotlight or the Finder, [redownload the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102417) . When you redownload an app, you don't have to pay for it again.

- If you want to download an app that a family member bought from the App Store, [redownload the app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102417) . Make sure that you're both using [Family Sharing](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105062) .

- If you’re using a Mac with Apple silicon, you might be able to use some iPhone or iPad apps that you purchased on your Mac. [Determine whether you're using a Mac with Apple silicon](https://support.apple.com/en-us/116943) , then redownload the iPhone or iPad app onto your Mac.

## If you can't find the App Store

Look for the App Store in these two places.

### In the Dock

Look for the App Store icon in the Dock.

[Image: The Dock is shown, and the pointer is on the App Store icon.]

### In the menu at the top of your screen

If you don't see the icon in the Dock, from the Apple  menu at the top of your screen, choose App Store.

[Image: The Apple menu is shown on a Mac desktop.]

If you can't download or update apps on your Mac, [try these tips](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102331) .
