# Locate and view your iPhone, iPad, or iPod on your computer

Your device appears in the Finder or the Apple Devices app when you connect it to your computer.

## View your device in the Finder

With [macOS Catalina or later](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033) , your connected device's name appears in [the Finder](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/organize-your-files-in-the-finder-mchlp2605/mac) sidebar, showing its details.

[Image: An iPhone listed under Locations in the Finder sidebar.]

If your Mac is using an earlier version of macOS, or you're using a Windows PC, your device appears in the [Apple Devices](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/welcome/windows) app. [If you don't see your device in the Finder, learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108643) .

## View your device in the Apple Devices app

Connect your device to your PC with a USB cable. Its icon then appears in the Apple Devices app sidebar.

[Image: Apple Devices app on Windows shows iPhone 16 Pro in sidebar.]

[If you don't see your device in the sidebar, learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/guide/devices-windows/device-sidebar-connected-windows-computer-mchld88ac7da/windows) .
