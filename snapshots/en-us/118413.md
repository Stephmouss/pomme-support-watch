# Redeem digital copies of DVDs or Blu-rays in the Apple TV app

Some DVD, Blu-ray, and 4K titles might include a Digital Copy of your movie in addition to the physical disc.

## Redeem digital copies with a content code

Learn what to do [if you can't redeem your content code](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118413#cannotredeem) .

## Redeem on your iPhone or iPad

- In the Apple TV app, tap your photo, initials, or the Account Settings button . You might need to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Tap Redeem Code.

[Image: An iPhone showing the Apple Account page in the Apple TV app with Redeem Code option highlighted.]

- Tap Enter Code Manually, then enter the 12-digit code printed on the insert included with your disc.

- Tap Redeem.

Learn what to do [if you can't redeem your content code](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118413#cannotredeem) .

## Redeem on your Mac

- Open the Apple TV app.

- From the Account menu in the menu bar, click Redeem. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account.

[Image: The Apple TV app on Mac showing the expanded Account menu with the Redeem option highlighted.]

- Click Enter Code Manually, then enter the 12-digit code printed on the insert included with your disc. You might be able to use the camera on your Mac to redeem your code.

- Click Redeem.

- You can download the digital copy of your movie now or download it later.

Learn what to do [if you can't redeem your content code](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118413#cannotredeem) .

## Redeem on your Windows PC

- In the Apple TV app, click your name in the sidebar. Or, click Sign In and sign in to your Apple Account.

- Click Redeem. You might need to sign in to your Apple Account.

- Enter the 12-digit code printed on the insert included with your disc.

- Click Redeem.

- You can download the digital copy of your movie now or download it later.

Learn what to do [if you can't redeem your content code](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118413#cannotredeem) .

## If you can't redeem the content code

- Look for an expiration date on the packaging.

- Make sure that you enter the correct characters. Some letters and numbers look alike.

- Make sure that the packaging on your item says that the Digital Copy code will work in your country or region. For example, you need a U.S. Apple Account to use a code from a U.S. DVD, and you need an Australian Apple Account to use a code from an Australian DVD.

- If your Digital Copy DVD, Blu-ray, or 4K title is damaged, scratched, or missing its redemption code or Digital Copy disc, contact the vendor for help.

- If you still can't redeem the code or download your Digital Copy, contact [Apple Support](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
