Develop novel technologies integrating sensors, actuators and communication interfaces. Design and develop software for complex embedded and mechatronic systems. Support prototype development, testing, and field demonstrations. Document research findings, methodologies, and technical developments for internal knowledge sharing and external publications.

6+ years of relevant experience developing embedded software for robotic control systems. Strong proficiency in C/C++ for real-time applications. Experience with control systems for electromechanical hardware. Experience integrating sensors and actuators into embedded systems Familiarity with ROS, Linux, and robotics software frameworks. Demonstrated success delivering complex multidisciplinary technical projects. Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Robotics, Computer Science, or a related field.

## Preferred qualifications

Advanced degree (BS/MS/PhD)in Robotics, Controls, Mechatronics, or a related field. Experience with tactile sensing or haptics in electromechanical systems. Experience bringing complex hardware-software systems from prototype to production.
