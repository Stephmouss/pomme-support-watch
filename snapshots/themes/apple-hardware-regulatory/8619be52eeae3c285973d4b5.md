# Senior Tactile Research Engineer - Special Projects (Robotics)

Team: Software and Services, Machine Learning and AI

Location: Santa Clara

## Summary

We are seeking research scientists and engineers with strong backgrounds across mechatronics, controls, haptics, robotics, simulation, and machine learning to drive breakthrough innovations in intelligent robotic systems. This position requires a hands-on approach, integrating software and hardware solutions to push the boundaries of perception, control, and intelligence. You will work alongside world-class researchers and engineers, contributing to technologies that power the Apple ecosystem.

## Responsibilities

Lead the exploration of novel sensing and actuation technologies. Define and evaluate robotic system architectures spanning, sensing, actuation, and controls. Guide multidisciplinary teams through prototyping, validation, and deployment. Collaborate with partners and researchers on novel technology applications. Support technical planning and development and drive strategy for next-generation robotic platforms.

## Minimum qualifications

10+ years of relevant experience developing complex mechatronic or robotic systems. Experience with sensing, actuation, controls, and system integration. Experience with a wide range of mechatronics prototyping tools and technologies. Experience leading multidisciplinary technical projects and teams. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills for complex systems. Experience developing products and technologies from concept through production. Strong communication and stakeholder management skills. Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Robotics, Physics, or a related field.

## Preferred qualifications

Advanced degree (BS/MS/PhD) in Engineering, Robotics, or a related field. Experience with a range of tactile sensing, haptics, and actuation technologies in robotic systems. Experience bringing complex hardware-software systems from prototype to production.
