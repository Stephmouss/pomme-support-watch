## Responsibilities

Implement and train state-of-the-art machine learning models for tactile-enabled robotic systems. Optimize and deploy machine learning models on physical systems, ensuring policing durability across sim-to-real-gaps. Curate and generate training data from simulation and real-world demonstrations ensuring adequate coverage of complex contact physics. Establish evaluation metrics and testing protocols to benchmark policy performance. Collaborate across disciplines leverage expertise and advance impact across multiple stakeholders. Document research findings, methodologies, and technical developments for internal knowledge sharing and external publications.

## Minimum qualifications

3+ years of relevant experience successfully deploying tactile-based policies in robotics. 6+ years of relevant experience in robotics and machine learning. Strong proficiency in Python and C++ with experience in complex, multi-language systems. Expertise in machine learning algorithms and deep learning frameworks such as PyTorch and TensorFlow. Strong software engineering skills, delivering clean, well-tested, and optimized code. Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills. Ability to work effectively in multidisciplinary teams. Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD degree in Engineering, Robotics, Computer Science, or a related field.

## Preferred qualifications

Functional understanding of tactile sensing and haptics technologies to solve challenges in interaction and manipulation. Strong foundation in robot kinematics, dynamics, motion planning, and control. Experience with a wide range of physics simulation software and their contact mechanics (e.g. Mujoco, IssacLab simulator, Drake, etc.). Successful record of employing machine learning techniques to achieve useful system performance.
