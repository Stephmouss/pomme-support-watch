# Health Sensing EE Engineer

Team: Hardware

Location: Shanghai

## Summary

Are you inventive and unusually adept at anticipating what the future holds? Do you have a passion for human health? As part of our Health Sensing group, you’ll design innovative ways for customers to learn about and manage their health. Join our team, and you’ll develop the hardware systems that monitor the human body and its data. You’ll work across a variety of disciplines to help develop sensors, systems, and applications for health, wellness, and fitness. You’ll help millions of customers find new ways their personal devices can serve as functional health tools. Join us, and you’ll empower people to make informed decisions that shape their well-being in daily life and beyond. The Health Sensing team design, develop, and launch next-generation Sensor Technologies in Apple products. The team features a collaborative and hands-on environment that fosters engineering excellence, creativity, and innovation.

## Description

The Health Sensing EE Engineer will own the hardware development, test and validation of components, modules and systems critical to Health Sensing features. The role will also drive software integration, hardware debug and FA, and data analysis to inform design decisions.

## Responsibilities

Managing multinational vendors and the projects designated for the team, which will span the lifecycle of a new product from initial design and functional specification of new hardware features and overall subsystem design all the way to supporting and validating these features (35%) Driving the development of component/module vendors and tester/evaluation plan bring-up to deliver scalable solution to support Apple mass production in a high quality way (35%) Reviewing and commenting on schematics and system reviews, developing analysis tools for hardware performance, performing complex system level tests, enhancing and resolving issues in existing products, and performing unit testing and integration testing (15%) Collaborating with hardware, electrical, quality, and test engineers that will be applying the health sensing feature, as well as writing design and architecture documents (15%) Up to 25% travel required.

## Minimum qualifications

3+ years of hands-on experience and proven track record in electrical or system hardware engineering Strong electrical engineering background on analog, mixed signal and failure analysis with mass production experience Proficiency in Python, MATLAB, LabView or similar scripting tools Experience in developing test plans, test cases, assessing risk, and filing appropriate defects Excellent written and verbal communication skills, as well as solid teamwork and leadership skills

## Preferred qualifications

Knowledge of system and firmware development is a huge plus Experience in working overseas manufacturers and vendors Experience in COMSOL, Ansys Q3D or similar multi-physics FEA simulation tools
