# Health Sensing HW – Sensor EE Architect

Team: Hardware

Location: San Diego Metro Area, San Francisco Bay Area

## Summary

The Health Sensing HW team is responsible for architecting and integrating the next generation of optical health sensors into Apple products, and we are looking for a sensing system EE architect to help us bring exciting technologies to customers.

## Description

You will design, develop, and launch next-generation optical health sensing technologies. You will be the main EE architect for the sensor system; you will analyze tradeoffs between system constraints and sensor performance, enhance product design, and drive failure analysis and design convergence from proof-of-concept all the way to mass production. You will collaborate with cross-functional teams - including Optical Engineers, Electrical Engineers, Mechanical Engineers, Process Engineers, Reliability Engineers, Test Engineers, and Firmware/Software Engineers - to deliver state-of-the-art sensing. You will apply your influence and lead the cross-functional teams through key milestones in design and integration.

## Responsibilities

Definition and trade-off studies on sensor system requirements and performance, and architect the sensor EE system Collaborate and guide the internal teams on optical module design and overall sensor system design Leadership on proof-of-concept hardware design, product design evaluation, product prototype builds, and mass production qualification. Leadership of failure analysis and corrective action implementation at manufacturing sites, including assembly processes, testing, and calibration; parametric-data analysis of end-of-line yield and post-reliability failures. Documentation of design and test requirements. Business travel up to 10% as required

## Minimum qualifications

BS and 10+ years of relevant industry experience required (Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Optics, or equivalent) Strong foundation in analog and mixed signal design. Schematic/PCB layout and components selection experience. Experience in design or manufacturing or integration of sensor products Scripting experience for data analysis and automation.

## Preferred qualifications

Strong background in optical sensor architecture, design and manufacturing. (Mechanical, EE, packaging, system thinking and requirements management) Hands-on experience with EE and Optical lab equipment. Experience supporting volume manufacturing, including test design, failure analysis, and statistical analysis. Excellent written and verbal communication skills and solid collaboration and leadership skills. Strong teamwork abilities and self-motivation to tackle technical challenges. Ability to travel internationally
