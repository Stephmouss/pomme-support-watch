# Pixel Development Engineer

Team: Hardware

Location: Grenoble

## Summary

Do you love taking on big challenges that require exceptionally creative solutions? Do you deeply understand how an incredible camera experience should work? As part of our Camera Technologies group, you’ll help design the innovative technology that allows each generation of Apple products to produce photos even more incredible than the last. You’ll design and develop groundbreaking ideas for everything involved in our camera systems, from sensor to lens. You’ll collaborate with teams across Apple to research, design, develop, test, and qualify camera hardware for Apple products. Together, we’ll craft a magical photography experience that redefines what millions of customers can expect from their cameras.

## Description

Evaluating sophisticated pixel technology from global image sensor vendors. Collaborating with global image sensor suppliers to develop new image sensors for different applications. Working with internal and external teams to assure that sufficient and correct testing is implemented at all stages to guarantee performance. Documenting and presenting your results to both internal and external engineering teams. Multi-functional collaboration to develop next generation camera products and features.

## Minimum qualifications

BS degree in the relevant fields. Experience in image sensor pixel and sensor development, sensor characterization, sensor bring up and debug, or wafer level testing. Familiar with image sensor technology, image sensor performance metrics, image sensor usage, and integration into camera systems. Awareness of general industry trends and technical challenges. Hands-on experience with silicon bring up, sensor characterization and issue debug. Ability to innovate and build new and unique solutions. Experience with data analysis tools (JMP, MATLAB, Excel etc.). English language fluency (written and verbal)

## Preferred qualifications

Master or Ph.D. degree in the relevant fields. Knowledgeable in all aspects of sensor usage that you can efficiently work with and support the camera team. Proven teamwork and leadership skills!
