# eSIM carriers and services in France

## France

### Wireless carriers that support eSIM Carrier Activation

With eSIM Carrier Activation, your carrier can assign an eSIM to your iPhone digitally at the time of purchase, or if you call your carrier for an eSIM after setup.

- [Bouygues Telecom](https://www.assistance.bouyguestelecom.fr/s/article/esim-iphone)

- [Free](https://assistance.free.fr/articles/968)

- [SFR](https://www.sfr.fr/offre-mobile/esim)

### Wireless carriers that support eSIM Quick Transfer

With eSIM Quick Transfer, you can transfer your phone number from your previous iPhone to your new iPhone without contacting your carrier. You can also convert your physical SIM to an eSIM.

- [Bouygues Telecom](https://www.assistance.bouyguestelecom.fr/s/article/transferer-esim)

- [Free](https://assistance.free.fr/articles/922)

- [Orange](https://assistance.orange.fr/mobile-tablette/tous-les-mobiles-et-tablettes/installer-et-utiliser/debuter-et-prendre-en-main/la-carte-sim/comment-activer-une-esim-lors-de-l-initialisation-d-un-iphone-_437716-967271)

- [SFR](https://www.sfr.fr/offre-mobile/esim)

### Wireless carriers that support other eSIM activation methods

These carriers support other ways of activating eSIM on iPhone, like scanning a QR code or using a carrier app.

- [BetterRoaming](https://www.betterroaming.com/fr/)

- [Bouygues Telecom](https://www.assistance.bouyguestelecom.fr/s/article/Tout-savoir-sur-la-e-Sim)

- [Free](https://assistance.free.fr/articles/telecharger-et-activer-mon-esim-968)

- [Orange](https://assistance.orange.fr/oid/935774)

- [SFR](https://www.sfr.fr/offre-mobile/esim)

- [Ubigi](https://cellulardata.ubigi.com/esim/)

### Wireless carriers that support eSIM activation on prepaid plans for international travelers

When traveling, you can stay connected through an international roaming plan from your existing carrier. Alternatively, these carriers offer prepaid plans if you would like a local line while traveling internationally.

- [Bouygues Telecom](https://www.bouyguestelecom.fr/carte-prepayee/vacation-prepaid-plan/)

- [Free](https://mobile.free.fr/en/voyages-forfait-europe-france)

- [Orange](https://boutique.orange.fr/vitrine/carte-prepayee-orange-holiday-eng/)

- [SFR](https://www.sfr.fr/offre-mobile/carte-sim-voyage-europe)
