# Business Development Manager - Wallet, Payments and Commerce

Team: Software and Services

Location: Paris

## Summary

Apple is looking for a Business Development Manager based in France to drive and scale payment services for our digital payments and commerce platforms in the French market, as well as help define and enable new Wallet capabilities—including Access, Ticketing, Loyalty, and Memberships. As our ideal candidate, you thrive in a dynamic environment focused on shaping next-generation user experiences. As a member of the Wallet, Payments, and Commerce team, you will partner with cross-functional groups to drive sales and marketing programs for Apple Gift Card and Direct Carrier Billing in France, while engaging external partners and ecosystem stakeholders to roll out Wallet Services into new areas. End-to-end project ownership and strong communication with business leaders will be critical for success. The ideal candidate will have strong working knowledge of the retail and eCommerce environment, experience in contract negotiation, projects and programs management, product and market development, capable of supporting successful launches and initiatives. You will be able to Identify new regional partners, negotiate business terms and be the “go to” person for the projects and relationships. Creativity, persistence and the ability to manage multiple parties and projects from initiation to completion while providing consistent updates and communication to business leaders will be crucial to the success in this role.

## Description

-Be the local "go to" person for the Apple Gift Card, Carrier Billing and Wallet products - Establish, develop and grow strong business relationships with Retail and eCommerce partners, Carriers and a diverse portfolio of ecosystem providers to drive products adoption - Develop strategy and tactics to drive substantive and measurable change in adoption rate, usage, penetration, and manage sales forecasts, merchandising, marketing budgets and track performance of programs - Drive implementation of payment and Wallet solutions ensuring all product requirements are completed to Apple standards - Manage multiple parties and projects from initiation to completion while providing consistent updates and communication to business leader

## Minimum qualifications

Proven track record in business development, partner management, or commercial leadership within digital payments, retail, carrier billing, or digital wallet ecosystems. Extensive experience leading end-to-end partner lifecycles—from structuring and negotiating complex commercial terms to managing senior strategic relationships. Demonstrated ability to manage complex, multi-stakeholder programs end-to-end within a multinational or matrixed organization. Exceptional executive communication, negotiation, and cross-functional leadership skills. Professional fluency in French and English (written and verbal).

## Preferred qualifications

Flexibility to handle directional changes and balance moving priorities and a portfolio of initiatives to ensure project success and drive impact Ability to contribute consistently and positively in a high-paced, constantly evolving and sometimes unpredictable work environment Self-starter and results oriented; demonstrated results building lasting relationships Degree education in relevant field desirable but candidates from all education backgrounds are encouraged to apply
