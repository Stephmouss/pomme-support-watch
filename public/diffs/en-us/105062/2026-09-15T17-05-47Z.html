7## How to get started with Family Sharing

9To set up Family Sharing, one adult in your household — the family organizer — invites the other family members. Each member uses their own Apple Account, so everyone’s experience is personalized and private.

27## How to manage a child's device with Family Sharing
27## How to manage a child's device with Family Sharing and parental controls
28
28
29Parents and guardians can create an Apple Account for children in their Family Sharing group that are under 13.* With their own Apple Account, a child can use their own device — and even make their own purchasing decisions. With Screen Time and parental controls, a parent or guardian can easily manage the child's device to help keep their experience safe, private, and age appropriate.
29The Family Sharing organizer or a parent/guardian, can create a Child Account for children in their Family Sharing group. With their own Child Account, a child can use their own device — and even make their own purchasing decisions. With Screen Time and parental controls, the organizer or parent/guardian can easily manage the child's device to help keep their experience safe, private, and age appropriate. A Child Account is required for children under 13 and available for children up to 18. *
