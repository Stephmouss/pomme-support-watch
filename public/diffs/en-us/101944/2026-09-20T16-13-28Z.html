1# When and how to use diagnostics mode with iPhone and iPad

2
3If you repair your iPhone using Self Service Repair, you might be asked to put your iPhone in diagnostics mode as part of System Configuration. Learn what to do.
3Diagnostics mode is a part of Recovery Assistant for iPhone and iPad. Diagnostics mode allows you to check for potential hardware or software issues on your device or to finish a repair for a replaced part.
4
4
5- Turn off your iPhone: Press and hold either volume button and the side button until the power-off slider appears. Then drag the slider and wait 30 seconds for your device to turn off.
5## Learn about diagnostics mode
6
6
7[Image: iphone-14-pro-power-off-diagram]
7Diagnostics mode allows you to run tests to verify if your device's hardware and software are functioning properly. It guides you through tests for components like the display, camera, and Face ID. You might use it to troubleshoot a specific concern or to finish a repair for a part that's been replaced.
8
8
9- Press and hold both the volume up and volume down buttons.

25## Start diagnostics mode

33## Learn about the tests
34
35You can run the following tests in diagnostics mode.
36
37### Mobile Resource Inspector
38
39Mobile Resource Inspector is an initial triage tool that checks the device's software version, verifies the presence of certain parts, and checks whether the parts pass basic functionality tests.
40
41### Audio
42
43Consider your surroundings before running this test; your device will play loud test tones through the speakers.
44
45This test asks you to verify that you hear certain tones and that they aren't distorted. Next, to confirm that each microphone can detect sound, the test asks you to record a short audio clip. The recording is used only during the test and is deleted after the test.
46
47### Display pixel anomalies
48
49This test helps identify pixel anomalies and other display-panel issues. The test shows a series of colors and patterns:
50
51- For each color or pattern, draw or tap on the display to indicate if you see any anomalies. If you mark an area accidentally, tap the rectangle to remove it.
52
53- Swipe left with two fingers to proceed through each color and pattern.
54
55- When you're finished, press Done.
56
57If you want to exit the test, press the side button or one of the volume buttons.
58
59### Multi-Touch
60
61A case or screen protector could interfere with this test; if you have a case or screen protector, remove it before starting the test.
62
63This test helps identify issues with Multi-Touch response:
64
65- Slide your finger over the whole display to identify any areas of the display that don't respond properly to touch.
66
67- As you slide your finger, blue rectangles appear on the display indicating areas that are responding to touch.
68
69### Face ID
70
71This test checks the Face ID sensors.
72
73- Hold the device about an arm's length away, facing you, until the test is finished.
74
75- This test can take about a minute. You don't need to stay still or look at the device during the test.
76
77The test doesn't take a photo, and the scans aren't saved.
78
79### Camera image quality
80
81This test helps identify camera image-quality issues for the front and rear cameras.
82
83- The test will ask you to take a photo using each camera. To evaluate image quality, use a solid white paper or wall, or a photo test chart.
84
85- After you take a photo, indicate any camera-quality issues by drawing or tapping the issue with your finger. If you mark an area of the photo accidentally, tap the rectangle to remove it.
86
87All photos taken for this test appear only during the test and aren't saved.
88
89## If diagnostics mode finds an issue
90
91If diagnostics mode finds an issue, you might need service. [Get service](https://getsupport.apple.com/?caller=kbase) .
92
93When evaluating your device, the service provider might run additional tests or might run one or more tests again.
