153## About cycle deviations
176## About cycle deviations
154
177
155Cycle Tracking can inform you if your logged cycle history from the previous six months shows a pattern of irregular cycles, infrequent periods, prolonged periods, or persistent spotting. If you receive an alert or notification about a possible cycle deviation, you’ll be able to review your logged cycle history. If it’s accurate, you’ll be able to learn about the cycle deviation detected and export the last 12 months of your cycle history as a PDF to share with your healthcare provider. When you have a recent or ongoing cycle factor logged, cycle deviation detection is turned off automatically.
178Cycle Tracking can inform you if your logged cycle history from the previous six months shows a pattern of irregular cycles, infrequent periods, prolonged periods, or persistent spotting.
