23## MacBook Pro with M5 Pro chip
23- [MacBook Pro with M2 Max or M3 Max chip](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149475)
24
24
25MacBook Pro models with the M5 Pro chip support up to three external displays simultaneously with the built-in display, based on the resolution and refresh rate of each external display. Closing the lid of your MacBook Pro with M5 Pro chip will not increase the number of external displays that can be supported.
25- [MacBook Pro with M2 Pro or M3 Pro chip](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149476)

27### One external display
27- [MacBook Pro with M3 chip](https://support.apple.com/en-us/149477)
28
28
29Supports one external display in the following configuration:

33### Two external displays
34
35Supports two external displays in any of these configurations:
36
37- One display up to a native resolution of 8K (7680 x 4320) at 60Hz, 5K (5120 x 2880) at 120Hz, or 4K (3840 x 2160) at 240Hz, plus a second display up to a native resolution of 5K (5120 x 2880) at 120Hz or 4K (3840 x 2160) at 200Hz over Thunderbolt or HDMI
38
39### Three external displays
40
41Supports three external displays in the following configuration:
42
43- Three displays up to a native resolution of 6K (6144 x 3456) at 60Hz or 4K (3840 x 2160) at 144Hz over Thunderbolt or HDMI
44
45Using a supported hub or daisy-chaining displays can enable up to 3 displays over a single Thunderbolt port, but does not increase the maximum number of displays that you can connect. Connect the display with the highest resolution first.

143## MacBook Pro with M3 chip
144
145MacBook Pro models introduced in 2023 or later with the M3 chip [support up to two external displays simultaneously with lid closed](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117373) , based on the resolution and refresh rate of each external display.

148
149Supports one external display in the following configuration:
150
151- One display up to a native resolution of 6K (6144 x 3456) at 60Hz over Thunderbolt
152
153- One display up to a native resolution of 4K (3840 x 2160) at 144Hz over HDMI
154
155### Two external displays
156
157Supports two external displays in the following configuration with lid closed:
158
159- One display up to a native resolution of 6K (6144 x 3456) at 60Hz, and one display up to a native resolution of 5K (5120 x 2880) at 60Hz over Thunderbolt
160
161- One display up to a native resolution of 6K (6144 x 3456) at 60Hz over Thunderbolt, and one display up to a native resolution of 4K (3840 x 2160) at 100Hz over HDMI
162
163Using a supported hub or daisy-chaining displays can enable up to 2 displays over a single Thunderbolt port, but does not increase the maximum number of displays that you can connect. Connect the display with the highest resolution first.

227## Maximum Display Support
228
229Available scaled resolutions for external displays in [Displays settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/displays-settings-on-mac-mh40768/mac) might vary by available system resources and type of connected displays. Scaled resolutions greater than the macOS default are not reflected in the maximum support listed above.
