45## Tips for getting the fastest charging speed
45## Tips for getting the fastest charging speed
46
46
47- Use the charger that came in the box with your Apple Watch, since that charger is guaranteed to provide the fastest charging speeds for your particular Apple Watch model. For example, the charger that comes with Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 charges those models faster than older chargers. To distinguish the charger that came with your Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4, look for model number A3277 printed on the part of the USB-C plug that you insert into your computer or power adapter.
48
47- Avoid extreme temperatures when charging your Apple Watch. Fast charging might not work in conditions that are too cold or too hot.
49- Avoid extreme temperatures when charging your Apple Watch. Fast charging might not work in conditions that are too cold or too hot.
48
50
49- Don't use your Apple Watch while it's charging. Charging takes longer if you keep the display on, install software updates, or transfer media when charging. Charging can also take longer if it's the first time you fast charge Apple Watch after pairing with iPhone.
