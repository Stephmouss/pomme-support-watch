81- Booking.com : 3% Daily Cash is only earned on eligible stays and car rentals, (a) booked through the exclusive Booking.com link provided [here](https://www.booking.com/c/apple-card/index.html?aid=2417311&label=mkt123sc-6a6d907e-0c26-4c5f-87f3-4fff6fb85c7d) , (b) when making payment in advance directly to Booking.com, (c) using an eligible rate type, (d) and using Apple Card with Apple Pay. Eligible stays include those paid in full, directly to Booking.com, before your check in date using any of the following eligible rate types at the time of booking: “Pay now”, “Pay later”, “Pay part now, part later”, “Pay on [selected date]”, or “Pay part now, the rest on [selected date]” (for any of the previous options where a payment is made later, the ‘later’ or ‘selected date’ must occur before check in and be paid directly to Booking.com). Stays booked using “Pay later” (if paid at check-in at property), “Pay when you stay”, or “Pay at the property” rate types will NOT earn 3% Daily Cash. Eligible car rentals include those paid directly to Booking.com using either eligible rate type at the time of booking: “Pay now” or “Pay part now, part later” (with “Pay part now, part later”, only the “Pay part now” portion will earn 3%). Car rentals booked using “Pay at pickup” rate type will NOT earn 3% Daily Cash. Eligible stays and car rentals which accept Apple Pay can be identified on the site with a badge labeled “Apple Card Benefits”, but for any badged stay or car rental you must then choose an eligible rate type and pay in advance directly to Booking.com using Apple Card with Apple Pay to earn 3% Daily Cash on the booking. Stays and car rentals booked through Booking.com’s mobile app or through Booking.com’s website, when not using the exclusive link provided, may not be eligible for 3% Daily Cash. Excluded from earning 3% Daily Cash are: (1) any bookings paid for using “Pay later” (if paid at property), “Pay when you stay”, “Pay at the property”, “Pay at pickup” or “Partner Offer” rate types, (2) any amounts paid using payment methods other than Apple Card with Apple Pay, (3) bookings in the categories of flights, flight + hotel (or any other combination of a package deal that includes flight, hotel and/or car rental), cruises, attractions, and airport taxis, (4) any amounts paid directly to third-party stay or car rental companies at any point including those paid at time of arrival (e.g. the “Pay part later” portions of car rental rates, deposits, local fees, taxes, or additional services added), (5) bookings made through (i) any corporate apps or websites that Booking.com provides to companies for employee corporate travel; or (ii) any B2B or partner-specific, co-branded or white label sites, (6) bookings made through Booking.com website and mobile app when accessed from a non-U.S. IP address. Booking.com is not responsible for fulfillment of Apple Card Daily Cash back.
