61## Share Screen Time settings across devices
62

64

66
67- Turn on Share Across Devices.
68
69### Using Screen Time with multiple devices
70
71If you use Screen Time to [manage your child's iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108806) — and you've turned on Share Across Devices on those devices and Apple Vision Pro — changes you make to Screen Time settings on one device will appear on all devices. Not all Screen Time features that are available on iPhone or iPad are also available on Vision Pro.
72
