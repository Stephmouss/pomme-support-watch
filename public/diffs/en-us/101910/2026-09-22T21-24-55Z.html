17## Mac Studio (2025) with M4 Max
17- [Mac Studio (2023) with M2 Ultra](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121267)

18
19Mac Studio (2025) models with the M4 Max chip support up to five external displays simultaneously, based on the resolution and refresh rate of each external display. You can connect displays to the Thunderbolt 5 ports and HDMI port on the back of the computer.

57## Mac Studio (2025) with M3 Ultra
58
59Mac Studio (2025) models with the M3 Ultra chip support up to eight external displays simultaneously, based on the resolution and refresh rate of each external display. You can connect displays to the Thunderbolt 5 ports and HDMI port on the front and back of the computer.
60
61Mac Studio (2025) with M3 Ultra has two port groups that each support up to four displays. An 8K (7680 x 4320) at 60Hz or 4K (3840 x 2160) at 240Hz display connected over HDMI uses the resources of two displays in that port group.
62
63- Port group 1: The two Thunderbolt ports on the front and the HDMI port on the back
64
65- Port group 2: The four Thunderbolt ports on the back

259## Maximum Display Support
260
261Available scaled resolutions for external displays in [Displays settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/displays-settings-on-mac-mh40768/mac) might vary by available system resources and type of connected displays. Scaled resolutions greater than the macOS default are not reflected in the maximum support listed above.
