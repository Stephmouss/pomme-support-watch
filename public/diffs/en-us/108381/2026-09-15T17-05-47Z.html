5## Before you start
6
7To restore your Apple Watch, you need an iPhone or an iPad. You can use a friend’s device; it doesn’t have to be your iPad or the iPhone that’s paired with your watch. No personal data or information from your watch is shared with the device that you use to restore it.
8
9The iPhone or iPad should have the latest version of iOS or iPadOS, and it needs to have Wi-Fi* and Bluetooth turned on.
10
