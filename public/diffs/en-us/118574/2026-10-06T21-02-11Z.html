1# How to use account recovery when you can’t reset your Apple Account password
1# What to expect during account recovery
2
2
3If you use two-factor authentication and can’t sign in or reset your password, you can regain access after an account recovery waiting period.
3Account recovery helps you regain access to your Apple Account when you can’t reset your password. Learn what to expect during the wait, how to check your status, and the few things to do (and not to do) while you wait. Apple Support can't shorten the wait period.
4
4
5## What is account recovery?
5If you haven't started account recovery, first try to change or reset your password to regain access to your account. [Learn how...](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102656)
6
6
7The account recovery process helps you reset your password and regain access to your account. If you can’t remember your Apple Account password, make sure you’ve tried everything else before starting account recovery – for example, using a different device that’s already signed in to change your password.
7## What to expect
8
8
9For security reasons, it might take several days or longer before you can use your account again after you start account recovery. Contacting Apple Support can’t help you shorten this time. This delay is inconvenient, but it’s important so that Apple can keep your account and information safe.
9- Confirmation email — You’ll receive a confirmation email (or iMessage for accounts that use only a phone number) with the specific date and time that you can expect to regain access to your Apple Account. This email arrives within 72 hours after you start account recovery.
10
10
11## Before starting account recovery
11- Wait period — For your security, account recovery might take several days or longer. This delay is inconvenient, but it’s important so that Apple can keep your account and information safe. Contacting Apple Support can’t help shorten the wait.
12
12
13Try other methods to access your account before starting account recovery.
13- Email and text or automated call when your account is ready — When your account is ready, Apple will contact you with next steps via email and by text or an automated phone call at the phone number used to start account recovery.
14
14
15- If you haven’t already, try to [reset your password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102656) on a trusted device.
15## What not to do during the wait
16
16
17- If you don’t know which email address or phone number you use with your Apple Account, try different email addresses or phone numbers that might be associated with your Apple Account.
17A few things will interrupt or cancel your recovery request.
18
18
19- If you don’t have a device that you can use, you can [reset your password using the Apple Support app](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102656#supportapp) on a family member’s iPhone or iPad. You can also visit an Apple Store and ask to use a device.
19- Don't contact Apple Support to try to shorten the wait. They can't shorten the wait. The waiting period is fixed.
20
20
21- If you previously set up an account recovery contact, they can also [help you reset your password](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102608) .
21- Don't submit another request . Unless you find additional recovery factors, submitting another won't speed up the process and might extend the wait.
22
22
23- If you know your password, but don’t have access to your trusted phone number for verification codes, [learn what to do](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122621#no-access) .
23## What to do with your Apple devices during the wait
24
24
25## Start account recovery
25- If you started account recovery on a device with iOS 17, iPadOS 17, or macOS 27 or later, you can continue to use the device that you used to start account recovery.
26
26
27If you’ve tried everything else, start the account recovery process.
27- If you started account recovery on the web, turn off all your Apple devices during the account recovery wait.

29### On your Apple device
29- If you have another Apple device where you’re already signed in to your Apple Account, you can change your Apple Account password on that device. Your account recovery request will be canceled and you’ll be able to access your account with the new password.
30
30
31The quickest and easiest way to start account recovery is right on your device. Once you start account recovery, don’t use your other Apple devices as that might interrupt or delay the recovery process.
31- If you can’t change your password on that device — for example, if you’ve forgotten the passcode — turn it off. Using an Apple device signed in to your account will cancel your account recovery request.
32
32
33- In Settings on an iPhone or iPad, or System Settings on a Mac, try to sign in on your device.
33## If you don't receive the confirmation email
34
34
35- If you don’t know your password, you’ll get the option to start account recovery.
35Wait the full 72 hours from when you started account recovery. Then check your spam or junk folders. If you still can’t find it, go to [iforgot.apple.com](https://iforgot.apple.com) and check whether your request is active.
36
36
37- Turn off all other devices that are currently signed in with your Apple Account until account recovery is complete. If your Apple Account is in use during your request, your account recovery will be cancelled automatically.
37- If it's active, you can find the expected time for recovery on the website. The email might have been delayed.
38
38
39- If you started your account recovery in Settings, System Settings, or the Apple Support app, you can continue to use that specific device during the account recovery period.
39- If it's not active, your request might not have been submitted successfully or it might have been canceled (for example, if you used a device where you are signed in with your Apple Account during the wait). You can submit a new request to start again.

41### On the web
41## How to check your status
42
42
43You can also start account recovery on the web. Don’t use your Apple devices after starting account recovery.
43- To check your status, review the confirmation email for your expected completion time or when more information will be available.
44
44
45- Go to [iforgot.apple.com](https://iforgot.apple.com) using your device’s browser.
45- You can also check your status on [iforgot.apple.com](https://iforgot.apple.com) . Make sure to enter the same information (including the same email address and phone number) that you used to start the request. Otherwise, you might extend the wait period.
46
46
47- Click Reset Password, then follow the onscreen steps to start account recovery.
47## When the wait is over
48
48
49- Turn off all devices that are currently signed in with your Apple Account until account recovery is complete. If possible, also turn off the device that you used to start account recovery on the web. If your Apple Account is in use during your request, your account recovery will be cancelled automatically.
49- Apple will send you an email and contact you by text or automated phone call at the phone number used to start account recovery with instructions to regain access to your account.
50
50
51## After you start account recovery
51- If you don't receive the text or call, you can go directly to apple.com/recover after the time shown in your confirmation email.
52
52
53You’ll get an email 1 with a confirmation of your request and the date and time when you can expect to regain access. This email arrives within 72 hours.
53## How to cancel
54
54
55It might be several days or more before you can reset your password. Contacting Apple Support can’t help you shorten this time.
55- If you remember your password and sign in successfully, your recovery request cancels automatically and you can use your Apple Account immediately.
56
56
571 If you have a phone number-based Apple Account without an email associated with it, you’ll receive this as an iMessage in the Messages app.
57- If you didn't request account recovery, someone may be trying to access your account. Use the cancellation link in the confirmation email immediately, then consider changing your password and reviewing your account security.
58
59### When the waiting period is over
60
61- Apple will send you a text or automated phone call with instructions to regain access to your account.
62
63- If you don’t receive the text or call, once the period specified in the original email is over, you can still go directly to [iforgot.apple.com](https://iforgot.apple.com/) . Follow the instructions to regain access to your Apple Account.
64
65### Can the waiting period be shortened?
66
67No. Contacting Apple Support can’t help you shorten this time.
68
69### Check when your account recovery request will be complete
70
71Check the email with the date and time when you can expect to regain access. This email arrives within 72 hours.
72
73You can also see how long it will be until your account is ready for recovery, or when more information will be available. Go to [iforgot.apple.com](https://iforgot.apple.com/) and enter the same Apple Account email address or phone number that you used to initiate the request.
74
75### If you need to cancel account recovery
76
77- If you remember your information and can sign in successfully, your wait period cancels automatically and you can use your Apple Account immediately.
78
79- To cancel a recovery request that you did not make, follow the instructions in your confirmation email.
