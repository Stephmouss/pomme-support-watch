103## How to delete a child's Apple Account
103## How to delete a child's Apple Account
104
104
105Children under 13 * can't be removed from a Family Sharing group without being transferred to another family group, or having their Apple Account deleted by a parent or guardian. If a child in the family group is under 13, the family organizer needs to delete the child's Apple Account and let the deletion take effect before they can remove the child from the family group.
105Children under 13 * can't be removed from a Family Sharing group without being transferred to another family group, or having their Apple Account deleted. To delete their Apple Account, a child can sign in to their account on Apple's [Data and Privacy page](https://privacy.apple.com) and select “Request to delete your account." The family organizer will receive a request to approve the account deletion via email. When the family organizer approves the request, they'll go through the process of deleting the child account.

106
107If your child is between 13-17 * , they can request deletion of their Apple Account, but it requires approval from a parent or guardian. The approval request is sent to the family organizer's primary Apple Account email address. When approving the request from this email, you'll go through the process of deleting the child's Apple Account.
107It takes time to process the deletion of the child account. The family organizer will not be able to disband a Family Sharing group or delete their own Apple Account while this request is processing.
