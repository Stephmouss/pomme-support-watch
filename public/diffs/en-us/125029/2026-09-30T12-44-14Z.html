166
167The following intelligence features are available exclusively to Apple Creator Studio subscribers:
168
169- Image Generation 1 in Keynote, Pages, Numbers, Pixelmator Pro, and Freeform
170
171- Shape Generation 1 in Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Pixelmator Pro
172
173- Super Resolution and Auto Crop in Keynote, Pages, Numbers, and Freeform
174
175- Generate Presentation 2,3 , Generate Presenter Notes 1,3 , and Slide Clean Up 3 in Keynote
176
177- Magic Fill in Numbers

179### Intelligence features system requirements
180
181The Generate Image, Generate Shape, Generate Presenter Notes, and Generate Presentation features require specific devices, iOS and macOS versions, and an Apple Account with iCloud enabled:
182
183- An iPhone 15 Pro or an iPhone 15 Pro Max or later running iOS 26 or later.
184
185- An iPad with an A17 Pro chip, or an iPad Pro or iPad Air with an M1 chip or later, running iPadOS 26 or later.
186
187- A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 26 or later.
188
189- An Apple Account [with iCloud set up and active](https://support.apple.com/guide/icloud/set-up-icloud-on-your-devices-mmfc0f1e2a/icloud) . Managed account configurations may affect the availability of intelligence features.

201### Usage limits of intelligence features
202
203The Generate Image, Generate Shape, Generate Presenter Notes, and Generate Presentation features are subject to limitations:
204
205- The Generate Presentation feature is powered by OpenAI and is available only in regions that [OpenAI supports](https://help.openai.com/articles/5347006-openai-api-supported-countries-and-territories) and in regions where [Apple Creator Studio is available](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
206
207- The Generate Image, Generate Shape, and Generate Presenter Notes features are powered by Google Cloud and are available in certain regions 1 where [Apple Creator Studio is available](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118205) .
208
209- Availability may be further limited by network restrictions or blocked by local restrictions, network providers, or government regulations.
210
211- The exact number of images, slides, and presenter notes that you can generate varies based on the complexity of the queries, server availability, and network availability. At a minimum per month, if used exclusively, you can:
212
213- Generate 50 images
214
215- Generate 250 shapes
216
217- Generate 50 presentations (with approximately 8–10 slides each)
218
219- Generate presenter notes for 700 slides
220
221- Usage limits reset each month.
222
223- To view your usage status:
224
225- On Mac, depending on the app you're using, choose Keynote, Pages, Numbers, or Freeform from the menu bar, choose Intelligence Features, then choose Show Usage Status.
226
227- On iPhone or iPad, open Keynote, Pages, Numbers, or Freeform, tap the More button , tap Intelligence Features, then tap Show Usage Status.
228
229- These features may lead to unexpected results. To report a concern with the results:
230
231- For a generated presentation, click or tap "Report a concern" in the alert that appears when slides have finished generating.
232
233- For generated presenter notes, click or tap the Report a concern button in the presenter notes after the notes have been generated.
234
235- For a generated image or a generated shape, click or tap the More button on the created image, then click or tap "Report a concern" in the menu.

237- These features do not integrate with personal ChatGPT and Google accounts.
