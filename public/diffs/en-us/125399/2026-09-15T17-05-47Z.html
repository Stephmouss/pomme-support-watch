5## How app exception requests work
5In iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27, Screen Time introduces new parental controls to help parents manage what kids access, when they use their devices, and how they stay safe while communicating with others. To access these updated parental controls, make sure every device in your Family Sharing group is using these software versions. The Family Sharing organizer or a parent/guardian also needs to update the parental controls for the family group.

37## How to respond to an app exception request
38
39If your child tries to download an app with an age rating higher than the age restriction that you set for them, they'll see a message that says content restrictions are enabled and they need to ask their parent or guardian for permission. If they ask for an exception, you'll receive a message in the Messages app. You can approve or decline the request right from the message notification or in the Messages app. If you don't approve the request within 24 hours, your child will have to send another request.
