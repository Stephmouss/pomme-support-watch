9If you create an account with an app or website that supports Sign in with Apple, you can choose to share your email address if you're familiar with the app or website, or hide your email address if you prefer more privacy. If you choose the Hide My Email option, only the app or website you created the account with can use this unique email address to communicate with you. When you select this option in Sign in with Apple, the Hide My Email addresses created use the address domain @privaterelay.appleid.com. This means that only emails sent from designated email addresses by the app or website will be automatically forwarded to the verified email address set on your Apple Account.
