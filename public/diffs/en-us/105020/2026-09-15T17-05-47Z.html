5### Identify your version of Siri
6
6
7- [iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105020#ipados)
7The features and interactions available to you depend on your device hardware, software version, and settings.
8
9To find out which version you have, open Settings on your device, then tap or click Siri. In some software versions, Siri preferences are in Settings under Apple Intelligence & Siri.
10
11- Siri AI (Beta): available for you to [turn on in Settings](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893) on [Apple Intelligence-capable devices](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121115) in supported languages and regions. When you have Siri AI, the word "beta" appears on the Siri logo in Settings.
12
13- Siri with Apple Intelligence: the Siri you have when your device uses Apple Intelligence and Siri AI is not enabled. Shown as "Siri Classic" when you have a choice between Siri versions.
14
15- Siri: for Apple TV, HomePod, and other devices that are not Apple Intelligence-capable; where Apple Intelligence isn't available in your region; or if you have turned off Apple Intelligence.
16
17On Apple Intelligence-capable models, Apple Watch uses the same version of Siri as its paired iPhone.

19## Talk to Siri
20
21For most devices, just say Siri or Hey Siri, then immediately say what you need.
22
23To adjust voice activation for Siri, open Settings on your device, tap or click Siri,* then tap or click Requests. On some versions of iOS and iPadOS, you might need to tap Talk & Type to Siri, Talk to Siri, or Listen for Siri.
24
25*On some versions of iOS and iPadOS, Siri preferences are in Settings under Apple Intelligence & Siri.
26
27## Type to Siri
28
29If you are using Siri AI (Beta), you can always type to Siri.
30
31- On iPhone or iPad, swipe down from the center of the screen, then type your request in the Search or ask field. You can also type to Siri in the Siri app, or touch and hold an item—text, an image, a document, and more—and tap Ask Siri to open the Search or ask field.
32
33- On Mac, press Command–Space or click the Siri button in the menu bar to open Spotlight, then type your request. You can also type to Siri in the Siri app, or Control-click an item and choose Ask Siri to type your request directly in the menu.
34
35For Siri with Apple Intelligence:
36
37- On iPhone or iPad, turn on [Type to Siri](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-siri-accessibility-settings-iphaff1d606/ios) , then double-tap the bottom of the screen and type your request.
38
39- On a Mac, Type to Siri by clicking the Siri button . On Apple Watch, press and hold the Digital Crown, then type your request.
40
41If you're not using Apple Intelligence, or if you want to always type your requests instead of speaking them, Type to Siri is an accessibility setting:
42
43- On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap Accessibility, tap Siri, then turn on Type to Siri.
44
45- On Mac, open System Settings, click Accessibility, click Siri, then turn on Type to Siri.
46
47- On Apple Watch, press the Digital Crown, open Settings, tap Accessibility, tap Siri, then turn on Type to Siri. Type to Siri isn't currently available with Siri AI on Apple Watch.
48
49After you turn on Type to Siri, activate Siri with a button, then type your request.
50
51## Activate Siri with a button
52
53Instead of saying Siri or Hey Siri, hold down a button or touch the device and speak your request.
54
55### On iPhone and iPad
56
57[Image: iPhone with arrow pointing to the Side button to activate Siri.]
58
59[Image: iPad with arrow pointing to the Top button to activate Siri.]
60
61Press the side button on iPhone, or the top button on iPad, then make your request. If your device has a Home button, press the Home button instead.
62
63To turn button activation on or off, open Settings, tap Siri, tap Requests, then turn on Press Side Button for Siri. On some versions of iOS and iPadOS, Siri preferences are in Settings under Apple Intelligence & Siri.
64
65Find out what to do if [Siri isn't working on your iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105037) .

67### On Mac
68
69On an Apple keyboard with function keys, press and hold the Dictation key or click the Siri button in the menu bar to activate Siri. If you have Siri AI, press Command-Spacebar to open Spotlight, then type your request to Siri.

23## How to use Siri on your iPhone
24
25Here are a few ways to activate Siri.
26
27### Use your voice
28
29Just say Siri* or Hey Siri, then immediately say what you need.
30
31Adjust voice activation for Siri: open Settings, tap Apple Intelligence & Siri (or Siri), then tap Talk & Type to Siri (or Talk to Siri).
32
33### Press and release the button
34
35[Image: iPhone with arrow pointing to the Side button to activate Siri.]
36
37- If your iPhone doesn't have a Home button, press the Side button, then make your request.
38
39- If your iPhone has a Home button, press the Home button, then make your request.
40
41- To make a longer request, press and hold the Side or Home button until you're finished with your request.
42
43Turn Side button activation for Siri on or off: open Settings, tap Apple Intelligence & Siri (or Siri), then tap Talk & Type to Siri (or Talk to Siri).
44
45### Type instead of speaking to Siri
46
47If you have both [Type to Siri](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-siri-accessibility-settings-iphaff1d606/ios) and Apple Intelligence turned on, double-tap the bottom of the screen and type your request. If you have Type to Siri turned on but not Apple Intelligence, press the button to activate Siri and type your request.
