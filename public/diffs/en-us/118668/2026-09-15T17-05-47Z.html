111## How to view podcast transcripts
112
113- Open the Podcasts app and start listening to an episode.
114
115- Tap the mini player at the bottom of the screen to expand the full screen player.
116
117- Tap the Transcript button . If the Transcript button is grayed out, transcripts are not available for that episode.
118
119In the transcript view you can:
120
121- Follow along with your favorite shows while reading time-synced transcripts.
122
123- Scroll forwards and backwards through the podcast and tap to play the section you want to listen to.
124
125- Tap and hold a section to select the text or share it.
126
127- Tap Search to find specific words and go directly to those sections.
128
129- View show chapters and tap them to go to that section of the podcast.
130
131- Find links to music and other content mentioned in the show, or follow mentioned podcasts.
132
133- Report an issue with the transcript by press and holding the section, tap Report a Concern, then follow the onscreen steps.
134
135Transcripts are not available in Belarus and Russia.
