19## Before you begin
20
21- On a [Mac with Apple silicon](https://support.apple.com/en-us/116943) , starting up from an external storage device requires a working built-in startup disk. If your built-in startup disk is experiencing a hardware failure, you can’t use an external startup disk instead.
22
23- If your built-in startup disk has a different major version of macOS than your external startup disk, be aware that a more recent macOS version on one disk can install changes designed to keep your Mac secure, and these changes can affect your computer's ability to use a significantly earlier version of macOS on the other disk.
