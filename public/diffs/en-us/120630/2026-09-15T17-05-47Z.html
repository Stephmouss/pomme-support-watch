53## Add an app back to your Home Screen
54
55If you can't find an app, you may need to [download it again](https://support.apple.com/102590) . If you find an app on a hidden Home Screen page or in the App Library, you can move it back to a Home Screen page:
56
57- In your App Library, touch and hold the app.
58
59- As you hold the app, drag it to the Home Screen page where you want to place the app.
60
61- Tap the Done button .
