5## What devices on your Apple Account can do
6
7When you sign in with your Apple Account on a device, it’s added to your Apple Account device list. This includes Android mobile devices, Windows PCs, and streaming devices that you use with your Apple Account for Apple Music or Apple TV. Depending on the device type, devices signed in to your Apple Account could be used to:

10
11- Receive two-factor authentication verification codes

6
18
7If your device is missing, you can [use Find My](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101593) to find your device and take additional actions that can help you recover it and keep your data safe.
19If you aren’t using your device anymore — or if you don’t recognize a listed device — you can remove it from the list. Devices that you remove from your account won’t have access to iCloud, Find My, or other Apple services, and won’t display verification codes for two-factor authentication. The device won’t reappear in the list unless you sign in on that device again.
