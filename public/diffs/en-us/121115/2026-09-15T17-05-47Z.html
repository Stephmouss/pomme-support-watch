1# How to get the next generation of Apple Intelligence
2
2
3Apple Intelligence features are integrated across apps and experiences to help users communicate, express themselves, and get things done. Feature availability can vary by platform, language, and region, as noted.
3The next generation of Apple Intelligence is integrated deeply into the OS and powers helpful features across apps that simplify the things you do every day, from browsing the web, to generating images in virtually any style, staying on top of your communication and schedule, editing photos, and more.

5## Turn on Apple Intelligence
5The next generation of Apple Intelligence is automatically available on eligible devices when you update to the 27.0 software releases across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, and visionOS in supported languages and regions.
6
6
7To get started with Apple Intelligence features on your compatible iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro, update your device to the latest software version, and ensure you have Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings under Apple Intelligence & Siri.
7Apple Intelligence also powers an entirely new version of Siri: Siri AI. Find out [how to get Siri AI (Beta)](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127893) .
8
9After you update your software, on-device Apple Intelligence models will start downloading; time to download varies. For the fastest model download experience, keep your device connected to Wi-Fi and power while models are downloading.
10
11To access Apple Intelligence features on a compatible Apple Watch, ensure it is paired with an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone.
