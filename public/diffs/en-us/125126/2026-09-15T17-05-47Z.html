7Emergency SOS via satellite can help you connect with public emergency services under exceptional circumstances when no other means of reaching emergency services are available. If you call or text emergency services and can't connect because you're somewhere with no cellular and Wi-Fi coverage, your Apple Watch Ultra 3 tries to connect you via satellite.
