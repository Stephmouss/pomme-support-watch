3With Screen Time, there are a number of settings and parental controls that you can use to help keep your child's device usage safe, private, and age appropriate.
3Screen Time comes with parental controls that can help you manage the apps and websites your child can access, who they communicate with, when they use their devices, and more.

5## What you can do with parental controls
5In iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27, Screen Time introduces new parental controls to help parents manage what kids access, when they use devices, and how they stay safe while communicating with others. To access these updated parental controls, make sure every device in your Family Sharing group is using these software versions. The Family Sharing organizer or a parent/guardian also needs to update the parental controls for the family group.
6
6
7Apple's parental controls are important tools that allow parents and guardians to choose how — and how often — their children use their devices. Parental controls are built directly into iPhone, iPad, and Mac, and can help you manage the downloads and purchases your child can make, who they can communicate with, and the apps and other content they can access.
7If you can't update every device in your Family Sharing group, you can continue to use the previous version of Screen Time and parental controls, or adjust the parental controls directly on the child's device.
