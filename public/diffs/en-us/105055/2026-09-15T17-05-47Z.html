17When Family Sharing is set up, the family organizer can turn on Ask to Buy. So when your child wants to make an eligible purchase or download — such as downloading an app from the App Store, buying an in-app purchase, or redownloading a shared app — they send you a request. You can use your own device to view information about the app and decide to approve or decline the request.
29When Family Sharing is set up, the family organizer or a parent/guardian can turn on Ask to Buy.
