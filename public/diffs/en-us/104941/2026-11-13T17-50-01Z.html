261### AppleCare One
262
263When you purchase a new product from Apple, you can [get an AppleCare One plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/127207) . If you don't buy AppleCare One when you purchase your new device, you have 60 days after purchase to buy an AppleCare One plan using your iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Vision Pro. You'll start with coverage for up to three devices, and you can [add more devices to your AppleCare One plan](https://support.apple.com/122224) anytime. For iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, Theft and Loss coverage is included in your AppleCare One plan.
