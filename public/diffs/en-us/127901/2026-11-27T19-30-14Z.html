29## What happens when you hit your daily usage limit
30
31When you hit your daily usage limit for a particular feature category, you’ll be notified. Each device has its own daily usage limit per feature category. Once you reach a limit, you can wait until your daily usage limit resets on that device or immediately increase your limit by upgrading to an eligible iCloud+ plan.
32
33### When your daily usage limit resets
34
35All feature categories reset daily at the same time, and limits do not roll over to subsequent days. Your limit resets around midnight in your current time zone except:
36
37- If you’re traveling internationally, your limit resets around midnight in the central time zone of the country or region of your Apple Account.
38
39- If you share an iCloud+ plan with a Family Sharing group, your limit resets based on the time zone of the iCloud+ plan purchaser.
40
41- If you’re not signed in to your Apple Account on your device, your limit resets around 24 hours after you started using that feature category.
42
43## How to increase your Apple Intelligence and Siri AI daily usage limit
44
45For more access, upgrade to an iCloud+ plan with at least 200 GB of storage. After you reach your daily usage limit on a device, you gain access to an increased usage limit that is shared by all devices signed in to your Apple Account and across any Family members sharing your plan, on a first-come, first-served basis.
