31## Store your photos and videos in iCloud
32
32
33By default, your photos and videos are stored on your device in their original, high-resolution version. This means that they use a lot of space on your iPhone and iPad. With [iCloud Photos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108782) , you can make more space available on your device and still have access to your entire full-resolution collection wherever you go.
33By default, your photos and videos are stored on your device in their original, high-resolution version. This means that they use a lot of space on your iPhone and iPad. When you use [iCloud Photos](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108782) , Optimize Storage is on by default, allowing you to make more space available on your device and still have access to your entire full-resolution collection wherever you go.
