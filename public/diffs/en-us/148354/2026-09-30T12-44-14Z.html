91- Siri Recap uses Private Cloud Compute. The Secure Exclave on Apple Watch encrypts the detected audio and transmits it to the Secure Exclave on the paired iPhone. The encrypted audio is then decrypted, transcribed, and condensed inside the iPhone Secure Exclave. To create a summary, Siri Recap sends an encrypted, condensed version of the conversation transcript from your iPhone to Private Cloud Compute. This condensed text is designed to be less than half the length of the original transcript and distills the conversation to its core meaning. Content suggestive of conversation tone, non-essential language, filler words, and redundancies are removed, while preserving the topics discussed. This condensed text is sent to PCC along with some contextual information to generate the summary. The finished, text-based summary is encrypted and sent from Private Cloud Compute back to iPhone and Apple Watch.
