5To use Walkie-Talkie, you and your friend both need Apple Watch Series 1 or later with watchOS 5.3 or later. You also both need to [set up the FaceTime app](https://support.apple.com/105088) on your iPhone with iOS 12.4 and be able to make and receive FaceTime audio calls. [The Walkie-Talkie app isn't available in all countries or regions](https://www.apple.com/watchos/feature-availability/#apps-walkie-talkie) .
5With watchOS 27, Walkie-Talkie has been discontinued. After you update your Apple Watch to watchOS 27, the Walkie-Talkie app will no longer appear on your watch, and it's not available to download from the App Store. If you’re using watchOS 26 or earlier, you won’t be able to start a Walkie-Talkie conversation with a friend who has updated to watchOS 27.
