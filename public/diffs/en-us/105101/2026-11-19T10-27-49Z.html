13Studio Display XDR is calibrated to a new color matching function, Apple CMF 2026, designed to improve visual consistency across different displays. To accurately measure Studio Display XDR, measurement instruments must support and be set to the calibration space of its preset. Studio Display XDR's general use presets are calibrated with Apple CMF 2026, but CIE 1931 is available through the use of [reference modes](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108321) . Apple is engaged in standardization efforts through the International Commission on Illumination (CIE) to develop and define an industry-wide standard and has partnered with calibration instrument and software vendors to add support for Apple CMF 2026.
13Studio Display XDR and MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display are calibrated to a new color matching function, Apple CMF 2026, designed to improve visual consistency across different displays. To accurately measure these displays, measurement instruments must support and be set to the calibration space of its preset. Studio Display XDR and MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display general use presets are calibrated with Apple CMF 2026, but CIE 1931 is available through the use of presets and reference modes. To learn more about Apple CMF 2026, see the [Display Color Calibration with Apple CMF 2026 Technology Overview](https://www.apple.com/iphone/pdf/Display_Color_Calibration_with_Apple_CMF_Tech_Overview_SEPT_2026.pdf) .
