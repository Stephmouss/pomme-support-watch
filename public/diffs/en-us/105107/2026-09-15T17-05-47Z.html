17## How it works
18
18
19The Family Sharing organizer sets up location sharing for the entire family group. After location sharing is turned on, the organizer's location is automatically shared with everyone, and any new family members added to the group later. Then, each family member can choose whether or not to share their location with the rest of the family group. When you agree to share your location, your family members see your location in the Find My app and Messages. In watchOS 6 or later, they can also see your location in the Find People app.
