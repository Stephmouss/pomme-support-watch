5When you share an iCloud+ subscription, everyone in the Family Sharing group uses their own Apple Account on their device. That way your photos, documents, and other information stay private, even though you're sharing access to the service. You simply share the features and space available in iCloud+ with your family members, so you only have one plan to manage. The Family Sharing organizer doesn't have to be the one who shares the iCloud+ subscription with the family group. You can allow another member to take over sharing iCloud+ with the entire family group.
