43## Use Face ID to make purchases
39Face ID works best when your iPhone or iPad is approximately 10–20 inches from your face. Face ID works in portrait and landscape orientation on iPhone 13 models and later using iOS 16 and later. For earlier models, Face ID works on iPhone only when it's in portrait orientation and you're facing the TrueDepth camera. On iPad, you can use Face ID when your iPad is in any orientation.

45You can use Face ID to authenticate purchases with Apple Pay and in the iTunes Store, App Store, and Book Store in Apple Books.
41You can [use Face ID with face masks](https://support.apple.com/102452) if you use iPhone 12 or later with iOS 15.4 and later. When you use Face ID with a mask on your iPhone, it only works in portrait orientation. Face ID with a mask does not work on iPad.

43Face ID works with many prescription or clear glasses. If you can't get Face ID to work with clear or prescription glasses, try to [add an alternate appearance](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-face-id-iph6d162927a/ios) while wearing your glasses. If you have an appearance that can look vastly different from facial hair, heavy makeup, or other changes, you can [set up an alternate appearance](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-face-id-iph6d162927a/ios) so Face ID still recognizes you.
