7## Using Apple power adapters
7On certain Apple power adapters, the AC plug (also known as a duckhead) is removable. You can replace it with an Apple extension cable to place the power adapter farther from the outlet, or replace it with an adapter for another country or region to charge your device while traveling. Just slide the duckhead off, then attach the extension cable or adapter.

11The design of your power adapter's AC connector can differ depending on when the power adapter was made. Because of updated regulations, new extension cables and power adapters use AC Connector 2, which has a pill-shaped design. AC Connector 1 has a notched design. Extension cables and duckheads aren't cross-compatible between AC connector types, so make sure that your accessory has the correct AC connector for your power adapter.
