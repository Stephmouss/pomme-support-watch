41## If you’re trying to sign in on a new Apple device
41- Your trusted phone number — This is a phone number that you’ve set up to receive two-factor authentication verification codes from Apple.
42
42
43If you’re not signed in to your Apple Account on an Apple device, you can select “Forgot password or don’t have an Apple Account?” during device setup on the Apple Account sign-in screen.
43- A code from your recovery contact — If you set up a recovery contact, reach out to make sure that your recovery contact is ready to provide the 6-digit code or to help regain access in person.
44
44
45If you skipped signing in during setup, you can reset your password in Settings:
45- Your Apple Account email address — Make sure that you can access the email address associated with your Apple Account.
46
46
47- iPhone or iPad: Open the Settings app, then tap Apple Account. Select “Sign in manually”, then tap “Forgot password?” and follow the onscreen instructions.
47- The credit card associated with your Apple Account — You might be asked to confirm the CVV of the credit card associated with your Apple Account. If the verification is declined by the card issuer, you get an alert that says the information you entered is incorrect (even if you correctly entered the CVV). If this happens, select "Don't have access to this card."

48
49- Mac: From the Apple menu , choose System Settings. Click Sign in, then click “Forgot password” and follow the onscreen instructions.
49- A recovery key or Security Keys for Apple Account — If you set up one of these optional security features, the process to regain access to your account is different. Learn how to regain access using your [recovery key](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109345) or your [physical security key](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102637) .
